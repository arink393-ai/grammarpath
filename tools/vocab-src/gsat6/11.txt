# 學測 L6 11：limp – mingle

literal || adj. || 字面的 || A literal translation is often not natural. || 逐字直譯往往不自然。 || literal meaning=字面意思 || The literal meaning ___ "break a leg" is very different from its real meaning. || of|for|to || the meaning of = …的意思。 || 「break a leg」的字面意思和真正的意思差很多。

lofty || adj. || 崇高的；高聳的 || She has lofty goals. || 她有崇高的目標。 || lofty ideals=崇高理想 || He has lofty dreams ___ changing the world. || of|for|in || dreams of = …的夢想。 || 他有改變世界的遠大夢想。

magnify || v. || 放大 || A microscope magnifies things. || 顯微鏡能放大東西。 || magnifying glass=放大鏡 || Use a magnifying glass ___ see the tiny words. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 用放大鏡看那些小字。
