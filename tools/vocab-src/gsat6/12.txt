# 學測 L6 12：miraculous – offspring
miraculous || adj. || 奇蹟般的 || He made a miraculous recovery. || 他奇蹟般地康復了。 || miraculous escape=奇蹟似的脫險 || It was miraculous ___ nobody was hurt in the crash. || that|what|which || It is + 形容詞 + that 子句。 || 撞車中沒有人受傷，真是奇蹟。
miscellaneous || adj. || 各式各樣的 || The box is full of miscellaneous items. || 箱子裡裝滿各種雜物。 || miscellaneous items=雜物 || The drawer is full ___ miscellaneous things. || of|with|in || be full of = 充滿…。 || 抽屜裡塞滿了各種雜物。
mischievous || adj. || 調皮的 || The mischievous boy hid his sister's shoes. || 那個調皮的男孩把姊姊的鞋藏起來。 || mischievous smile=淘氣的笑容 || The mischievous puppy chewed ___ my shoes. || up|on|over || chew up = 嚼爛。 || 那隻調皮的小狗把我的鞋子咬爛了。
mistress || n. || 女主人 || She is the mistress of the house. || 她是這個家的女主人。 || mistress of ceremonies=女司儀 || She served as the mistress ___ ceremonies at the wedding. || of|for|in || mistress of ceremonies = 女司儀。 || 她在婚禮上擔任女司儀。
mobilize || v. || 動員 || The school mobilized volunteers. || 學校動員了志工。 || mobilize support=動員支持 || The city mobilized hundreds of volunteers ___ clean the beach. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 市政府動員了數百名志工清理海灘。
modernization || n. || 現代化 || The modernization of the city took years. || 城市的現代化花了好幾年。 || modernization=現代化 || The modernization ___ the train system cost billions. || of|for|to || the modernization of = …的現代化。 || 鐵路系統現代化花費了數十億。
modernize || v. || 使現代化 || The school modernized its classrooms. || 學校讓教室現代化。 || modernize=更新設備 || The old factory ___ modernized last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那座舊工廠去年進行了現代化。
momentum || n. || 動力；氣勢 || The team gained momentum. || 球隊士氣大振。 || gain momentum=獲得動力 || The campaign is gaining momentum ___ young people. || among|between|along || among = 在…之中。 || 這項運動在年輕人之間越來越有聲勢。
monarch || n. || 君主 || The monarch ruled for 50 years. || 那位君主統治了五十年。 || absolute monarch=專制君主 || Queen Elizabeth II was the monarch ___ ruled the longest in British history. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 伊莉莎白二世是英國史上在位最久的君主。
monetary || adj. || 貨幣的 || The prize has no monetary value. || 這個獎品沒有金錢價值。 || monetary value=金錢價值 || The old letters have little monetary value, ___ they mean a lot to me. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 那些舊信沒什麼金錢價值，但對我意義重大。
monotony || n. || 單調 || I hate the monotony of my job. || 我討厭工作的單調。 || break the monotony=打破單調 || Music helps break the monotony ___ long car trips. || of|for|in || the monotony of = …的單調。 || 音樂有助於打破長途開車的單調。
monstrous || adj. || 巨大的；駭人的 || It was a monstrous wave. || 那是一道巨浪。 || monstrous=怪物般的 || A monstrous wave hit the ship ___ the storm. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 暴風雨中一道巨浪打中了船。
moody || adj. || 喜怒無常的 || Teenagers can be moody. || 青少年可能喜怒無常。 || moody=情緒化 || He gets moody ___ he is hungry. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 他餓的時候就會情緒化。
morale || n. || 士氣 || The win boosted the team's morale. || 這場勝利提振了球隊的士氣。 || boost morale=提振士氣 || The coach's speech boosted the morale ___ the players. || of|for|to || the morale of = …的士氣。 || 教練的演說提振了球員的士氣。
mortal || adj./n. || 凡人的；致命的 || All humans are mortal. || 人皆會死。 || mortal danger=生命危險 || Humans are mortal, ___ the gods in Greek myths are not. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 人類終有一死，但希臘神話中的神不會死。
motherhood || n. || 母親身分 || Motherhood changed her life. || 成為母親改變了她的人生。 || motherhood=母職 || She wrote a book about the joys ___ motherhood. || of|for|in || the joys of = …的喜悅。 || 她寫了一本關於為人母喜悅的書。
motto || n. || 座右銘 || My motto is "Never give up." || 我的座右銘是「永不放棄」。 || school motto=校訓 || "Never give up" is the motto ___ I live by. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 live by 的受詞。 || 「永不放棄」是我的座右銘。
mound || n. || 土堆；投手丘 || The pitcher stood on the mound. || 投手站在投手丘上。 || pitcher's mound=投手丘 || The pitcher stood ___ the mound, ready to throw. || on|in|at || on the mound = 在投手丘上。 || 投手站在投手丘上準備投球。
mourn || v. || 哀悼 || The nation mourned the death of the leader. || 全國哀悼領袖的逝世。 || mourn for=為…哀悼 || The whole country mourned ___ the victims. || for|to|with || mourn for = 為…哀悼。 || 全國為罹難者哀悼。
mournful || adj. || 悲傷的 || The song has a mournful tune. || 這首歌的曲調很哀傷。 || mournful sound=哀傷的聲音 || The dog gave a mournful cry ___ its owner left. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 主人離開時，狗發出哀傷的叫聲。
mow || v. || 割草 || He mows the lawn every week. || 他每週除草。 || mow the lawn=割草 || My dad asked me ___ mow the lawn. || to|for|of || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 我爸叫我去割草。
muse || n./v. || 繆思；沉思 || She was the artist's muse. || 她是那位藝術家的繆思。 || muse on=沉思… || The poet often mused ___ the meaning of life. || on|in|at || muse on = 沉思…。 || 那位詩人常沉思生命的意義。
mustache || n. || 小鬍子 || My father has a mustache. || 我爸爸留著小鬍子。 || grow a mustache=留鬍子 || The man ___ a mustache is my uncle. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 那個留小鬍子的男人是我叔叔。
mute || adj./v. || 啞的；靜音 || Please mute your microphone. || 請把麥克風靜音。 || mute button=靜音鍵 || Please mute your microphone ___ you are not speaking. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 不說話時請把麥克風靜音。
nag || v. || 嘮叨 || My mom always nags me about my room. || 我媽總是為我的房間嘮叨。 || nag sb about=為…對某人嘮叨 || My mom keeps nagging me ___ my homework. || about|to|with || nag sb about = 為…對某人嘮叨。 || 我媽一直為功課的事對我嘮叨。
narrate || v. || 敘述；旁白 || The story is narrated by a child. || 這個故事由一個孩子敘述。 || narrate a story=講述故事 || The documentary is narrated ___ a famous actor. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這部紀錄片由一位知名演員擔任旁白。
narrator || n. || 敘述者 || The narrator of the story is a dog. || 這個故事的敘述者是一隻狗。 || first-person narrator=第一人稱敘述者 || The narrator ___ the novel is a ten-year-old girl. || of|for|to || the narrator of = …的敘述者。 || 這本小說的敘述者是一個十歲的女孩。
nationalism || n. || 民族主義 || Nationalism grew during the war. || 戰爭期間民族主義高漲。 || rise of nationalism=民族主義興起 || The rise ___ nationalism led to many wars. || of|for|in || the rise of = …的興起。 || 民族主義的興起導致了許多戰爭。
navigate || v. || 導航；航行 || We used a map to navigate. || 我們用地圖導航。 || navigate=找路 || Sailors used the stars ___ navigate at night. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 水手們在夜晚利用星星導航。
navigation || n. || 導航 || My car has a navigation system. || 我的車有導航系統。 || navigation system=導航系統 || I couldn't find the place ___ the navigation app. || without|with|by || without + 名詞 = 沒有…。 || 沒有導航 App 我就找不到那個地方。
nearsighted || adj. || 近視的 || She is nearsighted. || 她近視。 || nearsighted=近視 || Many students become nearsighted ___ they use phones too much. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 許多學生因為太常用手機而近視。
nickel || n. || 鎳；五分錢 || A nickel is worth five cents. || 五分鎳幣值五分錢。 || nickel=五分錢 || A nickel is worth five cents, ___ a dime is worth ten. || and|or|so || 兩個並列的事實用 and。 || 五分硬幣值五分錢，十分硬幣值十分錢。
nostril || n. || 鼻孔 || Breathe in through your nostrils. || 用鼻孔吸氣。 || nostril=鼻孔 || Breathe in ___ your nostrils and out through your mouth. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 用鼻子吸氣，再用嘴巴吐氣。
notable || adj. || 值得注意的；著名的 || It was a notable achievement. || 那是一項了不起的成就。 || notable for=以…著稱 || The town is notable ___ its old temples. || for|of|with || be notable for = 以…著稱。 || 這個小鎮以古廟著稱。
notorious || adj. || 惡名昭彰的 || The area is notorious for crime. || 這區以犯罪猖獗出名。 || be notorious for=因…惡名昭彰 || This road is notorious ___ traffic jams. || for|of|with || be notorious for = 因…惡名昭彰。 || 這條路以塞車出名。
novice || n. || 新手 || I'm a novice at cooking. || 我是烹飪新手。 || novice=初學者 || The course is designed ___ novices. || for|to|with || be designed for = 為…設計。 || 這門課是為新手設計的。
nucleus || n. || 核心；原子核 || The nucleus is the center of an atom. || 原子核是原子的中心。 || cell nucleus=細胞核 || The nucleus is ___ the center of the cell. || at|in|on || at the center of = 在…的中心。 || 細胞核位於細胞的中心。
nude || adj./n. || 裸體的 || The museum has nude statues. || 博物館有裸體雕像。 || nude painting=裸體畫 || Many ancient Greek statues ___ nude. || are|is|be || 主詞 statues 複數，用 are。 || 許多古希臘雕像都是裸體的。
nurture || v. || 養育；培養 || Parents nurture their children. || 父母養育孩子。 || nurture talent=培養人才 || Good teachers nurture ___ students' talents. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 talents 前用所有格形容詞。 || 好老師培養學生的天賦。

oath || n. || 誓言 || The president took an oath. || 總統宣誓就職。 || take an oath=宣誓 || The witness took an oath ___ tell the truth. || to|for|of || take an oath to V = 發誓要…。 || 證人宣誓會說實話。
oatmeal || n. || 燕麥片 || I eat oatmeal for breakfast. || 我早餐吃燕麥。 || bowl of oatmeal=一碗燕麥粥 || I have a bowl of oatmeal ___ breakfast every day. || for|in|at || for breakfast = 當早餐。 || 我每天早餐吃一碗燕麥粥。
