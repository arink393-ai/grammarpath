# 學測 L6 01：abbreviate – antonym
abbreviate || v. || 縮寫 || "Doctor" is often abbreviated to "Dr." || 「Doctor」常縮寫為「Dr.」。 || abbreviate A to B=把 A 縮寫成 B || "United States" is often abbreviated ___ "U.S." || to|for|with || be abbreviated to = 被縮寫成…。 || 「United States」常被縮寫成「U.S.」。
abide || v. || 遵守；忍受 || All players must abide by the rules. || 所有球員都必須遵守規則。 || abide by=遵守… || Everyone must abide ___ the school rules. || by|with|to || abide by = 遵守…。 || 每個人都必須遵守校規。
aboriginal || adj./n. || 原住民的 || The museum shows aboriginal art. || 博物館展示原住民藝術。 || aboriginal culture=原住民文化 || The museum displays art made ___ aboriginal artists. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 博物館展出原住民藝術家創作的作品。
abound || v. || 大量存在 || Fish abound in this lake. || 這座湖裡有很多魚。 || abound in/with=充滿… || The forest abounds ___ wildlife. || with|of|to || abound with = 充滿…。 || 這片森林有大量的野生動物。
abstraction || n. || 抽象概念 || Freedom is an abstraction. || 自由是一個抽象概念。 || abstraction=抽象 || Young children find it hard to understand abstractions ___ "justice." || such as|so as|as such || such as = 例如。 || 幼童很難理解像「正義」這樣的抽象概念。
abundance || n. || 豐富 || There is an abundance of fruit in summer. || 夏天水果很豐富。 || in abundance=大量地 || Fruit grows ___ abundance in southern Taiwan. || in|with|by || in abundance = 大量地。 || 台灣南部盛產水果。
academy || n. || 學院；專科學校 || She studies at a music academy. || 她在音樂學院讀書。 || police academy=警察學校 || He was accepted ___ the police academy. || into|onto|from || be accepted into = 被…錄取。 || 他被警察學校錄取了。
accessory || n. || 配件 || She sells phone accessories. || 她賣手機配件。 || fashion accessory=時尚配件 || A good bag is an important accessory ___ any outfit. || for|of|with || an accessory for = …的配件。 || 好包包是任何穿搭的重要配件。
acclaim || n./v. || 稱讚 || The movie won critical acclaim. || 這部電影獲得影評人好評。 || critical acclaim=評論界的好評 || The novel was widely acclaimed ___ critics. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這本小說廣受評論家讚揚。
accomplish || v. || 完成；達成 || We accomplished our goal. || 我們達成了目標。 || accomplish a goal=達成目標 || You can accomplish anything ___ you work hard. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 只要你努力，就能完成任何事。
accordance || n. || 一致 || We acted in accordance with the rules. || 我們依照規則行事。 || in accordance with=依照… || The building was built in accordance ___ safety laws. || with|to|for || in accordance with = 依照…。 || 這棟大樓依照安全法規建造。
accordingly || adv. || 因此；相應地 || The plan changed, and we acted accordingly. || 計畫改了，我們也相應行動。 || act accordingly=相應地行動 || The weather is cold, ___ dress accordingly. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 天氣很冷，所以要穿得暖一點。
accountable || adj. || 應負責的 || You are accountable for your actions. || 你要為自己的行為負責。 || be accountable for=對…負責 || Leaders should be accountable ___ their decisions. || for|to|with || be accountable for = 對…負責。 || 領導者應該為自己的決定負責。
accumulate || v. || 累積 || Dust accumulated on the shelf. || 架子上積了灰塵。 || accumulate wealth=累積財富 || Dust had accumulated on the shelf ___ nobody cleaned it. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為沒人清理，架子上積滿了灰塵。
accumulation || n. || 累積 || The accumulation of snow closed the road. || 積雪封閉了道路。 || accumulation of=…的累積 || The accumulation ___ garbage on the beach is a big problem. || of|for|in || the accumulation of = …的累積。 || 海灘上垃圾的堆積是個大問題。
accusation || n. || 指控 || He denied the accusation. || 他否認這項指控。 || false accusation=誣告 || She made a false accusation ___ her neighbor. || against|to|with || an accusation against = 對…的指控。 || 她誣告了她的鄰居。
accustom || v. || 使習慣 || It took time to accustom myself to the cold. || 我花了時間才習慣寒冷。 || be accustomed to=習慣於… || I'm not accustomed to ___ up so early. || getting|get|got || be accustomed to + V-ing（to 是介系詞）。 || 我不習慣這麼早起床。
acne || n. || 青春痘 || Many teenagers have acne. || 許多青少年長青春痘。 || acne cream=痘痘藥膏 || Eating too much fried food may make your acne ___. || worse|bad|worst || make + 受詞 + 形容詞（比較級）。 || 吃太多油炸食物可能讓你的痘痘更嚴重。
acre || n. || 英畝 || The farm covers 100 acres. || 這座農場占地一百英畝。 || acres of land=數英畝的土地 || The farm covers more ___ 500 acres. || than|then|as || more than = 超過。 || 這座農場占地超過五百英畝。
adaptation || n. || 改編；適應 || The movie is an adaptation of a novel. || 這部電影改編自一本小說。 || film adaptation=電影改編 || The film is an adaptation ___ a famous novel. || of|for|from || an adaptation of = …的改編作品。 || 這部電影改編自一本知名小說。
addiction || n. || 上癮 || Phone addiction is common among teens. || 手機成癮在青少年中很常見。 || internet addiction=網路成癮 || He is trying to overcome his addiction ___ video games. || to|for|with || addiction to = 對…上癮。 || 他正努力克服對電玩的沉迷。
admiral || n. || 海軍上將 || The admiral led the fleet. || 海軍上將率領艦隊。 || fleet admiral=艦隊司令 || The admiral, ___ led the navy for years, retired. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位領導海軍多年的上將退休了。
adolescence || n. || 青春期 || Adolescence can be a difficult time. || 青春期可能是一段艱難的時期。 || during adolescence=青春期間 || Many changes happen ___ adolescence. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 青春期間會發生許多變化。
advisory || adj. || 諮詢的；警告的 || A typhoon advisory was issued. || 颱風警報已發布。 || advisory committee=諮詢委員會 || A heat advisory ___ issued for the weekend. || has been|have been|being || 現在完成被動式；主詞單數用 has been。 || 週末的高溫警報已經發布。
aesthetic || adj. || 美學的 || The design has aesthetic value. || 這個設計有美學價值。 || aesthetic value=美學價值 || The building is not only useful ___ also aesthetic. || but|and|or || not only A but also B。 || 這棟建築不僅實用，也很有美感。
affectionate || adj. || 深情的 || The cat is very affectionate. || 這隻貓很親人。 || affectionate hug=深情的擁抱 || My dog is very affectionate ___ children. || toward|with|of || be affectionate toward = 對…很親切。 || 我的狗對孩子很親。
affiliate || v./n. || 使附屬；分會 || The hospital is affiliated with the university. || 這家醫院隸屬於該大學。 || be affiliated with=隸屬於… || The school is affiliated ___ a famous university. || with|to|for || be affiliated with = 隸屬於…。 || 這所學校隸屬於一所知名大學。
affirm || v. || 斷言；確認 || He affirmed his support for the plan. || 他重申對計畫的支持。 || affirm one's support=重申支持 || The president affirmed ___ the country would stay neutral. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 affirmed 的受詞。 || 總統重申國家將保持中立。
airtight || adj. || 密封的 || Keep the cookies in an airtight box. || 把餅乾放在密封盒裡。 || airtight container=密封容器 || Keep the tea leaves in an airtight container ___ they stay fresh. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 把茶葉放在密封容器中以保持新鮮。
airway || n. || 呼吸道 || Make sure his airway is clear. || 確保他的呼吸道暢通。 || clear airway=暢通的呼吸道 || Smoke can damage your airways, ___ is dangerous. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句中 which 可指前面整句。 || 煙會傷害呼吸道，這很危險。
algebra || n. || 代數 || We are learning algebra in math class. || 我們在數學課學代數。 || basic algebra=基礎代數 || I find algebra harder ___ geometry. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 我覺得代數比幾何難。
alienate || v. || 使疏遠 || His rude words alienated his friends. || 他無禮的話讓朋友疏遠他。 || feel alienated=感到疏離 || Many new students feel alienated ___ their first weeks at school. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 許多新生在開學頭幾週感到疏離。
align || v. || 對齊；結盟 || Align the text to the left. || 把文字靠左對齊。 || align with=與…一致 || Our goals should align ___ the school's goals. || with|to|for || align with = 與…一致。 || 我們的目標應該與學校的目標一致。
allege || v. || 宣稱；指控 || He is alleged to have stolen the car. || 他被指控偷了那輛車。 || be alleged to V=被指稱… || The man is alleged ___ have stolen the money. || to|for|of || be alleged to + V = 被指稱…。 || 那名男子被指稱偷了錢。
alligator || n. || 短吻鱷 || We saw an alligator in the river. || 我們在河裡看到一隻短吻鱷。 || alligator=短吻鱷 || The alligator ___ we saw was over three meters long. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾動物並當 saw 的受詞。 || 我們看到的那隻短吻鱷超過三公尺長。
altitude || n. || 海拔；高度 || The plane is flying at a high altitude. || 飛機在高空飛行。 || high altitude=高海拔 || The plane is flying ___ an altitude of 10,000 meters. || at|in|on || at an altitude of = 在…高度。 || 飛機在一萬公尺的高度飛行。
aluminum || n. || 鋁 || Soda cans are made of aluminum. || 汽水罐是鋁做的。 || aluminum foil=鋁箔紙 || Wrap the fish ___ aluminum foil before you grill it. || in|with|by || wrap sth in = 用…包起來。 || 烤之前先用鋁箔紙把魚包起來。
ambiguity || n. || 模稜兩可 || There is some ambiguity in the rules. || 規則中有些模糊之處。 || avoid ambiguity=避免含糊 || Please write clearly ___ avoid ambiguity. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 請寫清楚以避免含糊。
amid || prep. || 在…之中 || The meeting continued amid protests. || 會議在抗議聲中繼續進行。 || amid=在…之中 || The game went on amid heavy rain, ___ surprised everyone. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句中 which 可指前面整句。 || 比賽在大雨中繼續進行，這讓大家都很驚訝。
amplify || v. || 放大 || Use a microphone to amplify your voice. || 用麥克風放大你的聲音。 || amplify sound=放大聲音 || The speaker is used ___ amplify the music. || to|for|of || be used to + 原形動詞 = 被用來…。 || 喇叭被用來放大音樂。
analogy || n. || 類比 || The teacher used an analogy to explain it. || 老師用類比來解釋。 || draw an analogy=做類比 || The teacher drew an analogy ___ the heart and a pump. || between|among|with || between A and B = A 和 B 之間。 || 老師把心臟和幫浦做了類比。
analytical || adj. || 分析的 || She has strong analytical skills. || 她有很強的分析能力。 || analytical skills=分析能力 || The job requires strong analytical skills, ___ she has. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句修飾物，只能用 which。 || 這份工作需要很強的分析能力，而她正好具備。
anchor || n./v. || 錨；主播 || The ship dropped anchor. || 船下錨了。 || news anchor=新聞主播 || She has worked as a news anchor ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她擔任新聞主播已經十年了。
animate || v./adj. || 使有生氣；動畫 || The movie was animated by computer. || 這部電影是電腦動畫製作的。 || animated film=動畫電影 || This animated film is popular ___ kids and adults. || with|to|for || be popular with = 受…歡迎。 || 這部動畫電影受到孩子和大人的喜愛。
annoyance || n. || 惱怒；討厭的事 || The noise is a constant annoyance. || 噪音一直令人煩躁。 || to one's annoyance=令某人惱火的是 || ___ my annoyance, the bus was late again. || To|For|With || to one's annoyance = 令某人惱火的是。 || 令我惱火的是，公車又遲到了。
anthem || n. || 國歌；頌歌 || We sang the national anthem. || 我們唱了國歌。 || national anthem=國歌 || Everyone stood up ___ the national anthem was played. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 演奏國歌時大家都站了起來。
antibiotic || n. || 抗生素 || The doctor gave me antibiotics. || 醫生開了抗生素給我。 || take antibiotics=服用抗生素 || Antibiotics don't work ___ viruses. || against|to|with || work against = 對…有效。 || 抗生素對病毒無效。
anticipation || n. || 期待 || The fans waited in anticipation. || 粉絲們滿懷期待地等待。 || in anticipation of=預期… || We bought umbrellas in anticipation ___ the rain. || of|for|to || in anticipation of = 預期…。 || 我們預料會下雨，所以買了傘。
antonym || n. || 反義詞 || "Hot" is the antonym of "cold." || 「hot」是「cold」的反義詞。 || antonym of=…的反義詞 || "Happy" is an antonym ___ "sad." || of|for|to || an antonym of = …的反義詞。 || 「happy」是「sad」的反義詞。
