# 學測 L6 10：intake – lighten
intake || n. || 攝取量 || Reduce your sugar intake. || 減少糖分攝取。 || sugar intake=糖分攝取 || Doctors advise us to reduce our intake ___ salt. || of|for|with || the intake of = …的攝取。 || 醫生建議我們減少鹽的攝取量。
intellect || n. || 智力 || She is a woman of great intellect. || 她是一位非常有智慧的女性。 || human intellect=人類智力 || She is admired ___ her sharp intellect. || for|of|with || be admired for = 因…而受欽佩。 || 她因敏銳的智慧而受人欽佩。
interpreter || n. || 口譯員 || She works as an interpreter. || 她擔任口譯員。 || sign language interpreter=手語翻譯員 || The president spoke ___ an interpreter. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 總統透過口譯員發言。
intersection || n. || 十字路口 || Turn left at the next intersection. || 在下一個路口左轉。 || busy intersection=繁忙的路口 || Turn right ___ the second intersection. || at|in|on || at + 地點（點）。 || 在第二個路口右轉。
intervene || v. || 介入；干預 || The teacher intervened in the fight. || 老師介入了這場打架。 || intervene in=介入… || The police had to intervene ___ the fight. || in|on|at || intervene in = 介入…。 || 警方不得不介入這場打鬥。
intimacy || n. || 親密 || Trust is important for intimacy. || 信任對親密關係很重要。 || emotional intimacy=情感上的親密 || Sharing secrets can build intimacy ___ friends. || between|among|along || between = 在…之間。 || 分享祕密能拉近朋友之間的距離。
intimidate || v. || 恐嚇 || Don't let him intimidate you. || 別讓他嚇到你。 || feel intimidated=感到害怕 || New students often feel intimidated ___ older students. || by|with|of || feel intimidated by = 被…嚇到。 || 新生常常被學長姊嚇到。
intonation || n. || 語調 || English intonation is important. || 英文語調很重要。 || rising intonation=上揚語調 || Questions often end ___ a rising intonation. || with|by|in || end with = 以…結尾。 || 疑問句常以上揚語調結尾。
intrigue || v./n. || 引起興趣；陰謀 || The story intrigued me. || 這個故事引起我的興趣。 || be intrigued by=對…感興趣 || I was intrigued ___ the old map. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 我對那張古地圖很感興趣。
intrude || v. || 打擾；闖入 || I don't want to intrude. || 我不想打擾。 || intrude on=打擾… || I'm sorry to intrude ___ your private time. || on|in|at || intrude on = 打擾…。 || 抱歉打擾你的私人時間。
intruder || n. || 入侵者 || The dog barked at the intruder. || 狗對入侵者吠叫。 || intruder alarm=防盜警報 || The alarm went off ___ the intruder broke in. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 入侵者闖入時警報響了。
invaluable || adj. || 非常寶貴的 || Your help was invaluable. || 你的幫助非常寶貴。 || invaluable experience=寶貴的經驗 || Her advice was invaluable ___ me. || to|for|with || be invaluable to = 對…極為寶貴。 || 她的建議對我來說非常寶貴。
invariably || adv. || 總是 || He is invariably late. || 他總是遲到。 || invariably=一成不變地 || She invariably ___ coffee in the morning. || drinks|drink|drinking || 主詞 She 單數，現在式動詞加 s。 || 她每天早上一定喝咖啡。
inventory || n. || 存貨；清單 || We need to check the inventory. || 我們需要清點存貨。 || take inventory=盤點 || The store takes inventory ___ the end of each month. || at|in|on || at the end of = 在…結束時。 || 這家店每個月底盤點存貨。
ironic || adj. || 諷刺的 || It's ironic that the fire station burned down. || 消防局燒掉了，真是諷刺。 || ironic=反諷的 || It is ironic ___ the doctor is a heavy smoker. || that|what|which || It is + 形容詞 + that 子句。 || 那位醫生是個老菸槍，真是諷刺。
irritable || adj. || 易怒的 || He gets irritable when he is tired. || 他累的時候容易發脾氣。 || irritable=煩躁 || She becomes irritable ___ she doesn't get enough sleep. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 她睡不夠時就會變得煩躁。
irritate || v. || 激怒；刺激 || His jokes irritate me. || 他的笑話讓我很煩。 || irritate the skin=刺激皮膚 || This soap may irritate your skin, ___ test it first. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 這塊肥皂可能刺激皮膚，所以先試用看看。
isle || n. || 小島 || We visited the Isle of Skye. || 我們去了斯凱島。 || Isle of Man=曼島 || The Isle of Wight is located ___ the south coast of England. || off|of|from || off the coast = 在…外海。 || 懷特島位於英格蘭南部外海。
itch || n./v. || 癢 || My arm itches. || 我的手臂好癢。 || itchy=癢的 || The mosquito bite itches, but don't scratch ___. || it|them|its || it 代替 bite（單數）。 || 蚊子咬的地方很癢，但不要抓。
ivy || n. || 常春藤 || Ivy covered the old wall. || 常春藤爬滿了老牆。 || Ivy League=常春藤聯盟 || The old wall is covered ___ ivy. || with|by|of || be covered with = 覆蓋著…。 || 那面老牆爬滿了常春藤。
jade || n. || 玉 || She wears a jade bracelet. || 她戴著玉鐲。 || jade bracelet=玉鐲 || The museum has a famous piece of jade ___ looks like a cabbage. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當主詞。 || 博物館有一件看起來像白菜的著名玉器。
janitor || n. || 工友 || The janitor cleans the school. || 工友打掃學校。 || school janitor=學校工友 || The janitor ___ cleans our school is very kind. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 打掃我們學校的工友很親切。
jasmine || n. || 茉莉花 || I like jasmine tea. || 我喜歡茉莉花茶。 || jasmine tea=茉莉花茶 || Would you like jasmine tea ___ green tea? || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 你要茉莉花茶還是綠茶？
jingle || n./v. || 叮噹聲；廣告歌 || The keys jingled in his pocket. || 鑰匙在他口袋裡叮噹作響。 || advertising jingle=廣告歌 || I can't get that jingle out ___ my head. || of|from|off || get sth out of one's head = 把…從腦中趕走。 || 那首廣告歌在我腦中揮之不去。
jockey || n. || 騎師 || The jockey rode the horse to victory. || 騎師騎著馬贏得勝利。 || disc jockey=DJ || The jockey ___ won the race is only 20. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 贏得比賽的騎師才二十歲。
jolly || adj. || 快活的 || Santa is a jolly old man. || 聖誕老人是個快活的老人。 || jolly=愉快的 || Santa Claus is famous ___ his jolly laugh. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 聖誕老人以快活的笑聲聞名。
joyous || adj. || 歡樂的 || It was a joyous occasion. || 那是一個歡樂的場合。 || joyous occasion=喜慶場合 || The wedding was a joyous occasion ___ everyone. || for|of|to || for + 對象 = 對…而言。 || 婚禮對每個人來說都是歡樂的場合。
junction || n. || 交叉口 || Turn left at the junction. || 在交叉口左轉。 || road junction=路口 || The accident happened ___ the junction of two highways. || at|in|on || at a junction = 在交叉口。 || 事故發生在兩條公路的交叉口。
kin || n. || 親屬 || He has no kin in Taiwan. || 他在台灣沒有親人。 || next of kin=近親 || Please write the name of your next ___ kin on the form. || of|for|to || next of kin = 最近的親屬。 || 請在表格上填寫你最近親屬的姓名。
kindle || v. || 點燃；激起 || The teacher kindled my interest in history. || 老師激起了我對歷史的興趣。 || kindle interest=激起興趣 || The trip kindled her interest ___ marine life. || in|on|for || interest in = 對…的興趣。 || 那趟旅行激起了她對海洋生物的興趣。
knowledgeable || adj. || 博學的 || He is knowledgeable about history. || 他對歷史很有研究。 || be knowledgeable about=對…很懂 || My uncle is very knowledgeable ___ wine. || about|to|for || be knowledgeable about = 對…很懂。 || 我叔叔對葡萄酒很有研究。
lad || n. || 少年 || He is a nice lad. || 他是個好小伙子。 || young lad=年輕小伙子 || When I was a lad, I ___ in the countryside. || lived|live|living || 過去的事用過去式。 || 我年少時住在鄉下。

layman || n. || 外行人 || Explain it in layman's terms. || 用外行人聽得懂的話解釋。 || in layman's terms=用淺顯的話 || Could you explain it ___ layman's terms? || in|on|with || in layman's terms = 用淺顯的話。 || 你能用淺顯的話解釋嗎？
