# 學測 L6 05：crackdown – disable
crackdown || n. || 嚴厲取締 || The police launched a crackdown on drunk driving. || 警方展開取締酒駕行動。 || crackdown on=對…的取締 || The government announced a crackdown ___ illegal parking. || on|in|at || a crackdown on = 對…的取締。 || 政府宣布取締違規停車。
cracker || n. || 餅乾；鞭炮 || I ate some crackers with cheese. || 我吃了一些餅乾配起司。 || cheese and crackers=起司配餅乾 || I had some crackers ___ cheese for a snack. || with|by|of || with = 搭配…。 || 我吃了一些餅乾配起司當點心。
cram || v. || 塞滿；臨時抱佛腳 || He crammed for the exam all night. || 他整晚臨時抱佛腳準備考試。 || cram for an exam=考前猛讀 || She crammed all her clothes ___ one small bag. || into|onto|from || cram A into B = 把 A 塞進 B。 || 她把所有衣服塞進一個小包包。
cramp || n. || 抽筋 || I got a cramp in my leg. || 我的腿抽筋了。 || leg cramp=腿抽筋 || He got a cramp ___ his leg while swimming. || in|on|at || a cramp in = …部位的抽筋。 || 他游泳時腿抽筋了。
crater || n. || 火山口；坑洞 || The moon has many craters. || 月球上有很多坑洞。 || volcanic crater=火山口 || There are many craters ___ the surface of the moon. || on|in|at || on the surface = 在表面上。 || 月球表面有很多坑洞。
credible || adj. || 可信的 || Is the witness credible? || 這位證人可信嗎？ || credible source=可靠來源 || Only use information from credible sources ___ your report. || in|on|at || in a report = 在報告中。 || 報告中只能使用可靠來源的資訊。
crocodile || n. || 鱷魚 || The crocodile opened its mouth. || 鱷魚張開了嘴。 || crocodile tears=假慈悲 || Don't swim in the river; there are crocodiles ___ it. || in|on|at || 在河裡用 in。 || 別在河裡游泳，裡面有鱷魚。
crossing || n. || 十字路口；穿越 || Use the pedestrian crossing. || 請走行人穿越道。 || pedestrian crossing=行人穿越道 || Cars must stop ___ the crossing when people are walking. || at|of|to || at + 地點（點）。 || 有人通過時，車子必須在穿越道前停下。
crutch || n. || 拐杖 || He walks on crutches. || 他拄著拐杖走路。 || on crutches=拄拐杖 || He has been walking ___ crutches since the accident. || on|in|at || walk on crutches = 拄著拐杖走路。 || 自從那場意外後，他一直拄著拐杖走路。
cub || n. || 幼獸 || The lion cub played with its mother. || 小獅子和媽媽玩耍。 || bear cub=小熊 || The bear cub stayed close ___ its mother. || to|of|for || close to = 靠近…。 || 小熊緊緊待在媽媽身邊。
cucumber || n. || 小黃瓜 || I put cucumber in my salad. || 我在沙拉裡放了小黃瓜。 || as cool as a cucumber=非常冷靜 || She stayed as cool ___ a cucumber during the test. || as|than|so || as…as = 和…一樣。 || 她在考試時非常冷靜。
cultivate || v. || 耕種；培養 || Farmers cultivate rice here. || 農夫在這裡種稻。 || cultivate a habit=培養習慣 || Parents should help children cultivate good habits ___ a young age. || from|since|in || from a young age = 從小。 || 父母應該從小幫孩子培養好習慣。
cumulative || adj. || 累積的 || The effects of stress are cumulative. || 壓力的影響是累積的。 || cumulative effect=累積效應 || The cumulative effect of poor sleep can be serious, ___ you should rest well. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 睡眠不足的累積效應可能很嚴重，所以要好好休息。
curb || v./n. || 抑制；路緣 || We must curb pollution. || 我們必須抑制汙染。 || curb spending=控制支出 || The government is trying ___ curb air pollution. || to|for|of || try to + 原形動詞。 || 政府正努力抑制空氣汙染。
curfew || n. || 宵禁；門禁 || My parents set a curfew of 10 p.m. || 我爸媽規定晚上十點門禁。 || impose a curfew=實施宵禁 || I have to be home ___ my 10 p.m. curfew. || before|after|since || 門禁「之前」到家，用 before。 || 我必須在晚上十點門禁前回家。
curry || n. || 咖哩 || I love chicken curry. || 我喜歡雞肉咖哩。 || chicken curry=雞肉咖哩 || This curry is too spicy ___ the kids. || for|to|of || too + 形容詞 + for sb。 || 這個咖哩對孩子們來說太辣了。
customary || adj. || 習慣的；慣例的 || It is customary to give red envelopes at New Year. || 過年發紅包是習俗。 || it is customary to V=照慣例… || It is customary ___ take off your shoes before entering a Japanese home. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 進日本人家裡之前脫鞋是習俗。
cynical || adj. || 憤世嫉俗的 || He is cynical about politics. || 他對政治很憤世嫉俗。 || be cynical about=對…抱持懷疑 || Many young people are cynical ___ politicians. || about|to|for || be cynical about = 對…抱持懷疑。 || 許多年輕人對政治人物抱持懷疑。
dazzle || v. || 使目眩；使驚嘆 || The bright lights dazzled me. || 明亮的燈光讓我目眩。 || be dazzled by=被…驚豔 || The audience was dazzled ___ her performance. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 觀眾被她的表演驚豔了。
deafen || v. || 使耳聾 || The noise was deafening. || 噪音震耳欲聾。 || deafening noise=震耳欲聾的噪音 || The music at the concert was ___ loud that it was deafening. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 演唱會的音樂大聲到震耳欲聾。
decisive || adj. || 決定性的；果斷的 || She is a decisive leader. || 她是個果斷的領導者。 || decisive moment=決定性的時刻 || A good leader must be decisive ___ times of crisis. || in|on|at || in times of = 在…時期。 || 好的領導者在危機時必須果斷。
dedication || n. || 奉獻 || Her dedication to her students is amazing. || 她對學生的奉獻令人驚嘆。 || dedication to=對…的奉獻 || We thank her ___ her dedication to the school. || for|of|with || thank sb for = 因…感謝某人。 || 我們感謝她對學校的奉獻。
deduct || v. || 扣除 || The fee will be deducted from your account. || 費用將從你的帳戶扣除。 || deduct from=從…扣除 || Points will be deducted ___ late homework. || for|with|by || deduct points for = 因…扣分。 || 遲交作業會被扣分。
deem || v. || 認為 || The plan was deemed a success. || 這個計畫被認為是成功的。 || be deemed=被認為… || The movie was deemed unsuitable ___ children. || for|to|with || unsuitable for = 不適合…。 || 這部電影被認為不適合兒童觀看。
default || n./adj. || 預設；違約 || The default language is English. || 預設語言是英文。 || by default=預設地 || The phone is set to English ___ default. || by|in|on || by default = 預設。 || 這支手機預設語言是英文。
defect || n. || 缺陷 || The car has a defect. || 這輛車有瑕疵。 || birth defect=先天缺陷 || The company recalled the cars ___ of a defect. || because|since|due || because of = 因為…。 || 公司因為瑕疵而召回這批車。
defiance || n. || 違抗 || In defiance of the rules, he left early. || 他違反規定提早離開了。 || in defiance of=不顧… || The protesters marched ___ defiance of the ban. || in|on|at || in defiance of = 不顧…。 || 抗議者不顧禁令進行遊行。
definitive || adj. || 決定性的；權威的 || This is the definitive guide to Taipei. || 這是台北的權威指南。 || definitive answer=確定的答案 || We haven't got a definitive answer ___ the doctor yet. || from|of|by || from + 人 = 來自…。 || 我們還沒得到醫生的明確答覆。
dental || adj. || 牙齒的 || I have a dental appointment. || 我約了看牙。 || dental care=牙齒保健 || Regular dental checkups are important ___ healthy teeth. || for|to|with || be important for = 對…很重要。 || 定期牙齒檢查對保持牙齒健康很重要。
deplete || v. || 耗盡 || We are depleting natural resources. || 我們正在耗盡自然資源。 || deplete resources=耗盡資源 || Overfishing has depleted the number ___ fish in the sea. || of|for|in || the number of = …的數量。 || 過度捕撈使海中的魚量減少。
deprive || v. || 剝奪 || Lack of sleep deprives you of energy. || 睡眠不足會讓你缺乏活力。 || deprive sb of=剝奪某人的… || The prisoners were deprived ___ food. || of|from|with || deprive sb of sth = 剝奪某人的某物。 || 囚犯被剝奪了食物。
descent || n. || 下降；血統 || The plane began its descent. || 飛機開始下降。 || of … descent=…血統 || She is ___ Japanese descent. || of|from|by || of … descent = 有…血統的。 || 她有日本血統。
despise || v. || 鄙視 || I despise people who lie. || 我鄙視說謊的人。 || despise sb=鄙視某人 || She despises people ___ are cruel to animals. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 她鄙視虐待動物的人。
destined || adj. || 命中注定的 || He was destined to become a star. || 他注定成為明星。 || be destined to V=注定… || She was destined ___ be a great singer. || to|for|of || be destined to + 原形動詞 = 注定…。 || 她注定要成為一位偉大的歌手。
detach || v. || 分離；拆下 || Detach the form and send it back. || 撕下表格寄回。 || detach A from B=把 A 從 B 拆下 || Please detach the coupon ___ the page. || from|of|off || detach A from B = 把 A 從 B 拆下。 || 請把優惠券從這一頁撕下來。
detain || v. || 拘留 || The man was detained by the police. || 那名男子被警方拘留。 || be detained=被拘留 || The suspect ___ detained for questioning. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 嫌犯被拘留接受訊問。
detention || n. || 拘留；留校察看 || He got detention for being late. || 他因遲到被留校。 || get detention=被留校 || He got detention ___ talking in class. || for|of|with || for + V-ing = 因為…。 || 他因上課講話被留校。
deter || v. || 阻止；嚇阻 || The cameras deter thieves. || 攝影機能嚇阻小偷。 || deter sb from=阻止某人… || High fines deter people ___ littering. || from|of|to || deter sb from V-ing = 阻止某人做…。 || 高額罰款能阻止人們亂丟垃圾。
detergent || n. || 洗潔劑 || Add some detergent to the washing machine. || 在洗衣機裡加一些洗衣精。 || laundry detergent=洗衣精 || We ran out ___ detergent, so I bought some more. || of|from|off || run out of = 用完…。 || 洗衣精用完了，所以我又買了一些。
devour || v. || 狼吞虎嚥；如饑似渴地讀 || The hungry boy devoured the pizza. || 飢餓的男孩狼吞虎嚥地吃掉披薩。 || devour a book=一口氣讀完一本書 || She devoured the novel ___ one night. || in|on|at || in + 一段時間 = 在…之內。 || 她一個晚上就把那本小說讀完了。
diabetes || n. || 糖尿病 || My grandfather has diabetes. || 我祖父有糖尿病。 || type 2 diabetes=第二型糖尿病 || Eating too much sugar can increase the risk ___ diabetes. || of|for|in || the risk of = …的風險。 || 吃太多糖會增加罹患糖尿病的風險。
dictate || v. || 口述；命令 || The teacher dictated the sentences. || 老師口述句子讓我們寫。 || dictate to=對…發號施令 || Don't let others dictate ___ you what to do. || to|for|with || dictate to sb = 對某人發號施令。 || 別讓別人對你指手畫腳。
dictation || n. || 聽寫 || We have a dictation test today. || 我們今天有聽寫測驗。 || dictation test=聽寫測驗 || The students wrote down the words ___ the dictation. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 學生們在聽寫時寫下了單字。
dictator || n. || 獨裁者 || The dictator ruled for 30 years. || 那位獨裁者統治了三十年。 || military dictator=軍事獨裁者 || The dictator was finally forced ___ leave the country. || to|for|of || be forced to + 原形動詞。 || 那位獨裁者最後被迫離開國家。
dictatorship || n. || 獨裁政權 || The country was under a dictatorship. || 這個國家曾處於獨裁統治下。 || military dictatorship=軍事獨裁 || People lived in fear ___ the dictatorship. || under|below|over || under + 政權 = 在…統治下。 || 在獨裁統治下，人們活在恐懼中。
diesel || n. || 柴油 || This truck runs on diesel. || 這輛卡車使用柴油。 || diesel engine=柴油引擎 || Many buses still run ___ diesel. || on|in|with || run on = 以…為燃料。 || 許多公車仍然使用柴油。
differentiate || v. || 區別 || Can you differentiate between the twins? || 你能分辨那對雙胞胎嗎？ || differentiate between=區分… || It's hard to differentiate ___ the two colors. || between|among|with || differentiate between = 區分…。 || 很難區分這兩種顏色。
diplomacy || n. || 外交 || The problem was solved through diplomacy. || 這個問題透過外交解決了。 || through diplomacy=透過外交 || The conflict was solved ___ diplomacy, not war. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 這場衝突是透過外交而非戰爭解決的。
directive || n. || 指示；指令 || The staff received a new directive. || 員工收到一項新指令。 || issue a directive=發布指令 || All schools must follow the new directive ___ the government. || from|of|by || from + 來源 = 來自…。 || 所有學校都必須遵照政府的新指令。
disable || v. || 使失能；停用 || The accident disabled him. || 那場意外讓他失能。 || disabled people=身心障礙者 || The building has ramps for people ___ are disabled. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 這棟建築為身心障礙者設有坡道。
