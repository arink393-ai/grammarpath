# 學測 L6 17：simplify – stepfather
simplify || v. || 簡化 || Let me simplify the problem. || 讓我把問題簡化。 || simplify=使簡單 || The teacher simplified the story ___ the young students could understand it. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 老師把故事簡化，讓年幼的學生能看懂。
simultaneous || adj. || 同時的 || There were two simultaneous explosions. || 同時發生了兩起爆炸。 || simultaneous interpretation=同步口譯 || She works as a simultaneous interpreter ___ international meetings. || at|on|to || at + 活動 = 在…。 || 她在國際會議上擔任同步口譯。
skeptical || adj. || 懷疑的 || I'm skeptical about the plan. || 我對這個計畫持懷疑態度。 || be skeptical about=對…懷疑 || Many scientists are skeptical ___ the new claim. || about|to|for || be skeptical about = 對…懷疑。 || 許多科學家對這個新說法持懷疑態度。
skim || v. || 略讀；撈去 || Skim the article first. || 先略讀這篇文章。 || skim through=瀏覽 || Skim ___ the text before you answer the questions. || through|across|along || skim through = 快速瀏覽。 || 回答問題前先快速瀏覽文章。
slang || n. || 俚語 || "Cool" is a slang word. || 「cool」是一個俚語。 || slang word=俚語 || Avoid using slang ___ formal writing. || in|on|at || in writing = 在寫作中。 || 正式寫作中避免使用俚語。
slash || v./n. || 大幅削減；斜線 || The store slashed its prices. || 那家店大幅降價。 || slash prices=大降價 || The store slashed prices ___ 50 percent. || by|with|for || by + 幅度。 || 那家店價格砍了一半。
slaughter || v./n. || 屠殺 || Thousands of animals were slaughtered. || 數千隻動物遭到屠宰。 || slaughter=屠宰 || The pigs ___ slaughtered at the farm. || were|was|be || 被動語態；pigs 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 豬隻在農場被屠宰。
slay || v. || 殺死（文學用法） || In the story, the hero slays a dragon. || 故事裡英雄屠了一條龍。 || slay a dragon=屠龍 || In the legend, a brave knight sets out ___ slay the dragon. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 傳說中，一位勇敢的騎士出發去屠龍。
sloppy || adj. || 草率的 || His work is sloppy. || 他的工作很草率。 || sloppy work=草率的工作 || The teacher was unhappy ___ his sloppy homework. || with|to|for || be unhappy with = 對…不滿。 || 老師對他草率的作業很不滿。
slum || n. || 貧民窟 || She grew up in a slum. || 她在貧民窟長大。 || urban slum=都市貧民窟 || Many children ___ the slums don't go to school. || in|on|at || in the slums = 在貧民窟裡。 || 貧民窟裡許多孩子沒上學。
slump || v./n. || 暴跌；癱坐 || Sales slumped last year. || 去年銷售暴跌。 || economic slump=經濟衰退 || Sales slumped ___ 30 percent last year. || by|with|for || by + 幅度。 || 去年銷售大跌三成。
sly || adj. || 狡猾的 || The fox is sly. || 狐狸很狡猾。 || sly smile=狡黠的微笑 || The sly fox tricked the crow ___ dropping the cheese. || into|onto|from || trick sb into V-ing = 騙某人做…。 || 狡猾的狐狸騙烏鴉丟下了起司。
smuggle || v. || 走私 || They smuggled drugs into the country. || 他們把毒品走私進國內。 || smuggle into=走私進… || The man tried to smuggle animals ___ the country. || into|onto|from || smuggle A into B = 把 A 走私進 B。 || 那名男子試圖把動物走私進國內。
sneaker || n. || 運動鞋 || I bought new sneakers. || 我買了新運動鞋。 || a pair of sneakers=一雙運動鞋 || I need a new pair ___ sneakers. || of|for|with || a pair of = 一雙…。 || 我需要一雙新運動鞋。
sneaky || adj. || 鬼鬼祟祟的 || That was a sneaky trick. || 那是個卑鄙的花招。 || sneaky=偷偷摸摸的 || It was sneaky ___ him to read my diary. || of|for|to || It is + 形容詞(人的特質) + of sb + to V。 || 他偷看我的日記真是卑鄙。
sneeze || v./n. || 打噴嚏 || Cover your mouth when you sneeze. || 打噴嚏時要遮住嘴巴。 || sneeze=噴嚏 || Cover your mouth ___ you sneeze. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 打噴嚏時要遮住嘴巴。
snore || v./n. || 打鼾 || My dad snores loudly. || 我爸打鼾很大聲。 || snore=打呼 || My dad snores ___ loudly that I can't sleep. || so|such|too || so + 副詞 + that…。 || 我爸打呼大聲到我睡不著。
sociable || adj. || 好交際的 || She is very sociable. || 她很好相處、愛交朋友。 || sociable=合群的 || He is sociable ___ makes friends easily. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的描述用 and。 || 他很合群，很容易交到朋友。
socialism || n. || 社會主義 || Socialism is a political system. || 社會主義是一種政治制度。 || socialism=社會主義 || Socialism and capitalism ___ two different economic systems. || are|is|be || 複合主詞（A and B）用複數動詞。 || 社會主義和資本主義是兩種不同的經濟制度。
socialist || adj./n. || 社會主義的 || He is a socialist. || 他是社會主義者。 || socialist party=社會黨 || The country was ruled by a socialist government ___ many years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 這個國家由社會主義政府統治了多年。
socialize || v. || 交際 || She likes to socialize with friends. || 她喜歡和朋友交際。 || socialize with=與…交往 || Students need time to socialize ___ their classmates. || with|to|for || socialize with = 與…交往。 || 學生需要時間和同學交流。
sociology || n. || 社會學 || She studies sociology. || 她讀社會學。 || sociology=社會學 || Sociology is the study ___ human society. || of|for|in || the study of = 對…的研究。 || 社會學是研究人類社會的學問。
solemn || adj. || 莊嚴的 || It was a solemn ceremony. || 那是一場莊嚴的典禮。 || solemn promise=鄭重的承諾 || He made a solemn promise ___ never lie again. || to|for|of || a promise to V = 做…的承諾。 || 他鄭重承諾再也不說謊。
solidarity || n. || 團結 || The workers showed solidarity. || 工人們展現團結。 || in solidarity with=與…團結一致 || People wore yellow ribbons ___ solidarity with the victims. || in|on|at || in solidarity with = 與…團結一致。 || 人們戴黃絲帶表示與受害者團結一心。
solitary || adj. || 獨自的 || He lives a solitary life. || 他過著孤獨的生活。 || solitary=孤獨的 || Tigers are solitary animals, ___ lions live in groups. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 老虎是獨居動物，但獅子群居。
solitude || n. || 獨處 || She enjoys solitude. || 她享受獨處。 || in solitude=獨自 || Some people enjoy reading ___ solitude. || in|on|at || in solitude = 獨自地。 || 有些人喜歡獨自閱讀。
soothe || v. || 安撫；緩和 || The music soothed the baby. || 音樂安撫了寶寶。 || soothe the pain=緩和疼痛 || The mother sang softly ___ soothe her crying baby. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 媽媽輕聲唱歌安撫哭泣的寶寶。
sorrowful || adj. || 悲傷的 || She had a sorrowful look. || 她神情悲傷。 || sorrowful=哀傷的 || She looked sorrowful ___ she heard the news. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 她聽到消息時看起來很悲傷。
sovereign || adj./n. || 有主權的；君主 || Every sovereign country has its own laws. || 每個主權國家都有自己的法律。 || sovereign state=主權國家 || A sovereign country makes ___ own laws. || its|it's|their || 單數名詞 country 的所有格用 its。 || 主權國家自己制定法律。
span || n./v. || 期間；跨越 || Her career spans 30 years. || 她的職業生涯長達三十年。 || attention span=注意力持續時間 || Young children have a short attention span, ___ lessons should be short. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 幼童的專注力短，所以課程應該短一點。
sparrow || n. || 麻雀 || A sparrow landed on the window. || 一隻麻雀停在窗戶上。 || sparrow=麻雀 || A sparrow was building a nest ___ the roof. || on|of|to || on the roof = 在屋頂上。 || 一隻麻雀在屋頂上築巢。
spectacle || n. || 壯觀的景象 || The fireworks were a spectacle. || 煙火是一場壯觀的景象。 || make a spectacle of oneself=出洋相 || The fireworks show was a spectacle ___ everyone enjoyed. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 enjoyed 的受詞。 || 煙火秀是大家都喜愛的壯觀場面。
spiral || n./adj. || 螺旋 || We climbed the spiral staircase. || 我們爬上螺旋樓梯。 || spiral staircase=螺旋梯 || We walked ___ the spiral staircase to the top of the tower. || up|on|at || walk up = 往上走。 || 我們沿著螺旋樓梯走到塔頂。
splendor || n. || 壯麗 || We admired the splendor of the palace. || 我們欣賞宮殿的壯麗。 || splendor=輝煌 || We were amazed ___ the splendor of the palace. || by|with|of || be amazed by = 對…感到驚嘆。 || 我們被宮殿的壯麗震撼了。
spokeswoman || n. || 女發言人 || The spokeswoman answered questions. || 女發言人回答了問題。 || spokeswoman=女發言人 || A spokeswoman ___ the company said the product was safe. || for|to|with || a spokeswoman for = …的發言人。 || 公司的女發言人表示產品是安全的。
spontaneous || adj. || 自發的 || The crowd broke into spontaneous applause. || 群眾自發地鼓掌。 || spontaneous=自然的 || Her spontaneous laugh made everyone ___. || smile|smiling|to smile || make + 受詞 + 原形動詞。 || 她自然的笑聲讓大家都笑了。
sportsmanship || n. || 運動家精神 || He showed good sportsmanship. || 他展現了良好的運動家精神。 || good sportsmanship=運動家精神 || Shaking hands after the game is a sign ___ good sportsmanship. || of|for|to || a sign of = …的表現。 || 比賽後握手是運動家精神的表現。
spotlight || n. || 聚光燈；焦點 || She loves being in the spotlight. || 她喜歡成為焦點。 || in the spotlight=成為焦點 || The young actor is ___ the spotlight now. || in|on|at || in the spotlight = 受到注目。 || 那位年輕演員現在是眾人焦點。
spur || v./n. || 激勵 || The prize spurred him to work harder. || 獎品激勵他更努力。 || on the spur of the moment=一時衝動 || We decided to go to the beach on the spur ___ the moment. || of|for|in || on the spur of the moment = 一時興起。 || 我們一時興起決定去海邊。
stabilize || v. || 使穩定 || The patient's condition has stabilized. || 病人的情況已經穩定。 || stabilize prices=穩定物價 || The government took steps ___ stabilize prices. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 政府採取措施穩定物價。
stagger || v. || 搖晃；使震驚 || He staggered home. || 他搖搖晃晃地回家。 || staggering=驚人的 || The injured man staggered ___ the road. || across|among|between || across = 越過…。 || 那名受傷的男子搖搖晃晃地穿過馬路。
staple || n./adj. || 主食；訂書針 || Rice is a staple food in Asia. || 米是亞洲的主食。 || staple food=主食 || Rice is a staple food ___ many Asian countries. || in|on|at || in + 國家。 || 米是許多亞洲國家的主食。

statesman || n. || 政治家 || He was a great statesman. || 他是一位偉大的政治家。 || elder statesman=元老政治家 || He is remembered ___ a great statesman. || as|like|for || be remembered as = 被記得是…。 || 他被世人記為一位偉大的政治家。
