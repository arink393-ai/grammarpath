# 學測 L6 07：endeavor – folklore
endeavor || n./v. || 努力 || She endeavored to finish on time. || 她努力準時完成。 || endeavor to V=努力… || We will endeavor ___ answer all your questions. || to|for|of || endeavor to + 原形動詞 = 努力…。 || 我們會盡力回答你所有的問題。
endowment || n. || 捐款；天賦 || The school received a large endowment. || 學校獲得一大筆捐款。 || endowment fund=捐贈基金 || The university received an endowment ___ a rich family. || from|of|by || from + 來源 = 來自…。 || 這所大學收到一個富裕家族的捐贈。
endurance || n. || 耐力 || Running a marathon requires endurance. || 跑馬拉松需要耐力。 || physical endurance=體能耐力 || Swimming can build ___ endurance. || your|you|yours || 名詞 endurance 前用所有格形容詞。 || 游泳可以增強你的耐力。
equalize || v. || 使相等 || The team equalized in the last minute. || 球隊在最後一分鐘扳平比分。 || equalize=使平等 || The government wants to equalize opportunities ___ all children. || for|to|of || opportunities for = 給…的機會。 || 政府希望讓所有孩子機會均等。
equate || v. || 等同 || Don't equate money with happiness. || 不要把金錢和快樂畫上等號。 || equate A with B=把 A 等同於 B || Many people equate success ___ wealth. || with|to|for || equate A with B = 把 A 等同於 B。 || 許多人把成功等同於財富。
escort || v./n. || 護送 || The police escorted him home. || 警察護送他回家。 || police escort=警察護送 || The singer was escorted ___ the stage by two guards. || to|at|in || escort sb to = 護送某人到…。 || 那位歌手由兩名警衛護送上台。
esteem || n./v. || 尊重 || She is held in high esteem. || 她備受尊敬。 || self-esteem=自尊 || The teacher is held ___ high esteem by her students. || in|on|at || hold sb in high esteem = 非常尊敬某人。 || 那位老師深受學生尊敬。
eternity || n. || 永恆 || The wait felt like an eternity. || 等待感覺像永恆一樣漫長。 || for eternity=永遠 || Waiting for the results felt ___ an eternity. || like|as|alike || feel like + 名詞 = 感覺像…。 || 等待結果感覺像永恆一樣漫長。
evacuate || v. || 撤離 || People were evacuated from the building. || 人們從大樓撤離。 || evacuate=疏散 || Residents ___ evacuated before the typhoon arrived. || were|was|be || 被動語態；Residents 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 颱風來臨前居民被撤離了。
evergreen || adj./n. || 常綠的 || Pine trees are evergreen. || 松樹是常綠樹。 || evergreen tree=常綠樹 || Evergreen trees keep their leaves ___ year round. || all|every|whole || all year round = 一整年。 || 常綠樹一整年都保有葉子。
evoke || v. || 喚起 || The song evokes happy memories. || 這首歌喚起快樂的回憶。 || evoke memories=喚起回憶 || The smell of the soup evoked memories ___ my childhood. || of|for|to || memories of = …的回憶。 || 湯的香味喚起了我童年的回憶。
examinee || n. || 考生 || Each examinee must bring an ID card. || 每位考生必須攜帶身分證。 || examinee=應試者 || Each examinee ___ allowed to use a pencil and an eraser. || is|are|be || each + 單數名詞，動詞用單數。 || 每位考生可以使用一支鉛筆和一塊橡皮擦。
examiner || n. || 主考官 || The examiner asked me some questions. || 主考官問了我一些問題。 || driving examiner=駕照考官 || The examiner asked ___ I had any questions. || if|that|what || ask if = 詢問是否…。 || 主考官問我是否有任何問題。
excel || v. || 擅長 || She excels at math. || 她數學很好。 || excel at/in=擅長… || He excels ___ sports, especially basketball. || at|on|to || excel at = 擅長…。 || 他擅長運動，尤其是籃球。
excerpt || n. || 摘錄 || We read an excerpt from the novel. || 我們讀了小說的一段摘錄。 || excerpt from=…的摘錄 || The teacher read an excerpt ___ a famous novel. || from|of|by || an excerpt from = 摘自…。 || 老師朗讀了一段知名小說的摘錄。
excess || n./adj. || 過量 || Don't eat to excess. || 不要吃太多。 || in excess of=超過… || Eating sugar ___ excess is bad for your health. || to|on|with || to excess = 過度地。 || 過度吃糖對健康有害。
exclusion || n. || 排除 || He felt a sense of exclusion. || 他有被排擠的感覺。 || social exclusion=社會排斥 || She studied hard to the exclusion ___ everything else. || of|for|from || to the exclusion of = 排除…。 || 她拚命讀書，其他事都不管。
exempt || adj./v. || 免除的 || Students are exempt from the fee. || 學生免繳費用。 || be exempt from=免於… || Children under six are exempt ___ the entrance fee. || from|of|with || be exempt from = 免於…。 || 六歲以下兒童免收門票。
exert || v. || 施加；盡力 || Don't exert yourself too much. || 別太勉強自己。 || exert pressure=施加壓力 || Parents should not exert too much pressure ___ their children. || on|to|for || exert pressure on = 對…施加壓力。 || 父母不應對孩子施加太多壓力。
expenditure || n. || 支出 || The government cut its expenditure. || 政府削減了支出。 || public expenditure=公共支出 || The government increased its expenditure ___ education. || on|in|at || expenditure on = 在…上的支出。 || 政府增加了教育方面的支出。
expiration || n. || 到期 || Check the expiration date. || 檢查有效期限。 || expiration date=有效期限 || Don't eat food ___ the expiration date. || after|before|since || 過了期限「之後」，用 after。 || 不要吃過期的食物。
expire || v. || 到期 || My passport will expire next month. || 我的護照下個月到期。 || expire=失效 || Your library card expired ___ month ago. || a|an|the || month 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 你的借書證一個月前就過期了。
extract || v./n. || 提取；拔出 || The dentist extracted my tooth. || 牙醫拔了我的牙。 || extract information=獲取資訊 || Oil is extracted ___ olives. || from|of|by || extract A from B = 從 B 提取 A。 || 油是從橄欖中提煉出來的。
extracurricular || adj. || 課外的 || She joins many extracurricular activities. || 她參加很多課外活動。 || extracurricular activities=課外活動 || Joining extracurricular activities can help you make ___ friends. || new|news|newly || 修飾名詞用形容詞。 || 參加課外活動能幫你交到新朋友。
eyelash || n. || 睫毛 || She has long eyelashes. || 她有長長的睫毛。 || false eyelashes=假睫毛 || There is an eyelash ___ your cheek. || on|in|at || 在臉頰上用 on。 || 你臉頰上有一根睫毛。
eyelid || n. || 眼皮 || My eyelid is twitching. || 我的眼皮在跳。 || double eyelid=雙眼皮 || She has double eyelids, ___ her sister doesn't. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 她有雙眼皮，但她妹妹沒有。
eyesight || n. || 視力 || Reading in the dark is bad for your eyesight. || 在暗處看書對視力不好。 || poor eyesight=視力差 || Using phones too much can hurt your eyesight, ___ take a break every 30 minutes. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 用手機太多會傷害視力，所以每三十分鐘休息一下。
fable || n. || 寓言 || Aesop's fables teach lessons. || 伊索寓言傳達教訓。 || Aesop's Fables=伊索寓言 || Every fable has a lesson ___ teaches us something. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當主詞。 || 每則寓言都有一個教導我們的道理。
faction || n. || 派系 || The party split into two factions. || 那個政黨分裂成兩派。 || rival faction=敵對派系 || The party split ___ two factions. || into|onto|from || split into = 分裂成…。 || 那個政黨分裂成兩個派系。
Fahrenheit || n./adj. || 華氏 || It is 90 degrees Fahrenheit. || 現在是華氏九十度。 || degrees Fahrenheit=華氏…度 || Water freezes ___ 32 degrees Fahrenheit. || at|in|on || at + 溫度。 || 水在華氏三十二度結冰。
falter || v. || 搖晃；結巴 || His voice faltered. || 他的聲音顫抖了。 || falter=猶豫 || Her voice faltered ___ she began to cry. || as|which|what || as = 當…的時候。 || 她開始哭時聲音顫抖了。
familiarity || n. || 熟悉 || Familiarity with the city helps. || 熟悉這座城市很有幫助。 || familiarity with=對…的熟悉 || His familiarity ___ the area made him a great guide. || with|to|for || familiarity with = 對…的熟悉。 || 他對這個區域很熟，所以是很棒的嚮導。
fascination || n. || 著迷 || He has a fascination with space. || 他對太空很著迷。 || fascination with=對…的著迷 || The boy has a fascination ___ dinosaurs. || with|to|of || fascination with = 對…的著迷。 || 那個男孩對恐龍很著迷。
feasible || adj. || 可行的 || Is the plan feasible? || 這個計畫可行嗎？ || feasible plan=可行的計畫 || It is not feasible ___ finish the work in one day. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 一天內完成這項工作是不可行的。
feeble || adj. || 虛弱的 || The old man is feeble. || 那位老人很虛弱。 || feeble excuse=站不住腳的藉口 || His excuse was ___ feeble that nobody believed it. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他的藉口牽強到沒人相信。
feminine || adj. || 女性的；陰柔的 || She has a feminine style. || 她的風格很女性化。 || feminine=女性特質的 || The dress has a soft, feminine look, ___ my sister loves. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句修飾物，只能用 which。 || 這件洋裝有柔美的女性風格，我妹妹很喜歡。
fertility || n. || 肥沃；生育力 || The soil's fertility is high. || 這片土壤很肥沃。 || fertility rate=生育率 || Taiwan's fertility rate is one of the lowest ___ the world. || in|on|at || in the world = 在世界上。 || 台灣的生育率是世界最低之一。
fertilizer || n. || 肥料 || Farmers use fertilizer on their crops. || 農夫在作物上施肥。 || chemical fertilizer=化學肥料 || Too much fertilizer can pollute rivers ___ lakes. || and|or|but || 兩者並列用 and。 || 過多的肥料會汙染河川和湖泊。
fiance || n. || 未婚夫（fiancé） || She introduced her fiance to us. || 她向我們介紹她的未婚夫。 || fiance=未婚夫 || Her fiance, ___ is a doctor, works in Taipei. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 她那位當醫生的未婚夫在台北工作。
fin || n. || 鰭 || Sharks have big fins. || 鯊魚有很大的鰭。 || shark fin=魚翅 || Fish use their fins ___ swim. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 魚用鰭游泳。
finite || adj. || 有限的 || Oil is a finite resource. || 石油是有限的資源。 || finite resources=有限資源 || Water is a finite resource, ___ we should save it. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 水是有限資源，所以我們應該節約。
firecracker || n. || 鞭炮 || People set off firecrackers at New Year. || 人們在過年時放鞭炮。 || set off firecrackers=放鞭炮 || People set ___ firecrackers during Chinese New Year. || off|up|out || set off = 燃放。 || 人們在農曆新年期間放鞭炮。
fireproof || adj. || 防火的 || The door is fireproof. || 這扇門是防火的。 || fireproof material=防火材料 || The documents are kept in a fireproof box ___ safety. || for|to|of || for safety = 為了安全。 || 這些文件存放在防火箱裡以策安全。
fishery || n. || 漁業；漁場 || Fishery is important in this town. || 漁業對這個小鎮很重要。 || fishery industry=漁業 || Many people in the village work ___ the fishery. || in|on|to || work in = 在…行業工作。 || 村裡很多人從事漁業。
flake || n. || 薄片 || Flakes of snow fell from the sky. || 雪花從天空飄落。 || snowflake=雪花; corn flakes=玉米片 || I eat corn flakes ___ milk for breakfast. || with|by|of || with = 搭配…。 || 我早餐吃玉米片配牛奶。
flaw || n. || 缺點；瑕疵 || There is a flaw in your plan. || 你的計畫有個缺陷。 || fatal flaw=致命缺點 || Everyone has flaws; nobody is ___. || perfect|perfectly|perfection || be 動詞後接形容詞。 || 每個人都有缺點，沒有人是完美的。
flourish || v. || 繁榮；茂盛 || Plants flourish in this climate. || 植物在這種氣候下生長茂盛。 || flourish=興盛 || Her business has flourished ___ she moved to Taipei. || since|for|from || since + 子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 自從她搬到台北，生意就蒸蒸日上。
flunk || v. || 不及格 || He flunked the math test. || 他數學考不及格。 || flunk a test=考試不及格 || He flunked the test ___ he didn't study. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 他因為沒讀書所以考不及格。
foe || n. || 敵人 || They are old foes. || 他們是宿敵。 || friend or foe=敵友 || The two countries were once foes but are now ___. || friends|friendly|friendship || be 動詞後接名詞（複數）。 || 這兩國曾是敵人，現在是朋友。
folklore || n. || 民間傳說 || The story comes from Taiwanese folklore. || 這個故事來自台灣民間傳說。 || local folklore=地方傳說 || The story comes ___ Taiwanese folklore. || from|of|by || come from = 來自…。 || 這個故事來自台灣的民間傳說。
