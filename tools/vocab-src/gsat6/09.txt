# 學測 L6 09：headphone – instinctive
headphone || n. || 耳機（常用 headphones） || I listen to music with headphones. || 我戴耳機聽音樂。 || wireless headphones=無線耳機 || Please use headphones ___ you listen to music on the bus. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 在公車上聽音樂時請戴耳機。
healthful || adj. || 有益健康的 || Eat healthful food. || 吃有益健康的食物。 || healthful diet=健康飲食 || A healthful diet includes plenty ___ vegetables. || of|for|with || plenty of = 大量的…。 || 健康的飲食包含大量蔬菜。
hearty || adj. || 豐盛的；熱情的 || We had a hearty breakfast. || 我們吃了一頓豐盛的早餐。 || hearty meal=豐盛的一餐 || We had a hearty breakfast ___ we went hiking. || before|after|since || 健行「之前」吃早餐，用 before。 || 我們去健行前吃了一頓豐盛的早餐。
hedge || n./v. || 樹籬 || He trimmed the hedge. || 他修剪了樹籬。 || hedge=籬笆 || The garden is surrounded ___ a tall hedge. || by|with|from || be surrounded by = 被…環繞。 || 花園被高高的樹籬圍繞著。
heighten || v. || 提高；加強 || The music heightened the tension. || 音樂加強了緊張感。 || heighten awareness=提高意識 || The campaign aims ___ heighten awareness of the problem. || to|for|at || aim to + 原形動詞 = 旨在…。 || 這個活動旨在提高大眾對這個問題的意識。
hemisphere || n. || 半球 || Taiwan is in the Northern Hemisphere. || 台灣位於北半球。 || Northern Hemisphere=北半球 || Australia is ___ the Southern Hemisphere. || in|on|at || in a hemisphere = 在…半球。 || 澳洲位於南半球。
heroic || adj. || 英勇的 || The firefighter made a heroic rescue. || 消防員英勇地救了人。 || heroic act=英勇的行為 || The firefighter was praised ___ his heroic act. || for|of|with || praise sb for = 因…稱讚某人。 || 那位消防員因英勇的行為而受到讚揚。
heroin || n. || 海洛因 || Heroin is a dangerous drug. || 海洛因是危險的毒品。 || heroin addiction=海洛因成癮 || He was arrested ___ selling heroin. || for|of|with || arrest sb for + 罪名。 || 他因販賣海洛因被捕。
heterosexual || adj./n. || 異性戀的 || The law protects both heterosexual and same-sex couples. || 法律同時保障異性戀與同性伴侶。 || heterosexual couple=異性戀伴侶 || The law treats heterosexual and same-sex couples ___ the same way. || in|on|at || in the same way = 以同樣的方式。 || 法律以同樣的方式對待異性戀與同性伴侶。
hierarchy || n. || 階級制度 || The company has a strict hierarchy. || 這家公司階級分明。 || social hierarchy=社會階層 || In the old days, there was a strict social hierarchy ___ the village. || in|on|to || in + 地方。 || 以前村子裡有嚴格的社會階層。
hijack || v. || 劫持 || The plane was hijacked. || 那架飛機遭劫持。 || hijack a plane=劫機 || The plane ___ hijacked on its way to Tokyo. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那架飛機在飛往東京途中遭到劫持。
hoarse || adj. || 嘶啞的 || My voice is hoarse from shouting. || 我喊到聲音沙啞。 || hoarse voice=沙啞的聲音 || Her voice was hoarse ___ singing all night. || from|of|with || from + V-ing = 由於…。 || 她唱了一整晚，聲音都沙啞了。
homosexual || adj./n. || 同性戀的 || Homosexual couples can marry in Taiwan. || 同性伴侶在台灣可以結婚。 || homosexual=同性戀 || In 2019, Taiwan allowed homosexual couples ___ marry. || to|for|of || allow sb to + 原形動詞。 || 2019 年台灣允許同性伴侶結婚。
honorary || adj. || 榮譽的 || She received an honorary degree. || 她獲頒榮譽學位。 || honorary degree=榮譽學位 || The singer received an honorary degree ___ the university. || from|of|by || from + 機構 = 來自…。 || 那位歌手獲得那所大學頒發的榮譽學位。
hospitable || adj. || 好客的 || The local people are very hospitable. || 當地人非常好客。 || hospitable=熱情好客 || The villagers were very hospitable ___ us. || to|for|with || be hospitable to = 對…熱情好客。 || 村民對我們非常熱情好客。
hospitality || n. || 好客；款待 || Thank you for your hospitality. || 謝謝你的款待。 || Southern hospitality=熱情款待 || Thank you ___ your warm hospitality. || for|of|with || thank sb for = 因…感謝某人。 || 謝謝你熱情的款待。
hospitalize || v. || 使住院 || He was hospitalized for a week. || 他住院一週。 || be hospitalized=住院 || My grandfather was hospitalized ___ a week last month. || for|in|since || for + 一段時間。 || 我祖父上個月住院一週。
hostel || n. || 青年旅館 || We stayed at a youth hostel. || 我們住青年旅館。 || youth hostel=青年旅館 || Staying in a hostel is cheaper ___ staying in a hotel. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 住青年旅館比住飯店便宜。
hover || v. || 盤旋 || A helicopter hovered above us. || 一架直升機在我們頭上盤旋。 || hover over=在…上空盤旋 || The bird hovered ___ the water. || above|on|in || above = 在…上方。 || 鳥在水面上方盤旋。
humiliate || v. || 羞辱 || He felt humiliated. || 他覺得受到羞辱。 || feel humiliated=感到丟臉 || She was humiliated ___ front of the whole class. || in|on|at || in front of = 在…前面。 || 她在全班面前被羞辱。
hunch || n. || 預感 || I have a hunch he will win. || 我有預感他會贏。 || have a hunch=有預感 || I have a hunch ___ it's going to rain. || that|what|which || that 引導同位語子句，說明 hunch 的內容。 || 我有預感要下雨了。
hurdle || n. || 障礙；跨欄 || We overcame many hurdles. || 我們克服了許多障礙。 || overcome a hurdle=克服障礙 || Language is the biggest hurdle ___ many new immigrants. || for|of|with || for + 對象 = 對…而言。 || 語言是許多新移民最大的障礙。
hybrid || n./adj. || 混合物；油電車 || He drives a hybrid car. || 他開油電混合車。 || hybrid car=油電混合車 || A hybrid car uses ___ gas and electricity. || both|either|neither || both A and B = A 和 B 都。 || 油電混合車同時使用汽油和電力。
hygiene || n. || 衛生 || Good hygiene prevents disease. || 良好的衛生能預防疾病。 || personal hygiene=個人衛生 || Washing your hands is an important part ___ personal hygiene. || of|for|in || part of = …的一部分。 || 洗手是個人衛生的重要一環。
hypocrite || n. || 偽君子 || Don't be a hypocrite. || 別當偽君子。 || hypocrite=偽善者 || He tells us not to lie, but he lies all the time; he is ___ hypocrite. || a|an|the || hypocrite 以子音 /h/ 開頭，用 a。 || 他叫我們不要說謊，自己卻老是說謊，真是個偽君子。
iceberg || n. || 冰山 || The ship hit an iceberg. || 那艘船撞上了冰山。 || tip of the iceberg=冰山一角 || The problems we see are only the tip ___ the iceberg. || of|for|in || the tip of the iceberg = 冰山一角。 || 我們看到的問題只是冰山一角。
illuminate || v. || 照亮；闡明 || The fireworks illuminated the sky. || 煙火照亮了夜空。 || illuminate=照明 || The streets were illuminated ___ colorful lights. || by|of|to || 被動語態中動作執行者／工具用 by。 || 街道被五顏六色的燈照亮。
imminent || adj. || 即將發生的 || A storm is imminent. || 暴風雨即將來臨。 || imminent danger=迫在眉睫的危險 || The typhoon is imminent, ___ stay indoors. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 颱風即將來襲，所以請待在室內。
imperative || adj./n. || 緊急的；必要的 || It is imperative that we act now. || 我們必須立即行動。 || it is imperative that=…是必要的 || It is imperative that he ___ a doctor right away. || see|sees|saw || It is imperative that + 主詞 + (should) + 原形動詞。 || 他必須立刻去看醫生。
imperial || adj. || 帝國的；皇家的 || We visited the imperial palace. || 我們參觀了皇宮。 || imperial palace=皇宮 || The Imperial Palace in Tokyo is open ___ the public on some days. || to|for|with || be open to the public = 對外開放。 || 東京的皇居某些日子對外開放。
implicit || adj. || 含蓄的；絕對的 || There was an implicit agreement. || 有一個默契。 || implicit trust=完全的信任 || She has implicit trust ___ her best friend. || in|on|at || trust in = 對…的信任。 || 她完全信任她最好的朋友。
imposing || adj. || 壯觀的；令人印象深刻的 || The building is imposing. || 這棟建築很壯觀。 || imposing building=宏偉的建築 || The museum is housed in an imposing building ___ the river. || by|in|of || by = 在…旁邊。 || 博物館位於河畔一棟宏偉的建築裡。
incline || v./n. || 傾向；斜坡 || I'm inclined to agree with you. || 我傾向於同意你。 || be inclined to V=傾向於… || I'm inclined ___ believe her story. || to|for|of || be inclined to + 原形動詞 = 傾向於…。 || 我傾向於相信她的說法。
inclusive || adj. || 包含的；包容的 || The price is inclusive of tax. || 價格含稅。 || inclusive of=包含… || The price is inclusive ___ breakfast. || of|for|with || be inclusive of = 包含…。 || 價格包含早餐。
incur || v. || 招致 || You will incur a fine if you are late. || 遲到的話你會被罰款。 || incur costs=產生費用 || You will incur a fine ___ you return the book late. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你逾期還書，就會被罰款。
indifference || n. || 冷漠 || His indifference hurt her. || 他的冷漠傷了她。 || indifference to=對…的冷漠 || I was shocked by his indifference ___ other people's pain. || to|for|with || indifference to = 對…的冷漠。 || 他對別人痛苦的冷漠讓我震驚。
indignant || adj. || 憤慨的 || She was indignant at the unfair rule. || 她對不公平的規定感到憤慨。 || be indignant at=對…憤慨 || The workers were indignant ___ the unfair treatment. || at|to|for || be indignant at = 對…感到憤慨。 || 工人們對不公平的待遇感到憤慨。
induce || v. || 引起；誘使 || Some medicines induce sleep. || 有些藥會讓人想睡。 || induce sleep=催眠 || Nothing could induce me ___ change my mind. || to|for|of || induce sb to V = 誘使某人做…。 || 沒有什麼能讓我改變主意。
industrialize || v. || 工業化 || Taiwan industrialized quickly. || 台灣快速工業化。 || industrialized country=工業化國家 || Taiwan became industrialized ___ the 1970s. || in|on|at || in + 年代。 || 台灣在 1970 年代工業化。
infectious || adj. || 傳染的；有感染力的 || Her laughter is infectious. || 她的笑聲很有感染力。 || infectious disease=傳染病 || Her smile is ___ infectious that everyone smiles back. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 她的笑容如此有感染力，大家都回以微笑。
infer || v. || 推論 || What can you infer from the text? || 你能從文章推論出什麼？ || infer from=從…推斷 || What can we infer ___ the last paragraph? || from|of|by || infer from = 從…推斷。 || 我們可以從最後一段推斷出什麼？
inflict || v. || 施加（痛苦） || The storm inflicted heavy damage. || 暴風雨造成重大損害。 || inflict damage on=對…造成損害 || The typhoon inflicted serious damage ___ the farms. || on|to|for || inflict A on B = 對 B 造成 A。 || 颱風對農場造成嚴重損害。
inhabit || v. || 居住於 || Many birds inhabit the island. || 許多鳥類棲息在這座島上。 || inhabit=棲息 || The island is inhabited ___ rare birds. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這座島上棲息著稀有鳥類。
inhabitant || n. || 居民 || The town has 5,000 inhabitants. || 這個小鎮有五千名居民。 || local inhabitant=當地居民 || Most inhabitants ___ the island are fishermen. || of|for|to || inhabitants of = …的居民。 || 島上的居民大多是漁民。
injustice || n. || 不公平 || We must fight injustice. || 我們必須對抗不公不義。 || social injustice=社會不公 || She has spent her life fighting ___ injustice. || against|to|with || fight against = 對抗…。 || 她一生都在對抗不公不義。
inland || adv./adj. || 內陸的 || They moved inland. || 他們搬到內陸。 || inland area=內陸地區 || The typhoon moved inland ___ weakened. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的動作用 and。 || 颱風往內陸移動並減弱。
innumerable || adj. || 無數的 || There are innumerable stars in the sky. || 天空中有無數的星星。 || innumerable=數不清的 || There are innumerable stars ___ the sky tonight. || in|on|at || in the sky = 在天空中。 || 今晚天空中有無數的星星。
inquire || v. || 詢問 || I called to inquire about the price. || 我打電話詢問價格。 || inquire about=詢問… || I'd like to inquire ___ the train schedule. || about|to|of || inquire about = 詢問…。 || 我想詢問火車時刻表。
insistence || n. || 堅持 || At my mother's insistence, I wore a coat. || 在媽媽的堅持下，我穿了外套。 || at one's insistence=在某人堅持下 || ___ my teacher's insistence, I entered the contest. || At|In|On || at one's insistence = 在某人堅持下。 || 在老師的堅持下，我參加了比賽。
instinctive || adj. || 本能的 || Her reaction was instinctive. || 她的反應是出於本能。 || instinctive reaction=本能反應 || Pulling your hand away from fire is ___ instinctive reaction. || an|a|the || instinctive 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 手碰到火就縮回是本能反應。
