# 學測 L6 03：brink – clam
brink || n. || 邊緣 || The company is on the brink of failure. || 這家公司瀕臨倒閉。 || on the brink of=瀕臨… || Many animals are ___ the brink of extinction. || on|in|at || on the brink of = 瀕臨…。 || 許多動物瀕臨絕種。
broaden || v. || 拓寬 || Travel broadens the mind. || 旅行能開闊眼界。 || broaden one's horizons=開闊眼界 || Reading can help you broaden ___ horizons. || your|you|yours || 名詞 horizons 前用所有格形容詞。 || 閱讀能幫助你開闊眼界。
brochure || n. || 小冊子 || Take a travel brochure. || 拿一份旅遊手冊。 || travel brochure=旅遊手冊 || You can find more information ___ this brochure. || in|on|at || 在冊子中用 in。 || 你可以在這本手冊中找到更多資訊。
broil || v. || 烤 || She broiled the fish. || 她烤了魚。 || broiled chicken=烤雞 || Broil the fish ___ ten minutes. || for|in|since || for + 一段時間。 || 把魚烤十分鐘。
brook || n. || 小溪 || A brook runs through the farm. || 一條小溪流經農場。 || babbling brook=潺潺小溪 || The children played ___ the brook all afternoon. || by|on|of || by = 在…旁邊。 || 孩子們整個下午在小溪邊玩。
broth || n. || 湯；高湯 || She made chicken broth. || 她煮了雞湯。 || chicken broth=雞湯 || Hot chicken broth is good ___ a cold. || for|to|with || be good for = 對…有益。 || 熱雞湯對感冒有幫助。
brotherhood || n. || 兄弟情誼 || They share a strong brotherhood. || 他們有深厚的兄弟情誼。 || sense of brotherhood=手足情誼 || The team has a strong sense of brotherhood ___ its players. || among|between|along || among = 在…之中。 || 球隊的隊員之間有很強的兄弟情誼。
bulky || adj. || 龐大的 || The box is too bulky to carry. || 這個箱子太大了搬不動。 || bulky item=大型物品 || The sofa is too bulky ___ fit through the door. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + to V。 || 沙發太大了，進不了門。
bureaucrat || n. || 官僚 || The bureaucrats delayed the plan. || 官員拖延了計畫。 || government bureaucrat=政府官僚 || The bureaucrat ___ handled my case was very slow. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 處理我案件的那位官員動作很慢。
bypass || v./n. || 繞過；繞道 || The new road bypasses the town. || 新路繞過了小鎮。 || heart bypass=心臟繞道手術 || We took the bypass ___ avoid the traffic in town. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們走外環道以避開鎮上的車潮。
caffeine || n. || 咖啡因 || Coffee contains caffeine. || 咖啡含有咖啡因。 || caffeine-free=無咖啡因 || Too much caffeine can keep you ___ at night. || awake|awaken|awaking || keep + 受詞 + 形容詞。 || 太多咖啡因會讓你晚上睡不著。
calculator || n. || 計算機 || You may use a calculator. || 你可以使用計算機。 || pocket calculator=口袋型計算機 || Students are not allowed ___ use calculators during the test. || to|for|of || be allowed to + 原形動詞。 || 考試時學生不准使用計算機。
calligraphy || n. || 書法 || My grandfather practices calligraphy. || 我祖父練書法。 || Chinese calligraphy=中國書法 || My grandfather has practiced calligraphy ___ fifty years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 我祖父練書法已經五十年了。
cape || n. || 披風；岬角 || The superhero wears a red cape. || 那位超級英雄穿紅色披風。 || Cape of Good Hope=好望角 || The boy ___ a red cape is pretending to be Superman. || in|at|on || in + 衣物 = 穿著…。 || 那個披著紅色披風的男孩在假扮超人。
capsule || n. || 膠囊 || Take one capsule a day. || 每天吃一顆膠囊。 || time capsule=時空膠囊 || We buried a time capsule ___ the school garden. || in|on|at || 埋在…裡用 in。 || 我們把一個時空膠囊埋在學校花園裡。
caption || n. || 圖說；字幕 || Read the caption under the photo. || 讀照片下方的說明文字。 || photo caption=圖說 || Write a short caption ___ each picture. || for|at|to || a caption for = 為…寫的說明。 || 為每張圖片寫一段簡短的說明。
captive || n./adj. || 俘虜；被俘的 || The soldiers were held captive. || 士兵們被俘虜了。 || hold sb captive=囚禁某人 || The animals ___ kept captive in small cages. || were|was|be || 被動語態；animals 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 那些動物被關在小籠子裡。
captivity || n. || 囚禁 || The animals were born in captivity. || 這些動物在圈養環境中出生。 || in captivity=被囚禁；圈養 || Some animals don't live long ___ captivity. || in|on|at || in captivity = 在圈養中。 || 有些動物在圈養環境下活不久。
cardboard || n. || 硬紙板 || The box is made of cardboard. || 這個箱子是硬紙板做的。 || cardboard box=紙箱 || The children made a house ___ cardboard boxes. || out of|to|into || make A out of B = 用 B 做成 A。 || 孩子們用紙箱做了一間房子。
cardinal || adj./n. || 基本的；樞機主教 || Honesty is a cardinal rule here. || 誠實是這裡的基本原則。 || cardinal rule=基本原則 || The cardinal rule ___ safety is to stay calm. || of|at|to || the rule of = …的原則。 || 安全的首要原則是保持冷靜。
carefree || adj. || 無憂無慮的 || Children live a carefree life. || 孩子們過著無憂無慮的生活。 || carefree days=無憂無慮的日子 || I miss the carefree days ___ I was a child. || when|which|what || 關係副詞 when 修飾時間名詞。 || 我懷念小時候無憂無慮的日子。
caretaker || n. || 看管人；照顧者 || The caretaker opened the gate. || 管理員打開了大門。 || caretaker=管理員 || She is the main caretaker ___ her sick mother. || of|at|to || the caretaker of = …的照顧者。 || 她是生病母親的主要照顧者。
carton || n. || 紙盒 || I bought a carton of milk. || 我買了一盒牛奶。 || a carton of=一盒… || Could you buy a carton ___ eggs on your way home? || of|for|with || a carton of = 一盒…。 || 你回家路上可以買一盒蛋嗎？
cashier || n. || 收銀員 || Please pay the cashier. || 請向收銀員付款。 || cashier=出納 || Please pay ___ the cashier near the door. || at|on|of || pay at = 在…付款。 || 請在門口附近的收銀台付款。
casualty || n. || 傷亡人員 || There were no casualties in the fire. || 火災中沒有人傷亡。 || heavy casualties=傷亡慘重 || Luckily, there were no casualties ___ the accident. || in|on|at || in an accident = 在事故中。 || 幸運的是，這場意外沒有人傷亡。
catastrophe || n. || 大災難 || The flood was a catastrophe. || 這場洪水是一場大災難。 || natural catastrophe=天災 || The earthquake was a catastrophe ___ the whole country. || for|of|with || a catastrophe for = 對…是一場災難。 || 這場地震對全國而言是一場災難。
cater || v. || 迎合；承辦宴席 || The restaurant caters for weddings. || 這家餐廳承辦婚宴。 || cater to=迎合… || The store caters ___ young customers. || to|of|with || cater to = 迎合…。 || 這家店迎合年輕顧客的需求。
caterpillar || n. || 毛毛蟲 || The caterpillar became a butterfly. || 毛毛蟲變成了蝴蝶。 || hungry caterpillar=好餓的毛毛蟲 || A caterpillar turns ___ a butterfly. || into|onto|from || turn into = 變成…。 || 毛毛蟲會變成蝴蝶。
cavity || n. || 蛀洞；腔 || I have a cavity in my tooth. || 我的牙齒有蛀洞。 || tooth cavity=蛀牙 || Eating too much candy can cause cavities ___ your teeth. || in|on|at || in your teeth = 在牙齒裡。 || 吃太多糖果會讓你蛀牙。
celery || n. || 芹菜 || I don't like celery. || 我不喜歡芹菜。 || celery stick=芹菜棒 || Cut the celery ___ small pieces. || into|onto|from || cut A into B = 把 A 切成 B。 || 把芹菜切成小塊。
cellular || adj. || 細胞的；行動電話的 || Please turn off your cellular phone. || 請關掉你的手機。 || cellular phone=手機 || Cellular phones ___ not allowed during the exam. || are|is|be || 主詞 phones 複數，用 are。 || 考試期間不准使用手機。
Celsius || adj./n. || 攝氏 || It's 30 degrees Celsius today. || 今天攝氏三十度。 || degrees Celsius=攝氏…度 || Water boils ___ 100 degrees Celsius. || at|in|on || at + 溫度。 || 水在攝氏一百度沸騰。
cement || n./v. || 水泥 || The workers mixed cement. || 工人在攪拌水泥。 || cement floor=水泥地 || The wall is made ___ cement. || of|at|by || be made of = 由…製成（看得出原料）。 || 這面牆是水泥做的。
census || n. || 人口普查 || The government takes a census every ten years. || 政府每十年做一次人口普查。 || take a census=做人口普查 || A census is taken ___ ten years. || every|each|all || every + 數字 + 複數名詞 = 每隔…。 || 每十年進行一次人口普查。
ceramic || adj./n. || 陶瓷的 || She makes ceramic cups. || 她做陶瓷杯。 || ceramic tile=瓷磚 || This ceramic vase was made ___ hand. || by|with|in || by hand = 手工。 || 這個陶瓷花瓶是手工做的。
certify || v. || 證明；認證 || The doctor certified that he was healthy. || 醫生證明他很健康。 || certified teacher=合格教師 || She is a certified teacher ___ ten years of experience. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 她是一位有十年經驗的合格教師。
chairwoman || n. || 女主席 || The chairwoman opened the meeting. || 女主席宣布會議開始。 || chairwoman=女主席 || The chairwoman, ___ was elected last year, gave a speech. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 去年當選的女主席發表了演說。
champagne || n. || 香檳 || They drank champagne at the wedding. || 他們在婚禮上喝香檳。 || a glass of champagne=一杯香檳 || They opened a bottle of champagne ___ celebrate. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他們開了一瓶香檳慶祝。
chant || v./n. || 反覆呼喊；誦經 || The fans chanted the team's name. || 球迷們反覆呼喊球隊的名字。 || chant slogans=呼喊口號 || The crowd chanted slogans ___ they marched. || as|which|what || as = 一邊…一邊…。 || 群眾一邊遊行一邊呼喊口號。
charitable || adj. || 慈善的 || She gives money to charitable groups. || 她捐錢給慈善團體。 || charitable organization=慈善機構 || All the money will be given ___ a charitable organization. || to|for|with || give sth to sb = 把某物給某人。 || 所有的錢都將捐給一個慈善機構。
checkup || n. || 健康檢查 || I go for a checkup every year. || 我每年做一次健康檢查。 || medical checkup=體檢 || You should have a checkup ___ least once a year. || at|in|on || at least = 至少。 || 你應該至少每年做一次健康檢查。
chemist || n. || 化學家；藥劑師 || She is a chemist. || 她是化學家。 || research chemist=研究化學家 || The chemist ___ discovered the new medicine won an award. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 發現新藥的那位化學家獲得了獎項。
chestnut || n. || 栗子 || We roasted chestnuts. || 我們烤了栗子。 || roasted chestnuts=烤栗子 || Roasted chestnuts are popular ___ winter. || in|on|at || 季節前用 in。 || 烤栗子在冬天很受歡迎。
chili || n. || 辣椒 || Add some chili to the soup. || 在湯裡加一些辣椒。 || chili sauce=辣椒醬 || Don't put too much chili ___ the soup. || in|on|at || 加在湯裡用 in。 || 湯裡不要加太多辣椒。
chimpanzee || n. || 黑猩猩 || Chimpanzees are very smart. || 黑猩猩非常聰明。 || chimpanzee=黑猩猩 || Chimpanzees are among the smartest animals ___ Earth. || on|in|at || on Earth = 在地球上。 || 黑猩猩是地球上最聰明的動物之一。
chirp || v./n. || 啾啾叫 || The birds chirped in the morning. || 鳥兒在早上啾啾叫。 || chirp=鳥鳴 || I woke up ___ the birds chirping outside. || to|with|by || wake up to = 醒來時聽見…。 || 我醒來時聽到外面鳥兒在啾啾叫。
cholesterol || n. || 膽固醇 || Eggs are high in cholesterol. || 蛋的膽固醇很高。 || high cholesterol=高膽固醇 || Eating too much fried food can raise your cholesterol, ___ is bad for your heart. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句中 which 可指前面整句。 || 吃太多油炸食物會提高你的膽固醇，這對心臟不好。
cigar || n. || 雪茄 || He smoked a cigar. || 他抽了一根雪茄。 || smoke a cigar=抽雪茄 || Smoking cigars is as harmful ___ smoking cigarettes. || as|than|so || as…as = 和…一樣。 || 抽雪茄和抽香菸一樣有害。
civilize || v. || 使開化 || Education can civilize people. || 教育可以使人文明。 || civilized society=文明社會 || In a civilized society, people should respect ___ other. || each|every|all || each other = 彼此。 || 在文明社會中，人們應該互相尊重。
clam || n. || 蛤蜊 || We had clam soup for dinner. || 我們晚餐喝了蛤蜊湯。 || clam soup=蛤蜊湯 || We dug for clams ___ the beach. || on|of|to || on the beach = 在海灘上。 || 我們在海灘上挖蛤蜊。
