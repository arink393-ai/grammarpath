# 學測 L6 19：thereafter – vice
thereafter || adv. || 此後 || He moved to Tokyo and lived there thereafter. || 他搬到東京，此後就一直住在那裡。 || shortly thereafter=此後不久 || She graduated in 2020 and found a job shortly thereafter, ___ she? || didn't|did|wasn't || 前句動詞為過去肯定，附加問句用 didn't she。 || 她 2020 年畢業，此後不久就找到工作了，不是嗎？
thermometer || n. || 溫度計 || The thermometer shows 30 degrees. || 溫度計顯示三十度。 || thermometer=體溫計 || The nurse took my temperature ___ a thermometer. || with|by|on || with + 工具 = 用…。 || 護理師用體溫計幫我量體溫。
tilt || v./n. || 傾斜 || He tilted his head. || 他歪著頭。 || tilt=傾斜 || The picture on the wall is tilted ___ the left. || to|at|on || to the left = 向左。 || 牆上的畫向左傾斜。
tiptoe || v./n. || 踮腳走 || She tiptoed into the room. || 她踮著腳走進房間。 || on tiptoe=踮著腳 || She tiptoed ___ the room so she wouldn't wake the baby. || into|onto|from || into = 進入…。 || 她踮著腳走進房間，以免吵醒寶寶。
tiresome || adj. || 令人厭煩的 || The long meeting was tiresome. || 冗長的會議令人厭煩。 || tiresome=累人的 || Doing the same thing every day can be ___ tiresome. || very|much|many || 形容詞前用 very 加強。 || 每天做同樣的事可能非常令人厭煩。
token || n. || 象徵；代幣 || He gave her a gift as a token of thanks. || 他送她一份禮物以表謝意。 || a token of=…的象徵 || Please accept this gift as a token ___ our thanks. || of|for|to || a token of = …的象徵。 || 請收下這份禮物，聊表我們的謝意。
tornado || n. || 龍捲風 || The tornado destroyed many houses. || 龍捲風摧毀了許多房屋。 || tornado=旋風 || Many houses ___ destroyed by the tornado. || were|was|be || 被動語態；houses 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 許多房屋被龍捲風摧毀。
torrent || n. || 急流；洪流 || The rain fell in torrents. || 大雨傾盆而下。 || a torrent of=一連串… || The rain came down ___ torrents. || in|on|with || in torrents = 傾盆地。 || 大雨傾盆而下。
trademark || n. || 商標；特徵 || The red hat is his trademark. || 紅帽子是他的招牌。 || registered trademark=註冊商標 || His big smile is his trademark, ___ everyone loves it. || and|or|but || 補充說明用 and。 || 燦爛的笑容是他的招牌，大家都很喜歡。
transcript || n. || 成績單；文字紀錄 || Please send your high school transcript. || 請寄送你的高中成績單。 || academic transcript=成績單 || You need to send your transcript ___ the university. || to|for|with || send sth to = 把某物寄給…。 || 你需要把成績單寄給那所大學。
transmit || v. || 傳送；傳染 || Mosquitoes can transmit diseases. || 蚊子會傳播疾病。 || transmit a disease=傳染疾病 || Some diseases are transmitted ___ mosquitoes. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 有些疾病是由蚊子傳播的。
transplant || n./v. || 移植 || He had a heart transplant. || 他接受了心臟移植。 || organ transplant=器官移植 || She needs a kidney transplant ___ survive. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她需要腎臟移植才能活下去。
treasury || n. || 國庫；寶庫 || The book is a treasury of stories. || 這本書是故事的寶庫。 || a treasury of=…的寶庫 || This museum is a treasury ___ ancient art. || of|for|to || a treasury of = …的寶庫。 || 這間博物館是古代藝術的寶庫。
trek || n./v. || 長途跋涉 || We went on a trek in the mountains. || 我們在山中長途健行。 || go on a trek=去健行 || We went ___ a three-day trek in Nepal. || on|in|at || go on a trek = 去健行。 || 我們在尼泊爾進行三天的健行。
trifle || n. || 瑣事 || Don't worry about such a trifle. || 別擔心這種小事。 || a trifle=一點點 || Don't argue ___ such a trifle. || over|in|at || argue over = 為…爭吵。 || 別為這種小事爭吵。
trillion || n. || 兆 || The company is worth a trillion dollars. || 這家公司市值一兆美元。 || trillion=兆 || The company is worth more ___ one trillion dollars. || than|then|as || more than = 超過。 || 這家公司市值超過一兆美元。
tropic || n. || 回歸線；熱帶 || The Tropic of Cancer passes through Taiwan. || 北回歸線經過台灣。 || Tropic of Cancer=北回歸線 || The Tropic of Cancer passes ___ Chiayi. || through|across|along || pass through = 穿過…。 || 北回歸線經過嘉義。
trout || n. || 鱒魚 || We caught some trout. || 我們釣到一些鱒魚。 || rainbow trout=虹鱒 || We caught three trout ___ the river. || in|on|at || 在河裡用 in。 || 我們在河裡釣到三條鱒魚。
trustee || n. || 受託人；董事 || She is a trustee of the museum. || 她是博物館的董事。 || board of trustees=董事會 || The board ___ trustees will meet next week. || of|for|to || board of trustees = 董事會。 || 董事會將在下週開會。
tuck || v. || 塞進；把…塞好 || Tuck your shirt in. || 把襯衫紮進去。 || tuck in=把…塞進去 || The mother tucked her child ___ bed. || into|onto|from || tuck sb into bed = 替某人蓋好被子。 || 媽媽替孩子蓋好被子。
turmoil || n. || 混亂 || The country is in turmoil. || 這個國家陷入動盪。 || in turmoil=陷入混亂 || The country was ___ turmoil after the election. || in|on|at || in turmoil = 陷入混亂。 || 選舉後這個國家陷入動盪。
twilight || n. || 黃昏；暮光 || We walked home at twilight. || 我們在黃昏時走回家。 || at twilight=黃昏時 || The sky turns purple ___ twilight. || at|in|on || at twilight = 在黃昏時。 || 黃昏時天空變成紫色。
twinkle || v./n. || 閃爍 || The stars twinkled in the sky. || 星星在天空中閃爍。 || twinkle=閃耀 || Stars twinkle ___ the night sky. || in|on|at || in the sky = 在天空中。 || 星星在夜空中閃爍。
unanimous || adj. || 一致同意的 || The decision was unanimous. || 這個決定是全體一致通過的。 || unanimous vote=全票通過 || The vote was unanimous; everyone agreed ___ the plan. || with|to|on || agree with = 同意…。 || 全票通過，大家都同意這個計畫。
unconditional || adj. || 無條件的 || A mother's love is unconditional. || 母愛是無條件的。 || unconditional love=無條件的愛 || Parents give their children unconditional ___. || love|loving|lovely || 形容詞 + 名詞。 || 父母給孩子無條件的愛。
underestimate || v. || 低估 || Don't underestimate your opponent. || 不要低估你的對手。 || underestimate=小看 || Never underestimate the power ___ hard work. || of|for|to || the power of = …的力量。 || 永遠不要低估努力的力量。
underpass || n. || 地下道 || Use the underpass to cross the road. || 走地下道過馬路。 || pedestrian underpass=行人地下道 || Please use the underpass ___ cross the busy road. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 請走地下道穿越繁忙的馬路。
underway || adj. || 進行中的 || The project is already underway. || 這個計畫已在進行中。 || get underway=開始 || Work on the new library is already underway ___ will finish next year. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的敘述用 and。 || 新圖書館的工程已經在進行，明年完工。
unification || n. || 統一 || The unification of Germany happened in 1990. || 德國在 1990 年統一。 || unification=合併 || The unification ___ Germany took place in 1990. || of|for|to || the unification of = …的統一。 || 德國統一發生在 1990 年。
unify || v. || 統一 || The king unified the country. || 國王統一了國家。 || unify=使一致 || The first emperor unified China ___ 221 BC. || in|on|at || 年份前用 in。 || 秦始皇在西元前 221 年統一中國。
unveil || v. || 揭幕；公布 || The company unveiled its new phone. || 公司發表了新手機。 || unveil a statue=為雕像揭幕 || The company will unveil its new phone ___ September. || in|on|at || 月份前用 in。 || 公司將在九月發表新手機。
upright || adj./adv. || 直立的；正直的 || Sit upright. || 坐直。 || upright=筆直 || Please keep your seat upright ___ takeoff. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 起飛期間請將椅背豎直。
uprising || n. || 起義 || The uprising was put down. || 起義被鎮壓了。 || popular uprising=民眾起義 || The uprising began ___ the people were tired of the cruel king. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為人民受夠了殘暴的國王，起義爆發了。
upward || adv./adj. || 向上 || Prices are moving upward. || 物價正在上漲。 || upward trend=上升趨勢 || The balloon floated upward ___ the sky. || into|onto|from || into = 進入…。 || 氣球向上飄進天空。
urgency || n. || 緊急 || There is a sense of urgency. || 有一種急迫感。 || sense of urgency=緊迫感 || The doctor spoke ___ a sense of urgency. || with|by|in || with + 抽象名詞 = 帶著…。 || 醫生帶著急迫感說話。
usher || n./v. || 引座員；引領 || The usher showed us to our seats. || 引座員帶我們到座位。 || usher in=開創 || The usher led us ___ our seats. || to|at|in || lead sb to = 帶某人到…。 || 引座員帶我們到座位。
utensil || n. || 器具 || Kitchen utensils are in the drawer. || 廚房用具在抽屜裡。 || kitchen utensils=廚房用具 || Chopsticks are the most common eating utensils ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || in + 國家。 || 筷子是台灣最常見的餐具。
utter || v./adj. || 說出；完全的 || He didn't utter a word. || 他一句話也沒說。 || utter nonsense=胡說八道 || She was so shocked that she couldn't utter ___ single word. || a|an|the || single 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 她震驚得說不出一句話。
vaccine || n. || 疫苗 || Have you had the flu vaccine? || 你打過流感疫苗了嗎？ || flu vaccine=流感疫苗 || The vaccine protects people ___ the virus. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 疫苗保護人們免受病毒感染。
vanilla || n. || 香草 || I'd like vanilla ice cream. || 我想要香草冰淇淋。 || vanilla ice cream=香草冰淇淋 || Would you like vanilla ___ chocolate ice cream? || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 你要香草還是巧克力冰淇淋？
vanity || n. || 虛榮 || His vanity made him buy expensive clothes. || 他的虛榮心讓他買昂貴的衣服。 || out of vanity=出於虛榮 || She bought the expensive bag out ___ vanity. || of|from|for || out of + 名詞 = 出於…。 || 她出於虛榮買了那個昂貴的包包。
vapor || n. || 蒸氣 || Water vapor rises from the lake. || 水蒸氣從湖面升起。 || water vapor=水蒸氣 || Water turns ___ vapor when it boils. || into|onto|from || turn into = 變成…。 || 水沸騰時會變成水蒸氣。
veil || n./v. || 面紗；掩蓋 || The bride wore a white veil. || 新娘戴著白色頭紗。 || wedding veil=婚紗頭紗 || The mountain was hidden ___ a veil of fog. || behind|among|between || behind = 在…後面。 || 山隱藏在一層薄霧後面。
velvet || n. || 天鵝絨 || She wore a velvet dress. || 她穿著天鵝絨洋裝。 || velvet=絲絨 || The dress is made ___ soft velvet. || of|by|with || be made of = 由…製成（看得出原料）。 || 這件洋裝是柔軟的天鵝絨做的。
versatile || adj. || 多才多藝的；多用途的 || She is a versatile actress. || 她是一位多才多藝的女演員。 || versatile=多功能的 || The tool is versatile; you can use it ___ many things. || for|to|with || use sth for = 把某物用於…。 || 這個工具用途很多，可以做很多事。
veterinarian || n. || 獸醫 || We took our dog to the veterinarian. || 我們帶狗去看獸醫。 || veterinarian=獸醫 || She wants to be a veterinarian ___ she loves animals. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 她想當獸醫，因為她愛動物。
veto || n./v. || 否決 || The president vetoed the bill. || 總統否決了法案。 || veto power=否決權 || The president has the power ___ veto new laws. || to|for|of || the power to V = 做…的權力。 || 總統有否決新法的權力。
vibrate || v. || 振動 || My phone vibrated in my pocket. || 我的手機在口袋裡震動。 || vibrate=震動 || Please set your phone to vibrate ___ the meeting. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 開會期間請將手機設為震動。
vibration || n. || 振動 || I felt the vibration of the train. || 我感覺到火車的震動。 || vibration=震動 || We could feel the vibration ___ the passing trucks. || from|of|by || from + 來源 = 來自…。 || 我們能感覺到經過的卡車帶來的震動。
vice || n. || 惡習 || Smoking is his only vice. || 抽菸是他唯一的惡習。 || vice president=副總統 || The vice president, ___ is 50, gave a speech. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 五十歲的副總統發表了演說。
