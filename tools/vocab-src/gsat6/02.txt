# 學測 L6 02：applaud – bribe
applaud || v. || 鼓掌；稱讚 || The audience applauded loudly. || 觀眾熱烈鼓掌。 || applaud sb for=因…讚揚某人 || We applaud her ___ her courage. || for|of|with || applaud sb for = 因…讚揚某人。 || 我們讚揚她的勇氣。
applicable || adj. || 適用的 || This rule is applicable to everyone. || 這條規則適用於每個人。 || be applicable to=適用於… || The discount is not applicable ___ sale items. || to|for|with || be applicable to = 適用於…。 || 這個折扣不適用於特價商品。
apprentice || n. || 學徒 || He worked as an apprentice to a baker. || 他在麵包師傅那裡當學徒。 || apprentice to=…的學徒 || He worked as an apprentice ___ a famous chef. || to|at|for || an apprentice to = …的學徒。 || 他在一位知名主廚那裡當學徒。
approximate || adj. || 大約的 || The approximate cost is 500 dollars. || 大約的費用是五百元。 || approximate number=大約的數字 || The approximate number of visitors ___ 2,000. || was|were|be || 主詞 The number 單數，用 was。 || 遊客的大約人數是兩千人。
archaeology || n. || 考古學 || She is interested in archaeology. || 她對考古學有興趣。 || study archaeology=研究考古學 || She has been interested in archaeology ___ she was a child. || since|for|from || since + 子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 她從小就對考古學有興趣。
archive || n. || 檔案館；檔案 || The old letters are kept in the archive. || 舊信件保存在檔案館。 || national archive=國家檔案館 || The old photos are kept ___ the city archive. || in|on|to || 在檔案館裡用 in。 || 這些老照片保存在市立檔案館。
arithmetic || n. || 算術 || My little brother is good at arithmetic. || 我弟弟擅長算術。 || basic arithmetic=基本算術 || My little brother is good ___ arithmetic. || at|in|on || be good at = 擅長…。 || 我弟弟擅長算術。
ascend || v. || 上升；攀登 || The plane began to ascend. || 飛機開始爬升。 || ascend the stairs=爬樓梯 || The climbers began ___ ascend the mountain at dawn. || to|for|of || begin to + 原形動詞。 || 登山者在黎明時開始攀登那座山。
aspire || v. || 渴望 || She aspires to be a doctor. || 她立志成為醫生。 || aspire to V=立志… || Many young people aspire ___ become famous. || to|for|of || aspire to + 原形動詞 = 渴望…。 || 許多年輕人渴望成名。
assassinate || v. || 暗殺 || The president was assassinated in 1963. || 那位總統在 1963 年遭暗殺。 || assassinate a leader=暗殺領袖 || The leader ___ assassinated while giving a speech. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那位領袖在演講時遭到暗殺。
asthma || n. || 氣喘 || She has asthma. || 她有氣喘。 || asthma attack=氣喘發作 || Air pollution can make asthma ___. || worse|bad|worst || make + 受詞 + 形容詞（比較級）。 || 空氣汙染會讓氣喘更嚴重。
astray || adv. || 迷路；誤入歧途 || The letter went astray. || 那封信寄丟了。 || go astray=迷路；遺失 || Bad friends can lead young people astray, ___ choose your friends carefully. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 損友會把年輕人帶壞，所以要謹慎交友。
astronaut || n. || 太空人 || She wants to be an astronaut. || 她想當太空人。 || astronaut=太空人 || The astronauts lived ___ the space station for six months. || on|of|at || on the space station = 在太空站上。 || 太空人在太空站上生活了六個月。
astronomer || n. || 天文學家 || The astronomer discovered a new star. || 那位天文學家發現了一顆新星。 || amateur astronomer=業餘天文學家 || The astronomer ___ discovered the comet was only 20. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 發現那顆彗星的天文學家才二十歲。
astronomy || n. || 天文學 || I'm interested in astronomy. || 我對天文學有興趣。 || study astronomy=研究天文學 || Astronomy is the study ___ stars and planets. || of|for|in || the study of = 對…的研究。 || 天文學是研究星星和行星的學問。
attendant || n. || 服務員 || The flight attendant served drinks. || 空服員送上飲料。 || flight attendant=空服員 || The flight attendant asked us ___ fasten our seat belts. || to|for|of || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 空服員請我們繫好安全帶。
audit || n./v. || 審計；旁聽 || The company had an audit. || 公司接受了審計。 || tax audit=稅務稽查 || The accounts ___ audited every year. || are|is|be || 被動語態；accounts 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 帳目每年都會被審計。
auditorium || n. || 禮堂 || The concert is in the auditorium. || 音樂會在禮堂舉行。 || school auditorium=學校禮堂 || All students gathered ___ the auditorium for the ceremony. || in|on|to || 在室內用 in。 || 全體學生在禮堂集合參加典禮。
avert || v. || 避免；轉移（目光） || We must avert a crisis. || 我們必須避免危機。 || avert one's eyes=轉移視線 || Quick action helped avert a disaster, ___ nobody was hurt. || and|but|or || 補充結果用 and。 || 迅速的行動避免了一場災難，而且沒有人受傷。
aviation || n. || 航空 || He works in the aviation industry. || 他在航空業工作。 || aviation industry=航空業 || He has worked ___ the aviation industry for years. || in|on|at || in an industry = 在…產業。 || 他在航空業工作多年了。
awesome || adj. || 很棒的；令人驚嘆的 || The concert was awesome! || 那場演唱會太棒了！ || awesome view=很棒的景色 || The view from the top was ___ awesome! || absolutely|absolute|absolution || 修飾形容詞用副詞。 || 從山頂看出去的景色真是太棒了！
awhile || adv. || 片刻 || Let's rest awhile. || 我們休息一下吧。 || rest awhile=休息片刻 || Why don't you sit down and rest awhile ___ I make tea? || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 我泡茶時，你何不坐下來休息一下？
bachelor || n. || 單身漢；學士 || She has a bachelor's degree. || 她有學士學位。 || bachelor's degree=學士學位 || She got her bachelor's degree ___ 2020. || in|on|at || 年份前用 in。 || 她在 2020 年取得學士學位。
backbone || n. || 脊椎；支柱 || Farmers are the backbone of the country. || 農民是國家的支柱。 || backbone of=…的中堅 || Small businesses are the backbone ___ the economy. || of|for|to || the backbone of = …的支柱。 || 小企業是經濟的支柱。
badge || n. || 徽章 || The police officer showed his badge. || 警察出示了他的警徽。 || name badge=名牌 || Please wear your name badge ___ all times. || at|in|on || at all times = 隨時。 || 請隨時配戴你的名牌。
banquet || n. || 宴會 || We attended a wedding banquet. || 我們參加了一場婚宴。 || wedding banquet=婚宴 || We were invited ___ a wedding banquet last Sunday. || to|for|at || be invited to = 受邀參加…。 || 我們上週日受邀參加一場婚宴。
barbarian || n. || 野蠻人 || The Romans called them barbarians. || 羅馬人稱他們為野蠻人。 || barbarian=野蠻人 || The city was attacked ___ barbarians from the north. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這座城市遭到北方野蠻人的攻擊。
bass || n. || 低音；貝斯 || He plays bass in a band. || 他在樂團裡彈貝斯。 || bass guitar=電貝斯 || He plays the bass ___ a rock band. || in|on|at || in a band = 在樂團裡。 || 他在一個搖滾樂團彈貝斯。
batter || n./v. || 麵糊；打擊手 || Pour the batter into the pan. || 把麵糊倒進鍋裡。 || pancake batter=鬆餅麵糊 || Pour the batter ___ the pan and cook for two minutes. || into|onto|from || pour into = 倒進…。 || 把麵糊倒進平底鍋煎兩分鐘。
beautify || v. || 美化 || They planted flowers to beautify the street. || 他們種花美化街道。 || beautify the city=美化市容 || The city planted trees ___ beautify the streets. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 市政府種樹以美化街道。
beep || n./v. || 嗶嗶聲 || The machine beeped. || 機器發出嗶聲。 || beep=嗶嗶叫 || Please leave a message ___ the beep. || after|before|since || 嗶聲「之後」留言，用 after。 || 請在嗶聲後留言。
beforehand || adv. || 事先 || Please call beforehand. || 請事先打電話。 || tell sb beforehand=事先告知 || If you can't come, please let me know beforehand, ___ you? || will|do|are || 祈使句的附加問句常用 will you。 || 如果你不能來，請事先告訴我，好嗎？
beverage || n. || 飲料 || No food or beverages are allowed. || 禁止飲食。 || alcoholic beverage=含酒精飲料 || Food and beverages ___ not allowed in the library. || are|is|be || 複合主詞（A and B）用複數動詞。 || 圖書館內禁止飲食。
bilateral || adj. || 雙邊的 || The two countries signed a bilateral agreement. || 兩國簽署了雙邊協議。 || bilateral trade=雙邊貿易 || Bilateral trade ___ the two countries has grown. || between|among|with || 兩國之間用 between。 || 兩國之間的雙邊貿易成長了。
blaze || n./v. || 火焰；熊熊燃燒 || Firefighters put out the blaze. || 消防員撲滅了大火。 || blaze of=一片… || The fire ___ blazing when the firefighters arrived. || was|were|is || 過去進行式；主詞單數用 was。 || 消防員抵達時大火正熊熊燃燒。
bleach || n./v. || 漂白劑；漂白 || Don't mix bleach with other cleaners. || 不要把漂白水和其他清潔劑混用。 || bleach clothes=漂白衣服 || Never mix bleach ___ other cleaning products. || with|to|for || mix A with B = 把 A 和 B 混合。 || 絕對不要把漂白水和其他清潔產品混在一起。
blond || adj./n. || 金髮的 || She has blond hair. || 她有一頭金髮。 || blond hair=金髮 || The girl ___ blond hair is my cousin. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 那個金髮女孩是我表妹。
blot || n./v. || 汙點；吸乾 || There is an ink blot on the page. || 頁面上有一團墨漬。 || a blot on=…的汙點 || The scandal was a blot ___ his career. || on|in|at || a blot on = …上的汙點。 || 那件醜聞是他職業生涯的汙點。
blunt || adj. || 鈍的；直率的 || The knife is blunt. || 這把刀很鈍。 || to be blunt=坦白說 || The knife is too blunt ___ cut the bread. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不能…。 || 這把刀太鈍了，切不了麵包。
bodily || adj. || 身體的 || The accident caused bodily harm. || 這場意外造成身體傷害。 || bodily harm=身體傷害 || He was lucky to escape ___ bodily harm. || without|with|by || without + 名詞 = 沒有…。 || 他很幸運，沒有受到身體傷害就逃過一劫。
booklet || n. || 小冊子 || Please read the booklet. || 請閱讀這本小冊子。 || information booklet=說明手冊 || You can find the rules ___ the booklet. || in|on|at || 在書冊中用 in。 || 你可以在小冊子裡找到規則。
bosom || n. || 胸懷 || She held the baby to her bosom. || 她把寶寶抱在懷裡。 || bosom friend=知己 || They have been bosom friends ___ childhood. || since|for|from || since + 時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 他們從小就是知己。
boulevard || n. || 林蔭大道 || We walked along the boulevard. || 我們沿著林蔭大道散步。 || tree-lined boulevard=林蔭大道 || There are many cafés ___ the boulevard. || along|among|between || along = 沿著…。 || 林蔭大道沿路有許多咖啡廳。
boxing || n. || 拳擊 || He is interested in boxing. || 他對拳擊有興趣。 || boxing match=拳擊賽 || He started taking boxing lessons ___ get fit. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他開始上拳擊課來健身。
boycott || v./n. || 抵制 || People boycotted the company. || 人們抵制那家公司。 || call for a boycott=呼籲抵制 || Many people decided ___ boycott the brand. || to|for|of || decide to + 原形動詞。 || 許多人決定抵制那個品牌。
brace || v./n. || 使做好準備；牙套 || Brace yourself for bad news. || 做好聽壞消息的準備。 || brace oneself=做好準備 || The town braced ___ for the typhoon. || itself|it|its || 主詞與受詞相同時用反身代名詞。 || 這個小鎮為颱風做好準備。
brassiere || n. || 胸罩（常簡稱 bra） || Brassiere is the full form of "bra." || brassiere 是 bra 的完整說法。 || brassiere=胸罩 || The word "bra" is short ___ "brassiere." || for|of|to || be short for = 是…的縮寫。 || 「bra」是「brassiere」的縮寫。
breadth || n. || 寬度；廣度 || The river is 50 meters in breadth. || 這條河寬五十公尺。 || breadth of knowledge=知識廣度 || I was impressed ___ the breadth of her knowledge. || by|of|to || be impressed by = 對…印象深刻。 || 她知識的廣博讓我印象深刻。
breakdown || n. || 故障；崩潰 || Our car had a breakdown on the highway. || 我們的車在高速公路上拋錨了。 || nervous breakdown=精神崩潰 || We were late ___ of a car breakdown. || because|since|due || because of = 因為…。 || 我們因為車子拋錨而遲到。
breakup || n. || 分手 || She was sad after the breakup. || 分手後她很難過。 || breakup=分手 || She cried for days ___ the breakup. || after|before|until || after + 名詞 = 在…之後。 || 分手後她哭了好幾天。
bribe || n./v. || 賄賂 || He tried to bribe the officer. || 他試圖賄賂那名警員。 || take a bribe=收賄 || The official was arrested ___ taking bribes. || for|of|with || arrest sb for + 罪名。 || 那名官員因收賄被捕。
