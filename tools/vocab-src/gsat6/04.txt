# 學測 L6 04：clasp – cozy
clasp || v./n. || 緊握；扣環 || She clasped her hands together. || 她雙手緊握。 || clasp one's hands=雙手緊握 || The child clasped his mother's hand ___ they crossed the street. || as|which|what || as = 當…的時候。 || 過馬路時，孩子緊握著媽媽的手。
clearance || n. || 清倉；許可 || The store is having a clearance sale. || 這家店正在清倉大拍賣。 || clearance sale=清倉拍賣 || I bought these shoes ___ a clearance sale. || at|on|in || at a sale = 在拍賣中。 || 我在清倉拍賣時買了這雙鞋。
climax || n. || 高潮 || The climax of the movie was exciting. || 電影的高潮很刺激。 || reach a climax=達到高潮 || The story reaches its climax ___ the last chapter. || in|on|at || in a chapter = 在…章節中。 || 這個故事在最後一章達到高潮。
clockwise || adv./adj. || 順時針地 || Turn the key clockwise. || 把鑰匙順時針轉。 || clockwise=順時針 || Turn the lid clockwise ___ close the bottle. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 把蓋子順時針轉以關緊瓶子。
clone || n./v. || 複製 || Scientists cloned a sheep. || 科學家複製了一隻羊。 || clone=複製品 || Dolly the sheep was the first animal ___ be cloned. || to|for|that || the first + 名詞 + to V。 || 桃莉羊是第一隻被複製的動物。
closure || n. || 關閉 || The road closure caused traffic jams. || 道路封閉造成塞車。 || road closure=道路封閉 || The closure of the factory ___ hundreds of jobs. || cost|costs|costing || 主詞 The closure 單數，過去式 cost。 || 工廠關閉使數百人失去工作。
coalition || n. || 聯盟 || The parties formed a coalition. || 這些政黨組成了聯盟。 || coalition government=聯合政府 || Three parties formed a coalition ___ win the election. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 三個政黨組成聯盟以贏得選舉。
coastline || n. || 海岸線 || Taiwan has a beautiful coastline. || 台灣有美麗的海岸線。 || rocky coastline=岩岸 || We drove ___ the coastline for hours. || along|among|between || along = 沿著…。 || 我們沿著海岸線開了好幾個小時的車。
collision || n. || 碰撞 || There was a collision between two cars. || 兩輛車相撞。 || head-on collision=正面相撞 || The collision ___ the bus and the truck injured ten people. || between|among|with || between A and B = A 和 B 之間。 || 公車與卡車相撞造成十人受傷。
colloquial || adj. || 口語的 || "Gonna" is a colloquial word. || 「gonna」是口語用字。 || colloquial English=口語英語 || Colloquial words are fine ___ chatting, but not in essays. || for|of|to || be fine for = 適合…。 || 口語字詞適合聊天，但不適合寫在文章裡。
comet || n. || 彗星 || We saw a comet in the sky. || 我們在天空看到一顆彗星。 || Halley's Comet=哈雷彗星 || Halley's Comet can be seen ___ 76 years. || every|each|all || every + 數字 + 複數名詞 = 每隔…。 || 哈雷彗星每七十六年可以看到一次。
commonplace || adj./n. || 平常的 || Smartphones are commonplace now. || 智慧型手機現在很普遍。 || commonplace=司空見慣 || Online shopping has become commonplace ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || in + 國家。 || 網路購物在台灣已經很普遍。
commonwealth || n. || 聯邦；共和國 || Canada is a member of the Commonwealth. || 加拿大是大英國協的一員。 || the Commonwealth=大英國協 || Australia is one of the members ___ the Commonwealth. || of|for|in || members of = …的成員。 || 澳洲是大英國協的成員之一。
communicative || adj. || 溝通的；健談的 || She is very communicative. || 她很健談。 || communicative skills=溝通能力 || The class focuses ___ communicative skills. || on|in|at || focus on = 專注於…。 || 這門課著重溝通能力。
comparative || adj. || 比較的 || "Bigger" is the comparative form of "big." || 「bigger」是「big」的比較級。 || comparative form=比較級 || "Better" is the comparative form ___ "good." || of|for|to || the form of = …的形式。 || 「better」是「good」的比較級。
compass || n. || 指南針 || Use a compass to find north. || 用指南針找北方。 || compass=羅盤 || We found our way ___ a compass. || with|by|on || with + 工具 = 用…。 || 我們用指南針找到了路。
compile || v. || 編纂 || She compiled a list of words. || 她編了一份單字表。 || compile a list=整理清單 || The teacher compiled a list ___ useful phrases. || of|for|with || a list of = 一份…的清單。 || 老師整理了一份實用片語清單。
complement || v./n. || 補足；互補 || The wine complements the food. || 這款酒和食物很搭。 || complement each other=互補 || The two players complement ___ other well. || each|every|one || each other = 彼此。 || 這兩位球員互補得很好。
complexion || n. || 膚色；氣色 || She has a fair complexion. || 她皮膚白皙。 || fair complexion=白皙的膚色 || She has a healthy complexion ___ she sleeps well. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為睡得好，她氣色很好。
comprehensive || adj. || 全面的 || We need a comprehensive plan. || 我們需要一個全面的計畫。 || comprehensive study=全面研究 || The book gives a comprehensive overview ___ Taiwan's history. || of|for|to || an overview of = …的概述。 || 這本書全面概述了台灣歷史。
compute || v. || 計算 || The computer can compute the answer quickly. || 電腦能快速算出答案。 || compute=計算 || It took the machine a second ___ compute the result. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 機器只花了一秒就算出結果。
computerize || v. || 電腦化 || The library computerized its records. || 圖書館把紀錄電腦化了。 || computerized system=電腦化系統 || All the records ___ computerized last year. || were|was|be || 被動語態；records 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 所有紀錄在去年都電腦化了。
comrade || n. || 同志；夥伴 || He fought with his comrades. || 他和戰友並肩作戰。 || comrade in arms=戰友 || He never forgot the comrades ___ fought beside him. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 他從沒忘記和他並肩作戰的戰友。
concession || n. || 讓步 || Both sides made concessions. || 雙方都做了讓步。 || make a concession=讓步 || Both sides had to make concessions ___ reach an agreement. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 雙方都必須讓步才能達成協議。
concise || adj. || 簡潔的 || Your answer should be concise. || 你的答案應該簡潔。 || clear and concise=清楚簡潔 || Please keep your answers clear ___ concise. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的形容詞用 and。 || 請讓你的答案清楚又簡潔。
condense || v. || 濃縮 || Can you condense the report into one page? || 你能把報告濃縮成一頁嗎？ || condensed milk=煉乳 || Please condense your essay ___ 300 words. || into|onto|from || condense A into B = 把 A 濃縮成 B。 || 請把你的文章濃縮成三百字。
confederation || n. || 聯邦；同盟 || The tribes formed a confederation. || 這些部落組成了聯盟。 || confederation=邦聯 || The confederation was made up ___ six small states. || of|by|with || be made up of = 由…組成。 || 這個邦聯由六個小邦組成。
congresswoman || n. || 女國會議員 || The congresswoman gave a speech. || 那位女國會議員發表了演說。 || congresswoman=女眾議員 || The congresswoman spoke ___ women's rights. || about|to|at || speak about = 談論…。 || 那位女國會議員談論女性權益。
conquest || n. || 征服 || The Spanish conquest of Mexico began in 1519. || 西班牙征服墨西哥始於 1519 年。 || conquest=征服 || The conquest ___ the city took three years. || of|for|to || the conquest of = 對…的征服。 || 征服那座城市花了三年。
conscientious || adj. || 認真負責的 || She is a conscientious student. || 她是個認真負責的學生。 || conscientious worker=盡責的員工 || He is conscientious ___ his work. || about|to|with || be conscientious about = 對…認真負責。 || 他對工作認真負責。
conserve || v. || 保存；節約 || We should conserve water. || 我們應該節約用水。 || conserve energy=節約能源 || Turn off the lights ___ conserve energy. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 關燈以節約能源。
consolation || n. || 安慰 || Her friends were a consolation to her. || 朋友們給了她安慰。 || consolation prize=安慰獎 || He didn't win, but he got a consolation prize ___ his effort. || for|of|with || for + 名詞 = 因為…。 || 他沒贏，但因為他的努力而獲得安慰獎。
console || v./n. || 安慰；遊戲主機 || She consoled her crying friend. || 她安慰哭泣的朋友。 || game console=遊戲主機 || She tried ___ console her friend after the game. || to|for|of || try to + 原形動詞。 || 比賽後她試著安慰她的朋友。
consonant || n. || 子音 || "B" is a consonant. || 「B」是子音。 || consonant sound=子音 || Use "a" before words ___ start with a consonant sound. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾 words 並當主詞。 || 以子音開頭的字前面用「a」。
conspiracy || n. || 陰謀 || He believes in conspiracy theories. || 他相信陰謀論。 || conspiracy theory=陰謀論 || Don't believe every conspiracy theory you read ___ the Internet. || on|in|at || on the Internet = 在網路上。 || 不要相信你在網路上讀到的每個陰謀論。
contention || n. || 爭論；論點 || The issue is a point of contention. || 這個議題是爭論的焦點。 || bone of contention=爭論點 || Money is often a source ___ contention in families. || of|for|to || a source of = …的來源。 || 錢常是家庭爭執的來源。
contestant || n. || 參賽者 || Each contestant sang a song. || 每位參賽者唱了一首歌。 || contestant=選手 || Each contestant ___ three minutes to perform. || has|have|having || each + 單數名詞，動詞用單數。 || 每位參賽者有三分鐘表演。
continuity || n. || 連續性 || We need continuity in teaching. || 教學需要連貫性。 || continuity=連貫 || A good teacher keeps continuity ___ lessons. || between|among|along || between = 在…之間。 || 好老師會讓課程之間保持連貫。
contradict || v. || 反駁；與…矛盾 || His actions contradict his words. || 他的行為和言語矛盾。 || contradict oneself=自相矛盾 || He often contradicts ___ when he talks. || himself|him|his || 主詞與受詞相同時用反身代名詞。 || 他說話時常自相矛盾。
convene || v. || 召開 || The committee will convene next week. || 委員會下週將開會。 || convene a meeting=召開會議 || A meeting will ___ convened next Monday. || be|is|been || 助動詞 will 後接 be + p.p.。 || 下週一將召開一場會議。
coral || n./adj. || 珊瑚 || Coral reefs are in danger. || 珊瑚礁正面臨危險。 || coral reef=珊瑚礁 || Many fish live ___ coral reefs. || among|between|along || among = 在…之中。 || 許多魚生活在珊瑚礁之間。
corpse || n. || 屍體 || The police found a corpse. || 警方發現了一具屍體。 || corpse=屍首 || The corpse ___ found near the river last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那具屍體昨晚在河邊被發現。
correspondence || n. || 通信 || She keeps all her correspondence. || 她保存所有的信件。 || business correspondence=商業書信 || The two writers kept up a correspondence ___ many years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 這兩位作家通信多年。
cosmetic || adj. || 美容的；表面的 || She had cosmetic surgery. || 她做了整形手術。 || cosmetic surgery=整形手術 || The changes were only cosmetic; nothing important ___ changed. || was|were|is || nothing 視為單數；過去被動 was + p.p.。 || 那些改變只是表面的，重要的部分都沒變。
cosmetics || n. || 化妝品 || She sells cosmetics online. || 她在網路上賣化妝品。 || cosmetics=化妝品 || Some cosmetics ___ tested on animals. || are|is|be || 被動語態；cosmetics 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 有些化妝品會在動物身上做測試。
counterpart || n. || 對應的人或物 || The minister met his Japanese counterpart. || 部長會見了日本的對口部長。 || counterpart=對等者 || Our principal met ___ counterpart from a Japanese school. || her|she|hers || 名詞 counterpart 前用所有格形容詞。 || 我們的校長和一所日本學校的校長會面。
coupon || n. || 優惠券 || Use this coupon to get 10% off. || 用這張優惠券可打九折。 || discount coupon=折價券 || You can get 20% off ___ this coupon. || with|by|on || with + 物品 = 用…。 || 用這張優惠券可以打八折。
courtyard || n. || 庭院 || The house has a small courtyard. || 這棟房子有一個小庭院。 || inner courtyard=中庭 || The children are playing ___ the courtyard. || in|on|at || 在庭院裡用 in。 || 孩子們在庭院裡玩。
cowardly || adj. || 膽小的 || It was cowardly of him to run away. || 他逃跑真是懦弱。 || cowardly act=懦弱的行為 || It was cowardly ___ him to blame others. || of|for|to || It is + 形容詞(人的特質) + of sb + to V。 || 他把錯推給別人真是懦弱。
cozy || adj. || 舒適的 || The room is warm and cozy. || 房間溫暖又舒適。 || cozy room=舒適的房間 || The café is small ___ cozy. || but|or|so || 前後語意稍有轉折，用 but。 || 這間咖啡廳雖小但很舒適。
