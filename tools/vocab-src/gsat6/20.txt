# 學測 L6 20：victor – zoom
victor || n. || 勝利者 || The victor received a gold medal. || 勝利者獲得金牌。 || the victor=勝者 || The victor ___ the match will play in the final. || of|for|to || the victor of = …的勝者。 || 這場比賽的勝者將進入決賽。
vigor || n. || 活力 || He works with great vigor. || 他充滿活力地工作。 || with vigor=精力充沛地 || The old man still exercises ___ vigor. || with|by|in || with + 抽象名詞 = 副詞。 || 那位老人仍然精神飽滿地運動。
vigorous || adj. || 劇烈的；精力充沛的 || Vigorous exercise is good for the heart. || 劇烈運動對心臟有益。 || vigorous exercise=激烈運動 || Do vigorous exercise ___ least three times a week. || at|in|on || at least = 至少。 || 每週至少做三次激烈運動。
villa || n. || 別墅 || They rented a villa by the sea. || 他們租了一棟海邊別墅。 || holiday villa=度假別墅 || We stayed in a villa ___ a private pool. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 我們住在一棟有私人泳池的別墅。
villain || n. || 壞人；反派 || The villain was caught in the end. || 反派最後被抓了。 || play the villain=演反派 || He always plays the villain ___ movies. || in|on|at || in movies = 在電影中。 || 他在電影中總是演反派。
vine || n. || 藤蔓；葡萄藤 || Grapes grow on vines. || 葡萄長在藤上。 || grape vine=葡萄藤 || Grapes grow ___ vines. || on|in|at || grow on = 長在…上。 || 葡萄長在藤上。
vineyard || n. || 葡萄園 || We visited a vineyard in France. || 我們參觀了法國的一座葡萄園。 || vineyard=葡萄酒莊 || We picked grapes ___ the vineyard. || in|on|to || 在園子裡用 in。 || 我們在葡萄園裡摘葡萄。
violinist || n. || 小提琴家 || She is a talented violinist. || 她是一位有天分的小提琴家。 || violinist=小提琴手 || The violinist ___ performed tonight is only 12. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 今晚表演的小提琴家才十二歲。
virgin || adj./n. || 原始的；處女 || This is a virgin forest. || 這是一片原始森林。 || virgin forest=原始森林 || Few virgin forests are left ___ the world. || in|on|at || in the world = 在世界上。 || 世界上已經沒剩多少原始森林了。
vitality || n. || 活力 || She is full of vitality. || 她充滿活力。 || full of vitality=充滿活力 || The city is full ___ vitality. || of|to|in || be full of = 充滿…。 || 這座城市充滿活力。
vocation || n. || 天職；職業 || Teaching is her vocation. || 教書是她的天職。 || vocation=使命 || She found her vocation ___ teaching. || in|on|at || find one's vocation in = 在…找到天職。 || 她在教學中找到了自己的天職。
vocational || adj. || 職業的 || He went to a vocational school. || 他讀職業學校。 || vocational school=職業學校 || Vocational schools teach students skills ___ specific jobs. || for|to|of || skills for = 用於…的技能。 || 職業學校教學生特定工作所需的技能。
vowel || n. || 母音 || A, E, I, O, and U are vowels. || A、E、I、O、U 是母音。 || vowel sound=母音 || Use "an" before words that start ___ a vowel sound. || with|by|from || start with = 以…開頭。 || 以母音開頭的字前面用「an」。
wag || v. || 搖擺 || The dog wagged its tail. || 狗搖著尾巴。 || wag its tail=搖尾巴 || The dog wagged ___ tail happily. || its|it's|their || 單數名詞 dog 的所有格用 its。 || 狗開心地搖著尾巴。
walnut || n. || 核桃 || Walnuts are good for the brain. || 核桃對大腦有益。 || walnut=胡桃 || Walnuts are rich ___ healthy fats. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 核桃富含健康的脂肪。
ward || n. || 病房 || He is in the children's ward. || 他在兒童病房。 || hospital ward=病房 || My grandmother is ___ ward 5 of the hospital. || in|of|to || in a ward = 在病房裡。 || 我奶奶在醫院的五號病房。
wardrobe || n. || 衣櫥 || Hang your clothes in the wardrobe. || 把衣服掛進衣櫥。 || wardrobe=衣櫃 || Hang your coat ___ the wardrobe. || in|on|at || 在衣櫥裡用 in。 || 把你的外套掛進衣櫥。
warrant || n./v. || 搜索令；證明正當 || The police had a search warrant. || 警方有搜索令。 || search warrant=搜索令 || The police can't search the house ___ a warrant. || without|with|by || without + 名詞 = 沒有…。 || 沒有搜索令警方不能搜查房子。
warranty || n. || 保固 || The phone has a one-year warranty. || 這支手機有一年保固。 || under warranty=在保固期內 || The TV is still ___ warranty, so the repair is free. || under|below|over || under warranty = 在保固期內。 || 這台電視還在保固期內，所以維修免費。
waterproof || adj. || 防水的 || This jacket is waterproof. || 這件外套防水。 || waterproof watch=防水手錶 || This watch is waterproof, ___ you can wear it while swimming. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 這支手錶防水，所以游泳時可以戴。
weary || adj. || 疲倦的；厭倦的 || I'm weary of his excuses. || 我厭倦了他的藉口。 || be weary of=厭倦… || The workers were weary ___ the long hours. || of|to|at || be weary of = 厭倦…。 || 工人們厭倦了長時間工作。
wharf || n. || 碼頭 || The boat is at the wharf. || 船停在碼頭。 || wharf=碼頭 || We had seafood ___ a restaurant on the wharf. || at|on|to || at + 地點 = 在…。 || 我們在碼頭上的一家餐廳吃了海鮮。
whiskey || n. || 威士忌 || He drinks whiskey. || 他喝威士忌。 || Scotch whiskey=蘇格蘭威士忌 || Taiwan makes some of the best whiskey ___ the world. || in|on|at || in the world = 在世界上。 || 台灣生產全世界數一數二的威士忌。
wholesale || adj./n. || 批發的 || We buy rice at wholesale prices. || 我們用批發價買米。 || wholesale price=批發價 || Stores buy goods ___ wholesale prices and sell them at retail prices. || at|in|on || at a price = 以…價格。 || 商店以批發價進貨，再以零售價賣出。
wholesome || adj. || 有益健康的 || Eat wholesome food. || 吃有益健康的食物。 || wholesome food=健康食物 || Wholesome food is good ___ growing children. || for|to|with || be good for = 對…有益。 || 健康的食物對成長中的孩子有益。
widow || n. || 寡婦 || She became a widow at 40. || 她四十歲時成了寡婦。 || widow=遺孀 || She became a widow ___ her husband died in an accident. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 她丈夫在意外中去世後，她成了寡婦。
withhold || v. || 扣留；保留 || He decided to withhold the information. || 他決定隱瞞這項資訊。 || withhold information=隱瞞資訊 || The company may withhold part of his salary ___ he is late too often. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果他太常遲到，公司可能會扣他部分薪水。
woe || n. || 悲哀；困難 || The flood added to the town's woes. || 洪水讓這個小鎮雪上加霜。 || financial woes=財務困境 || The typhoon added ___ the farmers' woes. || to|for|with || add to = 增加…。 || 颱風讓農民的困境雪上加霜。
woodpecker || n. || 啄木鳥 || A woodpecker was pecking at the tree. || 一隻啄木鳥在啄樹。 || woodpecker=啄木鳥 || A woodpecker was pecking ___ the tree. || at|of|to || peck at = 啄…。 || 一隻啄木鳥在啄樹。
workforce || n. || 勞動力 || Women make up half of the workforce. || 女性占勞動力的一半。 || workforce=全體員工 || More women have entered the workforce ___ recent years. || in|on|at || in recent years = 近年來。 || 近年來越來越多女性進入職場。
wrestle || v. || 摔角；搏鬥 || The boys wrestled on the grass. || 男孩們在草地上扭打。 || wrestle with=努力解決… || She wrestled ___ the math problem for an hour. || with|to|for || wrestle with = 努力應付…。 || 她跟那道數學題奮戰了一個小時。
wrinkle || n./v. || 皺紋 || My grandmother has many wrinkles. || 我奶奶有很多皺紋。 || wrinkle=皺褶 || Too much sun can cause wrinkles ___ your skin. || on|of|at || on the skin = 在皮膚上。 || 曬太多太陽會讓皮膚長皺紋。
yearn || v. || 渴望 || She yearned for home. || 她渴望回家。 || yearn for=渴望… || Many people yearn ___ a simpler life. || for|to|of || yearn for = 渴望…。 || 許多人渴望更簡單的生活。
yoga || n. || 瑜伽 || I do yoga every morning. || 我每天早上做瑜伽。 || do yoga=做瑜伽 || Yoga can help you relax ___ sleep better. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的動詞用 and。 || 瑜伽能幫助你放鬆並睡得更好。
yogurt || n. || 優格 || I eat yogurt for breakfast. || 我早餐吃優格。 || plain yogurt=原味優格 || I like to eat yogurt ___ fresh fruit. || with|by|of || with = 搭配…。 || 我喜歡吃優格配新鮮水果。
zoom || v. || 急速移動；變焦 || The car zoomed past us. || 車子從我們身邊急駛而過。 || zoom in=放大 || Zoom ___ to see the details of the photo. || in|on|at || zoom in = 放大。 || 放大來看照片的細節。
