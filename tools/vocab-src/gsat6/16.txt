# 學測 L6 16：restraint – simplicity
restraint || n. || 克制 || She showed great restraint. || 她表現出極大的克制。 || with restraint=克制地 || The police acted ___ restraint during the protest. || with|by|in || with + 抽象名詞 = 副詞。 || 抗議期間警方克制地行事。
retort || v./n. || 反駁 || "That's not true!" she retorted. || 「才不是！」她反駁道。 || retort=回嘴 || "It's your fault," he retorted ___ anger. || in|on|by || in anger = 生氣地。 || 「是你的錯，」他生氣地回嘴。
retrieve || v. || 取回 || I retrieved my bag from the lost and found. || 我從失物招領處取回了包包。 || retrieve data=取回資料 || The dog was trained ___ retrieve the ball. || to|for|of || be trained to + 原形動詞。 || 那隻狗受過撿球的訓練。
revelation || n. || 揭露；啟示 || The trip was a revelation to me. || 這趟旅行讓我大開眼界。 || come as a revelation=令人大開眼界 || The news came as a revelation ___ everyone. || to|at|with || come as a revelation to = 令…大開眼界。 || 這個消息讓每個人都大吃一驚。
revival || n. || 復興 || There has been a revival of interest in vinyl records. || 黑膠唱片重新受到關注。 || revival of=…的復興 || There has been a revival ___ interest in old films. || of|for|to || a revival of = …的復興。 || 人們對老電影的興趣再度興起。
revive || v. || 使復甦 || The doctor revived the man. || 醫生救醒了那個男人。 || revive the economy=振興經濟 || The government is trying ___ revive the economy. || to|for|of || try to + 原形動詞。 || 政府正努力振興經濟。
revolt || n./v. || 反叛 || The people revolted against the king. || 人民起義反抗國王。 || revolt against=反抗… || The farmers revolted ___ the high taxes. || against|to|with || revolt against = 反抗…。 || 農民起來反抗高稅。
revolve || v. || 旋轉；圍繞 || The Earth revolves around the sun. || 地球繞著太陽轉。 || revolve around=以…為中心 || His whole life revolves ___ his family. || around|among|along || revolve around = 以…為中心。 || 他的生活全以家庭為中心。
rigorous || adj. || 嚴格的 || The training is rigorous. || 訓練很嚴格。 || rigorous training=嚴格訓練 || The players went through rigorous training ___ the season. || before|after|since || 球季「之前」訓練，用 before。 || 球員在球季前接受了嚴格的訓練。
ripple || n./v. || 漣漪 || The stone made ripples in the water. || 石頭在水面激起漣漪。 || ripple effect=連鎖效應 || The news had a ripple effect ___ the whole industry. || on|to|for || have an effect on = 對…有影響。 || 這則消息對整個產業產生連鎖效應。
rivalry || n. || 競爭 || There is a strong rivalry between the two schools. || 兩校之間競爭激烈。 || sibling rivalry=手足競爭 || There has always been a rivalry ___ the two teams. || between|among|with || 兩方之間用 between。 || 兩隊之間一直存在競爭。
roam || v. || 漫遊 || Cows roam freely on the farm. || 牛在農場上自由漫步。 || roam the streets=在街上遊蕩 || Wild animals roam freely ___ the national park. || in|on|at || in a park = 在公園裡。 || 野生動物在國家公園裡自由漫遊。
robust || adj. || 強健的；穩固的 || The economy is robust. || 經濟很穩健。 || robust health=身體強健 || My grandfather is still robust ___ he is 90. || although|because|unless || although = 雖然，表讓步。 || 我祖父雖然九十歲了，仍然很硬朗。
rotate || v. || 旋轉；輪流 || The Earth rotates once a day. || 地球每天自轉一次。 || rotate=輪換 || The Earth rotates ___ its axis once a day. || on|in|at || rotate on its axis = 繞軸自轉。 || 地球每天繞軸自轉一次。
rotation || n. || 旋轉；輪流 || The Earth's rotation causes day and night. || 地球自轉造成日夜。 || in rotation=輪流 || The students clean the classroom ___ rotation. || in|on|at || in rotation = 輪流。 || 學生們輪流打掃教室。
royalty || n. || 皇室；版稅 || The writer earns royalties from her books. || 那位作家從書中賺取版稅。 || royalty=王室成員 || The singer receives royalties ___ every song sold. || on|in|at || royalties on = 對…抽取的版稅。 || 那位歌手每賣出一首歌都能收到版稅。
rubbish || n. || 垃圾；胡說 || Put the rubbish in the bin. || 把垃圾丟進垃圾桶。 || talk rubbish=胡說八道 || Please don't throw rubbish ___ the street. || on|of|at || on the street = 在街上。 || 請不要在街上亂丟垃圾。
rugged || adj. || 崎嶇的 || The coast is rugged. || 海岸崎嶇。 || rugged mountains=崎嶇的山 || We drove along the rugged coast ___ eastern Taiwan. || of|for|to || the coast of = …的海岸。 || 我們沿著台灣東部崎嶇的海岸開車。
ruthless || adj. || 無情的 || He is a ruthless businessman. || 他是個無情的生意人。 || ruthless=殘酷 || The ruthless king punished anyone ___ disagreed with him. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位無情的國王懲罰任何反對他的人。
salute || v./n. || 敬禮 || The soldier saluted the officer. || 士兵向軍官敬禮。 || salute=致敬 || The soldiers saluted ___ the general walked past. || as|which|what || as = 當…的時候。 || 將軍走過時，士兵們向他敬禮。
salvage || v./n. || 搶救 || They salvaged what they could from the fire. || 他們從火場中搶救能救的東西。 || salvage=打撈 || Workers tried to salvage the ship ___ it sank. || after|unless|so || 沉沒「之後」打撈，用 after。 || 船沉沒後，工人試圖打撈。
sanitation || n. || 公共衛生 || Good sanitation prevents disease. || 良好的公共衛生能預防疾病。 || sanitation worker=清潔人員 || Poor sanitation can lead ___ disease. || to|for|in || lead to = 導致…。 || 衛生條件差可能導致疾病。
savage || adj. || 野蠻的；兇猛的 || It was a savage attack. || 那是一次兇殘的攻擊。 || savage attack=兇猛攻擊 || The dog made a savage attack ___ the mail carrier. || on|to|for || an attack on = 對…的攻擊。 || 那隻狗兇猛地攻擊了郵差。
scenic || adj. || 風景優美的 || We took the scenic route. || 我們走了風景優美的路線。 || scenic spot=風景區 || Sun Moon Lake is one of the most scenic places ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || in + 國家。 || 日月潭是台灣風景最優美的地方之一。
scorn || n./v. || 輕蔑 || She looked at him with scorn. || 她輕蔑地看著他。 || pour scorn on=對…嗤之以鼻 || He looked at the cheap gift ___ scorn. || with|by|in || with + 抽象名詞 = 副詞。 || 他輕蔑地看著那份便宜的禮物。
scrape || v./n. || 刮；擦傷 || He scraped his knee. || 他擦傷了膝蓋。 || scrape=刮掉 || He scraped the mud ___ his shoes. || off|of|out || scrape A off B = 從 B 刮掉 A。 || 他刮掉鞋上的泥巴。
screwdriver || n. || 螺絲起子 || I need a screwdriver. || 我需要一把螺絲起子。 || screwdriver=起子 || Can you hand me the screwdriver ___ the toolbox? || in|on|at || 在工具箱裡用 in。 || 你可以把工具箱裡的螺絲起子遞給我嗎？
scroll || v./n. || 捲動；卷軸 || Scroll down to see more. || 往下捲動看更多。 || scroll down=往下滑 || Scroll ___ to the bottom of the page. || down|off|out || scroll down = 往下捲動。 || 往下捲到頁面底部。
scrutiny || n. || 仔細檢查 || The plan is under scrutiny. || 這個計畫正在接受審查。 || under scrutiny=受到審查 || The company's actions are ___ close scrutiny. || under|below|over || under scrutiny = 受到審查。 || 這家公司的行為正受到嚴密審查。
sculptor || n. || 雕刻家 || The sculptor made a statue. || 雕刻家做了一座雕像。 || famous sculptor=知名雕刻家 || The statue was made ___ a famous sculptor. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這座雕像是一位知名雕刻家做的。
seagull || n. || 海鷗 || Seagulls flew over the beach. || 海鷗飛越海灘。 || seagull=海鷗 || A seagull stole the sandwich ___ my hand! || from|of|at || steal sth from = 從…偷走某物。 || 一隻海鷗從我手上搶走了三明治！
seduce || v. || 引誘 || The low prices seduced many shoppers. || 低價吸引了許多購物者。 || be seduced by=被…誘惑 || Don't be seduced ___ ads that promise quick money. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 不要被承諾快速賺錢的廣告誘惑。
selective || adj. || 選擇性的；挑剔的 || She is selective about her friends. || 她交朋友很挑剔。 || be selective about=對…挑剔 || He is very selective ___ the food he eats. || about|to|with || be selective about = 對…挑剔。 || 他對吃的東西很挑剔。
