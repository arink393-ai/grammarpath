# 學測 L6 13：operative – permissible
operative || adj./n. || 運作中的；特務 || The new rule is now operative. || 新規定現已生效。 || operative=有效的 || The new law will become operative ___ January 1. || on|in|at || 特定日期用 on。 || 新法將於一月一日生效。
oppress || v. || 壓迫 || The king oppressed his people. || 國王壓迫他的人民。 || oppress=欺壓 || The people were oppressed ___ the cruel king for years. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 人民多年來受到殘暴國王的壓迫。
oppression || n. || 壓迫 || They fought against oppression. || 他們對抗壓迫。 || political oppression=政治壓迫 || The people fought ___ oppression for freedom. || against|to|with || fight against = 對抗…。 || 人民為了自由對抗壓迫。
ordeal || n. || 苦難；嚴峻考驗 || The flight was an ordeal. || 那趟飛行簡直是折磨。 || go through an ordeal=經歷苦難 || The hikers went ___ a terrible ordeal in the snow. || through|across|along || go through = 經歷…。 || 健行者在雪地裡經歷了一場可怕的磨難。
orderly || adj. || 整齊的；有秩序的 || The students left in an orderly way. || 學生們有秩序地離開。 || in an orderly manner=有秩序地 || Please leave the building ___ an orderly manner. || in|on|at || in a manner = 以…方式。 || 請有秩序地離開大樓。
organizer || n. || 組織者 || The organizers thanked the volunteers. || 主辦單位感謝志工們。 || event organizer=活動主辦人 || The organizer ___ the event thanked everyone for coming. || of|for|to || the organizer of = …的主辦人。 || 活動主辦人感謝大家的到來。
orient || v. || 使適應；定方位 || The program helps new students orient themselves. || 這個計畫幫助新生適應環境。 || orient oneself=熟悉環境 || It took me a few days ___ orient myself in the new city. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 我花了幾天才熟悉這座新城市。
oriental || adj. || 東方的 || The museum has oriental art. || 這間博物館有東方藝術。 || oriental art=東方藝術 || The museum is famous ___ its oriental art. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 這間博物館以東方藝術聞名。
originate || v. || 起源 || The game originated in China. || 這種遊戲起源於中國。 || originate from=起源於… || Many festivals originated ___ ancient legends. || from|of|by || originate from = 起源於…。 || 許多節日起源於古老的傳說。
ornament || n. || 裝飾品 || We hung ornaments on the Christmas tree. || 我們在聖誕樹上掛裝飾品。 || Christmas ornament=聖誕裝飾 || We decorated the tree ___ colorful ornaments. || with|by|of || decorate A with B = 用 B 裝飾 A。 || 我們用五顏六色的裝飾品裝飾聖誕樹。
orphanage || n. || 孤兒院 || She grew up in an orphanage. || 她在孤兒院長大。 || orphanage=育幼院 || The students donated toys ___ a local orphanage. || to|for|with || donate sth to = 把某物捐給…。 || 學生們把玩具捐給當地的育幼院。
orthodox || adj. || 正統的 || He uses orthodox methods. || 他使用傳統的方法。 || orthodox method=正統方法 || The doctor used both orthodox ___ new methods. || and|or|but || both A and B = A 和 B 都。 || 醫生同時使用傳統和新的方法。
ounce || n. || 盎司 || The letter weighs two ounces. || 這封信重兩盎司。 || an ounce of=一點點… || There isn't an ounce ___ truth in his story. || of|for|in || an ounce of = 一點點…。 || 他的故事沒有一點真實性。
outbreak || n. || 爆發 || There was an outbreak of flu. || 爆發了流感疫情。 || outbreak of=…的爆發 || The school closed after an outbreak ___ flu. || of|for|in || an outbreak of = …的爆發。 || 流感爆發後學校停課了。
outgoing || adj. || 外向的 || She is very outgoing. || 她非常外向。 || outgoing personality=外向的個性 || She is outgoing, ___ her brother is shy. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 她很外向，但她弟弟很害羞。
outing || n. || 出遊 || We went on a family outing. || 我們全家出遊。 || school outing=校外遠足 || We went ___ a school outing to the zoo. || on|in|at || go on an outing = 去郊遊。 || 我們去動物園校外遠足。
outlaw || v./n. || 宣布…為非法；不法之徒 || The government outlawed smoking in restaurants. || 政府禁止在餐廳吸菸。 || outlaw=取締 || Smoking ___ outlawed in public places. || has been|have been|being || 現在完成被動式；主詞 Smoking 單數用 has been。 || 公共場所禁止吸菸。
outlook || n. || 觀點；前景 || She has a positive outlook on life. || 她對人生抱持樂觀的看法。 || outlook on life=人生觀 || She has a positive outlook ___ life. || on|in|at || outlook on = 對…的看法。 || 她對人生抱持積極的看法。
outnumber || v. || 數量上超過 || Girls outnumber boys in our class. || 我們班女生比男生多。 || outnumber=多於 || In our class, girls outnumber boys ___ two to one. || by|with|in || by + 比例／差距。 || 我們班女生和男生的比例是二比一。
outrage || n./v. || 憤怒；暴行 || The decision caused public outrage. || 這個決定引起公憤。 || public outrage=公憤 || There was public outrage ___ the new tax. || over|of|with || outrage over = 對…的憤怒。 || 新稅引起公憤。
outrageous || adj. || 離譜的；駭人的 || The prices are outrageous. || 價格太離譜了。 || outrageous price=離譜的價格 || The price of this coffee is ___ outrageous! || absolutely|absolute|absoluteness || 修飾形容詞用副詞。 || 這杯咖啡的價格真是太離譜了！
outright || adj./adv. || 徹底的；直率地 || She won outright. || 她完勝。 || outright lie=徹頭徹尾的謊言 || That was an outright lie, ___ it? || wasn't|was|didn't || 前句 was 肯定，附加問句用 wasn't it。 || 那是徹頭徹尾的謊言，不是嗎？
outset || n. || 開始 || I knew from the outset that it would be hard. || 我一開始就知道會很難。 || from the outset=從一開始 || I told you ___ the outset that it would be difficult. || at|in|on || at the outset = 一開始。 || 我一開始就告訴過你會很困難。
outskirts || n. || 郊區 || They live on the outskirts of the city. || 他們住在城市的郊區。 || on the outskirts of=在…的郊區 || The airport is ___ the outskirts of the city. || on|in|at || on the outskirts of = 在…的郊區。 || 機場位於城市的郊區。
outward || adj./adv. || 外表的；向外的 || His outward calm hid his fear. || 他外表的平靜掩藏了恐懼。 || outward appearance=外表 || Don't judge people by their outward ___. || appearance|appear|appeared || 所有格 + 形容詞 + 名詞。 || 不要以外表評斷別人。
overdo || v. || 做得過頭 || Don't overdo it at the gym. || 健身時別做過頭。 || overdo it=過度 || Exercise is good, but don't overdo it, ___ you'll get hurt. || or|and|so || 命令句 + or = 否則…。 || 運動很好，但別過度，否則你會受傷。
overflow || v./n. || 溢出 || The river overflowed after the rain. || 雨後河水氾濫。 || overflow with=充滿… || The river overflowed its banks ___ the heavy rain. || after|before|until || 大雨「之後」氾濫，用 after。 || 大雨過後河水溢出河岸。
overhear || v. || 無意中聽到 || I overheard their conversation. || 我無意中聽到他們的對話。 || overhear=偷聽到 || I overheard them ___ about the party. || talking|to talk|talked || overhear + 受詞 + V-ing。 || 我無意中聽到他們在談論派對。
overlap || v./n. || 重疊 || The two meetings overlap. || 這兩個會議時間重疊。 || overlap with=與…重疊 || My piano lesson overlaps ___ the soccer practice. || with|to|for || overlap with = 與…重疊。 || 我的鋼琴課和足球練習時間重疊。
overwork || v./n. || 過度工作 || He got sick from overwork. || 他因過勞而生病。 || overwork=過勞 || He got sick ___ overwork. || from|of|with || from + 原因 = 由於…。 || 他因過勞而生病。
oyster || n. || 牡蠣 || I love oyster omelets. || 我愛吃蚵仔煎。 || oyster omelet=蚵仔煎 || Oyster omelets are one of the most popular foods ___ night markets. || at|in|on || at night markets = 在夜市。 || 蚵仔煎是夜市最受歡迎的食物之一。
ozone || n. || 臭氧 || The ozone layer protects the Earth. || 臭氧層保護地球。 || ozone layer=臭氧層 || The ozone layer protects us ___ harmful UV rays. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 臭氧層保護我們免受有害紫外線傷害。
packet || n. || 小包 || I bought a packet of seeds. || 我買了一小包種子。 || a packet of=一小包… || She opened a packet ___ chips. || of|for|with || a packet of = 一小包…。 || 她打開了一包洋芋片。
paddle || n./v. || 槳；划槳 || He paddled the canoe across the lake. || 他划獨木舟橫越湖面。 || paddle a canoe=划獨木舟 || We paddled the canoe ___ the river. || down|over|off || down the river = 順流而下。 || 我們划著獨木舟順河而下。
paperback || n. || 平裝書 || I bought the paperback version. || 我買了平裝版。 || in paperback=平裝版 || The novel is now available ___ paperback. || in|on|at || in paperback = 以平裝版發行。 || 這本小說現在有平裝版了。
paradox || n. || 矛盾；悖論 || It's a paradox that more choice makes us less happy. || 選擇越多反而越不快樂，真是矛盾。 || paradox=矛盾 || It is a paradox ___ the more you learn, the less you know. || that|what|which || It is a paradox that… = …是個矛盾。 || 學得越多反而越覺得自己懂得少，這是個矛盾。
paralyze || v. || 使癱瘓 || The accident paralyzed him. || 那場意外使他癱瘓。 || be paralyzed=癱瘓 || The city was paralyzed ___ the heavy snow. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這座城市因大雪而癱瘓。
parliament || n. || 國會 || Parliament passed the new law. || 國會通過了新法。 || member of parliament=國會議員 || The new law was passed ___ Parliament last week. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 新法在上週由國會通過。
pastime || n. || 消遣 || Reading is my favorite pastime. || 閱讀是我最喜歡的消遣。 || favorite pastime=最喜歡的消遣 || Baseball is a popular pastime ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || in + 國家。 || 棒球在台灣是受歡迎的休閒活動。
patriot || n. || 愛國者 || He was a true patriot. || 他是一位真正的愛國者。 || true patriot=真正的愛國者 || He was remembered ___ a great patriot. || as|like|for || be remembered as = 被記得是…。 || 他被世人記為一位偉大的愛國者。
patriotic || adj. || 愛國的 || They sang patriotic songs. || 他們唱愛國歌曲。 || patriotic song=愛國歌曲 || The crowd sang patriotic songs ___ waved flags. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的動作用 and。 || 群眾唱著愛國歌曲並揮舞旗幟。
peacock || n. || 孔雀 || The peacock spread its tail. || 孔雀開屏了。 || as proud as a peacock=非常驕傲 || The peacock spread ___ beautiful tail. || its|it's|their || 單數名詞 peacock 的所有格用 its。 || 孔雀展開牠美麗的尾巴。
pebble || n. || 鵝卵石 || The beach is covered with pebbles. || 海灘上布滿鵝卵石。 || pebble beach=礫石灘 || The children threw pebbles ___ the lake. || into|onto|from || throw into = 丟進…。 || 孩子們把小石頭丟進湖裡。
peek || v./n. || 偷看 || Don't peek at the answers! || 不要偷看答案！ || take a peek=偷看一下 || No peeking ___ your presents before Christmas! || at|to|on || peek at = 偷看…。 || 聖誕節前不准偷看你的禮物！
pending || adj./prep. || 懸而未決的；在…之前 || The case is still pending. || 這個案子仍懸而未決。 || pending=待決的 || The decision is pending ___ the test results come out. || until|unless|since || until = 直到…。 || 這個決定要等測試結果出來才會定案。
peninsula || n. || 半島 || Korea is on a peninsula. || 韓國位於一個半島上。 || peninsula=半島 || Korea is located ___ a peninsula. || on|in|at || on a peninsula = 在半島上。 || 韓國位於一個半島上。
perch || v./n. || 棲息 || A bird perched on the branch. || 一隻鳥棲息在樹枝上。 || perch on=棲息在… || A small bird perched ___ the window. || on|of|to || perch on = 棲息在…上。 || 一隻小鳥停在窗台上。
peril || n. || 危險 || The ship was in peril. || 那艘船處於危險中。 || in peril=處於危險中 || The climbers were ___ great peril during the storm. || in|on|at || in peril = 處於危險中。 || 暴風雨中登山者處於極大的危險中。
perish || v. || 死亡；毀滅 || Many people perished in the fire. || 許多人在火災中喪生。 || perish=喪生 || Hundreds of people perished ___ the earthquake. || in|on|at || in + 災難 = 在…中。 || 數百人在地震中喪生。
permissible || adj. || 允許的 || Is it permissible to take photos here? || 這裡可以拍照嗎？ || permissible=可容許的 || Is it permissible ___ use a dictionary during the test? || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 考試時可以使用字典嗎？
