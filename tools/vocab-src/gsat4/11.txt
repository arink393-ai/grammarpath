# 學測 L4 11：immigration – isolation
immigration || n. || 移民；入境 || We went through immigration at the airport. || 我們在機場通過入境檢查。 || immigration office=移民署 || You need to show your passport ___ immigration. || at|on|in || at immigration = 在入境審查處。 || 你需要在入境審查處出示護照。
impact || n./v. || 影響；衝擊 || Technology has a big impact on our lives. || 科技對我們的生活影響很大。 || have an impact on=對…有影響 || Social media has a huge impact ___ young people. || on|to|for || have an impact on = 對…有影響。 || 社群媒體對年輕人有巨大的影響。
imply || v. || 暗示 || What are you implying? || 你在暗示什麼？ || imply that=暗示… || His silence implied ___ he didn't agree. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 implied 的受詞。 || 他的沉默暗示他不同意。
impose || v. || 強加；徵收 || The government imposed a new tax. || 政府開徵了一項新稅。 || impose on=強加於… || Parents shouldn't impose their own dreams ___ their children. || on|to|with || impose A on B = 把 A 強加於 B。 || 父母不應該把自己的夢想強加在孩子身上。
impression || n. || 印象 || She made a good impression on everyone. || 她給大家留下了好印象。 || make an impression on=給…留下印象 || First impressions are important, ___ they? || aren't|don't|are || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：are → aren't。 || 第一印象很重要，不是嗎？
incident || n. || 事件 || The police are investigating the incident. || 警方正在調查這起事件。 || traffic incident=交通事故 || The incident happened ___ midnight. || at|in|on || at midnight = 在午夜。 || 這起事件發生在午夜。
including || prep. || 包括 || Ten people came, including me. || 來了十個人，包括我在內。 || including tax=含稅 || The price is 500 dollars including tax, ___ is quite reasonable. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句 which 指代前面的敘述。 || 價格含稅五百元，相當合理。
incredible || adj. || 難以置信的 || The view was incredible. || 景色美得令人難以置信。 || incredible story=難以置信的故事 || It's incredible ___ he finished the marathon at age 80. || that|what|which || It is incredible that… = …令人難以置信。 || 他八十歲還跑完馬拉松，真令人難以置信。
indication || n. || 跡象 || There is no indication of rain. || 沒有下雨的跡象。 || an indication of=…的跡象 || A high fever is often an indication ___ infection. || of|for|to || an indication of = …的跡象。 || 高燒常常是感染的跡象。
infant || n. || 嬰兒 || The infant is sleeping. || 嬰兒正在睡覺。 || newborn infant=新生兒 || Infants need ___ sleep a lot. || to|for|of || need to + 原形動詞。 || 嬰兒需要大量的睡眠。
infection || n. || 感染 || Wash the cut to prevent infection. || 清洗傷口以防感染。 || ear infection=耳朵感染 || Wash your hands often ___ prevent infection. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 常洗手以預防感染。
inflation || n. || 通貨膨脹 || Inflation is making everything more expensive. || 通膨讓所有東西都變貴了。 || high inflation=高通膨 || Because of inflation, prices have gone up ___ the past year. || over|since|by || over the past + 時間 = 在過去…期間。 || 由於通膨，過去一年物價上漲了。
influential || adj. || 有影響力的 || She is an influential writer. || 她是一位有影響力的作家。 || highly influential=非常有影響力 || He is one of the ___ influential leaders in the world. || most|more|much || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。 || 他是世界上最有影響力的領袖之一。
informative || adj. || 資訊豐富的 || The lecture was very informative. || 這場演講資訊非常豐富。 || informative article=內容豐富的文章 || The documentary was ___ informative that I learned a lot. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這部紀錄片資訊豐富到我學了很多。
ingredient || n. || 材料；成分 || What are the ingredients of this cake? || 這個蛋糕的材料是什麼？ || main ingredient=主要材料 || Fresh ingredients are the key ___ good cooking. || to|of|for || the key to = …的關鍵。 || 新鮮的食材是美味料理的關鍵。
initial || adj./n. || 最初的；首字母 || My initial reaction was surprise. || 我最初的反應是驚訝。 || initial stage=初期 || The project is still ___ its initial stage. || in|on|at || in the … stage = 在…階段。 || 這個計畫仍在初期階段。
injure || v. || 使受傷 || He was injured in the accident. || 他在事故中受傷。 || be injured in=在…中受傷 || Three people ___ injured in the car accident. || were|was|are || 被動語態；Three people 複數，過去用 were。 || 車禍中有三人受傷。
innocence || n. || 天真；清白 || He proved his innocence. || 他證明了自己的清白。 || prove one's innocence=證明清白 || The new evidence proved the man's innocence, ___ he was set free. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 新證據證明了那名男子的清白，所以他被釋放了。
input || n. || 輸入；意見 || Thank you for your input. || 謝謝你的意見。 || give input=提供意見 || We welcome input ___ students and parents. || from|of|by || input from = 來自…的意見。 || 我們歡迎學生和家長提供意見。
insert || v. || 插入 || Insert the card into the machine. || 把卡片插進機器裡。 || insert a coin=投幣 || Insert the coin ___ the slot. || into|in|onto || insert A into B = 把 A 插入 B。 || 把硬幣投入投幣孔。
inspection || n. || 檢查 || The car needs an inspection. || 這輛車需要檢驗。 || safety inspection=安全檢查 || All bags are subject ___ inspection at the airport. || to|for|with || be subject to = 需接受…。 || 機場的所有行李都需接受檢查。
inspiration || n. || 靈感；鼓舞 || Nature is her inspiration. || 大自然是她的靈感來源。 || source of inspiration=靈感來源 || The artist found inspiration ___ nature. || in|on|at || find inspiration in = 從…中找到靈感。 || 那位藝術家從大自然中找到靈感。
inspire || v. || 啟發；激勵 || Her story inspired me. || 她的故事啟發了我。 || inspire sb to V=激勵某人做… || Her speech inspired many students ___ harder. || to study|studying|study || inspire sb to + 原形動詞。 || 她的演講激勵了許多學生更加用功。
install || v. || 安裝 || He installed a new program. || 他安裝了新的程式。 || install software=安裝軟體 || The new air conditioner ___ installed yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 新的冷氣機昨天安裝好了。
instinct || n. || 本能 || Animals act on instinct. || 動物憑本能行動。 || by instinct=出於本能 || Birds know how to build nests ___ instinct. || by|with|from || by instinct = 出於本能。 || 鳥天生就知道如何築巢。
instruct || v. || 指示；教導 || The teacher instructed us to sit down. || 老師指示我們坐下。 || instruct sb to V=指示某人做… || The students were instructed ___ their phones off. || to turn|turning|turn || be instructed to + 原形動詞。 || 學生們被指示要把手機關機。
instructor || n. || 教練；講師 || She is a swimming instructor. || 她是游泳教練。 || driving instructor=駕訓教練 || The instructor ___ taught me to drive was very patient. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 教我開車的教練非常有耐心。
insult || v./n. || 侮辱 || Don't insult people. || 不要侮辱別人。 || insult sb=侮辱某人 || He felt insulted ___ her rude comment. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 他覺得被她無禮的評論侮辱了。
insurance || n. || 保險 || Do you have health insurance? || 你有健康保險嗎？ || health insurance=健康保險 || Everyone in Taiwan is covered ___ national health insurance. || by|with|from || be covered by = 由…承保。 || 台灣每個人都有全民健保。
intellectual || adj./n. || 智力的；知識分子 || Reading helps intellectual growth. || 閱讀有助於智力發展。 || intellectual property=智慧財產 || Reading is important ___ children's intellectual development. || for|to|of || be important for = 對…很重要。 || 閱讀對孩子的智力發展很重要。
intelligence || n. || 智力 || Dolphins have high intelligence. || 海豚的智力很高。 || artificial intelligence=人工智慧 || Artificial intelligence is changing the way we ___. || work|works|working || the way (that) we + 原形動詞。 || 人工智慧正在改變我們的工作方式。
intend || v. || 打算 || I intend to study abroad. || 我打算出國讀書。 || intend to V=打算做… || She intends ___ a doctor in the future. || to become|becoming|become || intend to + 原形動詞。 || 她打算將來成為醫生。
intense || adj. || 強烈的 || The heat was intense. || 熱氣非常強烈。 || intense training=高強度訓練 || The competition was ___ intense that only three teams made it to the final. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 競爭激烈到只有三支隊伍進入決賽。
intensity || n. || 強度 || The intensity of the storm surprised everyone. || 暴風雨的強度讓大家都很驚訝。 || high intensity=高強度 || The intensity ___ the earthquake was 6.5. || of|for|in || the intensity of = …的強度。 || 這場地震的規模是六點五。
intensive || adj. || 密集的 || She took an intensive English course. || 她上了英文密集課程。 || intensive care=加護病房 || The patient is still ___ intensive care. || in|on|at || in intensive care = 在加護病房。 || 那位病人還在加護病房。
intention || n. || 意圖 || I have no intention of leaving. || 我無意離開。 || have no intention of=無意… || I have no intention ___ giving up. || of|to|for || have no intention of + V-ing = 無意做…。 || 我無意放棄。
interact || v. || 互動 || Children learn by interacting with others. || 孩子透過與他人互動來學習。 || interact with=與…互動 || Teachers should interact ___ their students more. || with|to|for || interact with = 與…互動。 || 老師應該多和學生互動。
interaction || n. || 互動 || Social interaction is important for children. || 社交互動對孩子很重要。 || social interaction=社交互動 || There is a lot of interaction ___ teachers and students in this class. || between|among of|with || 兩群人之間用 between。 || 這堂課師生之間有很多互動。
interfere || v. || 干涉；妨礙 || Don't interfere in my business. || 別干涉我的事。 || interfere with=妨礙…; interfere in=干涉… || Playing video games too much can interfere ___ your studies. || with|in|on || interfere with = 妨礙…。 || 打太多電動會妨礙你的課業。
intermediate || adj. || 中級的 || I'm taking an intermediate English course. || 我在上中級英文課程。 || intermediate level=中級程度 || This book is suitable ___ intermediate learners. || for|to|with || be suitable for = 適合…。 || 這本書適合中級程度的學習者。
interpret || v. || 口譯；詮釋 || She interpreted for the president. || 她為總統擔任口譯。 || interpret A as B=把 A 詮釋為 B || His silence was interpreted ___ agreement. || as|for|to || interpret A as B = 把 A 理解為 B。 || 他的沉默被解讀為同意。
interruption || n. || 打斷 || Sorry for the interruption. || 抱歉打擾了。 || without interruption=不間斷地 || He worked for five hours ___ interruption. || without|with|within || without interruption = 不間斷地。 || 他不間斷地工作了五個小時。
intimate || adj. || 親密的 || They are intimate friends. || 他們是親密的朋友。 || intimate relationship=親密關係 || She has an intimate knowledge ___ the city because she grew up there. || of|for|in || knowledge of = 對…的了解。 || 她對這座城市非常熟悉，因為她在那裡長大。
intuition || n. || 直覺 || Trust your intuition. || 相信你的直覺。 || by intuition=憑直覺 || She made the decision based ___ her intuition. || on|in|at || based on = 根據…。 || 她根據直覺做了決定。
invade || v. || 侵略 || The army invaded the country. || 軍隊入侵了那個國家。 || invade a country=入侵國家 || The country ___ invaded by its neighbor in 1939. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那個國家在 1939 年遭鄰國入侵。
invasion || n. || 入侵 || The invasion began at dawn. || 入侵在黎明時展開。 || invasion of privacy=侵犯隱私 || Reading someone's diary is an invasion ___ privacy. || of|to|for || an invasion of = 對…的侵犯。 || 看別人的日記是侵犯隱私。
invention || n. || 發明 || The phone is a great invention. || 電話是一項偉大的發明。 || new invention=新發明 || Necessity is the mother ___ invention. || of|for|to || 諺語：Necessity is the mother of invention.（需要為發明之母） || 需要為發明之母。
investigation || n. || 調查 || The police started an investigation. || 警方展開了調查。 || under investigation=調查中 || The cause of the fire is still ___ investigation. || under|below|in || under investigation = 調查中。 || 火災原因仍在調查中。
isolate || v. || 隔離；孤立 || Sick patients were isolated. || 生病的患者被隔離了。 || be isolated from=與…隔絕 || The village was isolated ___ the outside world after the landslide. || from|of|with || be isolated from = 與…隔絕。 || 山崩後那個村莊與外界隔絕。
isolation || n. || 孤立；隔離 || He lived in isolation for years. || 他孤立地生活了好幾年。 || in isolation=孤立地 || The sick patient was kept ___ isolation for two weeks. || in|on|at || in isolation = 隔離中。 || 那名病患被隔離了兩週。
