# 學測 L4 03：bridegroom – colleague
bridegroom || n. || 新郎 || The bridegroom looked nervous. || 新郎看起來很緊張。 || bride and bridegroom=新娘與新郎 || The bridegroom ___ waiting for the bride at the church now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The bridegroom 用 is。 || 新郎現在正在教堂等新娘。
broke || adj. || 身無分文的 || I'm broke until payday. || 發薪日前我都沒錢。 || go broke=破產 || I spent all my money last week, ___ I'm broke now. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 我上週把錢花光了，所以現在沒錢了。
broom || n. || 掃帚 || She swept the floor with a broom. || 她用掃帚掃地。 || sweep with a broom=用掃帚掃 || She swept the floor ___ a broom. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 她用掃帚掃地。
brutal || adj. || 殘忍的 || It was a brutal attack. || 那是一次殘暴的攻擊。 || brutal truth=殘酷的事實 || The brutal war ___ thousands of lives. || took|takes|has took || 描述過去的事用過去式 took。 || 那場殘酷的戰爭奪走了數千條人命。
bulletin || n. || 公告；快報 || Check the bulletin board for news. || 看公告欄上的消息。 || bulletin board=公告欄 || The exam results will ___ posted on the bulletin board. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 考試結果將張貼在公告欄上。
burglar || n. || 竊賊 || A burglar broke into the house. || 一名竊賊闖入了房子。 || burglar alarm=防盜警報器 || The burglar broke into the house ___ the family was on vacation. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 竊賊趁那家人去度假時闖進了屋子。
cabinet || n. || 櫥櫃；內閣 || The cups are in the kitchen cabinet. || 杯子在廚房的櫥櫃裡。 || kitchen cabinet=廚房櫥櫃 || Keep the medicine in a cabinet ___ children can't reach it. || where|which|what || 關係副詞 where 修飾地點名詞。 || 把藥放在小孩拿不到的櫃子裡。
calculate || v. || 計算 || Calculate the total cost. || 算出總費用。 || calculate the cost=計算費用 || Can you calculate how much ___ costs altogether? || it|that it|is it || 間接問句用直述句語序：how much it costs。 || 你能算出總共多少錢嗎？
calculation || n. || 計算 || My calculation was wrong. || 我算錯了。 || make a calculation=計算 || According to my calculation, we ___ need three more hours. || will|are|have || 表示將來的推測用 will + 原形動詞。 || 根據我的計算，我們還需要三個小時。
calorie || n. || 卡路里 || This cake has a lot of calories. || 這個蛋糕熱量很高。 || burn calories=燃燒卡路里 || Running is a good way ___ burn calories. || to|for|of || a way to V = 做…的方法。 || 跑步是燃燒卡路里的好方法。
campaign || n. || 活動；競選 || The school started a campaign to save water. || 學校發起了節約用水的活動。 || election campaign=競選活動 || The campaign aims ___ people aware of plastic pollution. || to make|making|make || aim to + 原形動詞。 || 這個活動旨在讓大家意識到塑膠污染。
candidate || n. || 候選人；應徵者 || There are three candidates for mayor. || 有三位市長候選人。 || presidential candidate=總統候選人 || The candidate ___ wins the most votes will become mayor. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 得票最多的候選人將成為市長。
cane || n. || 手杖；甘蔗 || My grandpa walks with a cane. || 我爺爺拄著拐杖走路。 || sugar cane=甘蔗 || My grandpa can't walk ___ his cane. || without|with|within || without = 沒有…。 || 我爺爺沒有拐杖就不能走路。
canoe || n./v. || 獨木舟 || We paddled a canoe on the lake. || 我們在湖上划獨木舟。 || paddle a canoe=划獨木舟 || We went canoeing on the lake ___ Sunday. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我們星期天去湖上划獨木舟。
capacity || n. || 容量；能力 || The stadium has a capacity of 50,000. || 這座體育場可容納五萬人。 || full capacity=全滿 || The hall has a capacity ___ 500 people. || of|for|to || a capacity of + 數量 = 容量為…。 || 這個禮堂可容納五百人。
capital || n. || 首都；資本 || Taipei is the capital of Taiwan. || 台北是台灣的首都。 || capital city=首都 || Tokyo is the capital of Japan, ___ it is also its largest city. || and|but|or || 兩個並列的敘述用 and。 || 東京是日本的首都，也是最大的城市。
capitalist || n./adj. || 資本家；資本主義的 || The U.S. is a capitalist country. || 美國是資本主義國家。 || capitalist economy=資本主義經濟 || In a capitalist economy, most businesses ___ owned by private people. || are|is|be || 被動語態；businesses 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 在資本主義經濟中，大部分企業由私人擁有。
cargo || n. || 貨物 || The ship is carrying cargo to Japan. || 這艘船正載貨前往日本。 || cargo ship=貨輪 || The cargo ___ loaded onto the ship last night. || was|were|is being || 被動語態；cargo 不可數，過去用 was + p.p.。 || 貨物昨晚被裝上了船。
carrier || n. || 運送者；航空公司 || He works for a mail carrier. || 他在一家郵件運送公司工作。 || mail carrier=郵差 || The mail carrier delivers letters ___ every house in the neighborhood. || to|at|for || deliver sth to + 地點 = 把某物送到…。 || 郵差把信送到社區裡的每一戶。
carve || v. || 雕刻 || He carved a bird out of wood. || 他用木頭雕了一隻鳥。 || carve out of=用…雕刻 || The statue was carved ___ a single piece of stone. || from|by|with || carve from = 用…雕刻而成。 || 這座雕像是用一整塊石頭雕成的。
catalogue || n. || 目錄 || Look through the catalogue. || 翻閱這本目錄。 || online catalogue=線上目錄 || You can find all our products ___ the catalogue. || in|on|at of || 在目錄裡用 in。 || 你可以在目錄裡找到我們所有的產品。
category || n. || 類別 || Put the books into different categories. || 把書分成不同的類別。 || in the category of=屬於…類 || The books are divided ___ five categories. || into|in|to be || divide A into B = 把 A 分成 B。 || 這些書被分成五類。
cease || v. || 停止 || The rain finally ceased. || 雨終於停了。 || cease to exist=不復存在 || The factory ceased production ___ 2020. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 這家工廠在 2020 年停產。
celebration || n. || 慶祝 || There was a big celebration after the game. || 比賽後有一場盛大的慶祝。 || in celebration of=為了慶祝… || The party was held in celebration ___ her birthday. || of|for|to || in celebration of = 為了慶祝…。 || 這場派對是為了慶祝她的生日而舉辦的。
chamber || n. || 室；會議廳 || The meeting was held in the council chamber. || 會議在議事廳舉行。 || chamber music=室內樂 || She plays the violin in a chamber music group ___ practices every week. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（group）。 || 她在一個每週練習的室內樂團拉小提琴。
championship || n. || 錦標賽；冠軍頭銜 || Our team won the championship. || 我們隊贏得了冠軍。 || world championship=世界錦標賽 || Our team won the championship ___ the first time in ten years. || for|in|at || for the first time = 第一次。 || 我們隊十年來首度贏得冠軍。
characteristic || n./adj. || 特徵 || Honesty is her best characteristic. || 誠實是她最好的特質。 || typical characteristic=典型特徵 || One of the characteristics of this bird ___ its long tail. || is|are|be || 主詞 One of… 為單數，用 is。 || 這種鳥的特徵之一是牠的長尾巴。
charity || n. || 慈善；慈善機構 || She gives money to charity. || 她捐錢給慈善機構。 || for charity=為了慈善 || The concert raised money ___ charity. || for|to|of || raise money for = 為…募款。 || 這場音樂會為慈善機構募款。
chemistry || n. || 化學 || Chemistry is my favorite subject. || 化學是我最喜歡的科目。 || chemistry lab=化學實驗室 || We did an interesting experiment ___ chemistry class. || in|on|at || in class = 在課堂上。 || 我們在化學課做了一個有趣的實驗。
cherish || v. || 珍惜 || Cherish every moment. || 珍惜每一刻。 || cherish memories=珍惜回憶 || We should cherish the time ___ we spend with our family. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 spend 的受詞。 || 我們應該珍惜和家人相處的時光。
chew || v. || 咀嚼 || Chew your food well. || 食物要好好咀嚼。 || chew gum=嚼口香糖 || Chewing gum is not allowed ___ class. || in|on|at || in class = 在課堂上。 || 上課時不准嚼口香糖。
choke || v. || 噎住 || He choked on a fish bone. || 他被魚刺哽住了。 || choke on=被…噎住 || The child almost choked ___ a piece of candy. || on|with|by || choke on = 被…噎住。 || 那個孩子差點被一顆糖噎住。
chorus || n. || 合唱團；副歌 || She sings in the school chorus. || 她參加學校合唱團。 || school chorus=學校合唱團 || Everyone joined in when the chorus ___. || started|starts|starting || 描述過去的事用過去式 started。 || 副歌開始時大家都跟著唱。
circular || adj. || 圓形的 || The table is circular. || 這張桌子是圓的。 || circular shape=圓形 || The table is circular, ___ everyone can see each other during meetings. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這張桌子是圓的，所以開會時大家都看得到彼此。
circulate || v. || 循環；流傳 || Blood circulates through the body. || 血液在全身循環。 || circulate news=傳播消息 || The news circulated quickly ___ the school. || around|about of|to || around = 在…四處。 || 消息很快在學校傳開了。
circulation || n. || 循環；發行量 || Exercise improves blood circulation. || 運動能促進血液循環。 || blood circulation=血液循環 || Exercise is good ___ blood circulation. || for|to|with || be good for = 對…有益。 || 運動對血液循環有益。
circumstance || n. || 情況；環境 || Under no circumstances should you give up. || 無論如何你都不該放棄。 || under no circumstances=無論如何都不 || Under no circumstances ___ you tell anyone the password. || should|you should|shall you || 否定副詞放句首要倒裝：should + 主詞 + 原形。 || 無論如何你都不應該告訴任何人密碼。
civilian || n./adj. || 平民 || Many civilians were hurt in the war. || 很多平民在戰爭中受傷。 || civilian life=平民生活 || Many civilians ___ killed during the war. || were|was|are || 被動語態；civilians 複數，過去用 were。 || 戰爭期間許多平民喪生。
civilization || n. || 文明 || Ancient Egypt had a great civilization. || 古埃及有偉大的文明。 || ancient civilization=古文明 || The Egyptian civilization is one of ___ oldest in the world. || the|a|an || one of the + 最高級。 || 埃及文明是世界上最古老的文明之一。
clarify || v. || 澄清 || Could you clarify what you mean? || 你可以說明你的意思嗎？ || clarify a point=澄清一個觀點 || Could you clarify ___ you mean by that? || what|which|that || 名詞子句 what you mean = 你的意思。 || 你可以說明一下你那句話是什麼意思嗎？
clash || n./v. || 衝突 || There was a clash between the two groups. || 兩個團體之間發生了衝突。 || clash with=與…衝突 || The meeting clashes ___ my piano lesson. || with|to|for || clash with = 與…衝突（時間撞期）。 || 會議和我的鋼琴課撞期了。
classification || n. || 分類 || The classification of animals is important in biology. || 動物分類在生物學中很重要。 || classification system=分類系統 || The classification ___ plants helps scientists study them. || of|for|to || the classification of = …的分類。 || 植物的分類有助於科學家研究它們。
classify || v. || 分類 || Classify the words into nouns and verbs. || 把這些字分成名詞和動詞。 || classify A into B=把 A 分成 B || The books are classified ___ subject. || by|with|in to || classify by… = 依照…分類。 || 這些書是依照科目分類的。
claw || n. || 爪子 || The cat has sharp claws. || 那隻貓有鋒利的爪子。 || sharp claws=利爪 || The cat scratched me ___ its claws. || with|by|in || 用某物用 with。 || 那隻貓用爪子抓了我。
cliff || n. || 懸崖 || Don't stand too close to the cliff. || 不要站得離懸崖太近。 || cliff edge=懸崖邊 || The house stands ___ the edge of a cliff. || on|in|at of || on the edge of = 在…的邊緣。 || 那間房子矗立在懸崖邊。
clumsy || adj. || 笨拙的 || I'm so clumsy. I dropped my phone again. || 我好笨手笨腳，又把手機摔了。 || clumsy mistake=笨拙的錯誤 || He is so clumsy ___ he always drops things. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他笨手笨腳到總是掉東西。
coarse || adj. || 粗糙的 || The cloth feels coarse. || 這塊布摸起來很粗。 || coarse sand=粗沙 || This fabric feels ___ coarse to wear next to the skin. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不能…。 || 這塊布料太粗了，不適合貼身穿。
code || n. || 密碼；規範 || Enter the code to open the door. || 輸入密碼開門。 || dress code=服裝規定; secret code=密碼 || The school has a strict dress code ___ all students must follow. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 follow 的受詞。 || 學校有所有學生都必須遵守的嚴格服裝規定。
collapse || v./n. || 倒塌 || The old bridge collapsed. || 那座老橋倒塌了。 || collapse in an earthquake=在地震中倒塌 || Many buildings collapsed ___ the earthquake. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 地震時許多建築物倒塌了。
colleague || n. || 同事 || She is my colleague. || 她是我的同事。 || work colleague=同事 || I had lunch ___ my colleagues yesterday. || with|to|for || have lunch with sb = 和某人吃午餐。 || 我昨天和同事一起吃午餐。
