# 學測 L4 02：arch – breed
arch || n. || 拱門 || We walked under the stone arch. || 我們從石拱門下走過。 || stone arch=石拱 || The old bridge ___ a beautiful stone arch. || has|have|is || The old bridge 單數，用 has。 || 那座老橋有一個美麗的石拱。
arise || v. || 出現；產生 || Problems may arise at any time. || 問題隨時可能出現。 || arise from=由…引起 || If any problems arise, please ___ me know. || let|to let|letting || 祈使句用原形動詞；let + 受詞 + 原形。 || 如果出現任何問題，請讓我知道。
arms || n. || 武器 || The country sells arms to other nations. || 那個國家賣武器給其他國家。 || arms race=軍備競賽 || The soldiers laid down their arms ___ the war ended. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 戰爭結束時士兵們放下了武器。
artificial || adj. || 人造的 || These flowers are artificial. || 這些花是人造的。 || artificial intelligence=人工智慧 || Artificial intelligence is changing the way we ___. || live|lives|living || the way (that) we + 原形動詞。 || 人工智慧正在改變我們的生活方式。
artistic || adj. || 藝術的；有藝術天分的 || She is very artistic. || 她很有藝術天分。 || artistic talent=藝術天分 || She showed great artistic talent ___ a young age. || at|in|on || at a young age = 年紀很小時。 || 她年紀很小就展現出藝術天分。
ashamed || adj. || 感到羞愧的 || He was ashamed of his behavior. || 他對自己的行為感到羞愧。 || be ashamed of=對…感到羞愧 || You should be ashamed ___ yourself. || of|for|with || be ashamed of = 對…感到羞愧。 || 你應該感到羞愧。
aspect || n. || 方面 || We discussed every aspect of the problem. || 我們討論了這個問題的各個面向。 || in every aspect=在各方面 || Technology has changed every aspect ___ our lives. || of|in|to || every aspect of = …的每個方面。 || 科技改變了我們生活的每個面向。
aspirin || n. || 阿斯匹靈 || Take an aspirin for your headache. || 吃一顆阿斯匹靈治頭痛。 || take aspirin=服用阿斯匹靈 || You shouldn't take aspirin ___ an empty stomach. || on|in|with || on an empty stomach = 空腹。 || 你不應該空腹服用阿斯匹靈。
assemble || v. || 組裝；集合 || We assembled the furniture ourselves. || 我們自己組裝了家具。 || assemble a desk=組裝書桌 || The students assembled ___ the hall for the ceremony. || in|on|at || 在大廳裡用 in。 || 學生們在禮堂集合參加典禮。
assembly || n. || 集會；組裝 || We have a school assembly every Monday. || 我們每週一有朝會。 || school assembly=朝會 || The school assembly ___ held every Monday morning. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The school assembly 用 is + p.p.。 || 學校朝會每週一早上舉行。
assistance || n. || 協助 || Thank you for your assistance. || 謝謝你的協助。 || need assistance=需要協助 || If you need any assistance, please don't hesitate ___ ask. || to|for|of || hesitate to + 原形動詞。 || 如果需要任何協助，請儘管開口。
associate || v./n. || 聯想；同事 || People associate red with luck. || 人們把紅色和好運聯想在一起。 || associate A with B=把 A 和 B 聯想在一起 || In Chinese culture, the color red is associated ___ good luck. || with|to|by || be associated with = 與…有關聯。 || 在中華文化中，紅色與好運有關。
association || n. || 協會；關聯 || He is a member of the teachers' association. || 他是教師協會的會員。 || in association with=與…合作 || The event was organized in association ___ the city government. || with|to|by || in association with = 與…合作。 || 這個活動是與市政府合作舉辦的。
assurance || n. || 保證；自信 || He gave me his assurance. || 他給了我保證。 || give assurance=給予保證 || He gave me his assurance ___ the work would be done on time. || that|what|which || assurance that… = 保證…（同位語子句）。 || 他向我保證工作會準時完成。
assure || v. || 保證；使確信 || I assure you it's safe. || 我向你保證這很安全。 || assure sb of=向某人保證… || I can assure you ___ the food is fresh. || that|what|which || assure sb that… = 向某人保證…。 || 我可以向你保證食物是新鮮的。
athletic || adj. || 運動的；強健的 || He is tall and athletic. || 他又高又健壯。 || athletic ability=運動能力 || She is the ___ athletic girl in our class. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 她是我們班運動能力最好的女生。
atmosphere || n. || 氣氛；大氣 || The restaurant has a nice atmosphere. || 這家餐廳氣氛很好。 || relaxed atmosphere=輕鬆的氣氛 || The atmosphere at the party was ___ relaxed that everyone had fun. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 派對的氣氛輕鬆到每個人都玩得很開心。
atom || n. || 原子 || Everything is made of atoms. || 萬物都由原子組成。 || atom bomb=原子彈 || Everything around us is made ___ atoms. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…構成。 || 我們周遭的一切都是由原子構成的。
atomic || adj. || 原子的 || The atomic bomb was used in World War II. || 原子彈在二戰中被使用。 || atomic energy=原子能 || The atomic bomb ___ dropped on Hiroshima in 1945. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 原子彈於 1945 年投在廣島。
attraction || n. || 吸引力；景點 || Taipei 101 is a popular tourist attraction. || 台北 101 是熱門的觀光景點。 || tourist attraction=觀光景點 || Taroko Gorge is one of the most famous tourist attractions ___ Taiwan. || in|of|at || 最高級 + in + 地方。 || 太魯閣是台灣最有名的觀光景點之一。
audio || adj. || 聲音的 || The audio quality is very good. || 音質非常好。 || audio guide=語音導覽 || You can rent an audio guide ___ the entrance of the museum. || at|in|on || 在入口（地點）用 at。 || 你可以在博物館入口租語音導覽。
authentic || adj. || 真正的；道地的 || This restaurant serves authentic Italian food. || 這家餐廳供應道地的義大利菜。 || authentic food=道地料理 || The restaurant serves authentic Japanese food, ___ is why it is always full. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句 which 指代整個前句。 || 那家餐廳供應道地的日本料理，這就是它總是客滿的原因。
authority || n. || 權威；當局 || The local authorities closed the road. || 地方當局封閉了這條路。 || the authorities=當局 || The road was closed ___ the authorities because of the landslide. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 因為山崩，道路被當局封閉。
autobiography || n. || 自傳 || She wrote her autobiography. || 她寫了自傳。 || write an autobiography=寫自傳 || In his autobiography, he describes ___ he grew up poor. || how|what|which || 名詞子句 how… = 如何…。 || 他在自傳中描述了自己如何在貧困中長大。
autograph || n./v. || 親筆簽名 || Can I have your autograph? || 我可以要你的簽名嗎？ || ask for an autograph=要簽名 || The fans waited for hours ___ get the singer's autograph. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 粉絲們等了好幾個小時要那位歌手的簽名。
await || v. || 等待 || A big surprise awaits you. || 一個大驚喜在等著你。 || await a reply=等待回覆 || We are awaiting ___ reply from the school. || a|an|the more || reply 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 我們正在等待學校的回覆。
bald || adj. || 禿頭的 || My uncle is going bald. || 我叔叔快禿頭了。 || go bald=變禿 || The man ___ is bald is my math teacher. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那個禿頭的男人是我的數學老師。
ballet || n. || 芭蕾舞 || She has been taking ballet lessons since she was five. || 她從五歲就開始上芭蕾課。 || ballet dancer=芭蕾舞者 || She has taken ballet lessons ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她上芭蕾課已經十年了。
bandage || n./v. || 繃帶 || The nurse put a bandage on my arm. || 護理師在我手臂上綁了繃帶。 || put on a bandage=包紮 || The nurse wrapped a bandage ___ my ankle. || around|across|along || around = 環繞…。 || 護理師在我的腳踝纏上繃帶。
bankrupt || adj. || 破產的 || The company went bankrupt. || 那家公司破產了。 || go bankrupt=破產 || The company went bankrupt ___ the economic crisis. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 那家公司因經濟危機而破產。
bargain || n./v. || 便宜貨；討價還價 || This coat was a real bargain. || 這件大衣真便宜。 || bargain with=和…討價還價 || She bargained ___ the seller and got a lower price. || with|to|for || bargain with sb = 和某人討價還價。 || 她和賣家討價還價，拿到了更低的價格。
barrier || n. || 障礙 || Language can be a barrier. || 語言可能是一種障礙。 || language barrier=語言障礙 || The language barrier made it hard ___ him to make friends. || for|of|to || make it hard for sb to V。 || 語言障礙讓他很難交到朋友。
basin || n. || 盆；盆地 || Taipei is in a basin. || 台北位於盆地中。 || Taipei Basin=台北盆地 || Taipei is located ___ a basin. || in|on|at || 在盆地裡用 in。 || 台北位於盆地中。
battery || n. || 電池 || My phone battery is dead. || 我的手機沒電了。 || charge the battery=充電 || My phone battery ran out, so I couldn't call ___. || you|your|yours || call 後面接受詞，用受格 you。 || 我的手機沒電了，所以沒辦法打給你。
beggar || n. || 乞丐 || A beggar asked me for money. || 一個乞丐向我要錢。 || beggar=乞丐 || The beggar ___ sat outside the station every day was given a job. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那個每天坐在車站外的乞丐得到了一份工作。
behavior || n. || 行為 || His behavior was rude. || 他的行為很無禮。 || good behavior=良好的行為 || Parents should set a good example ___ their children's behavior. || for|of|to || set an example for sb = 為某人樹立榜樣。 || 父母應該為孩子的行為樹立好榜樣。
bin || n. || 箱；垃圾桶 || Throw it in the bin. || 把它丟進垃圾桶。 || recycling bin=回收桶 || Please put the bottles ___ the recycling bin. || in|on|at || 放進桶子裡用 in。 || 請把瓶子放進回收桶。
biography || n. || 傳記 || I'm reading a biography of Steve Jobs. || 我正在讀賈伯斯的傳記。 || biography of=…的傳記 || This biography tells the story of a man ___ changed the world. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 這本傳記講述一個改變世界的人的故事。
biology || n. || 生物學 || Biology is my favorite subject. || 生物是我最喜歡的科目。 || marine biology=海洋生物學 || She wants to major ___ biology in college. || in|on|at || major in = 主修…。 || 她想在大學主修生物。
blade || n. || 刀片；葉片 || The blade of the knife is sharp. || 這把刀的刀刃很鋒利。 || razor blade=刀片 || Be careful. The blade is ___ sharp. || very|much|many || 形容詞前面加 very 表示「很」。 || 小心，刀片非常鋒利。
blend || v./n. || 混合 || Blend the fruit and milk. || 把水果和牛奶打在一起。 || blend A with B=把 A 和 B 混合 || The song blends pop music ___ traditional Chinese music. || with|to|by || blend A with B = 把 A 和 B 混合。 || 這首歌融合了流行音樂與中國傳統音樂。
blessing || n. || 祝福；幸事 || Good health is a blessing. || 身體健康是一種福氣。 || a blessing in disguise=塞翁失馬 || Losing that job was a blessing ___ disguise. || in|with|by || a blessing in disguise = 因禍得福。 || 失去那份工作其實是因禍得福。
blink || v./n. || 眨眼 || She blinked in the bright light. || 她在強光下眨了眨眼。 || in the blink of an eye=一眨眼 || The magician made the coin disappear ___ the blink of an eye. || in|at|with || in the blink of an eye = 一眨眼之間。 || 魔術師一眨眼就讓硬幣消失了。
bloom || n./v. || 開花 || The flowers are in full bloom. || 花正盛開著。 || in full bloom=盛開 || The cherry blossoms are ___ full bloom now. || in|on|at || in full bloom = 盛開。 || 櫻花現在正盛開。
blossom || n./v. || 花；開花 || Cherry blossoms come out in spring. || 櫻花在春天綻放。 || cherry blossom=櫻花 || Every spring, thousands of people go to Alishan ___ see the cherry blossoms. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 每年春天都有成千上萬的人到阿里山賞櫻。
boast || v. || 自誇 || He likes to boast about his grades. || 他喜歡炫耀自己的成績。 || boast about=誇耀… || He often boasts ___ his rich parents. || about|to|with || boast about = 誇耀…。 || 他常常誇耀他有錢的父母。
bond || n./v. || 聯繫；羈絆 || There is a strong bond between them. || 他們之間有深厚的情誼。 || strong bond=深厚的連結 || Sharing meals helps strengthen the bond ___ family members. || between|among of|with || 兩者或成員之間的連結常用 between。 || 一起吃飯有助於加強家人之間的連結。
bounce || v./n. || 彈跳 || The ball bounced off the wall. || 球從牆上彈回來。 || bounce back=反彈；恢復 || The ball bounced ___ the wall and hit me. || off|of|out || bounce off = 從…彈開。 || 球從牆上彈開，打到了我。
bracelet || n. || 手鐲 || She wears a silver bracelet. || 她戴著一個銀手鐲。 || gold bracelet=金手鐲 || This bracelet ___ given to me by my grandma. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個手鐲是我奶奶送給我的。
breed || n./v. || 品種；繁殖 || What breed is your dog? || 你的狗是什麼品種？ || dog breed=狗的品種 || This breed of dog is known ___ being friendly with children. || for|as|with || be known for = 以…聞名。 || 這個品種的狗以對小孩友善著稱。
