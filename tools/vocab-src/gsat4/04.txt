# 學測 L4 04：combination – continuous
combination || n. || 組合 || The lock has a combination of four numbers. || 這個鎖的密碼是四個數字的組合。 || in combination with=與…結合 || Success comes from a combination ___ talent and hard work. || of|for|with || a combination of = …的組合。 || 成功來自天分與努力的結合。
comedy || n. || 喜劇 || I like watching comedies. || 我喜歡看喜劇。 || romantic comedy=浪漫喜劇 || This comedy is ___ funniest movie I have ever seen. || the|a|an || 最高級前面加 the。 || 這部喜劇是我看過最好笑的電影。
commander || n. || 指揮官 || The commander gave orders to the soldiers. || 指揮官對士兵下達命令。 || military commander=軍事指揮官 || The soldiers followed the commander's orders ___ question. || without|with|within || without question = 毫無疑問地。 || 士兵們毫無異議地服從指揮官的命令。
comment || n./v. || 評論 || Do you have any comments? || 你有任何意見嗎？ || make a comment=發表評論 || The teacher made some helpful comments ___ my essay. || on|in|at || comment on = 對…的評論。 || 老師對我的作文提出了一些有用的意見。
commerce || n. || 商業 || E-commerce is growing fast. || 電子商務成長快速。 || e-commerce=電子商務 || E-commerce has changed the way people ___. || shop|shops|shopping || the way (that) people + 原形動詞。 || 電子商務改變了人們購物的方式。
commit || v. || 犯（罪）；承諾 || He committed a crime. || 他犯了罪。 || commit a crime=犯罪; be committed to=致力於… || She is committed ___ helping poor children. || to|for|with || be committed to + V-ing = 致力於…。 || 她致力於幫助貧童。
communication || n. || 溝通 || Good communication is important. || 良好的溝通很重要。 || communication skills=溝通技巧 || Good communication skills are important ___ any job. || in|at|on || in any job = 在任何工作中。 || 良好的溝通技巧在任何工作中都很重要。
community || n. || 社區；群體 || She helps people in her community. || 她幫助社區裡的人。 || local community=當地社區 || Volunteers helped clean the park in ___ community. || our|us|ours || 名詞 community 前用所有格形容詞 our。 || 志工們幫忙清理我們社區的公園。
companion || n. || 同伴 || The dog is my best companion. || 這隻狗是我最好的夥伴。 || travel companion=旅伴 || A good book can be a great companion ___ a long trip. || on|in|at || on a trip = 在旅途中。 || 一本好書可以是長途旅行的好夥伴。
competition || n. || 比賽；競爭 || She won first prize in the competition. || 她在比賽中得到第一名。 || enter a competition=參加比賽 || There is strong competition ___ the two companies. || between|among|in || 兩者之間用 between。 || 這兩家公司之間競爭激烈。
competitive || adj. || 競爭激烈的；有競爭力的 || The job market is very competitive. || 就業市場競爭非常激烈。 || competitive price=有競爭力的價格 || The entrance exam is ___ competitive than it was ten years ago. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 入學考試比十年前更競爭。
competitor || n. || 競爭者 || The company is our main competitor. || 那家公司是我們的主要競爭對手。 || main competitor=主要競爭對手 || There were over 100 competitors ___ the race. || in|on|at || in the race = 在比賽中。 || 這場比賽有一百多名參賽者。
complicate || v. || 使複雜 || Don't complicate things. || 別把事情弄複雜。 || complicate matters=使事情複雜 || Adding more rules will only complicate the problem, ___ it? || won't|will|doesn't || 肯定句（will）的附加問句用否定 won't。 || 增加更多規定只會讓問題更複雜，不是嗎？
compose || v. || 作曲；組成 || Mozart composed many famous works. || 莫札特創作了許多名曲。 || be composed of=由…組成 || Water is composed ___ hydrogen and oxygen. || of|from|by || be composed of = 由…組成。 || 水由氫和氧組成。
composer || n. || 作曲家 || Beethoven was a great composer. || 貝多芬是偉大的作曲家。 || famous composer=知名作曲家 || Beethoven, ___ was deaf in his later years, was a great composer. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 晚年失聰的貝多芬是一位偉大的作曲家。
composition || n. || 作文；作品；組成 || I wrote a composition about my family. || 我寫了一篇關於家人的作文。 || English composition=英文作文 || Please hand in your composition ___ Friday. || by|until|since || by + 時間 = 在…之前（截止）。 || 請在星期五之前交作文。
concentrate || v. || 專心 || I can't concentrate with this noise. || 這噪音讓我無法專心。 || concentrate on=專心於… || Please concentrate ___ your work. || on|in|at || concentrate on = 專心於…。 || 請專心工作。
concentration || n. || 專注力 || Reading requires concentration. || 閱讀需要專注力。 || lose concentration=分心 || Lack of sleep can affect your concentration ___ class. || in|on|at || in class = 在課堂上。 || 睡眠不足會影響你上課的專注力。
concept || n. || 概念 || The concept is hard to understand. || 這個概念很難理解。 || basic concept=基本概念 || It took me a long time ___ understand the concept. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 我花了很久才理解這個概念。
concerning || prep. || 關於 || I have a question concerning the homework. || 我有一個關於作業的問題。 || concerning=關於 || Please contact me ___ you have any questions concerning the trip. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你對這趟旅行有任何問題，請聯絡我。
concrete || adj./n. || 具體的；混凝土 || Can you give me a concrete example? || 你能給我一個具體的例子嗎？ || concrete example=具體的例子 || The building is made ___ concrete and steel. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這棟建築是由混凝土和鋼鐵建成的。
conductor || n. || 指揮；列車長 || The conductor led the orchestra. || 指揮家帶領管弦樂團。 || orchestra conductor=樂團指揮 || The conductor raised his hands, and the music ___. || began|begins|beginning || 描述過去的事用過去式 began。 || 指揮舉起雙手，音樂隨即響起。
conference || n. || 會議 || She attended a conference in Tokyo. || 她參加了在東京舉行的會議。 || press conference=記者會 || The conference will ___ held in Taipei next month. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 會議將於下個月在台北舉行。
confess || v. || 坦承 || He confessed that he broke the window. || 他坦承是他打破了窗戶。 || confess to=坦承… || He confessed ___ breaking the vase. || to|for|of || confess to + V-ing = 坦承做了…。 || 他坦承打破了花瓶。
confidence || n. || 信心 || She speaks with confidence. || 她說話充滿自信。 || with confidence=自信地; lack confidence=缺乏自信 || Practice will help you build ___ confidence. || your|you|yours || 名詞 confidence 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 練習能幫助你建立自信。
confusion || n. || 困惑；混亂 || There was some confusion about the time. || 對時間有些混淆。 || in confusion=困惑地 || He looked at me ___ confusion. || in|with|by || in confusion = 困惑地。 || 他困惑地看著我。
congratulate || v. || 恭喜 || I congratulated her on her success. || 我恭喜她成功。 || congratulate sb on=為…恭喜某人 || We congratulated him ___ winning the contest. || on|for|of || congratulate sb on + V-ing = 恭喜某人…。 || 我們恭喜他贏得比賽。
congress || n. || 國會 || The U.S. Congress passed a new law. || 美國國會通過了一項新法律。 || member of Congress=國會議員 || The new law ___ passed by Congress last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這項新法律去年由國會通過。
conquer || v. || 征服；克服 || You can conquer your fears. || 你可以克服恐懼。 || conquer one's fear=克服恐懼 || She finally conquered her fear ___ public speaking. || of|for|to || a fear of = 對…的恐懼。 || 她終於克服了對公開演講的恐懼。
conscience || n. || 良心 || He has a guilty conscience. || 他良心不安。 || guilty conscience=良心不安 || Let your conscience be ___ guide. || your|you|yours || 名詞 guide 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 讓你的良心引導你。
consequence || n. || 後果 || You must face the consequences. || 你必須承擔後果。 || as a consequence=因此 || He didn't study. ___ a consequence, he failed the test. || As|For|In || as a consequence = 因此、結果。 || 他沒讀書，結果考試不及格。
consequent || adj. || 隨之發生的 || The storm and the consequent flood damaged many homes. || 暴風雨和隨之而來的水災損壞了許多房屋。 || consequent=隨之而來的 || The heavy rain and the consequent flooding ___ the roads. || closed|close|closing || 描述過去的事用過去式 closed。 || 大雨和隨之而來的淹水讓道路封閉了。
conservative || adj. || 保守的 || My parents are quite conservative. || 我的父母相當保守。 || conservative view=保守的看法 || My grandparents are ___ conservative than my parents. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 我的祖父母比我父母更保守。
consist || v. || 由…組成 || The team consists of ten players. || 這支隊伍由十名球員組成。 || consist of=由…組成 || The test consists ___ three parts. || of|in|with || consist of = 由…組成。 || 這份考試由三個部分組成。
consistent || adj. || 一致的；始終如一的 || His work is always consistent. || 他的工作表現始終如一。 || be consistent with=與…一致 || His words are not consistent ___ his actions. || with|to|for || be consistent with = 與…一致。 || 他言行不一。
constitute || v. || 構成 || Women constitute half of the population. || 女性佔人口的一半。 || constitute a problem=構成問題 || Students under 15 constitute about 20 percent ___ the members. || of|in|for || percent of = …的百分之…。 || 十五歲以下的學生約佔會員的百分之二十。
constitution || n. || 憲法 || The constitution protects freedom of speech. || 憲法保障言論自由。 || national constitution=憲法 || Freedom of speech is protected ___ the constitution. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 言論自由受到憲法保障。
construct || v. || 建造 || They are constructing a new bridge. || 他們正在建造一座新橋。 || construct a building=建造大樓 || A new library is ___ constructed near our school. || being|been|be || 現在進行式被動：is being + p.p.。 || 我們學校附近正在興建一座新圖書館。
construction || n. || 建設；構造 || The road is under construction. || 這條路正在施工。 || under construction=施工中 || The new MRT line is still ___ construction. || under|below|in || under construction = 施工中。 || 新捷運線仍在施工中。
constructive || adj. || 建設性的 || Thank you for your constructive advice. || 謝謝你有建設性的建議。 || constructive criticism=建設性的批評 || Constructive criticism can help you ___. || improve|improves|improving || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 有建設性的批評能幫助你進步。
consult || v. || 諮詢 || You should consult a doctor. || 你應該去看醫生。 || consult a doctor=諮詢醫生 || You should consult a doctor ___ taking any medicine. || before|after|since || 吃藥「之前」諮詢，用 before。 || 服用任何藥物之前你應該先諮詢醫生。
consultant || n. || 顧問 || He works as a consultant. || 他擔任顧問。 || business consultant=企業顧問 || The company hired a consultant ___ help solve the problem. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 公司聘請了一位顧問來幫忙解決問題。
consume || v. || 消耗；吃喝 || Cars consume a lot of fuel. || 汽車消耗大量燃料。 || consume energy=消耗能源 || Americans consume more meat ___ people in most other countries. || than|then|as || 比較級 more + than。 || 美國人比大多數其他國家的人吃更多肉。
consumer || n. || 消費者 || Consumers want lower prices. || 消費者想要更低的價格。 || consumer protection=消費者保護 || More and more consumers ___ shopping online. || are|is|be || 現在進行式；consumers 複數用 are。 || 越來越多消費者在網路上購物。
container || n. || 容器 || Put the food in a container. || 把食物放進容器裡。 || plastic container=塑膠容器 || Store the leftovers in a container ___ a lid. || with|of|by || with = 帶有…。 || 把剩菜裝在有蓋子的容器裡保存。
content || n./adj. || 內容；滿足的 || The content of the book is interesting. || 這本書的內容很有趣。 || be content with=滿足於… || He is content ___ his simple life. || with|of|to || be content with = 滿足於…。 || 他對簡單的生活很滿足。
contest || n. || 比賽 || She won the singing contest. || 她贏了歌唱比賽。 || speech contest=演講比賽 || More than 50 students took part ___ the contest. || in|on|at || take part in = 參加…。 || 五十多名學生參加了這場比賽。
context || n. || 上下文；背景 || Guess the meaning from the context. || 從上下文猜意思。 || in this context=在此情況下 || You can often guess the meaning of a word ___ the context. || from|of|with || from + 名詞 = 從…（推斷）。 || 你常常可以從上下文猜出一個字的意思。
continual || adj. || 不斷的 || I'm tired of his continual complaints. || 我受夠了他不斷的抱怨。 || continual noise=不斷的噪音 || The continual noise from the street made it hard ___ me to sleep. || for|of|to || make it hard for sb to V。 || 街上不斷的噪音讓我很難入睡。
continuous || adj. || 連續的 || It rained for three continuous days. || 連續下了三天雨。 || continuous rain=連續降雨 || After three days of continuous rain, many roads ___ flooded. || were|was|are || 被動語態；roads 複數，過去用 were。 || 連續下了三天雨後，許多道路都淹水了。
