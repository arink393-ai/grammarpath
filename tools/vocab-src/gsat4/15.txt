# 學測 L4 15：peep – privilege
peep || v./n. || 偷看 || The kid peeped through the window. || 那個小孩從窗戶偷看。 || peep at=偷看… || Don't peep ___ the answers before you finish. || at|to|on || peep at = 偷看…。 || 寫完之前不要偷看答案。
peer || n./v. || 同儕；凝視 || Teenagers are influenced by their peers. || 青少年受同儕影響。 || peer pressure=同儕壓力 || Many teenagers feel pressure ___ their peers. || from|of|by || pressure from = 來自…的壓力。 || 很多青少年感受到來自同儕的壓力。
penalty || n. || 處罰；罰金 || The penalty for littering is 1,200 dollars. || 亂丟垃圾的罰款是一千二百元。 || death penalty=死刑 || There is a heavy penalty ___ drunk driving. || for|of|to || a penalty for = 對…的處罰。 || 酒駕會受到重罰。
percent || n. || 百分之… || Sixty percent of the students are girls. || 百分之六十的學生是女生。 || percent=百分比 || About 30 percent of the water ___ wasted every day. || is|are|be || percent of + 不可數名詞，動詞用單數 is。 || 每天約有百分之三十的水被浪費。
percentage || n. || 百分比 || A large percentage of students have phones. || 大部分學生都有手機。 || a large percentage of=大部分的 || A large percentage ___ teenagers use social media every day. || of|in|for || a percentage of = …的百分比。 || 很大比例的青少年每天使用社群媒體。
perfection || n. || 完美 || Nobody can achieve perfection. || 沒有人能達到完美。 || to perfection=完美地 || The steak was cooked ___ perfection. || to|in|with || to perfection = 完美地。 || 牛排煎得恰到好處。
perfume || n. || 香水 || She wears expensive perfume. || 她擦昂貴的香水。 || wear perfume=擦香水 || The perfume ___ she bought in Paris smells wonderful. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 bought 的受詞。 || 她在巴黎買的香水聞起來很棒。
permanent || adj. || 永久的 || This is not a permanent job. || 這不是一份長期的工作。 || permanent address=永久地址 || The damage to his eyes was permanent, ___ he couldn't see clearly again. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 他眼睛的損傷是永久性的，所以再也看不清楚了。
persuasion || n. || 說服 || After much persuasion, he agreed. || 經過一番勸說，他同意了。 || powers of persuasion=說服力 || After a lot of persuasion, my parents let me ___ to the concert. || go|to go|going || let + 受詞 + 原形動詞。 || 經過一番說服，我爸媽讓我去聽演唱會了。
persuasive || adj. || 有說服力的 || She gave a persuasive speech. || 她發表了一場很有說服力的演講。 || persuasive argument=有說服力的論點 || His argument was ___ persuasive that everyone agreed with him. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他的論點很有說服力，大家都同意他。
pessimistic || adj. || 悲觀的 || Don't be so pessimistic. || 別那麼悲觀。 || be pessimistic about=對…悲觀 || He is pessimistic ___ his chances of winning. || about|of|with || be pessimistic about = 對…悲觀。 || 他對自己贏的機會很悲觀。
pest || n. || 害蟲 || Farmers use chemicals to kill pests. || 農夫用化學藥劑殺害蟲。 || pest control=除蟲 || Some birds help farmers ___ eating pests. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 有些鳥藉由吃害蟲來幫助農夫。
phenomenon || n. || 現象 || The northern lights are a natural phenomenon. || 北極光是一種自然現象。 || natural phenomenon=自然現象 || A rainbow is a natural phenomenon ___ happens after rain. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（phenomenon）。 || 彩虹是一種在雨後出現的自然現象。
philosopher || n. || 哲學家 || Confucius was a great philosopher. || 孔子是一位偉大的哲學家。 || Greek philosopher=希臘哲學家 || Confucius, ___ lived over 2,500 years ago, was a great philosopher. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 生活在兩千五百多年前的孔子是一位偉大的哲學家。
philosophical || adj. || 哲學的；達觀的 || He is philosophical about losing. || 他對輸贏很看得開。 || philosophical question=哲學問題 || She was philosophical ___ losing the game. || about|of|with || be philosophical about = 對…看得開。 || 她對輸掉比賽看得很開。
philosophy || n. || 哲學 || She studies philosophy at university. || 她在大學讀哲學。 || philosophy of life=人生觀 || My philosophy ___ life is to enjoy every day. || of|for|in || philosophy of life = 人生哲學。 || 我的人生哲學是享受每一天。
photography || n. || 攝影 || He is interested in photography. || 他對攝影有興趣。 || nature photography=自然攝影 || She took up photography ___ a hobby. || as|like|for || take up… as a hobby = 把…當作嗜好。 || 她把攝影當作嗜好。
physical || adj. || 身體的；物理的 || Physical exercise is good for you. || 身體運動對你有益。 || physical education=體育 || Regular exercise improves both physical ___ mental health. || and|or|but || both A and B = A 和 B 都。 || 規律運動能改善身心健康。
physician || n. || 醫師 || She is a family physician. || 她是家庭醫師。 || family physician=家庭醫師 || You should consult a physician ___ you feel sick. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你覺得不舒服，應該諮詢醫師。
physicist || n. || 物理學家 || Einstein was a famous physicist. || 愛因斯坦是著名的物理學家。 || famous physicist=知名物理學家 || The physicist ___ theory changed science won the Nobel Prize. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位理論改變了科學的物理學家獲得了諾貝爾獎。
physics || n. || 物理學 || Physics is a difficult subject. || 物理是一門困難的科目。 || quantum physics=量子物理 || Physics ___ my favorite subject in high school. || was|were|are || physics 視為單數；過去的事用 was。 || 物理是我高中時最喜歡的科目。
pickle || n. || 醃菜 || I like pickles in my hamburger. || 我喜歡漢堡裡有醃黃瓜。 || pickled cucumber=醃黃瓜 || Can you take the pickles ___ of my burger? || out|off|away || take out of = 從…拿出來。 || 你可以把我漢堡裡的醃黃瓜拿掉嗎？
pioneer || n./v. || 先驅 || He was a pioneer in computer science. || 他是電腦科學的先驅。 || pioneer in=…的先驅 || She was a pioneer ___ the field of medicine. || in|at|on || a pioneer in = …領域的先驅。 || 她是醫學領域的先驅。
plentiful || adj. || 豐富的 || Food is plentiful here. || 這裡食物很充足。 || plentiful supply=充足的供應 || Fruit is plentiful and cheap ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 夏天水果充足又便宜。
plot || n./v. || 情節；陰謀 || The plot of the movie is interesting. || 這部電影的情節很有趣。 || the plot of=…的情節 || The plot ___ the novel is very complicated. || of|for|to || the plot of = …的情節。 || 這本小說的情節非常複雜。
plum || n. || 李子；梅子 || Plum blossoms bloom in winter. || 梅花在冬天綻放。 || plum blossom=梅花 || The plum blossom is the national flower ___ Taiwan. || of|for|in || the national flower of = …的國花。 || 梅花是台灣的國花。
plumber || n. || 水電工 || Call a plumber to fix the pipe. || 打電話叫水電工來修水管。 || call a plumber=叫水電工 || The pipe was fixed ___ a plumber. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 水管由水電工修好了。
poisonous || adj. || 有毒的 || Some mushrooms are poisonous. || 有些蘑菇有毒。 || poisonous snake=毒蛇 || Some snakes are poisonous, ___ you should stay away from them. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 有些蛇有毒，所以你應該遠離牠們。
polish || v./n. || 擦亮 || He polished his shoes. || 他擦亮了鞋子。 || shoe polish=鞋油 || He polished his shoes until they ___. || shone|shine|shining || 描述過去的事用過去式 shone。 || 他把鞋子擦到閃閃發亮。
popularity || n. || 受歡迎 || The singer's popularity is growing. || 那位歌手越來越受歡迎。 || gain popularity=越來越受歡迎 || Bubble tea has gained popularity ___ over the world. || all|every|whole || all over the world = 全世界。 || 珍珠奶茶在全世界越來越受歡迎。
portable || adj. || 可攜帶的 || I have a portable charger. || 我有一個行動電源。 || portable charger=行動電源 || This speaker is small and portable, ___ you can take it anywhere. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這個喇叭小巧好攜帶，所以你可以帶去任何地方。
portray || v. || 描繪；扮演 || The movie portrays life in the 1950s. || 這部電影描繪了 1950 年代的生活。 || portray sb as=把某人描繪成… || In the movie, the king is portrayed ___ a kind man. || as|like|for || be portrayed as = 被描繪成…。 || 電影中，國王被描繪成一位仁慈的人。
possess || v. || 擁有 || He possesses great talent. || 他擁有過人的天分。 || possess a skill=擁有技能 || She possesses a talent ___ few people have. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 have 的受詞。 || 她擁有很少人具備的天分。
possession || n. || 擁有物；財產 || He lost all his possessions in the fire. || 他在火災中失去所有財產。 || personal possessions=個人物品 || Please keep your personal possessions ___ you at all times. || with|by|to || with sb = 在某人身上。 || 請隨時將個人物品帶在身上。
postage || n. || 郵資 || How much is the postage? || 郵資多少錢？ || postage stamp=郵票 || The postage for this package ___ 60 dollars. || is|are|be || The postage 不可數，用 is。 || 這個包裹的郵資是六十元。
potential || adj./n. || 潛在的；潛力 || She has great potential. || 她很有潛力。 || reach one's potential=發揮潛力 || Every student has the potential ___ succeed. || to|for|of || the potential to + 原形動詞。 || 每個學生都有成功的潛力。
precise || adj. || 精確的 || Please be more precise. || 請說得更精確一點。 || precise time=確切的時間 || Can you tell me the precise time ___ the accident happened? || when|which|what || 關係副詞 when 修飾時間名詞 time。 || 你能告訴我事故發生的確切時間嗎？
predict || v. || 預測 || It is hard to predict the future. || 預測未來很難。 || predict the weather=預報天氣 || Scientists predict ___ the temperature will keep rising. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 predict 的受詞。 || 科學家預測氣溫將持續上升。
prediction || n. || 預測 || His prediction was correct. || 他的預測是正確的。 || make a prediction=做預測 || The weather prediction ___ tomorrow is sunny. || for|of|to || the prediction for + 時間 = …的預報。 || 明天的天氣預報是晴天。
pregnancy || n. || 懷孕 || She felt tired during her pregnancy. || 她懷孕期間覺得很累。 || during pregnancy=懷孕期間 || Women should not drink alcohol ___ pregnancy. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 女性懷孕期間不應飲酒。
pregnant || adj. || 懷孕的 || She is six months pregnant. || 她懷孕六個月了。 || get pregnant=懷孕 || Please give your seat to pregnant women ___ the MRT. || on|in|at || on the MRT = 在捷運上。 || 在捷運上請讓座給孕婦。
presentation || n. || 報告；簡報 || I have to give a presentation tomorrow. || 我明天要上台報告。 || give a presentation=做簡報 || She gave a presentation ___ climate change in class. || on|in|at || a presentation on = 關於…的報告。 || 她在課堂上做了一個關於氣候變遷的報告。
preservation || n. || 保存；保護 || The preservation of old buildings is important. || 保存古老建築很重要。 || wildlife preservation=野生動物保育 || The preservation ___ historic buildings helps us remember the past. || of|for|to || the preservation of = …的保存。 || 保存歷史建築幫助我們記住過去。
preserve || v. || 保存 || We must preserve our traditions. || 我們必須保存傳統。 || preserve food=保存食物 || Salt was used ___ preserve food in the past. || to|for|so || be used to + 原形動詞 = 被用來…。 || 過去鹽被用來保存食物。
prevention || n. || 預防 || Prevention is better than cure. || 預防勝於治療。 || disease prevention=疾病預防 || Prevention is better ___ cure. || than|then|to || 比較級 better + than。 || 預防勝於治療。
prime || adj./n. || 首要的 || The prime minister gave a speech. || 首相發表了演說。 || prime minister=首相、總理 || Safety is our prime concern ___ the trip. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 安全是我們在旅途中的首要考量。
primitive || adj. || 原始的 || Primitive people lived in caves. || 原始人住在洞穴裡。 || primitive tools=原始工具 || Primitive people used stone tools ___ hunt animals. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 原始人使用石器來獵捕動物。
priority || n. || 優先事項 || Safety is our top priority. || 安全是我們的首要考量。 || top priority=第一優先 || Health should be your top priority, ___ it? || shouldn't|should|isn't || 肯定句（should）的附加問句用否定 shouldn't。 || 健康應該是你的第一優先，不是嗎？
privacy || n. || 隱私 || Everyone needs some privacy. || 每個人都需要一點隱私。 || invasion of privacy=侵犯隱私 || Reading someone's diary is an invasion ___ privacy. || of|to|for || an invasion of = 對…的侵犯。 || 看別人的日記是侵犯隱私。
privilege || n. || 特權；榮幸 || It's a privilege to meet you. || 能見到您是我的榮幸。 || have the privilege of=有幸… || It is a privilege ___ work with such talented people. || to|for|of || It is a privilege to + 原形動詞。 || 能和如此有才華的人共事是我的榮幸。
