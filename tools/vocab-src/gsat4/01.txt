# 學測 L4 01：abandon – aquarium
abandon || v. || 拋棄；放棄 || They had to abandon the car in the snow. || 他們不得不把車丟在雪地裡。 || abandon hope=放棄希望; abandoned pet=被棄養的寵物 || Many pets ___ abandoned by their owners every year. || are|is|be || 被動語態；pets 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 每年都有許多寵物被主人棄養。
absolute || adj. || 絕對的；完全的 || I have absolute trust in her. || 我完全信任她。 || absolute silence=一片寂靜 || There was absolute silence ___ the teacher walked in. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 老師走進來時全場一片寂靜。
absorb || v. || 吸收 || Plants absorb water through their roots. || 植物透過根部吸收水分。 || be absorbed in=專心於… || She was so absorbed ___ the book that she didn't hear me. || in|on|with || be absorbed in = 專注於…。 || 她看書看得太專心，沒聽到我說話。
abstract || adj. || 抽象的 || Love is an abstract idea. || 愛是一個抽象的概念。 || abstract art=抽象藝術 || Abstract art is hard ___ some people to understand. || for|of|to || 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 抽象藝術對某些人來說很難理解。
academic || adj. || 學術的；學業的 || She has an excellent academic record. || 她的學業成績非常優異。 || academic performance=學業表現 || His academic performance has improved ___ he started studying harder. || since|for|until || since + 過去時間子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 自從他更用功以來，學業表現進步了。
accent || n. || 口音 || He speaks English with a British accent. || 他說英文帶有英國口音。 || strong accent=濃厚的口音 || She speaks Chinese ___ a slight accent. || with|in|by || speak with an accent = 帶著口音說話。 || 她說中文帶有一點口音。
acceptance || n. || 接受；錄取 || She got a letter of acceptance from the university. || 她收到了大學的錄取通知。 || letter of acceptance=錄取通知 || She was thrilled ___ she received the letter of acceptance. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 她收到錄取通知時欣喜若狂。
access || n./v. || 使用權；接近 || Students have access to the library. || 學生可以使用圖書館。 || have access to=可以使用… || Not everyone has access ___ the Internet. || to|for|of || have access to = 可以取得／使用…。 || 並非每個人都能上網。
accidental || adj. || 意外的 || The fire was accidental. || 那場火災是意外。 || accidental death=意外死亡 || The police concluded ___ the fire was accidental. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 concluded 的受詞。 || 警方判定那場火災是意外。
accompany || v. || 陪同；伴隨 || Children must be accompanied by an adult. || 兒童必須由成人陪同。 || accompany sb=陪伴某人 || Children under 12 must ___ accompanied by an adult. || be|is|been || must 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 十二歲以下兒童必須由成人陪同。
accountant || n. || 會計師 || My mother is an accountant. || 我媽媽是會計師。 || certified accountant=執業會計師 || My aunt works ___ an accountant for a big company. || as|like|for || work as + 職業 = 擔任…。 || 我阿姨在一家大公司擔任會計師。
accuracy || n. || 準確性 || Accuracy is important in math. || 數學講求準確。 || with accuracy=準確地 || The machine can measure the temperature ___ great accuracy. || with|in|by || with accuracy = 準確地。 || 這台機器能非常準確地測量溫度。
accuse || v. || 指控 || He was accused of stealing. || 他被控偷竊。 || accuse sb of=指控某人… || The man was accused ___ stealing a car. || of|for|with || accuse sb of + V-ing = 指控某人做…。 || 那名男子被控偷車。
acid || n./adj. || 酸 || Lemons contain acid. || 檸檬含有酸。 || acid rain=酸雨 || Acid rain is caused ___ air pollution. || by|with|from of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 酸雨是空氣污染造成的。
acknowledge || v. || 承認；致謝 || He acknowledged his mistake. || 他承認了自己的錯誤。 || acknowledge a mistake=承認錯誤 || He finally acknowledged ___ he had made a mistake. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 acknowledged 的受詞。 || 他終於承認自己犯了錯。
acquaintance || n. || 熟人；認識 || He is just an acquaintance, not a close friend. || 他只是點頭之交，不是好朋友。 || make the acquaintance of=結識… || She is an old acquaintance of mine ___ I met in college. || whom|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當受詞，用 whom。 || 她是我在大學認識的老朋友。
acquire || v. || 獲得；習得 || Children acquire language quickly. || 兒童學習語言很快。 || acquire a skill=習得技能 || It takes years ___ acquire a new language. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 學會一種新語言需要好幾年。
adapt || v. || 適應；改編 || She adapted quickly to the new school. || 她很快就適應了新學校。 || adapt to=適應… || It took him a month ___ adapt to life in Canada. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 他花了一個月才適應加拿大的生活。
addict || n./v. || 成癮者；使上癮 || He is a video game addict. || 他沉迷於電玩。 || be addicted to=對…上癮 || Many teenagers are addicted ___ their phones. || to|with|on || be addicted to = 對…上癮。 || 很多青少年對手機成癮。
adequate || adj. || 足夠的 || Make sure you get adequate sleep. || 確保你有充足的睡眠。 || adequate sleep=充足的睡眠 || Adequate sleep is necessary ___ good health. || for|of|to be || be necessary for = 對…是必要的。 || 充足的睡眠是健康的必要條件。
admirable || adj. || 令人欽佩的 || Her courage is admirable. || 她的勇氣令人欽佩。 || admirable quality=令人欽佩的特質 || It is admirable ___ him to help the poor. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 他幫助窮人令人欽佩。
admiration || n. || 欽佩 || I have great admiration for her. || 我非常欽佩她。 || admiration for=對…的欽佩 || The audience looked at the dancer ___ admiration. || with|in|by || with admiration = 欽佩地。 || 觀眾欽佩地看著那位舞者。
admission || n. || 入場；入學許可 || Admission is free. || 免費入場。 || admission fee=入場費 || Admission to the museum is free ___ students. || for|to|of || free for sb = 對某人免費。 || 學生免費入館。
adopt || v. || 採用；收養 || They adopted a baby girl. || 他們收養了一個女嬰。 || adopt a child=收養孩子; adopt a plan=採用計畫 || The dog ___ we adopted from the shelter is very friendly. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 adopted 的受詞。 || 我們從收容所領養的狗非常友善。
agency || n. || 代理機構 || She works for a travel agency. || 她在一家旅行社工作。 || travel agency=旅行社 || We booked our trip ___ a travel agency. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 我們透過旅行社訂了行程。
agent || n. || 代理人；經紀人 || The singer's agent arranged the concert. || 那位歌手的經紀人安排了演唱會。 || travel agent=旅行社職員; secret agent=特務 || The agent ___ booked our flight was very helpful. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 幫我們訂機票的旅行社職員很幫忙。
aggressive || adj. || 有侵略性的；積極的 || The dog became aggressive. || 那隻狗變得具攻擊性。 || aggressive behavior=攻擊行為 || Some dogs become aggressive ___ they feel threatened. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 有些狗在感到威脅時會變得具攻擊性。
agreeable || adj. || 令人愉快的；同意的 || The weather is agreeable today. || 今天天氣很宜人。 || agreeable to=同意… || Is the plan agreeable ___ everyone? || to|with|for || be agreeable to = 為…所接受。 || 這個計畫大家都能接受嗎？
alcohol || n. || 酒精 || Don't drink alcohol and drive. || 酒後不要開車。 || drink alcohol=喝酒 || People under 18 are not allowed ___ alcohol. || to buy|buying|buy || be allowed to + 原形動詞。 || 未滿十八歲者不准購買酒精飲料。
alert || adj./v. || 警覺的；警告 || Stay alert while driving. || 開車時要保持警覺。 || stay alert=保持警覺 || Drivers need to stay alert ___ they drive at night. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 駕駛在夜間開車時需要保持警覺。
allowance || n. || 零用錢；津貼 || I get an allowance every week. || 我每週都有零用錢。 || weekly allowance=每週零用錢 || She saves part of her allowance ___ month. || every|each of|all || every + 單數名詞 = 每…。 || 她每個月存下一部分零用錢。
alternative || adj./n. || 替代的；選擇 || We have no alternative. || 我們別無選擇。 || alternative energy=替代能源 || Solar power is a clean alternative ___ fossil fuels. || to|of|for with || an alternative to = …的替代品。 || 太陽能是化石燃料的乾淨替代方案。
amateur || adj./n. || 業餘的 || He is an amateur photographer. || 他是業餘攝影師。 || amateur athlete=業餘運動員 || Although he is ___ amateur, he takes great photos. || an|a|the || amateur 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 雖然他是業餘的，但拍的照片很棒。
ambiguous || adj. || 模稜兩可的 || His answer was ambiguous. || 他的回答模稜兩可。 || ambiguous meaning=含糊的意思 || The sentence is ambiguous, ___ it can be understood in two ways. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這個句子模稜兩可，可以有兩種理解。
ambitious || adj. || 有抱負的 || She is an ambitious student. || 她是個有抱負的學生。 || ambitious plan=雄心勃勃的計畫 || He is so ambitious ___ he wants to start his own company at 20. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他雄心勃勃到二十歲就想創業。
analysis || n. || 分析 || The analysis shows interesting results. || 分析顯示了有趣的結果。 || data analysis=資料分析 || A careful analysis ___ the data is needed. || of|for|to || an analysis of = 對…的分析。 || 需要仔細分析這些資料。
analyze || v. || 分析 || Scientists analyze the data. || 科學家分析資料。 || analyze data=分析資料 || The data ___ analyzed by a team of experts last week. || was|were being|is || 被動語態；data 在此視為不可數，過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這些資料上週由專家團隊進行了分析。
ancestor || n. || 祖先 || We honor our ancestors during Tomb Sweeping Day. || 我們在清明節祭祖。 || worship ancestors=祭祖 || People visit the graves of ___ ancestors on Tomb Sweeping Day. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 ancestors 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 人們在清明節去掃祖先的墓。
anniversary || n. || 週年紀念 || Today is our wedding anniversary. || 今天是我們的結婚週年紀念日。 || wedding anniversary=結婚紀念日 || The school will celebrate ___ 100th anniversary next year. || its|it's|their || the school 單數，所有格用 its。 || 學校明年將慶祝創校一百週年。
annoy || v. || 使惱怒 || The noise annoyed me. || 那噪音讓我很煩。 || be annoyed with=對…感到惱怒 || I was annoyed ___ him for being late again. || with|of|at to || be annoyed with sb = 對某人感到惱怒。 || 他又遲到了，讓我很生氣。
annual || adj. || 每年的 || The school has an annual sports day. || 學校每年舉辦一次運動會。 || annual event=年度活動 || The annual festival ___ held in October every year. || is|are|was being || 被動語態；習慣性用現在式 is + p.p.。 || 這個年度節慶每年十月舉行。
anxiety || n. || 焦慮 || Exams cause anxiety for many students. || 考試讓很多學生焦慮。 || feel anxiety=感到焦慮 || Exercise can help reduce stress ___ anxiety. || and|but|or || 兩者並列用 and。 || 運動可以幫助減輕壓力和焦慮。
apology || n. || 道歉 || Please accept my apology. || 請接受我的道歉。 || make an apology=道歉; owe sb an apology=欠某人一個道歉 || I think you owe her ___ apology. || an|a|the || apology 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我認為你欠她一個道歉。
apparent || adj. || 明顯的 || It was apparent that she was tired. || 很明顯她累了。 || for no apparent reason=莫名其妙地 || It was apparent ___ nobody wanted to go. || that|what|which || It is apparent that… = 很明顯…。 || 很明顯沒有人想去。

application || n. || 申請；應用 || I sent my application yesterday. || 我昨天寄出了申請書。 || application form=申請表 || Please fill out the application form ___ black ink. || in|with|by || in + 墨水顏色 = 用…寫。 || 請用黑色墨水填寫申請表。
