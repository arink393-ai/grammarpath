# 學測 L4 06：defense – disturb
defense || n. || 防禦 || The team has a strong defense. || 這支隊伍的防守很強。 || self-defense=自衛 || She learned karate for self-defense ___ she often walks home alone at night. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 她學空手道防身，因為她常晚上獨自走路回家。
defensible || adj. || 可防禦的；站得住腳的 || His argument is defensible. || 他的論點站得住腳。 || defensible position=可防守的位置 || The castle was built on a hill ___ it would be easily defensible. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 城堡建在山丘上，以便容易防守。
defensive || adj. || 防禦的 || He became defensive when I asked. || 我問的時候他變得很防衛。 || defensive player=防守球員 || He got defensive ___ I asked about his grades. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 我問起他的成績時，他變得很防衛。
definite || adj. || 明確的 || I need a definite answer. || 我需要一個明確的答覆。 || definite answer=明確的答覆 || Please give me ___ definite answer by tomorrow. || a|an|the || definite 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 請在明天之前給我明確的答覆。
delicate || adj. || 精緻的；脆弱的 || This vase is very delicate. || 這個花瓶很脆弱。 || delicate skin=細緻的肌膚 || The vase is so delicate ___ you must handle it with care. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這個花瓶非常脆弱，你必須小心拿。
delight || n./v. || 高興 || To my delight, I passed the test. || 令我高興的是，我通過了考試。 || to one's delight=令某人高興的是 || ___ my delight, my favorite singer came to Taiwan. || To|For|With || to one's delight = 令某人高興的是。 || 令我高興的是，我最喜歡的歌手來台灣了。
delightful || adj. || 令人愉快的 || We had a delightful evening. || 我們度過了一個愉快的夜晚。 || delightful time=愉快的時光 || It was ___ delightful evening. || a|an|the || delightful 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 那是一個愉快的夜晚。
demand || n./v. || 要求；需求 || There is a high demand for this product. || 這項產品的需求很高。 || in demand=需求量大 || English teachers are in great demand ___ Asia. || in|on|at || 在某地區用 in。 || 英文老師在亞洲需求量很大。
demonstrate || v. || 示範；證明 || The teacher demonstrated the experiment. || 老師示範了實驗。 || demonstrate how to V=示範如何… || The teacher demonstrated ___ to use the machine. || how|what|which || how to + 原形動詞。 || 老師示範了如何使用這台機器。
demonstration || n. || 示範；示威 || The chef gave a cooking demonstration. || 主廚做了一場烹飪示範。 || give a demonstration=示範 || Thousands of people joined the demonstration ___ the new law. || against|for of|with || against = 反對…。 || 數千人參加了反對新法的示威活動。
dense || adj. || 密集的；濃密的 || The forest is very dense. || 森林非常茂密。 || dense fog=濃霧 || The fog was ___ dense that we couldn't see the road. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 霧濃到我們看不見路。
depart || v. || 出發 || The train departs at 9:00. || 火車九點出發。 || depart from=從…出發 || The flight departs ___ Gate 5. || from|at of|to || depart from = 從…出發。 || 班機從五號登機門出發。
departure || n. || 出發 || The departure time is 10 a.m. || 出發時間是上午十點。 || departure time=出發時間 || Please arrive two hours ___ departure. || before|after|since || 出發「之前」抵達，用 before。 || 請在出發前兩小時抵達。
dependent || adj. || 依賴的 || Babies are dependent on their parents. || 嬰兒依賴父母。 || be dependent on=依賴… || Many young people are too dependent ___ their parents. || on|to|of || be dependent on = 依賴…。 || 很多年輕人太依賴父母了。
depression || n. || 沮喪；憂鬱症 || He suffered from depression. || 他罹患憂鬱症。 || suffer from depression=罹患憂鬱症 || People ___ suffer from depression should get professional help. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 罹患憂鬱症的人應該尋求專業協助。
deserve || v. || 應得 || You deserve a break. || 你應該好好休息一下。 || deserve to V=應得… || She worked hard, so she deserves ___ the prize. || to win|winning|win || deserve to + 原形動詞。 || 她很努力，所以她應該得獎。
desperate || adj. || 絕望的；極度渴望的 || He was desperate for money. || 他急需用錢。 || be desperate for=極度需要… || The people in the flooded area are desperate ___ food and water. || for|of|to || be desperate for = 極度需要…。 || 災區的人們急需食物和水。
despite || prep. || 儘管 || Despite the rain, we went out. || 儘管下雨，我們還是出去了。 || despite=儘管 || Despite ___ tired, she kept working. || being|be|was || despite + 名詞/動名詞；be 改為 being。 || 儘管很累，她還是繼續工作。
destruction || n. || 破壞 || The earthquake caused great destruction. || 地震造成嚴重的破壞。 || destruction of the environment=環境破壞 || The destruction ___ the rainforest is a serious problem. || of|for|to || the destruction of = …的破壞。 || 雨林的破壞是個嚴重的問題。
detective || n. || 偵探 || Sherlock Holmes is a famous detective. || 福爾摩斯是有名的偵探。 || detective story=偵探小說 || The detective, ___ solved many cases, is very famous. || who has|which has|who have || 非限定關係子句修飾人，用 who；主詞單數用 has。 || 那位破了許多案件的偵探非常有名。
determination || n. || 決心 || With determination, you can succeed. || 有了決心，你就能成功。 || with determination=堅定地 || She finished the marathon ___ great determination. || with|in|by || with determination = 堅定地。 || 她憑著堅強的決心跑完了馬拉松。
device || n. || 裝置 || A phone is a useful device. || 手機是很有用的裝置。 || electronic device=電子裝置 || Please turn off all electronic devices ___ the plane takes off. || before|after|since || 起飛「之前」關閉，用 before。 || 飛機起飛前請關閉所有電子裝置。
devise || v. || 設計；想出 || They devised a new plan. || 他們想出了一個新計畫。 || devise a plan=擬定計畫 || The team devised a new way ___ reduce waste. || to|for|of || a way to V = 做…的方法。 || 團隊想出了減少浪費的新方法。
devote || v. || 奉獻 || She devoted her life to helping others. || 她一生奉獻於助人。 || devote oneself to=致力於… || He devoted himself ___ teaching. || to|for|with || devote oneself to = 致力於…。 || 他獻身於教育。
dew || n. || 露水 || The grass is wet with dew. || 草地上沾滿了露水。 || morning dew=晨露 || The grass was wet ___ dew in the morning. || with|of|by || be wet with = 被…弄濕。 || 早上草地被露水沾濕了。
diagram || n. || 圖表 || Look at the diagram on page 5. || 看第五頁的圖表。 || draw a diagram=畫圖表 || The diagram shows ___ the machine works. || how|what|which || 名詞子句 how… = …如何運作。 || 這張圖表顯示這台機器如何運作。
differ || v. || 不同 || Our opinions differ. || 我們的意見不同。 || differ from=與…不同 || My opinion differs ___ yours. || from|to|with of || differ from = 與…不同。 || 我的意見和你的不同。
digest || v./n. || 消化；摘要 || Some foods are hard to digest. || 有些食物很難消化。 || digest food=消化食物 || Fatty food is hard ___ digest. || to|for|of || 形容詞 + to V。 || 油膩的食物很難消化。
digital || adj. || 數位的 || We live in a digital age. || 我們生活在數位時代。 || digital camera=數位相機 || Digital books are becoming ___ popular. || more and more|most and most|much and much || 比較級 and 比較級 = 越來越…。 || 電子書越來越受歡迎。
dignity || n. || 尊嚴 || Everyone deserves to be treated with dignity. || 每個人都應受到有尊嚴的對待。 || with dignity=有尊嚴地 || Everyone should be treated ___ dignity and respect. || with|in|by || with dignity = 有尊嚴地。 || 每個人都應該受到尊重與有尊嚴的對待。
diligence || n. || 勤勉 || Her diligence paid off. || 她的勤奮得到了回報。 || with diligence=勤奮地 || Her success is the result ___ her diligence. || of|for|from || the result of = …的結果。 || 她的成功是勤奮的結果。
diligent || adj. || 勤勉的 || He is a diligent student. || 他是個勤奮的學生。 || diligent worker=勤奮的員工 || She is the ___ diligent student in our class. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 她是我們班最勤奮的學生。
diploma || n. || 文憑 || She received her high school diploma. || 她拿到了高中文憑。 || high school diploma=高中文憑 || You need a high school diploma ___ apply for this job. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 你需要高中文憑才能應徵這份工作。
diplomat || n. || 外交官 || Her father is a diplomat. || 她父親是外交官。 || career diplomat=職業外交官 || As a diplomat, he has lived ___ many countries. || in|on|at || 住在國家用 in。 || 身為外交官，他住過很多國家。
disability || n. || 殘疾 || People with disabilities need our help. || 身心障礙者需要我們的幫助。 || people with disabilities=身心障礙者 || The building has special facilities ___ people with disabilities. || for|to|of || for + 人 = 為…而設。 || 這棟建築為身心障礙者設有特殊設施。
disadvantage || n. || 缺點；不利條件 || Every plan has advantages and disadvantages. || 每個計畫都有優缺點。 || at a disadvantage=處於劣勢 || One disadvantage of living in the city ___ the high cost of living. || is|are|be || 主詞 One disadvantage 為單數，用 is。 || 住在城市的一個缺點是生活費很高。
disaster || n. || 災難 || The earthquake was a terrible disaster. || 那場地震是一場可怕的災難。 || natural disaster=天災 || Typhoons and earthquakes are natural disasters ___ often happen in Taiwan. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（disasters）。 || 颱風和地震是台灣常發生的天然災害。
discipline || n./v. || 紀律 || The school has strict discipline. || 這所學校紀律嚴格。 || self-discipline=自律 || Self-discipline is the key ___ success. || to|of|for || the key to = …的關鍵。 || 自律是成功的關鍵。
disguise || v./n. || 偽裝 || He disguised himself as a doctor. || 他偽裝成醫生。 || in disguise=偽裝的 || The thief disguised himself ___ a police officer. || as|like|for || disguise oneself as = 偽裝成…。 || 小偷偽裝成警察。
disgust || n./v. || 厭惡 || She looked at the food with disgust. || 她厭惡地看著那些食物。 || in disgust=厭惡地 || He left the room ___ disgust. || in|with|by || in disgust = 厭惡地。 || 他厭惡地離開了房間。
dismiss || v. || 解散；開除 || The teacher dismissed the class. || 老師宣布下課。 || dismiss a class=下課 || The class was dismissed ___ the bell rang. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 鐘聲響時老師宣布下課。
disorder || n. || 混亂；失調 || The room was in disorder. || 房間一片凌亂。 || eating disorder=飲食失調 || After the party, the house was ___ complete disorder. || in|on|at || in disorder = 凌亂。 || 派對過後，房子一片凌亂。
dispute || n./v. || 爭論；糾紛 || There is a dispute over the land. || 這塊土地有糾紛。 || dispute over=對…的爭議 || The two countries have a dispute ___ the island. || over|to|with || a dispute over = 對…的爭議。 || 兩國對這座島嶼有領土爭議。
distinct || adj. || 明顯不同的 || The two languages are distinct. || 這兩種語言截然不同。 || be distinct from=與…不同 || Taiwanese culture is distinct ___ other Asian cultures. || from|to|with || be distinct from = 與…有明顯不同。 || 台灣文化與其他亞洲文化截然不同。
distinguish || v. || 區別 || Can you distinguish between the twins? || 你分得出那對雙胞胎嗎？ || distinguish A from B=區分 A 和 B || Can you distinguish right ___ wrong? || from|to|with || distinguish A from B = 區分 A 和 B。 || 你能分辨是非嗎？
distinguished || adj. || 傑出的 || He is a distinguished scientist. || 他是一位傑出的科學家。 || distinguished guest=貴賓 || She is distinguished ___ her work in medicine. || for|by|with of || be distinguished for = 以…著稱。 || 她以醫學上的成就而著稱。
distribute || v. || 分配；分發 || The teacher distributed the test papers. || 老師發下了考卷。 || distribute food=分發食物 || Food was distributed ___ the people in need. || to|for|with || distribute A to B = 把 A 分發給 B。 || 食物被分發給有需要的人。
distribution || n. || 分配 || The distribution of wealth is unequal. || 財富分配不均。 || distribution of=…的分配 || The distribution ___ food was organized by volunteers. || of|for|to || the distribution of = …的分配。 || 食物的分發是由志工組織的。
district || n. || 區 || Da'an is a district in Taipei. || 大安是台北的一個區。 || school district=學區 || Xinyi District is famous ___ Taipei 101. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 信義區以台北 101 聞名。
disturb || v. || 打擾 || Please don't disturb me. || 請別打擾我。 || do not disturb=請勿打擾 || Sorry to disturb you, but could you help me ___ this? || with|for|to || help sb with sth = 幫某人做某事。 || 抱歉打擾你，可以幫我處理這個嗎？
