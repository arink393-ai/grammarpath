# 學測 L4 05：contrary – defend
contrary || n./adj. || 相反 || Contrary to what I expected, the test was easy. || 和我預期的相反，考試很簡單。 || on the contrary=相反地; contrary to=與…相反 || Contrary ___ popular belief, bats are not blind. || to|with|of || contrary to = 與…相反。 || 與一般看法相反，蝙蝠並不是瞎子。
contrast || n./v. || 對比 || There is a big contrast between the two cities. || 這兩座城市形成強烈對比。 || in contrast=相較之下 || ___ contrast to his brother, he is very quiet. || In|On|At || in contrast to = 與…對比。 || 和他哥哥相比，他非常安靜。
contribute || v. || 貢獻；導致 || Everyone contributed ideas. || 每個人都貢獻了點子。 || contribute to=促成；對…有貢獻 || Smoking contributes ___ many health problems. || to|for|with || contribute to = 促成、導致…。 || 抽菸導致許多健康問題。
contribution || n. || 貢獻 || She made a great contribution to science. || 她對科學做出了巨大的貢獻。 || make a contribution to=對…做出貢獻 || He made an important contribution ___ the project. || to|for|of || make a contribution to = 對…做出貢獻。 || 他對這個計畫做出了重要貢獻。
convenience || n. || 便利 || For your convenience, we open 24 hours. || 為了您的方便，我們二十四小時營業。 || convenience store=便利商店; for one's convenience=為了…的方便 || Please reply ___ your earliest convenience. || at|in|on || at one's earliest convenience = 盡早（在方便時）。 || 請在方便時盡早回覆。
convention || n. || 大會；慣例 || The comic convention attracted thousands of fans. || 漫畫博覽會吸引了數千名粉絲。 || social convention=社會慣例 || The convention ___ held in the Taipei World Trade Center every year. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The convention 用 is + p.p.。 || 這個大會每年在台北世貿中心舉行。
conventional || adj. || 傳統的 || She doesn't like conventional ideas. || 她不喜歡傳統的觀念。 || conventional wisdom=一般看法 || Online learning is different ___ conventional classroom learning. || from|to be|with || be different from = 與…不同。 || 線上學習和傳統的課堂學習不同。
converse || v. || 交談 || They conversed in English. || 他們用英文交談。 || converse with=與…交談 || She conversed ___ the foreign guests in French. || with|to|at || converse with = 與…交談。 || 她用法文和外國賓客交談。
convey || v. || 傳達 || Words cannot convey my feelings. || 言語無法傳達我的感受。 || convey a message=傳達訊息 || It's hard to convey feelings ___ words. || in|with|by || in words = 用言語。 || 很難用言語傳達感受。
convince || v. || 說服；使相信 || I convinced him to come. || 我說服他來了。 || be convinced that=確信… || I'm convinced ___ he is telling the truth. || that|what|which || be convinced that… = 確信…。 || 我確信他說的是實話。
cooperate || v. || 合作 || We need to cooperate. || 我們需要合作。 || cooperate with=與…合作 || The two schools cooperated ___ each other on the project. || with|to|for || cooperate with = 與…合作。 || 兩所學校在這個計畫上互相合作。
cooperation || n. || 合作 || Thank you for your cooperation. || 謝謝你的合作。 || in cooperation with=與…合作 || The event was held in cooperation ___ the city government. || with|to|by || in cooperation with = 與…合作。 || 這個活動是與市政府合作舉辦的。
cooperative || adj. || 合作的 || The students were very cooperative. || 學生們非常配合。 || cooperative attitude=合作的態度 || The students were ___ cooperative that the project finished early. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 學生們非常配合，專案提早完成了。
cope || v. || 應付 || How do you cope with stress? || 你如何應付壓力？ || cope with=應付… || It's hard ___ cope with so much homework. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 要應付這麼多功課很難。
copper || n. || 銅 || The wire is made of copper. || 這條電線是銅做的。 || copper wire=銅線 || Copper is used ___ make electrical wires. || to|for|so || be used to + 原形動詞 = 被用來…。 || 銅被用來製造電線。
cord || n. || 繩；電線 || The phone cord is too short. || 電話線太短了。 || power cord=電源線 || Be careful not to trip ___ the cord. || over|on|in || trip over = 被…絆倒。 || 小心別被電線絆倒。
correspond || v. || 符合；通信 || The results correspond with our expectations. || 結果符合我們的預期。 || correspond with=與…相符；與…通信 || His actions don't correspond ___ his words. || with|to be|for || correspond with = 與…相符。 || 他的行為與言語不符。
costume || n. || 服裝；戲服 || She wore a witch costume for Halloween. || 她萬聖節穿巫婆裝。 || Halloween costume=萬聖節服裝 || The kids dressed up ___ costumes for the party. || in|with|on || dress up in = 穿上…打扮。 || 孩子們穿上戲服參加派對。
cottage || n. || 小屋 || They live in a small cottage. || 他們住在一間小屋裡。 || country cottage=鄉間小屋 || The cottage ___ we stayed in was near a lake. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 stayed in 的受詞。 || 我們住的小屋在湖邊。
council || n. || 會議；委員會 || She is on the student council. || 她是學生會的成員。 || student council=學生會 || She was elected ___ the student council last year. || to|in|at || be elected to + 組織 = 當選為…的成員。 || 她去年當選學生會成員。
counter || n. || 櫃台 || Pay at the counter. || 在櫃台付款。 || check-in counter=報到櫃台 || Please pay ___ the counter. || at|on|in || 在櫃台（地點）用 at。 || 請到櫃台付款。
courageous || adj. || 勇敢的 || It was courageous of her to speak up. || 她勇於發聲真是勇敢。 || courageous act=英勇的行為 || It was courageous ___ him to save the child. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 他救了那個孩子真是勇敢。
courtesy || n. || 禮貌 || Please show courtesy to others. || 請對別人有禮貌。 || common courtesy=基本禮貌 || It's common courtesy ___ say "thank you." || to|for|of || It is + 名詞 + to V。 || 說「謝謝」是基本禮貌。
coward || n. || 懦夫 || Don't be a coward. || 別當懦夫。 || coward=膽小鬼 || He called me a coward ___ I didn't dare to jump. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 他叫我膽小鬼，因為我不敢跳。
crack || n./v. || 裂縫；破裂 || There is a crack in the glass. || 玻璃上有一道裂縫。 || crack a joke=說笑話 || There is a crack ___ the wall. || in|on|at || 在牆「裡」的裂縫用 in。 || 牆上有一道裂縫。
craft || n. || 工藝 || She sells handmade crafts. || 她賣手工藝品。 || handicraft=手工藝 || The market sells crafts ___ by local artists. || made|making|makes || 過去分詞 made 表被動（被製作的）。 || 這個市集販售當地藝術家手作的工藝品。
creation || n. || 創造；作品 || This dish is the chef's own creation. || 這道菜是主廚自己的創作。 || the creation of=…的創造 || The creation ___ the website took three months. || of|for|to || the creation of = …的創建。 || 建置這個網站花了三個月。
creativity || n. || 創造力 || Art encourages creativity. || 藝術能激發創造力。 || show creativity=展現創意 || Art classes help students develop ___ creativity. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 creativity 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 美術課幫助學生培養創造力。
creep || v. || 爬行；躡手躡腳 || Cats creep quietly toward birds. || 貓會悄悄地接近鳥。 || creep up=悄悄靠近 || He tried to creep into the house ___ waking his parents. || without|with|within || without + V-ing = 不…。 || 他試著悄悄溜進屋裡，不吵醒父母。
critic || n. || 評論家 || The movie critic loved the film. || 那位影評人很喜歡這部電影。 || film critic=影評人 || The movie was praised ___ critics. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這部電影受到影評人的讚揚。
critical || adj. || 批判的；關鍵的 || This is a critical moment. || 這是關鍵時刻。 || critical thinking=批判性思考 || Critical thinking is an important skill ___ students. || for|to|of || important for sb = 對某人很重要。 || 批判性思考對學生來說是重要的技能。
criticism || n. || 批評 || He can't take criticism. || 他無法接受批評。 || accept criticism=接受批評 || The plan received a lot of criticism ___ the public. || from|of|by || criticism from = 來自…的批評。 || 這個計畫受到大眾許多批評。
criticize || v. || 批評 || Don't criticize others. || 不要批評別人。 || criticize sb for=因…批評某人 || The government was criticized ___ its slow response. || for|of|with || criticize sb for sth = 因某事批評某人。 || 政府因反應遲緩而受到批評。
cruelty || n. || 殘忍 || Cruelty to animals is a crime. || 虐待動物是犯罪。 || cruelty to animals=虐待動物 || Cruelty ___ animals should be punished. || to|for|of || cruelty to = 對…的殘忍。 || 虐待動物的行為應該受到懲罰。
crunchy || adj. || 酥脆的 || I like crunchy cookies. || 我喜歡酥脆的餅乾。 || crunchy snack=酥脆的零食 || The fried chicken is crunchy on the outside ___ soft on the inside. || and|but|or || 兩個特點並列用 and。 || 炸雞外酥內嫩。
crush || v./n. || 壓碎；迷戀 || Crush the garlic. || 把大蒜壓碎。 || have a crush on=暗戀某人 || She has had a crush ___ him since middle school. || on|with|for || have a crush on sb = 暗戀某人。 || 她從國中就暗戀他。
cube || n. || 立方體；塊 || Put some ice cubes in the glass. || 在杯子裡放一些冰塊。 || ice cube=冰塊; sugar cube=方糖 || Can I have a few ice cubes ___ my drink? || in|on|at || 放在飲料裡用 in。 || 可以在我的飲料裡加幾顆冰塊嗎？
cue || n. || 提示 || The actor waited for his cue. || 演員等待出場提示。 || on cue=恰好在這時 || The actor walked onto the stage ___ he heard his cue. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 演員聽到提示時走上舞台。
cunning || adj. || 狡猾的 || The fox is cunning. || 狐狸很狡猾。 || cunning plan=狡猾的計畫 || The cunning fox tricked the crow ___ dropping the cheese. || into|in|to || trick sb into + V-ing = 騙某人做…。 || 狡猾的狐狸騙烏鴉把起司掉下來。
curiosity || n. || 好奇心 || Curiosity killed the cat. || 好奇心會害死貓。 || out of curiosity=出於好奇 || I asked the question out ___ curiosity. || of|from|for || out of curiosity = 出於好奇。 || 我出於好奇問了這個問題。
curl || v./n. || 捲曲 || She has curly hair. She curls it every morning. || 她每天早上都把頭髮捲一捲。 || curl up=蜷縮 || The cat curled up ___ the sofa and fell asleep. || on|in|at || 在沙發上用 on。 || 貓蜷縮在沙發上睡著了。
curse || n./v. || 詛咒 || The witch put a curse on the princess. || 女巫對公主下了詛咒。 || put a curse on=對…下咒 || The princess was under a curse ___ made her sleep for 100 years. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a curse）。 || 公主中了一個讓她沉睡百年的詛咒。
curve || n./v. || 曲線；彎道 || Slow down at the curve. || 在彎道減速。 || sharp curve=急彎 || Drive slowly ___ there is a sharp curve ahead. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 開慢一點，因為前面有急轉彎。
cushion || n. || 靠墊 || She put a cushion on the chair. || 她在椅子上放了一個靠墊。 || sofa cushion=沙發靠墊 || She sat ___ a soft cushion on the floor. || on|in|at || 坐在墊子上用 on。 || 她坐在地板上的軟墊上。
damp || adj. || 潮濕的 || The clothes are still damp. || 衣服還是濕濕的。 || damp weather=潮濕的天氣 || The clothes are still damp ___ it rained all day. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 衣服還是濕的，因為下了一整天雨。
deadline || n. || 截止期限 || The deadline is Friday. || 截止日期是星期五。 || meet a deadline=趕上期限 || You must hand in the report ___ the deadline. || before|after|since || 截止「之前」交，用 before。 || 你必須在截止日期前交報告。
declare || v. || 宣布；申報 || The government declared a state of emergency. || 政府宣布進入緊急狀態。 || declare war=宣戰 || The typhoon day off ___ declared by the mayor last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 市長昨晚宣布了颱風假。
decoration || n. || 裝飾 || We put up Christmas decorations. || 我們掛上聖誕裝飾。 || Christmas decorations=聖誕裝飾 || The kids helped us put ___ the decorations. || up|on|off || put up = 張掛、布置。 || 孩子們幫我們掛上裝飾品。
defeat || v./n. || 擊敗 || Our team defeated them 5 to 2. || 我們隊以五比二擊敗他們。 || suffer a defeat=吃敗仗 || The champion ___ defeated in the first round. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 衛冕冠軍在第一輪就被擊敗了。
defend || v. || 防衛；辯護 || The soldiers defended the city. || 士兵們保衛這座城市。 || defend oneself=自衛 || She learned karate ___ defend herself. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她學空手道來保護自己。
