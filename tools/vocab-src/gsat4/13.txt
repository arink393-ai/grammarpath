# 學測 L4 13：maturity – negotiate
maturity || n. || 成熟 || She showed great maturity for her age. || 以她的年紀來說，她表現得很成熟。 || emotional maturity=情緒成熟 || She showed a lot of maturity ___ handling the problem. || in|on|at || in + V-ing = 在…方面。 || 她在處理這個問題時表現得很成熟。
maximum || adj./n. || 最大的；最大量 || The maximum speed is 100 km/h. || 最高速限是時速一百公里。 || at the maximum=最多 || The elevator can carry a maximum ___ 12 people. || of|for|in || a maximum of = 最多…。 || 這台電梯最多可載十二人。
measurable || adj. || 可測量的 || The results are measurable. || 結果是可以衡量的。 || measurable goal=可衡量的目標 || Set goals ___ are clear and measurable. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（goals）。 || 設定清楚且可衡量的目標。
measure || n./v. || 措施；測量 || The government took measures to stop the virus. || 政府採取措施阻止病毒傳播。 || take measures=採取措施 || The government took measures ___ prevent the spread of the disease. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 政府採取措施以防止疾病擴散。
mechanic || n. || 技工 || The mechanic fixed my car. || 技工修好了我的車。 || car mechanic=汽車技師 || I took my car to a mechanic ___ it made a strange noise. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 我把車送去給技工修，因為它發出奇怪的聲音。
mechanical || adj. || 機械的 || The plane had a mechanical problem. || 飛機出現了機械故障。 || mechanical problem=機械故障 || The flight was delayed ___ a mechanical problem. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 班機因機械故障而延誤。
memorable || adj. || 難忘的 || It was a memorable trip. || 那是一趟難忘的旅行。 || memorable experience=難忘的經驗 || It was the ___ memorable trip I have ever taken. || most|more|much || 最高級 the most + 形容詞。 || 那是我去過最難忘的旅行。
memorial || n./adj. || 紀念館；紀念的 || We visited the Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall. || 我們參觀了中正紀念堂。 || memorial hall=紀念館 || The memorial ___ built to remember the victims of the war. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這座紀念碑是為了紀念戰爭的受難者而建的。
memorize || v. || 記住 || I need to memorize these words. || 我需要背這些單字。 || memorize words=背單字 || It's easier to memorize words ___ you use them in sentences. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你把單字用在句子裡，會比較容易記住。
merchant || n. || 商人 || The merchant sold silk. || 那位商人賣絲綢。 || wealthy merchant=富商 || In the past, merchants traveled ___ the Silk Road to trade goods. || along|across|through || travel along = 沿著…旅行。 || 過去商人沿著絲路做貿易。
mercy || n. || 仁慈；憐憫 || Show some mercy. || 發發慈悲吧。 || at the mercy of=任由…擺布 || The small boat was ___ the mercy of the storm. || at|in|on || at the mercy of = 任由…擺布。 || 那艘小船任由暴風雨擺布。
mere || adj. || 僅僅的 || She was a mere child at the time. || 她當時只不過是個孩子。 || a mere=僅僅 || The mere thought of the exam makes me ___. || nervous|nervously|nerve || make + 受詞 + 形容詞。 || 光是想到考試就讓我緊張。
merit || n. || 優點 || The plan has many merits. || 這個計畫有很多優點。 || merits and demerits=優缺點 || Each plan has ___ own merits. || its|it's|their || Each plan 單數，所有格用 its。 || 每個計畫都有其優點。
messenger || n. || 信差 || The messenger delivered the letter. || 信差送來了信。 || messenger app=通訊軟體 || The messenger ___ sent to deliver the good news. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 信差被派去傳遞好消息。
messy || adj. || 凌亂的 || Your room is messy. || 你的房間好亂。 || messy room=凌亂的房間 || His room is ___ messy that he can never find anything. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他的房間亂到他什麼都找不到。
microscope || n. || 顯微鏡 || We looked at cells under a microscope. || 我們在顯微鏡下觀察細胞。 || under a microscope=在顯微鏡下 || Bacteria can only be seen ___ a microscope. || under|below|with || under a microscope = 在顯微鏡下。 || 細菌只能在顯微鏡下看到。
mild || adj. || 溫和的 || The weather is mild today. || 今天天氣很溫和。 || mild weather=溫和的天氣 || The winter in Taiwan is mild compared ___ Japan. || to|with|as || compared to = 與…相比。 || 和日本相比，台灣的冬天很溫和。
mill || n. || 磨坊；工廠 || The old mill is by the river. || 那座老磨坊在河邊。 || paper mill=造紙廠 || The old mill ___ used to grind wheat. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那座老磨坊過去被用來磨小麥。
millionaire || n. || 百萬富翁 || He became a millionaire at 30. || 他三十歲就成了百萬富翁。 || self-made millionaire=白手起家的富翁 || He became a millionaire ___ selling products online. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 他靠網路銷售成了百萬富翁。
miner || n. || 礦工 || The miners work underground. || 礦工在地底下工作。 || coal miner=煤礦工 || The miners ___ trapped underground were rescued after two days. || who were|which were|who was || 關係代名詞修飾人用 who；miners 複數用 were。 || 受困地底的礦工在兩天後獲救了。
mineral || n. || 礦物；礦物質 || Milk is rich in minerals. || 牛奶富含礦物質。 || mineral water=礦泉水 || Vegetables are rich ___ vitamins and minerals. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 蔬菜富含維他命和礦物質。
minimum || adj./n. || 最低的；最少量 || The minimum age is 18. || 最低年齡是十八歲。 || minimum wage=基本工資 || You need a minimum ___ 60 points to pass. || of|for|in || a minimum of = 最少…。 || 你至少需要六十分才能及格。
minister || n. || 部長；牧師 || The Minister of Education gave a speech. || 教育部長發表了演說。 || prime minister=總理 || The Minister of Education, ___ visited our school, praised the students. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 來訪我們學校的教育部長稱讚了學生們。
ministry || n. || （政府的）部 || The Ministry of Education made a new policy. || 教育部制定了新政策。 || Ministry of Education=教育部 || The new policy was announced ___ the Ministry of Education. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 新政策由教育部公布。
mischief || n. || 惡作劇 || The kids got into mischief. || 孩子們在搗蛋。 || get into mischief=調皮搗蛋 || The boys are always getting ___ mischief. || into|in|to || get into mischief = 調皮搗蛋。 || 這些男孩總是在搗蛋。
miserable || adj. || 悲慘的 || He felt miserable after the breakup. || 分手後他覺得很痛苦。 || feel miserable=感到痛苦 || She felt miserable ___ she failed the exam. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 她考試不及格，覺得很難過。
misfortune || n. || 不幸 || He had the misfortune to lose his job. || 他不幸失業了。 || have the misfortune to V=不幸… || She had the misfortune ___ lose her wallet on the trip. || to|of|for || have the misfortune to + 原形動詞。 || 她不幸在旅途中弄丟了皮夾。
mislead || v. || 誤導 || The ad may mislead customers. || 這則廣告可能誤導消費者。 || mislead sb=誤導某人 || Some advertisements are designed ___ mislead consumers. || to|for|so || be designed to + 原形動詞。 || 有些廣告是設計來誤導消費者的。
misunderstand || v. || 誤解 || Don't misunderstand me. || 別誤會我。 || misunderstand sb=誤解某人 || Please don't misunderstand me. I didn't mean ___ hurt you. || to|for|of || mean to + 原形動詞 = 有意…。 || 請別誤會，我不是故意要傷害你。
moderate || adj. || 適度的 || Moderate exercise is good for you. || 適度的運動對你有益。 || moderate exercise=適度運動 || Doctors suggest doing moderate exercise ___ least three times a week. || at|in|for || at least = 至少。 || 醫生建議一週至少做三次適度的運動。
modest || adj. || 謙虛的 || She is modest about her success. || 她對自己的成功很謙虛。 || be modest about=對…謙虛 || Although he is famous, he remains modest ___ his achievements. || about|of|with || be modest about = 對…謙虛。 || 雖然他很有名，他對自己的成就仍很謙虛。
modesty || n. || 謙虛 || Modesty is a good quality. || 謙虛是一種好品德。 || with modesty=謙虛地 || She accepted the award ___ modesty. || with|in|by || with modesty = 謙虛地。 || 她謙虛地接受了這個獎項。
monitor || n./v. || 監控；螢幕 || Parents should monitor their kids' screen time. || 父母應該監控孩子使用螢幕的時間。 || computer monitor=電腦螢幕 || The patient's heart rate is ___ monitored all the time. || being|been|be || 現在進行式被動：is being + p.p.。 || 病人的心跳一直受到監測。
monument || n. || 紀念碑 || The monument was built to honor the soldiers. || 這座紀念碑是為了紀念士兵而建。 || historic monument=歷史古蹟 || The monument was built ___ honor the heroes of the war. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 這座紀念碑是為了紀念戰爭英雄而建的。
moreover || adv. || 此外 || The house is big. Moreover, it's cheap. || 這間房子很大，而且很便宜。 || moreover=此外 || The job pays well. Moreover, it ___ interesting. || is|are|be || it 單數，用 is。 || 這份工作薪水高，此外也很有趣。
motivate || v. || 激勵 || Good teachers motivate their students. || 好老師會激勵學生。 || motivate sb to V=激勵某人做… || The coach motivated the players ___ harder. || to try|trying|try || motivate sb to + 原形動詞。 || 教練激勵球員更加努力。
motivation || n. || 動機 || She lacks motivation to study. || 她缺乏讀書的動機。 || lack of motivation=缺乏動力 || What is your motivation ___ learning English? || for|of|to || motivation for = …的動機。 || 你學英文的動機是什麼？
mountainous || adj. || 多山的 || Taiwan is very mountainous. || 台灣多山。 || mountainous area=山區 || About two-thirds of Taiwan ___ mountainous. || is|are|be || 分數 of + 單數地名，動詞用單數 is。 || 台灣約三分之二的土地是山地。
muddy || adj. || 泥濘的 || The road is muddy after the rain. || 雨後路很泥濘。 || muddy shoes=沾滿泥巴的鞋子 || Take off your muddy shoes ___ you come in. || before|after|since || 進來「之前」脫鞋，用 before。 || 進來之前把你沾滿泥巴的鞋子脫掉。
mule || n. || 騾子 || He is as stubborn as a mule. || 他像騾子一樣固執。 || as stubborn as a mule=非常固執 || He is as stubborn ___ a mule. || as|like|than || as + 形容詞 + as = 和…一樣。 || 他跟騾子一樣固執。
multiple || adj. || 多重的 || This is a multiple-choice test. || 這是選擇題考試。 || multiple-choice=選擇題 || The test consists ___ multiple-choice questions. || of|in|with || consist of = 由…組成。 || 這份考試由選擇題組成。
murderer || n. || 謀殺犯 || The murderer was caught. || 那名謀殺犯被抓到了。 || murderer=兇手 || The murderer ___ sentenced to life in prison. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那名兇手被判無期徒刑。
murmur || v./n. || 低語 || She murmured something to me. || 她對我低聲說了些什麼。 || in a murmur=低聲地 || She murmured something ___ a low voice. || in|with|by || in a low voice = 低聲地。 || 她低聲說了些什麼。
mutual || adj. || 相互的；共同的 || We have mutual friends. || 我們有共同的朋友。 || mutual friend=共同朋友; mutual respect=互相尊重 || A good friendship is based ___ mutual respect. || on|in|at || be based on = 以…為基礎。 || 良好的友誼建立在互相尊重的基礎上。
mysterious || adj. || 神祕的 || The house looks mysterious. || 那棟房子看起來很神祕。 || mysterious man=神祕男子 || A mysterious man ___ a black hat was standing at the door. || in|with|on || in + 衣物 = 穿戴著…。 || 一個戴著黑帽子的神祕男子站在門口。
namely || adv. || 即 || Two students, namely Tom and Amy, won prizes. || 兩位學生，即湯姆和艾咪，得了獎。 || namely=也就是 || Only one student, namely Amy, ___ the test. || passed|pass|passing || 描述過去的事用過去式 passed。 || 只有一個學生，也就是艾咪，通過了考試。
nationality || n. || 國籍 || What is your nationality? || 你的國籍是什麼？ || dual nationality=雙重國籍 || People of different nationalities ___ at this school. || study|studies|studying || People 複數，動詞用原形。 || 不同國籍的人在這所學校就讀。
needy || adj. || 貧困的 || We donated food to needy families. || 我們捐食物給貧困家庭。 || the needy=窮人 || The money will be used ___ help needy children. || to|for|so || be used to + 原形動詞 = 被用來…。 || 這筆錢將用來幫助貧困兒童。
neglect || v./n. || 忽視 || Don't neglect your health. || 不要忽視你的健康。 || neglect one's duty=怠忽職守 || The old house ___ neglected for years. || had been|has|was being been || 過去完成被動：had been + p.p.（在過去某時之前已經被…）。 || 那棟老房子多年來一直無人照顧。
negotiate || v. || 協商 || The two sides negotiated a deal. || 雙方協商出一份協議。 || negotiate with=與…協商 || The workers negotiated ___ the company for higher pay. || with|to|for || negotiate with = 與…協商。 || 工人們與公司協商加薪。
