# 學測 L4 07：diverse – evaluation
diverse || adj. || 多樣的 || Taiwan has a diverse culture. || 台灣有多元的文化。 || culturally diverse=文化多元的 || Taiwan is home to people ___ diverse backgrounds. || from|to|at || from + 背景 = 來自…。 || 台灣是來自多元背景的人們的家園。
diversity || n. || 多樣性 || We should respect cultural diversity. || 我們應該尊重文化多樣性。 || cultural diversity=文化多樣性 || The rainforest is famous ___ its diversity of plants and animals. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 雨林以其動植物的多樣性聞名。
divine || adj. || 神的；神聖的 || The temple is a place of divine worship. || 這座廟是祭拜神明的地方。 || divine power=神力 || People believed that the emperor had divine power ___ came from heaven. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（power）。 || 人們相信皇帝擁有來自上天的神力。
divorce || n./v. || 離婚 || Her parents got divorced. || 她的父母離婚了。 || get divorced=離婚 || Her parents got divorced ___ she was five. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 她五歲時父母離婚了。
dodge || v. || 閃避 || He dodged the ball. || 他閃過了球。 || dodge a question=迴避問題 || He tried to dodge the question ___ changing the subject. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 他試圖藉由轉移話題來迴避問題。
dominant || adj. || 主要的；佔優勢的 || English is the dominant language online. || 英文是網路上的主要語言。 || dominant role=主導角色 || English plays a dominant role ___ international business. || in|on|at || play a role in = 在…中扮演角色。 || 英文在國際商務中扮演主導角色。
dominate || v. || 支配；主導 || Smartphones dominate our lives. || 智慧型手機主宰了我們的生活。 || dominate the market=主導市場 || The market ___ dominated by a few big companies. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The market 用 is + p.p.。 || 市場被少數幾家大公司主導。
draft || n./v. || 草稿 || This is only the first draft. || 這只是初稿。 || first draft=初稿 || Please hand in your first draft ___ Friday. || by|until|since || by + 時間 = 在…之前（截止）。 || 請在星期五之前交初稿。
dread || v./n. || 懼怕 || I dread going to the dentist. || 我很怕看牙醫。 || dread + V-ing=害怕做… || Many students dread ___ speeches in front of the class. || giving|to giving|give || dread 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 很多學生害怕在全班面前演講。
drift || v. || 漂流 || The boat drifted out to sea. || 那艘船漂向大海。 || drift away=漂走 || The empty boat drifted slowly ___ the river. || down|to down|at || drift down the river = 順著河漂流而下。 || 那艘空船慢慢順著河漂流而下。
drill || n./v. || 演習；鑽孔 || We had a fire drill at school. || 我們在學校進行了消防演習。 || fire drill=消防演習 || During the earthquake drill, students were told ___ under their desks. || to hide|hiding|hide || be told to + 原形動詞。 || 地震演習時，學生被告知躲到桌子底下。
drowsy || adj. || 昏昏欲睡的 || This medicine may make you drowsy. || 這個藥可能會讓你想睡。 || feel drowsy=昏昏欲睡 || This medicine may make you drowsy, so don't drive ___ taking it. || after|before|since || 吃藥「之後」不要開車，用 after。 || 這個藥可能讓你想睡，所以吃完後不要開車。
durable || adj. || 耐用的 || These shoes are very durable. || 這雙鞋非常耐穿。 || durable material=耐用的材料 || These boots are made of durable leather, ___ they will last for years. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這雙靴子是耐用的皮革做的，所以可以穿好幾年。
dusty || adj. || 布滿灰塵的 || The old books are dusty. || 那些舊書布滿灰塵。 || dusty room=滿是灰塵的房間 || The room was dusty ___ nobody had cleaned it for months. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 房間布滿灰塵，因為好幾個月沒人打掃。
dye || n./v. || 染料；染色 || She dyed her hair red. || 她把頭髮染成紅色。 || hair dye=染髮劑 || She wants to dye her hair ___ summer vacation. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 她想在暑假期間染頭髮。
dynamic || adj. || 充滿活力的 || She is a dynamic leader. || 她是一位充滿活力的領導者。 || dynamic city=充滿活力的城市 || Taipei is one of the most dynamic ___ in Asia. || cities|city|citys || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞 cities。 || 台北是亞洲最有活力的城市之一。
dynasty || n. || 朝代 || The Tang Dynasty was a golden age. || 唐朝是一個黃金時代。 || Tang Dynasty=唐朝 || Li Bai was a poet ___ lived during the Tang Dynasty. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 李白是生活在唐朝的詩人。
earnest || adj. || 認真的 || He is an earnest student. || 他是個認真的學生。 || in earnest=認真地 || It started to rain ___ earnest. || in|with|by || in earnest = 認真地；（雨）真正下起來。 || 雨開始真正下大了。
earphone || n. || 耳機 || He is listening to music with his earphones. || 他戴著耳機聽音樂。 || wear earphones=戴耳機 || Listening to loud music through earphones ___ damage your hearing. || can|cans|could to || 助動詞 can 後接原形動詞。 || 用耳機聽大聲的音樂會傷害聽力。
economic || adj. || 經濟的 || The country has economic problems. || 這個國家有經濟問題。 || economic growth=經濟成長 || Economic growth has slowed down ___ the past two years. || over|since|by || over the past + 時間 = 在過去…期間。 || 過去兩年經濟成長趨緩。
economical || adj. || 節省的；經濟實惠的 || This car is very economical. || 這輛車很省油。 || economical car=省油的車 || Taking the MRT is ___ economical than taking a taxi. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 搭捷運比搭計程車經濟實惠。
economics || n. || 經濟學 || She studies economics at university. || 她在大學讀經濟學。 || major in economics=主修經濟學 || Economics ___ the study of how people use money and resources. || is|are|be || economics 是學科名稱，視為單數，用 is。 || 經濟學是研究人們如何運用金錢和資源的學問。
economist || n. || 經濟學家 || The economist predicted a recession. || 那位經濟學家預測會有經濟衰退。 || leading economist=頂尖經濟學家 || Many economists believe ___ prices will keep rising. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 believe 的受詞。 || 許多經濟學家相信物價會持續上漲。
economy || n. || 經濟 || The economy is growing. || 經濟正在成長。 || the world economy=世界經濟 || Tourism is important ___ the economy of Taiwan. || to|at|with || be important to = 對…很重要。 || 觀光業對台灣經濟很重要。
efficiency || n. || 效率 || We need to improve our efficiency. || 我們需要提高效率。 || energy efficiency=能源效率 || The new system improved the efficiency ___ the factory. || of|for|to || the efficiency of = …的效率。 || 新系統提高了工廠的效率。
elastic || adj./n. || 有彈性的 || This material is elastic. || 這種材料有彈性。 || elastic band=橡皮筋 || This material is elastic, so it will return ___ its original shape. || to|for|with || return to = 回到…。 || 這種材料有彈性，所以會恢復原狀。
electronics || n. || 電子產品；電子學 || He works in the electronics industry. || 他在電子業工作。 || consumer electronics=消費性電子產品 || Taiwan is famous ___ its electronics industry. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 台灣以電子產業聞名。
elegant || adj. || 優雅的 || She looks elegant in that dress. || 她穿那件洋裝看起來很優雅。 || elegant style=優雅的風格 || She looks elegant ___ that black dress. || in|with|on || in + 衣服 = 穿著…。 || 她穿那件黑洋裝看起來很優雅。
elementary || adj. || 基本的；初級的 || This is an elementary English book. || 這是一本初級英文書。 || elementary school=小學 || My little brother goes to ___ elementary school near our home. || an|a|the more || elementary 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我弟弟讀我們家附近的一所小學。
eliminate || v. || 消除；淘汰 || We must eliminate the problem. || 我們必須消除這個問題。 || be eliminated from=從…中被淘汰 || Our team ___ eliminated in the first round. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我們隊在第一輪就被淘汰了。
elsewhere || adv. || 在別處 || Let's go elsewhere. || 我們去別的地方吧。 || elsewhere=別處 || This restaurant is full. Let's eat elsewhere, ___ we? || shall|will|do || Let's 的附加問句用 shall we。 || 這家餐廳客滿了，我們去別處吃，好嗎？
embassy || n. || 大使館 || She works at the embassy. || 她在大使館工作。 || the Japanese embassy=日本大使館 || You need to apply for a visa ___ the embassy. || at|in|on || 在某個機構（地點）用 at。 || 你需要到大使館申請簽證。
emerge || v. || 出現 || The sun emerged from behind the clouds. || 太陽從雲層後面出現了。 || emerge from=從…出現 || The sun emerged ___ behind the clouds. || from|of|out || emerge from = 從…出現。 || 太陽從雲層後面出現了。
emphasis || n. || 強調 || The school puts emphasis on reading. || 學校重視閱讀。 || put emphasis on=強調… || Our school puts a lot of emphasis ___ English. || on|in|at || put emphasis on = 強調、重視…。 || 我們學校非常重視英文。
empire || n. || 帝國 || The Roman Empire lasted for centuries. || 羅馬帝國延續了數個世紀。 || the Roman Empire=羅馬帝國 || The Roman Empire, ___ lasted for centuries, was very powerful. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 延續數個世紀的羅馬帝國非常強大。
enclose || v. || 附上；圍住 || Please enclose a photo with your application. || 請在申請書中附上一張照片。 || enclose a check=附上支票 || Please find the photo enclosed ___ this letter. || with|by|to || enclosed with = 隨…附上。 || 請見隨信附上的照片。
encounter || v./n. || 遭遇 || We encountered many problems. || 我們遇到了許多問題。 || encounter difficulties=遭遇困難 || If you encounter any difficulties, don't hesitate ___ ask for help. || to|for|of || hesitate to + 原形動詞。 || 如果遇到任何困難，儘管尋求協助。
endanger || v. || 危及 || Pollution endangers wildlife. || 污染危害野生動物。 || endangered species=瀕危物種 || Many animals are endangered ___ humans destroy their homes. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 許多動物瀕臨絕種，因為人類破壞了牠們的棲地。
endure || v. || 忍受 || She endured the pain. || 她忍受了疼痛。 || endure pain=忍受疼痛 || He couldn't endure the noise ___ longer. || any|some|no || not… any longer = 不再…。 || 他再也忍受不了噪音了。
engineering || n. || 工程學 || He studies engineering. || 他讀工程學。 || electrical engineering=電機工程 || She decided to major ___ engineering. || in|on|at || major in = 主修…。 || 她決定主修工程學。
enormous || adj. || 巨大的 || The house is enormous. || 那棟房子非常大。 || enormous amount=巨大的數量 || The project cost an enormous amount ___ money. || of|in|for || an amount of = …的數量。 || 這個計畫花了一大筆錢。
ensure || v. || 確保 || Please ensure that the door is locked. || 請確保門是鎖好的。 || ensure that=確保… || Please ensure ___ all the lights are turned off. || that|what|which || ensure that… = 確保…。 || 請確保所有燈都關了。
enthusiasm || n. || 熱忱 || She has great enthusiasm for music. || 她對音樂充滿熱忱。 || with enthusiasm=熱情地 || The students welcomed the new teacher ___ enthusiasm. || with|in|by || with enthusiasm = 熱情地。 || 學生們熱情地歡迎新老師。
equality || n. || 平等 || We believe in gender equality. || 我們相信性別平等。 || gender equality=性別平等 || Taiwan is known ___ its progress in gender equality. || for|as|with || be known for = 以…聞名。 || 台灣以性別平等的進步聞名。
era || n. || 時代 || We live in the digital era. || 我們生活在數位時代。 || a new era=新時代 || The invention of the Internet marked the beginning ___ a new era. || of|for|to || the beginning of = …的開始。 || 網際網路的發明標誌著新時代的開始。
essential || adj./n. || 必要的 || Water is essential for life. || 水是生命所必需的。 || essential for=對…不可或缺 || Sleep is essential ___ good health. || for|to be|with || be essential for = 對…不可或缺。 || 睡眠對健康不可或缺。
estimate || v./n. || 估計 || It's estimated that 1,000 people came. || 據估計有一千人到場。 || it is estimated that=據估計… || It is estimated ___ more than 1,000 people attended the concert. || that|what|which || It is estimated that… = 據估計…。 || 據估計有超過一千人參加了演唱會。
ethnic || adj. || 種族的；民族的 || Taiwan has many ethnic groups. || 台灣有許多族群。 || ethnic group=族群 || There are many ethnic groups ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 台灣有許多族群。
evaluate || v. || 評估 || Teachers evaluate students' progress. || 老師評估學生的進步。 || evaluate performance=評估表現 || Students' work will ___ evaluated at the end of the semester. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 學生的作品將在學期末接受評量。
evaluation || n. || 評估 || The evaluation will take a week. || 評估需要一週。 || self-evaluation=自我評估 || The results of the evaluation ___ announced next week. || will be|will|is || 未來被動：will be + p.p.。 || 評估結果將於下週公布。
