# 學測 L4 10：grind – immigrate
grind || v. || 磨碎 || Grind the coffee beans. || 把咖啡豆磨碎。 || grind coffee=磨咖啡豆 || You need a machine ___ grind coffee beans. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 你需要一台機器來磨咖啡豆。
guarantee || v./n. || 保證 || I guarantee you will like it. || 我保證你會喜歡。 || money-back guarantee=退款保證 || Hard work doesn't always guarantee success, ___ it helps. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 努力不一定保證成功，但是有幫助。
guardian || n. || 監護人；守護者 || Her aunt is her legal guardian. || 她阿姨是她的法定監護人。 || legal guardian=法定監護人 || Students under 18 need a signature from their parents ___ guardians. || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 未滿十八歲的學生需要父母或監護人簽名。
guilt || n. || 罪惡感 || He felt a lot of guilt. || 他感到非常內疚。 || feeling of guilt=罪惡感 || She was filled ___ guilt after lying to her mom. || with|of|by || be filled with = 充滿…。 || 她對媽媽說謊後充滿罪惡感。
guilty || adj. || 有罪的；內疚的 || He felt guilty about lying. || 他對說謊感到內疚。 || feel guilty=感到內疚; be guilty of=犯了…罪 || The man was found guilty ___ stealing. || of|for|with || be guilty of = 犯了…罪。 || 那名男子被判竊盜罪成立。
gulf || n. || 海灣 || The ship sailed into the gulf. || 船駛進了海灣。 || the Gulf of Mexico=墨西哥灣 || The Gulf of Mexico ___ located south of the United States. || is|are|be || 被動 be located；地名單數用 is。 || 墨西哥灣位於美國南方。
habitual || adj. || 習慣性的 || He is a habitual liar. || 他習慣說謊。 || habitual behavior=習慣性行為 || Being late has become ___ habitual problem for him. || a|an|the || habitual 以子音 /h/ 開頭，用 a。 || 遲到已經成為他的習慣性問題。
halt || n./v. || 停止 || The train came to a halt. || 火車停了下來。 || come to a halt=停止 || The car came ___ a sudden halt. || to|at|in || come to a halt = 停下來。 || 車子突然停了下來。
handwriting || n. || 筆跡 || Your handwriting is beautiful. || 你的字很漂亮。 || neat handwriting=工整的字跡 || His handwriting is so messy ___ nobody can read it. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他的字潦草到沒人看得懂。
hardship || n. || 艱難 || They went through many hardships. || 他們經歷了許多困苦。 || face hardship=面對困境 || She overcame many hardships ___ she became successful. || before|after|since || 成功「之前」克服困難，用 before。 || 她在成功之前克服了許多困難。
hardware || n. || 硬體；五金 || The computer hardware needs to be updated. || 電腦硬體需要更新了。 || hardware store=五金行 || I bought a hammer ___ the hardware store. || at|on|to of || 在商店（地點）用 at。 || 我在五金行買了一把鐵鎚。
harmony || n. || 和諧 || We should live in harmony with nature. || 我們應該與自然和諧共處。 || in harmony with=與…和諧 || People should live in harmony ___ nature. || with|to|for || in harmony with = 與…和諧。 || 人們應該與自然和諧共處。
harsh || adj. || 嚴厲的；惡劣的 || The weather in winter is harsh. || 冬天的天氣很惡劣。 || harsh words=嚴厲的話 || Don't be ___ harsh on yourself. || so|such|much || so + 形容詞 = 那麼…。 || 別對自己那麼嚴苛。
haste || n. || 匆忙 || Haste makes waste. || 欲速則不達。 || in haste=匆忙地 || He left ___ haste and forgot his keys. || in|with|by || in haste = 匆忙地。 || 他匆忙離開，忘了帶鑰匙。
hasten || v. || 加快 || We need to hasten the process. || 我們需要加快進度。 || hasten to V=趕緊做… || The rain hastened the end ___ the game. || of|for|to || the end of = …的結束。 || 雨加速了比賽的結束。
hatred || n. || 憎恨 || Hatred leads to violence. || 仇恨導致暴力。 || racial hatred=種族仇恨 || We should replace hatred ___ love. || with|by|to || replace A with B = 用 B 取代 A。 || 我們應該用愛取代仇恨。
hawk || n. || 鷹 || A hawk flew over the field. || 一隻老鷹飛過田野。 || eyes like a hawk=眼尖 || The hawk can see a mouse ___ far away. || from|of|at || from far away = 從遠處。 || 老鷹從很遠的地方就能看見老鼠。
helicopter || n. || 直升機 || The injured man was taken by helicopter. || 傷者被直升機送走。 || by helicopter=搭直升機 || The climbers were rescued ___ helicopter. || by|with|on || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 登山者被直升機救了出來。
herd || n./v. || 獸群 || A herd of cows was eating grass. || 一群牛在吃草。 || a herd of=一群（牛等） || The farmer herded the sheep ___ the barn. || into|in|onto of || herd A into B = 把 A 趕進 B。 || 農夫把羊趕進了穀倉。
hesitation || n. || 猶豫 || She answered without hesitation. || 她毫不猶豫地回答。 || without hesitation=毫不猶豫 || He agreed to help ___ hesitation. || without|with|within || without hesitation = 毫不猶豫。 || 他毫不猶豫地答應幫忙。
hive || n. || 蜂巢 || The bees returned to the hive. || 蜜蜂回到蜂巢。 || bee hive=蜂巢 || Don't go near the hive, ___ you may get stung. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 別靠近蜂巢，否則可能會被螫。
homeland || n. || 祖國；家鄉 || He returned to his homeland. || 他回到了祖國。 || return to one's homeland=返回祖國 || After twenty years abroad, he finally returned ___ his homeland. || to|at|in || return to = 回到…。 || 在國外二十年後，他終於回到了祖國。
honeymoon || n. || 蜜月 || They went to Bali for their honeymoon. || 他們去峇里島度蜜月。 || on one's honeymoon=度蜜月 || The newly married couple spent ___ honeymoon in Japan. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 honeymoon 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 那對新婚夫妻在日本度蜜月。
hook || n./v. || 鉤子 || Hang your coat on the hook. || 把外套掛在鉤子上。 || coat hook=掛衣鉤 || Hang your bag ___ the hook behind the door. || on|in|at || 掛在鉤子上用 on。 || 把你的包包掛在門後的鉤子上。
horizon || n. || 地平線 || The sun rose above the horizon. || 太陽從地平線升起。 || on the horizon=即將發生 || The ship slowly disappeared ___ the horizon. || below|under of|beneath of || below = 在…之下。 || 船慢慢消失在地平線下。
horrify || v. || 使驚恐 || The news horrified everyone. || 這則新聞讓大家都很驚恐。 || be horrified by=被…嚇壞 || We were horrified ___ the news of the accident. || by|with|of || be horrified by = 被…嚇壞。 || 我們被那起事故的消息嚇壞了。
hose || n. || 水管 || Water the garden with a hose. || 用水管替花園澆水。 || garden hose=花園水管 || He washed the car ___ a hose. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 他用水管洗車。
household || n./adj. || 家庭；家用的 || Every household should recycle. || 每戶人家都應該做回收。 || household chores=家務 || Everyone in the family should help ___ household chores. || with|to|for || help with sth = 幫忙做某事。 || 家裡每個人都應該幫忙做家事。
housework || n. || 家事 || I help my mom with the housework. || 我幫媽媽做家事。 || do housework=做家事 || Doing housework ___ children learn responsibility. || helps|help|helping || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，動詞加 s。 || 做家事能幫助孩子學會負責任。
humanity || n. || 人類；人性 || War is a crime against humanity. || 戰爭是違反人道的罪行。 || crime against humanity=違反人道罪 || Kindness is one of the best qualities ___ humanity. || of|in|for || the qualities of = …的特質。 || 善良是人性最美好的特質之一。
humidity || n. || 濕度 || The humidity is high today. || 今天濕度很高。 || high humidity=高濕度 || The humidity in Taiwan is especially high ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 台灣的濕度在夏天特別高。
hurricane || n. || 颶風 || The hurricane destroyed many houses. || 颶風摧毀了許多房屋。 || hurricane season=颶風季 || Many houses ___ destroyed by the hurricane. || were|was|are || 被動語態；houses 複數，過去用 were。 || 許多房屋被颶風摧毀。
hush || v./n. || 使安靜 || Hush! The baby is sleeping. || 噓！寶寶在睡覺。 || hush up=掩蓋 || A hush fell over the room ___ the teacher walked in. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 老師走進來時，房間一片安靜。
hydrogen || n. || 氫 || Water is made of hydrogen and oxygen. || 水是由氫和氧組成的。 || hydrogen gas=氫氣 || Water consists ___ hydrogen and oxygen. || of|in|with || consist of = 由…組成。 || 水由氫和氧組成。
identical || adj. || 完全相同的 || The twins are identical. || 那對雙胞胎長得一模一樣。 || identical twins=同卵雙胞胎 || My bag is identical ___ yours. || to|with|as || be identical to = 和…完全相同。 || 我的包包和你的一模一樣。
identification || n. || 身分證明 || Please show your identification. || 請出示你的身分證明。 || identification card=身分證 || You need to show some identification ___ you check in. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 辦理入住時你需要出示身分證明。
identify || v. || 辨認 || Can you identify the man in the photo? || 你能認出照片裡的人嗎？ || identify with=認同… || The victim ___ identified by her family. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 受害者由她的家人指認身分。
idiom || n. || 成語；慣用語 || "Break a leg" is an English idiom. || 「break a leg」是一個英文慣用語。 || English idiom=英文慣用語 || Idioms are phrases ___ meanings are different from the individual words. || whose|which|that || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 慣用語是意思與個別單字不同的片語。
idle || adj. || 閒置的；懶散的 || Don't stay idle all day. || 不要整天閒著。 || idle time=閒暇時間 || The machines were idle ___ the holiday. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 假期期間機器都閒置著。
idol || n. || 偶像 || He is my idol. || 他是我的偶像。 || pop idol=流行偶像 || The singer is an idol ___ many teenagers. || to|of|with || an idol to sb = 某人的偶像。 || 那位歌手是許多青少年的偶像。
ignorance || n. || 無知 || Ignorance is not an excuse. || 無知不是藉口。 || out of ignorance=出於無知 || He made the mistake out ___ ignorance. || of|from|for || out of + 名詞 = 出於…。 || 他出於無知而犯了這個錯。
ignorant || adj. || 無知的 || He is ignorant of the rules. || 他不知道規則。 || be ignorant of=對…無知 || Many people are ignorant ___ the dangers of smoking. || of|about to|with || be ignorant of = 對…不了解。 || 很多人不了解抽菸的危險。
illustrate || v. || 說明；畫插圖 || The book is illustrated with pictures. || 這本書配有插圖。 || illustrate a point=說明論點 || The story book ___ illustrated by a famous artist. || was|were|is being || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這本故事書是由一位知名畫家繪製插圖的。
illustration || n. || 插圖 || The book has beautiful illustrations. || 這本書有美麗的插圖。 || book illustration=書籍插圖 || The illustrations ___ the book are colorful. || in|on|at || 在書裡用 in。 || 書裡的插圖色彩繽紛。
imaginary || adj. || 虛構的 || The story is about an imaginary world. || 這個故事是關於一個虛構的世界。 || imaginary friend=幻想中的朋友 || Many children have imaginary friends ___ they play with. || that|what|who of || 關係代名詞 that 修飾（擬人的）朋友並當 play with 的受詞。 || 很多孩子有和他們一起玩的想像朋友。
imaginative || adj. || 富有想像力的 || She is an imaginative writer. || 她是一位富有想像力的作家。 || imaginative story=富想像力的故事 || Children are often ___ imaginative than adults. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 孩子通常比大人更有想像力。
imitate || v. || 模仿 || The parrot can imitate human voices. || 鸚鵡能模仿人的聲音。 || imitate sb=模仿某人 || Parrots can imitate the sounds ___ they hear. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 hear 的受詞。 || 鸚鵡能模仿牠們聽到的聲音。
imitation || n. || 模仿；仿製品 || This is an imitation of a famous painting. || 這是一幅名畫的仿製品。 || cheap imitation=廉價仿製品 || This bag is only an imitation ___ a famous brand. || of|for|to || an imitation of = …的仿製品。 || 這個包包只是某個名牌的仿冒品。
immigrant || n. || 移民 || Many immigrants live in this city. || 這座城市住著很多移民。 || new immigrant=新住民 || Many new immigrants ___ Southeast Asia live in Taiwan. || from|of|in || from + 地方 = 來自…。 || 許多來自東南亞的新住民住在台灣。
immigrate || v. || 移民（移入） || His family immigrated to Canada. || 他一家移民到加拿大。 || immigrate to=移民到… || His family immigrated ___ the United States ten years ago. || to|at|in || immigrate to = 移民到…。 || 他一家人十年前移民到美國。
