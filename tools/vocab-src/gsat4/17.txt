# 學測 L4 17：refusal – rusty
refusal || n. || 拒絕 || His refusal surprised everyone. || 他的拒絕讓大家很驚訝。 || refusal to V=拒絕做… || Her refusal ___ help us was disappointing. || to|for|of || refusal to + 原形動詞。 || 她拒絕幫我們，讓人很失望。
regarding || prep. || 關於 || I have a question regarding the test. || 我有一個關於考試的問題。 || regarding=關於 || Please contact us ___ you have any questions regarding the course. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你對課程有任何問題，請聯絡我們。
register || v./n. || 登記；註冊 || You need to register online. || 你需要在線上註冊。 || register for=報名… || You must register ___ the course before Friday. || for|to|in of || register for = 報名…。 || 你必須在星期五之前報名這門課。
registration || n. || 註冊；登記 || Registration starts next week. || 註冊從下週開始。 || registration fee=報名費 || The registration fee ___ 500 dollars. || is|are|be || The registration fee 單數，用 is。 || 報名費是五百元。
regulate || v. || 調節；管理 || The government regulates prices. || 政府管理物價。 || regulate traffic=管制交通 || The use of drones ___ strictly regulated in this area. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The use 單數用 is + p.p.。 || 這個區域嚴格管制無人機的使用。
regulation || n. || 規定 || We must follow the regulations. || 我們必須遵守規定。 || safety regulations=安全規定 || All students must follow the school regulations, ___ they will be punished. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 所有學生都必須遵守校規，否則會受處罰。
rejection || n. || 拒絕 || He is afraid of rejection. || 他害怕被拒絕。 || fear of rejection=害怕被拒絕 || She didn't give up ___ many rejections. || despite|although|because || despite + 名詞 = 儘管…。 || 儘管被拒絕了很多次，她還是沒有放棄。
relaxation || n. || 放鬆 || Music helps with relaxation. || 音樂有助於放鬆。 || for relaxation=為了放鬆 || She does yoga ___ relaxation. || for|to|with || for + 名詞 = 為了…。 || 她做瑜伽來放鬆。
relevant || adj. || 相關的 || The information is not relevant. || 這些資訊不相關。 || be relevant to=與…相關 || This information is not relevant ___ our topic. || to|with|for || be relevant to = 與…相關。 || 這個資訊和我們的主題無關。
relieve || v. || 緩解 || This medicine will relieve the pain. || 這個藥會緩解疼痛。 || relieve stress=紓壓 || Exercise can help relieve stress ___ anxiety. || and|but|or || 兩者並列用 and。 || 運動能幫助紓解壓力和焦慮。
reluctant || adj. || 不情願的 || He was reluctant to help. || 他不太情願幫忙。 || be reluctant to V=不願意做… || She was reluctant ___ her secret. || to share|sharing|share || be reluctant to + 原形動詞。 || 她不太願意分享她的祕密。
remark || n./v. || 評論 || He made a rude remark. || 他說了一句無禮的話。 || make a remark=發表評論 || He made a remark ___ my new haircut. || about|of|to || a remark about = 關於…的評論。 || 他對我的新髮型發表了評論。
remarkable || adj. || 非凡的 || She made remarkable progress. || 她有顯著的進步。 || remarkable progress=顯著的進步 || It is remarkable ___ he learned Chinese in only one year. || that|what|which || It is remarkable that… = …很了不起。 || 他只花一年就學會中文，真了不起。
remedy || n. || 治療法；補救 || Ginger tea is a good remedy for colds. || 薑茶是治感冒的好方法。 || remedy for=…的療法 || Honey is a natural remedy ___ a sore throat. || for|of|to || a remedy for = …的療法。 || 蜂蜜是治療喉嚨痛的天然療法。
renew || v. || 更新；續約 || I need to renew my passport. || 我需要更新護照。 || renew a passport=換護照 || You should renew your passport ___ it expires. || before|after|since || 到期「之前」更新，用 before。 || 你應該在護照到期前換發。
repetition || n. || 重複 || Learning a language requires repetition. || 學習語言需要反覆練習。 || repetition=重複 || New words are easier to remember ___ repetition. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 透過反覆練習，新單字比較容易記住。
representation || n. || 代表；表現 || The painting is a representation of peace. || 這幅畫象徵和平。 || representation of=…的象徵 || The dove is often used as a representation ___ peace. || of|for|to || a representation of = …的象徵。 || 鴿子常被用來象徵和平。
reputation || n. || 名聲 || The school has a good reputation. || 這所學校名聲很好。 || good reputation=好名聲 || The restaurant has a reputation ___ excellent service. || for|of|with || have a reputation for = 以…聞名。 || 這家餐廳以優質服務聞名。
rescue || v./n. || 救援 || The firefighters rescued the cat. || 消防員救了那隻貓。 || rescue team=救援隊 || The climbers ___ rescued by a helicopter. || were|was|are || 被動語態；climbers 複數，過去用 were。 || 登山者被直升機救了出來。
research || n./v. || 研究 || She is doing research on climate change. || 她正在研究氣候變遷。 || do research on=研究… || Scientists are doing research ___ new medicines. || on|in|at of || do research on = 研究…。 || 科學家正在研究新藥。
researcher || n. || 研究員 || The researcher found something new. || 研究員發現了新東西。 || medical researcher=醫學研究者 || The researcher ___ discovered the new medicine won a prize. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 發現新藥的研究員得了獎。
resemble || v. || 像 || She resembles her mother. || 她長得像她媽媽。 || closely resemble=極為相像 || She resembles her mother ___ many ways. || in|on|at || in many ways = 在許多方面。 || 她在許多方面都很像她媽媽。
reservation || n. || 預約 || I made a reservation for dinner. || 我預訂了晚餐。 || make a reservation=預約 || I'd like to make a reservation ___ two at 7 p.m. || for|of|to || a reservation for + 人數 = …人的預約。 || 我想預訂晚上七點兩人的位子。
resign || v. || 辭職 || He resigned from his job. || 他辭去了工作。 || resign from=從…辭職 || She resigned ___ her job to travel around the world. || from|of|with || resign from = 從…辭職。 || 她辭職去環遊世界。
resignation || n. || 辭職 || He handed in his resignation. || 他遞了辭呈。 || letter of resignation=辭職信 || He handed ___ his resignation last Friday. || in|on|out || hand in = 繳交。 || 他上週五遞出了辭呈。
resistance || n. || 抵抗 || Exercise builds resistance to disease. || 運動能增強對疾病的抵抗力。 || resistance to=對…的抵抗力 || Vitamin C can increase your resistance ___ colds. || to|for|with || resistance to = 對…的抵抗力。 || 維他命 C 可以增強你對感冒的抵抗力。
resolution || n. || 決心；解決 || My New Year's resolution is to exercise more. || 我的新年新希望是多運動。 || New Year's resolution=新年新希望 || My New Year's resolution is ___ more books. || to read|reading to|read || be 動詞 + to V 當補語。 || 我的新年新希望是多讀書。
resolve || v. || 解決；決心 || We need to resolve this problem. || 我們需要解決這個問題。 || resolve a problem=解決問題 || The problem ___ resolved after a long discussion. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 經過長時間討論後，問題解決了。
respectable || adj. || 值得尊敬的；體面的 || He has a respectable job. || 他有一份體面的工作。 || respectable family=體面的家庭 || She comes from a respectable family ___ values education. || that|what|who of || 關係代名詞 that 修飾（家庭）並當主詞。 || 她出身於一個重視教育的體面家庭。
respectful || adj. || 恭敬的 || Be respectful to your elders. || 要尊敬長輩。 || be respectful to=對…恭敬 || Students should be respectful ___ their teachers. || to|for|with || be respectful to = 對…恭敬。 || 學生應該尊敬老師。
restore || v. || 恢復；修復 || The old temple was restored. || 那座古廟被修復了。 || restore peace=恢復和平 || The old temple ___ restored last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那座古廟去年被修復了。
restriction || n. || 限制 || There are restrictions on phone use. || 手機使用有限制。 || age restriction=年齡限制 || There are some restrictions ___ what you can bring on the plane. || on|in|of || restrictions on = 對…的限制。 || 可以帶上飛機的東西有一些限制。
retain || v. || 保留 || Please retain your receipt. || 請保留收據。 || retain information=記住資訊 || Please retain your ticket ___ you leave the station. || until|since|by || until = 直到…。 || 在離開車站之前請保留你的車票。
retreat || n./v. || 撤退 || The army was forced to retreat. || 軍隊被迫撤退。 || retreat from=從…撤退 || The soldiers were forced ___ retreat. || to|for|of || be forced to + 原形動詞。 || 士兵們被迫撤退。
reunion || n. || 團聚 || We had a family reunion at New Year. || 過年時我們家人團聚。 || family reunion=家庭團聚; class reunion=同學會 || We have a family reunion ___ Chinese New Year every year. || at|on|in of || 在節日期間用 at。 || 我們每年農曆新年都會家人團聚。
revenge || n./v. || 報復 || He wanted revenge. || 他想報仇。 || take revenge on=向…報仇 || He wanted to take revenge ___ the man who hurt his family. || on|to|for || take revenge on sb = 向某人報仇。 || 他想向傷害他家人的那個男人報仇。
revise || v. || 修改；複習 || Please revise your essay. || 請修改你的作文。 || revise an essay=修改文章 || Please revise your essay ___ handing it in. || before|after|since || 繳交「之前」修改，用 before。 || 交作文之前請先修改。
revision || n. || 修改；複習 || The book needs some revision. || 這本書需要一些修訂。 || make revisions=修改 || The teacher asked us to make some revisions ___ our essays. || to|for|of || revisions to = 對…的修改。 || 老師要我們修改作文。
revolution || n. || 革命 || The Industrial Revolution changed the world. || 工業革命改變了世界。 || Industrial Revolution=工業革命 || The Industrial Revolution began ___ Britain in the 18th century. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 工業革命於十八世紀始於英國。
revolutionary || adj./n. || 革命性的 || The smartphone was a revolutionary invention. || 智慧型手機是一項革命性的發明。 || revolutionary idea=革命性的想法 || The smartphone was a revolutionary invention ___ changed our lives. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（invention）。 || 智慧型手機是一項改變我們生活的革命性發明。
reward || n./v. || 獎賞 || He got a reward for finding the dog. || 他因為找到那隻狗而獲得獎賞。 || as a reward=作為獎賞 || She was rewarded ___ her hard work. || for|of|with to || be rewarded for = 因…而得到獎賞。 || 她因努力而得到獎賞。
rhyme || n./v. || 押韻 || "Cat" rhymes with "hat." || cat 和 hat 押韻。 || nursery rhyme=童謠 || The word "cat" rhymes ___ "hat." || with|to|for || rhyme with = 和…押韻。 || cat 這個字和 hat 押韻。
rhythm || n. || 節奏 || The song has a nice rhythm. || 這首歌節奏很好。 || sense of rhythm=節奏感 || The dancers moved ___ the rhythm of the music. || to|with of|at || move to the rhythm = 隨著節奏移動。 || 舞者們隨著音樂的節奏舞動。
riddle || n. || 謎語 || Can you solve this riddle? || 你能解開這個謎語嗎？ || solve a riddle=解謎 || Nobody could solve the riddle ___ the teacher gave us. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 gave 的受詞。 || 沒有人能解開老師給我們的謎語。
robber || n. || 強盜 || The robber took all the money. || 強盜拿走了所有的錢。 || bank robber=銀行搶匪 || The robber ___ caught by the police two hours later. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 強盜兩小時後被警方逮捕。
romance || n. || 愛情；浪漫 || It's a story of romance and adventure. || 這是一個愛情與冒險的故事。 || romance novel=言情小說 || She enjoys reading romance novels ___ her free time. || in|on|at || in one's free time = 在空閒時。 || 她喜歡在空閒時看言情小說。
route || n. || 路線 || Which route should we take? || 我們該走哪條路線？ || bus route=公車路線 || This is the fastest route ___ the airport. || to|for|at || the route to = 通往…的路線。 || 這是去機場最快的路線。
