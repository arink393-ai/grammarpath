# 學測 L4 12：issue – margin
issue || n./v. || 議題；發行 || Climate change is an important issue. || 氣候變遷是一個重要議題。 || social issue=社會議題 || We discussed several social issues ___ class today. || in|on|at || in class = 在課堂上。 || 我們今天在課堂上討論了幾個社會議題。
jealousy || n. || 嫉妒 || Jealousy can ruin a friendship. || 嫉妒會毀了一段友誼。 || out of jealousy=出於嫉妒 || She said those mean things out ___ jealousy. || of|from|for || out of + 名詞 = 出於…。 || 她出於嫉妒說了那些刻薄的話。
keen || adj. || 熱衷的；敏銳的 || She is keen on music. || 她熱衷於音樂。 || be keen on=熱衷於… || He is keen ___ playing basketball. || on|in|at || be keen on = 熱衷於…。 || 他熱衷於打籃球。
kettle || n. || 水壺 || Put the kettle on. || 把水壺放上去燒水。 || electric kettle=電熱水壺 || The water in the kettle ___ boiling now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The water 用 is。 || 水壺裡的水正在沸騰。
kneel || v. || 跪下 || He kneels to pray every night. || 他每晚跪下禱告。 || kneel down=跪下 || He had to kneel down ___ fix the tire. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他得跪下來修輪胎。
knob || n. || 把手；旋鈕 || Turn the knob to open the door. || 轉動門把開門。 || door knob=門把 || Turn the knob ___ the left to open the door. || to|at|in || turn to the left = 向左轉。 || 把門把向左轉就能開門。
labor || n. || 勞動 || The job requires hard labor. || 這份工作需要辛勤的勞動。 || Labor Day=勞動節 || Many workers get a day off ___ Labor Day. || on|in|at || 特定的節日（日子）用 on。 || 許多勞工在勞動節放假一天。
laboratory || n. || 實驗室 || The students are working in the laboratory. || 學生們在實驗室工作。 || science laboratory=科學實驗室 || You must wear safety glasses ___ you are in the laboratory. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 在實驗室時必須戴護目鏡。
lag || v./n. || 落後 || He lagged behind the others. || 他落後其他人。 || jet lag=時差 || I had terrible jet lag ___ flying to New York. || after|before|since || after + V-ing = 在…之後。 || 飛到紐約之後，我的時差很嚴重。
landmark || n. || 地標 || Taipei 101 is a famous landmark. || 台北 101 是著名的地標。 || famous landmark=著名地標 || Taipei 101, ___ is Taiwan's most famous landmark, was completed in 2004. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物並當主詞，用 which。 || 台灣最著名的地標台北 101 於 2004 年完工。
landscape || n. || 風景 || The landscape here is beautiful. || 這裡的風景很美。 || natural landscape=自然景觀 || The landscape of Hualien is ___ beautiful that many tourists visit it. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 花蓮的風景美到吸引許多遊客。
largely || adv. || 主要地 || The success was largely due to her. || 成功主要歸功於她。 || largely due to=主要由於… || The team's success was largely due ___ hard work. || to|for|of || due to = 由於…。 || 隊伍的成功主要歸功於努力。
launch || v./n. || 發射；推出 || The company launched a new phone. || 公司推出了新手機。 || launch a product=推出產品 || The new phone ___ launched last month. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 新手機上個月推出。
lawful || adj. || 合法的 || It is not lawful to drive without a license. || 無照駕駛是不合法的。 || lawful act=合法行為 || It is not lawful ___ anyone under 18 to buy cigarettes. || for|of|to || It is + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 未滿十八歲的人購買香菸是不合法的。
lean || v./adj. || 倚靠；瘦的 || Don't lean on the door. || 不要靠在門上。 || lean against=靠著… || He leaned ___ the wall and closed his eyes. || against|at|to || lean against = 靠著…。 || 他靠著牆閉上眼睛。
learned || adj. || 博學的 || He is a learned man. || 他是一位博學的人。 || learned scholar=博學的學者 || He is a learned man ___ has read thousands of books. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 他是一位讀過數千本書的博學之人。
lecture || n./v. || 演講；講課 || The professor gave a lecture on history. || 教授做了一場關於歷史的演講。 || give a lecture=演講 || The professor gave a lecture ___ climate change. || on|in|at || a lecture on = 關於…的演講。 || 教授做了一場關於氣候變遷的演講。
lecturer || n. || 講師 || She is a lecturer at the university. || 她是大學講師。 || university lecturer=大學講師 || The lecturer asked the students ___ questions after the class. || to ask|asking|ask || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 講師請學生在下課後提問。
legend || n. || 傳說 || There is a legend about this lake. || 這座湖有一個傳說。 || according to legend=根據傳說 || ___ to legend, a dragon lives in the lake. || According|Accord|Accordingly || according to = 根據…。 || 根據傳說，湖裡住著一條龍。
leisurely || adj./adv. || 從容的 || We took a leisurely walk. || 我們悠閒地散步。 || leisurely walk=悠閒的散步 || We enjoyed a leisurely breakfast ___ Sunday morning. || on|in|at || 特定某天的早上用 on。 || 我們星期天早上悠閒地吃了早餐。
lengthen || v. || 延長 || The days lengthen in summer. || 夏天白天變長。 || lengthen the time=延長時間 || The school decided to lengthen the summer vacation ___ one week. || by|in|to || by + 數量 = 增加了…。 || 學校決定把暑假延長一週。
liar || n. || 說謊者 || Don't trust him. He's a liar. || 別相信他，他是個騙子。 || big liar=大騙子 || Nobody believes him anymore ___ he is a liar. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 沒有人再相信他了，因為他是個騙子。
librarian || n. || 圖書館員 || The librarian helped me find a book. || 圖書館員幫我找到一本書。 || school librarian=學校圖書館員 || The librarian asked us ___ quiet. || to be|being|be || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 圖書館員要我們保持安靜。
license || n./v. || 執照 || You need a license to drive. || 開車需要駕照。 || driver's license=駕照 || You must be at least 18 ___ get a driver's license. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 你必須年滿十八歲才能考駕照。
lifeguard || n. || 救生員 || The lifeguard saved the boy. || 救生員救了那個男孩。 || lifeguard=救生員 || Only swim ___ a lifeguard is on duty. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 只在救生員值勤時游泳。
limitation || n. || 限制 || Everyone has limitations. || 每個人都有極限。 || time limitation=時間限制 || Know your limitations and don't push ___ too hard. || yourself|you|your || 主詞與受詞同一人用反身代名詞 yourself。 || 了解自己的極限，別把自己逼太緊。
linen || n. || 亞麻布；寢具 || The sheets are made of linen. || 床單是亞麻做的。 || bed linen=寢具 || Linen clothes are cool ___ comfortable in summer. || and|but|or || 兩個優點並列用 and。 || 亞麻衣服在夏天穿起來涼爽又舒服。
lipstick || n. || 口紅 || She put on some lipstick. || 她擦了一點口紅。 || put on lipstick=擦口紅 || She always puts on lipstick ___ she goes out. || before|after|since || 出門「之前」擦口紅，用 before。 || 她出門前總會擦口紅。
liquor || n. || 烈酒 || Liquor is not sold to minors. || 烈酒不賣給未成年人。 || liquor store=酒類專賣店 || Liquor ___ not be sold to people under 18. || must|musts|to must || 助動詞 must 後接原形；被動 be + p.p.。 || 烈酒不得販售給未滿十八歲的人。
literary || adj. || 文學的 || She loves literary works. || 她熱愛文學作品。 || literary work=文學作品 || He won a literary prize ___ his first novel. || for|of|with || win a prize for = 因…得獎。 || 他因第一本小說獲得文學獎。
literature || n. || 文學 || I'm interested in English literature. || 我對英國文學有興趣。 || English literature=英國文學 || She wants to study literature ___ college. || in|on|of || in college = 在大學。 || 她想在大學讀文學。
loan || n./v. || 貸款；借出 || He took out a loan to buy a house. || 他貸款買房子。 || bank loan=銀行貸款 || He took out a loan ___ buy a new car. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他貸款買了一輛新車。
lobster || n. || 龍蝦 || We had lobster for dinner. || 我們晚餐吃龍蝦。 || lobster=龍蝦 || Lobster is ___ expensive than shrimp. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 龍蝦比蝦子貴。
logic || n. || 邏輯 || There is no logic in his argument. || 他的論點沒有邏輯。 || logic=邏輯 || I can't follow the logic ___ his argument. || of|for|to || the logic of = …的邏輯。 || 我無法理解他論點的邏輯。
logical || adj. || 合乎邏輯的 || That's a logical answer. || 那是個合乎邏輯的答案。 || logical thinking=邏輯思考 || It's logical ___ bring an umbrella when it looks like rain. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 看起來快下雨時帶傘是合理的。
loosen || v. || 鬆開 || He loosened his tie. || 他鬆開了領帶。 || loosen up=放鬆 || He loosened his belt ___ eating a big dinner. || after|before|until || after + V-ing = 在…之後。 || 吃完一頓大餐後，他鬆開了皮帶。
lousy || adj. || 糟糕的 || The weather is lousy today. || 今天天氣很糟。 || lousy food=難吃的食物 || The service at the restaurant was ___ lousy that we will never go back. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那家餐廳的服務差到我們再也不會去了。
loyal || adj. || 忠誠的 || Dogs are loyal to their owners. || 狗對主人很忠誠。 || be loyal to=對…忠誠 || Dogs are loyal ___ their owners. || to|for|with || be loyal to = 對…忠誠。 || 狗對主人很忠誠。
loyalty || n. || 忠誠 || His loyalty to the team is admirable. || 他對球隊的忠誠令人欽佩。 || customer loyalty=顧客忠誠度 || She showed great loyalty ___ her friends. || to|for|with || loyalty to = 對…的忠誠。 || 她對朋友非常忠誠。
luxurious || adj. || 奢華的 || They stayed in a luxurious hotel. || 他們住在一間豪華飯店。 || luxurious hotel=豪華飯店 || The hotel was the ___ luxurious one I have ever stayed in. || most|more|much || 最高級 the most + 形容詞。 || 這是我住過最豪華的飯店。
luxury || n. || 奢侈；奢侈品 || They live in luxury. || 他們過著奢華的生活。 || live in luxury=過奢華的生活 || For many people, a car is a luxury ___ a necessity. || rather than|instead|other than to || rather than = 而不是…。 || 對很多人來說，汽車是奢侈品而不是必需品。
machinery || n. || 機械（總稱） || The factory has new machinery. || 這家工廠有新的機械設備。 || farm machinery=農業機械 || The farm machinery ___ very expensive. || is|are|be || machinery 不可數，用 is。 || 農業機械非常昂貴。
magnetic || adj. || 磁性的；有魅力的 || The fridge magnet is magnetic. || 冰箱磁鐵是有磁性的。 || magnetic field=磁場 || The Earth has a magnetic field ___ protects us from the sun's radiation. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a magnetic field）。 || 地球有一個保護我們免受太陽輻射的磁場。
magnificent || adj. || 壯麗的 || The view is magnificent. || 景色很壯麗。 || magnificent view=壯麗的景色 || The palace is ___ magnificent than I expected. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 這座宮殿比我預期的更壯麗。
makeup || n. || 化妝品 || She doesn't wear makeup. || 她不化妝。 || put on makeup=化妝 || She spent an hour putting ___ her makeup. || on|in|up || put on makeup = 化妝。 || 她花了一個小時化妝。
manual || n./adj. || 手冊；手動的 || Read the manual before using the machine. || 使用機器前先讀說明書。 || user manual=使用手冊 || Read the manual carefully ___ you use the machine. || before|after|since || 使用「之前」讀說明書，用 before。 || 使用機器之前要仔細閱讀說明書。
manufacture || v./n. || 製造 || The company manufactures cars. || 這家公司製造汽車。 || manufacture goods=製造商品 || Most of the world's computer chips ___ manufactured in Taiwan. || are|is|be || 被動語態；chips 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 世界上大部分的電腦晶片是在台灣製造的。
manufacturer || n. || 製造商 || He works for a car manufacturer. || 他在一家汽車製造商工作。 || car manufacturer=汽車製造商 || The car manufacturer, ___ factory is in Taoyuan, hires 3,000 workers. || whose|which|who || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那家工廠位於桃園的汽車製造商雇用了三千名員工。
marathon || n. || 馬拉松 || She ran a marathon last year. || 她去年跑了一場馬拉松。 || run a marathon=跑馬拉松 || He trained for six months ___ running the marathon. || before|after|since || 跑馬拉松「之前」訓練，用 before。 || 他在跑馬拉松之前訓練了六個月。
margin || n. || 頁邊空白；差額 || Write your notes in the margin. || 把筆記寫在頁邊空白處。 || by a small margin=以些微差距 || Our team won the game ___ a small margin. || by|with|in || by a margin = 以…差距。 || 我們隊以些微差距贏了比賽。
