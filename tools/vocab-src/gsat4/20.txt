# 學測 L4 20：troublesome – youthful
troublesome || adj. || 麻煩的 || The problem is troublesome. || 這個問題很麻煩。 || troublesome child=令人頭痛的孩子 || Filling out all these forms is ___ troublesome. || very|much|many || 形容詞前面加 very 表示「很」。 || 填寫所有這些表格很麻煩。
tumble || v./n. || 跌倒 || The boy tumbled down the stairs. || 男孩從樓梯上摔下來。 || tumble down=跌落 || The boy tumbled down the stairs ___ he was running. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 男孩在奔跑時從樓梯上摔了下來。
twig || n. || 細枝 || The bird built a nest with twigs. || 鳥用細枝築巢。 || dry twig=枯枝 || The bird built its nest ___ small twigs. || with|by|of || with + 材料 = 用…。 || 鳥用小樹枝築巢。
underneath || prep./adv. || 在…下面 || The cat is hiding underneath the car. || 貓躲在車子底下。 || underneath=在…底下 || I found my keys underneath the sofa ___ I was cleaning. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 我打掃時在沙發底下找到了鑰匙。
universal || adj. || 普遍的 || Music is a universal language. || 音樂是一種世界共通的語言。 || universal language=共通語言 || Music is a universal language ___ everyone can understand. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 understand 的受詞。 || 音樂是每個人都能理解的共通語言。
urge || v./n. || 催促；敦促 || The doctor urged him to stop smoking. || 醫生敦促他戒菸。 || urge sb to V=敦促某人做… || The teacher urged us ___ harder for the exam. || to study|studying|study || urge sb to + 原形動詞。 || 老師敦促我們為考試更加用功。
urgent || adj. || 緊急的 || This is an urgent matter. || 這是件緊急的事。 || urgent message=緊急訊息 || I have an urgent message ___ the manager. || for|to|of || a message for sb = 給某人的訊息。 || 我有一則緊急訊息要給經理。
usage || n. || 用法；使用 || The usage of this word is tricky. || 這個字的用法很難。 || water usage=用水量 || Our water usage ___ increased a lot this summer. || has|have|is || 現在完成式；usage 單數用 has。 || 我們今年夏天的用水量增加了很多。
vacancy || n. || 空缺；空房 || Do you have any vacancies? || 你們還有空房嗎？ || job vacancy=職缺 || Sorry, there are no vacancies ___ the hotel tonight. || in|on|at of || 在飯店裡用 in。 || 抱歉，飯店今晚沒有空房了。
vain || adj. || 徒勞的；自負的 || All his efforts were in vain. || 他所有的努力都白費了。 || in vain=徒勞 || We tried to save the tree, but ___ vain. || in|on|at || in vain = 徒勞地。 || 我們試圖救那棵樹，但徒勞無功。
vast || adj. || 廣大的 || The desert is vast. || 沙漠一望無際。 || vast majority=絕大多數 || The vast majority of students ___ smartphones. || have|has|having || the majority of + 複數名詞，動詞用複數 have。 || 絕大多數學生都有智慧型手機。
vegetarian || n./adj. || 素食者 || She is a vegetarian. || 她吃素。 || vegetarian food=素食 || She has been a vegetarian ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她吃素已經十年了。
vessel || n. || 船；容器 || The vessel sailed across the ocean. || 那艘船橫越大洋。 || blood vessel=血管 || The fishing vessel ___ caught in a storm last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那艘漁船昨晚遇上了暴風雨。
violate || v. || 違反 || He violated the rules. || 他違反了規定。 || violate the law=違法 || Anyone ___ violates the rules will be punished. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 任何違反規定的人都會受到處罰。
violation || n. || 違反 || This is a violation of the law. || 這違反了法律。 || traffic violation=交通違規 || Running a red light is a violation ___ traffic laws. || of|to|for || a violation of = 對…的違反。 || 闖紅燈違反交通法規。
virtue || n. || 美德 || Patience is a virtue. || 耐心是一種美德。 || by virtue of=憑藉… || Patience is a virtue ___ everyone should learn. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 learn 的受詞。 || 耐心是每個人都應該學習的美德。
virus || n. || 病毒 || The virus spread quickly. || 病毒迅速傳播。 || computer virus=電腦病毒 || The virus spread quickly ___ the country. || across|along|through of || across = 遍及…。 || 病毒迅速在全國擴散。
visual || adj. || 視覺的 || Pictures are visual aids. || 圖片是視覺輔助工具。 || visual aid=視覺輔助 || Visual aids help students ___ the lesson better. || understand|understanding|to understanding || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 視覺輔助工具幫助學生更了解課程內容。
vital || adj. || 極重要的 || Water is vital to life. || 水對生命至關重要。 || be vital to=對…極為重要 || Sleep is vital ___ your health. || to|for|with of || be vital to = 對…極為重要。 || 睡眠對你的健康極為重要。
voluntary || adj. || 自願的 || The activity is voluntary. || 這個活動是自願參加的。 || voluntary work=志工工作 || Joining the club is voluntary, ___ you don't have to. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 參加社團是自願的，所以你不一定要參加。
volunteer || n./v. || 志工；自願 || She works as a volunteer at the hospital. || 她在醫院當志工。 || volunteer work=志工服務 || Many students volunteer ___ help clean the beach. || to|for|of || volunteer to + 原形動詞 = 自願做…。 || 許多學生自願幫忙淨灘。
voyage || n. || 航行 || The voyage took three months. || 這趟航行花了三個月。 || long voyage=長途航行 || The ship's first voyage ___ in 1912. || was|were|is || 過去的事，主詞單數用 was。 || 那艘船的首航是在 1912 年。
waken || v. || 喚醒 || The noise wakened the baby. || 噪音吵醒了寶寶。 || waken sb=叫醒某人 || The baby ___ wakened by the loud noise. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 寶寶被很大的聲音吵醒了。
website || n. || 網站 || Check the school website for news. || 查看學校網站上的消息。 || official website=官方網站 || You can find more information ___ our website. || on|in|at || on a website = 在網站上。 || 你可以在我們的網站上找到更多資訊。
welfare || n. || 福利 || The government cares about people's welfare. || 政府關心人民的福利。 || social welfare=社會福利 || The government should care about the welfare ___ its people. || of|for|to || the welfare of = …的福利。 || 政府應該關心人民的福利。
wink || v./n. || 眨眼示意 || He winked at me. || 他對我眨了眨眼。 || wink at=對…眨眼 || He winked ___ me to show it was a joke. || at|to|on || wink at = 對…眨眼。 || 他對我眨眼表示那是個玩笑。
wit || n. || 機智 || He is known for his wit. || 他以機智聞名。 || at one's wits' end=智窮計盡 || She is famous ___ her wit and humor. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 她以機智和幽默聞名。
witch || n. || 女巫 || The witch cast a spell. || 女巫施了咒語。 || wicked witch=邪惡的女巫 || The princess ___ turned into a frog by the witch. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 公主被女巫變成了一隻青蛙。
withdraw || v. || 提款；撤回 || I need to withdraw some money. || 我需要領一些錢。 || withdraw money=提款 || I need to withdraw some money ___ the ATM. || from|of|at of || withdraw from = 從…提取。 || 我需要從提款機領一些錢。
witness || n./v. || 目擊者 || The witness saw the accident. || 目擊者看到了那場事故。 || eye witness=目擊證人 || The police asked the witness ___ what he saw. || to describe|describing|describe || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 警方請目擊者描述他所看到的事。
workout || n. || 鍛鍊 || I do a 30-minute workout every day. || 我每天鍛鍊三十分鐘。 || daily workout=每日鍛鍊 || A short workout every morning ___ me feel energetic. || makes|make|making || 主詞 A short workout 單數，動詞加 s。 || 每天早上短暫的鍛鍊讓我精神充沛。
workplace || n. || 職場 || Safety in the workplace is important. || 職場安全很重要。 || in the workplace=在職場 || Good communication is important ___ the workplace. || in|on|at of || in the workplace = 在職場中。 || 良好的溝通在職場上很重要。
wreck || n./v. || 殘骸；毀壞 || The storm wrecked the boat. || 暴風雨摧毀了那艘船。 || shipwreck=船難 || The ship ___ wrecked in a storm 100 years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那艘船在一百年前的暴風雨中失事。
yawn || v./n. || 打呵欠 || He yawned because he was tired. || 他打呵欠，因為他累了。 || give a yawn=打個呵欠 || He kept yawning ___ the boring lecture. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 在無聊的課堂上他一直打呵欠。
youthful || adj. || 年輕的；有朝氣的 || She has a youthful face. || 她有一張年輕的臉。 || youthful energy=青春活力 || Although she is 60, she still looks ___ youthful. || very|much|many || 形容詞前面加 very 表示「很」。 || 雖然她六十歲了，看起來還是很年輕。
