# 學測 L4 08：eventual – fort
eventual || adj. || 最終的 || The eventual winner was a young girl. || 最後的贏家是一位年輕女孩。 || eventual result=最終結果 || The eventual winner of the contest ___ only fifteen years old. || was|were|is being || 過去的事，主詞單數用 was。 || 比賽最後的贏家只有十五歲。
evidence || n. || 證據 || There is no evidence that he did it. || 沒有證據顯示是他做的。 || scientific evidence=科學證據 || There is no evidence ___ the man stole the money. || that|what|which || evidence that… = …的證據（同位語子句）。 || 沒有證據顯示那名男子偷了錢。
evident || adj. || 明顯的 || It was evident that she was upset. || 很明顯她不高興。 || it is evident that=很明顯… || It is evident ___ he didn't study for the test. || that|what|which || It is evident that… = 很明顯…。 || 很明顯他沒有準備考試。
exaggerate || v. || 誇大 || Don't exaggerate. || 別誇大其詞。 || exaggerate the problem=誇大問題 || He tends ___ exaggerate everything. || to|for|in || tend to + 原形動詞。 || 他總是傾向誇大每件事。
exception || n. || 例外 || There is an exception to every rule. || 每條規則都有例外。 || with the exception of=除了…之外 || Everyone passed the test ___ the exception of Tom. || with|in|by || with the exception of = 除了…之外。 || 除了湯姆以外，每個人都通過了考試。
exhaust || v./n. || 使筋疲力盡；廢氣 || The long hike exhausted us. || 長途健行讓我們筋疲力盡。 || car exhaust=汽車廢氣 || Car exhaust is one of the main causes ___ air pollution. || of|for|to || the cause of = …的原因。 || 汽車廢氣是空氣污染的主要原因之一。
exhibit || n./v. || 展覽品；展出 || The museum exhibits ancient art. || 這間博物館展出古代藝術品。 || on exhibit=展出中 || The paintings ___ exhibited in the museum until next month. || will be|will|are being be || 未來被動：will be + p.p.。 || 這些畫將在博物館展出到下個月。
expand || v. || 擴大 || The company plans to expand. || 公司打算擴張。 || expand a business=擴展事業 || The company is planning to expand its business ___ Japan next year. || to|at|of || expand to + 地點 = 擴展到…。 || 這家公司計畫明年把業務擴展到日本。
expansion || n. || 擴張 || The expansion of the city is fast. || 這座城市擴張得很快。 || rapid expansion=快速擴張 || The expansion ___ the airport will take three years. || of|for|to || the expansion of = …的擴建。 || 機場的擴建工程將花三年。
experimental || adj. || 實驗性的 || The drug is still experimental. || 這種藥仍在實驗階段。 || experimental stage=實驗階段 || The new medicine is still ___ the experimental stage. || at|of|on || at the … stage = 在…階段。 || 這種新藥仍在實驗階段。
explanation || n. || 解釋 || Can you give me an explanation? || 你能給我一個解釋嗎？ || give an explanation=解釋 || The teacher gave ___ clear explanation of the rule. || a|an|the || clear 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 老師對這條規則做了清楚的解釋。
explosion || n. || 爆炸 || There was a loud explosion. || 發生了一聲巨響的爆炸。 || gas explosion=氣爆 || The explosion ___ caused by a gas leak. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這場爆炸是瓦斯外洩造成的。
explosive || adj./n. || 爆炸性的；炸藥 || The police found explosives in the car. || 警方在車內找到了炸藥。 || explosive growth=爆炸性成長 || Explosives ___ not allowed on the plane. || are|is|be || Explosives 複數，用 are。 || 飛機上禁止攜帶爆裂物。
expose || v. || 暴露；揭發 || Don't expose your skin to the sun too long. || 別讓皮膚在陽光下曝曬太久。 || be exposed to=暴露於… || Children should not be exposed ___ violent video games. || to|with|for || be exposed to = 接觸、暴露於…。 || 兒童不應該接觸暴力電玩。
exposure || n. || 暴露；接觸 || Too much exposure to the sun is bad for your skin. || 過度曝曬對皮膚不好。 || exposure to=接觸… || Too much exposure ___ screens can hurt your eyes. || to|with|of || exposure to = 接觸…。 || 過度接觸螢幕會傷害眼睛。
extend || v. || 延長；延伸 || They extended the deadline. || 他們延長了截止日期。 || extend a deadline=延長期限 || The deadline ___ extended to next Friday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 截止日期延到了下週五。
extent || n. || 程度；範圍 || To some extent, I agree with you. || 在某種程度上，我同意你的看法。 || to some extent=在某種程度上 || ___ some extent, the plan was successful. || To|In|At || to some extent = 在某種程度上。 || 在某種程度上，這個計畫是成功的。
facial || adj. || 臉部的 || She has a facial treatment every month. || 她每個月做一次臉部保養。 || facial expression=臉部表情 || You can often tell how people feel ___ their facial expressions. || from|of|with || tell … from = 從…看出…。 || 你常常可以從人們的臉部表情看出他們的感受。
facility || n. || 設施 || The school has good sports facilities. || 這所學校有很好的運動設施。 || sports facilities=運動設施 || The school has excellent facilities ___ students with disabilities. || for|to|of || for + 人 = 為…而設。 || 這所學校為身心障礙學生提供了良好的設施。
faithful || adj. || 忠實的 || Dogs are faithful animals. || 狗是忠誠的動物。 || be faithful to=對…忠誠 || The dog remained faithful ___ its owner. || to|for|with || be faithful to = 對…忠誠。 || 那隻狗始終對主人忠心耿耿。
fame || n. || 名聲 || She rose to fame after the show. || 那個節目之後她就成名了。 || rise to fame=成名 || The singer rose ___ fame after winning the contest. || to|in|at || rise to fame = 成名。 || 那位歌手贏得比賽後一舉成名。
fantastic || adj. || 極好的 || We had a fantastic time. || 我們玩得非常開心。 || fantastic view=絕佳的景色 || The view from the top was ___ fantastic that we didn't want to leave. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 山頂的景色美到我們都不想離開。
fantasy || n. || 幻想 || He lives in a fantasy world. || 他活在幻想的世界裡。 || fantasy novel=奇幻小說 || Harry Potter is a fantasy series ___ many children love. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 love 的受詞。 || 《哈利波特》是許多孩子喜愛的奇幻系列。
farewell || n. || 告別 || We had a farewell party for her. || 我們為她辦了一場歡送會。 || farewell party=歡送會 || We held a farewell party ___ our teacher who is retiring. || for|to|of || for + 人 = 為…。 || 我們為即將退休的老師辦了一場歡送會。
fasten || v. || 繫緊 || Please fasten your seat belt. || 請繫好安全帶。 || fasten a seat belt=繫安全帶 || Please fasten your seat belt ___ the plane takes off. || before|after|since || 起飛「之前」繫好，用 before。 || 飛機起飛前請繫好安全帶。
fatal || adj. || 致命的 || The accident was fatal. || 那起事故是致命的。 || fatal mistake=致命的錯誤 || Driving after drinking can lead ___ a fatal accident. || to|for|in || lead to = 導致…。 || 酒後開車可能導致致命的車禍。
favorable || adj. || 有利的；贊同的 || The weather is favorable for a picnic. || 天氣很適合野餐。 || favorable weather=有利的天氣 || The weather was favorable ___ the outdoor concert. || for|to be|with || be favorable for = 對…有利。 || 天氣很適合舉辦戶外音樂會。
fax || n./v. || 傳真 || Please fax me the document. || 請把文件傳真給我。 || send a fax=傳真 || Please fax the document ___ me as soon as possible. || to|for|with || fax sth to sb = 把某物傳真給某人。 || 請儘快把文件傳真給我。
feast || n. || 盛宴 || We had a big feast on New Year's Eve. || 我們在除夕吃了一頓大餐。 || wedding feast=喜宴 || The whole family gathered ___ a feast on New Year's Eve. || for|to|with || gather for + 活動 = 為…而聚集。 || 除夕全家人團聚吃年夜飯。
feedback || n. || 回饋 || Thank you for your feedback. || 謝謝你的回饋。 || give feedback=給予回饋 || The teacher gave us some useful feedback ___ our presentations. || on|in|at || feedback on = 對…的回饋。 || 老師對我們的報告給了一些有用的回饋。
ferry || n. || 渡輪 || We took a ferry to the island. || 我們搭渡輪到島上。 || take a ferry=搭渡輪 || We went to Green Island ___ ferry. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by（中間不加冠詞）。 || 我們搭渡輪去綠島。
fertile || adj. || 肥沃的 || The soil here is very fertile. || 這裡的土壤非常肥沃。 || fertile soil=肥沃的土壤 || The land is fertile, ___ farmers can grow many crops. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這片土地很肥沃，所以農夫可以種很多作物。
fetch || v. || 拿來 || Can you fetch me a glass of water? || 你可以幫我拿一杯水嗎？ || play fetch=玩丟接遊戲 || The dog ran to fetch the ball ___ I threw it. || after|before|until || 丟「之後」去撿，用 after。 || 我把球丟出去之後，狗跑去把它撿回來。
fiction || n. || 小說；虛構 || I like reading science fiction. || 我喜歡讀科幻小說。 || science fiction=科幻小說 || Truth is stranger ___ fiction. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 事實比小說更離奇。
fierce || adj. || 兇猛的；激烈的 || The competition was fierce. || 競爭很激烈。 || fierce competition=激烈的競爭 || The competition for the job was ___ fierce that only one out of 100 applicants got it. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這份工作的競爭激烈到一百名申請者只錄取一人。
finance || n./v. || 財務；資助 || She works in finance. || 她在金融業工作。 || personal finance=個人理財 || The project ___ financed by the government. || was|were|be || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個計畫由政府資助。
financial || adj. || 財務的 || The company has financial problems. || 這家公司有財務問題。 || financial support=經濟支援 || He needs financial support ___ his studies. || for|to|of || support for = 對…的支持。 || 他需要經濟援助來完成學業。
fireplace || n. || 壁爐 || We sat by the fireplace. || 我們坐在壁爐旁。 || by the fireplace=在壁爐旁 || The family sat ___ the fireplace on the cold night. || by|in|on || by = 在…旁邊。 || 寒冷的夜晚，一家人坐在壁爐旁。
flatter || v. || 奉承 || You're flattering me. || 你過獎了。 || be flattered=受寵若驚 || I'm flattered ___ you remember my name. || that|what|which || be flattered that… = 對…感到受寵若驚。 || 你記得我的名字讓我受寵若驚。
flea || n. || 跳蚤 || The dog has fleas. || 那隻狗有跳蚤。 || flea market=跳蚤市場 || We bought some old books ___ a flea market. || at|in|on || at a market = 在市集。 || 我們在跳蚤市場買了一些舊書。
flee || v. || 逃跑 || People had to flee the fire. || 人們不得不逃離火災。 || flee from=從…逃離 || Many people had to flee ___ their homes because of the war. || from|of|out || flee from = 從…逃離。 || 許多人因戰爭而不得不逃離家園。
flexible || adj. || 有彈性的 || My schedule is flexible. || 我的時間很有彈性。 || flexible schedule=彈性的時間表 || My working hours are flexible, ___ I can pick up my kids after school. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 我的上班時間很有彈性，所以可以放學後接孩子。
fluent || adj. || 流利的 || She is fluent in three languages. || 她能流利地說三種語言。 || be fluent in=精通…語 || She is fluent ___ English and Japanese. || in|at|with || be fluent in = 精通…語言。 || 她精通英文和日文。
flush || v. || 沖水；臉紅 || Please flush the toilet. || 請沖馬桶。 || flush the toilet=沖馬桶 || Don't forget ___ flush the toilet. || to|for|of || forget to V = 忘記要做。 || 別忘了沖馬桶。
foam || n. || 泡沫 || The waves left foam on the beach. || 浪花在沙灘上留下泡沫。 || foam cup=保麗龍杯 || The coffee has a thick layer ___ foam on top. || of|with|in || a layer of = 一層…。 || 咖啡上面有一層厚厚的泡沫。
forbid || v. || 禁止 || Smoking is forbidden here. || 這裡禁止吸菸。 || forbid sb to V=禁止某人做… || My parents forbid me ___ out late at night. || to go|going|go || forbid sb to + 原形動詞。 || 我父母禁止我深夜外出。
forecast || n./v. || 預報 || The weather forecast says it will rain. || 氣象預報說會下雨。 || weather forecast=氣象預報 || According ___ the weather forecast, it will rain tomorrow. || to|with|for || according to = 根據…。 || 根據氣象預報，明天會下雨。
formation || n. || 形成；隊形 || The formation of the rock took millions of years. || 這塊岩石的形成花了數百萬年。 || rock formation=岩層 || The formation ___ the canyon took millions of years. || of|for|in || the formation of = …的形成。 || 這座峽谷的形成花了數百萬年。
formula || n. || 公式；配方 || Learn this math formula. || 學會這個數學公式。 || math formula=數學公式 || There is no magic formula ___ success. || for|of|to || a formula for = …的祕訣。 || 成功沒有神奇的祕訣。
fort || n. || 堡壘 || Fort San Domingo is in Tamsui. || 紅毛城在淡水。 || old fort=古堡 || The fort ___ built by the Spanish in 1628. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這座堡壘是西班牙人在 1628 年建造的。
