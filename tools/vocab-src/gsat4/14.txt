# 學測 L4 14：nevertheless – peculiar
nevertheless || adv. || 然而 || It was raining. Nevertheless, we went out. || 下著雨，然而我們還是出門了。 || nevertheless=然而 || The test was hard. Nevertheless, most students ___ it. || passed|pass|passes || 描述過去的事用過去式 passed。 || 考試很難，然而大多數學生都通過了。
nightmare || n. || 惡夢 || I had a nightmare last night. || 我昨晚做了惡夢。 || have a nightmare=做惡夢 || I had a nightmare ___ I was being chased by a monster. || in which|which|what || 關係代名詞 which 前加介系詞 in（= in the nightmare）。 || 我做了一個被怪物追的惡夢。
noble || adj. || 高貴的；崇高的 || Helping others is a noble act. || 幫助他人是崇高的行為。 || noble cause=崇高的目標 || It is noble ___ you to donate your time to help others. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你貢獻時間幫助他人是很崇高的。
nonsense || n. || 胡說 || That's nonsense! || 那是胡說八道！ || talk nonsense=胡說 || Stop talking nonsense and tell me ___ really happened. || what|which|that || 名詞子句 what happened = 發生了什麼事。 || 別胡說了，告訴我到底發生了什麼事。
nowadays || adv. || 現今 || Nowadays, many people shop online. || 現在很多人網購。 || nowadays=如今 || Nowadays, more and more people ___ their phones to pay. || use|uses|using || people 複數，動詞用原形。 || 如今越來越多人用手機付款。
nuclear || adj. || 核子的 || Nuclear energy is controversial. || 核能具有爭議性。 || nuclear power=核能 || Some people are against nuclear power ___ it can be dangerous. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 有些人反對核能，因為它可能很危險。
numerous || adj. || 許多的 || She has won numerous awards. || 她贏得了許多獎項。 || numerous times=無數次 || I have told you numerous times not ___ be late. || to|for|of || tell sb not to + 原形動詞。 || 我已經告訴你無數次不要遲到了。
nursery || n. || 托兒所；苗圃 || My little brother goes to a nursery. || 我弟弟上托兒所。 || nursery school=幼兒園 || Parents can leave their children at the nursery ___ they are at work. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 父母上班時可以把孩子留在托兒所。
nutritious || adj. || 有營養的 || Vegetables are nutritious. || 蔬菜很有營養。 || nutritious food=營養的食物 || A nutritious breakfast helps you ___ well at school. || do|to doing|doing || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 營養的早餐幫助你在學校表現良好。
obedience || n. || 服從 || Dogs can be trained in obedience. || 狗可以接受服從訓練。 || obedience training=服從訓練 || The dog was trained ___ obedience. || in|on|with || train in = 接受…的訓練。 || 那隻狗接受過服從訓練。
obedient || adj. || 順從的 || The dog is very obedient. || 那隻狗非常聽話。 || obedient child=聽話的孩子 || The dog is obedient ___ its owner. || to|for|with || be obedient to = 服從…。 || 那隻狗很聽主人的話。
objection || n. || 反對 || Do you have any objections? || 你有任何異議嗎？ || have an objection to=反對… || I have no objection ___ your plan. || to|for|with || have an objection to = 反對…。 || 我不反對你的計畫。
objective || n./adj. || 目標；客觀的 || Our main objective is to win. || 我們的主要目標是贏。 || objective opinion=客觀的意見 || The main objective ___ this course is to improve your speaking. || of|for|to || the objective of = …的目標。 || 這門課的主要目標是提升你的口說能力。
observation || n. || 觀察 || The patient is under observation. || 病人在觀察中。 || under observation=觀察中 || The patient will stay in the hospital ___ observation. || for|in|to || for observation = 為了觀察。 || 病人將留院觀察。
obstacle || n. || 障礙 || Lack of money is the biggest obstacle. || 缺錢是最大的障礙。 || overcome obstacles=克服障礙 || Fear is the biggest obstacle ___ success. || to|of|for || an obstacle to = …的障礙。 || 恐懼是成功最大的障礙。
obtain || v. || 獲得 || You need to obtain a visa. || 你需要取得簽證。 || obtain information=取得資訊 || You must obtain permission ___ you use the lab. || before|after|since || 使用「之前」取得許可，用 before。 || 使用實驗室之前必須取得許可。
occasional || adj. || 偶爾的 || We have occasional rain in winter. || 冬天偶爾下雨。 || occasional visit=偶爾的拜訪 || Apart from occasional rain, the weather ___ nice during our trip. || was|were|is || 過去的事，主詞 the weather 用 was。 || 除了偶爾下雨之外，我們旅行期間天氣都很好。
occupation || n. || 職業 || What is your occupation? || 你的職業是什麼？ || by occupation=職業是… || Please write your name and occupation ___ the form. || on|of|at || 寫在表格上用 on。 || 請在表格上寫下你的姓名和職業。
occupy || v. || 佔據 || The seat is occupied. || 這個座位有人坐。 || be occupied with=忙於… || She is occupied ___ her work right now. || with|in|by || be occupied with = 忙於…。 || 她現在正忙著工作。
offend || v. || 冒犯 || I didn't mean to offend you. || 我無意冒犯你。 || be offended by=被…冒犯 || She was offended ___ his rude comment. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 她被他無禮的評論冒犯了。
offense || n. || 冒犯；罪行 || No offense, but I disagree. || 無意冒犯，但我不同意。 || no offense=無意冒犯; take offense=生氣 || Don't take offense ___ what he said. || at|to|with || take offense at = 對…生氣。 || 別因他說的話生氣。
offensive || adj. || 冒犯的 || His words were offensive. || 他的話很冒犯人。 || offensive language=冒犯的言語 || Some jokes can be offensive ___ certain people. || to|for|with || be offensive to = 冒犯…。 || 有些笑話可能會冒犯某些人。
opera || n. || 歌劇 || We went to see an opera. || 我們去看了一齣歌劇。 || Chinese opera=戲曲 || Taiwanese opera is a traditional art ___ has a long history. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（art）。 || 歌仔戲是一種有悠久歷史的傳統藝術。
oppose || v. || 反對 || Many people oppose the new law. || 許多人反對新法律。 || be opposed to=反對… || Many parents are opposed ___ the new rule. || to|for|with || be opposed to = 反對…。 || 許多家長反對新規定。
option || n. || 選擇 || You have two options. || 你有兩個選擇。 || have no option but to V=別無選擇只好… || We had no option but ___ wait. || to|for|of || have no option but to + 原形動詞。 || 我們別無選擇只好等待。
orbit || n./v. || 軌道 || The moon orbits the Earth. || 月球繞著地球轉。 || in orbit=在軌道上 || The satellite has been ___ orbit for ten years. || in|on|at || in orbit = 在軌道上。 || 這顆衛星已經在軌道上運行十年了。
orchestra || n. || 管弦樂團 || She plays violin in the orchestra. || 她在管弦樂團拉小提琴。 || symphony orchestra=交響樂團 || She has played in the school orchestra ___ three years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她在學校管弦樂團演奏已經三年了。
orientation || n. || 新生訓練；方向 || New students have orientation on Monday. || 新生週一有新生訓練。 || new student orientation=新生訓練 || All freshmen must attend the orientation ___ the first day of school. || on|in|at || 特定的日子用 on。 || 所有新生都必須在開學第一天參加新生訓練。
orphan || n. || 孤兒 || He became an orphan at a young age. || 他年紀很小就成了孤兒。 || orphan=孤兒 || The orphans ___ taken care of by the church. || are|is|be || 被動語態；orphans 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 這些孤兒由教會照顧。
otherwise || adv. || 否則 || Hurry up, otherwise we'll be late. || 快點，否則我們會遲到。 || otherwise=否則 || You should study hard; otherwise, you ___ fail the exam. || will|would have|are || 表示將會發生的結果用 will + 原形動詞。 || 你應該用功讀書，否則考試會不及格。
outcome || n. || 結果 || We are waiting for the outcome. || 我們在等結果。 || final outcome=最終結果 || Nobody knows what the outcome ___ the game will be. || of|for|to || the outcome of = …的結果。 || 沒有人知道比賽結果會如何。
outstanding || adj. || 傑出的 || She is an outstanding student. || 她是一位傑出的學生。 || outstanding performance=傑出的表現 || She received an award ___ her outstanding performance. || for|of|with || an award for = 因…而得的獎。 || 她因表現傑出而獲獎。
oval || adj./n. || 橢圓形的 || The table is oval. || 這張桌子是橢圓形的。 || oval face=鵝蛋臉 || An egg is ___ oval shape. || an|a|the || oval 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 蛋是橢圓形的。
overcoat || n. || 大衣 || Put on your overcoat. It's cold. || 穿上大衣，天氣很冷。 || winter overcoat=冬季大衣 || He took off his overcoat ___ he entered the room. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 他進房間時脫下了大衣。
overcome || v. || 克服 || You can overcome your fear. || 你可以克服你的恐懼。 || overcome difficulties=克服困難 || With hard work, you can overcome any difficulty ___ you face. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 face 的受詞。 || 只要努力，你就能克服所面臨的任何困難。
overlook || v. || 忽略；俯瞰 || Don't overlook the details. || 不要忽略細節。 || overlook a mistake=忽略錯誤 || The hotel room overlooks the sea, ___ is beautiful. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句 which 指代整個前句或前面名詞。 || 飯店房間可以俯瞰大海，景色很美。
overnight || adv./adj. || 一夜之間；過夜 || She became famous overnight. || 她一夜成名。 || stay overnight=過夜 || We stayed overnight ___ a small hotel. || at|on|to || 在某個地點（旅館）用 at。 || 我們在一間小旅館過夜。
overthrow || v. || 推翻 || The people wanted to overthrow the king. || 人民想要推翻國王。 || overthrow a government=推翻政府 || The king ___ overthrown by his people in 1789. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 國王在 1789 年被人民推翻。
oxygen || n. || 氧氣 || We need oxygen to live. || 我們需要氧氣才能生存。 || oxygen mask=氧氣罩 || Plants produce oxygen ___ we need to breathe. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 need 的受詞。 || 植物產生我們呼吸所需的氧氣。
pace || n. || 步調；速度 || Walk at your own pace. || 按照你自己的步調走。 || at one's own pace=按自己的步調 || Everyone learns ___ their own pace. || at|in|on || at one's own pace = 按自己的步調。 || 每個人都按自己的步調學習。
panel || n. || 小組；面板 || A panel of judges chose the winner. || 評審團選出了優勝者。 || solar panel=太陽能板 || Solar panels ___ installed on the roof of our school last year. || were|was|are || 被動語態；panels 複數，過去用 were。 || 去年我們學校屋頂裝設了太陽能板。
parachute || n./v. || 降落傘 || He jumped out of the plane with a parachute. || 他帶著降落傘跳出飛機。 || parachute jump=跳傘 || The soldiers landed safely ___ parachutes. || with|by|in || with + 名詞 = 用…。 || 士兵們用降落傘安全著陸。
paragraph || n. || 段落 || Read the first paragraph. || 讀第一段。 || opening paragraph=開頭段落 || Each paragraph should have a main idea ___ is supported by examples. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a main idea）。 || 每一段都應該有一個由例子支持的主旨。
partial || adj. || 部分的 || The plan was only a partial success. || 這個計畫只是部分成功。 || partial success=部分成功 || She is partial ___ chocolate cake. || to|for|with || be partial to = 偏愛…。 || 她偏愛巧克力蛋糕。
participation || n. || 參與 || Thank you for your participation. || 謝謝你的參與。 || class participation=課堂參與 || Active participation ___ class is part of your grade. || in|on|at || participation in = 參與…。 || 積極參與課堂是成績的一部分。
partnership || n. || 合夥關係 || The two companies formed a partnership. || 兩家公司建立了合作關係。 || in partnership with=與…合作 || The event was held in partnership ___ a local charity. || with|to|for || in partnership with = 與…合作。 || 這個活動是與當地一家慈善機構合作舉辦的。
passive || adj. || 被動的 || Don't be so passive. || 別那麼被動。 || passive voice=被動語態 || Students should be active, not passive, ___ class. || in|on|at || in class = 在課堂上。 || 學生在課堂上應該主動，而不是被動。
pasta || n. || 義大利麵食 || I had pasta for dinner. || 我晚餐吃義大利麵。 || pasta with sauce=義大利麵佐醬 || Italian people eat pasta ___ often than people in other countries. || more|most|much || 比較級 more often + than。 || 義大利人比其他國家的人更常吃義大利麵。
paw || n. || 爪子 || The cat licked its paw. || 貓舔了舔牠的爪子。 || cat's paw=貓掌 || The dog gave me ___ paw when I said "shake." || its|it's|their || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 我說「握手」時，那隻狗把牠的爪子給我。
peculiar || adj. || 奇怪的；特有的 || There is a peculiar smell in the room. || 房間裡有一股奇怪的味道。 || peculiar to=…所特有的 || This custom is peculiar ___ Taiwan. || to|for|with || be peculiar to = 為…所特有。 || 這個習俗是台灣特有的。
