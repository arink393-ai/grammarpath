# 學測 L4 09：fortunate – grief
fortunate || adj. || 幸運的 || We are fortunate to have good friends. || 我們很幸運有好朋友。 || be fortunate to V=很幸運能… || I was fortunate ___ meet such a good teacher. || to|for|of || be fortunate to + 原形動詞。 || 我很幸運遇到這麼好的老師。
fossil || n. || 化石 || Scientists found dinosaur fossils. || 科學家發現了恐龍化石。 || fossil fuel=化石燃料 || The dinosaur fossils ___ discovered in China last year. || were|was|are || 被動語態；fossils 複數，過去用 were。 || 這些恐龍化石是去年在中國發現的。
foundation || n. || 基礎；基金會 || Reading is the foundation of learning. || 閱讀是學習的基礎。 || lay the foundation=奠定基礎 || Good habits lay the foundation ___ a successful life. || for|of|to || lay the foundation for = 為…奠定基礎。 || 好習慣為成功的人生奠定基礎。
founder || n. || 創辦人 || He is the founder of the company. || 他是這家公司的創辦人。 || company founder=公司創辦人 || The founder ___ the school was a kind teacher. || of|for|to || the founder of = …的創辦人。 || 這所學校的創辦人是一位親切的老師。
fragile || adj. || 易碎的 || Be careful. The glass is fragile. || 小心，玻璃很易碎。 || fragile item=易碎品 || The box is marked "fragile," so please handle it ___ care. || with|by|in || with care = 小心地。 || 箱子上標示「易碎」，所以請小心搬運。
frame || n./v. || 框架；相框 || I put the photo in a frame. || 我把照片放進相框裡。 || photo frame=相框 || The photo ___ framed and hung on the wall. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這張照片被裱框掛在牆上。
frequency || n. || 頻率 || The frequency of earthquakes in Taiwan is high. || 台灣地震的頻率很高。 || high frequency=高頻率 || The frequency ___ typhoons has increased in recent years. || of|for|in || the frequency of = …的頻率。 || 近年來颱風的頻率增加了。
freshman || n. || 新生；大一生 || She is a freshman in college. || 她是大一新生。 || college freshman=大一新生 || As a freshman, she felt a little nervous ___ the first day. || on|in|at || 特定的日子用 on。 || 身為新生，她在第一天有點緊張。
frost || n. || 霜 || There was frost on the grass this morning. || 今天早上草地上結了霜。 || morning frost=晨霜 || The windows were covered ___ frost this morning. || with|of|at || be covered with = 覆蓋著…。 || 今天早上窗戶上結了一層霜。
frown || v./n. || 皺眉 || She frowned at me. || 她對我皺眉。 || frown at=對…皺眉 || The teacher frowned ___ the noisy students. || at|to|with || frown at = 對…皺眉。 || 老師對吵鬧的學生皺眉。
frustrate || v. || 使挫折 || The problem frustrated him. || 這個問題讓他很挫折。 || frustrated=感到挫折的 || The difficult math problem frustrated me ___ I couldn't solve it. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 那道數學難題讓我很挫折，因為我解不出來。
frustration || n. || 挫折 || He shouted in frustration. || 他沮喪地大叫。 || in frustration=沮喪地 || She threw the paper away ___ frustration. || in|on|by || in frustration = 沮喪地。 || 她沮喪地把紙丟掉。
functional || adj. || 功能性的 || The kitchen is small but functional. || 廚房雖小但很實用。 || functional design=實用設計 || The design is not only beautiful ___ also functional. || but|and|or || not only A but also B = 不僅 A 而且 B。 || 這個設計不但美觀而且實用。
fundamental || adj. || 基本的 || Education is a fundamental right. || 受教育是基本權利。 || fundamental change=根本的改變 || Freedom of speech is a fundamental right ___ everyone should have. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 have 的受詞。 || 言論自由是每個人都應享有的基本權利。
funeral || n. || 葬禮 || Many people attended the funeral. || 很多人參加了葬禮。 || attend a funeral=參加葬禮 || Many people came to the funeral ___ say goodbye to him. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 很多人前來參加葬禮向他道別。
furious || adj. || 狂怒的 || She was furious with me. || 她對我非常生氣。 || be furious with=對…非常生氣 || Dad was furious ___ me for coming home late. || with|of|to || be furious with sb = 對某人非常生氣。 || 爸爸因為我晚歸而對我大發雷霆。
furnish || v. || 布置家具；提供 || The apartment is fully furnished. || 這間公寓附全套家具。 || furnished apartment=附家具的公寓 || The room ___ furnished with a bed and a desk. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The room 用 is + p.p.。 || 房間配有一張床和一張書桌。
furthermore || adv. || 此外 || The hotel is cheap. Furthermore, it's near the station. || 這間旅館很便宜，此外還離車站很近。 || furthermore=此外 || The apartment is large. Furthermore, it ___ a beautiful view. || has|have|having || it 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 這間公寓很大，此外還有美麗的景觀。
gallery || n. || 美術館；畫廊 || We visited an art gallery. || 我們參觀了一間美術館。 || art gallery=美術館 || The gallery, ___ opened last year, shows works by young artists. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 去年開幕的這間藝廊展出年輕藝術家的作品。
gaze || v./n. || 凝視 || She gazed at the stars. || 她凝視著星星。 || gaze at=凝視… || They gazed ___ the beautiful sunset. || at|to|on || gaze at = 凝視…。 || 他們凝視著美麗的夕陽。
gear || n. || 裝備；齒輪 || Bring your camping gear. || 帶上你的露營裝備。 || camping gear=露營裝備 || Don't forget ___ bring your rain gear. || to|for|of || forget to V = 忘記要做。 || 別忘了帶雨具。
gender || n. || 性別 || Gender should not limit your dreams. || 性別不應限制你的夢想。 || gender equality=性別平等 || Everyone should be treated equally regardless ___ gender. || of|for|to || regardless of = 不論…。 || 不論性別，每個人都應受到平等對待。
gene || n. || 基因 || Eye color is decided by genes. || 眼睛的顏色由基因決定。 || gene=基因 || Some diseases are passed down ___ genes. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 有些疾病是透過基因遺傳的。
generation || n. || 世代 || The young generation uses phones a lot. || 年輕世代大量使用手機。 || younger generation=年輕一代; generation gap=代溝 || The tradition has been passed down ___ generation to generation. || from|of|in || from generation to generation = 世代相傳。 || 這個傳統世代相傳。
generosity || n. || 慷慨 || Thank you for your generosity. || 謝謝你的慷慨。 || show generosity=展現慷慨 || We were touched ___ his generosity. || by|with|of || be touched by = 被…感動。 || 我們被他的慷慨所感動。
genius || n. || 天才 || Einstein was a genius. || 愛因斯坦是個天才。 || musical genius=音樂天才 || Mozart was a musical genius ___ started composing at the age of five. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 莫札特是一位五歲就開始作曲的音樂天才。
genuine || adj. || 真正的；真誠的 || This bag is made of genuine leather. || 這個包包是真皮做的。 || genuine leather=真皮 || She showed genuine concern ___ the poor. || for|of|to || concern for = 對…的關心。 || 她對窮人表現出真誠的關心。
germ || n. || 細菌 || Wash your hands to kill germs. || 洗手殺菌。 || spread germs=散播病菌 || Washing your hands can stop germs ___ spreading. || from|of|to || stop A from V-ing = 阻止 A 做…。 || 洗手可以阻止病菌傳播。
gifted || adj. || 有天分的 || She is a gifted musician. || 她是一位有天分的音樂家。 || gifted student=資優生 || She is gifted ___ music. || in|on|with || be gifted in = 在…方面有天分。 || 她在音樂方面很有天分。
gigantic || adj. || 巨大的 || The ship is gigantic. || 那艘船很巨大。 || gigantic wave=巨浪 || A gigantic wave hit the coast ___ the storm. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 暴風雨期間一道巨浪襲擊了海岸。
giggle || v./n. || 咯咯笑 || The girls giggled at the joke. || 女孩們聽到笑話咯咯地笑。 || giggle at=對…咯咯笑 || The little girls giggled ___ the funny clown. || at|to|with || giggle at = 對…咯咯笑。 || 小女孩們對著滑稽的小丑咯咯笑。
ginger || n. || 薑 || Ginger tea is good for colds. || 薑茶對感冒有益。 || ginger tea=薑茶 || Ginger tea can help you feel warm ___ winter. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 薑茶能幫助你在冬天感到溫暖。
glimpse || n./v. || 瞥見 || I caught a glimpse of the singer. || 我瞥見了那位歌手。 || catch a glimpse of=瞥見… || We caught a glimpse ___ the famous actor at the airport. || of|at|to || catch a glimpse of = 瞥見…。 || 我們在機場瞥見了那位知名演員。
globe || n. || 地球；地球儀 || People around the globe watched the game. || 全世界的人都在看這場比賽。 || around the globe=全世界 || The song became popular ___ over the globe. || all|every|whole || all over the globe = 全世界。 || 這首歌紅遍全球。
glorious || adj. || 光榮的；壯麗的 || It was a glorious day. || 那是燦爛的一天。 || glorious victory=光榮的勝利 || The team celebrated their glorious victory ___ a big party. || with|by|in || celebrate with = 以…慶祝。 || 隊伍用一場盛大的派對慶祝光榮的勝利。
goods || n. || 商品 || The store sells sports goods. || 這家店賣運動用品。 || sports goods=運動用品 || The goods ___ delivered to the store yesterday. || were|was|is || goods 複數，過去被動用 were。 || 這批貨昨天送到了店裡。
gown || n. || 長禮服 || She wore a beautiful gown to the party. || 她穿了一件漂亮的禮服去派對。 || evening gown=晚禮服; wedding gown=婚紗 || The bride looked beautiful ___ her white gown. || in|with|on || in + 衣服 = 穿著…。 || 新娘穿著白色禮服看起來很美。
grace || n. || 優雅；恩典 || She danced with grace. || 她優雅地跳舞。 || with grace=優雅地 || The ballet dancer moved ___ great grace. || with|in|by || with grace = 優雅地。 || 那位芭蕾舞者動作非常優雅。
graceful || adj. || 優雅的 || She is a graceful dancer. || 她是一位優雅的舞者。 || graceful movement=優雅的動作 || She is the ___ graceful dancer in the group. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 她是團體中最優雅的舞者。
gracious || adj. || 親切的；有禮的 || She was a gracious host. || 她是一位親切的主人。 || gracious host=親切的主人 || It was gracious ___ her to invite us to dinner. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 她邀請我們吃晚餐真是親切。
graduation || n. || 畢業 || Her graduation ceremony is next week. || 她的畢業典禮在下週。 || graduation ceremony=畢業典禮 || After graduation, he plans ___ abroad. || to study|studying|study || plan to + 原形動詞。 || 畢業後，他打算出國讀書。
grammar || n. || 文法 || English grammar is not easy. || 英文文法不容易。 || grammar mistake=文法錯誤 || There are some grammar mistakes ___ your essay. || in|on|at || 在文章中用 in。 || 你的作文裡有一些文法錯誤。
grammatical || adj. || 文法的 || This sentence is not grammatical. || 這個句子文法不正確。 || grammatical error=文法錯誤 || Please check your essay ___ grammatical errors. || for|of|to || check sth for = 檢查…中是否有…。 || 請檢查你的作文有沒有文法錯誤。
graph || n. || 圖表 || The graph shows the results. || 這張圖表顯示了結果。 || line graph=折線圖 || The graph shows ___ the price of oil has changed. || how|what|which || 名詞子句 how… = …如何變化。 || 這張圖表顯示了油價如何變化。
grateful || adj. || 感激的 || I'm grateful for your help. || 我很感激你的幫忙。 || be grateful for=感激… || I'm very grateful ___ your kindness. || for|to|of || be grateful for + 事物 = 感激…。 || 我非常感激你的好意。
gratitude || n. || 感激 || She expressed her gratitude to the doctor. || 她向醫生表達了感激之意。 || express gratitude=表達謝意 || He gave her flowers as a sign ___ gratitude. || of|for|to || a sign of = …的表示。 || 他送她花表示感謝。
grave || n./adj. || 墳墓；嚴重的 || We visited Grandpa's grave. || 我們去掃爺爺的墓。 || grave danger=嚴重的危險 || People clean the graves of their ancestors ___ Tomb Sweeping Day. || on|in|at || 特定的節日（日子）用 on。 || 人們在清明節打掃祖先的墳墓。
greasy || adj. || 油膩的 || The food is too greasy. || 這食物太油了。 || greasy food=油膩的食物 || Eating too much greasy food is bad ___ your health. || for|to|with || be bad for = 對…有害。 || 吃太多油膩的食物對健康不好。
greeting || n. || 問候 || She sent me a New Year greeting. || 她寄給我新年問候。 || greeting card=賀卡 || We sent greeting cards to our friends ___ Christmas. || at|of|in || 在節日期間用 at。 || 我們在聖誕節寄賀卡給朋友。
grief || n. || 悲傷 || She was full of grief. || 她充滿悲傷。 || in grief=悲傷地 || He was filled ___ grief when his dog died. || with|of|by || be filled with = 充滿…。 || 他的狗死的時候，他悲痛不已。
