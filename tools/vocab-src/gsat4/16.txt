# 學測 L4 16：procedure – refund
procedure || n. || 程序 || Follow the procedure carefully. || 仔細遵循程序。 || safety procedure=安全程序 || Please follow the safety procedures ___ there is a fire. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果發生火災，請遵循安全程序。
proceed || v. || 繼續進行 || Please proceed to Gate 5. || 請前往五號登機門。 || proceed to=前往… || Passengers should proceed ___ Gate 5 for boarding. || to|at|for || proceed to = 前往…。 || 旅客請前往五號登機門登機。
productive || adj. || 有生產力的 || I had a productive day. || 我今天效率很高。 || productive meeting=有成效的會議 || I'm ___ productive in the morning than at night. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 我早上比晚上更有效率。
profession || n. || 職業 || Teaching is a noble profession. || 教書是崇高的職業。 || by profession=職業是… || She is a lawyer ___ profession. || by|in|of || by profession = 職業是…。 || 她的職業是律師。
professional || adj./n. || 專業的；專業人士 || He is a professional photographer. || 他是職業攝影師。 || professional athlete=職業運動員 || You should ask a professional ___ advice. || for|to|of || ask sb for sth = 向某人要求某物。 || 你應該向專業人士尋求建議。
profitable || adj. || 有利可圖的 || The business is very profitable. || 這門生意很賺錢。 || profitable business=賺錢的生意 || The company became profitable ___ three years. || after|before|since || after + 時間 = 在…之後。 || 這家公司三年後開始獲利。
prominent || adj. || 著名的；突出的 || He is a prominent scientist. || 他是一位著名的科學家。 || prominent figure=知名人物 || She is one of the most prominent ___ in Taiwan. || writers|writer|writing || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。 || 她是台灣最著名的作家之一。
promising || adj. || 有前途的 || She is a promising young artist. || 她是一位有前途的年輕藝術家。 || promising future=光明的前途 || He is a promising young player ___ may become a star one day. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 他是一位有朝一日可能成為明星的潛力新秀。
promotion || n. || 升職；促銷 || She got a promotion last month. || 她上個月升職了。 || get a promotion=升職 || He was given a promotion ___ his hard work. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 他因為工作努力而獲得升遷。
prompt || adj./v. || 迅速的；促使 || Thank you for your prompt reply. || 謝謝你迅速回覆。 || prompt reply=迅速回覆 || The accident prompted the city ___ build a new traffic light. || to|for|of || prompt sb to + 原形動詞 = 促使某人做…。 || 這起事故促使市政府設置新的紅綠燈。
pronunciation || n. || 發音 || Her English pronunciation is good. || 她的英文發音很好。 || correct pronunciation=正確的發音 || You can improve ___ pronunciation by listening to native speakers. || your|you|yours || 名詞 pronunciation 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 你可以藉由聽母語人士說話來改善發音。
proposal || n. || 提議；求婚 || The committee accepted my proposal. || 委員會接受了我的提案。 || make a proposal=提出提議 || She said yes ___ his marriage proposal. || to|for|with || say yes to = 答應…。 || 她答應了他的求婚。
prosper || v. || 繁榮 || The town prospered after the railway was built. || 鐵路建成後，小鎮繁榮起來。 || prosper=興旺 || The town began to prosper ___ the railway was built. || after|before|until || 鐵路建好「之後」繁榮，用 after。 || 鐵路建成後，小鎮開始繁榮起來。
prosperity || n. || 繁榮 || The country enjoyed years of prosperity. || 這個國家享有多年的繁榮。 || economic prosperity=經濟繁榮 || People wish each other health and prosperity ___ Chinese New Year. || at|in|on || 在節日期間用 at。 || 過年時人們互相祝福健康和興旺。
prosperous || adj. || 繁榮的 || Taipei is a prosperous city. || 台北是一座繁榮的城市。 || prosperous economy=繁榮的經濟 || The city became ___ prosperous than ever before. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 這座城市變得比以往任何時候都更繁榮。
protein || n. || 蛋白質 || Eggs are rich in protein. || 蛋富含蛋白質。 || high protein=高蛋白 || Eggs and milk are rich ___ protein. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 蛋和牛奶富含蛋白質。
protest || n./v. || 抗議 || People protested against the new law. || 人們抗議新法律。 || protest against=抗議… || Thousands of people protested ___ the new tax. || against|for|to || protest against = 抗議…。 || 數千人抗議新稅制。
psychological || adj. || 心理的 || Stress can cause psychological problems. || 壓力會造成心理問題。 || psychological health=心理健康 || Too much stress can lead ___ psychological problems. || to|for|in || lead to = 導致…。 || 太多壓力可能導致心理問題。
psychologist || n. || 心理學家 || She went to see a psychologist. || 她去看了心理師。 || school psychologist=學校心理師 || If you feel stressed, you can talk ___ the school psychologist. || to|at|for || talk to sb = 和某人談。 || 如果你覺得有壓力，可以找學校心理師談談。
psychology || n. || 心理學 || She is studying psychology. || 她在讀心理學。 || child psychology=兒童心理學 || She wants to major ___ psychology in college. || in|on|at || major in = 主修…。 || 她想在大學主修心理學。
publication || n. || 出版；刊物 || The book is ready for publication. || 這本書準備出版了。 || date of publication=出版日期 || The magazine stopped publication ___ 2020. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 這本雜誌在 2020 年停刊了。
publicity || n. || 宣傳；關注 || The movie got a lot of publicity. || 這部電影獲得大量宣傳。 || publicity campaign=宣傳活動 || The event received a lot of publicity ___ the media. || from|of|by || publicity from = 來自…的關注。 || 這個活動受到媒體的廣泛報導。
publish || v. || 出版 || The book was published last year. || 這本書去年出版。 || publish a book=出版書籍 || Her first novel ___ published when she was only 20. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 她的第一本小說在她二十歲時出版。
publisher || n. || 出版商 || The publisher accepted her book. || 出版社接受了她的書。 || book publisher=出版社 || The publisher ___ printed my book is based in Taipei. || that|who|whom || 關係代名詞 that 修飾（出版社）並當主詞。 || 印我書的那家出版社在台北。
pursue || v. || 追求 || She wants to pursue her dream. || 她想追求自己的夢想。 || pursue a dream=追求夢想 || He moved to New York ___ pursue a career in music. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他搬到紐約追求音樂事業。
pursuit || n. || 追求 || The pursuit of happiness is important. || 追求幸福很重要。 || in pursuit of=追求… || He left his hometown in pursuit ___ a better life. || of|for|to || in pursuit of = 追求…。 || 他為了追求更好的生活而離開家鄉。
quarrel || n./v. || 爭吵 || They had a quarrel about money. || 他們為錢吵了一架。 || quarrel with=和…爭吵 || She often quarrels ___ her brother about small things. || with|to|for || quarrel with sb = 和某人爭吵。 || 她常常為小事和哥哥吵架。
quilt || n. || 被子 || My grandma made this quilt. || 這條被子是我奶奶做的。 || cotton quilt=棉被 || The quilt ___ my grandma made is very warm. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 made 的受詞。 || 奶奶做的被子很溫暖。
quotation || n. || 引文；報價 || This is a quotation from Shakespeare. || 這是莎士比亞的一句名言。 || famous quotation=名言 || The speech began with a quotation ___ a famous poet. || from|of|by || a quotation from = 引自…的話。 || 演講以一位知名詩人的名言開場。
radar || n. || 雷達 || The plane was detected by radar. || 飛機被雷達偵測到。 || radar screen=雷達螢幕 || The ship ___ detected by radar last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那艘船昨晚被雷達偵測到。
rage || n./v. || 盛怒 || He was in a rage. || 他暴怒。 || in a rage=盛怒之下; road rage=路怒 || He shouted ___ a rage. || in|with|by || in a rage = 盛怒之下。 || 他在盛怒之下大吼。
rainfall || n. || 降雨量 || The rainfall this year is low. || 今年的降雨量很少。 || heavy rainfall=大量降雨 || The heavy rainfall caused floods ___ many areas. || in|on|at || 在…地區用 in。 || 大雨造成許多地區淹水。
raisin || n. || 葡萄乾 || I like raisin bread. || 我喜歡葡萄乾麵包。 || raisin bread=葡萄乾麵包 || Raisins are made ___ grapes. || from|of|by || be made from = 由…製成（看不出原料）。 || 葡萄乾是用葡萄做成的。
realistic || adj. || 實際的；逼真的 || Set realistic goals. || 設定實際可行的目標。 || realistic goal=實際的目標 || It's important ___ set realistic goals. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 設定實際可行的目標很重要。
rebel || n./v. || 叛亂者；反抗 || Teenagers often rebel against their parents. || 青少年常反抗父母。 || rebel against=反抗… || Many teenagers rebel ___ their parents' rules. || against|to|with || rebel against = 反抗…。 || 很多青少年會反抗父母的規定。
recall || v. || 回想 || I can't recall his name. || 我想不起他的名字。 || recall + V-ing=記得做過… || I can't recall ___ him before. || meeting|to meet|meet || recall + V-ing = 記得做過…。 || 我不記得以前見過他。
reception || n. || 接待處；歡迎會 || Please ask at reception. || 請到櫃台詢問。 || wedding reception=喜宴 || Please leave your key ___ reception when you check out. || at|on|in || at reception = 在櫃台。 || 退房時請把鑰匙留在櫃台。
recipe || n. || 食譜 || Can you give me the recipe? || 你可以給我食譜嗎？ || recipe for=…的做法 || This is my grandma's recipe ___ beef noodles. || for|of|to || a recipe for = …的食譜。 || 這是我奶奶的牛肉麵食譜。
recognition || n. || 認可；表揚 || She received recognition for her work. || 她的工作得到了肯定。 || in recognition of=為表彰… || He received an award in recognition ___ his hard work. || of|for|to || in recognition of = 為表彰…。 || 他因努力工作而獲頒獎項表揚。
recovery || n. || 康復；恢復 || He made a quick recovery. || 他很快就康復了。 || make a recovery=康復 || We wish you a speedy recovery ___ your illness. || from|of|for || recovery from = 從…中康復。 || 祝你早日康復。
recreation || n. || 娛樂；消遣 || Reading is my favorite recreation. || 閱讀是我最喜歡的休閒活動。 || recreation center=休閒中心 || The park provides space for recreation ___ exercise. || and|but|or || 兩者並列用 and。 || 公園提供休閒和運動的空間。
recycle || v. || 回收 || We should recycle paper. || 我們應該回收紙張。 || recycle bottles=回收瓶子 || Paper can ___ recycled many times. || be|is|been || can 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 紙可以被回收很多次。
reduction || n. || 減少 || There is a reduction in prices. || 價格降低了。 || price reduction=降價 || There has been a big reduction ___ the number of students. || in|of|for || a reduction in = …的減少。 || 學生人數大幅減少。
refer || v. || 提到；參考 || He referred to the book in his speech. || 他在演講中提到了那本書。 || refer to=提到；參考 || You can refer ___ the dictionary if you don't know the word. || to|for|at || refer to = 參考、查閱。 || 如果不知道這個字，可以查字典。
reference || n. || 參考；提及 || Keep this book for reference. || 留著這本書作參考。 || for reference=以供參考 || Keep this dictionary ___ future reference. || for|to|of || for future reference = 以供日後參考。 || 留著這本字典以供日後參考。
reflect || v. || 反射；反映 || The lake reflects the mountains. || 湖面倒映著群山。 || reflect on=反省 || The results reflect ___ hard the students worked. || how|what|which || 名詞子句 how + 形容詞/副詞 = 多麼…。 || 結果反映出學生們有多努力。
reflection || n. || 倒影；反省 || She looked at her reflection in the mirror. || 她看著鏡中的自己。 || on reflection=經過思考 || She saw her reflection ___ the water. || in|on|at || 在水中的倒影用 in。 || 她看到自己在水中的倒影。
reform || n./v. || 改革 || The government plans education reform. || 政府計畫教育改革。 || education reform=教育改革 || The education reform ___ been discussed for years. || has|have|is || 現在完成式被動；The reform 單數用 has。 || 教育改革已經討論了好幾年。
refugee || n. || 難民 || Many refugees fled the war. || 許多難民逃離戰爭。 || refugee camp=難民營 || Thousands of refugees ___ forced to leave their homes. || were|was|are || 被動語態；refugees 複數，過去用 were。 || 數千名難民被迫離開家園。
refund || n./v. || 退款 || Can I get a refund? || 我可以退款嗎？ || full refund=全額退款 || You can get a full refund ___ you return the item within seven days. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 七天內退回商品就能全額退款。
