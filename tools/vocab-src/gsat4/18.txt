# 學測 L4 18：sacrifice – strengthen
sacrifice || n./v. || 犧牲 || Parents make many sacrifices for their children. || 父母為孩子做出許多犧牲。 || make sacrifices=做出犧牲 || She sacrificed her free time ___ help others. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她犧牲自己的空閒時間去幫助別人。
satellite || n. || 衛星 || The satellite was launched into space. || 衛星被發射到太空。 || satellite TV=衛星電視 || The satellite ___ launched into space last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這顆衛星去年被發射到太空。
satisfaction || n. || 滿足 || Helping others gives me satisfaction. || 幫助別人讓我很滿足。 || job satisfaction=工作滿意度 || She finished the project ___ great satisfaction. || with|in|by || with satisfaction = 滿意地。 || 她滿意地完成了這個專案。
scarcely || adv. || 幾乎不 || I could scarcely believe it. || 我簡直不敢相信。 || scarcely ever=幾乎從不 || I had scarcely arrived home ___ it started to rain. || when|than|then || scarcely… when… = 一…就…。 || 我一到家就開始下雨了。
scenery || n. || 風景 || The scenery in Taroko is beautiful. || 太魯閣的風景很美。 || beautiful scenery=美麗的風景 || The scenery here ___ so beautiful that I took many photos. || was|were|are || scenery 不可數；過去的事用 was。 || 這裡的風景美到我拍了很多照片。
scold || v. || 責罵 || Mom scolded me for being late. || 媽媽因為我遲到罵了我。 || scold sb for=因…責罵某人 || The teacher scolded him ___ talking in class. || for|of|with || scold sb for + V-ing = 因…責罵某人。 || 老師因他上課講話而罵了他。
scoop || n./v. || 勺；舀 || I'd like two scoops of ice cream. || 我要兩球冰淇淋。 || a scoop of=一勺… || Can I have two scoops ___ chocolate ice cream? || of|for|with || a scoop of = 一球（勺）…。 || 可以給我兩球巧克力冰淇淋嗎？
scratch || v./n. || 抓；刮 || The cat scratched my hand. || 貓抓傷了我的手。 || from scratch=從零開始 || He built the company ___ scratch. || from|of|at || from scratch = 從零開始。 || 他白手起家建立了這家公司。
sculpture || n. || 雕塑 || The museum has many sculptures. || 博物館有許多雕塑作品。 || stone sculpture=石雕 || This sculpture ___ made by a famous artist 100 years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這件雕塑是一位知名藝術家在一百年前創作的。
secure || adj./v. || 安全的；確保 || Keep your password secure. || 保管好你的密碼。 || feel secure=感到安心 || Make sure your password is secure ___ nobody can guess it. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 確保你的密碼安全，讓別人猜不到。
seize || v. || 抓住 || Seize the opportunity. || 抓住機會。 || seize the chance=把握機會 || You should seize every opportunity ___ practice English. || to|for|of || an opportunity to + 原形動詞。 || 你應該把握每個練習英文的機會。
settler || n. || 移居者 || The first settlers arrived in the 17th century. || 第一批移民在十七世紀抵達。 || early settlers=早期移民 || The early settlers ___ came to Taiwan were mostly farmers. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 早期來台灣的移民大多是農夫。
severe || adj. || 嚴重的；嚴厲的 || The storm caused severe damage. || 暴風雨造成嚴重損害。 || severe weather=惡劣天氣 || The typhoon caused severe damage ___ many towns. || to|for|in of || damage to = 對…的損害。 || 颱風對許多城鎮造成嚴重損害。
sew || v. || 縫 || My grandma taught me to sew. || 奶奶教我縫紉。 || sew a button=縫鈕扣 || Can you sew this button ___ my shirt? || on|in|at || sew A on B = 把 A 縫在 B 上。 || 你可以幫我把這顆鈕扣縫在襯衫上嗎？
shade || n. || 陰涼處 || Let's sit in the shade. || 我們坐在陰涼處吧。 || in the shade=在陰涼處 || It's too hot. Let's rest ___ the shade of that tree. || in|on|at || in the shade = 在陰涼處。 || 太熱了，我們在那棵樹的樹蔭下休息吧。
shady || adj. || 陰涼的；可疑的 || We found a shady spot for our picnic. || 我們找到一個陰涼的地方野餐。 || shady spot=陰涼處 || We had lunch in a shady spot ___ a big tree. || under|below|beneath of || under = 在…下面。 || 我們在一棵大樹下的陰涼處吃午餐。
shameful || adj. || 可恥的 || Cheating is shameful. || 作弊是可恥的。 || shameful act=可恥的行為 || It is shameful ___ cheat on a test. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 考試作弊是可恥的。
shave || v. || 刮鬍子 || He shaves every morning. || 他每天早上刮鬍子。 || shave off=剃掉 || He shaves ___ he goes to work. || before|after|since || 上班「之前」刮鬍子，用 before。 || 他上班前刮鬍子。
shelter || n./v. || 庇護所 || We took shelter from the rain. || 我們躲雨。 || take shelter=躲避; animal shelter=動物收容所 || We adopted a cat ___ an animal shelter. || from|of|at of || adopt from = 從…領養。 || 我們從動物收容所領養了一隻貓。
shift || n./v. || 輪班；轉變 || She works the night shift. || 她上夜班。 || night shift=夜班 || There has been a shift ___ the way people shop. || in|on|at || a shift in = …的轉變。 || 人們購物的方式已經發生轉變。
sightseeing || n. || 觀光 || We went sightseeing in Tokyo. || 我們在東京觀光。 || go sightseeing=去觀光 || We spent the whole day ___ sightseeing in Kyoto. || going|to go|go || spend + 時間 + V-ing。 || 我們花了一整天在京都觀光。
signature || n. || 簽名 || Please put your signature here. || 請在這裡簽名。 || sign one's signature=簽名 || The form needs your parents' signature ___ you can join the trip. || before|after|since || 參加「之前」需要簽名，用 before。 || 參加旅行之前，表格需要家長簽名。
significance || n. || 重要性 || This event has great significance. || 這個事件意義重大。 || historical significance=歷史意義 || Do you understand the significance ___ this festival? || of|for|to || the significance of = …的意義。 || 你了解這個節日的意義嗎？
sincerity || n. || 真誠 || I appreciate your sincerity. || 我很欣賞你的真誠。 || with sincerity=真誠地 || She apologized ___ sincerity. || with|in|by || with sincerity = 真誠地。 || 她真誠地道歉。
singular || adj./n. || 單數的 || "Child" is singular. || child 是單數。 || singular noun=單數名詞 || The plural of "child" is "children," and the singular form ___ "child." || is|are|be || the singular form 單數，用 is。 || child 的複數是 children，單數是 child。
site || n. || 地點；網站 || This is the site of the new school. || 這裡是新學校的預定地。 || construction site=工地; web site=網站 || Workers must wear helmets ___ the construction site. || at|in|on of || at the site = 在現場。 || 工人在工地必須戴安全帽。
sketch || n./v. || 素描 || She made a sketch of the tree. || 她畫了一幅樹的素描。 || make a sketch=畫素描 || The artist made a quick sketch ___ my face. || of|for|to || a sketch of = …的素描。 || 畫家快速畫了一張我的臉部素描。
skyscraper || n. || 摩天大樓 || Taipei 101 is a famous skyscraper. || 台北 101 是一棟著名的摩天大樓。 || tall skyscraper=高聳的摩天大樓 || Taipei 101 was the tallest skyscraper ___ the world from 2004 to 2010. || in|of|on || 最高級 + in the world。 || 台北 101 在 2004 到 2010 年間是世界最高的摩天大樓。
slight || adj. || 輕微的 || I have a slight headache. || 我有點頭痛。 || slight change=輕微的變化 || There has been a slight change ___ the plan. || in|to|of || a change in = …的改變。 || 計畫有了些微的變動。
slogan || n. || 標語 || "Just do it" is a famous slogan. || 「Just do it」是一句有名的標語。 || advertising slogan=廣告標語 || The slogan ___ the campaign is "Save Water, Save Life." || of|for|in || the slogan of = …的標語。 || 這個活動的標語是「省水，救生命」。
socket || n. || 插座 || Plug it into the socket. || 把它插進插座。 || electric socket=電插座 || Don't put your fingers ___ the socket. || into|onto|at || put A into B = 把 A 放進 B。 || 不要把手指伸進插座裡。
software || n. || 軟體 || He designs computer software. || 他設計電腦軟體。 || install software=安裝軟體 || The new software ___ installed on all school computers last week. || was|were|is || 被動語態；software 不可數，過去用 was。 || 新軟體上週安裝在學校所有電腦上。
solar || adj. || 太陽的 || Solar energy is clean. || 太陽能很乾淨。 || solar energy=太陽能; solar panel=太陽能板 || Solar energy is ___ cleaner than coal. || much|many|very || 比較級前面用 much 加強語氣。 || 太陽能比煤乾淨得多。
spade || n. || 鏟子 || He dug a hole with a spade. || 他用鏟子挖了一個洞。 || call a spade a spade=直言不諱 || He dug the garden ___ a spade. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 他用鏟子翻整花園。
spare || adj./v. || 空閒的；備用的 || What do you do in your spare time? || 你空閒時做什麼？ || spare time=空閒時間 || I like to read ___ my spare time. || in|on|at || in one's spare time = 在空閒時。 || 我喜歡在空閒時閱讀。
spark || n./v. || 火花；引發 || The fire started from a spark. || 火災從一個火花開始。 || spark interest=引起興趣 || The movie sparked my interest ___ history. || in|on|for || interest in = 對…的興趣。 || 這部電影引發了我對歷史的興趣。
spear || n. || 矛 || He caught the fish with a spear. || 他用矛捕魚。 || spear fishing=魚叉捕魚 || In ancient times, people hunted animals ___ spears. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 古時候人們用矛狩獵。
species || n. || 物種 || Many species are in danger. || 許多物種瀕臨危險。 || endangered species=瀕危物種 || The black bear is a species ___ is protected in Taiwan. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a species）。 || 黑熊是台灣受保護的物種。
spiritual || adj. || 精神的 || Music gives me spiritual comfort. || 音樂給我精神上的安慰。 || spiritual life=精神生活 || For many people, religion provides spiritual comfort ___ hard times. || in|on|at || in hard times = 在困難時期。 || 對許多人來說，宗教在困難時期提供精神上的慰藉。
splendid || adj. || 壯麗的；極好的 || We had a splendid view of the city. || 我們能欣賞城市的絕佳景色。 || splendid idea=極好的主意 || The palace was ___ splendid that everyone was amazed. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那座宮殿壯麗到讓每個人都驚嘆不已。
split || v./n. || 分開 || Let's split the bill. || 我們平分帳單吧。 || split the bill=分攤費用 || Let's split the cake ___ four pieces. || into|in|to be || split A into B = 把 A 分成 B。 || 我們把蛋糕分成四塊吧。
sprinkle || v./n. || 撒；灑 || Sprinkle some salt on the fries. || 在薯條上撒一些鹽。 || sprinkle with=撒上… || She sprinkled the cake ___ sugar. || with|by|of || sprinkle A with B = 在 A 上撒 B。 || 她在蛋糕上撒了糖。
stab || v./n. || 刺 || The man was stabbed with a knife. || 那名男子被刀刺傷。 || stab in the back=背後捅刀 || The man ___ stabbed in the arm during the fight. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那名男子在打鬥中手臂被刺傷。
statistic || n. || 統計數字 || The statistics show a rise in prices. || 統計數字顯示物價上漲。 || according to statistics=根據統計 || ___ to the statistics, more people are reading e-books. || According|Accord|Accordingly || according to = 根據…。 || 根據統計，越來越多人閱讀電子書。
status || n. || 地位；狀態 || He has a high social status. || 他社會地位很高。 || social status=社會地位 || Please check the status ___ your order online. || of|for|to || the status of = …的狀態。 || 請上網查詢你訂單的狀態。
stem || n./v. || 莖；起源 || The flower has a long stem. || 這朵花有很長的莖。 || stem from=起源於… || Many health problems stem ___ a lack of sleep. || from|of|out || stem from = 起源於…。 || 許多健康問題源自睡眠不足。
stereo || n. || 音響 || He turned on the stereo. || 他打開了音響。 || car stereo=汽車音響 || Please turn ___ the stereo. It's too loud. || down|off of|up || turn down = 調小聲。 || 請把音響調小聲，太吵了。
stingy || adj. || 小氣的 || Don't be so stingy. || 別那麼小氣。 || stingy with=對…吝嗇 || He is stingy ___ his money. || with|of|about to || be stingy with = 對…吝嗇。 || 他對錢很吝嗇。
stocking || n. || 長襪 || She hung a stocking for Christmas. || 她為聖誕節掛了一隻長襪。 || Christmas stocking=聖誕襪 || Children hang stockings ___ Christmas Eve. || on|in|at || 特定的日子用 on。 || 孩子們在聖誕夜掛上長襪。
strengthen || v. || 加強 || Exercise strengthens your body. || 運動能強健身體。 || strengthen relationship=加強關係 || Regular exercise can strengthen your muscles ___ bones. || and|or|but || 兩者並列用 and。 || 規律運動能強化你的肌肉和骨骼。
