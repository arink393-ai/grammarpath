# 學測 L4 19：stripe – youthful
stripe || n. || 條紋 || Zebras have black and white stripes. || 斑馬有黑白條紋。 || with stripes=有條紋的 || He is wearing a shirt ___ blue stripes. || with|of|in || with = 有…的。 || 他穿著一件有藍色條紋的襯衫。
strive || v. || 努力 || We should strive for excellence. || 我們應該力求卓越。 || strive for=努力爭取… || We should always strive ___ our best. || to do|doing|do || strive to + 原形動詞 = 努力做…。 || 我們應該永遠盡力做到最好。
stroke || n. || 中風；一筆 || My grandpa had a stroke last year. || 我爺爺去年中風了。 || have a stroke=中風 || He had a stroke ___ he was 70 years old. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 他七十歲時中風了。
submarine || n. || 潛水艇 || The submarine went deep under the sea. || 潛水艇潛入深海。 || nuclear submarine=核子潛艇 || The submarine can stay underwater ___ months. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 這艘潛水艇可以在水下待好幾個月。
sue || v. || 控告 || He sued the company. || 他控告了那家公司。 || sue sb for=因…控告某人 || She sued the company ___ unfair treatment. || for|of|with || sue sb for sth = 因某事控告某人。 || 她因受到不公平對待而控告那家公司。
suggestion || n. || 建議 || Do you have any suggestions? || 你有任何建議嗎？ || make a suggestion=提出建議 || Thank you ___ your suggestion. || for|to|of || thank you for + 名詞。 || 謝謝你的建議。
summarize || v. || 總結 || Please summarize the story. || 請總結這個故事。 || summarize the main points=總結要點 || Please summarize the article ___ 100 words. || in|with|by || in + 字數 = 用…字。 || 請用一百字總結這篇文章。
surgeon || n. || 外科醫師 || The surgeon did the operation. || 外科醫師進行了手術。 || heart surgeon=心臟外科醫師 || The surgeon ___ did my operation was very skillful. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 幫我動手術的外科醫師技術非常好。
surgery || n. || 手術 || He had surgery on his knee. || 他的膝蓋動了手術。 || have surgery=動手術 || He needs to have surgery ___ his heart. || on|in|at || surgery on + 身體部位。 || 他需要動心臟手術。
surrender || v./n. || 投降 || The army surrendered. || 軍隊投降了。 || surrender to=向…投降 || The soldiers surrendered ___ the enemy. || to|for|with || surrender to = 向…投降。 || 士兵們向敵人投降。
surroundings || n. || 環境 || The hotel has beautiful surroundings. || 這間飯店周遭環境很美。 || natural surroundings=自然環境 || It takes time for children to get used ___ new surroundings. || to|for|with || get used to = 習慣於…。 || 孩子需要時間習慣新環境。
suspicious || adj. || 可疑的 || The police found a suspicious bag. || 警方發現一個可疑的包包。 || be suspicious of=對…起疑 || The police are suspicious ___ the man's story. || of|to|with || be suspicious of = 懷疑…。 || 警方對那名男子的說法起疑。
sway || v. || 搖擺 || The trees swayed in the wind. || 樹在風中搖曳。 || sway in the wind=隨風搖曳 || The tall trees were swaying ___ the strong wind. || in|with|by || in the wind = 在風中。 || 高大的樹在強風中搖晃。
syllable || n. || 音節 || "Banana" has three syllables. || banana 有三個音節。 || stressed syllable=重音節 || The word "computer" has three syllables, and the stress is ___ the second one. || on|in|at || the stress is on = 重音在…。 || computer 有三個音節，重音在第二個音節。
sympathetic || adj. || 同情的 || She was sympathetic to my problem. || 她很同情我的問題。 || be sympathetic to=同情… || My teacher was very sympathetic ___ my situation. || to|for|with || be sympathetic to = 同情…。 || 我的老師很同情我的處境。
sympathy || n. || 同情 || I have sympathy for the victims. || 我同情那些受害者。 || feel sympathy for=同情… || Everyone felt sympathy ___ the family who lost their home. || for|to|with || sympathy for = 對…的同情。 || 每個人都同情那個失去家園的家庭。
systematic || adj. || 有系統的 || She has a systematic way of studying. || 她讀書很有系統。 || systematic approach=有系統的方法 || A systematic study plan can help you learn ___ efficiently. || more|most|much || 多音節副詞的比較級用 more。 || 有系統的讀書計畫能幫助你更有效率地學習。
technician || n. || 技術員 || A technician fixed my computer. || 一位技術人員修好了我的電腦。 || lab technician=實驗室技術員 || The computer ___ repaired by a technician yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 電腦昨天由技術人員修好了。
technological || adj. || 科技的 || We live in an age of technological change. || 我們生活在科技變遷的時代。 || technological progress=科技進步 || Technological progress has made our lives ___ convenient. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 科技進步讓我們的生活更加便利。
telegraph || n. || 電報 || The telegraph was invented in the 1800s. || 電報在十九世紀被發明。 || telegraph=電報 || Before the telephone, people sent messages ___ telegraph. || by|with|on || by + 通訊方式 = 以…方式。 || 在電話發明之前，人們用電報傳送訊息。
telescope || n. || 望遠鏡 || We looked at the moon through a telescope. || 我們用望遠鏡看月亮。 || through a telescope=透過望遠鏡 || We looked at the stars ___ a telescope. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 我們透過望遠鏡看星星。
tendency || n. || 傾向 || He has a tendency to be late. || 他有遲到的傾向。 || have a tendency to V=有…的傾向 || She has a tendency ___ worry too much. || to|for|of || a tendency to + 原形動詞。 || 她有過度擔心的傾向。
tense || adj. || 緊張的 || The atmosphere was tense. || 氣氛很緊張。 || tense situation=緊張的情勢 || The atmosphere in the room was ___ tense that nobody spoke. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 房間裡的氣氛緊張到沒有人說話。
tension || n. || 緊張 || There is tension between the two countries. || 兩國之間關係緊張。 || reduce tension=緩和緊張 || There is growing tension ___ the two countries. || between|among|in || 兩者之間用 between。 || 兩國之間的緊張關係日益升高。
terror || n. || 恐怖 || The children screamed in terror. || 孩子們驚恐地尖叫。 || in terror=驚恐地 || The people ran away ___ terror. || in|with|by || in terror = 驚恐地。 || 人們驚恐地逃跑。
theme || n. || 主題 || The theme of the party is the 1980s. || 派對的主題是 1980 年代。 || theme park=主題樂園; main theme=主旨 || The main theme ___ the novel is friendship. || of|for|in || the theme of = …的主題。 || 這本小說的主題是友誼。
thorough || adj. || 徹底的 || The police made a thorough search. || 警方進行了徹底的搜查。 || thorough check=徹底的檢查 || The doctor gave me a thorough check-up ___ I felt sick. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 醫生幫我做了徹底的檢查，因為我覺得不舒服。
thoughtful || adj. || 體貼的 || It was thoughtful of you to call. || 你打電話來真是體貼。 || thoughtful gift=貼心的禮物 || It was thoughtful ___ you to remember my birthday. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你記得我的生日真是貼心。
tickle || v. || 搔癢 || Don't tickle me! || 別搔我癢！ || tickle sb=搔某人癢 || The baby laughed ___ his father tickled him. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 爸爸搔寶寶癢時，寶寶笑了。
timetable || n. || 時刻表 || Check the train timetable. || 查看火車時刻表。 || train timetable=火車時刻表 || According ___ the timetable, the next train leaves at 3:15. || to|with|for || according to = 根據…。 || 根據時刻表，下一班火車三點十五分開。
timid || adj. || 膽小的 || The timid girl didn't speak. || 那個害羞的女孩沒說話。 || timid person=膽小的人 || He is too timid ___ speak in front of the class. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不敢…。 || 他太膽小了，不敢在全班面前說話。
tolerable || adj. || 可忍受的 || The pain is tolerable. || 疼痛還可以忍受。 || tolerable level=可忍受的程度 || The heat was hardly tolerable, ___ we stayed inside. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 熱到幾乎無法忍受，所以我們待在室內。
tolerance || n. || 容忍 || We should show tolerance toward others. || 我們應該包容他人。 || tolerance toward=對…的包容 || We should show tolerance ___ people with different opinions. || toward|with|of || tolerance toward = 對…的包容。 || 我們應該包容意見不同的人。
tolerant || adj. || 寬容的 || She is tolerant of other people's mistakes. || 她對別人的錯誤很寬容。 || be tolerant of=容忍… || A good teacher is tolerant ___ students' mistakes. || of|with|to be || be tolerant of = 容忍…。 || 好老師會包容學生的錯誤。
tolerate || v. || 容忍 || I can't tolerate the noise. || 我無法忍受噪音。 || tolerate sb=容忍某人 || Bullying should not ___ tolerated at school. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 學校不應該容忍霸凌。
tomb || n. || 墳墓 || They visited the tomb of the king. || 他們參觀了國王的陵墓。 || Tomb Sweeping Day=清明節 || People visit their ancestors' tombs ___ Tomb Sweeping Day. || on|in|at || 特定的節日（日子）用 on。 || 人們在清明節去掃祖先的墓。
tortoise || n. || 陸龜 || The tortoise moves slowly. || 陸龜移動得很慢。 || the tortoise and the hare=龜兔賽跑 || In the story, the tortoise won the race ___ the hare fell asleep. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 故事中，烏龜贏了比賽，因為兔子睡著了。
torture || n./v. || 折磨 || Waiting is torture. || 等待是種折磨。 || torture=酷刑 || It is cruel ___ torture animals. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 虐待動物是殘忍的。
tragedy || n. || 悲劇 || The accident was a tragedy. || 那場意外是一場悲劇。 || Shakespeare's tragedies=莎士比亞悲劇 || Romeo and Juliet is a tragedy ___ by Shakespeare. || written|writing|wrote || 過去分詞 written 表被動（被寫的）。 || 《羅密歐與茱麗葉》是莎士比亞寫的悲劇。
tragic || adj. || 悲慘的 || It was a tragic accident. || 那是一場悲慘的意外。 || tragic ending=悲劇結局 || The movie has a tragic ending ___ made everyone cry. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（ending）。 || 這部電影有一個讓大家都哭了的悲劇結局。
transfer || v./n. || 轉移；轉乘 || You need to transfer at Taipei Main Station. || 你需要在台北車站轉車。 || transfer to=轉到… || You need to transfer ___ the blue line at Taipei Main Station. || to|at|in || transfer to = 轉乘到…。 || 你需要在台北車站轉乘藍線。
transform || v. || 轉變 || The caterpillar transforms into a butterfly. || 毛毛蟲會變成蝴蝶。 || transform into=轉變成… || The old factory was transformed ___ an art center. || into|to be|as || transform A into B = 把 A 轉變成 B。 || 舊工廠被改造成藝術中心。
translate || v. || 翻譯 || Can you translate this into English? || 你可以把這個翻成英文嗎？ || translate A into B=把 A 翻譯成 B || Please translate this sentence ___ English. || into|to be|on || translate A into B = 把 A 翻譯成 B。 || 請把這個句子翻譯成英文。
translation || n. || 翻譯 || I read the English translation. || 我讀的是英文譯本。 || in translation=以譯本形式 || I read the book ___ Chinese translation. || in|on|by || in translation = 以譯本。 || 我讀的是這本書的中文譯本。
translator || n. || 譯者 || She works as a translator. || 她擔任翻譯。 || professional translator=專業譯者 || She works ___ a translator for a big company. || as|like|for || work as + 職業 = 擔任…。 || 她在一家大公司擔任翻譯。
transportation || n. || 交通運輸 || Public transportation in Taipei is convenient. || 台北的大眾運輸很方便。 || public transportation=大眾運輸 || Public transportation is ___ convenient in Taipei than in other cities. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 台北的大眾運輸比其他城市方便。
tremble || v. || 顫抖 || Her hands were trembling. || 她的手在發抖。 || tremble with fear=嚇得發抖 || The little boy was trembling ___ fear. || with|in|by || tremble with = 因…而發抖。 || 小男孩嚇得發抖。
