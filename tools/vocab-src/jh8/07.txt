# 八年級 07：married – peach
married || adj. || 已婚的 || My sister got married last year. || 我姊姊去年結婚了。 || get married=結婚; be married to=和…結婚 || My aunt got married ___ 2020. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 我阿姨在 2020 年結婚。
mask || n. || 口罩；面具 || Wear a mask when you are sick. || 生病時要戴口罩。 || wear a mask=戴口罩 || You should wear a mask ___ you have a cold. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 感冒時你應該戴口罩。
matter || n./v. || 事情；要緊 || What's the matter? || 怎麼了？ || what's the matter=怎麼了; no matter=無論 || What's the matter ___ you? || with|to|for || What's the matter with sb? = 某人怎麼了？ || 你怎麼了？
meal || n. || 一餐 || We have three meals a day. || 我們一天吃三餐。 || three meals a day=一日三餐 || Wash your hands before ___ meal. || each|all|both || each + 單數名詞 = 每一…。 || 每餐飯前都要洗手。
mean || v. || 意思是 || What does this word mean? || 這個字是什麼意思？ || mean to V=打算做… || What ___ this word mean? || does|do|is || 一般動詞 mean 的疑問句，this word 單數用 does。 || 這個字是什麼意思？
medicine || n. || 藥 || Take this medicine after meals. || 飯後吃這個藥。 || take medicine=吃藥 || Take this medicine three times ___ day. || a|an|the || 每天幾次用 a day。 || 這個藥一天吃三次。
medium || adj. || 中等的 || I'd like a medium Coke. || 我要一杯中杯可樂。 || medium size=中號 || I'd like a medium Coke ___ no ice. || with|by|of || with no ice = 去冰。 || 我要一杯中杯可樂，去冰。
meeting || n. || 會議 || We have a meeting at three. || 我們三點開會。 || have a meeting=開會 || The meeting will start ___ three o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 會議將在三點開始。
member || n. || 成員 || She is a member of the club. || 她是社團的成員。 || family member=家人; club member=社員 || How many members ___ there in your club? || are|is|do || There be 句型問數量：How many… are there? || 你們社團有幾位成員？
metro || n. || 捷運 || I take the metro to school. || 我搭捷運上學。 || take the metro=搭捷運; metro station=捷運站 || It takes me twenty minutes ___ go to school by metro. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 我搭捷運上學要花二十分鐘。
middle || n./adj. || 中間 || The table is in the middle of the room. || 桌子在房間中央。 || in the middle of=在…的中間 || Don't stop in the middle ___ the road. || of|at|in || in the middle of = 在…的中間。 || 不要停在馬路中間。
mile || n. || 英里 || The beach is two miles away. || 海灘在兩英里外。 || miles away=幾英里遠 || The beach is two miles away ___ here. || from|of|to || A is … away from B = A 離 B 有…遠。 || 海灘離這裡兩英里。
mind || n./v. || 心；介意 || Do you mind opening the window? || 你介意打開窗戶嗎？ || never mind=沒關係; change one's mind=改變主意 || Do you mind ___ the door? || closing|to close|close || mind 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 你介意關門嗎？
minute || n. || 分鐘 || Wait a minute, please. || 請等一下。 || wait a minute=等一下; in a minute=馬上 || The bus will come ___ five minutes. || in|after|at || in + 一段時間 = 在…之後（未來）。 || 公車五分鐘後會來。
mistake || n. || 錯誤 || Everyone makes mistakes. || 每個人都會犯錯。 || make a mistake=犯錯; by mistake=不小心地 || I took your pen ___ mistake. Sorry! || by|in|with || by mistake = 不小心、弄錯。 || 我不小心拿了你的筆，抱歉！
modern || adj. || 現代的 || Taipei is a modern city. || 台北是一座現代化城市。 || modern life=現代生活; modern art=現代藝術 || Taipei is ___ modern than it was twenty years ago. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 台北比二十年前更現代化。
moment || n. || 片刻；時刻 || Just a moment, please. || 請稍等一下。 || at the moment=此刻; for a moment=片刻 || He is busy ___ the moment. || at|in|on || at the moment = 此刻、目前。 || 他此刻很忙。
mop || v./n. || 拖地；拖把 || I mop the floor every Saturday. || 我每個星期六拖地。 || mop the floor=拖地 || It's your turn ___ mop the floor. || to|for|of || It's your turn to V = 輪到你做…。 || 輪到你拖地了。
motorcycle || n. || 摩托車 || My dad rides a motorcycle to work. || 我爸爸騎摩托車上班。 || ride a motorcycle=騎摩托車 || You must wear a helmet when you ride ___ motorcycle. || a|an|the more || motorcycle 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 騎摩托車時一定要戴安全帽。
mountain || n. || 山 || Yushan is the highest mountain in Taiwan. || 玉山是台灣最高的山。 || climb a mountain=爬山 || Yushan is the ___ mountain in Taiwan. || highest|higher|high || 三者以上比較用最高級 the highest。 || 玉山是台灣最高的山。
museum || n. || 博物館 || We visited the museum yesterday. || 我們昨天參觀了博物館。 || art museum=美術館; National Palace Museum=故宮博物院 || We ___ the National Palace Museum last Sunday. || visited|visit|have visited || last Sunday 是過去，用過去式。 || 我們上星期天參觀了故宮博物院。
nail || n. || 指甲；釘子 || Don't bite your nails. || 不要咬指甲。 || cut one's nails=剪指甲 || You should cut your nails ___ a week. || once|one|first || once a week = 一週一次。 || 你應該一週剪一次指甲。
national || adj. || 國家的 || The national flag is red, blue and white. || 國旗是紅、藍、白色的。 || national park=國家公園; national flag=國旗 || Kenting is one of the national parks ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 墾丁是台灣的國家公園之一。
nature || n. || 自然 || We should protect nature. || 我們應該保護大自然。 || in nature=在自然界; nature lover=大自然愛好者 || We should protect nature ___ future generations. || for|to|of || for + 人 = 為了…。 || 我們應該為了後代子孫保護大自然。
neighbor || n. || 鄰居 || Our neighbor has a friendly dog. || 我們的鄰居有一隻友善的狗。 || next-door neighbor=隔壁鄰居 || Our neighbor ___ a friendly dog. || has|have|having || Our neighbor 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 我們的鄰居有一隻友善的狗。
newspaper || n. || 報紙 || My grandpa reads the newspaper every morning. || 我爺爺每天早上看報紙。 || read the newspaper=看報紙 || I read about it ___ the newspaper. || in|on|at || 在報紙上讀到用 in the newspaper。 || 我在報紙上讀到這件事。
nobody || pron. || 沒有人 || Nobody knows the answer. || 沒有人知道答案。 || nobody else=沒有別人 || Nobody ___ the answer. || knows|know|knowing || nobody 視為單數，動詞加 s。 || 沒有人知道答案。
noodle || n. || 麵 || I want a bowl of beef noodles. || 我要一碗牛肉麵。 || beef noodles=牛肉麵; a bowl of noodles=一碗麵 || I want ___ bowl of noodles. || a|an|two || bowl 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 我想要一碗麵。
note || n. || 筆記；便條 || Take notes in class. || 上課要做筆記。 || take notes=做筆記; leave a note=留紙條 || You should take notes ___ class. || in|on|at || in class = 上課時。 || 你上課時應該做筆記。
nothing || pron. || 沒有東西 || There is nothing in the box. || 盒子裡什麼都沒有。 || nothing else=沒有別的; for nothing=免費、白費 || There ___ nothing in the box. || is|are|be || nothing 視為單數，There is。 || 盒子裡什麼都沒有。
notice || v./n. || 注意到；公告 || Did you notice anything strange? || 你有注意到什麼奇怪的事嗎？ || notice board=公告欄 || Did you notice ___ strange last night? || anything|something|nothing || 疑問句用 anything。 || 你昨晚有注意到什麼奇怪的事嗎？
o'clock || adv. || …點鐘 || It's seven o'clock. || 現在七點。 || at six o'clock=在六點 || I get up ___ six o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 我六點起床。
office || n. || 辦公室 || My mom works in an office. || 我媽媽在辦公室工作。 || post office=郵局; office worker=上班族 || My dad works in ___ office. || an|a|the more || office 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我爸爸在一間辦公室工作。
officer || n. || 警官；官員 || The police officer helped the old man. || 那位警官幫助了那位老人。 || police officer=警察 || A police officer ___ me find the way yesterday. || helped|help|helps || yesterday 是過去，用過去式 helped。 || 昨天一位警察幫我找到路。
once || adv. || 一次；曾經 || I go swimming once a week. || 我一週游泳一次。 || once a week=一週一次; at once=立刻 || I have been to Japan once, ___ I want to go again. || and|or|because || 連接兩個並列的句子用 and。 || 我去過日本一次，而且我還想再去。
other || adj./pron. || 其他的 || I have two cats. One is black, and the other is white. || 我有兩隻貓，一隻是黑的，另一隻是白的。 || the other=（兩者中）另一個; other people=其他人 || Some students like math, and other students ___ English. || like|likes|liking || other students 複數，動詞用原形。 || 有些學生喜歡數學，其他學生喜歡英文。
outside || adv./prep. || 在外面 || Let's play outside. || 我們去外面玩吧。 || go outside=到外面 || It's sunny. Let's ___ outside. || play|playing|plays || Let's 後面接原形動詞。 || 天氣晴朗，我們去外面玩吧。
own || adj./v. || 自己的；擁有 || I have my own room. || 我有自己的房間。 || on one's own=獨自; one's own=自己的 || She has ___ own room. || her|she|hers || one's own 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 她有自己的房間。
pack || v. || 打包 || Pack your bag for the trip. || 為旅行打包你的包包。 || pack up=打包; a pack of=一包 || Have you packed your bag ___? || yet|already|still || 現在完成式的疑問句用 yet（了沒）。 || 你打包好行李了沒？
package || n. || 包裹 || I got a package from my aunt. || 我收到阿姨寄來的包裹。 || send a package=寄包裹 || There is a package ___ you at the door. || for|to|of || 「給某人的」用 for sb。 || 門口有一個你的包裹。
papaya || n. || 木瓜 || Papaya milk is popular in Taiwan. || 木瓜牛奶在台灣很受歡迎。 || papaya milk=木瓜牛奶 || Papaya milk is one of the most popular ___ in Taiwan. || drinks|drink|drinking || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。 || 木瓜牛奶是台灣最受歡迎的飲料之一。
pass || v. || 經過；傳遞；通過 || Please pass me the salt. || 請把鹽遞給我。 || pass the test=考試及格; pass by=經過 || Please pass ___ the salt. || me|I|my || pass sb sth：後面的人用受格 me。 || 請把鹽遞給我。
past || n./prep. || 過去；經過 || It's ten past three. || 現在三點十分。 || in the past=在過去; walk past=走過 || In the past, people ___ letters, not e-mails. || wrote|write|writes || in the past 表示過去，用過去式 wrote。 || 過去人們寫信，而不是寄電子郵件。
paste || v./n. || 貼上；糊 || Copy and paste the text. || 複製並貼上文字。 || copy and paste=複製貼上; toothpaste=牙膏 || Copy the sentence ___ paste it here. || and|but|or || 連接兩個動作用 and。 || 複製句子然後貼在這裡。
pay || v. || 付錢 || I'll pay for the tickets. || 我會付票錢。 || pay for=支付…; pay attention to=注意 || Please pay attention ___ the teacher. || to|for|at || pay attention to = 注意…。 || 請注意聽老師說。
peach || n. || 桃子 || Peaches are sweet and juicy. || 桃子又甜又多汁。 || peach juice=桃子汁 || Peaches ___ sweet and juicy. || are|is|am || Peaches 複數，用 are。 || 桃子又甜又多汁。
