# 八年級 12：true – yummy
true || adj. || 真的 || Is it true? || 是真的嗎？ || come true=實現; true story=真實故事 || Is it true ___ you won the contest? || that|what|which || It is true that… = …是真的；that 引導名詞子句。 || 你贏了比賽是真的嗎？
turtle || n. || 烏龜 || The turtle walks slowly. || 烏龜走得很慢。 || sea turtle=海龜 || The turtle walks ___ slowly than the rabbit. || more|most|much || 副詞 slowly 的比較級用 more slowly。 || 烏龜走得比兔子慢。
twice || adv. || 兩次 || I brush my teeth twice a day. || 我一天刷兩次牙。 || twice a week=一週兩次 || I go swimming twice ___ week. || a|an|the || 一週幾次用 a week。 || 我一週游泳兩次。
type || n./v. || 類型；打字 || What type of music do you like? || 你喜歡哪種音樂？ || type of=…的類型 || What type ___ music do you like? || of|for|to || type of = …的類型。 || 你喜歡哪種類型的音樂？
typhoon || n. || 颱風 || A typhoon is coming. || 颱風要來了。 || typhoon day off=颱風假 || There was no school yesterday ___ the typhoon. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞 the typhoon，用 because of。 || 昨天因為颱風不用上學。
ugly || adj. || 醜的 || The duckling was ugly. || 那隻小鴨很醜。 || ugly duckling=醜小鴨 || The ugly duckling became ___ beautiful swan. || a|an|the more || beautiful 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 醜小鴨變成了一隻美麗的天鵝。
under || prep. || 在…下面 || The cat is under the table. || 貓在桌子下面。 || under the tree=在樹下; under 18=未滿十八歲 || The cat is sleeping under the table, ___ it? || isn't|doesn't|wasn't || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || 貓在桌子底下睡覺，不是嗎？
understand || v. || 了解 || I don't understand this question. || 我不懂這個問題。 || understand sb=了解某人 || Do you understand? Yes, I ___. || do|am|understand do || 用 Do you 問，就用 do 回答。 || 你懂嗎？懂。
unhappy || adj. || 不快樂的 || Why are you unhappy? || 你為什麼不開心？ || feel unhappy=覺得不開心 || She looks unhappy. What happened ___ her? || to|for|with || happen to sb = 發生在某人身上。 || 她看起來不開心，她怎麼了？
uniform || n. || 制服 || We wear uniforms at school. || 我們在學校穿制服。 || school uniform=學校制服 || Students have to wear ___ uniforms on Mondays. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 uniforms 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 學生星期一必須穿制服。
until || prep./conj. || 直到 || Wait here until I come back. || 在這裡等到我回來。 || until now=直到現在 || Wait here until I ___ back. || come|will come|came || 時間子句 until… 用現在式代替未來式。 || 在這裡等到我回來。
upload || v. || 上傳 || I uploaded the photos to the Internet. || 我把照片上傳到網路。 || upload photos=上傳照片 || I uploaded the photos ___ my blog. || to|at|in || upload A to B = 把 A 上傳到 B。 || 我把照片上傳到我的部落格。
useful || adj. || 有用的 || This dictionary is very useful. || 這本字典很有用。 || useful information=有用的資訊 || A dictionary is ___ useful tool. || a|an|the more || useful 以子音音 /j/ 開頭，用 a。 || 字典是很有用的工具。
usually || adv. || 通常 || I usually take the bus to school. || 我通常搭公車上學。 || usually=通常 || She usually ___ the bus to school. || takes|take|taking || She 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 她通常搭公車上學。
vacation || n. || 假期 || We went to Japan during summer vacation. || 我們暑假去了日本。 || summer vacation=暑假; on vacation=度假中 || What did you do ___ summer vacation? || during|while|when || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 你暑假做了什麼？
video || n. || 影片 || We watched a video in class. || 我們上課時看了一部影片。 || video game=電玩; watch a video=看影片 || Don't play video games ___ late at night. || until|since|for || until = 直到。 || 不要玩電動玩到深夜。
violin || n. || 小提琴 || She plays the violin well. || 她小提琴拉得很好。 || play the violin=拉小提琴 || She has played ___ violin for six years. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 她拉小提琴已經六年了。
visitor || n. || 訪客；遊客 || Many visitors come to Taipei 101 every day. || 每天有很多遊客來台北 101。 || foreign visitors=外國遊客 || Many visitors ___ to Taipei 101 every year. || come|comes|coming || Many visitors 複數，動詞用原形。 || 每年有很多遊客來台北 101。
voice || n. || 聲音；嗓音 || She has a beautiful voice. || 她有美妙的嗓音。 || in a low voice=低聲地; lose one's voice=失聲 || Please speak ___ a low voice in the library. || in|with|by || in a … voice = 用…的聲音。 || 在圖書館請低聲說話。
waiter || n. || 服務生 || The waiter brought us the menu. || 服務生拿菜單給我們。 || waiter=男服務生 || The waiter brought ___ the menu. || us|we|our || brought 後面的人用受格 us。 || 服務生拿菜單給我們。
waitress || n. || 女服務生 || The waitress is very friendly. || 那位女服務生很友善。 || waitress=女服務生 || The waitress asked ___ we wanted to drink. || what|which of|who || what 引導名詞子句（我們想喝什麼）。 || 女服務生問我們想喝什麼。
wallet || n. || 皮夾 || I lost my wallet. || 我弄丟了我的皮夾。 || leather wallet=皮夾 || Is this ___ wallet, Ben? || your|you|yours || 名詞 wallet 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 班，這是你的皮夾嗎？
watermelon || n. || 西瓜 || We eat watermelon in summer. || 我們夏天吃西瓜。 || a slice of watermelon=一片西瓜 || Would you like a slice ___ watermelon? || of|for|in || a slice of = 一片…。 || 你要來一片西瓜嗎？
wave || v./n. || 揮手；波浪 || She waved goodbye to us. || 她向我們揮手道別。 || wave goodbye=揮手道別; ocean wave=海浪 || She waved ___ us from the train. || to|of|for || wave to sb = 向某人揮手。 || 她從火車上向我們揮手。
weak || adj. || 虛弱的 || I feel weak today. || 我今天覺得很虛弱。 || weak point=弱點 || He was sick, so he felt ___ weak to go to school. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而無法…。 || 他生病了，虛弱到無法去上學。
welcome || v./adj. || 歡迎 || Welcome to Taiwan! || 歡迎來到台灣！ || you're welcome=不客氣; welcome to=歡迎來到 || Welcome ___ our school! || to|at|in || welcome to + 地點 = 歡迎來到…。 || 歡迎來到我們學校！
whether || conj. || 是否 || I don't know whether he will come. || 我不知道他是否會來。 || whether or not=是否 || I don't know whether it ___ rain tomorrow. || will|is|does || 名詞子句 whether… 講未來用 will。 || 我不知道明天是否會下雨。
while || conj. || 當…的時候 || I listen to music while I study. || 我讀書時會聽音樂。 || while=當…時、然而 || The phone rang while I ___ a shower. || was taking|took|am taking || while + 過去進行式，表示當時正在做的事。 || 我正在洗澡時電話響了。
wide || adj. || 寬的 || The river is very wide. || 這條河很寬。 || wide road=寬的道路 || The river is very wide, ___ we can't swim across it. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這條河很寬，所以我們游不過去。
wind || n. || 風 || The wind is strong today. || 今天風很大。 || strong wind=強風 || The wind was ___ strong that the trees fell down. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 風大到樹都倒了。
windy || adj. || 風大的 || It's windy in Hsinchu. || 新竹風很大。 || windy day=颳風的日子 || ___ is very windy in Hsinchu. || It|This|He || 描述天氣時主詞用 It。 || 新竹風很大。
wise || adj. || 有智慧的 || My grandpa is a wise man. || 我爺爺是一位有智慧的人。 || wise choice=明智的選擇 || It was wise ___ you to save money. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你存錢是明智的。
without || prep. || 沒有 || I can't live without my phone. || 沒有手機我活不下去。 || without doubt=毫無疑問 || He left without ___ goodbye. || saying|say|said || 介系詞 without 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 他沒說再見就離開了。
wonderful || adj. || 極好的 || We had a wonderful time. || 我們度過了很美好的時光。 || wonderful time=美好的時光 || We had a wonderful time ___ the beach. || at|in|of || at the beach = 在海邊。 || 我們在海邊度過了美好的時光。
workbook || n. || 練習簿 || Do the exercises in your workbook. || 做你練習簿上的習題。 || English workbook=英文練習簿 || Please finish page 10 ___ your workbook. || in|on|at || 在練習簿裡用 in。 || 請寫完練習簿第十頁。
worker || n. || 工人 || The workers are building a bridge. || 工人們正在建橋。 || factory worker=工廠工人 || The workers ___ building a new bridge. || are|is|does || 現在進行式；The workers 複數用 are。 || 工人們正在建一座新橋。
world || n. || 世界 || I want to travel around the world. || 我想環遊世界。 || around the world=全世界; in the world=在世界上 || Mount Everest is the highest mountain ___ the world. || in|of|on || 最高級 + in the world = 世界上最…。 || 聖母峰是世界上最高的山。
worry || v. || 擔心 || Don't worry about me. || 不用擔心我。 || worry about=擔心… || Don't worry ___ me. I'm fine. || about|of|at || worry about = 擔心…。 || 不用擔心我，我很好。
writer || n. || 作家 || She wants to be a writer. || 她想當作家。 || famous writer=有名的作家 || She has the potential ___ a great writer. || to become|becoming|became || the potential to V = 有做…的潛力。 || 她有成為偉大作家的潛力。
wrong || adj. || 錯誤的 || Your answer is wrong. || 你的答案錯了。 || go wrong=出錯; What's wrong?=怎麼了？ || What's wrong ___ you? || with|to|for || What's wrong with sb? = 某人怎麼了？ || 你怎麼了？
yet || adv. || 還（沒）；已經 || I haven't finished my homework yet. || 我還沒寫完功課。 || not yet=還沒 || I ___ finished my homework yet. || haven't|didn't|don't || 現在完成式的否定 haven't + p.p.，常搭配 yet。 || 我還沒寫完功課。
yummy || adj. || 好吃的 || This cake is yummy! || 這個蛋糕好好吃！ || yummy food=好吃的食物 || These cookies are ___ yummy! || so|such|much || so + 形容詞 = 好…。 || 這些餅乾好好吃！
