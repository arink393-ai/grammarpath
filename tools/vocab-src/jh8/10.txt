# 八年級 10：shorts – strange
shorts || n. || 短褲 || He is wearing shorts. || 他穿著短褲。 || a pair of shorts=一條短褲 || These shorts ___ too big for me. || are|is|am || shorts 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 這條短褲對我來說太大了。
shoulder || n. || 肩膀 || My shoulder hurts. || 我的肩膀好痛。 || shoulder to shoulder=肩並肩 || He carried the box ___ his shoulder. || on|in|at || 扛在肩膀上用 on。 || 他把箱子扛在肩上。
shout || v. || 大叫 || Don't shout at me. || 不要對我大吼。 || shout at=對…吼叫; shout for help=大聲呼救 || Don't shout ___ your brother. || at|to|for || shout at sb = 對某人吼叫（生氣）。 || 不要對你弟弟大吼。
shower || n. || 淋浴；陣雨 || I take a shower every night. || 我每天晚上淋浴。 || take a shower=淋浴 || I usually take a shower ___ dinner. || after|during|while || after + 名詞 = 在…之後。 || 我通常晚餐後洗澡。
shy || adj. || 害羞的 || She is shy with strangers. || 她對陌生人很害羞。 || be shy with=對…害羞 || Don't be shy. Come and join ___. || us|we|our || join 後面接受詞，用受格 us。 || 別害羞，來加入我們吧。
sidewalk || n. || 人行道 || Walk on the sidewalk. || 走人行道。 || on the sidewalk=在人行道上 || Please walk ___ the sidewalk, not on the road. || on|in|at || 在人行道上用 on。 || 請走人行道，不要走在馬路上。
sight || n. || 視力；景象 || The sunset was a beautiful sight. || 夕陽是一幅美麗的景象。 || at first sight=第一眼; out of sight=看不見 || The sunset is ___ beautiful sight. || a|an|the more || beautiful 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 夕陽是一幅美麗的景象。
sign || n./v. || 標誌；簽名 || The sign says "No Parking." || 標誌上寫著「禁止停車」。 || sign one's name=簽名; road sign=路標 || Please sign your name at the bottom ___ the page. || of|in|to || at the bottom of = 在…的底部。 || 請在頁面最下方簽名。
simple || adj. || 簡單的 || The question is simple. || 這個問題很簡單。 || simple life=簡單的生活 || This question is ___ simpler than that one. || much|many|more || 比較級前面用 much 加強語氣。 || 這個問題比那個簡單得多。
since || prep./conj. || 自從；既然 || I have lived here since 2020. || 我從 2020 年起就住在這裡。 || ever since=自從; since then=從那時起 || I ___ lived here since I was five. || have|had|am || since 搭配現在完成式 have + p.p.。 || 我從五歲起就住在這裡。
singer || n. || 歌手 || She is a famous singer. || 她是一位有名的歌手。 || pop singer=流行歌手 || She is the most famous singer ___ Taiwan. || in|of|at || 最高級 + in + 地方。 || 她是台灣最有名的歌手。
sir || n. || 先生（尊稱） || Can I help you, sir? || 先生，需要幫忙嗎？ || Dear Sir=敬啟者 || Excuse me, sir. ___ this your wallet? || Is|Are|Does || this your wallet 單數，用 Is。 || 先生，不好意思，這是您的皮夾嗎？
slide || n./v. || 溜滑梯；滑動 || The kids are playing on the slide. || 孩子們在玩溜滑梯。 || go down the slide=溜滑梯 || The children took turns ___ down the slide. || going|go|to going || take turns + V-ing = 輪流做…。 || 孩子們輪流溜滑梯。
smart || adj. || 聰明的 || She is a smart girl. || 她是個聰明的女孩。 || smart phone=智慧型手機 || She is smart. She ___ the answers to all the questions. || knows|know|knowing || She 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 她很聰明，所有問題的答案她都知道。
smell || v./n. || 聞起來；氣味 || The cake smells good. || 這蛋糕聞起來很香。 || smell good=聞起來很香 || The flowers smell ___. || nice|nicely|nicer than || 感官動詞 smell 後面接形容詞。 || 這些花聞起來很香。
smoke || v./n. || 抽菸；煙 || Smoking is bad for your health. || 抽菸對你的健康有害。 || no smoking=禁止吸菸 || You ___ not smoke here. || must|should to|have || must not = 禁止、不可以。 || 你不可以在這裡抽菸。
snack || n. || 點心；零食 || I like to eat snacks after school. || 我喜歡放學後吃點心。 || have a snack=吃點心 || Don't eat too many snacks ___ dinner. || before|after|until || 晚餐「之前」別吃太多零食，用 before。 || 晚餐前別吃太多零食。
snowy || adj. || 下雪的 || It was snowy in Japan. || 日本那時在下雪。 || snowy day=下雪天 || It ___ snowy when we were in Hokkaido. || was|is|were || 過去的天氣，It 用 was。 || 我們在北海道時正在下雪。
soldier || n. || 士兵 || The soldiers are very brave. || 士兵們非常勇敢。 || brave soldier=勇敢的士兵 || The soldiers ___ very brave. || are|is|am || The soldiers 複數，用 are。 || 士兵們非常勇敢。
someone || pron. || 某人 || Someone is knocking on the door. || 有人在敲門。 || someone else=別人 || Someone ___ knocking on the door. || is|are|be || someone 視為單數，用 is。 || 有人在敲門。
something || pron. || 某事；某物 || I want something to drink. || 我想喝點東西。 || something to drink=喝的東西 || I want something ___ drink. || to|for|of || something to V = 可以…的東西。 || 我想喝點東西。
sometimes || adv. || 有時候 || Sometimes I walk to school. || 我有時候走路上學。 || sometimes=有時 || Sometimes my brother ___ to school. || walks|walk|walking || my brother 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 我弟弟有時候走路上學。
somewhere || adv. || 某處 || I left my keys somewhere. || 我把鑰匙忘在某個地方了。 || somewhere else=別的地方 || Let's go somewhere ___ this weekend. || fun|funny|funs || somewhere 後面接形容詞修飾：somewhere fun。 || 我們這週末去個好玩的地方吧。
sore || adj. || 痛的 || I have a sore throat. || 我喉嚨痛。 || sore throat=喉嚨痛; sore eyes=眼睛痠痛 || I have ___ sore throat. || a|an|the || sore 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 我喉嚨痛。
sound || n./v. || 聲音；聽起來 || That sounds great! || 聽起來很棒！ || sound like=聽起來像; sound good=聽起來不錯 || Let's go to the beach. That sounds ___! || great|greatly|greatest || 感官動詞 sound 後面接形容詞。 || 我們去海邊吧。聽起來很棒！
south || n. || 南方 || Kaohsiung is in the south of Taiwan. || 高雄在台灣南部。 || in the south=在南部 || Kaohsiung is ___ the south of Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在某地區的某方位用 in the south of。 || 高雄在台灣南部。
space || n. || 太空；空間 || I want to travel to space. || 我想去太空旅行。 || outer space=外太空; space station=太空站 || There isn't enough space ___ my desk. || on|in|at || 在桌面上用 on。 || 我的桌上沒有足夠的空間。
special || adj. || 特別的 || Today is a special day. || 今天是特別的日子。 || special gift=特別的禮物 || Is there anything special ___ your birthday? || about|of|for || anything special about… = …有什麼特別之處。 || 你的生日有什麼特別的嗎？
spell || v. || 拼寫 || How do you spell your name? || 你的名字怎麼拼？ || spell a word=拼字 || How ___ you spell this word? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 這個字怎麼拼？
spend || v. || 花費（時間、金錢） || I spend two hours on homework every day. || 我每天花兩小時寫功課。 || spend time on=花時間在…; spend money on=花錢在… || I spend two hours ___ my homework every day. || on|in|for || spend 時間/金錢 + on + 名詞。 || 我每天花兩個小時寫功課。
spider || n. || 蜘蛛 || I'm afraid of spiders. || 我怕蜘蛛。 || spider web=蜘蛛網 || A spider ___ eight legs. || has|have|is || A spider 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 蜘蛛有八隻腳。
spoon || n. || 湯匙 || Eat your soup with a spoon. || 用湯匙喝湯。 || a spoon of=一湯匙 || Eat your soup ___ a spoon. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 用湯匙喝湯。
sports || n. || 運動 || I like sports. || 我喜歡運動。 || play sports=做運動; sports day=運動會 || What sports ___ you like? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你喜歡什麼運動？
square || n./adj. || 正方形；廣場 || A square has four sides. || 正方形有四個邊。 || town square=城鎮廣場 || A square ___ four sides of the same length. || has|have|is || A square 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 正方形有四個等長的邊。
stairs || n. || 樓梯 || Go up the stairs. || 走上樓梯。 || go upstairs=上樓; take the stairs=走樓梯 || Don't run ___ the stairs. || on|in|at || 在樓梯上用 on。 || 不要在樓梯上奔跑。
stamp || n. || 郵票 || I need a stamp for this letter. || 這封信需要一張郵票。 || collect stamps=集郵 || He has collected stamps ___ he was ten. || since|for|when || since + 過去時間子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 他從十歲起就開始集郵。
stand || v. || 站立 || Please stand up. || 請站起來。 || stand up=站起來; stand in line=排隊 || Please stand ___ line. || in|on|at || stand in line = 排隊。 || 請排隊。
start || v. || 開始 || The movie starts at seven. || 電影七點開始。 || start to V=開始做…; start with=從…開始 || It started ___ rain after lunch. || to|for|at || start to + 原形動詞 = 開始做…。 || 午餐後開始下雨了。
station || n. || 車站 || Where is the train station? || 火車站在哪裡？ || train station=火車站; police station=警察局 || How can I get ___ the train station? || to|at|in || get to + 地點 = 到達…。 || 我要怎麼去火車站？
steak || n. || 牛排 || I'd like a steak, please. || 請給我一份牛排。 || beef steak=牛排 || How would you like your steak? Medium, ___. || please|pleased|pleasing || 客氣的回答加 please。 || 你的牛排要幾分熟？五分熟，麻煩了。
still || adv. || 仍然 || It's still raining. || 還在下雨。 || still=仍然; stand still=靜止不動 || It is still ___ outside. || raining|rain|rains || 現在進行式 be + V-ing。 || 外面還在下雨。
stomach || n. || 胃；肚子 || My stomach hurts. || 我的胃好痛。 || stomachache=胃痛; on an empty stomach=空腹 || I ate ___ much, and now my stomach hurts. || too|so|very || too much = 太多（帶有負面結果）。 || 我吃太多了，現在胃好痛。
store || n. || 商店 || Let's go to the store. || 我們去商店吧。 || convenience store=便利商店 || There is a convenience store ___ the corner. || on|in|of || 在街角用 on the corner（或 at the corner）。 || 街角有一家便利商店。
story || n. || 故事 || Tell me a story. || 講個故事給我聽。 || tell a story=講故事; love story=愛情故事 || Grandpa told ___ a story last night. || us|we|our || told 後面的人用受格 us。 || 爺爺昨晚講了一個故事給我們聽。
straight || adv./adj. || 直直地；直的 || Go straight and turn left. || 直走然後左轉。 || go straight=直走; straight hair=直髮 || Go straight ___ turn right at the corner. || and|but|or || 連接兩個指令用 and。 || 直走，然後在轉角右轉。
strange || adj. || 奇怪的 || That's strange. || 真奇怪。 || strange sound=奇怪的聲音 || I heard a strange sound ___ I was sleeping. || while|during|since || while + 進行中的動作 = 在…的時候。 || 我睡覺的時候聽到一個奇怪的聲音。
