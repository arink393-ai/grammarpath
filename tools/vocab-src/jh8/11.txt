# 八年級 11：stranger – truck
stranger || n. || 陌生人 || Don't talk to strangers. || 不要跟陌生人說話。 || talk to strangers=和陌生人說話 || Don't go anywhere ___ strangers. || with|to|at || go with sb = 跟某人走。 || 不要跟陌生人去任何地方。
strawberry || n. || 草莓 || I love strawberry ice cream. || 我愛草莓冰淇淋。 || strawberry jam=草莓果醬 || These strawberries ___ very sweet. || are|is|am || These strawberries 複數，用 are。 || 這些草莓很甜。
street || n. || 街道 || Be careful when you cross the street. || 過馬路時要小心。 || cross the street=過馬路; on the street=在街上 || There are many people ___ the street. || on|in|at || 在街上用 on the street。 || 街上有很多人。
string || n. || 細繩；弦 || Tie it with a string. || 用細繩把它綁起來。 || a piece of string=一條繩子 || Tie the box ___ a string. || with|by|of || 用某樣東西用 with。 || 用繩子把箱子綁起來。
strong || adj. || 強壯的 || He is tall and strong. || 他又高又壯。 || strong wind=強風 || He is strong ___ he exercises every day. || because|so|but || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 他很強壯，因為他每天運動。
stupid || adj. || 愚蠢的 || That was a stupid mistake. || 那是個愚蠢的錯誤。 || stupid question=笨問題 || There are no stupid questions. Ask ___ you want. || whatever|however|whenever || whatever = 任何…的東西。 || 沒有愚蠢的問題，你想問什麼都可以。
subject || n. || 科目 || What's your favorite subject? || 你最喜歡的科目是什麼？ || favorite subject=最喜歡的科目 || What ___ your favorite subject? || is|are|do || your favorite subject 單數，用 is。 || 你最喜歡的科目是什麼？
successful || adj. || 成功的 || She is a successful businesswoman. || 她是一位成功的女企業家。 || be successful in=在…成功 || He was successful ___ his job. || in|on|at || be successful in = 在…方面成功。 || 他的工作很成功。
sugar || n. || 糖 || Don't put too much sugar in your coffee. || 咖啡別加太多糖。 || add sugar=加糖; no sugar=無糖 || How ___ sugar do you want in your tea? || much|many|long || sugar 不可數，用 How much。 || 你的茶要加多少糖？
supermarket || n. || 超市 || My mom goes to the supermarket every week. || 我媽媽每週去超市。 || at the supermarket=在超市 || We bought some fruit ___ the supermarket. || at|on|to || 在某個地點（店）用 at。 || 我們在超市買了一些水果。
surf || v. || 衝浪；瀏覽（網路） || I like to surf the Internet. || 我喜歡上網。 || surf the Internet=上網; go surfing=去衝浪 || My brother likes to surf ___ the summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 我哥哥喜歡在夏天衝浪。
surprise || n./v. || 驚喜；使驚訝 || What a nice surprise! || 真是個驚喜！ || to one's surprise=令某人驚訝的是; surprise party=驚喜派對 || To our surprise, the cat ___ the door by itself yesterday. || opened|opens|open || yesterday 是過去，用過去式。 || 昨天讓我們驚訝的是，貓自己把門打開了。
surprised || adj. || 感到驚訝的 || I was surprised to see him. || 看到他我很驚訝。 || be surprised at=對…感到驚訝 || I was surprised ___ the news. || at|to|for || be surprised at = 對…感到驚訝。 || 我對這個消息感到很驚訝。
sweet || adj. || 甜的 || This cake is too sweet. || 這個蛋糕太甜了。 || sweet dreams=祝好夢 || This cake is ___ sweet for me. || too|to|two || too + 形容詞 = 太…。 || 這個蛋糕對我來說太甜了。
swing || n./v. || 鞦韆；搖擺 || The kids are playing on the swings. || 孩子們在盪鞦韆。 || play on the swings=盪鞦韆 || The girl ___ playing on the swing now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The girl 用 is。 || 那個女孩現在正在盪鞦韆。
tape || n. || 膠帶 || Use tape to fix the paper. || 用膠帶把紙黏好。 || tape measure=捲尺 || Can I use your tape? I need to fix ___ book. || my|me|mine || 名詞 book 前用所有格形容詞 my。 || 我可以用你的膠帶嗎？我需要修我的書。
taste || v./n. || 嚐起來；味道 || The soup tastes good. || 這湯嚐起來很好喝。 || taste good=嚐起來很好 || This soup tastes ___. || delicious|deliciously|more delicious than || 感官動詞 taste 後面接形容詞。 || 這湯嚐起來很美味。
teach || v. || 教 || She teaches English. || 她教英文。 || teach sb sth=教某人某事; teach sb to V=教某人做… || My mom teaches me ___ cook. || how to|what to|to how || teach sb how to V = 教某人如何做…。 || 我媽媽教我怎麼做菜。
teenager || n. || 青少年 || Many teenagers like pop music. || 很多青少年喜歡流行音樂。 || teenagers=青少年們 || This singer is popular ___ teenagers. || with|to|for || be popular with = 受…歡迎。 || 這位歌手很受青少年歡迎。
temple || n. || 寺廟 || We visited a temple in Tainan. || 我們參觀了台南的一座廟。 || visit a temple=參觀寺廟 || People pray ___ the temple. || at|on|to || 在某個地點用 at。 || 人們在廟裡祈禱。
tennis || n. || 網球 || Let's play tennis. || 我們來打網球吧。 || play tennis=打網球; tennis court=網球場 || He has played tennis ___ five years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 他打網球已經五年了。
terrible || adj. || 可怕的；糟糕的 || The weather is terrible today. || 今天天氣很糟。 || terrible weather=糟糕的天氣 || The traffic was terrible, ___ we were late. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 交通非常糟，所以我們遲到了。
theater || n. || 劇院；電影院 || Let's go to the movie theater. || 我們去電影院吧。 || movie theater=電影院 || The movie theater is next ___ the bank. || to|of|at || next to = 在…旁邊。 || 電影院在銀行旁邊。
thick || adj. || 厚的 || This book is very thick. || 這本書很厚。 || thick book=厚書 || This book is very thick. It ___ 500 pages. || has|have|is || It 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 這本書很厚，有五百頁。
thirsty || adj. || 口渴的 || I'm thirsty. Can I have some water? || 我口渴了，可以給我一些水嗎？ || feel thirsty=覺得口渴 || I'm thirsty. Can I have ___ water? || some|a|many || water 不可數，請求時用 some。 || 我口渴了，可以給我一些水嗎？
though || conj. || 雖然 || Though it was raining, we went out. || 雖然在下雨，我們還是出去了。 || although=雖然; even though=即使 || Though he was tired, he ___ to finish his homework. || kept working|kept work|keep working || keep + V-ing = 持續做…；描述過去用 kept。 || 雖然他很累，他還是繼續寫功課。
throat || n. || 喉嚨 || I have a sore throat. || 我喉嚨痛。 || sore throat=喉嚨痛 || I have a sore throat, ___ I can't sing today. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 我喉嚨痛，所以今天不能唱歌。
through || prep. || 穿過；透過 || We walked through the park. || 我們穿過了公園。 || go through=經過、經歷; through the window=透過窗戶 || We walked through the park ___ get to school. || to|for|in || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們穿過公園去學校。
throw || v. || 丟；扔 || Don't throw trash on the ground. || 不要亂丟垃圾。 || throw away=丟掉; throw a ball=丟球 || Don't throw trash ___ the ground. || on|in|at || 丟在地面上用 on the ground。 || 不要把垃圾丟在地上。
ticket || n. || 票 || I bought two tickets for the concert. || 我買了兩張演唱會的票。 || buy a ticket=買票; ticket for=…的票 || How ___ are the tickets for the concert? || much|many|long || 問價錢用 How much。 || 演唱會的票多少錢？
tidy || adj./v. || 整齊的；整理 || Keep your room tidy. || 保持你的房間整齊。 || tidy up=整理 || Please tidy ___ your room before dinner. || up|on|off || tidy up = 整理乾淨。 || 晚餐前請把你的房間整理好。
tip || n. || 小費；訣竅 || Here are some tips for learning English. || 這是一些學英文的訣竅。 || give a tip=給小費; useful tips=實用的訣竅 || Here are some tips ___ learning English. || for|of|to || tips for + 名詞/V-ing = …的訣竅。 || 這是一些學英文的訣竅。
toast || n. || 吐司 || I have toast for breakfast. || 我早餐吃吐司。 || a piece of toast=一片吐司 || I'd like two pieces ___ toast. || of|for|in || a piece of = 一片…。 || 我要兩片吐司。
together || adv. || 一起 || Let's go together. || 我們一起去吧。 || get together=相聚; work together=一起合作 || My family and I ___ dinner together every night. || eat|eats|eating || 主詞 My family and I 複數，用原形。 || 我和家人每天晚上一起吃晚餐。
tooth || n. || 牙齒 || I have a bad tooth. || 我有一顆蛀牙。 || teeth=牙齒（複數）; brush one's teeth=刷牙 || My tooth hurts. I ___ go to the dentist. || have to|has to|having to || have to + 原形動詞 = 必須；主詞 I 用 have to。 || 我牙齒痛，我必須去看牙醫。
topic || n. || 主題 || What's the topic of your report? || 你報告的主題是什麼？ || topic sentence=主題句 || What's the topic ___ your speech? || of|for|to || the topic of = …的主題。 || 你演講的主題是什麼？
total || n./adj. || 總數；全部的 || The total is 500 dollars. || 總共是五百元。 || in total=總共 || There are thirty students ___ total. || in|on|at || in total = 總共。 || 總共有三十個學生。
touch || v. || 觸摸 || Don't touch the paintings. || 不要觸摸畫作。 || keep in touch=保持聯絡 || Let's keep ___ touch. || in|on|at || keep in touch = 保持聯絡。 || 我們保持聯絡吧。
towel || n. || 毛巾 || Can I have a clean towel? || 可以給我一條乾淨的毛巾嗎？ || bath towel=浴巾 || Can I have ___ clean towel? || a|an|the more || clean 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 可以給我一條乾淨的毛巾嗎？
traffic || n. || 交通 || The traffic is heavy today. || 今天交通很擁擠。 || traffic jam=塞車; traffic light=紅綠燈 || The traffic ___ very heavy this morning. || was|were|is being || 過去的事，traffic 不可數用 was。 || 今天早上交通很擁擠。
train || n. || 火車 || We took the train to Taichung. || 我們搭火車去台中。 || by train=搭火車; train station=火車站 || We went to Taichung ___ train. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 我們搭火車去台中。
trash || n. || 垃圾 || Please pick up the trash. || 請把垃圾撿起來。 || take out the trash=倒垃圾; trash can=垃圾桶 || Please pick ___ the trash on the beach. || up|on|off || pick up = 撿起來。 || 請把沙灘上的垃圾撿起來。
treat || v./n. || 對待；請客 || It's my treat. || 我請客。 || treat sb to=請某人吃… || Please treat others ___ you want to be treated. || as|like to|for || as = 如同、像…一樣（接子句）。 || 請用你希望被對待的方式對待別人。
trick || n. || 把戲；訣竅 || He showed us a card trick. || 他表演了一個紙牌把戲給我們看。 || magic trick=魔術把戲; trick or treat=不給糖就搗蛋 || He showed ___ a magic trick. || us|we|our || showed 後面的人用受格 us。 || 他表演了一個魔術給我們看。
trouble || n. || 麻煩 || I'm in trouble. || 我有麻煩了。 || in trouble=有麻煩; have trouble V-ing=做…有困難 || I have trouble ___ up early. || getting|to get|get || have trouble + V-ing = 做…有困難。 || 我早起有困難。
truck || n. || 卡車 || The truck is carrying a lot of boxes. || 這輛卡車載著很多箱子。 || garbage truck=垃圾車 || The garbage truck comes ___ eight every night. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 垃圾車每天晚上八點來。
