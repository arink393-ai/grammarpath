# 八年級 01：a little – blackboard
a little || phr. || 一點點 || I can speak a little Japanese. || 我會說一點日文。 || a little bit=一點點; a little water=一點水 || There is a little ___ in the bottle. || milk|milks|apples || a little 後面接不可數名詞 milk。 || 瓶子裡還有一點牛奶。
able || adj. || 能夠的 || She is able to swim across the river. || 她能游過那條河。 || be able to V=能夠做… || I was able ___ the test last week. || to pass|pass|passing || be able to + 原形動詞。 || 我上週考試及格了。
abroad || adv. || 在國外 || My cousin is studying abroad. || 我表哥在國外讀書。 || study abroad=出國留學; travel abroad=出國旅行 || My sister ___ abroad since 2023. || has studied|studies|studied || since + 過去時間點，用現在完成式。 || 我姊姊從 2023 年起就在國外讀書。
across || prep. || 橫越；在…對面 || The bank is across the street. || 銀行在街道對面。 || across from=在…對面; walk across=走過 || The bank is across the street ___ the park. || from|of|to || across (the street) from = 在…的（街道）對面。 || 銀行在公園的街道對面。
act || v./n. || 行動；表演 || We need to act now. || 我們現在就需要行動。 || act like=表現得像…; act in a play=演戲 || Don't act ___ a baby. || like|as|with || act like = 表現得像…。 || 別表現得像個小嬰兒。
action || n. || 行動；動作 || Actions speak louder than words. || 行動勝於言語。 || take action=採取行動; action movie=動作片 || Actions speak ___ than words. || louder|loud|loudest || 兩者比較用比較級 louder。 || 行動勝於言語。
actress || n. || 女演員 || The actress is famous in Korea. || 那位女演員在韓國很有名。 || movie actress=電影女演員 || The actress ___ in many movies since 2015. || has acted|acts|was acting || since + 過去時間點，用現在完成式。 || 那位女演員從 2015 年起演過很多部電影。
actually || adv. || 事實上 || Actually, I don't like coffee. || 事實上，我不喜歡咖啡。 || actually=其實 || He looks young, but actually he ___ forty. || is|was|are || 描述現在的事實用現在式 is。 || 他看起來很年輕，但其實他四十歲了。
afraid || adj. || 害怕的 || I'm afraid of the dark. || 我怕黑。 || be afraid of=害怕…; I'm afraid that=恐怕… || My little brother is afraid ___ dogs. || of|at|for || be afraid of = 害怕…。 || 我弟弟怕狗。
agree || v. || 同意 || I agree with you. || 我同意你的看法。 || agree with sb=同意某人; agree to V=同意做… || I don't agree ___ you. || with|to|at || agree with sb = 同意某人。 || 我不同意你的看法。
ahead || adv. || 在前面；提前 || Go ahead and eat. || 你先吃吧。 || go ahead=請便、繼續; ahead of=在…之前 || Go ahead ___ start without me. || and|but|or || 連接兩個祈使動作用 and。 || 你們先開始，不用等我。
airplane || n. || 飛機 || We took an airplane to Japan. || 我們搭飛機去日本。 || by airplane=搭飛機; take an airplane=搭飛機 || We went to Japan ___ airplane last summer. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by（中間不加冠詞）。 || 我們去年夏天搭飛機去日本。
airport || n. || 機場 || My dad picked me up at the airport. || 爸爸到機場接我。 || at the airport=在機場 || When I arrived at the airport, my parents ___ for me. || were waiting|wait|are waiting || 過去某時「正在」做的事用過去進行式。 || 我到機場時，爸媽正在等我。
allow || v. || 允許 || Smoking is not allowed here. || 這裡不准抽菸。 || allow sb to V=允許某人做… || My parents don't allow me ___ video games at night. || to play|play|playing || allow sb to + 原形動詞。 || 我爸媽不准我晚上玩電動。
almost || adv. || 幾乎 || It's almost ten o'clock. || 快十點了。 || almost all=幾乎全部; almost every day=幾乎每天 || I almost ___ the bus this morning. || missed|miss|misses || this morning 已經過去，用過去式 missed。 || 我今天早上差點錯過公車。
already || adv. || 已經 || I have already finished my homework. || 我已經寫完功課了。 || already done=已經完成 || I have already ___ my homework. || finished|finish|finishing || 現在完成式 have + p.p.。 || 我已經寫完功課了。
always || adv. || 總是 || She always gets up early. || 她總是很早起床。 || as always=一如往常 || She is always ___ time for class. || on|of|at || on time = 準時。 || 她上課總是很準時。
America || n. || 美國；美洲 || My uncle lives in America. || 我叔叔住在美國。 || North America=北美洲; South America=南美洲 || Have you ___ been to America? || ever|never yet|already ever || 現在完成式問「曾經」用 Have you ever + p.p.? || 你曾經去過美國嗎？
American || adj./n. || 美國的；美國人 || He is an American teacher. || 他是一位美國老師。 || American food=美式食物 || My English teacher is ___ American. || an|a|the || American 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我的英文老師是美國人。
angle || n. || 角度 || Look at it from a different angle. || 從不同的角度看它。 || right angle=直角; from another angle=從另一個角度 || How many right angles ___ a square have? || does|do|is || a square 單數，一般動詞疑問句用 does。 || 正方形有幾個直角？
another || adj./pron. || 另一個 || Can I have another cookie? || 我可以再吃一塊餅乾嗎？ || another one=另一個; one another=互相 || Can I have another cookie? They ___ delicious. || are|is|be || They（cookies）複數，用 are。 || 我可以再吃一塊餅乾嗎？它們好好吃。
anyone || pron. || 任何人 || Is anyone home? || 有人在家嗎？ || anyone else=其他任何人 || Does anyone ___ a pen? || have|has|having || Does 後面接原形動詞 have。 || 有人有筆嗎？
anything || pron. || 任何事物 || Do you want anything to drink? || 你想喝點什麼嗎？ || anything else=其他東西 || I didn't eat anything ___ breakfast. || for|of|in || 「早餐吃…」用 for breakfast。 || 我早餐什麼都沒吃。
apartment || n. || 公寓 || They live in a small apartment. || 他們住在一間小公寓。 || apartment building=公寓大樓 || We live ___ the fifth floor of an apartment building. || on|in|at || 在第幾樓用 on。 || 我們住在一棟公寓大樓的五樓。
appear || v. || 出現；似乎 || A rainbow appeared in the sky. || 天空出現了一道彩虹。 || appear in=出現在… || A cat suddenly appeared ___ the door. || at|on|of || 出現在門口用 at the door。 || 一隻貓突然出現在門口。
area || n. || 地區；區域 || This area is very quiet. || 這個地區很安靜。 || parking area=停車區; rural area=鄉村地區 || There ___ many shops in this area. || are|is|be || many shops 複數，There are。 || 這個地區有很多商店。
around || prep./adv. || 在…周圍；大約 || We walked around the lake. || 我們繞著湖散步。 || around the world=全世界; turn around=轉身 || I want to travel around the world ___ day. || some|any|one of || some day = 將來有一天。 || 我希望有一天能環遊世界。
arrive || v. || 到達 || We arrived in Tokyo at night. || 我們晚上抵達東京。 || arrive at=到達（小地方）; arrive in=到達（大地方） || We arrived ___ Tokyo at ten last night. || in|at|to || 到達大城市、國家用 arrive in。 || 我們昨晚十點抵達東京。
attack || v./n. || 攻擊 || The dog attacked the cat. || 那隻狗攻擊了那隻貓。 || heart attack=心臟病發; attack sb=攻擊某人 || The dog ___ attacked by a snake last night. || was|is|were || 過去的被動語態 was + p.p.。 || 那隻狗昨晚被一條蛇攻擊。
autumn || n. || 秋天 || Leaves turn red in autumn. || 秋天樹葉會變紅。 || in autumn=在秋天; autumn leaves=秋葉 || The weather is cooler in autumn ___ in summer. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 秋天的天氣比夏天涼爽。
badminton || n. || 羽毛球 || We play badminton after school. || 我們放學後打羽毛球。 || play badminton=打羽毛球 || Would you like ___ badminton with me? || to play|play|playing || would like to + 原形動詞。 || 你想跟我打羽毛球嗎？
bake || v. || 烘烤 || My mom bakes bread every weekend. || 我媽媽每個週末烤麵包。 || bake a cake=烤蛋糕; bake cookies=烤餅乾 || My mom baked a cake ___ my birthday. || for|to|at || for + 場合 = 為了…。 || 我媽媽為我的生日烤了一個蛋糕。
bakery || n. || 麵包店 || There is a bakery near my house. || 我家附近有一家麵包店。 || bakery shop=麵包店 || The bakery is next ___ the bank. || to|of|at || next to = 在…旁邊。 || 麵包店在銀行旁邊。
balcony || n. || 陽台 || My mom grows flowers on the balcony. || 我媽媽在陽台種花。 || on the balcony=在陽台上 || There are some flowers ___ the balcony. || on|in|at || 在陽台上用 on。 || 陽台上有一些花。
baseball || n. || 棒球 || Baseball is popular in Taiwan. || 棒球在台灣很受歡迎。 || play baseball=打棒球; baseball game=棒球比賽 || Baseball is ___ popular sport in Taiwan. || a|an|the more || popular 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 棒球在台灣是一項受歡迎的運動。
basket || n. || 籃子 || Put the apples in the basket. || 把蘋果放進籃子裡。 || shopping basket=購物籃; a basket of=一籃 || There are some eggs ___ the basket. || in|on|at || 在籃子裡用 in。 || 籃子裡有一些蛋。
because || conj. || 因為 || I stayed home because I was sick. || 我待在家因為我生病了。 || because of=因為（+名詞） || I stayed home because I ___ sick yesterday. || was|am|were || yesterday 是過去，主詞 I 用 was。 || 我昨天待在家，因為我生病了。
become || v. || 成為；變得 || She wants to become a doctor. || 她想成為醫生。 || become friends=成為朋友 || He wants to become a teacher ___ the future. || in|on|at || in the future = 在未來。 || 他將來想當老師。
before || prep./conj. || 在…之前 || Wash your hands before dinner. || 晚餐前洗手。 || before long=不久; the day before=前一天 || I always brush my teeth before I ___ to bed. || go|went|will go || 時間子句 before… 表示習慣，用現在式 go。 || 我總是在睡覺前刷牙。
behind || prep. || 在…後面 || The cat is hiding behind the sofa. || 貓躲在沙發後面。 || behind the door=門後; fall behind=落後 || Who is standing behind ___? || you|your|yours || 介系詞後面用受格 you。 || 誰站在你後面？
believe || v. || 相信 || I believe you. || 我相信你。 || believe in=信任、相信…存在 || I believe ___ you can do it. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 believe 的受詞。 || 我相信你做得到。
belong || v. || 屬於 || This book belongs to me. || 這本書是我的。 || belong to=屬於 || Who does this bag belong ___? || to|for|of || belong to = 屬於…。 || 這個包包是誰的？
beside || prep. || 在…旁邊 || Come and sit beside me. || 過來坐在我旁邊。 || beside the window=窗邊 || Come and sit beside ___. || me|I|my || 介系詞後面用受格 me。 || 過來坐在我旁邊。
between || prep. || 在…之間 || The bank is between the post office and the school. || 銀行在郵局和學校之間。 || between A and B=在 A 和 B 之間 || The park is between the library ___ the hospital. || and|or|to || between A and B = 在 A 和 B 之間。 || 公園在圖書館和醫院之間。
bicycle || n. || 腳踏車 || I ride my bicycle to school. || 我騎腳踏車上學。 || ride a bicycle=騎腳踏車 || He goes to school ___ bicycle. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by（中間不加冠詞）。 || 他騎腳踏車上學。
blackboard || n. || 黑板 || The teacher is writing on the blackboard. || 老師正在黑板上寫字。 || on the blackboard=在黑板上 || Please look ___ the blackboard. || at|on|to || look at = 看…。 || 請看黑板。
