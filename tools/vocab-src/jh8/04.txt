# 八年級 04：e-mail – follow
e-mail || n./v. || 電子郵件 || I'll send you an e-mail. || 我會寄電子郵件給你。 || send an e-mail=寄電子郵件; e-mail address=電子郵件地址 || I'll send ___ an e-mail tonight. || you|your|yours || send 後面的人用受格 you。 || 我今晚會寄電子郵件給你。
earthquake || n. || 地震 || There was a big earthquake last night. || 昨晚有一場大地震。 || strong earthquake=強烈地震 || There ___ a big earthquake last night. || was|were|is || 過去的 There be，a big earthquake 單數用 was。 || 昨晚有一場大地震。
Easter || n. || 復活節 || Children look for eggs at Easter. || 孩子們在復活節找彩蛋。 || Easter egg=復活節彩蛋 || Children look for colorful eggs ___ Easter. || at|in|to || 在節日期間用 at。 || 孩子們在復活節尋找彩蛋。
either || adv./pron. || 也（不）；兩者之一 || I don't like it, either. || 我也不喜歡。 || either A or B=不是 A 就是 B || I don't like fish. My sister ___ like it, either. || doesn't|don't|isn't || 否定句的「也不」用 either；主詞 My sister 用 doesn't。 || 我不喜歡魚，我姊姊也不喜歡。
elementary school || n. || 小學 || My brother is in elementary school. || 我弟弟讀小學。 || elementary school student=小學生 || When I was in elementary school, I ___ to school every day. || walked|walk|have walked || 描述過去的習慣用過去式。 || 我讀小學時每天走路上學。
elephant || n. || 大象 || Elephants have long noses. || 大象有長鼻子。 || baby elephant=小象 || An elephant is ___ than a horse. || heavier|heavy|heaviest || 兩者比較用比較級 heavier（y 改 i 加 er）。 || 大象比馬重。
else || adv. || 其他的；另外 || What else do you need? || 你還需要什麼？ || someone else=別人; or else=否則 || What else ___ you want to buy? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你還想買什麼？
engineer || n. || 工程師 || My mother is an engineer. || 我媽媽是工程師。 || software engineer=軟體工程師 || My uncle is ___ engineer. || an|a|two || engineer 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我叔叔是工程師。
enough || adj./adv. || 足夠的 || We have enough time. || 我們有足夠的時間。 || enough money=足夠的錢; old enough=年紀夠大 || There ___ enough chairs for everyone. || aren't|isn't|doesn't || There be 看後面名詞：chairs 複數，用 aren't。 || 椅子不夠每個人坐。
enter || v. || 進入 || Please knock before you enter. || 進來之前請敲門。 || enter a room=進入房間; enter a contest=參加比賽 || Please knock on the door ___ you enter. || before|after|until || 進入「之前」敲門，用 before。 || 進來之前請敲門。
envelope || n. || 信封 || Put the letter in the envelope. || 把信放進信封裡。 || stamp an envelope=在信封上貼郵票 || Write the address ___ the envelope. || on|in|at || 寫在信封「上」用 on。 || 在信封上寫地址。
eraser || n. || 橡皮擦 || May I borrow your eraser? || 我可以借你的橡皮擦嗎？ || use an eraser=用橡皮擦 || May I borrow your eraser? I can't find ___. || mine|my|me || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 mine。 || 我可以借你的橡皮擦嗎？我找不到我的。
error || n. || 錯誤 || There are some errors in your essay. || 你的作文有一些錯誤。 || make an error=犯錯; spelling error=拼字錯誤 || There ___ three spelling errors in your essay. || are|is|be || three spelling errors 複數，There are。 || 你的作文有三個拼字錯誤。
eve || n. || 前夕 || We have a big dinner on New Year's Eve. || 我們在除夕吃大餐。 || New Year's Eve=除夕; Christmas Eve=聖誕夜 || We have a big dinner ___ New Year's Eve. || on|in|at || 特定的日子前面用 on。 || 我們在除夕吃大餐。
even || adv. || 甚至 || He didn't even say goodbye. || 他甚至沒有說再見。 || even if=即使; even though=即使 || He didn't even say goodbye ___ he left. || when|what|where || when 引導時間子句「當他離開時」。 || 他離開時甚至沒說再見。
ever || adv. || 曾經 || Have you ever been to Japan? || 你曾經去過日本嗎？ || ever since=自從; forever=永遠 || Have you ever ___ to Japan? || been|went|go || 現在完成式問經驗：Have you ever been to…? || 你曾經去過日本嗎？
everyone || pron. || 每個人 || Everyone likes her. || 每個人都喜歡她。 || everyone else=其他每個人 || Everyone ___ happy today. || is|are|be || everyone 視為單數，用 is。 || 今天每個人都很開心。
everything || pron. || 每件事；一切 || Everything is ready. || 一切都準備好了。 || everything else=其他所有東西 || Everything ___ ready for the party. || is|are|be || everything 視為單數，用 is。 || 派對的一切都準備好了。
example || n. || 例子 || Can you give me an example? || 你能舉個例子嗎？ || for example=例如; set an example=樹立榜樣 || Can you give me ___ example? || an|a|the || example 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 你能給我一個例子嗎？
excellent || adj. || 極好的 || Your English is excellent. || 你的英文非常好。 || excellent job=做得非常好 || You did an excellent job. I'm proud ___ you. || of|for|to || be proud of = 以…為榮。 || 你做得非常好，我以你為榮。
except || prep. || 除了…之外 || Everyone came except Tom. || 除了湯姆，大家都來了。 || except for=除了… || Everyone ___ to the party except Tom yesterday. || came|come|comes || yesterday 是過去，用過去式 came。 || 昨天除了湯姆，大家都來參加派對了。
excited || adj. || 興奮的 || I'm excited about the trip. || 我對這趟旅行感到興奮。 || excited about=對…感到興奮 || I'm excited ___ the school trip. || about|of|on || be excited about = 對…感到興奮。 || 我對校外教學感到很興奮。
exciting || adj. || 令人興奮的 || The game was very exciting. || 那場比賽非常刺激。 || exciting news=令人興奮的消息 || The game was ___ exciting that we shouted. || so|such|very || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 那場比賽刺激到我們都大叫了。
excuse || n./v. || 藉口；原諒 || Excuse me. Where is the station? || 不好意思，車站在哪裡？ || excuse me=不好意思; make an excuse=找藉口 || Excuse me, ___ you tell me the way to the station? || could|are|do || 禮貌的請求用 Could you + 原形動詞？ || 不好意思，您可以告訴我去車站的路嗎？
exercise || n./v. || 運動；練習 || Exercise is good for your health. || 運動對健康有益。 || do exercise=做運動; regular exercise=規律運動 || You should exercise at least three times ___ week. || a|the|an || 每週幾次用 a week。 || 你應該一週至少運動三次。
expect || v. || 預期；期待 || I didn't expect to see you here. || 我沒想到會在這裡見到你。 || expect sb to V=期待某人做… || My parents expect me ___ hard. || to study|study|studying || expect sb to + 原形動詞。 || 我爸媽期望我用功讀書。
expensive || adj. || 昂貴的 || This bag is too expensive. || 這個包包太貴了。 || too expensive=太貴 || This phone is ___ expensive one in the store. || the most|more|most || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 這支手機是店裡最貴的。
experience || n. || 經驗；經歷 || It was a great experience. || 那是一次很棒的經驗。 || have experience=有經驗; work experience=工作經驗 || Traveling abroad was a great experience ___ me. || for|to|of || 「對某人來說」用 for sb。 || 出國旅行對我來說是很棒的經驗。
explain || v. || 解釋 || Can you explain this word to me? || 你可以解釋這個字給我聽嗎？ || explain sth to sb=向某人解釋某事 || Can you explain this rule ___ me? || to|for|with || explain sth to sb = 向某人解釋某事。 || 你可以向我解釋這個規則嗎？
fact || n. || 事實 || In fact, I don't like coffee. || 事實上，我不喜歡咖啡。 || in fact=事實上; the fact that=…的事實 || In fact, he ___ never been to Japan. || has|have|is || 現在完成式；主詞 he 用 has。 || 事實上，他從沒去過日本。
factory || n. || 工廠 || My father works in a factory. || 我爸爸在工廠工作。 || car factory=汽車工廠 || There are two hundred workers ___ the factory. || in|on|of || 在工廠裡用 in。 || 這間工廠有兩百名工人。
fail || v. || 失敗；不及格 || Don't be sad if you fail. || 失敗了也別難過。 || fail a test=考試不及格; fail to V=未能做… || He failed the test ___ he didn't study. || because|so|but || 前面是結果、後面是原因，用 because。 || 他考試不及格，因為他沒讀書。
famous || adj. || 有名的 || Taiwan is famous for its night markets. || 台灣以夜市聞名。 || be famous for=以…聞名 || Taiwan is famous ___ its night markets. || for|of|at || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 台灣以夜市聞名。
farmer || n. || 農夫 || The farmer grows rice. || 那位農夫種稻米。 || rice farmer=稻農 || The farmers ___ busy in the fields now. || are|is|be || The farmers 複數，用 are。 || 農夫們現在在田裡忙著。
favorite || adj. || 最喜愛的 || What's your favorite food? || 你最喜歡的食物是什麼？ || favorite food=最喜歡的食物 || What is ___ favorite subject? || your|you|yours || 名詞 subject 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 你最喜歡的科目是什麼？
festival || n. || 節日 || The Moon Festival is my favorite festival. || 中秋節是我最喜歡的節日。 || Lantern Festival=元宵節; Dragon Boat Festival=端午節 || What ___ you do during the Lantern Festival last year? || did|do|were || last year 是過去，用 did。 || 去年元宵節你做了什麼？
fever || n. || 發燒 || She has a high fever. || 她發高燒。 || have a fever=發燒 || She had a fever, ___ she didn't go to school. || so|but|because || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 她發燒了，所以沒去上學。
fight || v./n. || 打架；對抗 || Don't fight with your brother. || 不要跟你弟弟打架。 || fight with=和…打架; fight for=為…奮鬥 || Don't fight ___ your brother. || with|to|at || fight with sb = 和某人打架。 || 不要跟你弟弟打架。
file || n. || 檔案 || Save the file on your computer. || 把檔案存在你的電腦裡。 || save a file=存檔 || Did you send me the file? Yes, I ___ it yesterday. || sent|send|have sent || yesterday 是過去，用過去式 sent。 || 你把檔案傳給我了嗎？有，我昨天傳了。
fill || v. || 裝滿；填寫 || Fill the glass with water. || 把杯子裝滿水。 || fill in=填寫; fill A with B=用 B 裝滿 A || Fill the bottle ___ water. || with|of|by || fill A with B = 用 B 裝滿 A。 || 把瓶子裝滿水。
finally || adv. || 最後；終於 || We finally arrived home. || 我們終於到家了。 || finally=終於 || After two hours, we finally ___ home. || arrived|arrive|arriving || 描述過去的事用過去式 arrived。 || 兩個小時後，我們終於到家了。
finger || n. || 手指 || I cut my finger. || 我割到手指了。 || little finger=小指; point one's finger=指著 || Don't point your finger ___ people. || at|of|on || point at = 指著…。 || 不要用手指指著別人。
finish || v. || 完成 || Have you finished your homework? || 你寫完功課了嗎？ || finish + V-ing=做完… || I finished ___ my homework at nine. || doing|to do|do || finish 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我九點寫完功課。
fisherman || n. || 漁夫 || The fisherman caught a big fish. || 那位漁夫捕到一條大魚。 || fishermen=漁夫（複數） || The fisherman ___ a big fish yesterday. || caught|catches|catch || yesterday 是過去，用過去式 caught。 || 那位漁夫昨天捕到一條大魚。
fix || v. || 修理 || Can you fix my bike? || 你可以修我的腳踏車嗎？ || fix a car=修車 || Can you fix my bike ___ me? || for|to|with || 替某人做某事用 for。 || 你可以幫我修腳踏車嗎？
follow || v. || 跟隨；遵守 || Follow me. || 跟我來。 || follow the rules=遵守規則; follow sb=跟隨某人 || Please follow the rules, ___ you will be punished. || or|and|but || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 請遵守規則，否則你會被處罰。
