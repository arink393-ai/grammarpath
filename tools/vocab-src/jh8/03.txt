# 八年級 03：clap – during
clap || v. || 拍手 || Everyone clapped after the show. || 表演結束後大家都鼓掌。 || clap one's hands=拍手 || If you're happy, clap ___ hands. || your|you|yours || 名詞 hands 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 如果你很開心，就拍拍手。
clear || adj. || 清楚的；晴朗的 || The sky is clear today. || 今天天空很晴朗。 || make it clear=說清楚; clear sky=晴朗的天空 || Is everything clear? Yes, it's ___ clear now. || very|much|many || 形容詞前面加 very 表示「很」。 || 都清楚了嗎？是的，現在很清楚了。
clerk || n. || 店員 || The clerk helped me find a shirt. || 店員幫我找到一件襯衫。 || shop clerk=店員 || The clerk asked me what size I ___. || wanted|want|wants || 主要子句是過去式，名詞子句也用過去式。 || 店員問我要什麼尺寸。
cloudy || adj. || 多雲的 || It's cloudy today. || 今天多雲。 || cloudy day=陰天 || It was cloudy yesterday, ___ it didn't rain. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 昨天多雲，但沒有下雨。
coffee || n. || 咖啡 || My dad drinks coffee every morning. || 我爸爸每天早上喝咖啡。 || a cup of coffee=一杯咖啡 || How ___ cups of coffee do you drink a day? || many|much|long || cups 可數複數，用 How many。 || 你一天喝幾杯咖啡？
collect || v. || 收集 || He collects stamps. || 他收集郵票。 || collect stamps=集郵; collect money=募款 || He collects stamps. He ___ collected over 500 stamps so far. || has|have|is || so far 搭配現在完成式；主詞 He 用 has。 || 他收集郵票，到目前為止已經收集超過五百張。
comb || n./v. || 梳子；梳 || She combs her hair every morning. || 她每天早上梳頭髮。 || comb one's hair=梳頭 || She is combing ___ hair now. || her|she|hers || 名詞 hair 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 她正在梳頭髮。
comfortable || adj. || 舒服的 || This sofa is very comfortable. || 這張沙發很舒服。 || feel comfortable=感到自在 || This bed is ___ comfortable than that one. || more|most|much more than || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more + 形容詞。 || 這張床比那張舒服。
common || adj. || 常見的；共同的 || "Chen" is a common family name. || 「陳」是常見的姓。 || in common=共同的; common sense=常識 || We have a lot ___ common. || in|on|at || have ... in common = 有…共同點。 || 我們有很多共同點。
convenient || adj. || 方便的 || It's convenient to live near a MRT station. || 住在捷運站附近很方便。 || convenient for sb=對某人方便 || Is Friday convenient ___ you? || for|to|with || be convenient for sb = 對某人來說方便。 || 星期五你方便嗎？
copy || v./n. || 抄寫；影本 || Don't copy your classmate's homework. || 不要抄同學的作業。 || make a copy=影印一份 || Don't copy your classmate's homework. It ___ wrong. || is|are|am || It 單數，用 is。 || 不要抄同學的作業，那是不對的。
corner || n. || 角落；轉角 || Turn left at the corner. || 在轉角左轉。 || at the corner=在轉角; in the corner=在角落 || There is a lamp ___ the corner of the room. || in|at|on || 房間的角落（內部）用 in the corner。 || 房間的角落有一盞燈。
correct || adj. || 正確的 || Your answer is correct. || 你的答案是正確的。 || correct answer=正確答案 || Choose the correct answer ___ write it down. || and|but|or || 連接兩個動作用 and。 || 選出正確答案並寫下來。
cost || v./n. || 花費（金錢）；費用 || The bag costs 500 dollars. || 這個包包要五百元。 || cost sb money=花某人多少錢 || How much ___ the bag cost? || does|do|is || 一般動詞 cost 的疑問句，the bag 用 does。 || 這個包包多少錢？
count || v. || 數；計算 || Can you count from one to ten in English? || 你會用英文從一數到十嗎？ || count on=依靠; count to ten=數到十 || You can count ___ me. || on|in|at || count on = 依靠…。 || 你可以依靠我。
country || n. || 國家；鄉下 || Japan is a beautiful country. || 日本是個美麗的國家。 || in the country=在鄉下; foreign country=外國 || How many countries ___ you visited? || have|did|are || 現在完成式問經驗：have + p.p.。 || 你去過幾個國家？
course || n. || 課程；過程 || I'm taking an English course. || 我在上英文課程。 || of course=當然; online course=線上課程 || Can you help me? Of course I ___. || can|do|am || 用 Can you 問，就用 can 回答。 || 你可以幫我嗎？我當然可以。
cousin || n. || 堂（表）兄弟姊妹 || My cousin lives in Tainan. || 我的表哥住在台南。 || my cousin=我的表親 || My cousin is two years ___ than me. || older|old|oldest || 兩者比較用比較級 older。 || 我表哥比我大兩歲。
cover || v./n. || 覆蓋；封面 || Snow covered the mountain. || 雪覆蓋了山。 || be covered with=被…覆蓋; book cover=書的封面 || The mountain is covered ___ snow. || with|by|of || be covered with = 被…覆蓋。 || 山上覆蓋著雪。
cross || v. || 越過 || Look both ways before you cross the street. || 過馬路前要看左右兩邊。 || cross the street=過馬路 || Be careful ___ you cross the street. || when|what|who || when 引導時間子句。 || 過馬路時要小心。
dangerous || adj. || 危險的 || It's dangerous to swim here. || 在這裡游泳很危險。 || dangerous animal=危險的動物 || It is dangerous ___ in the river. || to swim|swim|swimming to || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 在河裡游泳很危險。
date || n. || 日期；約會 || What's the date today? || 今天幾月幾號？ || up to date=最新的; date of birth=出生日期 || What's the date ___? || today|tomorrow's|last || 問今天的日期：What's the date today? || 今天幾月幾號？
daughter || n. || 女兒 || They have one son and two daughters. || 他們有一個兒子和兩個女兒。 || only daughter=獨生女 || Mrs. Lin's daughter ___ a nurse. || is|are|am || Mrs. Lin's daughter 單數，用 is。 || 林太太的女兒是護理師。
dead || adj. || 死的 || The plant is dead. || 這株植物死了。 || dead body=屍體 || The fish in the tank were dead because nobody ___ them. || fed|feeds|feeding || 過去發生的事用過去式 fed（feed 的過去式）。 || 魚缸裡的魚死了，因為沒有人餵牠們。
deal || n./v. || 交易；處理 || It's a deal! || 一言為定！ || a great deal of=大量的; deal with=處理 || How do you deal ___ stress? || with|to|at || deal with = 處理、應付。 || 你怎麼處理壓力？
dear || adj. || 親愛的 || Dear John, how are you? || 親愛的約翰，你好嗎？ || Dear Sir=敬啟者 || Dear Amy, thank you ___ your letter. || for|to|of || thank you for + 名詞 = 謝謝你的…。 || 親愛的艾咪，謝謝你的來信。
death || n. || 死亡 || His death made everyone sad. || 他的死讓大家很難過。 || life and death=生死 || The news of his death ___ everyone sad. || made|make|making || 過去的事用過去式 made；make + 受詞 + 形容詞。 || 他死亡的消息讓大家很難過。
decide || v. || 決定 || We decided to go hiking. || 我們決定去健行。 || decide to V=決定做… || We decided ___ hiking on Sunday. || to go|going|go || decide to + 原形動詞。 || 我們決定星期天去健行。
deep || adj. || 深的 || The lake is very deep. || 這個湖很深。 || deep water=深水; take a deep breath=深呼吸 || The lake is very deep, ___ don't swim in it. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這個湖很深，所以不要在裡面游泳。
define || v. || 下定義 || Can you define this word? || 你可以解釋這個字的意思嗎？ || define A as B=把 A 定義為 B || The dictionary defines "happy" ___ "feeling pleasure." || as|to|for || define A as B = 把 A 定義為 B。 || 字典把 happy 定義為「感到愉悅」。
delicious || adj. || 美味的 || The beef noodles are delicious. || 這牛肉麵很好吃。 || delicious food=美味的食物 || These cookies smell ___ delicious. || so|such|much || so + 形容詞 = 好…。 || 這些餅乾聞起來好好吃。
dentist || n. || 牙醫 || I go to the dentist twice a year. || 我一年看兩次牙醫。 || go to the dentist=去看牙醫 || How often ___ you go to the dentist? || do|are|does || How often 問頻率；主詞 you 用 do。 || 你多久看一次牙醫？
department store || n. || 百貨公司 || Let's go shopping at the department store. || 我們去百貨公司購物吧。 || at the department store=在百貨公司 || The department store ___ open until ten tonight. || is|are|does || The department store 單數，用 is。 || 百貨公司今晚營業到十點。
dictionary || n. || 字典 || Look up the word in the dictionary. || 用字典查這個字。 || look up=查（字典） || Look ___ the word in your dictionary. || up|at|for || look up = 查閱（字典）。 || 在你的字典裡查這個字。
different || adj. || 不同的 || My hobbies are different from my sister's. || 我的嗜好和我姊姊的不同。 || be different from=與…不同 || My hobbies are different ___ yours. || from|of|with || be different from = 與…不同。 || 我的嗜好和你的不同。
difficult || adj. || 困難的 || Math is difficult for me. || 數學對我來說很難。 || difficult question=難題 || This test is ___ difficult than the last one. || more|most|very || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 這次考試比上次難。
dig || v. || 挖 || The dog is digging a hole. || 狗正在挖洞。 || dig a hole=挖洞 || The dog ___ digging a hole in the garden. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The dog 用 is。 || 狗正在花園裡挖洞。
dining room || n. || 飯廳 || We eat dinner in the dining room. || 我們在飯廳吃晚餐。 || in the dining room=在飯廳 || There is a big table ___ the dining room. || in|on|at || 在房間裡用 in。 || 飯廳裡有一張大桌子。
doctor || n. || 醫生 || You should see a doctor. || 你應該去看醫生。 || see a doctor=看醫生 || You look sick. You ___ see a doctor. || should|must not|don't || 給建議用 should + 原形動詞。 || 你看起來生病了，應該去看醫生。
dot || n. || 點 || Connect the dots. || 把點連起來。 || polka dots=圓點圖案 || The dress has many small dots ___ it. || on|in|at || 在衣服表面上用 on。 || 這件洋裝上有很多小圓點。
download || v. || 下載 || I downloaded a new app. || 我下載了一個新的 app。 || download an app=下載 app || Don't download apps ___ you ask your parents. || before|because|so || before + 子句 = 在…之前。 || 在問過爸媽之前不要下載 app。
dozen || n. || 一打（十二個） || I bought a dozen eggs. || 我買了一打雞蛋。 || a dozen=一打; dozens of=幾十個 || There are twelve eggs ___ a dozen. || in|on|of || 在…之中用 in。 || 一打有十二顆蛋。
drawer || n. || 抽屜 || The keys are in the drawer. || 鑰匙在抽屜裡。 || open the drawer=打開抽屜 || Put your socks ___ the drawer. || in|on|at || 放進抽屜裡用 in。 || 把你的襪子放進抽屜。
driver || n. || 司機 || The bus driver is very kind. || 那位公車司機很親切。 || bus driver=公車司機; taxi driver=計程車司機 || The taxi driver drove ___ fast. || too|to|two || too + 副詞 = 太…。 || 那位計程車司機開得太快了。
drop || v./n. || 掉落；一滴 || Don't drop the glass. || 別讓杯子掉了。 || drop by=順道拜訪; a drop of=一滴 || I dropped my phone ___ I was running. || while|during|until || while + 進行中的動作 = 在…的時候。 || 我跑步的時候手機掉了。
during || prep. || 在…期間 || Don't talk during the test. || 考試期間不要說話。 || during the day=白天期間; during summer vacation=暑假期間 || What did you do during the summer vacation? I ___ to Japan. || went|go|have gone || 描述過去的事用過去式 went。 || 你暑假做了什麼？我去了日本。
