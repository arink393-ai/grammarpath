# 八年級 05：fool – however
fool || n./v. || 傻瓜；愚弄 || Don't be a fool. || 別當傻瓜。 || April Fool's Day=愚人節; make a fool of=愚弄 || Don't make a fool ___ yourself. || of|to|for || make a fool of = 愚弄、出醜。 || 別讓自己出醜。
foreign || adj. || 外國的 || Can you speak any foreign languages? || 你會說任何外語嗎？ || foreign language=外語; foreign country=外國 || English is ___ foreign language for us. || a|an|the || foreign 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 英文對我們來說是一種外語。
foreigner || n. || 外國人 || Many foreigners visit Taipei 101. || 很多外國人參觀台北 101。 || talk to foreigners=和外國人交談 || A foreigner asked me the way, and I ___ him in English. || helped|help|have helped || 描述過去的事用過去式 helped。 || 一位外國人問我路，我用英文幫了他。
forget || v. || 忘記 || Don't forget your homework. || 別忘了你的功課。 || forget to V=忘記要做…; forget V-ing=忘記做過… || Don't forget ___ the door. || to lock|locking|lock || forget to V = 忘記要去做（還沒做）。 || 別忘了鎖門。
fox || n. || 狐狸 || The fox is very clever. || 狐狸很聰明。 || as clever as a fox=像狐狸一樣聰明 || The fox is ___ than the rabbit. || cleverer|cleverest|clever || 兩者比較用比較級 cleverer。 || 狐狸比兔子聰明。
friendly || adj. || 友善的 || The people here are friendly. || 這裡的人很友善。 || be friendly to=對…友善 || The new classmate is friendly ___ everyone. || to|at|for || be friendly to = 對…友善。 || 新同學對大家都很友善。
fries || n. || 薯條 || I'd like a burger and fries. || 我要一個漢堡和薯條。 || French fries=薯條 || Would you like some ketchup with your fries? Yes, ___. || please|pleased|pleasing || 接受別人的提議：Yes, please. || 你的薯條要加番茄醬嗎？好，麻煩了。
fry || v. || 油炸；煎 || Mom is frying some eggs. || 媽媽正在煎蛋。 || fried rice=炒飯; fried chicken=炸雞 || Mom ___ frying eggs in the kitchen now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；Mom 用 is。 || 媽媽現在正在廚房煎蛋。
future || n. || 未來 || What do you want to be in the future? || 你將來想做什麼？ || in the future=在未來 || What do you want ___ in the future? || to be|be|being || want to + 原形動詞。 || 你將來想做什麼？
garbage || n. || 垃圾 || Take out the garbage, please. || 請把垃圾拿出去倒。 || take out the garbage=倒垃圾; garbage truck=垃圾車 || Please take ___ the garbage. || out|off|up || take out = 拿出去（倒垃圾）。 || 請把垃圾拿出去倒。
gas || n. || 瓦斯；汽油；氣體 || The car needs gas. || 這台車需要加油。 || gas station=加油站 || Turn off the gas ___ you leave the kitchen. || before|after|until || 離開「之前」關瓦斯，用 before。 || 離開廚房前要關瓦斯。
glasses || n. || 眼鏡 || My grandpa wears glasses. || 我爺爺戴眼鏡。 || wear glasses=戴眼鏡; a pair of glasses=一副眼鏡 || Where ___ my glasses? || are|is|am || glasses 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 我的眼鏡在哪裡？
glue || n./v. || 膠水；黏 || Use glue to stick the paper. || 用膠水把紙黏起來。 || glue stick=口紅膠 || Can I use your glue? I left ___ at home. || mine|my|me || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 mine。 || 我可以用你的膠水嗎？我的忘在家裡了。
goat || n. || 山羊 || The goat is eating grass. || 山羊正在吃草。 || mountain goat=山羊 || There ___ some goats on the hill. || are|is|be || some goats 複數，There are。 || 山丘上有一些山羊。
goose || n. || 鵝 || The goose is swimming in the pond. || 鵝在池塘裡游泳。 || geese=鵝（複數） || A goose is ___ than a duck. || bigger|big|biggest || 兩者比較用比較級 bigger。 || 鵝比鴨子大。
grade || n. || 年級；成績 || I'm in the eighth grade. || 我讀八年級。 || good grades=好成績; eighth grade=八年級 || She studies hard, ___ she always gets good grades. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 她很用功，所以成績總是很好。
gram || n. || 公克 || This apple is 200 grams. || 這顆蘋果兩百公克。 || 500 grams=五百公克 || How ___ does this apple weigh? It's 200 grams. || much|many|long || 問重量用 How much does… weigh? || 這顆蘋果多重？兩百公克。
grape || n. || 葡萄 || I like grape juice. || 我喜歡葡萄汁。 || a bunch of grapes=一串葡萄; grape juice=葡萄汁 || How ___ grapes do you want? || many|much|long || grapes 可數複數，用 How many。 || 你要幾顆葡萄？
ground || n. || 地面 || The leaves fell to the ground. || 葉子掉到地上。 || on the ground=在地上 || Don't sit ___ the ground. It's wet. || on|in|at || 在地面上用 on。 || 別坐在地上，地是濕的。
guava || n. || 芭樂 || Guavas are popular in Taiwan. || 芭樂在台灣很受歡迎。 || guava juice=芭樂汁 || Guavas ___ rich in vitamin C. || are|is|am || Guavas 複數，用 are。 || 芭樂富含維他命 C。
gym || n. || 體育館；健身房 || We have PE in the gym. || 我們在體育館上體育課。 || go to the gym=去健身房 || My dad goes to the gym three times ___ week. || a|an|the || 每週幾次用 a week。 || 我爸爸一週去健身房三次。
habit || n. || 習慣 || Reading is a good habit. || 閱讀是個好習慣。 || good habit=好習慣; break a habit=戒掉習慣 || Reading before bed ___ a good habit. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 睡前閱讀是個好習慣。
half || n. || 一半 || Half of the students are girls. || 一半的學生是女生。 || half an hour=半小時; half of=…的一半 || I waited for half ___ hour. || an|a|the || hour 以母音音開頭，用 an：half an hour。 || 我等了半小時。
Halloween || n. || 萬聖節 || Kids wear costumes on Halloween. || 孩子們在萬聖節穿變裝服。 || Halloween party=萬聖節派對 || Kids go trick-or-treating ___ Halloween night. || on|in|at || 特定某天的晚上用 on。 || 孩子們在萬聖夜挨家挨戶要糖果。
handsome || adj. || 英俊的 || He is tall and handsome. || 他又高又帥。 || handsome man=帥哥 || He is the ___ handsome boy in our class. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 他是我們班最帥的男生。
hang || v. || 懸掛 || Hang your coat on the wall. || 把你的外套掛在牆上。 || hang up=掛斷電話; hang out=閒晃 || Let's hang ___ at the mall this weekend. || out|up|on || hang out = 出去閒晃、玩。 || 我們這週末去購物中心逛逛吧。
happen || v. || 發生 || What happened? || 發生了什麼事？ || happen to sb=發生在某人身上 || What happened ___ you? || to|for|at || happen to sb = 發生在某人身上。 || 你怎麼了？
hard-working || adj. || 勤奮的 || She is a hard-working student. || 她是一個勤奮的學生。 || hard-working student=勤奮的學生 || He is ___ hard-working that he gets good grades. || so|such|very || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 他非常勤奮，所以成績很好。
headache || n. || 頭痛 || I have a headache. || 我頭痛。 || have a headache=頭痛 || I have ___ headache. I need to rest. || a|an|the || headache 以子音開頭，have a headache。 || 我頭痛，需要休息。
health || n. || 健康 || Smoking is bad for your health. || 抽菸對你的健康有害。 || good health=身體健康; health care=醫療保健 || Exercise is good ___ your health. || for|to|at || be good for = 對…有益。 || 運動對你的健康有益。
healthy || adj. || 健康的 || Eating fruit keeps you healthy. || 吃水果讓你保持健康。 || stay healthy=保持健康; healthy food=健康食物 || Eating fruit every day ___ you healthy. || keeps|keep|keeping || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，動詞加 s。 || 每天吃水果讓你保持健康。
heat || n./v. || 熱；加熱 || Heat the soup before you eat it. || 喝湯前先加熱。 || heat up=加熱; summer heat=夏天的炎熱 || Heat the milk ___ you drink it. || before|after|until || 喝之前先加熱，用 before。 || 喝牛奶之前先加熱。
heavy || adj. || 重的 || This box is too heavy. || 這個箱子太重了。 || heavy rain=大雨; heavy bag=重的包包 || This box is too heavy ___ me to carry. || for|of|to || too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V = 對某人來說太…而無法…。 || 這個箱子太重，我搬不動。
height || n. || 身高；高度 || What's your height? || 你身高多少？ || the height of=…的高度 || My height is 160 centimeters, and my brother's height ___ 170. || is|are|has || my brother's height 單數，用 is。 || 我身高一百六十公分，我哥哥一百七十公分。
helpful || adj. || 有幫助的 || Thank you. You're very helpful. || 謝謝，你幫了大忙。 || be helpful to=對…有幫助 || This book is very helpful ___ me. || to|for|at || be helpful to sb = 對某人有幫助。 || 這本書對我很有幫助。
hide || v. || 躲藏；隱藏 || The cat hides under the bed. || 貓躲在床底下。 || hide and seek=捉迷藏 || The cat always hides ___ the bed. || under|on|in || under = 在…下面。 || 貓總是躲在床底下。
hike || v./n. || 健行 || We like to hike in the mountains. || 我們喜歡去山裡健行。 || go hiking=去健行 || We ___ going to hike in the mountains this weekend. || are|will|do || be going to + 原形動詞；主詞 We 用 are。 || 我們這週末要去山裡健行。
history || n. || 歷史 || I'm interested in history. || 我對歷史有興趣。 || history class=歷史課; in history=在歷史上 || I'm interested ___ history. || in|on|at || be interested in = 對…有興趣。 || 我對歷史有興趣。
hit || v. || 打；撞 || The ball hit the window. || 球打到窗戶。 || hit the ball=擊球; be hit by=被…撞到 || The man ___ hit by a car yesterday. || was|is|were || 過去的被動語態 was + p.p.。 || 那個人昨天被車撞了。
hold || v. || 握住；舉辦 || Hold my hand. || 握住我的手。 || hold on=等一下; hold a party=舉辦派對 || Hold ___, please. I'll get him for you. || on|up|off || hold on = 等一下（電話用語）。 || 請稍等，我幫你叫他。
holiday || n. || 假日 || We had a great holiday in Kenting. || 我們在墾丁度過了很棒的假期。 || on holiday=度假中; public holiday=國定假日 || What did you do ___ the holiday? || during|while|when || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 你假期做了什麼？
honest || adj. || 誠實的 || Be honest with me. || 對我誠實一點。 || be honest with=對…誠實 || He is ___ honest boy. || an|a|the || honest 的 h 不發音，以母音音開頭，用 an。 || 他是一個誠實的男孩。
hope || v./n. || 希望 || I hope you get well soon. || 希望你早日康復。 || hope to V=希望做…; I hope so=希望如此 || I hope ___ you again soon. || to see|see|seeing || hope to + 原形動詞。 || 我希望很快再見到你。
hospital || n. || 醫院 || My grandpa is in the hospital. || 我爺爺住院了。 || in the hospital=住院; go to the hospital=去醫院 || My grandma ___ in the hospital since last week. || has been|is|was || since + 過去時間，用現在完成式 has been。 || 我奶奶從上週起就住院了。
housewife || n. || 家庭主婦 || My mom is a housewife. || 我媽媽是家庭主婦。 || housewives=家庭主婦（複數） || My aunt is a housewife. She takes care ___ three kids. || of|for|to || take care of = 照顧。 || 我阿姨是家庭主婦，她照顧三個小孩。
however || adv. || 然而 || It rained. However, we still went out. || 下雨了，然而我們還是出去了。 || however=然而 || It was raining. However, we still ___ out. || went|go|goes || 描述過去的事用過去式 went。 || 下著雨，然而我們還是出去了。
