# 八年級 09：reach – shopkeeper
reach || v. || 到達；伸手拿到 || We reached the top of the mountain. || 我們到達了山頂。 || reach the top=到達頂端; reach for=伸手去拿 || The book is too high. I can't reach ___. || it|them|its || the book 單數物品，受格用 it。 || 那本書太高了，我拿不到。
really || adv. || 真的 || I really like this song. || 我真的很喜歡這首歌。 || really good=真的很好 || I really ___ this song. || like|likes|liking || 主詞 I，動詞用原形。 || 我真的很喜歡這首歌。
reason || n. || 理由；原因 || Tell me the reason. || 告訴我原因。 || the reason for=…的理由; for this reason=因此 || What is the reason ___ your absence? || for|of|to || the reason for = …的理由。 || 你缺席的原因是什麼？
refrigerator || n. || 冰箱 || Put the milk in the refrigerator. || 把牛奶放進冰箱。 || in the refrigerator=在冰箱裡 || Is there any juice ___ the refrigerator? || in|on|at || 在冰箱裡用 in。 || 冰箱裡有果汁嗎？
relative || n. || 親戚 || We visit our relatives during Chinese New Year. || 我們過年時去拜訪親戚。 || visit relatives=拜訪親戚 || We visit our relatives ___ Chinese New Year. || during|while|when || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 我們過年期間去拜訪親戚。
remember || v. || 記得 || Remember to bring your umbrella. || 記得帶你的雨傘。 || remember to V=記得要做; remember V-ing=記得做過 || Remember ___ your umbrella tomorrow. || to bring|bringing|brought || remember to V = 記得要去做（還沒做）。 || 明天記得帶雨傘。
repeat || v. || 重複 || Please repeat after me. || 請跟著我唸。 || repeat after me=跟我唸 || Could you repeat that, please? I ___ hear you. || didn't|don't|wasn't || 剛剛發生的事用過去式否定 didn't。 || 你可以再說一次嗎？我剛剛沒聽到。
report || n./v. || 報告；報導 || I have to write a report on pollution. || 我得寫一份關於污染的報告。 || weather report=氣象報告; write a report=寫報告 || I have to finish my report ___ Friday. || by|until|at || by + 時間 = 在…之前（截止）。 || 我必須在星期五之前完成報告。
reporter || n. || 記者 || The reporter asked many questions. || 那位記者問了很多問題。 || news reporter=新聞記者 || The reporter ___ the singer after the concert yesterday. || interviewed|interviews|interview || yesterday 是過去，用過去式。 || 記者昨天在演唱會後訪問了那位歌手。
rest || n./v. || 休息；其餘 || You should get some rest. || 你應該休息一下。 || take a rest=休息; the rest of=其餘的 || You look tired. You ___ take a rest. || should|must not|don't || 給建議用 should + 原形動詞。 || 你看起來很累，應該休息一下。
restaurant || n. || 餐廳 || Let's eat at that restaurant. || 我們去那家餐廳吃吧。 || Chinese restaurant=中式餐廳 || This is the best restaurant ___ town. || in|on|at || 最高級 + in + 地方 = 在…中最…。 || 這是鎮上最好的餐廳。
restroom || n. || 洗手間 || Where is the restroom? || 洗手間在哪裡？ || go to the restroom=上廁所 || Excuse me, ___ is the restroom? || where|what|which || 問地點用 where。 || 不好意思，洗手間在哪裡？
rich || adj. || 富有的；豐富的 || He is rich but not happy. || 他很有錢但不快樂。 || rich in=富含… || Fruit is rich ___ vitamins. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 水果富含維他命。
rise || v. || 上升 || The sun rises in the east. || 太陽從東方升起。 || prices rise=物價上漲 || The sun rises ___ the east. || in|from|at || 在某方位用 in the east。 || 太陽從東方升起。
roll || v./n. || 滾動；捲 || The ball rolled down the hill. || 球滾下山坡。 || egg roll=蛋捲; roll up=捲起來 || The ball rolled ___ the hill. || down|under|off of || roll down = 滾下。 || 球滾下山坡。
root || n. || 根 || Plants take in water through their roots. || 植物透過根吸收水分。 || take root=生根 || Plants take in water ___ their roots. || through|across|along || through = 透過、經由。 || 植物透過根部吸收水分。
rope || n. || 繩子 || Hold the rope tightly. || 緊緊抓住繩子。 || jump rope=跳繩 || The kids ___ jumping rope on the playground now. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；The kids 複數用 are。 || 孩子們現在正在操場上跳繩。
row || n. || 排；列 || I sit in the first row. || 我坐在第一排。 || in a row=一排、連續 || She sits in the front row ___ class. || in|on|at || in class = 上課時。 || 她上課時坐在前排。
rule || n. || 規則 || Follow the rules. || 遵守規則。 || follow the rules=遵守規則; break the rules=違反規則 || You must follow the school rules, ___ you will be punished. || or|and|but || 祈使意味 + or = 否則…。 || 你必須遵守校規，否則會被處罰。
safe || adj. || 安全的 || It's not safe to swim here. || 在這裡游泳不安全。 || safe place=安全的地方; stay safe=注意安全 || It is not safe ___ alone at night. || to walk|walk|walking to || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 晚上獨自走路不安全。
sail || v. || 航行 || We sailed across the lake. || 我們划船穿越那座湖。 || go sailing=去航行 || They are going to sail ___ Japan next month. || to|at|in || sail to + 地點 = 航行到…。 || 他們下個月要航行到日本。
sale || n. || 出售；拍賣 || The shoes are on sale. || 這雙鞋在特價。 || on sale=特價中; for sale=出售 || These shoes are on sale. They are ___ cheaper than usual. || much|many|very || 比較級前面用 much 加強語氣。 || 這雙鞋在特價，比平常便宜很多。
salesman || n. || 推銷員 || The salesman tried to sell me a phone. || 那位推銷員試著賣我一支手機。 || car salesman=汽車業務員 || The salesman tried ___ me a new phone. || to sell|selling|sell || try to + 原形動詞 = 試著做…。 || 那位推銷員試著賣我一支新手機。
scared || adj. || 害怕的 || I'm scared of the dark. || 我怕黑。 || be scared of=害怕… || My sister is scared ___ spiders. || of|at|for || be scared of = 害怕…。 || 我妹妹怕蜘蛛。
science || n. || 科學 || Science is my favorite subject. || 科學是我最喜歡的科目。 || science class=自然課; science fiction=科幻 || I'm interested ___ science. || in|on|at || be interested in = 對…有興趣。 || 我對科學有興趣。
scooter || n. || 機車；滑板車 || My mom rides a scooter to work. || 我媽媽騎機車上班。 || ride a scooter=騎機車 || There ___ many scooters on the streets of Taipei. || are|is|be || many scooters 複數，There are。 || 台北的街上有很多機車。
screen || n. || 螢幕 || Don't look at the screen for too long. || 不要盯著螢幕太久。 || computer screen=電腦螢幕 || Don't look at the screen ___ too long. || for|in|at || for + 一段時間。 || 不要盯著螢幕太久。
season || n. || 季節 || Summer is my favorite season. || 夏天是我最喜歡的季節。 || four seasons=四季; rainy season=雨季 || There ___ four seasons in a year. || are|is|have || four seasons 複數，There are。 || 一年有四個季節。
seat || n. || 座位 || Is this seat taken? || 這個位子有人坐嗎？ || take a seat=坐下; window seat=靠窗座位 || Please take ___ seat. || a|an|the more || take a seat = 請坐。 || 請坐。
secretary || n. || 秘書 || The secretary answers the phone. || 秘書負責接電話。 || personal secretary=私人秘書 || The secretary ___ answering the phone now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The secretary 用 is。 || 秘書現在正在接電話。
seed || n. || 種子 || Plant the seeds in spring. || 在春天播種。 || plant seeds=播種 || We planted some seeds ___ the garden last week. || in|on|at || 在花園裡用 in。 || 我們上週在花園裡播了一些種子。
seesaw || n. || 蹺蹺板 || The kids are playing on the seesaw. || 孩子們在玩蹺蹺板。 || play on the seesaw=玩蹺蹺板 || Two kids are playing ___ the seesaw. || on|in|at || 在遊樂設施上用 on。 || 兩個小孩在玩蹺蹺板。
seldom || adv. || 很少 || He seldom eats breakfast. || 他很少吃早餐。 || seldom=很少 || He seldom ___ late for school. || is|be|are || 頻率副詞放在 be 動詞後；He 用 is。 || 他上學很少遲到。
senior high school || n. || 高中 || My brother is a senior high school student. || 我哥哥是高中生。 || senior high school student=高中生 || My brother ___ in senior high school now. || is|are|am || My brother 單數，用 is。 || 我哥哥現在讀高中。
sentence || n. || 句子 || Make a sentence with this word. || 用這個字造句。 || make a sentence=造句 || Please make a sentence ___ this word. || with|by|of || 用某個字造句用 with。 || 請用這個字造句。
serious || adj. || 嚴肅的；嚴重的 || Are you serious? || 你是認真的嗎？ || serious problem=嚴重的問題 || Air pollution is ___ serious problem. || a|an|the more || serious 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 空氣污染是一個嚴重的問題。
service || n. || 服務 || The service here is very good. || 這裡的服務很好。 || customer service=客服 || The service in this restaurant ___ excellent. || is|are|be || The service 不可數，用 is。 || 這家餐廳的服務非常好。
set || v./n. || 設定；一套 || Set the alarm for six. || 把鬧鐘設在六點。 || set the table=擺餐具; a set of=一套 || Set the alarm ___ six o'clock. || for|on|in || set the alarm for + 時間 = 把鬧鐘設在…。 || 把鬧鐘設在六點。
several || adj. || 幾個 || I have several friends in Japan. || 我在日本有幾個朋友。 || several times=好幾次 || I have been to Japan several ___. || times|time|timing || several + 複數名詞 times。 || 我去過日本好幾次。
shake || v. || 搖動 || Shake hands with him. || 跟他握手。 || shake hands=握手; shake one's head=搖頭 || Please shake hands ___ your partner. || with|to|at || shake hands with = 和…握手。 || 請和你的夥伴握手。
shape || n. || 形狀 || What shape is it? || 它是什麼形狀？ || in the shape of=…的形狀; stay in shape=保持身材 || What shape ___ the moon tonight? || is|are|does || the moon 單數，用 is。 || 今晚的月亮是什麼形狀？
share || v. || 分享 || Let's share the pizza. || 我們分享披薩吧。 || share A with B=和 B 分享 A || Can you share your notes ___ me? || with|to|for || share sth with sb = 和某人分享某物。 || 你可以跟我分享你的筆記嗎？
sharp || adj. || 鋒利的；（時間）整 || The knife is very sharp. || 這把刀很鋒利。 || at seven sharp=七點整 || The knife is sharp. Be careful ___ it. || with|on|at || be careful with = 小心使用…。 || 刀子很鋒利，使用時要小心。
sheep || n. || 綿羊 || There are many sheep on the farm. || 農場上有很多綿羊。 || count sheep=數羊 || There ___ many sheep on the farm. || are|is|be || sheep 單複數同形；many sheep 是複數，用 are。 || 農場上有很多綿羊。
shine || v. || 照耀；發光 || The sun is shining. || 太陽正照耀著。 || shine on=照在…上 || The sun ___ shining brightly today. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The sun 用 is。 || 今天陽光燦爛。
shopkeeper || n. || 店主 || The shopkeeper is very friendly. || 那位店主很友善。 || shopkeeper=老闆（店主） || The shopkeeper gave ___ a discount. || us|we|our || gave 後面的人用受格 us。 || 店主給了我們折扣。
