# 八年級 06：hunt – market
hunt || v. || 打獵；搜尋 || Lions hunt at night. || 獅子在夜間獵食。 || hunt for=尋找; go hunting=去打獵 || Owls hunt ___ night. || at|in|on || at night = 在夜間。 || 貓頭鷹在夜間獵食。
hurry || v./n. || 趕快；匆忙 || Hurry up! We're late. || 快點！我們遲到了。 || hurry up=趕快; in a hurry=匆忙地 || Hurry up, ___ we will miss the train. || or|and|so || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 快點，不然我們會錯過火車。
hurt || v. || 受傷；傷害 || I hurt my leg. || 我的腿受傷了。 || get hurt=受傷; hurt oneself=傷到自己 || Be careful, or you'll hurt ___. || yourself|you|your || 主詞與受詞同一人用反身代名詞 yourself。 || 小心點，不然你會傷到自己。
husband || n. || 丈夫 || Her husband is a doctor. || 她的丈夫是醫生。 || husband and wife=夫妻 || Mrs. Wang and ___ husband are teachers. || her|she|hers || 名詞 husband 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 王太太和她的先生都是老師。
important || adj. || 重要的 || Health is important. || 健康很重要。 || it is important to V=做…很重要 || It is important ___ enough sleep. || to get|get|got || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 睡飽是很重要的。
inch || n. || 英寸 || The pizza is twelve inches wide. || 這披薩寬十二英寸。 || inch by inch=一點一點地 || There are twelve inches ___ a foot. || in|on|of || 在…之中用 in。 || 一英尺有十二英寸。
insect || n. || 昆蟲 || An ant is an insect. || 螞蟻是昆蟲。 || kinds of insects=各種昆蟲 || An insect ___ six legs. || has|have|is || An insect 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 昆蟲有六隻腳。
inside || prep./adv. || 在…裡面 || It's raining. Let's stay inside. || 在下雨，我們待在裡面吧。 || inside the house=在屋子裡 || It's raining, ___ let's stay inside. || so|but|because || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 在下雨，所以我們待在屋內吧。
interest || n. || 興趣 || I have an interest in art. || 我對藝術有興趣。 || show interest in=對…表現興趣 || She has a strong interest ___ music. || in|on|at || interest in = 對…的興趣。 || 她對音樂很有興趣。
interested || adj. || 感興趣的 || I'm interested in science. || 我對科學有興趣。 || be interested in=對…感興趣 || I'm interested ___ learning Japanese. || in|on|at || be interested in = 對…感興趣。 || 我對學日文有興趣。
interesting || adj. || 有趣的 || The story is interesting. || 這個故事很有趣。 || interesting book=有趣的書 || This book is ___ interesting than that one. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 這本書比那本有趣。
Internet || n. || 網際網路 || You can find information on the Internet. || 你可以在網路上找到資訊。 || on the Internet=在網路上; surf the Internet=上網 || You can find a lot of information ___ the Internet. || on|in|at || 在網路上用 on the Internet。 || 你可以在網路上找到很多資訊。
interview || n./v. || 面試；訪問 || I have a job interview tomorrow. || 我明天有一個工作面試。 || job interview=工作面試 || The reporter interviewed the singer ___ the concert. || after|while|during of || after + 名詞 = 在…之後。 || 記者在演唱會之後訪問了那位歌手。
invite || v. || 邀請 || She invited me to her party. || 她邀請我參加她的派對。 || invite sb to=邀請某人參加… || Thank you for inviting me ___ your birthday party. || to|at|for || invite sb to + 活動 = 邀請某人參加…。 || 謝謝你邀請我參加你的生日派對。
island || n. || 島 || Taiwan is a beautiful island. || 台灣是一座美麗的島嶼。 || on the island=在島上 || Taiwan is ___ island. || an|a|the || island 的 s 不發音，以母音開頭，用 an。 || 台灣是一座島嶼。
item || n. || 項目；物品 || How many items are on the list? || 清單上有幾項東西？ || an item on the list=清單上的一項 || There ___ ten items on the shopping list. || are|is|be || ten items 複數，There are。 || 購物清單上有十項東西。
jog || v. || 慢跑 || My dad jogs every morning. || 我爸爸每天早上慢跑。 || go jogging=去慢跑 || My dad ___ jogging every morning. || goes|go|going || My dad 第三人稱單數，go 加 es。 || 我爸爸每天早上去慢跑。
join || v. || 加入；參加 || Do you want to join us? || 你想加入我們嗎？ || join a club=參加社團 || Would you like to join our club? It ___ a lot of fun. || is|are|am || It 單數，用 is。 || 你想加入我們的社團嗎？很好玩喔。
joy || n. || 喜悅 || She cried for joy. || 她喜極而泣。 || for joy=因為高興; jump for joy=高興得跳起來 || The kids jumped ___ joy. || for|with|in || jump for joy = 高興得跳起來。 || 孩子們高興得跳起來。
junior high school || n. || 國中 || I'm a junior high school student. || 我是國中生。 || junior high school student=國中生 || There ___ over 1,000 students in our junior high school. || are|is|be || over 1,000 students 複數，There are。 || 我們國中有一千多名學生。
kill || v. || 殺死 || The fire killed many trees. || 那場火燒死了很多樹。 || kill time=消磨時間 || Many trees ___ killed in the fire. || were|was|are || 過去的被動語態；Many trees 複數用 were。 || 很多樹在那場火災中被燒死。
kilogram || n. || 公斤 || I weigh forty kilograms. || 我體重四十公斤。 || two kilograms=兩公斤 || This bag of rice ___ five kilograms. || weighs|weigh|weighing || 主詞 This bag 單數，動詞加 s。 || 這袋米重五公斤。
knock || v. || 敲 || Someone is knocking on the door. || 有人在敲門。 || knock on the door=敲門 || Please knock ___ you come in. || before|after|until || 進來「之前」敲門，用 before。 || 進來之前請敲門。
knowledge || n. || 知識 || Books give us knowledge. || 書給我們知識。 || knowledge of=…的知識 || Books give ___ knowledge about the world. || us|we|our || give 後面的人用受格 us。 || 書給我們關於世界的知識。
land || n./v. || 陸地；降落 || The plane landed safely. || 飛機安全降落了。 || on land=在陸地上 || The plane will land ___ ten minutes. || in|on|at || in + 一段時間 = 在…之後（未來）。 || 飛機將在十分鐘後降落。
language || n. || 語言 || How many languages can you speak? || 你會說幾種語言？ || foreign language=外語; body language=肢體語言 || How ___ languages can you speak? || many|much|long || languages 可數複數，用 How many。 || 你會說幾種語言？
lawyer || n. || 律師 || My aunt is a lawyer. || 我阿姨是律師。 || become a lawyer=成為律師 || She wants to be a lawyer ___ she grows up. || when|what|where || when 引導時間子句「當她長大時」。 || 她長大後想當律師。
lead || v. || 帶領 || He will lead the team. || 他將帶領這支隊伍。 || lead to=通往、導致; lead sb to=帶某人到… || This road leads ___ the station. || to|at|for || lead to = 通往…。 || 這條路通往車站。
leader || n. || 領導者 || She is our class leader. || 她是我們的班長。 || class leader=班長; team leader=隊長 || Who is the leader ___ your team? || of|for|to || the leader of = …的領導者。 || 你們隊的隊長是誰？
least || adj./adv. || 最少的 || At least eat some bread. || 至少吃點麵包。 || at least=至少 || You should drink at least eight glasses of water ___ day. || a|an|the || 每天用 a day。 || 你一天應該至少喝八杯水。
lend || v. || 借出 || Can you lend me your pen? || 你可以借我你的筆嗎？ || lend sb sth=借某物給某人 || Can you lend ___ some money? || me|I|my || lend sb sth：lend 後面的人用受格 me。 || 你可以借我一些錢嗎？
lesson || n. || 課 || We have a piano lesson on Friday. || 我們星期五有鋼琴課。 || piano lesson=鋼琴課; learn a lesson=學到教訓 || I have a piano lesson ___ Friday evening. || on|in|at || 特定某天的晚上用 on。 || 我星期五晚上有鋼琴課。
let || v. || 讓 || Let me help you. || 讓我幫你。 || let sb V=讓某人做…; let's=讓我們 || Let me ___ you. || help|to help|helping || let + 受詞 + 原形動詞。 || 讓我幫你。
letter || n. || 信；字母 || I wrote a letter to my friend. || 我寫了一封信給朋友。 || write a letter=寫信 || I wrote a letter ___ my pen pal last night. || to|for|at || write a letter to sb = 寫信給某人。 || 我昨晚寫了一封信給我的筆友。
level || n. || 程度；層級 || What level are you in? || 你在哪個級別？ || high level=高水準; level up=升級 || This book is at an easy level, ___ it's good for beginners. || so|but|because || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這本書程度簡單，所以適合初學者。
lie || v./n. || 說謊；躺；謊言 || Don't tell a lie. || 不要說謊。 || tell a lie=說謊; lie down=躺下 || Don't lie ___ your parents. || to|at|for || lie to sb = 對某人說謊。 || 不要對你父母說謊。
living room || n. || 客廳 || We watch TV in the living room. || 我們在客廳看電視。 || in the living room=在客廳 || My family ___ watching TV in the living room now. || is|are|am || 現在進行式；My family 視為一個整體用 is。 || 我家人現在正在客廳看電視。
lonely || adj. || 寂寞的 || He felt lonely in the new city. || 他在新城市感到寂寞。 || feel lonely=覺得寂寞 || He felt lonely ___ he moved to a new city. || after|during|until || after + 子句 = 在…之後。 || 他搬到新城市後感到很寂寞。
lovely || adj. || 可愛的；美好的 || What a lovely day! || 多美好的一天！ || lovely day=美好的一天 || What ___ lovely day it is! || a|an|the || 感嘆句：What + a + 形容詞 + 名詞 + 主詞 + 動詞！ || 今天天氣多好啊！
machine || n. || 機器 || This machine can make coffee. || 這台機器可以煮咖啡。 || washing machine=洗衣機; vending machine=販賣機 || This machine ___ made in Japan. || was|is being|were || 過去製造的被動語態 was + p.p.。 || 這台機器是日本製的。
magic || n./adj. || 魔術；魔法 || He can do magic tricks. || 他會變魔術。 || magic trick=魔術把戲; magic show=魔術表演 || He showed us a magic trick. It was ___ amazing! || so|such|much || so + 形容詞 = 好…。 || 他表演了一個魔術給我們看，好神奇！
mailman || n. || 郵差 || The mailman comes at ten every day. || 郵差每天十點來。 || mail carrier=郵差 || The mailman ___ here at ten every morning. || comes|come|coming || The mailman 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 郵差每天早上十點來這裡。
main || adj. || 主要的 || What is the main idea of this story? || 這個故事的主旨是什麼？ || main idea=主旨; main road=主要道路 || What ___ the main idea of the article? || is|are|does || the main idea 單數，用 is。 || 這篇文章的主旨是什麼？
mark || n./v. || 記號；分數；標示 || Put a mark next to the right answer. || 在正確答案旁邊做記號。 || question mark=問號; full marks=滿分 || She got full marks ___ the math test. || on|at|of || 在考試上得到的分數用 on the test。 || 她數學考試拿了滿分。
marker || n. || 麥克筆 || Write your name with a marker. || 用麥克筆寫你的名字。 || red marker=紅色麥克筆 || Can I borrow your marker? Mine ___ run out of ink. || has|have|is || 現在完成式；Mine（= my marker）單數用 has。 || 我可以借你的麥克筆嗎？我的沒水了。
market || n. || 市場 || My mom buys vegetables at the market. || 我媽媽在市場買菜。 || night market=夜市; go to the market=去市場 || Let's go to the night market ___ dinner tonight. || for|to|at || go to… for dinner = 去…吃晚餐。 || 我們今晚去夜市吃晚餐吧。
