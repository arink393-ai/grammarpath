# 八年級 08：pear – rat
pear || n. || 梨子 || This pear is sweet. || 這顆梨子很甜。 || Asian pear=水梨 || This pear is ___ than that apple. || sweeter|sweet|sweetest || 兩者比較用比較級 sweeter。 || 這顆梨子比那顆蘋果甜。
perhaps || adv. || 也許 || Perhaps it will rain tomorrow. || 明天也許會下雨。 || perhaps not=也許不會 || Perhaps he ___ busy now. || is|be|are || perhaps 是副詞，不影響動詞；主詞 he 用 is。 || 他現在也許很忙。
person || n. || 人 || She is a kind person. || 她是個好人。 || in person=親自; people=人（複數） || He is the kindest person I ___ ever met. || have|has|am || 現在完成式；主詞 I 用 have。 || 他是我見過最親切的人。
picnic || n. || 野餐 || Let's have a picnic in the park. || 我們在公園野餐吧。 || have a picnic=野餐; go on a picnic=去野餐 || Let's go ___ a picnic this weekend. || on|to|for || go on a picnic = 去野餐。 || 我們這週末去野餐吧。
piece || n. || 一片；一塊 || Can I have a piece of cake? || 我可以吃一塊蛋糕嗎？ || a piece of=一片/塊…; piece by piece=一片片地 || Can I have two pieces ___ paper? || of|for|in || a piece of = 一片…。 || 可以給我兩張紙嗎？
pin || n. || 大頭針；別針 || She wears a pin on her coat. || 她在外套上別了一個胸針。 || safety pin=安全別針 || Be careful. There is a pin ___ the floor. || on|in|at || 在地板上用 on。 || 小心，地上有一根針。
pipe || n. || 管子；煙斗 || The water pipe is broken. || 水管破了。 || water pipe=水管 || The water pipe ___ broken, so we have no water. || is|are|am || The water pipe 單數，用 is。 || 水管破了，所以我們沒有水。
planet || n. || 行星 || Earth is the planet we live on. || 地球是我們居住的行星。 || the planet Earth=地球 || Earth is the planet ___ we live on. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（the planet）。 || 地球是我們居住的行星。
player || n. || 選手 || He is a good basketball player. || 他是個好籃球選手。 || basketball player=籃球選手; CD player=CD 播放器 || He is a good basketball player. He plays ___ than me. || better|good|best || 兩者比較用比較級 better（good/well 的比較級）。 || 他是個好籃球選手，他打得比我好。
pleasure || n. || 樂趣；榮幸 || It's my pleasure. || 這是我的榮幸。 || my pleasure=不客氣; with pleasure=樂意 || Thank you for your help. It's ___ pleasure. || my|me|mine || It's my pleasure. = 不客氣、我的榮幸。 || 謝謝你的幫忙。不客氣。
pocket || n. || 口袋 || He put his hands in his pockets. || 他把手放進口袋裡。 || pocket money=零用錢 || He put the key ___ his pocket. || in|on|at || 放進口袋裡用 in。 || 他把鑰匙放進口袋。
point || v./n. || 指；重點 || Don't point at people. || 不要用手指著別人。 || point at=指著; the point is=重點是 || It's not polite to point ___ people. || at|to|on || point at = 指著…。 || 用手指著別人是不禮貌的。
police || n. || 警察 || Call the police! || 快報警！ || call the police=報警; police station=警察局 || The police ___ looking for the thief. || are|is|does || police 視為複數，用 are。 || 警察正在找那個小偷。
polite || adj. || 有禮貌的 || She is always polite to others. || 她對別人總是很有禮貌。 || be polite to=對…有禮貌 || It's polite ___ say "thank you." || to|for|of || It's + 形容詞 + to V。 || 說「謝謝」是有禮貌的。
pond || n. || 池塘 || There are fish in the pond. || 池塘裡有魚。 || fish pond=魚池 || Some ducks ___ swimming in the pond. || are|is|does || 現在進行式；Some ducks 複數用 are。 || 有一些鴨子正在池塘裡游泳。
pop || adj./n. || 流行的；流行音樂 || I like K-pop music. || 我喜歡韓國流行音樂。 || pop music=流行音樂; pop star=流行歌星 || She is a pop singer ___ Korea. || from|of|to || from + 地方 = 來自…。 || 她是來自韓國的流行歌手。
popcorn || n. || 爆米花 || Let's buy popcorn at the movies. || 我們在電影院買爆米花吧。 || a bag of popcorn=一包爆米花 || I'd like a bag ___ popcorn. || of|for|with || a bag of = 一包…。 || 我要一包爆米花。
popular || adj. || 受歡迎的 || This song is very popular. || 這首歌很受歡迎。 || popular with=受…歡迎 || This singer is popular ___ teenagers. || with|to|for || be popular with = 受…歡迎。 || 這位歌手很受青少年歡迎。
pork || n. || 豬肉 || I don't eat pork. || 我不吃豬肉。 || braised pork rice=滷肉飯 || Braised pork rice is ___ popular dish in Taiwan. || a|an|the most || popular 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 滷肉飯在台灣是一道受歡迎的料理。
possible || adj. || 可能的 || Is it possible to finish today? || 今天有可能完成嗎？ || as soon as possible=儘快; if possible=如果可能的話 || Please call me back as soon ___ possible. || as|so|than || as soon as possible = 儘快。 || 請儘快回電給我。
post office || n. || 郵局 || I went to the post office to send a letter. || 我去郵局寄信。 || go to the post office=去郵局 || I went to the post office ___ send a package. || to|for|at || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我去郵局寄包裹。
postcard || n. || 明信片 || I sent my friend a postcard from Japan. || 我從日本寄明信片給朋友。 || send a postcard=寄明信片 || I sent ___ a postcard from Japan. || her|she|hers || sent 後面的人用受格 her。 || 我從日本寄了一張明信片給她。
pot || n. || 鍋子；壺 || Mom is cooking soup in a pot. || 媽媽用鍋子煮湯。 || hot pot=火鍋; teapot=茶壺 || Let's have hot pot ___ it's cold today. || because|so|but || 表示原因用 because。 || 我們吃火鍋吧，因為今天很冷。
pound || n. || 磅；英鎊 || This fish weighs two pounds. || 這條魚重兩磅。 || two pounds=兩磅 || This fish ___ two pounds. || weighs|weigh|weight || This fish 單數，動詞 weigh 加 s。 || 這條魚重兩磅。
power || n. || 力量；電力 || Knowledge is power. || 知識就是力量。 || power off=斷電; power bank=行動電源 || Turn off the power ___ you fix the lamp. || before|after|until || 修燈「之前」先關電，用 before。 || 修燈之前先關掉電源。
practice || v./n. || 練習 || Practice makes perfect. || 熟能生巧。 || practice + V-ing=練習做… || I practice ___ the piano every day. || playing|to play|play || practice 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我每天練習彈鋼琴。
pray || v. || 祈禱 || People pray at the temple. || 人們在廟裡祈禱。 || pray for=為…祈禱 || We pray ___ peace. || for|to|at || pray for = 為…祈禱。 || 我們為和平祈禱。
prepare || v. || 準備 || She is preparing for the exam. || 她正在準備考試。 || prepare for=為…做準備 || She is preparing ___ the final exam. || for|to|of || prepare for + 名詞 = 為…做準備。 || 她正在為期末考做準備。
present || n. || 禮物；現在 || This present is for you. || 這份禮物是給你的。 || birthday present=生日禮物; at present=目前 || My aunt gave ___ a present for my birthday. || me|I|my || gave 後面的人用受格 me。 || 我阿姨送我一份生日禮物。
pretty || adj./adv. || 漂亮的；相當 || She is a pretty girl. || 她是個漂亮的女孩。 || pretty good=相當好 || The test was pretty easy, ___ everyone passed. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 考試相當簡單，所以大家都及格了。
prize || n. || 獎品；獎項 || She won first prize. || 她得了第一名。 || win a prize=得獎; first prize=首獎 || She won first prize ___ the speech contest. || in|on|at of || win a prize in + 比賽 = 在…中得獎。 || 她在演講比賽中得到第一名。
probably || adv. || 大概 || It will probably rain tomorrow. || 明天大概會下雨。 || most probably=很可能 || He will probably ___ late. || be|is|being || will 後面接原形動詞 be。 || 他大概會遲到。
problem || n. || 問題 || No problem! || 沒問題！ || no problem=沒問題; solve a problem=解決問題 || Can you help me with this math problem? ___ problem! || No|Not|Nothing || No problem! = 沒問題！ || 你可以幫我解這道數學題嗎？沒問題！
program || n. || 節目；程式 || My favorite TV program is on now. || 我最喜歡的電視節目正在播。 || TV program=電視節目 || What's your favorite TV program? I like ___ cooking shows. || watching|to watching|watch || like 後面可接動名詞 V-ing。 || 你最喜歡的電視節目是什麼？我喜歡看烹飪節目。
proud || adj. || 驕傲的；自豪的 || I'm proud of you. || 我以你為榮。 || be proud of=以…為榮 || My parents are proud ___ me. || of|for|at || be proud of = 以…為榮。 || 我的父母以我為榮。
public || adj. || 公共的 || Don't make noise in public places. || 在公共場所不要製造噪音。 || in public=公開地; public place=公共場所 || You shouldn't smoke ___ public places. || in|on|at || 在…場所裡用 in。 || 你不應該在公共場所抽菸。
pumpkin || n. || 南瓜 || We make pumpkin pie at Halloween. || 我們在萬聖節做南瓜派。 || pumpkin pie=南瓜派 || This pumpkin is the ___ one on the farm. || biggest|bigger|big || 三者以上比較用最高級 the biggest。 || 這顆南瓜是農場裡最大的。
puppy || n. || 小狗 || The puppy is so cute. || 那隻小狗好可愛。 || cute puppy=可愛的小狗 || The puppy is chasing ___ tail. || its|it's|it || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 那隻小狗在追自己的尾巴。
purple || adj./n. || 紫色（的） || She likes purple. || 她喜歡紫色。 || purple dress=紫色洋裝 || Her favorite color is purple, ___ she has many purple clothes. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 她最喜歡的顏色是紫色，所以她有很多紫色的衣服。
quarter || n. || 四分之一；十五分鐘 || It's a quarter past six. || 現在六點十五分。 || a quarter past=…點十五分 || I'll meet you ___ a quarter to eight. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 我七點四十五分跟你碰面。
question || n. || 問題 || May I ask you a question? || 我可以問你一個問題嗎？ || ask a question=問問題; answer a question=回答問題 || May I ask ___ a question? || you|your|yours || ask 後面的人用受格 you。 || 我可以問你一個問題嗎？
quite || adv. || 相當 || The movie was quite good. || 那部電影相當好看。 || quite a few=相當多 || The test was quite difficult, ___ I passed it. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 考試相當難，但我通過了。
quiz || n. || 小考 || We have a quiz every Friday. || 我們每週五有小考。 || pop quiz=突擊小考 || We ___ a math quiz tomorrow. || will have|had|have had || tomorrow 是未來，用 will + 原形動詞。 || 我們明天有數學小考。
rainbow || n. || 彩虹 || Look! There's a rainbow in the sky. || 你看！天空有一道彩虹。 || see a rainbow=看見彩虹 || A rainbow ___ seven colors. || has|have|is || A rainbow 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 彩虹有七種顏色。
raise || v. || 舉起；飼養；募集 || Raise your hand if you have a question. || 有問題就舉手。 || raise one's hand=舉手; raise money=募款 || Raise your hand ___ you want to answer. || if|so|but || if 引導條件子句「如果…」。 || 如果你想回答就舉手。
rat || n. || 老鼠 || There is a rat in the kitchen! || 廚房裡有一隻老鼠！ || rat=大老鼠 || The cat ran after the rat, but it ___ get it. || didn't|doesn't|wasn't || 描述過去的否定用 didn't + 原形動詞。 || 貓追那隻老鼠，但沒抓到。
