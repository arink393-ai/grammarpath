# 學測 L5 13：partly – premium
partly || adv. || 部分地 || The accident was partly my fault. || 這場意外有部分是我的錯。 || partly because=部分是因為 || I was late, partly ___ the traffic was heavy. || because|because of|although || partly because + 子句。 || 我遲到了，部分是因為交通擁擠。
passionate || adj. || 熱情的 || She is passionate about music. || 她熱愛音樂。 || be passionate about=熱衷於… || He is passionate ___ protecting the environment. || about|to|for || be passionate about = 熱衷於…。 || 他熱衷於保護環境。
pastry || n. || 酥皮點心 || This bakery sells delicious pastries. || 這家麵包店賣美味的酥皮點心。 || French pastry=法式點心 || This bakery is famous ___ its French pastry. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 這家麵包店以法式點心聞名。
patch || n. || 補丁；一小塊 || His jeans have a patch on the knee. || 他的牛仔褲膝蓋上有補丁。 || a patch of=一小塊… || There was a patch ___ ice on the road. || of|for|in || a patch of = 一小塊…。 || 路上有一小塊結冰。
patent || n. || 專利 || He has a patent on his invention. || 他的發明有專利。 || apply for a patent=申請專利 || The inventor applied ___ a patent for his new machine. || for|to|with || apply for = 申請…。 || 那位發明家為他的新機器申請專利。
pathetic || adj. || 可悲的；差勁的 || That excuse is pathetic. || 那個藉口真可悲。 || pathetic excuse=差勁的藉口 || His excuse was ___ pathetic that nobody believed him. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他的藉口差勁到沒人相信他。
patrol || v./n. || 巡邏 || Police patrol the streets at night. || 警察晚上在街上巡邏。 || on patrol=巡邏中 || Two police officers were ___ patrol near the school. || on|in|at || on patrol = 巡邏中。 || 兩名警察在學校附近巡邏。
patron || n. || 贊助者；顧客 || He is a patron of the arts. || 他是藝術贊助者。 || regular patron=常客 || The library thanked the patron ___ donated 1,000 books. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 圖書館感謝那位捐贈一千本書的贊助者。
peasant || n. || 農民 || The peasants worked in the fields. || 農民在田裡工作。 || peasant farmer=小農 || In the past, most peasants ___ very poor. || were|was|are || 過去的事實；peasants 複數用 were。 || 過去大多數農民都很窮。
pedal || n./v. || 踏板；踩踏板 || Push the pedal to start the bike. || 踩踏板讓腳踏車前進。 || gas pedal=油門 || He pedaled as fast ___ he could. || as|than|so || as…as one can = 盡可能地…。 || 他盡全力踩著踏板。
pedestrian || n. || 行人 || Pedestrians should use the crosswalk. || 行人應該走斑馬線。 || pedestrian crossing=行人穿越道 || Drivers must stop ___ pedestrians are crossing the road. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 行人過馬路時，駕駛必須停車。
penetrate || v. || 穿透 || The sunlight couldn't penetrate the thick forest. || 陽光無法穿透茂密的森林。 || penetrate the market=打入市場 || The light was too weak ___ penetrate the thick fog. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + to V。 || 光線太微弱，無法穿透濃霧。
pension || n. || 退休金 || My grandfather lives on a pension. || 我祖父靠退休金生活。 || live on a pension=靠退休金生活 || My grandparents live ___ a small pension. || on|in|with || live on = 靠…生活。 || 我祖父母靠微薄的退休金生活。
perceive || v. || 察覺；認為 || She perceived a change in his attitude. || 她察覺到他態度的改變。 || be perceived as=被視為… || He is perceived ___ a strong leader. || as|like|for || be perceived as = 被視為…。 || 他被視為一位強而有力的領導者。
perception || n. || 認知；看法 || Our perception of beauty has changed. || 我們對美的認知已經改變。 || public perception=大眾觀感 || Social media can change our perception ___ beauty. || of|for|to || perception of = 對…的認知。 || 社群媒體可能改變我們對美的認知。
performer || n. || 表演者 || The performer sang beautifully. || 那位表演者唱得很好聽。 || street performer=街頭藝人 || The street performer ___ we saw yesterday was amazing. || whom|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當 saw 的受詞，用 whom。 || 我們昨天看到的街頭藝人很厲害。
persist || v. || 堅持；持續 || The rain persisted all week. || 雨持續了一整週。 || persist in=堅持… || He persisted ___ asking the same question. || in|on|at || persist in + V-ing = 堅持做…。 || 他堅持問同一個問題。
personnel || n. || 人員；人事 || All personnel must wear ID cards. || 所有人員都必須配戴識別證。 || personnel department=人事部 || Only authorized personnel ___ allowed to enter. || are|is|be || personnel 指人員時視為複數，用 are。 || 只有授權人員才能進入。
perspective || n. || 觀點 || Try to see things from a different perspective. || 試著從不同觀點看事情。 || from a perspective=從…的角度 || Try to look at the problem ___ a different perspective. || from|of|by || from a perspective = 從…的觀點。 || 試著從不同的觀點看這個問題。
pessimism || n. || 悲觀 || His pessimism annoys me. || 他的悲觀讓我很煩。 || deep pessimism=深深的悲觀 || There is a lot of pessimism ___ the future of the economy. || about|to|for || pessimism about = 對…的悲觀。 || 大家對經濟前景很悲觀。
petition || n. || 請願書 || We signed a petition to save the park. || 我們連署請願拯救公園。 || sign a petition=連署請願 || More than 10,000 people signed the petition ___ the new law. || against|to|with || against = 反對…。 || 超過一萬人連署反對新法。
petty || adj. || 瑣碎的；小氣的 || Don't argue about petty things. || 不要為小事爭吵。 || petty crime=輕罪 || Don't waste time arguing ___ petty things. || about|to|with || argue about = 為…爭吵。 || 不要浪費時間為小事爭吵。
phase || n. || 階段 || The project is in its final phase. || 這個計畫正處於最後階段。 || final phase=最後階段 || The project is now ___ its final phase. || in|on|at || in a phase = 在…階段。 || 這個計畫現在正處於最後階段。
photographic || adj. || 攝影的 || She has a photographic memory. || 她有過目不忘的記憶力。 || photographic memory=過目不忘 || With a photographic memory, she can remember everything ___ she reads. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾 everything。 || 她有過目不忘的記憶力，讀過的東西都記得。
pickup || n. || 小貨車；接送 || He drives a pickup truck. || 他開一輛小貨車。 || pickup truck=小貨車 || The pickup time ___ the airport is 3 p.m. || at|in|on || at + 地點（點）。 || 機場的接送時間是下午三點。
pier || n. || 碼頭 || We walked along the pier. || 我們沿著碼頭散步。 || fishing pier=釣魚碼頭 || We walked ___ the pier and watched the sunset. || along|among|between || along = 沿著…。 || 我們沿著碼頭散步，看著夕陽。
pillar || n. || 柱子；支柱 || The temple has red pillars. || 這座廟有紅色的柱子。 || pillar of society=社會中堅 || She is a pillar ___ the community. || of|for|in || a pillar of = …的支柱。 || 她是社區的中堅份子。
pipeline || n. || 管線 || The oil pipeline was damaged. || 輸油管受損了。 || in the pipeline=籌備中 || A new project is ___ the pipeline. || in|on|at || in the pipeline = 籌備中。 || 一項新計畫正在籌備中。
pirate || n./v. || 海盜；盜版 || The pirates attacked the ship. || 海盜攻擊了那艘船。 || pirated DVD=盜版 DVD || It is illegal ___ download pirated movies. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 下載盜版電影是違法的。
pitcher || n. || 投手；水罐 || He is the team's best pitcher. || 他是隊上最好的投手。 || starting pitcher=先發投手 || The pitcher threw the ball ___ fast that no one could hit it. || so|such|too || so + 副詞 + that…。 || 投手球投得快到沒人打得到。
placement || n. || 安置；分班 || We took a placement test. || 我們參加了分班考試。 || placement test=分班考試 || All new students must take a placement test ___ classes begin. || before|after|since || 開學「之前」考試，用 before。 || 所有新生在開學前都必須參加分班考試。
plea || n. || 懇求 || She made a plea for help. || 她懇求幫助。 || make a plea=提出懇求 || The family made a plea ___ help on TV. || for|of|to || a plea for = 懇求…。 || 那家人在電視上懇求幫助。
plead || v. || 懇求；辯護 || He pleaded with his father for more money. || 他懇求父親給他更多錢。 || plead guilty=認罪 || The man decided to plead guilty ___ the crime. || to|for|with || plead guilty to = 承認犯了…罪。 || 那名男子決定認罪。
pledge || n./v. || 保證；誓言 || They pledged to protect the forest. || 他們發誓保護森林。 || make a pledge=立誓 || The mayor pledged ___ build more parks. || to|for|of || pledge to + 原形動詞 = 承諾做…。 || 市長承諾興建更多公園。
plunge || v./n. || 驟降；跳入 || Stock prices plunged. || 股價暴跌。 || plunge into=投入… || He plunged ___ the cold water. || into|onto|from || plunge into = 跳入…。 || 他跳進了冰冷的水中。
plural || adj./n. || 複數 || "Children" is the plural of "child." || 「children」是「child」的複數。 || plural form=複數形 || "Mice" is the plural form ___ "mouse." || of|for|to || the plural of = …的複數。 || 「mice」是「mouse」的複數形。
poetic || adj. || 詩意的 || The movie is very poetic. || 這部電影很有詩意。 || poetic language=詩意的語言 || Her writing is ___ poetic that it feels like music. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 她的文字如此有詩意，讀起來像音樂。
poke || v./n. || 戳 || Don't poke me! || 別戳我！ || poke fun at=取笑… || It's rude to poke fun ___ other people. || at|on|to || poke fun at = 取笑…。 || 取笑別人是很沒禮貌的。
porch || n. || 門廊 || We sat on the porch. || 我們坐在門廊上。 || front porch=前廊 || Grandpa likes to sit ___ the porch in the evening. || on|in|at || 坐在門廊上用 on。 || 爺爺喜歡傍晚坐在門廊上。
portfolio || n. || 作品集 || She showed her portfolio at the interview. || 她在面試時展示了作品集。 || art portfolio=藝術作品集 || You need to prepare a portfolio ___ apply to art school. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 你需要準備作品集才能申請藝術學校。
practitioner || n. || 從業人員 || She is a medical practitioner. || 她是一名醫療從業人員。 || general practitioner=家庭醫生 || You should see a general practitioner ___ you feel sick. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你覺得不舒服，應該去看家庭醫生。
precaution || n. || 預防措施 || Take precautions against fire. || 要採取防火措施。 || take precautions=採取預防措施 || We should take precautions ___ the typhoon. || against|to|with || precautions against = 防範…的措施。 || 我們應該採取措施防範颱風。
predator || n. || 掠食者 || Lions are predators. || 獅子是掠食者。 || natural predator=天敵 || Small animals hide to protect themselves ___ predators. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 小動物躲起來以保護自己免受掠食者攻擊。
preference || n. || 偏好 || Do you have a preference? || 你有偏好嗎？ || have a preference for=偏好… || I have a preference ___ tea over coffee. || for|to|of || a preference for A over B = 偏好 A 勝過 B。 || 比起咖啡我更喜歡茶。
prejudice || n. || 偏見 || We should fight against prejudice. || 我們應該對抗偏見。 || prejudice against=對…的偏見 || There is still prejudice ___ people with disabilities. || against|to|with || prejudice against = 對…的偏見。 || 社會上仍有對身心障礙者的偏見。
preliminary || adj. || 初步的 || The preliminary results look good. || 初步結果看起來不錯。 || preliminary round=初賽 || Our team passed the preliminary round ___ ease. || with|by|in || with ease = 輕鬆地。 || 我們隊輕鬆通過了初賽。
premature || adj. || 過早的 || The baby was born premature. || 寶寶早產了。 || premature baby=早產兒 || It is premature ___ say who will win. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 現在說誰會贏還太早。
premier || n./adj. || 首相；首要的 || The premier gave a speech. || 首相發表了演說。 || premier league=超級聯賽 || The premier, ___ was elected last year, gave a speech. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 去年當選的首相發表了演說。
premise || n. || 前提 || The premise of the story is simple. || 這個故事的前提很簡單。 || basic premise=基本前提 || The movie is based ___ a simple premise. || on|in|at || be based on = 以…為基礎。 || 這部電影建立在一個簡單的前提上。
premium || n./adj. || 保險費；優質的 || We sell premium tea. || 我們販售高級茶葉。 || premium quality=優質 || These premium tea leaves cost more ___ ordinary ones. || than|then|as || 比較級 more + than。 || 這些優質茶葉比一般的貴。
