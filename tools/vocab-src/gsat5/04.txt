# 學測 L5 04：collector – confrontation
collector || n. || 收藏家 || He is a stamp collector. || 他是集郵家。 || art collector=藝術收藏家 || The painting was bought by a collector ___ lives in Paris. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 這幅畫被一位住在巴黎的收藏家買走了。
colonial || adj. || 殖民的 || The city has many colonial buildings. || 這座城市有許多殖民時期的建築。 || colonial period=殖民時期 || Tainan has many buildings from the colonial period, ___ attract tourists. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，只能用 which。 || 台南有許多殖民時期的建築，吸引了觀光客。
columnist || n. || 專欄作家 || She is a columnist for a newspaper. || 她是報紙專欄作家。 || newspaper columnist=報紙專欄作家 || She has worked as a columnist ___ more than ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她擔任專欄作家已經超過十年。
combat || n./v. || 戰鬥；對抗 || We must combat climate change. || 我們必須對抗氣候變遷。 || combat crime=打擊犯罪 || The government is taking action ___ combat crime. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 政府正在採取行動打擊犯罪。
comedian || n. || 喜劇演員 || The comedian made everyone laugh. || 那位喜劇演員逗得大家哈哈大笑。 || stand-up comedian=單口喜劇演員 || The comedian made the audience ___ for two hours. || laugh|to laugh|laughing at || 使役動詞 make + 受詞 + 原形動詞。 || 那位喜劇演員讓觀眾笑了兩個小時。
commentary || n. || 評論；實況報導 || He gave a live commentary on the game. || 他為比賽做實況轉播。 || live commentary=實況轉播 || I listened to the commentary ___ the game on the radio. || on|to|at || commentary on = 對…的評論／轉播。 || 我在收音機上聽比賽的實況轉播。
commentator || n. || 評論員；播報員 || He is a sports commentator. || 他是體育主播。 || sports commentator=體育主播 || The commentator, ___ voice was very loud, got excited. || whose|who|which || whose + 名詞 表示「…的」。 || 那位聲音很大的播報員變得很激動。
commission || n. || 委員會；佣金 || The salesman gets a commission on each sale. || 業務員每賣出一筆都有佣金。 || on commission=按佣金計酬 || The salesman earns a commission ___ every car he sells. || on|in|at || a commission on = 對…的佣金。 || 這位業務員每賣出一輛車就賺取佣金。
commitment || n. || 承諾；投入 || She has a strong commitment to her work. || 她非常投入工作。 || make a commitment=做出承諾 || His commitment ___ the team is impressive. || to|for|with || commitment to = 對…的投入。 || 他對團隊的投入令人佩服。
commodity || n. || 商品；日用品 || Rice is an important commodity. || 米是重要的商品。 || basic commodity=基本商品 || The price of basic commodities ___ risen this year. || has|have|is || 主詞是 The price（單數），用 has。 || 基本商品的價格今年上漲了。
communism || n. || 共產主義 || Communism failed in many countries. || 共產主義在許多國家失敗了。 || fall of communism=共產主義的瓦解 || Communism was a system ___ the government controlled everything. || in which|which|what || in which = where，引導形容詞子句。 || 共產主義是一種由政府掌控一切的制度。
communist || n./adj. || 共產主義者；共產主義的 || China is ruled by the Communist Party. || 中國由共產黨統治。 || Communist Party=共產黨 || The country ___ ruled by a communist government for decades. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那個國家曾由共產政府統治數十年。
commute || v./n. || 通勤 || I commute to work by MRT. || 我搭捷運通勤上班。 || daily commute=每天通勤 || My father commutes to Taipei ___ train every day. || by|on|with || by + 交通工具（無冠詞）。 || 我爸爸每天搭火車通勤到台北。
commuter || n. || 通勤者 || The train was full of commuters. || 火車上擠滿了通勤族。 || commuter train=通勤列車 || The train was full ___ commuters this morning. || of|with|in || be full of = 充滿…。 || 今天早上火車上擠滿了通勤族。
compact || adj. || 小巧的；緊密的 || This camera is very compact. || 這台相機很小巧。 || compact car=小型車 || This camera is compact enough ___ fit in your pocket. || to|for|that || enough to + 原形動詞 = 足以…。 || 這台相機小到可以放進口袋。
comparable || adj. || 可比較的；相當的 || The two cars are comparable in price. || 這兩輛車價格差不多。 || be comparable to=與…相當 || His salary is comparable ___ mine. || to|at|for || be comparable to = 與…相當。 || 他的薪水和我的差不多。
compassion || n. || 同情；憐憫 || She showed compassion for the poor. || 她對窮人表現出同情心。 || compassion for=對…的同情 || We should show compassion ___ people in need. || for|of|with || compassion for = 對…的同情。 || 我們應該對需要幫助的人展現同情心。
compassionate || adj. || 有同情心的 || She is a compassionate nurse. || 她是一位有愛心的護理師。 || compassionate person=有同情心的人 || She is the most compassionate person ___ I have ever met. || that|which|what || 最高級後面的關係代名詞用 that。 || 她是我見過最有同情心的人。
compatible || adj. || 相容的 || This app is compatible with iPhones. || 這個應用程式與 iPhone 相容。 || be compatible with=與…相容 || Is this software compatible ___ my computer? || with|to|for || be compatible with = 與…相容。 || 這個軟體和我的電腦相容嗎？
compel || v. || 強迫 || The rain compelled us to stay inside. || 這場雨迫使我們待在室內。 || be compelled to V=被迫做… || The bad weather compelled us ___ cancel the trip. || to|for|from || compel sb to + 原形動詞。 || 惡劣的天氣迫使我們取消旅行。
compensate || v. || 補償 || Nothing can compensate for the loss. || 沒有什麼能彌補這個損失。 || compensate for=補償… || The company will compensate you ___ the damage. || for|to|with || compensate sb for sth = 為…補償某人。 || 公司會賠償你的損失。
compensation || n. || 賠償 || He received compensation for his injury. || 他因受傷獲得賠償。 || receive compensation=獲得賠償 || The workers asked ___ compensation after the accident. || for|to|of || ask for = 要求…。 || 意外發生後，工人們要求賠償。
competence || n. || 能力；勝任 || She showed great competence in her job. || 她在工作上表現出很強的能力。 || language competence=語言能力 || Nobody doubts her competence ___ a teacher. || as|for|like || as + 身分 = 作為…。 || 沒有人懷疑她作為老師的能力。
competent || adj. || 能幹的 || He is a competent driver. || 他是一位能幹的駕駛。 || be competent at=擅長… || She is competent ___ handling difficult customers. || at|to|on || be competent at = 能勝任…。 || 她很能處理難纏的客人。
complexity || n. || 複雜性 || The complexity of the problem surprised us. || 問題的複雜性讓我們很驚訝。 || the complexity of=…的複雜性 || We were surprised ___ the complexity of the problem. || by|with|for || be surprised by = 對…感到驚訝。 || 我們對這個問題的複雜性感到驚訝。
compliance || n. || 遵守 || All products must be in compliance with safety rules. || 所有產品都必須符合安全規定。 || in compliance with=遵照… || The factory is in compliance ___ the new safety rules. || with|to|for || in compliance with = 遵照…。 || 這間工廠符合新的安全規定。
complication || n. || 併發症；複雜化 || There were complications after the surgery. || 手術後出現了併發症。 || serious complication=嚴重併發症 || The surgery went well, and there were no complications ___ all. || at|in|of || not…at all = 一點也不。 || 手術很順利，完全沒有併發症。
compliment || n./v. || 稱讚 || She paid me a compliment on my dress. || 她稱讚了我的洋裝。 || pay sb a compliment=稱讚某人 || He complimented her ___ her excellent English. || on|of|at || compliment sb on sth = 稱讚某人某事。 || 他稱讚她英文很好。
comply || v. || 遵守 || All drivers must comply with traffic laws. || 所有駕駛都必須遵守交通法規。 || comply with=遵守… || All visitors must comply ___ the museum's rules. || with|to|for || comply with = 遵守…。 || 所有參觀者都必須遵守博物館的規定。
component || n. || 零件；成分 || The factory makes computer components. || 這間工廠生產電腦零件。 || key component=關鍵要素 || Trust is a key component ___ any friendship. || of|for|with || a component of = …的組成要素。 || 信任是任何友誼的關鍵要素。
compound || n./adj. || 化合物；複合的 || Water is a compound of hydrogen and oxygen. || 水是氫和氧的化合物。 || compound word=複合字 || "Sunflower" is a compound word made ___ two words. || of|by|with || be made of / made up of = 由…組成。 || 「Sunflower」是由兩個字組成的複合字。
comprehend || v. || 理解 || I can't comprehend why he did that. || 我無法理解他為什麼那麼做。 || fail to comprehend=無法理解 || I can't comprehend ___ he quit his job. || why|what|which || why 引導名詞子句，表示原因。 || 我無法理解他為什麼辭職。
comprehension || n. || 理解力 || The test checks reading comprehension. || 這個測驗考閱讀理解。 || reading comprehension=閱讀理解 || Reading comprehension is an important part ___ the English test. || of|for|in || a part of = …的一部分。 || 閱讀理解是英文考試的重要部分。
comprise || v. || 包含；組成 || The team comprises ten players. || 這支隊伍由十名球員組成。 || be comprised of=由…組成 || The book is comprised ___ twelve chapters. || of|by|with || be comprised of = 由…組成。 || 這本書由十二章組成。
compromise || n./v. || 妥協 || They reached a compromise. || 他們達成了妥協。 || reach a compromise=達成妥協 || After a long talk, they finally reached a compromise ___ the price. || on|in|at || a compromise on = 在…上的妥協。 || 經過長談，他們終於在價格上達成妥協。
compulsory || adj. || 義務的；強制的 || English is a compulsory subject. || 英文是必修科目。 || compulsory education=義務教育 || Education is compulsory ___ children in Taiwan. || for|to|of || be compulsory for sb = 對某人是強制的。 || 在台灣，教育對兒童是義務性的。
conceal || v. || 隱藏 || He tried to conceal his feelings. || 他試圖隱藏自己的感受。 || conceal the truth=隱瞞真相 || He tried to conceal the truth ___ his parents. || from|to|with || conceal sth from sb = 對某人隱瞞某事。 || 他試圖對父母隱瞞真相。
concede || v. || 承認；讓步 || He conceded that he was wrong. || 他承認自己錯了。 || concede defeat=認輸 || He finally conceded ___ he had made a mistake. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 conceded 的受詞。 || 他終於承認自己犯了錯。
conceive || v. || 構想；想像 || I can't conceive of a world without music. || 我無法想像沒有音樂的世界。 || conceive of=想像… || It is hard to conceive ___ life without the Internet. || of|about|for || conceive of = 想像…。 || 很難想像沒有網路的生活。
conception || n. || 概念 || He has no conception of how hard it is. || 他完全不知道那有多難。 || have no conception of=對…毫無概念 || He has no conception ___ how hard his parents work. || of|for|to || have no conception of = 對…毫無概念。 || 他完全不知道父母工作有多辛苦。
condemn || v. || 譴責 || Everyone condemned the attack. || 大家都譴責這次攻擊。 || condemn sb for=為…譴責某人 || The attack ___ condemned by leaders around the world. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這次攻擊遭到世界各國領袖的譴責。
conduct || v./n. || 進行；指揮；行為 || The scientists conducted an experiment. || 科學家進行了一項實驗。 || conduct a survey=做調查 || The survey was conducted ___ 500 students. || among|between|along || among + 三者以上 = 在…之中。 || 這項調查是在五百名學生之間進行的。
confession || n. || 坦白；認罪 || He made a full confession. || 他完全認罪了。 || make a confession=坦白 || I have a confession ___ make: I broke your vase. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 修飾名詞 confession。 || 我要坦白一件事：我打破了你的花瓶。
confidential || adj. || 機密的 || This information is confidential. || 這份資料是機密的。 || strictly confidential=極機密 || Please keep this information confidential ___ everyone. || from|to|with || keep sth from sb = 不讓某人知道某事。 || 請對所有人保密這份資料。
confine || v. || 限制；監禁 || He was confined to bed for a week. || 他臥床了一週。 || be confined to=被限制在… || Please confine your speech ___ five minutes. || to|in|with || confine A to B = 把 A 限制在 B 之內。 || 請把你的演講限制在五分鐘以內。
conform || v. || 遵守；順從 || Students must conform to the school rules. || 學生必須遵守校規。 || conform to=遵守… || Not everyone wants ___ conform to society's expectations. || to|for|of || want to + 原形動詞。 || 不是每個人都想順從社會的期待。
confront || v. || 面對；對抗 || She confronted her fears. || 她面對了自己的恐懼。 || be confronted with=面臨… || We were confronted ___ a difficult problem. || with|to|for || be confronted with = 面臨…。 || 我們面臨了一個困難的問題。
confrontation || n. || 對峙；衝突 || There was a confrontation between police and protesters. || 警方和抗議者之間發生了衝突。 || avoid confrontation=避免衝突 || There was a confrontation ___ the police and the protesters. || between|among|with || 兩方之間用 between。 || 警方和抗議民眾之間發生了衝突。
