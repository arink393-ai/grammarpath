# 學測 L5 15：reassure – scrap
reassure || v. || 使安心 || The doctor reassured me that I was fine. || 醫生向我保證我沒事。 || reassure sb that=向某人保證… || The teacher reassured the students ___ the test would be easy. || that|what|which || reassure sb that + 子句。 || 老師向學生保證考試會很簡單。
rebellion || n. || 叛亂；反抗 || The rebellion was put down quickly. || 叛亂很快就被平定了。 || teenage rebellion=青少年的叛逆 || Teenage rebellion is a normal part ___ growing up. || of|for|in || part of = …的一部分。 || 青少年叛逆是成長過程中正常的一部分。
recession || n. || 經濟衰退 || Many people lost jobs during the recession. || 許多人在經濟衰退期間失業。 || economic recession=經濟衰退 || Many people lost their jobs ___ the recession. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 經濟衰退期間許多人失業了。
recipient || n. || 接受者 || She was the recipient of an award. || 她是一個獎項的得主。 || award recipient=得獎者 || Each recipient ___ the scholarship will get 50,000 dollars. || of|for|to || the recipient of = …的接受者。 || 每位獎學金得主將獲得五萬元。
recite || v. || 背誦 || She recited a poem. || 她背誦了一首詩。 || recite a poem=背誦詩 || The students were asked ___ recite the poem in class. || to|for|of || be asked to + 原形動詞。 || 學生被要求在課堂上背誦那首詩。
recommend || v. || 推薦；建議 || Can you recommend a good book? || 你能推薦一本好書嗎？ || recommend that=建議… || The doctor recommended that he ___ more water. || drink|drinks|drank || recommend that + 主詞 + (should) + 原形動詞。 || 醫生建議他多喝水。
recommendation || n. || 推薦；建議 || I bought this book on your recommendation. || 我照你的推薦買了這本書。 || letter of recommendation=推薦信 || I need a letter of recommendation ___ my teacher. || from|of|at || from + 人 = 來自…。 || 我需要一封老師寫的推薦信。
recruit || v./n. || 招募 || The company is recruiting new staff. || 公司正在招募新員工。 || new recruit=新成員 || The club is trying ___ recruit new members. || to|for|of || try to + 原形動詞。 || 社團正在努力招募新成員。
refuge || n. || 避難所 || They took refuge in a cave. || 他們躲進山洞避難。 || take refuge=避難 || During the storm, we took refuge ___ a small temple. || in|on|to || take refuge in = 在…避難。 || 暴風雨中我們在一間小廟避難。
regardless || adv. || 不管 || He went regardless of the danger. || 他不顧危險還是去了。 || regardless of=不管… || Everyone is welcome, regardless ___ age. || of|to|for || regardless of = 不管…。 || 不論年齡，歡迎所有人。
regime || n. || 政權 || The regime controlled the media. || 那個政權控制媒體。 || military regime=軍事政權 || Many people escaped ___ the regime to find freedom. || from|of|with || escape from = 逃離…。 || 許多人逃離那個政權以尋找自由。
rehearsal || n. || 排練 || We have a rehearsal tonight. || 我們今晚要彩排。 || dress rehearsal=彩排 || The actors are ___ rehearsal for the new play. || in|on|at || in rehearsal = 排練中。 || 演員們正在排練新戲。
reinforce || v. || 加強 || Practice reinforces learning. || 練習能加強學習。 || reinforce a wall=加固牆壁 || Review the words often ___ reinforce your memory. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 常常複習單字以加強記憶。
reminder || n. || 提醒物 || This is a reminder about tomorrow's meeting. || 這是明天會議的提醒。 || friendly reminder=溫馨提醒 || The photo is a reminder ___ our happy days together. || of|for|to || a reminder of = 使人想起…的東西。 || 這張照片讓人想起我們一起度過的快樂時光。
removal || n. || 移除 || The removal of the old tree took a day. || 移除老樹花了一天。 || snow removal=除雪 || The removal of the old building ___ planned for next month. || is|are|be || 主詞 The removal 單數，用 is。 || 舊建築的拆除計畫在下個月進行。
render || v. || 使成為；提供 || The news rendered him speechless. || 這個消息讓他說不出話來。 || render sb speechless=使某人說不出話 || The accident rendered him unable ___ walk. || to|for|of || be unable to + 原形動詞。 || 那場意外使他無法走路。
rental || n./adj. || 租賃 || We got a rental car. || 我們租了一輛車。 || rental car=租來的車 || The rental fee ___ the bike is 100 dollars a day. || for|of|to || fee for = …的費用。 || 這輛腳踏車的租金是一天一百元。
repay || v. || 償還；報答 || I will repay you next week. || 我下週會還你錢。 || repay a loan=償還貸款 || How can I ever repay you ___ your kindness? || for|of|with || repay sb for sth = 報答某人的…。 || 我要怎麼報答你的好意呢？
republican || adj./n. || 共和黨員；共和的 || He is a Republican. || 他是共和黨員。 || Republican Party=共和黨 || The Republican candidate won the election ___ a small margin. || by|with|for || by + 差距。 || 共和黨候選人以些微差距贏得選舉。
resemblance || n. || 相似 || There is a strong resemblance between the sisters. || 這對姊妹長得很像。 || bear a resemblance to=與…相似 || She bears a strong resemblance ___ her mother. || to|with|for || bear a resemblance to = 與…相似。 || 她長得很像她媽媽。
reservoir || n. || 水庫 || The reservoir is almost empty. || 水庫幾乎乾了。 || water reservoir=水庫 || The reservoir is almost empty ___ it hasn't rained for months. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為好幾個月沒下雨，水庫快見底了。
residence || n. || 住所 || This is the president's official residence. || 這是總統的官邸。 || official residence=官邸 || The old house was once the residence ___ a famous writer. || of|for|to || the residence of = …的住所。 || 那棟老房子曾經是一位知名作家的住所。
resident || n. || 居民 || Local residents complained about the noise. || 當地居民抱怨噪音。 || local resident=當地居民 || Residents ___ the area complained about the noise. || of|for|to || residents of = …的居民。 || 這個地區的居民抱怨噪音。
residential || adj. || 住宅的 || This is a quiet residential area. || 這是一個安靜的住宅區。 || residential area=住宅區 || Factories are not allowed ___ residential areas. || in|on|at || in an area = 在…區域。 || 住宅區內不准設工廠。
resort || n. || 度假勝地 || We stayed at a beach resort. || 我們住在海灘度假村。 || beach resort=海灘度假村 || We spent our holiday ___ a beach resort in Kenting. || at|on|to || at + 地點 = 在…。 || 我們在墾丁的一個海灘度假村度假。
respondent || n. || 受訪者 || Most respondents agreed with the plan. || 大多數受訪者同意這個計畫。 || survey respondent=調查受訪者 || More than half of the respondents ___ they were happy. || said|says|saying || 主詞 half of the respondents，過去調查用過去式。 || 超過一半的受訪者說他們很快樂。
resume || v. || 重新開始 || Classes will resume next week. || 課程下週恢復。 || resume work=復工 || The game resumed ___ the rain stopped. || after|before|until || 雨停「之後」恢復，用 after。 || 雨停後比賽恢復進行。
retail || n./adj. || 零售 || She works in retail. || 她從事零售業。 || retail price=零售價 || The retail price of this phone is higher ___ I expected. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 這支手機的零售價比我預期的高。
revenue || n. || 收入；稅收 || The company's revenue increased. || 公司的營收增加了。 || tax revenue=稅收 || Tourism brings a lot of revenue ___ the city. || to|at|with || bring A to B = 為 B 帶來 A。 || 觀光為這座城市帶來大量收入。
reverse || v./adj./n. || 反轉；相反的 || Put the car in reverse. || 把車子打到倒車檔。 || in reverse order=以相反順序 || Please read the numbers ___ reverse order. || in|on|at || in reverse order = 以相反的順序。 || 請以相反的順序唸這些數字。
rhetoric || n. || 修辭；花言巧語 || His speech was full of rhetoric. || 他的演講充滿華麗的辭藻。 || political rhetoric=政治修辭 || His speech was full ___ empty rhetoric. || of|with|in || be full of = 充滿…。 || 他的演講充滿空洞的花言巧語。
rib || n. || 肋骨；排骨 || He broke a rib in the accident. || 他在事故中斷了一根肋骨。 || pork ribs=豬肋排 || These pork ribs ___ delicious! || are|is|be || 主詞 ribs 複數，用 are。 || 這些豬肋排真好吃！
ridge || n. || 山脊 || We walked along the ridge. || 我們沿著山脊走。 || mountain ridge=山脊 || We hiked ___ the mountain ridge for two hours. || along|among|between || along = 沿著…。 || 我們沿著山脊健行了兩個小時。
ridiculous || adj. || 荒謬的 || That's a ridiculous idea. || 那是個荒謬的點子。 || look ridiculous=看起來可笑 || It's ridiculous ___ pay 500 dollars for a cup of coffee. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 花五百元買一杯咖啡真是荒謬。
rifle || n. || 步槍 || The soldier carried a rifle. || 士兵扛著步槍。 || hunting rifle=獵槍 || The soldier ___ a rifle stood at the gate. || with|of|in || with + 物品 = 帶著…。 || 那名帶著步槍的士兵站在門口。
rigid || adj. || 嚴格的；僵硬的 || The school has rigid rules. || 這間學校規定很嚴格。 || rigid rules=嚴格的規定 || The rules are ___ rigid that students feel stressed. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 規定嚴格到讓學生覺得有壓力。
rim || n. || 邊緣 || There is a crack on the rim of the cup. || 杯緣有一道裂痕。 || rim of a cup=杯緣 || There is salt ___ the rim of the glass. || on|of|to || 在表面上用 on。 || 玻璃杯緣上有鹽。
riot || n./v. || 暴動 || The police stopped the riot. || 警方平息了暴動。 || riot police=鎮暴警察 || The riot police were sent ___ control the crowd. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 鎮暴警察被派去控制群眾。
rip || v./n. || 撕裂 || He ripped his jeans. || 他把牛仔褲扯破了。 || rip off=敲竹槓 || Fifty dollars for a bottle of water? That's a rip-off, ___ it? || isn't|is|doesn't || 前句 is 肯定，附加問句用 isn't it。 || 一瓶水五十元？太坑人了，不是嗎？
risky || adj. || 有風險的 || It's risky to invest all your money. || 把所有錢拿去投資很冒險。 || risky business=冒險的事 || It is risky ___ swim alone in the sea. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 獨自在海裡游泳很危險。

rival || n./v. || 對手 || The two schools are rivals. || 這兩所學校是對手。 || main rival=主要對手 || The two teams have been rivals ___ many years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 這兩隊多年來一直是對手。
rod || n. || 桿；棒 || He bought a new fishing rod. || 他買了一支新的釣竿。 || fishing rod=釣竿 || He caught a big fish ___ his new fishing rod. || with|by|on || with + 工具 = 用…。 || 他用新釣竿釣到一條大魚。
