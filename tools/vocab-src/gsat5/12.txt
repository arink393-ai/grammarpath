# 學測 L5 12：monopoly – particle
monopoly || n. || 壟斷 || The company has a monopoly on the market. || 這家公司壟斷了市場。 || have a monopoly on=壟斷… || No company should have a monopoly ___ the market. || on|in|at || a monopoly on = 對…的壟斷。 || 沒有任何公司應該壟斷市場。
morality || n. || 道德 || The story teaches morality to children. || 這個故事教孩子道德。 || public morality=公共道德 || The story teaches children ___ morality. || about|to|for || teach sb about sth = 教某人有關…。 || 這個故事教孩子有關道德的事。
mortality || n. || 死亡率 || The infant mortality rate has dropped. || 嬰兒死亡率已經下降。 || mortality rate=死亡率 || The mortality rate of the disease ___ dropped thanks to new medicine. || has|have|is || 主詞 rate 單數，用 has。 || 由於新藥，這種疾病的死亡率已經下降。
mortgage || n. || 房貸 || They took out a mortgage to buy the house. || 他們辦了房貸買房子。 || pay off a mortgage=還清房貸 || It took them twenty years ___ pay off their mortgage. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 他們花了二十年才還清房貸。
motive || n. || 動機 || What was the motive for the crime? || 犯罪動機是什麼？ || motive for=…的動機 || The police are trying to find the motive ___ the crime. || for|of|to || a motive for = …的動機。 || 警方正試圖找出犯罪動機。
mount || v./n. || 增加；登上 || Pressure is mounting on the government. || 政府承受的壓力越來越大。 || Mount Jade=玉山 || Have you ever climbed Mount Jade, ___ highest mountain in Taiwan? || the|a|an || 最高級前用 the。 || 你爬過台灣最高的山——玉山嗎？
mumble || v. || 喃喃自語 || He mumbled something. || 他咕噥了一些話。 || mumble to oneself=喃喃自語 || Stop mumbling ___ speak clearly. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的命令句用 and。 || 別再咕噥了，說清楚一點。
municipal || adj. || 市立的 || She works at the municipal library. || 她在市立圖書館工作。 || municipal government=市政府 || The municipal library is open ___ 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. || from|between|since || from A to B = 從 A 到 B。 || 市立圖書館從早上九點開到晚上九點。
muscular || adj. || 肌肉發達的 || He has a muscular body. || 他身材很壯。 || muscular arms=結實的手臂 || He became muscular ___ working out every day. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 他藉由每天健身練出了肌肉。
mustard || n. || 芥末 || I like mustard on my hot dog. || 我喜歡在熱狗上加芥末。 || mustard sauce=芥末醬 || Would you like some mustard ___ your sandwich? || on|in|at || 塗在上面用 on。 || 你的三明治要加芥末嗎？
myth || n. || 神話；迷思 || It's a myth that goldfish have short memories. || 金魚記憶很短是個迷思。 || Greek myth=希臘神話 || It is a myth ___ we only use 10% of our brains. || that|what|which || It is a myth that… = …是個迷思。 || 我們只用了大腦的百分之十是個迷思。
naive || adj. || 天真的 || Don't be so naive. || 別這麼天真。 || naive idea=天真的想法 || It was naive ___ me to trust him. || of|for|to || It is + 形容詞(人的特質) + of sb + to V。 || 我相信他真是太天真了。
narrative || n./adj. || 敘述；故事 || The book has a strong narrative. || 這本書的敘事很強。 || narrative writing=記敘文 || The narrative of the novel is told ___ the point of view of a child. || from|in|by || from the point of view of = 從…的觀點。 || 這本小說的敘事是從一個孩子的觀點出發。
nasty || adj. || 令人厭惡的；惡劣的 || That was a nasty thing to say. || 那話說得很惡毒。 || nasty smell=難聞的氣味 || He was really nasty ___ his little sister. || to|for|with || be nasty to sb = 對某人很壞。 || 他對妹妹真的很壞。
negotiation || n. || 談判 || The negotiation lasted for hours. || 談判持續了好幾個小時。 || under negotiation=談判中 || The price is still ___ negotiation. || under|below|in || under negotiation = 協商中。 || 價格仍在協商中。
neutral || adj. || 中立的 || Switzerland is a neutral country. || 瑞士是中立國。 || remain neutral=保持中立 || The teacher tried to stay neutral ___ the two students argued. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 兩個學生爭吵時，老師試著保持中立。
nominate || v. || 提名 || She was nominated for an award. || 她被提名角逐一個獎項。 || be nominated for=被提名… || The movie ___ nominated for five awards. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這部電影獲得五項提名。
nomination || n. || 提名 || The film received three nominations. || 這部電影獲得三項提名。 || receive a nomination=獲得提名 || She received a nomination ___ Best Actress. || for|of|to || a nomination for = …獎項的提名。 || 她獲得最佳女主角的提名。
nominee || n. || 被提名人 || The nominees for Best Actor were announced. || 最佳男主角的提名人選公布了。 || award nominee=獎項提名人 || All the nominees ___ invited to the ceremony. || were|was|be || 被動語態；nominees 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 所有被提名人都受邀參加典禮。
nonprofit || adj./n. || 非營利的 || She works for a nonprofit organization. || 她在非營利組織工作。 || nonprofit organization=非營利組織 || She works for a nonprofit ___ helps homeless people. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（組織）並當主詞。 || 她在一個幫助街友的非營利組織工作。
norm || n. || 常態；規範 || Working from home is now the norm. || 在家工作現在已是常態。 || social norm=社會規範 || Online learning has become the norm ___ many students. || for|of|to || for + 對象 = 對…而言。 || 線上學習對許多學生已成為常態。
noticeable || adj. || 明顯的 || There is a noticeable change. || 有明顯的變化。 || noticeable difference=明顯的差異 || There is a noticeable difference ___ the two pictures. || between|among|with || 兩者之間用 between。 || 這兩張圖有明顯的差異。
notify || v. || 通知 || Please notify us of any changes. || 如有任何變更請通知我們。 || notify sb of=通知某人… || Please notify the office ___ any change of address. || of|for|to || notify sb of sth = 通知某人某事。 || 地址如有變更請通知辦公室。
notion || n. || 概念；想法 || I have no notion of what he means. || 我完全不懂他的意思。 || the notion that=…的想法 || I don't agree with the notion ___ money brings happiness. || that|what|which || that 引導同位語子句，說明 notion 的內容。 || 我不同意「金錢帶來快樂」這個想法。
nowhere || adv. || 無處 || There is nowhere to park. || 沒有地方停車。 || nowhere to V=無處可… || There was nowhere ___ sit on the train. || to|for|of || nowhere to V = 無處可…。 || 火車上沒有地方可以坐。
nutrient || n. || 營養素 || Vegetables are full of nutrients. || 蔬菜富含營養素。 || essential nutrient=必需營養素 || Vegetables are full ___ nutrients. || of|with|in || be full of = 充滿…。 || 蔬菜富含營養素。
nutrition || n. || 營養 || Good nutrition is important for children. || 良好的營養對孩子很重要。 || good nutrition=良好營養 || Good nutrition is important ___ growing children. || for|to|with || be important for = 對…很重要。 || 良好的營養對成長中的孩子很重要。
obligation || n. || 義務 || You have no obligation to buy anything. || 你沒有義務購買任何東西。 || under no obligation=沒有義務 || You are under no obligation ___ buy anything. || to|for|of || an obligation to V = 做…的義務。 || 你沒有義務購買任何東西。
obscure || adj. || 不為人知的；模糊的 || He is an obscure poet. || 他是一位不知名的詩人。 || obscure meaning=晦澀的含意 || The poem was written by an obscure poet ___ name nobody remembers. || whose|who|which || whose + 名詞 表示「…的」。 || 這首詩是一位沒人記得名字的無名詩人寫的。
observer || n. || 觀察者 || She is a careful observer. || 她是個細心的觀察者。 || neutral observer=中立觀察者 || To a casual observer, everything seemed normal, ___ something was wrong. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 對一般觀察者而言一切看似正常，但事情不對勁。
odds || n. || 機率 || The odds of winning are low. || 贏的機率很低。 || against all odds=儘管困難重重 || ___ all odds, the small team won the championship. || Against|For|With || against all odds = 儘管困難重重。 || 儘管困難重重，那支小隊伍仍贏得了冠軍。
offering || n. || 供品；提供物 || They made an offering at the temple. || 他們在廟裡獻上供品。 || make an offering=獻供品 || People bring offerings ___ the temple during festivals. || to|for|with || bring sth to + 地方。 || 人們在節慶時帶供品到廟裡。
olive || n. || 橄欖 || I like olive oil. || 我喜歡橄欖油。 || olive oil=橄欖油 || Cook the vegetables ___ a little olive oil. || in|on|at || cook in oil = 用油烹煮。 || 用一點橄欖油煮蔬菜。
operational || adj. || 運作的 || The new airport will be operational next year. || 新機場明年將開始運作。 || fully operational=全面運作 || The new MRT line will be operational ___ the end of the year. || by|until|since || by + 時間 = 在…之前。 || 新的捷運線將在年底前開始營運。
opponent || n. || 對手 || He beat his opponent easily. || 他輕鬆擊敗對手。 || political opponent=政治對手 || She shook hands ___ her opponent after the game. || with|to|for || shake hands with = 與…握手。 || 比賽後她和對手握手。
opposition || n. || 反對 || There was strong opposition to the plan. || 這個計畫遭到強烈反對。 || in opposition to=反對… || There was strong opposition ___ the new tax. || to|for|with || opposition to = 對…的反對。 || 新稅遭到強烈反對。
opt || v. || 選擇 || Many students opt for online classes. || 許多學生選擇線上課程。 || opt for=選擇… || Many people opt ___ public transportation in Taipei. || for|to|with || opt for = 選擇…。 || 在台北，許多人選擇大眾運輸。
optimism || n. || 樂觀 || She faces the future with optimism. || 她樂觀地面對未來。 || with optimism=樂觀地 || She faced her illness ___ optimism. || with|by|in || with + 抽象名詞 = 副詞。 || 她樂觀地面對自己的病。
optional || adj. || 選修的；可選擇的 || The trip is optional. || 這趟旅行可自由參加。 || optional course=選修課 || The field trip is optional, ___ you don't have to go. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 校外教學是自由參加的，所以你不一定要去。
orchard || n. || 果園 || We picked apples in the orchard. || 我們在果園裡摘蘋果。 || apple orchard=蘋果園 || We spent the afternoon picking apples ___ the orchard. || in|on|at || 在果園裡用 in。 || 我們整個下午在果園裡摘蘋果。
organism || n. || 生物；有機體 || Bacteria are tiny organisms. || 細菌是微小的生物。 || living organism=生物 || All living organisms need water ___ survive. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 所有生物都需要水才能生存。
originality || n. || 原創性 || Her work shows great originality. || 她的作品很有原創性。 || lack originality=缺乏原創性 || The judges praised the painting ___ its originality. || for|of|with || praise sb/sth for = 因…稱讚。 || 評審稱讚這幅畫的原創性。
outfit || n. || 全套服裝 || She wore a new outfit to the party. || 她穿了一套新衣服去派對。 || nice outfit=好看的服裝 || She spent an hour choosing an outfit ___ the party. || for|to|with || for + 目的 = 為了…。 || 她花了一個小時挑選參加派對的服裝。
outlet || n. || 插座；暢貨中心；出口 || Is there an outlet near the desk? || 書桌附近有插座嗎？ || outlet mall=暢貨中心 || Sports are a good outlet ___ stress. || for|to|of || an outlet for = 宣洩…的出口。 || 運動是宣洩壓力的好方法。
output || n. || 產量；輸出 || The factory's output increased. || 工廠的產量增加了。 || energy output=能量輸出 || The factory's output ___ doubled since last year. || has|have|is || 主詞 output 單數，用 has。 || 這間工廠的產量從去年以來加倍了。
outsider || n. || 局外人 || I felt like an outsider at the party. || 我在派對上覺得自己像個局外人。 || feel like an outsider=覺得格格不入 || As a new student, she felt like ___ outsider. || an|a|the || outsider 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 身為新生，她覺得自己像個局外人。
overall || adj./adv. || 整體的 || Overall, it was a good trip. || 整體來說，那是一趟愉快的旅行。 || overall=整體而言 || Overall, the students did well ___ the exam. || on|of|for || do well on an exam = 考得好。 || 整體而言，學生這次考試考得不錯。
overhead || adv./adj. || 在頭頂上 || A plane flew overhead. || 一架飛機從頭頂飛過。 || overhead bin=頭頂置物櫃 || Please put your bag in the overhead bin ___ the flight. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 飛行期間請把包包放在頭頂置物櫃。
oversee || v. || 監督 || She oversees the whole project. || 她監督整個計畫。 || oversee a project=監督計畫 || Who will oversee the project ___ the manager is away? || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 經理不在時誰來監督這個計畫？
overtake || v. || 超越；超車 || Never overtake on a curve. || 絕對不要在彎道超車。 || overtake a car=超車 || It is dangerous to overtake another car ___ a curve. || on|in|to || on a curve = 在彎道上。 || 在彎道上超車很危險。
overturn || v. || 翻覆；推翻 || The truck overturned on the highway. || 卡車在高速公路上翻覆。 || overturn a decision=推翻決定 || The court overturned the decision ___ new evidence was found. || after|before|until || 發現新證據「之後」推翻，用 after。 || 發現新證據後，法院推翻了判決。
overwhelm || v. || 壓倒；使不知所措 || I was overwhelmed by all the work. || 我被所有的工作壓得喘不過氣。 || be overwhelmed by=被…壓垮 || She was overwhelmed ___ joy when she heard the news. || with|by|of || be overwhelmed with + 情緒 = 充滿…。 || 她聽到消息時欣喜若狂。
parallel || adj./n. || 平行的 || The two lines are parallel. || 這兩條線是平行的。 || parallel to=與…平行 || The road runs parallel ___ the river. || to|with|for || parallel to = 與…平行。 || 這條路與河流平行。
participant || n. || 參與者 || Each participant got a T-shirt. || 每位參加者都拿到一件 T 恤。 || active participant=積極參與者 || Each participant ___ given a free T-shirt. || was|were|be || each + 單數名詞，用單數 was。 || 每位參加者都拿到一件免費 T 恤。
particle || n. || 微粒 || There are dust particles in the air. || 空氣中有灰塵微粒。 || dust particle=灰塵微粒 || Tiny particles ___ dust can cause allergies. || of|for|with || particles of = …的微粒。 || 微小的灰塵微粒可能引起過敏。
