# 學測 L5 06：deadly – dissolve
deadly || adj. || 致命的 || The virus is deadly. || 這種病毒是致命的。 || deadly disease=致命疾病 || The snake ___ bit him was deadly. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當主詞。 || 咬他的那條蛇是致命的。
debris || n. || 殘骸；碎片 || Debris from the storm covered the road. || 暴風雨留下的殘骸覆蓋了道路。 || clear debris=清除殘骸 || Workers spent days clearing debris ___ the road. || from|of|at || clear sth from = 從…清除某物。 || 工人花了好幾天清除道路上的殘骸。
debut || n. || 首次登台 || The singer made her debut at 16. || 那位歌手十六歲出道。 || make one's debut=首次登台 || The singer made her debut ___ the age of 16. || at|in|on || at the age of = 在…歲時。 || 那位歌手在十六歲時出道。
decay || v./n. || 腐爛；蛀牙 || Sugar can cause tooth decay. || 糖會造成蛀牙。 || tooth decay=蛀牙 || Eating too much sugar can lead ___ tooth decay. || to|for|in || lead to = 導致…。 || 吃太多糖會導致蛀牙。
deceive || v. || 欺騙 || He deceived his friends. || 他欺騙了朋友。 || deceive sb into=騙某人做… || Don't let his smile deceive you; he is not ___ nice as he looks. || as|so|such || not as…as = 不像…一樣。 || 別被他的笑容騙了，他沒有看起來那麼好。
decent || adj. || 像樣的；體面的 || He has a decent job. || 他有一份像樣的工作。 || decent job=像樣的工作 || Everyone deserves ___ decent place to live. || a|an|the || decent 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 每個人都應該有像樣的住處。
declaration || n. || 宣言；聲明 || The Declaration of Independence was signed in 1776. || 《獨立宣言》於 1776 年簽署。 || make a declaration=發表聲明 || The Declaration of Independence ___ signed in 1776. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 《獨立宣言》在 1776 年簽署。
decline || v./n. || 下降；婉拒 || The number of students has declined. || 學生人數下降了。 || decline an invitation=婉拒邀請 || The number of babies born in Taiwan ___ declined in recent years. || has|have|is || the number of…為單數主詞，用 has。 || 近年來台灣出生的嬰兒數量下降了。
dedicate || v. || 奉獻 || She dedicated her life to helping the poor. || 她一生奉獻於幫助窮人。 || dedicate oneself to=致力於… || She dedicated her life to ___ the poor. || helping|help|helped || dedicate…to + V-ing（to 是介系詞）。 || 她一生致力於幫助窮人。
defendant || n. || 被告 || The defendant said he was innocent. || 被告說他是無辜的。 || the defendant=被告 || The defendant ___ found not guilty. || was|were|be || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 被告被判無罪。
deficit || n. || 赤字 || The country has a large budget deficit. || 這個國家有龐大的預算赤字。 || budget deficit=預算赤字 || The government must find a way ___ reduce the deficit. || to|for|of || a way to V = 做…的方法。 || 政府必須找到方法減少赤字。
defy || v. || 違抗；不服從 || Many teens defy their parents. || 許多青少年會違抗父母。 || defy the law=違法; defy description=難以形容 || The protesters chose to defy the police ___ refusing to leave. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 抗議者以拒絕離開來違抗警方。
delegate || n./v. || 代表；委派 || She was chosen as a delegate. || 她被選為代表。 || delegate tasks=分派任務 || A good leader knows how ___ delegate tasks. || to|for|of || 疑問詞 + to V。 || 好的領導者知道如何分派任務。
delegation || n. || 代表團 || A delegation from Japan visited our school. || 一個日本代表團參觀了我們學校。 || trade delegation=貿易代表團 || A delegation ___ Japan visited our school yesterday. || from|of|to || from + 地方 = 來自…。 || 一個來自日本的代表團昨天參觀了我們學校。
deliberate || adj. || 故意的；慎重的 || It was a deliberate mistake. || 那是故意犯的錯。 || deliberate attempt=蓄意的企圖 || I don't think it was a deliberate mistake; he just forgot, ___ he? || didn't|did|wasn't || 前句動詞 forgot 為過去肯定，附加問句用 didn't he。 || 我不認為那是故意的錯，他只是忘了，不是嗎？
democrat || n. || 民主主義者；民主黨員 || He is a Democrat. || 他是民主黨員。 || Democratic Party=民主黨 || Both the Democrats ___ the Republicans supported the law. || and|or|nor || both A and B = A 和 B 都。 || 民主黨和共和黨都支持這條法律。
denial || n. || 否認 || He is in denial about his problem. || 他不肯承認自己有問題。 || in denial=拒絕承認 || Despite his denial, everyone knew he ___ the money. || had taken|has taken|takes || 比過去（knew）更早的動作用過去完成式。 || 儘管他否認，大家都知道他拿了錢。
density || n. || 密度 || Taipei has a high population density. || 台北的人口密度很高。 || population density=人口密度 || The population density of Taipei is ___ than that of Tainan. || higher|high|highest || 有 than 用比較級。 || 台北的人口密度比台南高。
depict || v. || 描繪 || The painting depicts a village. || 這幅畫描繪一個村莊。 || depict sb as=把某人描繪成… || The movie depicts the hero ___ a brave young man. || as|like|for || depict A as B = 把 A 描繪成 B。 || 這部電影把主角描繪成一位勇敢的年輕人。
deploy || v. || 部署 || Troops were deployed to the area. || 軍隊被部署到該地區。 || deploy troops=部署軍隊 || Soldiers ___ deployed to help after the earthquake. || were|was|be || 被動語態；Soldiers 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 地震後軍人被派去協助救災。
depress || v. || 使沮喪 || The bad news depressed him. || 壞消息使他沮喪。 || feel depressed=感到沮喪 || Rainy days always depress me, ___ I stay home and read. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 雨天總讓我心情低落，所以我待在家看書。
deputy || n. || 副手；代理人 || She is the deputy mayor. || 她是副市長。 || deputy mayor=副市長 || The deputy mayor spoke ___ behalf of the mayor. || on|in|at || on behalf of = 代表…。 || 副市長代表市長發言。
derive || v. || 得到；源自 || Many English words derive from Latin. || 許多英文字源自拉丁文。 || derive from=源自… || Many English words are derived ___ Latin. || from|of|by || be derived from = 源自…。 || 許多英文字源自拉丁文。
descend || v. || 下降 || The plane began to descend. || 飛機開始下降。 || descend from=是…的後代 || The plane began ___ descend toward the airport. || to|for|of || begin to + 原形動詞。 || 飛機開始朝機場下降。
descriptive || adj. || 描述的 || Use descriptive words in your writing. || 在寫作中使用描述性的字詞。 || descriptive writing=描寫文 || Try to use more descriptive words ___ your essay. || in|on|at || in an essay = 在文章中。 || 試著在你的文章中使用更多描述性的字詞。
despair || n. || 絕望 || He was in despair after losing his job. || 他失業後陷入絕望。 || in despair=絕望地 || She cried ___ despair when she heard the news. || in|on|at || in despair = 絕望地。 || 她聽到消息時絕望地哭了。
destination || n. || 目的地 || Japan is a popular travel destination. || 日本是熱門的旅遊目的地。 || travel destination=旅遊地點 || We finally reached our destination ___ midnight. || at|in|on || at + 時刻。 || 我們終於在午夜抵達目的地。
destiny || n. || 命運 || She believes in destiny. || 她相信命運。 || control one's destiny=掌握命運 || You are the master ___ your own destiny. || of|for|to || the master of = …的主人。 || 你是自己命運的主人。
destructive || adj. || 破壞性的 || The typhoon was very destructive. || 這次颱風很有破壞力。 || destructive force=破壞力 || It was the most destructive typhoon ___ has ever hit the island. || that|which|what || 最高級後面的關係代名詞用 that。 || 那是有史以來侵襲該島最具破壞力的颱風。
devotion || n. || 奉獻；熱愛 || Her devotion to her students is admirable. || 她對學生的奉獻令人欽佩。 || devotion to=對…的奉獻 || Her devotion ___ her family is admirable. || to|for|with || devotion to = 對…的奉獻。 || 她對家人的奉獻令人欽佩。
diagnose || v. || 診斷 || The doctor diagnosed him with flu. || 醫生診斷他得了流感。 || be diagnosed with=被診斷出… || My uncle was diagnosed ___ diabetes last year. || with|of|as || be diagnosed with = 被診斷出…。 || 我叔叔去年被診斷出糖尿病。
diagnosis || n. || 診斷 || The doctor made a diagnosis quickly. || 醫生很快做出了診斷。 || early diagnosis=早期診斷 || Early diagnosis can save many lives, ___ regular checkups are important. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 早期診斷可以救很多人命，所以定期檢查很重要。
dialect || n. || 方言 || My grandmother speaks a local dialect. || 我奶奶說當地方言。 || local dialect=當地方言 || My grandmother speaks a dialect ___ I can't understand. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 understand 的受詞。 || 我奶奶說一種我聽不懂的方言。
diameter || n. || 直徑 || The circle has a diameter of 10 cm. || 這個圓的直徑是十公分。 || in diameter=直徑… || The tree is about two meters ___ diameter. || in|on|at || in diameter = 直徑是…。 || 這棵樹的直徑約兩公尺。
diaper || n. || 尿布 || The baby needs a new diaper. || 寶寶需要換新尿布。 || change a diaper=換尿布 || Could you change the baby's diaper ___ I make dinner? || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 我做晚餐的時候，你可以幫寶寶換尿布嗎？
digestion || n. || 消化 || Fiber helps digestion. || 纖維有助於消化。 || good for digestion=有助消化 || Eating slowly is good ___ your digestion. || for|to|with || be good for = 對…有益。 || 慢慢吃對消化有益。
dilemma || n. || 兩難 || I'm in a dilemma. || 我陷入兩難。 || in a dilemma=進退兩難 || She was ___ a dilemma about which job to accept. || in|on|at || in a dilemma = 進退兩難。 || 她對於要接受哪份工作感到兩難。
dimension || n. || 尺寸；層面 || What are the dimensions of the room? || 這個房間的尺寸是多少？ || new dimension=新的層面 || Living abroad added a new dimension ___ her life. || to|for|with || add A to B = 為 B 增添 A。 || 出國生活為她的人生增添了新的層面。
diminish || v. || 減少 || His fear diminished over time. || 他的恐懼隨著時間減少了。 || diminish the risk=降低風險 || The pain will diminish ___ a few days. || in|at|on || in + 一段時間 = 在…之後。 || 疼痛在幾天後就會減輕。
diplomatic || adj. || 外交的；圓滑的 || The two countries have diplomatic relations. || 兩國有外交關係。 || diplomatic relations=外交關係 || She gave a diplomatic answer ___ upsetting anyone. || without|with|by || without + V-ing = 不…。 || 她給了一個圓滑的回答，沒有得罪任何人。
directory || n. || 名錄；目錄 || Look up the number in the phone directory. || 在電話簿裡查號碼。 || phone directory=電話簿 || You can find the store ___ the mall directory. || in|on|at || 在名錄中用 in。 || 你可以在購物中心的樓層指南中找到那家店。
disapprove || v. || 不贊成 || My parents disapprove of my plan. || 我父母不贊成我的計畫。 || disapprove of=不贊成… || My parents disapprove ___ my staying out late. || of|to|with || disapprove of = 不贊成…。 || 我爸媽不贊成我晚歸。
disclose || v. || 揭露 || He refused to disclose his salary. || 他拒絕透露他的薪水。 || disclose information=揭露資訊 || The company refused ___ disclose the details. || to|for|of || refuse to + 原形動詞。 || 公司拒絕透露細節。
disconnect || v. || 切斷 || The phone was disconnected. || 電話被切斷了。 || disconnect from=與…斷線 || My Internet was disconnected ___ I didn't pay the bill. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為我沒繳帳單，網路被切斷了。
discourse || n. || 論述；談話 || Political discourse has become more negative. || 政治論述變得更負面了。 || public discourse=公共論述 || Social media has changed the way ___ public discourse happens. || that|what|who || the way that = …的方式。 || 社群媒體改變了公共論述發生的方式。
discriminate || v. || 歧視；區別 || It is wrong to discriminate against women. || 歧視女性是不對的。 || discriminate against=歧視… || Companies should not discriminate ___ older workers. || against|to|with || discriminate against = 歧視…。 || 公司不應歧視年長的員工。
discrimination || n. || 歧視 || Racial discrimination is illegal. || 種族歧視是違法的。 || racial discrimination=種族歧視 || Discrimination ___ people because of their race is wrong. || against|to|with || discrimination against = 對…的歧視。 || 因種族而歧視他人是錯的。
disrupt || v. || 擾亂 || The typhoon disrupted flights. || 颱風擾亂了航班。 || disrupt traffic=干擾交通 || Flights ___ disrupted by the typhoon yesterday. || were|was|be || 被動語態；Flights 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 昨天航班因颱風而受到影響。
dissolve || v. || 溶解 || Sugar dissolves in water. || 糖會溶於水。 || dissolve in=溶解於… || Stir the coffee ___ the sugar dissolves. || until|unless|since || until = 直到…。 || 攪拌咖啡直到糖溶解為止。
