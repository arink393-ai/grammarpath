# 學測 L5 01：abnormal – apt
abnormal || adj. || 不正常的 || The test showed abnormal results. || 檢查顯示結果異常。 || abnormal behavior=異常行為 || The doctor said the results were abnormal, ___ I needed more tests. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 醫生說結果異常，所以我需要做更多檢查。
abolish || v. || 廢除 || Slavery was abolished in the 1800s. || 奴隸制度在十九世紀被廢除。 || abolish a law=廢除法律 || Many people believe the death penalty should ___ abolished. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 許多人認為死刑應該被廢除。
abortion || n. || 墮胎 || Abortion is a controversial topic. || 墮胎是具爭議性的議題。 || abortion law=墮胎法 || Abortion is a topic ___ people have very different opinions about. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 about 的受詞。 || 墮胎是人們看法差異很大的議題。
abrupt || adj. || 突然的；唐突的 || The car came to an abrupt stop. || 車子突然停了下來。 || abrupt change=突然的改變 || The meeting came to an abrupt end ___ the fire alarm went off. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 火災警報響起時，會議突然結束了。
absurd || adj. || 荒謬的 || That's an absurd idea. || 那是個荒謬的點子。 || absurd idea=荒謬的想法 || It is absurd ___ pay so much for a T-shirt. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 花那麼多錢買一件 T 恤真是荒謬。
abundant || adj. || 豐富的 || Taiwan has abundant fruit. || 台灣的水果很豐富。 || abundant resources=豐富的資源 || The island is abundant ___ natural resources. || in|of|with || be abundant in = 富含…。 || 這座島擁有豐富的天然資源。
abuse || n./v. || 濫用；虐待 || Drug abuse is a serious problem. || 藥物濫用是嚴重的問題。 || drug abuse=藥物濫用; child abuse=虐童 || Children who ___ abused need special care. || are|is|be || 被動語態；Children 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 受虐兒童需要特別的照顧。
accelerate || v. || 加速 || The car accelerated quickly. || 車子迅速加速。 || accelerate growth=加速成長 || The government took steps ___ accelerate economic growth. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 政府採取措施以加速經濟成長。
accessible || adj. || 可到達的；容易取得的 || The museum is accessible by MRT. || 搭捷運可以到博物館。 || be accessible to=可供…使用 || The building is accessible ___ people in wheelchairs. || to|at|with || be accessible to = 可供…使用。 || 這棟大樓可供坐輪椅的人使用。
accommodate || v. || 容納；提供住宿 || The hotel can accommodate 500 guests. || 這間飯店可容納五百位客人。 || accommodate guests=接待客人 || The hall is large enough ___ accommodate 1,000 people. || to|for|that || enough to + 原形動詞 = 足以…。 || 這個禮堂大到足以容納一千人。
accommodation || n. || 住宿 || The price includes accommodation. || 價格包含住宿。 || hotel accommodation=飯店住宿 || The trip price includes flights ___ accommodation. || and|or|but || 兩者並列用 and。 || 旅遊費用包含機票和住宿。
accord || n. || 一致；協議 || The two countries signed a peace accord. || 兩國簽署了和平協議。 || in accord with=與…一致; of one's own accord=自願地 || She cleaned her room ___ her own accord. || of|by|with || of one's own accord = 自願地。 || 她自願打掃了自己的房間。
accounting || n. || 會計 || She studies accounting. || 她讀會計。 || accounting firm=會計師事務所 || She decided to major ___ accounting. || in|on|at || major in = 主修…。 || 她決定主修會計。
acquaint || v. || 使認識 || Let me acquaint you with the rules. || 讓我向你介紹規則。 || be acquainted with=熟悉… || Are you acquainted ___ the new teacher? || with|to|of || be acquainted with = 認識、熟悉…。 || 你認識新老師嗎？
acquisition || n. || 獲得；習得 || Language acquisition is easier for children. || 兒童習得語言比較容易。 || language acquisition=語言習得 || Language acquisition is faster ___ children than for adults. || for|to|with || 「對某人來說」用 for sb。 || 兒童習得語言的速度比成人快。
activist || n. || 社運人士 || She is an environmental activist. || 她是一位環保運動人士。 || environmental activist=環保人士 || The activist, ___ fights for animal rights, gave a speech. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位為動物權益奮鬥的社運人士發表了演說。
acute || adj. || 急性的；敏銳的 || He has acute pain in his stomach. || 他的胃劇烈疼痛。 || acute pain=劇痛; acute sense=敏銳的感官 || Dogs have an acute sense ___ smell. || of|for|in || a sense of = …的感官。 || 狗有敏銳的嗅覺。
administration || n. || 行政；管理 || She works in school administration. || 她在學校行政部門工作。 || business administration=企業管理 || He wants to study business administration ___ college. || in|on|of || in college = 在大學。 || 他想在大學讀企業管理。
administrative || adj. || 行政的 || She has an administrative job. || 她做的是行政工作。 || administrative staff=行政人員 || The administrative office ___ open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. || is|are|be || The administrative office 單數，用 is。 || 行政辦公室從早上八點開到下午五點。
administrator || n. || 管理者 || The school administrator made a new rule. || 學校行政人員訂了新規定。 || system administrator=系統管理員 || Please contact the administrator ___ you forget your password. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你忘了密碼，請聯絡管理員。
adolescent || n./adj. || 青少年 || Adolescents need a lot of sleep. || 青少年需要大量的睡眠。 || adolescent years=青春期 || Many adolescents feel pressure ___ their peers. || from|of|with || pressure from = 來自…的壓力。 || 許多青少年感受到來自同儕的壓力。
adore || v. || 崇拜；非常喜愛 || She adores her grandmother. || 她非常喜愛她的奶奶。 || adore sb=非常喜愛某人 || The kids adore ___ new teacher. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 teacher 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 孩子們非常喜歡他們的新老師。
adverse || adj. || 不利的 || The medicine has some adverse effects. || 這種藥有一些副作用。 || adverse effects=不良影響 || Lack of sleep can have adverse effects ___ your health. || on|to|for || have an effect on = 對…有影響。 || 睡眠不足會對你的健康造成不良影響。
advocate || v./n. || 提倡；擁護者 || She advocates for animal rights. || 她提倡動物權益。 || advocate for=為…發聲 || Many doctors advocate a diet ___ is low in sugar. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當主詞。 || 許多醫生提倡低糖的飲食。
affection || n. || 喜愛 || She shows great affection for her pets. || 她對寵物表現出極大的喜愛。 || affection for=對…的喜愛 || The old man has deep affection ___ his hometown. || for|to|with || affection for = 對…的喜愛。 || 那位老人對家鄉有深厚的感情。
afterward || adv. || 之後 || We had dinner and went to a movie afterward. || 我們吃了晚餐，之後去看電影。 || shortly afterward=不久之後 || We'll have lunch first and go shopping afterward, ___ we? || shall|will|do || Let's / We'll 提議的附加問句常用 shall we。 || 我們先吃午餐，之後再去逛街，好嗎？
agenda || n. || 議程 || What's on the agenda today? || 今天的議程是什麼？ || on the agenda=在議程上 || What is the first item ___ the agenda? || on|in|at || on the agenda = 在議程上。 || 議程上的第一項是什麼？
aggression || n. || 侵略；攻擊性 || Violent games may cause aggression. || 暴力遊戲可能引起攻擊性。 || act of aggression=侵略行為 || Some studies show that violent games can lead ___ aggression. || to|for|in || lead to = 導致…。 || 有些研究顯示暴力遊戲可能導致攻擊性。
agony || n. || 極大的痛苦 || He was in agony after the accident. || 他在事故後痛苦不堪。 || in agony=痛苦地 || He cried out ___ agony when he broke his leg. || in|with|by || in agony = 痛苦地。 || 他摔斷腿時痛苦地大叫。
agricultural || adj. || 農業的 || Taiwan has many agricultural products. || 台灣有許多農產品。 || agricultural products=農產品 || Taiwan exports agricultural products ___ as mangoes and tea. || such|like|so || such as = 例如。 || 台灣出口芒果、茶葉等農產品。
aisle || n. || 走道 || Would you like a window seat or an aisle seat? || 你要靠窗還是靠走道的座位？ || aisle seat=靠走道座位 || I'd like an aisle seat ___ I can get up easily. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 我想要靠走道的座位，這樣起身比較方便。
alcoholic || adj./n. || 含酒精的；酗酒者 || Beer is an alcoholic drink. || 啤酒是含酒精的飲料。 || alcoholic drink=酒精飲料 || Alcoholic drinks are not sold ___ people under 18. || to|for|with || sell sth to sb = 把某物賣給某人。 || 含酒精的飲料不賣給未滿十八歲的人。
alien || n./adj. || 外星人；陌生的 || Do you believe in aliens? || 你相信外星人存在嗎？ || alien life=外星生命 || Do you think aliens ___ to Earth one day? || will come|come|came || 未來的事用 will + 原形動詞。 || 你認為外星人有一天會來地球嗎？
allergic || adj. || 過敏的 || I'm allergic to cats. || 我對貓過敏。 || be allergic to=對…過敏 || My brother is allergic ___ peanuts. || to|with|of || be allergic to = 對…過敏。 || 我弟弟對花生過敏。
allergy || n. || 過敏 || She has a food allergy. || 她有食物過敏。 || food allergy=食物過敏 || He has an allergy ___ seafood. || to|with|of || an allergy to = 對…的過敏。 || 他對海鮮過敏。
alliance || n. || 聯盟 || The two countries formed an alliance. || 兩國結成同盟。 || form an alliance=結盟 || The two companies formed an alliance ___ compete with bigger rivals. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 兩家公司結盟以對抗更大的競爭對手。
allocate || v. || 分配 || The money was allocated to schools. || 這筆錢被分配給學校。 || allocate funds=分配經費 || The government allocated more money ___ education this year. || to|at|with || allocate A to B = 把 A 分配給 B。 || 政府今年分配了更多經費給教育。
ally || n./v. || 盟友 || The U.S. is an ally of Japan. || 美國是日本的盟友。 || close ally=親密盟友 || The two countries ___ been close allies since the war, and the alliance makes each ally safer. || have|has|are || 主詞 The two countries 複數，現在完成式用 have + p.p.。 || 這兩國自戰爭以來一直是親密盟友，同盟讓每個盟國更安全。
alongside || prep./adv. || 在旁邊；與…一起 || She worked alongside her father. || 她和父親一起工作。 || alongside=並肩 || She worked alongside her father ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 她和父親並肩工作了十年。
alter || v. || 改變 || We need to alter our plans. || 我們需要改變計畫。 || alter a plan=更改計畫 || The dress needs ___ altered because it is too long. || to be|being|be || need to be + p.p. = 需要被…。 || 這件洋裝太長，需要修改。
alternate || adj./v. || 交替的；輪流 || We take turns on alternate days. || 我們隔天輪流。 || on alternate days=每隔一天 || My sister and I alternate ___ washing the dishes. || in|on|at || alternate in + V-ing = 輪流做…。 || 我姊姊和我輪流洗碗。
amend || v. || 修正 || The law was amended last year. || 這條法律去年修正了。 || amend a law=修法 || The law ___ amended to protect children better. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這條法律經過修正以更妥善保護兒童。
ample || adj. || 充足的 || We have ample time. || 我們有充足的時間。 || ample time=充足的時間 || There is ample space ___ everyone in the new classroom. || for|to|of || space for = 容納…的空間。 || 新教室有足夠的空間容納每個人。
analyst || n. || 分析師 || He works as a data analyst. || 他擔任資料分析師。 || data analyst=資料分析師 || The analyst predicted ___ prices would rise. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 predicted 的受詞。 || 那位分析師預測物價會上漲。
anonymous || adj. || 匿名的 || The donation was anonymous. || 這筆捐款是匿名的。 || anonymous letter=匿名信 || The money was donated by someone ___ wanted to remain anonymous. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 這筆錢是由一位想保持匿名的人捐的。
anticipate || v. || 預期 || We didn't anticipate any problems. || 我們沒預料到會有任何問題。 || anticipate problems=預期問題 || We didn't anticipate ___ the trip would take so long. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 anticipate 的受詞。 || 我們沒預料到這趟旅程會花這麼久。
antique || n./adj. || 古董 || This vase is an antique. || 這個花瓶是古董。 || antique shop=古董店 || This antique clock ___ made over 200 years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個古董鐘是兩百多年前製造的。
applause || n. || 掌聲 || The audience gave a big round of applause. || 觀眾報以熱烈的掌聲。 || a round of applause=一陣掌聲 || Let's give her a big round ___ applause! || of|for|with || a round of applause = 一陣掌聲。 || 讓我們給她熱烈的掌聲！
appliance || n. || 家電 || We bought some new kitchen appliances. || 我們買了一些新的廚房電器。 || home appliance=家電 || Please turn off all electrical appliances ___ you leave the house. || before|after|since || 出門「之前」關掉，用 before。 || 出門前請關掉所有電器。
apt || adj. || 易於…的；恰當的 || He is apt to forget things. || 他容易忘東忘西。 || be apt to V=易於… || Children are apt ___ catch colds in winter. || to|for|of || be apt to + 原形動詞 = 容易…。 || 孩子在冬天容易感冒。
