# 學測 L5 10：implication – league
implication || n. || 含意；影響 || What are the implications of this decision? || 這個決定有什麼影響？ || implications for=對…的影響 || The new law has serious implications ___ small businesses. || for|to|with || implications for = 對…的影響。 || 新法對小型企業有嚴重的影響。
impulse || n. || 衝動 || I bought it on impulse. || 我一時衝動買了它。 || on impulse=一時衝動 || She bought the dress ___ impulse. || on|in|at || on impulse = 一時衝動。 || 她一時衝動買了那件洋裝。
incentive || n. || 誘因；激勵 || Bonuses are an incentive to work harder. || 獎金是努力工作的誘因。 || incentive to V=做…的誘因 || The prize gave students an incentive ___ read more. || to|for|of || an incentive to V = 做…的誘因。 || 這個獎品激勵學生多讀書。
incorporate || v. || 包含；納入 || We incorporated your ideas into the plan. || 我們把你的想法納入計畫中。 || incorporate A into B=把 A 納入 B || The teacher incorporated games ___ her lessons. || into|onto|from || incorporate A into B = 把 A 納入 B。 || 老師把遊戲融入她的課程中。
index || n. || 索引；指數 || Look up the word in the index. || 在索引中查這個字。 || price index=物價指數 || You can find the topic in the index ___ the back of the book. || at|in|on || at the back of = 在…的後面。 || 你可以在書後面的索引找到這個主題。
indifferent || adj. || 漠不關心的 || He is indifferent to politics. || 他對政治漠不關心。 || be indifferent to=對…漠不關心 || How can you be so indifferent ___ other people's pain? || to|for|with || be indifferent to = 對…漠不關心。 || 你怎麼能對別人的痛苦如此漠不關心？
indigenous || adj. || 原住民的；本土的 || Taiwan has 16 indigenous tribes. || 台灣有十六個原住民族。 || indigenous people=原住民 || The festival celebrates the culture ___ Taiwan's indigenous people. || of|for|with || the culture of = …的文化。 || 這個節慶慶祝台灣原住民的文化。
indispensable || adj. || 不可或缺的 || Phones are indispensable in modern life. || 手機在現代生活中不可或缺。 || be indispensable to=對…不可或缺 || Water is indispensable ___ all living things. || to|with|from || be indispensable to = 對…不可或缺。 || 水對所有生物都是不可或缺的。
indulge || v. || 沉溺；縱容 || I indulged in some chocolate cake. || 我放縱自己吃了些巧克力蛋糕。 || indulge in=沉溺於… || Once in a while, it's OK to indulge ___ your favorite food. || in|on|at || indulge in = 沉溺於…。 || 偶爾放縱自己吃喜歡的食物也沒關係。
inevitable || adj. || 不可避免的 || Change is inevitable. || 改變是不可避免的。 || inevitable result=必然的結果 || It is inevitable ___ we make mistakes when we learn. || that|what|which || It is + 形容詞 + that 子句。 || 學習時犯錯是不可避免的。
infect || v. || 感染 || The virus infected thousands of people. || 這種病毒感染了數千人。 || be infected with=感染… || Thousands of people were infected ___ the virus. || with|by|of || be infected with = 感染…。 || 數千人感染了這種病毒。
infinite || adj. || 無限的 || The universe seems infinite. || 宇宙似乎是無限的。 || infinite patience=無限的耐心 || A good teacher needs infinite patience ___ young children. || with|to|of || patience with = 對…的耐心。 || 好老師對幼童需要無限的耐心。
infrastructure || n. || 基礎建設 || The country needs better infrastructure. || 這個國家需要更好的基礎建設。 || public infrastructure=公共建設 || The government will spend more money ___ infrastructure. || on|in|at || spend money on = 花錢在…。 || 政府將花更多錢在基礎建設上。
inherent || adj. || 固有的 || There are risks inherent in any sport. || 任何運動都有固有的風險。 || inherent risk=固有風險 || There are risks inherent ___ every business. || in|on|at || be inherent in = 是…所固有的。 || 每種生意都有其固有的風險。
inherit || v. || 繼承 || She inherited a house from her grandmother. || 她從祖母那裡繼承了一棟房子。 || inherit from=從…繼承 || He inherited his good looks ___ his father. || from|of|by || inherit sth from sb = 從某人繼承某物。 || 他從父親那裡遺傳了好相貌。
initiate || v. || 開始；發起 || The school initiated a recycling program. || 學校發起了一項回收計畫。 || initiate a program=發起計畫 || The program ___ initiated by a group of students. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個計畫是由一群學生發起的。
initiative || n. || 主動；新計畫 || She took the initiative to clean the classroom. || 她主動打掃教室。 || take the initiative=採取主動 || He took the initiative ___ help the new student. || to|for|of || take the initiative to V = 主動做…。 || 他主動幫助新同學。
inject || v. || 注射；注入 || The nurse injected the medicine. || 護理師注射了藥物。 || inject A into B=把 A 注入 B || The new coach injected energy ___ the team. || into|onto|from || inject A into B = 把 A 注入 B。 || 新教練為球隊注入了活力。
injection || n. || 注射 || The doctor gave me an injection. || 醫生給我打了一針。 || flu injection=流感疫苗注射 || The child cried ___ the nurse gave him an injection. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 護理師幫那孩子打針時他哭了。
inning || n. || （棒球）局 || The game went into the tenth inning. || 比賽進入第十局。 || ninth inning=第九局 || Our team scored three runs ___ the ninth inning. || in|on|at || 在某一局用 in。 || 我們隊在第九局得了三分。
innovation || n. || 創新 || Innovation drives the economy. || 創新推動經濟。 || technological innovation=科技創新 || The company is famous ___ its innovation. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 這家公司以創新聞名。
innovative || adj. || 創新的 || She has many innovative ideas. || 她有很多創新的點子。 || innovative idea=創新的想法 || The company is looking for people ___ are innovative. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 公司在找有創新能力的人。
inquiry || n. || 詢問；調查 || We received many inquiries about the job. || 我們收到很多關於這份工作的詢問。 || make an inquiry=詢問 || We received many inquiries ___ the new product. || about|to|for || inquiries about = 關於…的詢問。 || 我們收到很多關於新產品的詢問。
insane || adj. || 瘋狂的 || That idea is insane! || 那個點子太瘋狂了！ || go insane=發瘋 || It's insane ___ drive so fast in the rain. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 雨天開那麼快真是瘋了。
insight || n. || 洞察力；深刻見解 || The book gives insight into Japanese culture. || 這本書讓人深入了解日本文化。 || insight into=對…的深刻理解 || The trip gave me insight ___ local culture. || into|onto|from || insight into = 對…的深刻理解。 || 這趟旅行讓我深入了解當地文化。
installation || n. || 安裝；裝置 || The installation of the air conditioner took two hours. || 冷氣安裝花了兩個小時。 || art installation=裝置藝術 || The installation of the new software took longer ___ expected. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 新軟體的安裝花的時間比預期還久。
institute || n. || 機構；學院 || He works at a research institute. || 他在研究機構工作。 || research institute=研究機構 || She works ___ a research institute in Taipei. || at|on|to || at + 機構 = 在…。 || 她在台北的一家研究機構工作。
institution || n. || 機構；制度 || Banks are financial institutions. || 銀行是金融機構。 || financial institution=金融機構 || Universities are institutions ___ people go to learn. || where|which|what || 關係副詞 where 修飾地點。 || 大學是人們求學的機構。
intact || adj. || 完整無缺的 || The vase arrived intact. || 花瓶完好無缺地送達。 || remain intact=保持完整 || Surprisingly, the old house remained intact ___ the earthquake. || after|while|since || after + 名詞 = 在…之後。 || 令人驚訝的是，那棟老房子在地震後仍完好無損。
integrate || v. || 整合；融入 || It takes time to integrate into a new culture. || 融入新文化需要時間。 || integrate into=融入… || It takes time for immigrants ___ integrate into a new society. || to|for|of || It takes time for sb + to V。 || 移民需要時間融入新社會。
integration || n. || 整合；融合 || Social integration is important for immigrants. || 社會融合對移民很重要。 || social integration=社會融合 || The integration of technology ___ teaching has changed education. || into|onto|from || the integration of A into B = 把 A 整合進 B。 || 把科技融入教學改變了教育。
integrity || n. || 正直 || He is a man of integrity. || 他是個正直的人。 || a man of integrity=正直的人 || She is a person ___ integrity. || of|with|for || a person of + 抽象名詞 = 具有…的人。 || 她是一個正直的人。
intensify || v. || 加強 || The typhoon is expected to intensify. || 颱風預計會增強。 || intensify efforts=加強努力 || The typhoon is expected ___ intensify tonight. || to|for|of || be expected to + 原形動詞。 || 颱風預計今晚會增強。
intent || n./adj. || 意圖；專注的 || He was intent on winning. || 他一心想贏。 || be intent on=一心想… || She is intent ___ winning the gold medal. || on|in|at || be intent on = 一心想要…。 || 她一心想贏得金牌。
interference || n. || 干涉 || I don't want any interference from my parents. || 我不希望父母干涉。 || interference in=對…的干涉 || He doesn't like interference ___ his private life. || in|on|to || interference in = 對…的干涉。 || 他不喜歡別人干涉他的私生活。
interior || n./adj. || 內部 || The interior of the car is clean. || 車子內部很乾淨。 || interior design=室內設計 || She studied interior design ___ university. || at|on|to || at university = 在大學。 || 她在大學讀室內設計。
interpretation || n. || 詮釋；口譯 || There are different interpretations of the poem. || 這首詩有不同的詮釋。 || interpretation of=對…的詮釋 || Everyone has a different interpretation ___ the poem. || of|for|to || interpretation of = 對…的詮釋。 || 每個人對這首詩的詮釋都不同。
interval || n. || 間隔 || Buses leave at 10-minute intervals. || 公車每十分鐘發一班。 || at intervals=每隔一段時間 || The buses leave ___ 15-minute intervals. || at|in|on || at … intervals = 每隔…。 || 公車每十五分鐘發一班。
intervention || n. || 干預 || Early intervention can help children with learning problems. || 早期介入能幫助有學習問題的孩子。 || early intervention=早期介入 || Without government intervention, the problem ___ get worse. || will|is|has || 未來的推測用 will。 || 沒有政府的介入，問題會更加惡化。
investigator || n. || 調查員 || The investigators found the cause of the fire. || 調查人員找到了火災的原因。 || private investigator=私家偵探 || The investigators are trying to find out ___ caused the fire. || what|which|that || what 引導名詞子句，並當子句的主詞。 || 調查人員正試圖找出火災的起因。
irony || n. || 諷刺 || The irony is that he hates water but became a swimmer. || 諷刺的是他討厭水卻成了游泳選手。 || the irony is that=諷刺的是… || The irony is ___ the fire station burned down. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當主詞補語。 || 諷刺的是消防局燒掉了。
journalism || n. || 新聞學；新聞業 || She studied journalism in college. || 她在大學讀新聞。 || career in journalism=新聞工作生涯 || She has worked in journalism ___ twenty years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她從事新聞業已經二十年了。
journalist || n. || 記者 || The journalist interviewed the mayor. || 記者訪問了市長。 || freelance journalist=自由記者 || The journalist ___ interviewed the president is my aunt. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位訪問總統的記者是我阿姨。
judicial || adj. || 司法的 || The judicial system must be fair. || 司法制度必須公平。 || judicial system=司法制度 || A fair judicial system is important ___ a democracy. || in|on|at || in a democracy = 在民主國家中。 || 公平的司法制度在民主國家中很重要。
jug || n. || 壺；罐 || She poured water from the jug. || 她從水壺倒水。 || water jug=水壺 || She filled the jug ___ fresh milk. || with|of|by || fill A with B = 把 B 裝滿 A。 || 她把壺裝滿了新鮮牛奶。
jury || n. || 陪審團 || The jury found him guilty. || 陪審團判他有罪。 || jury duty=陪審義務 || The jury took two days ___ reach a decision. || to|for|of || take + 時間 + to V。 || 陪審團花了兩天才做出決定。
justify || v. || 為…辯護；證明正當 || Nothing can justify violence. || 沒有什麼能為暴力辯護。 || justify one's actions=為自己的行為辯護 || How can you justify ___ so much money on games? || spending|to spend|spend || justify + V-ing。 || 你如何為花那麼多錢在遊戲上找理由？
juvenile || adj./n. || 青少年的 || Juvenile crime is a social problem. || 青少年犯罪是社會問題。 || juvenile crime=青少年犯罪 || Juvenile crime has become a serious problem ___ many cities. || in|on|at || in + 城市 = 在…。 || 青少年犯罪在許多城市已成為嚴重問題。
kidnap || v. || 綁架 || The boy was kidnapped. || 那個男孩被綁架了。 || kidnap sb=綁架某人 || The businessman ___ kidnapped on his way home. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那位商人在回家的路上被綁架了。
landlord || n. || 房東 || My landlord raised the rent. || 我的房東漲房租了。 || landlord and tenant=房東與房客 || I have to pay the rent ___ my landlord every month. || to|for|with || pay sth to sb = 付某物給某人。 || 我每個月必須付房租給房東。
laser || n. || 雷射 || The doctor used a laser to fix my eyes. || 醫生用雷射治療我的眼睛。 || laser surgery=雷射手術 || She had laser surgery ___ improve her eyesight. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她做了雷射手術來改善視力。
lawmaker || n. || 立法者 || Lawmakers passed a new bill. || 立法者通過了一項新法案。 || lawmaker=立法委員 || The lawmakers voted ___ the new bill. || for|to|with || vote for = 投票贊成…。 || 立法委員們投票支持新法案。
lawsuit || n. || 訴訟 || He filed a lawsuit against the company. || 他對那家公司提起訴訟。 || file a lawsuit=提起訴訟 || She filed a lawsuit ___ her former boss. || against|to|with || a lawsuit against = 對…提起的訴訟。 || 她對前老闆提起訴訟。
layer || n. || 層 || The cake has three layers. || 這個蛋糕有三層。 || ozone layer=臭氧層 || The ozone layer protects us ___ harmful sunlight. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 臭氧層保護我們免受有害陽光傷害。
league || n. || 聯盟 || He plays in a basketball league. || 他在一個籃球聯盟打球。 || major league=大聯盟 || He dreams of playing ___ the major leagues. || in|on|at || play in a league = 在聯盟打球。 || 他夢想在大聯盟打球。
