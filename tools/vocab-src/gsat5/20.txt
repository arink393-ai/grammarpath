# 學測 L5 20：vague – yield
vague || adj. || 模糊的 || He gave a vague answer. || 他給了一個含糊的回答。 || vague memory=模糊的記憶 || I have only a vague memory ___ my grandfather. || of|for|to || a memory of = 對…的記憶。 || 我對祖父只有模糊的記憶。
valid || adj. || 有效的 || Your passport is still valid. || 你的護照仍然有效。 || valid ticket=有效的票 || This ticket is valid ___ three days. || for|since|in || be valid for + 一段時間 = 有效期…。 || 這張票三天內有效。
variable || adj./n. || 多變的；變數 || The weather is variable in spring. || 春天的天氣多變。 || variable weather=多變的天氣 || The weather in spring is more variable ___ in summer. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 春天的天氣比夏天多變。
variation || n. || 變化 || There is a lot of variation in prices. || 價格差異很大。 || variation in=…的變化 || There is a lot of variation ___ the prices of phones. || in|on|at || variation in = …的差異。 || 手機的價格差異很大。
vein || n. || 靜脈 || The nurse put a needle in my vein. || 護理師把針刺入我的靜脈。 || in the same vein=同樣地 || Blood flows back ___ the heart through the veins. || to|at|from || flow back to = 流回…。 || 血液經由靜脈流回心臟。
vendor || n. || 小販 || I bought a drink from a street vendor. || 我向街頭小販買了一杯飲料。 || street vendor=街頭小販 || I bought some fruit ___ a street vendor. || from|of|by || buy sth from sb = 向某人買某物。 || 我向一位街頭小販買了一些水果。
venture || n./v. || 冒險事業 || The new business venture was a success. || 新的事業很成功。 || joint venture=合資企業 || Starting a business is ___ risky venture. || a|an|the || risky 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 創業是一項有風險的冒險。
venue || n. || 場地 || The venue for the concert has changed. || 音樂會的場地改了。 || concert venue=演唱會場地 || The venue ___ the wedding is a beautiful garden. || for|at|to || the venue for = …的場地。 || 婚禮的場地是一座美麗的花園。
verbal || adj. || 口頭的；言語的 || We made a verbal agreement. || 我們做了口頭協議。 || verbal skills=語言能力 || Verbal bullying can hurt ___ much as physical bullying. || as|so|too || as much as = 和…一樣多。 || 言語霸凌和肢體霸凌一樣傷人。
verdict || n. || 判決 || The jury reached a verdict. || 陪審團做出判決。 || reach a verdict=做出判決 || The verdict ___ announced this morning. || was|were|is || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 判決在今天早上宣布。
version || n. || 版本 || I prefer the original version. || 我比較喜歡原版。 || latest version=最新版本 || Please download the latest version ___ the app. || of|for|to || a version of = …的版本。 || 請下載這個應用程式的最新版本。
versus || prep. || 對抗 || It's Taiwan versus Japan tonight. || 今晚是台灣對日本。 || A versus B=A 對 B || The game is Taiwan versus Korea, and it starts ___ 7 p.m. || at|in|on || 時刻前用 at。 || 比賽是台灣對韓國，晚上七點開始。
vertical || adj. || 垂直的 || Draw a vertical line. || 畫一條垂直線。 || vertical line=垂直線 || The climber went up the vertical wall ___ using any ropes. || without|with|by || without + V-ing = 沒有…。 || 那位攀登者沒有使用任何繩索就爬上了垂直的岩壁。
veteran || n./adj. || 退伍軍人；老手 || My grandfather is a war veteran. || 我祖父是退伍軍人。 || veteran teacher=資深教師 || She is a veteran teacher ___ 30 years of experience. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 她是一位有三十年經驗的資深老師。
via || prep. || 經由 || I'll send it via email. || 我會透過電子郵件寄出。 || via email=透過電子郵件 || We flew to London via Dubai ___ saved a lot of money. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的動作用 and。 || 我們經杜拜飛往倫敦，省了很多錢。
viable || adj. || 可行的 || Is this plan viable? || 這個計畫可行嗎？ || viable option=可行的選擇 || Solar power is a viable option ___ many homes. || for|of|to || for + 對象 = 對…而言。 || 對許多家庭來說，太陽能是可行的選擇。
vicious || adj. || 惡毒的；兇猛的 || The dog is vicious. || 那隻狗很兇。 || vicious circle=惡性循環 || Stress and poor sleep can become a vicious circle, ___ is hard to break. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句修飾物，只能用 which。 || 壓力和睡不好會形成難以打破的惡性循環。
viewer || n. || 觀眾 || The show has millions of viewers. || 這個節目有數百萬觀眾。 || TV viewer=電視觀眾 || The show is popular ___ young viewers. || among|between|along || among = 在…之中。 || 這個節目在年輕觀眾中很受歡迎。
viewpoint || n. || 觀點 || Everyone has a different viewpoint. || 每個人的觀點不同。 || from one's viewpoint=從某人的觀點 || ___ my viewpoint, the plan won't work. || From|Of|By || from one's viewpoint = 從某人的觀點。 || 從我的觀點來看，這個計畫行不通。
vinegar || n. || 醋 || Add some vinegar to the salad. || 在沙拉裡加一些醋。 || rice vinegar=米醋 || Mix the oil ___ the vinegar before adding them to the salad. || with|to|for || mix A with B = 把 A 和 B 混合。 || 加入沙拉前先把油和醋混合。
virtual || adj. || 虛擬的 || We took a virtual tour of the museum. || 我們參觀了博物館的虛擬導覽。 || virtual reality=虛擬實境 || Students can visit the museum ___ a virtual tour. || through|across|along || through = 透過…。 || 學生可以透過虛擬導覽參觀博物館。
visa || n. || 簽證 || I need a visa to visit that country. || 我需要簽證才能去那個國家。 || apply for a visa=申請簽證 || You need ___ apply for a visa before you go. || to|for|of || need to + 原形動詞。 || 出發前你需要申請簽證。
vocal || adj./n. || 聲音的；直言的 || She is very vocal about her opinions. || 她對自己的看法直言不諱。 || vocal cords=聲帶 || She is very vocal ___ animal rights. || about|to|with || be vocal about = 對…直言不諱。 || 她對動物權益直言不諱。
volcano || n. || 火山 || The volcano erupted last night. || 那座火山昨晚爆發了。 || active volcano=活火山 || There are many volcanoes ___ Japan. || in|on|at || in + 國家。 || 日本有很多火山。
vomit || v./n. || 嘔吐 || He felt sick and vomited. || 他覺得不舒服就吐了。 || feel like vomiting=想吐 || The child vomited ___ eating too much candy. || after|before|until || 吃太多糖「之後」吐，用 after。 || 那孩子吃太多糖果後吐了。
voucher || n. || 禮券；優惠券 || I got a 500-dollar voucher. || 我拿到一張五百元禮券。 || gift voucher=禮券 || You can use this voucher ___ any of our stores. || at|on|to || at + 商店 = 在…。 || 你可以在我們任何一家店使用這張禮券。
vow || n./v. || 誓言；發誓 || They made their wedding vows. || 他們許下婚禮誓言。 || wedding vows=婚禮誓言 || He vowed ___ never smoke again. || to|for|of || vow to + 原形動詞 = 發誓要…。 || 他發誓再也不抽菸了。
vulnerable || adj. || 脆弱的；易受傷害的 || Old people are vulnerable to the flu. || 老人容易感染流感。 || be vulnerable to=易受…傷害 || Children are more vulnerable ___ the virus. || to|for|with || be vulnerable to = 易受…傷害。 || 兒童更容易受到病毒感染。
warehouse || n. || 倉庫 || The goods are stored in a warehouse. || 貨物存放在倉庫裡。 || warehouse store=倉儲量販店 || The goods ___ stored in a warehouse near the port. || are|is|be || 被動語態；goods 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 貨物存放在港口附近的倉庫裡。
warrior || n. || 戰士 || He fought like a warrior. || 他像戰士一樣奮戰。 || brave warrior=勇敢的戰士 || The warrior ___ fought bravely became a hero. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位英勇作戰的戰士成了英雄。
wary || adj. || 小心的；警惕的 || Be wary of strangers. || 要提防陌生人。 || be wary of=提防… || You should be wary ___ emails asking for your password. || of|for|to || be wary of = 提防…。 || 你應該提防要求密碼的電子郵件。
weird || adj. || 奇怪的 || That's a weird idea. || 那是個奇怪的想法。 || weird feeling=奇怪的感覺 || I heard a weird noise ___ I was sleeping. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 我睡覺時聽到一個奇怪的聲音。
whatsoever || adv. || 一點也（不） || I have no idea whatsoever. || 我完全不知道。 || no…whatsoever=一點也沒有 || There is no doubt whatsoever ___ he is guilty. || that|what|which || no doubt that… = 毫無疑問…。 || 毫無疑問他是有罪的。
wheelchair || n. || 輪椅 || She uses a wheelchair. || 她使用輪椅。 || in a wheelchair=坐輪椅 || The building has ramps for people ___ wheelchairs. || in|on|at || in a wheelchair = 坐輪椅。 || 這棟建築為坐輪椅的人設有坡道。
whereabouts || n. || 下落 || His whereabouts are unknown. || 他的下落不明。 || whereabouts=下落 || Nobody knows the whereabouts ___ the missing girl. || of|for|to || the whereabouts of = …的下落。 || 沒有人知道失蹤女孩的下落。
whereas || conj. || 然而 || I like tea, whereas my sister likes coffee. || 我喜歡茶，然而我姊姊喜歡咖啡。 || whereas=然而 || I enjoy reading, whereas my brother ___ playing games. || prefers|prefer|preferring || 主詞 my brother 單數，現在式動詞加 s。 || 我喜歡閱讀，而我哥哥比較喜歡打電動。
whine || v./n. || 發牢騷；哀鳴 || Stop whining! || 別再抱怨了！ || whine about=抱怨… || Stop whining ___ your homework and just do it. || about|to|for || whine about = 抱怨…。 || 別再抱怨作業了，快去做。
widespread || adj. || 普遍的 || The flu is widespread this year. || 今年流感很普遍。 || widespread use=普遍使用 || The use of smartphones is widespread ___ teenagers. || among|between|along || among = 在…之中。 || 智慧型手機在青少年之間很普遍。
wig || n. || 假髮 || The actor wore a wig. || 那位演員戴了假髮。 || wear a wig=戴假髮 || The actor wore a wig ___ he could look older. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 那位演員戴了假髮，好讓自己看起來比較老。
wilderness || n. || 荒野 || They got lost in the wilderness. || 他們在荒野中迷路了。 || in the wilderness=在荒野中 || The hikers spent three days ___ the wilderness. || in|on|at || in the wilderness = 在荒野中。 || 健行者在荒野中待了三天。
wildlife || n. || 野生動物 || We should protect wildlife. || 我們應該保護野生動物。 || wildlife park=野生動物園 || The national park is home ___ a lot of wildlife. || to|for|of || be home to = 是…的棲息地。 || 這座國家公園是許多野生動物的家。
windshield || n. || 擋風玻璃 || A stone cracked the windshield. || 一顆石頭打裂了擋風玻璃。 || windshield wiper=雨刷 || The windshield ___ cracked by a stone on the highway. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 擋風玻璃在高速公路上被石頭打裂。
wither || v. || 枯萎 || The flowers withered in the heat. || 花在高溫下枯萎了。 || wither away=凋零 || The plants withered ___ nobody watered them. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為沒人澆水，植物枯萎了。
witty || adj. || 機智的；風趣的 || He is a witty speaker. || 他是位風趣的演講者。 || witty remark=妙語 || Everyone laughed at his witty remark, ___ made the meeting less tense. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句中 which 可指前面整句。 || 大家都被他的妙語逗笑了，讓會議沒那麼緊張。
workshop || n. || 工作坊；工作室 || I joined a writing workshop. || 我參加了一個寫作工作坊。 || writing workshop=寫作工作坊 || I signed up ___ a pottery workshop this weekend. || for|to|with || sign up for = 報名…。 || 我報名了這週末的陶藝工作坊。
worship || v./n. || 崇拜；禮拜 || People worship at the temple. || 人們在廟裡參拜。 || place of worship=宗教場所 || A temple is a place ___ people worship gods. || where|which|what || 關係副詞 where 修飾地點。 || 廟是人們祭拜神明的地方。
worthwhile || adj. || 值得的 || The trip was worthwhile. || 這趟旅行很值得。 || worthwhile experience=值得的經驗 || It is worthwhile ___ visit the old street. || to|for|of || It is worthwhile + to V。 || 那條老街值得一遊。
worthy || adj. || 值得的 || His idea is worthy of attention. || 他的點子值得關注。 || be worthy of=值得… || Her effort is worthy ___ praise. || of|for|to || be worthy of = 值得…。 || 她的努力值得讚揚。
yacht || n. || 遊艇 || The rich man owns a yacht. || 那位富翁擁有一艘遊艇。 || luxury yacht=豪華遊艇 || They spent the weekend sailing ___ a yacht. || on|of|to || on a yacht = 在遊艇上。 || 他們週末在遊艇上航行。
yield || v./n. || 產出；讓步 || The farm yields a lot of rice. || 這座農場產出大量稻米。 || yield to=讓步給… || Drivers must yield ___ pedestrians at crosswalks. || to|for|with || yield to = 讓…先行。 || 駕駛在斑馬線前必須禮讓行人。
