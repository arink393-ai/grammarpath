# 學測 L5 19：thrill – vacuum
thrill || n./v. || 興奮；使興奮 || Riding a roller coaster is a thrill. || 坐雲霄飛車很刺激。 || be thrilled with=對…感到興奮 || She was thrilled ___ her birthday present. || with|of|to || be thrilled with = 對…感到非常興奮。 || 她對生日禮物感到非常興奮。
thriller || n. || 驚悚片 || I like watching thrillers. || 我喜歡看驚悚片。 || psychological thriller=心理驚悚片 || The thriller was ___ exciting that I couldn't stop watching. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這部驚悚片刺激到我停不下來。
thrive || v. || 茁壯成長；興盛 || Plants thrive in sunlight. || 植物在陽光下茁壯成長。 || thrive on=以…為樂 || Some people thrive ___ pressure. || on|in|at || thrive on = 在…下表現更好。 || 有些人在壓力下反而表現更好。
throne || n. || 王位 || The prince will take the throne. || 王子將繼承王位。 || take the throne=登基 || The queen sat ___ the throne for 70 years. || on|in|at || sit on the throne = 在位。 || 那位女王在位七十年。
thrust || v./n. || 猛推；刺 || He thrust the money into my hand. || 他把錢塞進我手裡。 || thrust A into B=把 A 塞進 B || He thrust the letter ___ my hand and ran away. || into|onto|from || thrust A into B = 把 A 塞進 B。 || 他把信塞進我手裡就跑了。
tick || n./v. || 滴答聲；打勾 || Tick the correct box. || 在正確的方框打勾。 || tick the box=打勾 || Please tick the box ___ you agree. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你同意，請打勾。
tile || n. || 磁磚 || The bathroom floor has white tiles. || 浴室地板鋪著白色磁磚。 || floor tile=地磚 || The floor is covered ___ white tiles. || with|of|by || be covered with = 覆蓋著…。 || 地板鋪著白色磁磚。
tin || n. || 錫；罐頭 || We opened a tin of tuna. || 我們打開一罐鮪魚。 || a tin of=一罐… || Can you pass me a tin ___ beans? || of|for|with || a tin of = 一罐…。 || 你可以遞給我一罐豆子嗎？
toll || n. || 通行費；死傷人數 || The death toll rose to 100. || 死亡人數增加到一百人。 || death toll=死亡人數 || The death toll from the earthquake ___ risen to 200. || has|have|is || 主詞 The death toll 單數，用 has。 || 地震的死亡人數已上升到兩百人。
torch || n. || 火炬；手電筒 || The runner carried the Olympic torch. || 跑者手持奧運聖火。 || Olympic torch=奧運聖火 || The Olympic torch ___ carried through many cities. || was|were|is || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 奧運聖火被傳遞經過許多城市。
torment || n./v. || 折磨 || He was tormented by guilt. || 他受罪惡感折磨。 || in torment=痛苦中 || He was tormented ___ guilt after the accident. || by|at|for || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 意外發生後，他飽受罪惡感折磨。
tournament || n. || 錦標賽 || Our team won the tournament. || 我們隊贏得了錦標賽。 || tennis tournament=網球錦標賽 || Sixteen teams took part ___ the tournament. || in|on|at || take part in = 參加…。 || 十六支隊伍參加了這次錦標賽。
toxic || adj. || 有毒的 || These chemicals are toxic. || 這些化學物質有毒。 || toxic waste=有毒廢料 || Some plants are toxic ___ dogs and cats. || to|at|with || be toxic to = 對…有毒。 || 有些植物對貓狗有毒。
trait || n. || 特質 || Kindness is her best trait. || 善良是她最好的特質。 || personality trait=人格特質 || Honesty is a trait ___ I admire in people. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 admire 的受詞。 || 誠實是我欣賞的人格特質。
traitor || n. || 叛徒 || He was called a traitor. || 他被稱為叛徒。 || traitor to=…的叛徒 || He was seen as a traitor ___ his country. || to|of|for || a traitor to = …的叛徒。 || 他被視為國家的叛徒。
transaction || n. || 交易 || The bank charges a fee for each transaction. || 銀行對每筆交易收取手續費。 || online transaction=線上交易 || The bank charges a small fee ___ each transaction. || for|of|to || fee for = …的費用。 || 銀行每筆交易收取少許手續費。
transformation || n. || 轉變 || The city has gone through a transformation. || 這座城市經歷了轉變。 || complete transformation=徹底改變 || The old factory went through a complete transformation ___ an art center. || into|onto|from || transformation into = 轉變成…。 || 舊工廠徹底改造成藝術中心。
transit || n. || 運輸；過境 || The package was lost in transit. || 包裹在運送途中遺失了。 || public transit=大眾運輸 || The package was damaged ___ transit. || in|on|at || in transit = 運送途中。 || 包裹在運送途中受損了。
transition || n. || 過渡；轉變 || The transition from high school to college is hard. || 從高中到大學的過渡期很辛苦。 || transition period=過渡期 || The transition ___ high school to college can be difficult. || from|of|in || from A to B = 從 A 到 B。 || 從高中到大學的轉變可能很困難。
transmission || n. || 傳播；傳送 || Wash your hands to stop the transmission of disease. || 洗手以阻止疾病傳播。 || disease transmission=疾病傳播 || Masks can reduce the transmission ___ the virus. || of|for|with || the transmission of = …的傳播。 || 口罩可以減少病毒傳播。
transparent || adj. || 透明的 || The glass is transparent. || 玻璃是透明的。 || transparent process=透明的過程 || The government should be more transparent ___ how it spends money. || about|to|for || be transparent about = 對…公開透明。 || 政府應該對如何花錢更加透明。
trauma || n. || 創傷 || She suffered trauma after the accident. || 她在事故後受到創傷。 || emotional trauma=情緒創傷 || It took her years to recover ___ the trauma. || from|of|with || recover from = 從…中恢復。 || 她花了好幾年才從創傷中恢復。
treaty || n. || 條約 || The two countries signed a peace treaty. || 兩國簽署了和平條約。 || peace treaty=和平條約 || The peace treaty ___ signed in 1951. || was|were|is || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 和平條約在 1951 年簽署。
tribute || n. || 致敬 || The concert was a tribute to the singer. || 這場音樂會是向那位歌手致敬。 || pay tribute to=向…致敬 || The fans paid tribute ___ the late singer. || to|for|with || pay tribute to = 向…致敬。 || 粉絲們向已故的歌手致敬。
trigger || v./n. || 引發；扳機 || Stress can trigger headaches. || 壓力可能引發頭痛。 || trigger an allergy=引起過敏 || Dust can trigger an allergy ___ some people. || in|on|at || in + 人 = 在…身上。 || 灰塵可能引起某些人過敏。
trim || v./n. || 修剪 || I need to trim my hair. || 我需要修剪頭髮。 || trim the hedge=修剪樹籬 || I'd like to get my hair trimmed ___ a little. || just|only|very || just a little = 只要一點點。 || 我想把頭髮稍微修一下就好。
triple || adj./v. || 三倍的 || Prices have tripled. || 價格漲了三倍。 || triple the size=三倍大 || The price of the house has tripled ___ 2010. || since|for|from || since + 時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 這棟房子的價格從 2010 年以來漲了三倍。
trivial || adj. || 瑣碎的 || Don't worry about trivial things. || 不要擔心瑣碎的事。 || trivial matter=小事 || They argued ___ something trivial. || about|to|for || argue about = 為…爭吵。 || 他們為了一件小事吵架。
trophy || n. || 獎盃 || Our team won the trophy. || 我們隊贏得獎盃。 || win a trophy=贏得獎盃 || The trophy ___ our team won is in the school hall. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 won 的受詞。 || 我們隊贏得的獎盃放在學校大廳。
tuition || n. || 學費 || College tuition is expensive. || 大學學費很貴。 || tuition fees=學費 || She works part-time ___ pay her tuition. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她打工來付學費。
tumor || n. || 腫瘤 || The doctor found a tumor. || 醫生發現了一顆腫瘤。 || brain tumor=腦瘤 || The tumor ___ removed in a two-hour operation. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 腫瘤在兩小時的手術中被切除了。
tuna || n. || 鮪魚 || I had a tuna sandwich for lunch. || 我午餐吃了鮪魚三明治。 || tuna sandwich=鮪魚三明治 || I'd like a tuna sandwich ___ a cup of tea. || and|or|but || 兩者並列用 and。 || 我想要一份鮪魚三明治和一杯茶。
ultimate || adj. || 最終的；極致的 || Our ultimate goal is to win. || 我們的最終目標是贏。 || ultimate goal=最終目標 || My ultimate goal is ___ become a doctor. || to|for|of || to V 當主詞補語。 || 我的最終目標是成為醫生。
uncover || v. || 揭露 || The reporter uncovered the truth. || 記者揭露了真相。 || uncover the truth=揭露真相 || The truth was finally uncovered ___ a young reporter. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 真相終於被一位年輕記者揭露。
undergo || v. || 經歷；接受 || He will undergo surgery next week. || 他下週要接受手術。 || undergo surgery=接受手術 || He has to undergo surgery ___ his knee. || on|in|at || surgery on = 針對…的手術。 || 他必須接受膝蓋手術。

underline || v. || 在…下面畫線；強調 || Underline the important words. || 把重要的字畫底線。 || underline the key words=在關鍵字下畫線 || Please underline the words ___ you don't know. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 know 的受詞。 || 請把你不認識的字畫底線。
