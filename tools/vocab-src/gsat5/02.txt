# 學測 L5 02：architect – boxer
architect || n. || 建築師 || The architect designed the museum. || 那位建築師設計了這座博物館。 || famous architect=知名建築師 || Taipei 101 ___ designed by a Taiwanese architect. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 台北 101 是由一位台灣建築師設計的。
architecture || n. || 建築；建築學 || I'm interested in architecture. || 我對建築有興趣。 || modern architecture=現代建築 || The city is famous ___ its modern architecture. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 這座城市以現代建築聞名。
arena || n. || 競技場 || The concert was held in a big arena. || 演唱會在一座大型場館舉行。 || sports arena=體育館 || The arena can hold more ___ 15,000 people. || than|then|as || more than = 超過。 || 這座場館可容納超過一萬五千人。
arouse || v. || 引起；激起 || The news aroused public interest. || 這則新聞引起大眾的興趣。 || arouse interest=引起興趣 || The movie aroused my interest ___ history. || in|on|for || interest in = 對…的興趣。 || 這部電影激起了我對歷史的興趣。
array || n. || 一系列 || The shop sells a wide array of goods. || 這家店販售各式各樣的商品。 || a wide array of=各式各樣的 || The market offers a wide array ___ fresh fruit. || of|for|in || an array of = 一系列的…。 || 市場提供各式各樣的新鮮水果。
arrogant || adj. || 傲慢的 || He is too arrogant to listen to others. || 他太傲慢，聽不進別人的話。 || arrogant attitude=傲慢的態度 || He is ___ arrogant to admit his mistakes. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不會…。 || 他太傲慢了，不肯承認錯誤。
articulate || v./adj. || 清楚表達；口齒清晰的 || She is very articulate. || 她口才很好。 || articulate one's ideas=清楚表達想法 || It's hard for young children ___ articulate their feelings. || to|for|of || It is hard for sb + to V。 || 幼童很難清楚表達自己的感受。
ass || n. || 驢子；傻瓜 || Don't make an ass of yourself. || 別讓自己出醜。 || make an ass of oneself=出洋相 || He made ___ ass of himself at the party. || an|a|the || ass 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 他在派對上出盡洋相。
assault || n./v. || 攻擊；襲擊 || He was arrested for assault. || 他因傷害罪被捕。 || physical assault=肢體攻擊 || The man was arrested ___ assault. || for|of|with || arrest sb for + 罪名。 || 那名男子因傷害罪被捕。
assert || v. || 主張；斷言 || He asserted that he was innocent. || 他堅稱自己是無辜的。 || assert oneself=堅持己見 || The man asserted ___ he had never seen the stolen car. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 asserted 的受詞。 || 那名男子堅稱他從未見過那輛贓車。
asset || n. || 資產 || She is a great asset to the team. || 她是團隊的一大資產。 || valuable asset=寶貴的資產 || Being able to speak English is an asset ___ any job. || in|on|of || an asset in = 在…方面的優勢。 || 會說英文在任何工作中都是一項優勢。
assumption || n. || 假設 || Your assumption is wrong. || 你的假設是錯的。 || make an assumption=做假設 || Don't make assumptions ___ people based on their looks. || about|to|for || assumptions about = 對…的假設。 || 不要根據外表對人做出假設。
athletics || n. || 田徑運動 || She is good at athletics. || 她擅長田徑。 || athletics team=田徑隊 || Athletics ___ running, jumping, and throwing events. || includes|include|including || athletics 視為單數，動詞加 s。 || 田徑包括跑、跳和投擲項目。
attendance || n. || 出席 || Attendance at the meeting is required. || 會議必須出席。 || perfect attendance=全勤 || Attendance ___ all classes is required. || at|in|on || attendance at = 出席…。 || 所有課程都必須出席。
attic || n. || 閣樓 || We keep old things in the attic. || 我們把舊東西放在閣樓。 || in the attic=在閣樓 || We found some old photos ___ the attic. || in|on|at || 在閣樓裡用 in。 || 我們在閣樓裡找到了一些舊照片。
attorney || n. || 律師 || He hired an attorney. || 他請了一位律師。 || defense attorney=辯護律師 || You should talk to an attorney ___ you sign the contract. || before|after|since || 簽約「之前」諮詢，用 before。 || 簽約之前你應該先諮詢律師。
attribute || v./n. || 歸因於；特質 || She attributes her success to hard work. || 她把成功歸功於努力。 || attribute A to B=把 A 歸因於 B || He attributes his good health ___ regular exercise. || to|for|with || attribute A to B = 把 A 歸因於 B。 || 他把自己的健康歸功於規律運動。
auction || n./v. || 拍賣 || The painting was sold at auction. || 這幅畫在拍賣會上售出。 || at auction=在拍賣會上 || The painting was sold ___ auction for one million dollars. || at|in|on || at auction = 在拍賣會上。 || 這幅畫在拍賣會上以一百萬美元售出。
authorize || v. || 授權 || Only managers can authorize payments. || 只有經理能核准付款。 || be authorized to V=被授權做… || Only authorized people ___ allowed to enter this room. || are|is|be || 被動語態；people 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 只有經授權的人才能進入這個房間。
autonomy || n. || 自主權 || Students should have some autonomy in learning. || 學生在學習上應該有一些自主權。 || personal autonomy=個人自主 || Teenagers want more autonomy ___ their parents. || from|of|with || autonomy from = 不受…控制的自主權。 || 青少年希望能更不受父母控制。
awe || n. || 敬畏 || We looked at the mountains in awe. || 我們敬畏地望著群山。 || in awe=敬畏地 || The children watched the fireworks ___ awe. || in|with|by || in awe = 敬畏地、驚嘆地。 || 孩子們驚嘆地看著煙火。
backyard || n. || 後院 || The kids are playing in the backyard. || 孩子們在後院玩。 || in the backyard=在後院 || We had a barbecue ___ the backyard last night. || in|on|at || 在院子裡用 in。 || 我們昨晚在後院烤肉。
ballot || n. || 選票 || Voters cast their ballots. || 選民投下他們的選票。 || cast a ballot=投票 || Voters must put their ballots ___ the box. || in|on|at || 放進箱子裡用 in。 || 選民必須把選票投進票箱。
ban || v./n. || 禁止 || Smoking is banned in restaurants. || 餐廳內禁止吸菸。 || ban on=對…的禁令 || The city introduced a ban ___ plastic bags. || on|in|for || a ban on = 對…的禁令。 || 市政府實施了塑膠袋禁令。
banner || n. || 橫幅 || They held up a banner. || 他們舉起了布條。 || welcome banner=歡迎布條 || The students hung a banner ___ the school gate. || over|under|through || over = 在…上方。 || 學生們在校門上方掛了一條布條。
barren || adj. || 貧瘠的 || The land is barren. || 這塊土地很貧瘠。 || barren land=荒地 || Nothing can grow ___ this barren land. || on|in|at || 在土地上用 on。 || 這片貧瘠的土地上什麼都長不出來。
batch || n. || 一批 || She baked a batch of cookies. || 她烤了一批餅乾。 || a batch of=一批… || The first batch ___ cookies was burned. || of|for|in || a batch of = 一批…。 || 第一批餅乾烤焦了。
behalf || n. || 代表 || I'm writing on behalf of my class. || 我代表全班寫信。 || on behalf of=代表… || She accepted the award ___ behalf of her team. || on|in|at || on behalf of = 代表…。 || 她代表她的團隊領獎。
belongings || n. || 財物 || Don't leave your belongings unattended. || 請勿將個人物品置於無人看管處。 || personal belongings=個人物品 || Please take all your belongings with you ___ you leave the train. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 下車時請帶走所有隨身物品。
beloved || adj./n. || 心愛的 || She lost her beloved dog. || 她失去了心愛的狗。 || beloved pet=心愛的寵物 || The singer is beloved ___ millions of fans. || by|with|to || be beloved by = 受…喜愛。 || 那位歌手受到數百萬粉絲的喜愛。
beneficial || adj. || 有益的 || Exercise is beneficial to health. || 運動對健康有益。 || be beneficial to=對…有益 || Reading is beneficial ___ children's development. || to|for|with || be beneficial to = 對…有益。 || 閱讀對孩子的發展有益。
betray || v. || 背叛 || He betrayed his friend. || 他背叛了他的朋友。 || betray sb's trust=辜負某人的信任 || She felt hurt ___ her best friend betrayed her. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 她因為最好的朋友背叛她而感到受傷。
beware || v. || 小心 || Beware of the dog. || 小心有狗。 || beware of=小心… || Beware ___ pickpockets in crowded places. || of|from|with || beware of = 小心…。 || 在擁擠的地方要小心扒手。
bias || n. || 偏見 || The judge showed no bias. || 法官毫無偏見。 || bias against=對…的偏見 || We should not have any bias ___ people from other countries. || against|to|with || bias against = 對…的偏見。 || 我們不應對其他國家的人有任何偏見。
bid || n./v. || 出價；努力 || He made a bid for the painting. || 他為那幅畫出了價。 || make a bid=出價 || He made a bid ___ the old car at the auction. || for|of|to || a bid for = 對…的出價。 || 他在拍賣會上對那輛老車出價。
biological || adj. || 生物的 || She is studying biological science. || 她在讀生物科學。 || biological parents=親生父母 || He was raised by his grandparents, not by his biological ___. || parents|parent's|parenting || biological parents = 親生父母（複數）。 || 他是由祖父母撫養長大，而不是親生父母。
bizarre || adj. || 古怪的 || That's a bizarre story. || 那是個古怪的故事。 || bizarre behavior=怪異的行為 || His behavior was ___ bizarre that everyone stared at him. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他的行為古怪到大家都盯著他看。
blast || n./v. || 爆炸；強風 || There was a loud blast. || 傳來一聲巨大的爆炸聲。 || have a blast=玩得很開心 || We had a blast ___ the party last night. || at|in|on || at the party = 在派對上。 || 我們昨晚在派對上玩得超開心。
blur || n./v. || 模糊 || Everything became a blur. || 一切都變得模糊了。 || blurred vision=視力模糊 || Without my glasses, everything is ___ blur. || a|an|the || blur 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 沒有眼鏡，一切都是模糊一片。
blush || v./n. || 臉紅 || She blushed when he smiled at her. || 他對她微笑時，她臉紅了。 || blush with=因…而臉紅 || She blushed ___ embarrassment. || with|in|by || blush with = 因…而臉紅。 || 她尷尬得臉紅了。
bodyguard || n. || 保鑣 || The singer has two bodyguards. || 那位歌手有兩名保鑣。 || personal bodyguard=私人保鑣 || The president was protected ___ his bodyguards. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 總統受到保鑣保護。
bolt || n./v. || 門閂；閃電 || He bolted the door. || 他閂上了門。 || a bolt of lightning=一道閃電 || The news came like a bolt ___ the blue. || from|of|in || a bolt from the blue = 晴天霹靂。 || 這個消息就像晴天霹靂。
bonus || n. || 獎金 || Workers get a bonus at the end of the year. || 員工年底有獎金。 || year-end bonus=年終獎金 || All the workers received a bonus ___ their hard work. || for|of|with || for + 名詞 = 因為…。 || 所有員工都因努力工作而獲得獎金。
boom || n./v. || 繁榮；隆隆聲 || There was a boom in online shopping. || 網路購物大幅成長。 || baby boom=嬰兒潮 || There has been a boom ___ online shopping in recent years. || in|of|for || a boom in = …的激增。 || 近年來網路購物大幅成長。
boost || v./n. || 提升 || Exercise can boost your energy. || 運動能提升你的活力。 || boost confidence=增強信心 || Winning the contest boosted her confidence, ___ she decided to enter another one. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 贏得比賽增強了她的信心，所以她決定再參加另一場。
booth || n. || 攤位；亭 || There is a phone booth on the corner. || 轉角有一個電話亭。 || phone booth=電話亭 || The company set up a booth ___ the trade show. || at|on|to || at + 活動場所。 || 這家公司在商展上設了一個攤位。
boredom || n. || 無聊 || He played games out of boredom. || 他因為無聊而打電動。 || out of boredom=出於無聊 || He started drawing out ___ boredom. || of|from|for || out of + 名詞 = 出於…。 || 他出於無聊開始畫畫。
bound || v./n. || 跳躍；界限 || The dog bounded across the field. || 狗跳躍著跑過草地。 || out of bounds=禁區 || This area is out ___ bounds for students. || of|from|off || out of bounds = 禁止進入的。 || 這個區域禁止學生進入。
boundary || n. || 邊界 || The river forms the boundary between the two towns. || 這條河是兩鎮的邊界。 || national boundary=國界 || The river forms the boundary ___ the two cities. || between|among|of || 兩者之間用 between。 || 這條河是兩座城市的邊界。
boxer || n. || 拳擊手 || He is a professional boxer. || 他是職業拳擊手。 || professional boxer=職業拳擊手 || The boxer ___ won the match is only 19. || who|which|whom || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 贏得比賽的拳擊手才十九歲。
