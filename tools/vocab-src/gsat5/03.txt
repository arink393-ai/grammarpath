# 學測 L5 03：breakthrough – collective
breakthrough || n. || 突破 || Scientists made a breakthrough in cancer research. || 科學家在癌症研究上有了突破。 || make a breakthrough=取得突破 || Scientists have made a major breakthrough ___ cancer research. || in|on|at || a breakthrough in = 在…方面的突破。 || 科學家在癌症研究上取得重大突破。
briefcase || n. || 公事包 || He carries a black briefcase to work. || 他帶著黑色公事包上班。 || leather briefcase=皮製公事包 || He left his briefcase ___ the taxi. || in|on|at || 在計程車裡用 in。 || 他把公事包忘在計程車上了。
bronze || n./adj. || 青銅；銅牌 || She won a bronze medal. || 她贏得一面銅牌。 || bronze medal=銅牌 || She won a bronze medal ___ the 2024 Olympics. || at|on|to || at + 賽事 = 在…比賽中。 || 她在 2024 年奧運贏得一面銅牌。
browse || v. || 瀏覽 || I like to browse in bookstores. || 我喜歡在書店裡隨意瀏覽。 || browse the Internet=上網瀏覽 || I spent the afternoon browsing ___ the bookstore. || in|on|to || 在書店裡用 in。 || 我整個下午都在書店裡瀏覽。
bruise || n./v. || 瘀青 || He had a bruise on his arm. || 他手臂上有瘀青。 || bruise easily=容易瘀青 || He fell off his bike and got a bruise ___ his knee. || on|in|at || 身體表面的傷用 on。 || 他從腳踏車上摔下來，膝蓋瘀青了。
bulk || n. || 大量；大部分 || We buy rice in bulk. || 我們大量採購米。 || in bulk=大量地 || The bulk of the work ___ done by volunteers. || was|were|be || the bulk of + 不可數名詞，動詞用單數；被動 was + p.p.。 || 大部分的工作是由志工完成的。
bully || v./n. || 霸凌；惡霸 || He was bullied at school. || 他在學校被霸凌。 || cyberbully=網路霸凌 || No student should be bullied ___ his or her classmates. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 沒有學生應該被同學霸凌。
bureau || n. || 局；處 || She works for the tourism bureau. || 她在觀光局工作。 || tourism bureau=觀光局 || You can get free maps ___ the tourism bureau. || from|of|to || get sth from + 地方 = 從…取得。 || 你可以從觀光局拿到免費地圖。
bureaucracy || n. || 官僚體制 || There is too much bureaucracy in the government. || 政府裡有太多官僚作風。 || cut bureaucracy=減少官僚程序 || Many people complain ___ the bureaucracy of the system. || about|to|for || complain about = 抱怨…。 || 許多人抱怨這套制度的官僚作風。
burial || n. || 埋葬 || The burial took place on Sunday. || 葬禮在週日舉行。 || burial ground=墓地 || The burial will take place ___ Sunday morning. || on|in|at || 特定某天的早上用 on。 || 下葬將在週日早上舉行。
butcher || n. || 肉販 || I bought some pork from the butcher. || 我向肉販買了一些豬肉。 || butcher's shop=肉舖 || I bought some beef ___ the butcher's shop. || at|on|to || at + 商店 = 在…店。 || 我在肉舖買了一些牛肉。
calcium || n. || 鈣 || Milk is rich in calcium. || 牛奶富含鈣質。 || calcium intake=鈣攝取量 || Milk is rich ___ calcium. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 牛奶富含鈣質。
canal || n. || 運河 || The Panama Canal connects two oceans. || 巴拿馬運河連接兩大洋。 || canal boat=運河船 || We took a boat ride ___ the canal in Venice. || along|among|between || along = 沿著…。 || 我們在威尼斯沿著運河搭船遊覽。
canvas || n. || 帆布；畫布 || The artist painted on canvas. || 畫家在畫布上作畫。 || canvas bag=帆布包 || The artist painted a beautiful sunset ___ canvas. || on|in|at || 畫在畫布上用 on。 || 畫家在畫布上畫了一幅美麗的夕陽。
capability || n. || 能力 || The robot has the capability to learn. || 這個機器人有學習的能力。 || beyond one's capability=超出能力 || This task is beyond my capability, ___ I need your help. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 這件工作超出我的能力，所以我需要你的幫忙。
carbon || n. || 碳 || We should reduce carbon emissions. || 我們應該減少碳排放。 || carbon footprint=碳足跡 || Riding a bike is a good way ___ reduce your carbon footprint. || to|for|of || a way to V = 做…的方法。 || 騎腳踏車是減少碳足跡的好方法。
carnival || n. || 嘉年華會 || The town holds a carnival every summer. || 這個小鎮每年夏天舉辦嘉年華。 || summer carnival=夏日嘉年華 || We had a great time ___ the carnival last weekend. || at|on|to || at + 活動 = 在…。 || 我們上週末在嘉年華玩得很開心。
casino || n. || 賭場 || He lost all his money at the casino. || 他在賭場輸光了所有的錢。 || casino resort=賭場度假村 || He lost all his money ___ the casino. || at|on|to || at + 地點 = 在…。 || 他在賭場輸光了所有的錢。
cathedral || n. || 大教堂 || The cathedral was built 500 years ago. || 這座大教堂建於五百年前。 || old cathedral=古老的大教堂 || The cathedral, ___ was built in the 1500s, attracts many tourists. || which|that|who || 非限定關係子句修飾物，只能用 which。 || 這座建於十六世紀的大教堂吸引許多觀光客。
caution || n. || 謹慎；警告 || Drive with caution in the rain. || 雨天開車要小心。 || with caution=謹慎地 || Please drive ___ caution on rainy days. || with|by|in || with caution = 謹慎地。 || 雨天請小心駕駛。
cautious || adj. || 謹慎的 || Be cautious when crossing the street. || 過馬路時要小心。 || be cautious about=對…謹慎 || You should be cautious ___ giving out personal information online. || about|to|for || be cautious about = 對…謹慎。 || 你在網路上提供個人資料時應該謹慎。
celebrity || n. || 名人 || Many celebrities came to the party. || 許多名人來參加派對。 || famous celebrity=知名人士 || The celebrity ___ photos were posted online was very angry. || whose|who|which || whose + 名詞 表示「…的」。 || 那位照片被上傳到網路的名人非常生氣。
cemetery || n. || 墓地 || His grandfather is buried in this cemetery. || 他祖父葬在這座墓園。 || public cemetery=公墓 || We visit my grandfather's grave ___ the cemetery every year. || in|on|to || 在墓園裡用 in。 || 我們每年去墓園掃祖父的墓。
ceremony || n. || 典禮 || The wedding ceremony was beautiful. || 婚禮儀式很美。 || opening ceremony=開幕典禮 || The graduation ceremony will be held ___ June 20. || on|in|at || 特定日期用 on。 || 畢業典禮將在六月二十日舉行。
certainty || n. || 確定性 || I can't say with certainty. || 我無法確定地說。 || with certainty=確定地 || No one can predict the future ___ certainty. || with|by|in || with certainty = 確定地。 || 沒有人能確定地預測未來。
certificate || n. || 證書 || She received a certificate for finishing the course. || 她完成課程後獲得證書。 || birth certificate=出生證明 || You will receive a certificate ___ you finish the course. || after|before|until || 完成課程「之後」獲得，用 after。 || 完成課程後你會拿到證書。
chaos || n. || 混亂 || The city was in chaos after the earthquake. || 地震後城市一片混亂。 || in chaos=陷入混亂 || The city was ___ chaos after the typhoon. || in|on|at || in chaos = 陷入混亂。 || 颱風過後城市一片混亂。
chapel || n. || 小教堂 || They got married in a small chapel. || 他們在一間小教堂結婚。 || wedding chapel=婚禮教堂 || They got married ___ a small chapel by the sea. || in|on|to || 在建築物裡用 in。 || 他們在海邊一間小教堂結婚。
characterize || v. || 以…為特徵 || The city is characterized by its old buildings. || 這座城市以古老建築為特色。 || be characterized by=以…為特色 || Taiwan's summers are characterized ___ heat and heavy rain. || by|with|of || be characterized by = 以…為特徵。 || 台灣的夏天以炎熱和大雨為特色。
chef || n. || 主廚 || The chef cooked a great meal. || 主廚煮了一頓美味的餐點。 || head chef=主廚 || My dream is ___ become a chef. || to|for|of || to V 當主詞補語。 || 我的夢想是成為一名主廚。
choir || n. || 合唱團 || She sings in the school choir. || 她在學校合唱團唱歌。 || church choir=教會合唱團 || She has sung in the school choir ___ she was ten. || since|for|from || since + 時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 她從十歲起就在學校合唱團唱歌。
chord || n. || 和弦 || He played a chord on the guitar. || 他在吉他上彈了一個和弦。 || guitar chord=吉他和弦 || It took me a week ___ learn this guitar chord. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 我花了一個星期學會這個吉他和弦。
chore || n. || 家務雜事 || I help with the chores at home. || 我在家幫忙做家事。 || household chores=家事 || Everyone in my family helps ___ the household chores. || with|on|for || help with = 幫忙…。 || 我家每個人都幫忙做家事。
chronic || adj. || 慢性的 || He suffers from chronic back pain. || 他有慢性背痛。 || chronic disease=慢性病 || My grandfather suffers ___ a chronic disease. || from|of|with || suffer from = 罹患…。 || 我祖父罹患一種慢性病。
chubby || adj. || 圓胖的 || The baby has chubby cheeks. || 寶寶有胖嘟嘟的臉頰。 || chubby cheeks=胖嘟嘟的臉頰 || The baby ___ chubby cheeks is my nephew. || with|in|of || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 那個臉頰胖嘟嘟的寶寶是我外甥。
chunk || n. || 一大塊 || He cut the meat into chunks. || 他把肉切成大塊。 || a chunk of=一大塊… || A big chunk ___ my salary goes to rent. || of|for|in || a chunk of = 一大塊／一大部分的…。 || 我薪水的一大部分都花在房租上。
circuit || n. || 電路；巡迴 || The circuit is broken. || 電路壞了。 || electric circuit=電路 || The lights went out ___ the circuit was overloaded. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 因為電路超載，燈熄滅了。
cite || v. || 引用 || She cited a famous poem in her speech. || 她在演講中引用了一首名詩。 || cite an example=舉例 || You must cite the sources ___ you used in your report. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 used 的受詞。 || 你必須在報告中註明所使用的資料來源。
citizenship || n. || 公民身分 || He applied for citizenship. || 他申請了公民身分。 || apply for citizenship=申請公民身分 || He applied ___ Canadian citizenship last year. || for|to|with || apply for = 申請…。 || 他去年申請了加拿大公民身分。
civic || adj. || 公民的；市民的 || Voting is a civic duty. || 投票是公民的責任。 || civic duty=公民義務 || Voting is a civic duty ___ every citizen should take seriously. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 take 的受詞。 || 投票是每個公民都應該認真看待的義務。
clarity || n. || 清晰 || The teacher explained the rule with clarity. || 老師清楚地解釋了規則。 || with clarity=清楚地 || The teacher explained the grammar rule ___ great clarity. || with|by|in || with + 抽象名詞 = 副詞。 || 老師非常清楚地解釋了文法規則。
clause || n. || 子句；條款 || This sentence has two clauses. || 這個句子有兩個子句。 || relative clause=關係子句 || A relative clause gives more information ___ a noun. || about|to|for || information about = 關於…的資訊。 || 關係子句提供名詞的更多資訊。
cling || v. || 緊抓；黏住 || Wet clothes cling to your body. || 濕衣服會黏在身上。 || cling to=緊抓… || The frightened child tried ___ cling to his mother. || to|for|of || try to + 原形動詞。 || 受驚的孩子想緊抓著媽媽。
clinical || adj. || 臨床的 || The new drug is in clinical trials. || 這種新藥正在進行臨床試驗。 || clinical trial=臨床試驗 || The new drug is still ___ clinical trials. || in|on|at || in trials = 試驗中。 || 這種新藥還在臨床試驗中。
cluster || n./v. || 群；串 || A cluster of grapes hung from the vine. || 一串葡萄掛在藤上。 || a cluster of=一群／一串… || A cluster of stars ___ shining in the night sky. || was|were|be || a cluster of + 複數名詞，主詞是 cluster，用單數 was。 || 一群星星在夜空中閃耀。
cocaine || n. || 古柯鹼 || Cocaine is an illegal drug. || 古柯鹼是一種非法毒品。 || cocaine use=吸食古柯鹼 || The man was arrested ___ selling cocaine. || for|of|with || arrest sb for + 罪名。 || 那名男子因販賣古柯鹼被捕。
coffin || n. || 棺材 || The coffin was carried into the church. || 棺木被抬進教堂。 || wooden coffin=木棺 || The coffin ___ carried slowly into the church. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 棺木被緩緩抬進教堂。
cognitive || adj. || 認知的 || Reading improves cognitive skills. || 閱讀能提升認知能力。 || cognitive development=認知發展 || Playing chess can help improve children's cognitive ___. || skills|skill's|skilled || cognitive skills = 認知能力（複數名詞）。 || 下棋有助於提升孩子的認知能力。
coherent || adj. || 連貫的；條理清楚的 || Your essay is not very coherent. || 你的文章不太連貫。 || coherent argument=條理分明的論點 || A good essay should be clear ___ coherent. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的形容詞用 and。 || 好文章應該清楚而且連貫。
coincidence || n. || 巧合 || What a coincidence! || 好巧啊！ || by coincidence=巧合地 || ___ coincidence, we were born on the same day. || By|In|With || by coincidence = 巧合地。 || 巧合的是，我們同一天出生。
collaboration || n. || 合作 || The project was a collaboration between two schools. || 這個計畫是兩校合作的成果。 || in collaboration with=與…合作 || The book was written in collaboration ___ a famous scientist. || with|to|for || in collaboration with = 與…合作。 || 這本書是與一位知名科學家合作撰寫的。
collective || adj. || 集體的 || It was a collective decision. || 那是一個集體決定。 || collective effort=集體努力 || The project succeeded ___ the collective effort of the whole class. || because of|because|although || because of + 名詞片語。 || 這個計畫因為全班的集體努力而成功。
