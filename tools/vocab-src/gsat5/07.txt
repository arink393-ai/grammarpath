# 學測 L5 07：distinction – estate
distinction || n. || 區別；卓越 || There is a clear distinction between the two. || 兩者之間有明顯區別。 || make a distinction=區分; with distinction=優異地 || It is important to make a distinction ___ facts and opinions. || between|among|with || between A and B = A 和 B 之間。 || 區分事實和意見很重要。
distinctive || adj. || 獨特的 || She has a distinctive voice. || 她有獨特的嗓音。 || distinctive feature=顯著特徵 || The building is easy to recognize ___ its distinctive shape. || because of|because|although || because of + 名詞片語。 || 這棟建築因為獨特的外形而容易辨認。
distract || v. || 使分心 || Don't distract me while I'm studying. || 我讀書時別讓我分心。 || distract sb from=使某人從…分心 || Phones can distract students ___ their studies. || from|of|with || distract sb from sth = 使某人從某事分心。 || 手機會讓學生無法專心讀書。
doctrine || n. || 教條；學說 || The church teaches its doctrine. || 教會傳授其教義。 || religious doctrine=宗教教義 || The doctrine was taught to everyone ___ joined the church. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 每個加入教會的人都被傳授這套教義。
document || n./v. || 文件；記錄 || Please sign this document. || 請在這份文件上簽名。 || official document=正式文件 || Please read the document carefully ___ you sign it. || before|after|since || 簽名「之前」先讀，用 before。 || 簽名之前請仔細閱讀這份文件。
documentary || n. || 紀錄片 || We watched a documentary about whales. || 我們看了一部關於鯨魚的紀錄片。 || nature documentary=自然紀錄片 || I watched a documentary ___ climate change last night. || about|for|to || about = 關於…。 || 我昨晚看了一部關於氣候變遷的紀錄片。
domain || n. || 領域；網域 || Science is not my domain. || 科學不是我的專長領域。 || public domain=公共領域 || This website's domain name ends ___ ".tw". || with|by|on || end with = 以…結尾。 || 這個網站的網域名稱以「.tw」結尾。
dome || n. || 圓頂 || The church has a golden dome. || 那座教堂有金色的圓頂。 || glass dome=玻璃圓頂 || The building ___ a glass dome is the new library. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 那棟有玻璃圓頂的建築是新圖書館。
donate || v. || 捐贈 || She donated money to the hospital. || 她捐錢給醫院。 || donate blood=捐血 || He donates blood ___ three months. || every|each|all || every + 數字 + 複數名詞 = 每隔…。 || 他每三個月捐一次血。
donation || n. || 捐款 || Thank you for your donation. || 謝謝你的捐款。 || make a donation=捐款 || They made a donation ___ the children's hospital. || to|for|with || make a donation to = 捐款給…。 || 他們捐了一筆錢給兒童醫院。
donor || n. || 捐贈者 || He is a blood donor. || 他是捐血者。 || organ donor=器官捐贈者 || The hospital thanked the donor ___ gift saved many lives. || whose|who|which || whose + 名詞 表示「…的」。 || 醫院感謝那位捐贈者，他的捐贈救了許多人。
doorway || n. || 門口 || She stood in the doorway. || 她站在門口。 || in the doorway=在門口 || A tall man was standing ___ the doorway. || in|on|to || 站在門口用 in the doorway。 || 一個高個子男人站在門口。
dough || n. || 麵團 || She kneaded the dough. || 她揉麵團。 || pizza dough=披薩麵團 || Leave the dough ___ rise for an hour. || to|for|in || leave sth to V = 讓某物…。 || 讓麵團發酵一個小時。
dreadful || adj. || 可怕的；糟透的 || The weather was dreadful. || 天氣糟透了。 || dreadful mistake=可怕的錯誤 || The food was ___ dreadful that we left early. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 食物糟到我們提早離開了。
driveway || n. || 車道 || He parked the car in the driveway. || 他把車停在車道上。 || in the driveway=在車道上 || Please don't park your car ___ my driveway. || in|at|to || in the driveway = 在車道上。 || 請不要把車停在我家車道上。
drought || n. || 乾旱 || The drought killed many crops. || 乾旱使許多農作物死亡。 || severe drought=嚴重乾旱 || The farmers suffered a lot ___ the drought. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 乾旱期間農夫們吃了很多苦。
ecological || adj. || 生態的 || The oil spill was an ecological disaster. || 漏油事件是一場生態浩劫。 || ecological balance=生態平衡 || We must protect the ecological balance ___ the ocean. || of|for|with || the balance of = …的平衡。 || 我們必須保護海洋的生態平衡。
ecology || n. || 生態 || She studies the ecology of rivers. || 她研究河川生態。 || marine ecology=海洋生態 || Pollution has a big impact ___ the ecology of the area. || on|to|for || have an impact on = 對…有影響。 || 汙染對該地區的生態有很大的影響。
ecosystem || n. || 生態系統 || Coral reefs are fragile ecosystems. || 珊瑚礁是脆弱的生態系統。 || marine ecosystem=海洋生態系 || Every animal plays a role ___ the ecosystem. || in|on|at || play a role in = 在…中扮演角色。 || 每種動物在生態系統中都扮演一個角色。
ego || n. || 自我；自尊 || He has a big ego. || 他很自負。 || big ego=自視甚高 || His ego was hurt ___ he lost the game. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 他輸了比賽時自尊心受到了傷害。
elaborate || adj./v. || 精心的；詳細說明 || She made an elaborate plan. || 她擬定了一個詳盡的計畫。 || elaborate on=詳細說明… || Could you elaborate ___ your idea? || on|in|at || elaborate on = 詳細說明…。 || 你可以詳細說明你的想法嗎？
eligible || adj. || 有資格的 || You are eligible for the scholarship. || 你有資格申請獎學金。 || be eligible for=有資格… || Students ___ grades are above 90 are eligible for the scholarship. || whose|who|which || whose + 名詞 表示「…的」。 || 成績在九十分以上的學生有資格申請獎學金。
eloquent || adj. || 雄辯的；有說服力的 || She gave an eloquent speech. || 她發表了一場雄辯的演說。 || eloquent speaker=口才好的演說者 || He is the most eloquent speaker ___ I have ever heard. || that|which|what || 最高級後面的關係代名詞用 that。 || 他是我聽過口才最好的演講者。
embrace || v./n. || 擁抱；欣然接受 || She embraced her son. || 她擁抱了兒子。 || embrace change=擁抱改變 || Young people are usually quick ___ embrace new technology. || to|for|in || be quick to + 原形動詞 = 很快就…。 || 年輕人通常很快就能接受新科技。
emission || n. || 排放 || We must reduce carbon emissions. || 我們必須減少碳排放。 || carbon emissions=碳排放 || Cars are a major source ___ carbon emissions. || of|for|to || a source of = …的來源。 || 汽車是碳排放的主要來源。
enterprise || n. || 企業 || Small enterprises need support. || 小企業需要支持。 || small enterprise=小型企業 || The government offers help ___ small enterprises. || to|for|with || offer help to = 提供協助給…。 || 政府提供協助給小型企業。
enthusiastic || adj. || 熱情的；熱衷的 || She is enthusiastic about music. || 她熱衷音樂。 || be enthusiastic about=熱衷於… || The students were enthusiastic ___ the school trip. || about|to|of || be enthusiastic about = 熱衷於…。 || 學生們對校外教學很熱衷。
entitle || v. || 給予權利；為…命名 || You are entitled to a free drink. || 你可以免費喝一杯飲料。 || be entitled to=有權… || Every worker is entitled ___ ten days of paid leave. || to|for|of || be entitled to + 名詞 = 有權享有…。 || 每位員工都有權享有十天的有薪假。
entity || n. || 實體 || The two companies are separate entities. || 這兩家公司是不同的實體。 || legal entity=法人 || The two schools are separate entities, ___ they share a campus. || although|because|so || although = 雖然，表讓步。 || 雖然兩所學校共用校園，但它們是各自獨立的單位。
entrepreneur || n. || 企業家 || He is a young entrepreneur. || 他是年輕的企業家。 || successful entrepreneur=成功的企業家 || She became a successful entrepreneur ___ the age of 25. || at|in|on || at the age of = 在…歲時。 || 她在二十五歲時成為成功的企業家。
envious || adj. || 羨慕的 || I'm envious of her success. || 我羨慕她的成功。 || be envious of=羨慕… || Many people are envious ___ his wealth. || of|for|with || be envious of = 羨慕…。 || 許多人羨慕他的財富。
envision || v. || 想像；展望 || I envision a better future. || 我展望更美好的未來。 || envision a future=展望未來 || Can you envision ___ in ten years? || yourself|you|your || 受詞與主詞相同時用反身代名詞。 || 你能想像十年後的自己嗎？
epidemic || n. || 流行病 || The flu epidemic spread fast. || 流感疫情迅速擴散。 || flu epidemic=流感疫情 || Schools were closed ___ the epidemic. || because of|because|although || because of + 名詞片語。 || 因為流行病，學校停課了。
episode || n. || 一集；事件 || I watched the last episode. || 我看了最後一集。 || final episode=最後一集 || I've watched every episode of the show ___ the first one. || since|for|from || since + 時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 從第一集起我每一集都有看。
equation || n. || 方程式 || Solve the equation. || 解這個方程式。 || solve an equation=解方程式 || It took me an hour ___ solve the equation. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 我花了一個小時解這個方程式。
equity || n. || 公平；股權 || We believe in equity in education. || 我們相信教育公平。 || gender equity=性別平等 || The goal is to bring equity ___ all students. || to|for|with || bring A to B = 為 B 帶來 A。 || 目標是為所有學生帶來公平。
equivalent || adj./n. || 相等的；等同物 || One dollar is equivalent to 30 NT dollars. || 一美元相當於三十元新台幣。 || be equivalent to=相當於… || One US dollar is equivalent ___ about 30 NT dollars. || to|with|for || be equivalent to = 相當於…。 || 一美元相當於大約三十元新台幣。
erect || v./adj. || 豎立；直立的 || A statue was erected in the park. || 公園裡豎立了一座雕像。 || erect a statue=立雕像 || A statue of the poet ___ erected in the park last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 去年公園裡立了一座那位詩人的雕像。
errand || n. || 差事 || I have to run some errands. || 我得去辦點事。 || run errands=跑腿辦事 || My mom asked me ___ run an errand for her. || to|for|of || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 我媽叫我幫她跑個腿。
erupt || v. || 爆發 || The volcano erupted last year. || 火山去年爆發了。 || erupt into=突然爆發成… || The volcano ___ erupted three times since 2000. || has|have|is || 現在完成式；主詞單數用 has。 || 這座火山自 2000 年以來已爆發三次。
escalator || n. || 手扶梯 || Take the escalator to the second floor. || 搭手扶梯到二樓。 || take the escalator=搭手扶梯 || Please stand on the right ___ you are on the escalator. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 搭手扶梯時請靠右站。
essence || n. || 本質；精華 || The essence of the problem is money. || 問題的本質在於錢。 || in essence=本質上 || ___ essence, the two plans are the same. || In|On|At || in essence = 本質上。 || 本質上，這兩個計畫是一樣的。
estate || n. || 地產；財產 || He works in real estate. || 他從事房地產業。 || real estate=房地產 || Real estate prices in Taipei are higher ___ in other cities. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 台北的房地產價格比其他城市高。
