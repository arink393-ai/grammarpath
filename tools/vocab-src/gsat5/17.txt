# 學測 L5 17：sob – strain
sob || v./n. || 啜泣 || The little girl began to sob. || 小女孩開始啜泣。 || sob one's heart out=哭得很傷心 || The little girl sobbed ___ she lost her doll. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 小女孩因為弄丟了洋娃娃而啜泣。
sober || adj. || 清醒的；嚴肅的 || He is never sober on weekends. || 他週末從來沒清醒過。 || stay sober=保持清醒 || You should stay sober ___ you have to drive. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你要開車，就應該保持清醒。
soften || v. || 使變軟 || Soften the butter before using it. || 使用前先把奶油放軟。 || soften the blow=減輕打擊 || Leave the butter out ___ soften it. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 把奶油放在外面讓它變軟。
sole || adj./n. || 唯一的；鞋底 || She was the sole survivor. || 她是唯一的倖存者。 || sole survivor=唯一倖存者 || He was the sole person ___ knew the password. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 他是唯一知道密碼的人。
solo || adj./n. || 獨自的；獨奏 || She sang a solo. || 她獨唱了一首歌。 || go solo=單飛 || She traveled solo ___ Europe last summer. || around|among|between || travel around = 在…四處旅行。 || 她去年夏天獨自在歐洲旅行。
sophisticated || adj. || 精密的；世故的 || This is a sophisticated machine. || 這是一台精密的機器。 || sophisticated technology=精密科技 || The new phone has ___ sophisticated camera. || a|an|the || sophisticated 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 這支新手機有一台精密的相機。
sophomore || n. || 二年級生 || She is a sophomore in college. || 她是大學二年級學生。 || sophomore year=二年級 || My brother is a sophomore ___ National Taiwan University. || at|on|to || at + 學校 = 在…。 || 我哥哥是台大二年級學生。
souvenir || n. || 紀念品 || I bought a souvenir for my mom. || 我買了一個紀念品給媽媽。 || souvenir shop=紀念品店 || I bought this cup ___ a souvenir in Japan. || as|like|for || as = 作為…。 || 我在日本買了這個杯子當紀念品。
sovereignty || n. || 主權 || Every country has sovereignty over its land. || 每個國家對其領土擁有主權。 || national sovereignty=國家主權 || Every country has sovereignty ___ its own territory. || over|on|at || sovereignty over = 對…的主權。 || 每個國家對自己的領土擁有主權。
sow || v. || 播種 || Farmers sow seeds in spring. || 農夫在春天播種。 || sow seeds=播種 || Farmers usually sow seeds ___ spring. || in|on|at || 季節前用 in。 || 農夫通常在春天播種。
spacious || adj. || 寬敞的 || The living room is spacious. || 客廳很寬敞。 || spacious room=寬敞的房間 || The new classroom is much more spacious ___ the old one. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 新教室比舊的寬敞多了。
sparkle || v./n. || 閃耀 || Her eyes sparkled with joy. || 她的眼睛閃耀著喜悅。 || sparkle with=閃耀著… || The lake sparkled ___ the sunlight. || in|on|at || in the sunlight = 在陽光下。 || 湖面在陽光下閃閃發亮。
specialist || n. || 專家 || You should see a skin specialist. || 你應該去看皮膚科專科醫生。 || heart specialist=心臟科醫生 || She is a specialist ___ children's diseases. || in|on|at || a specialist in = …的專家。 || 她是兒童疾病的專家。
specialize || v. || 專攻 || He specializes in Chinese history. || 他專攻中國歷史。 || specialize in=專攻… || This restaurant specializes ___ seafood. || in|on|at || specialize in = 專門做…。 || 這家餐廳專賣海鮮。
specialty || n. || 特產；專長 || Pineapple cake is a Taiwanese specialty. || 鳳梨酥是台灣特產。 || local specialty=當地特產 || Beef noodles are the specialty ___ this restaurant. || of|for|to || the specialty of = …的招牌菜。 || 牛肉麵是這家餐廳的招牌菜。
specify || v. || 具體說明 || Please specify the size you want. || 請註明你要的尺寸。 || specify a date=指定日期 || Please specify ___ you want the books delivered. || when|which|what || when 引導名詞子句，表示時間。 || 請說明你希望什麼時候送書。
specimen || n. || 標本；樣本 || The museum has many insect specimens. || 博物館有很多昆蟲標本。 || blood specimen=血液樣本 || The scientist collected specimens ___ the forest. || from|of|by || from + 地方 = 從…。 || 科學家從森林收集標本。
spectacular || adj. || 壯觀的 || The view was spectacular. || 景色很壯觀。 || spectacular view=壯觀的景色 || The fireworks were ___ spectacular that everyone cheered. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 煙火壯觀到每個人都歡呼。
spectator || n. || 觀眾 || Thousands of spectators watched the game. || 數千名觀眾觀看比賽。 || spectator sport=觀賞性運動 || The spectators cheered ___ the team scored. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 球隊得分時觀眾歡呼了。
spectrum || n. || 光譜；範圍 || We support a wide spectrum of activities. || 我們支持各式各樣的活動。 || a wide spectrum of=範圍廣泛的… || The club attracts students from a wide spectrum ___ backgrounds. || of|for|in || a spectrum of = 一系列的…。 || 這個社團吸引來自各種背景的學生。
speculate || v. || 推測 || People speculated about the cause. || 人們對原因議論紛紛。 || speculate about=推測… || Everyone speculated ___ why she quit. || about|to|for || speculate about = 推測…。 || 大家都在猜她為什麼辭職。
sphere || n. || 球體；領域 || The Earth is a sphere. || 地球是一個球體。 || sphere of influence=勢力範圍 || The Earth is not a perfect sphere, ___ it? || is|isn't|does || 前句 is not 否定，附加問句用肯定 is it。 || 地球不是完美的球體，對吧？
spicy || adj. || 辣的 || I love spicy food. || 我愛吃辣。 || spicy food=辣的食物 || This curry is too spicy ___ me to eat. || for|to|of || too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 這個咖哩太辣了，我吃不下。
spine || n. || 脊椎 || Sit up straight to protect your spine. || 坐直以保護脊椎。 || spine injury=脊椎受傷 || Sitting for too long is bad ___ your spine. || for|to|with || be bad for = 對…有害。 || 久坐對你的脊椎不好。
sponge || n. || 海綿 || Use a sponge to wash the dishes. || 用海綿洗碗。 || like a sponge=像海綿一樣（吸收） || Children's brains are like a sponge, ___ they learn quickly. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 孩子的大腦像海綿一樣，所以學得很快。
sponsor || v./n. || 贊助；贊助者 || The company sponsored the concert. || 那家公司贊助了這場音樂會。 || main sponsor=主要贊助商 || The event ___ sponsored by a local bank. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個活動是由一家當地銀行贊助的。
sponsorship || n. || 贊助 || The team is looking for sponsorship. || 這支隊伍正在尋找贊助。 || corporate sponsorship=企業贊助 || The team got sponsorship ___ a sports company. || from|of|by || from + 來源 = 來自…。 || 這支隊伍獲得一家運動公司的贊助。
spouse || n. || 配偶 || You may bring your spouse to the party. || 你可以帶配偶參加派對。 || spouse=配偶 || Employees and their spouses ___ invited to the dinner. || are|is|be || 主詞複數，用 are。 || 員工和他們的配偶都受邀參加晚宴。
squad || n. || 小隊 || He is on the football squad. || 他是足球隊的隊員。 || police squad=警察小隊 || He was chosen ___ the national squad. || for|to|of || be chosen for = 被選入…。 || 他被選入國家隊。
squash || v./n. || 壓扁；南瓜 || Don't squash the bread. || 不要把麵包壓扁。 || squash soup=南瓜湯 || My sandwich got squashed ___ the bottom of my bag. || at|in|on || at the bottom of = 在…的底部。 || 我的三明治在包包底部被壓扁了。
squat || v./n. || 蹲 || He squatted down to look at the flower. || 他蹲下來看花。 || do squats=做深蹲 || I do fifty squats ___ morning. || every|each|all || every morning = 每天早上。 || 我每天早上做五十下深蹲。
stability || n. || 穩定 || Economic stability is important. || 經濟穩定很重要。 || political stability=政治穩定 || People want stability ___ their lives. || in|on|at || stability in = 在…中的穩定。 || 人們希望生活穩定。
stack || n./v. || 一疊；堆疊 || There is a stack of books on the desk. || 桌上有一疊書。 || a stack of=一疊… || There is a stack ___ papers on my desk. || of|for|in || a stack of = 一疊…。 || 我桌上有一疊文件。
stain || n./v. || 汙漬 || There is a coffee stain on my shirt. || 我的襯衫上有咖啡漬。 || coffee stain=咖啡漬 || How can I get this stain ___ my shirt? || out of|into|onto || get sth out of = 把…弄出來。 || 我要怎麼把襯衫上的汙漬弄掉？
stake || n. || 利害關係；樁 || There is a lot at stake. || 這關係重大。 || at stake=瀕臨危險；有關係 || Your health is ___ stake if you don't sleep enough. || at|in|on || at stake = 處於危險中。 || 如果你睡眠不足，你的健康就岌岌可危。
stall || n./v. || 攤位；熄火 || She runs a food stall at the night market. || 她在夜市擺小吃攤。 || food stall=小吃攤 || We bought some snacks ___ a stall at the night market. || from|of|by || buy sth from = 向…購買。 || 我們在夜市的一個攤位買了一些小吃。
stance || n. || 立場 || What is your stance on this issue? || 你對這個議題的立場是什麼？ || take a stance=表明立場 || What is the mayor's stance ___ the new law? || on|in|at || a stance on = 對…的立場。 || 市長對新法的立場是什麼？
startle || v. || 使嚇一跳 || The noise startled me. || 那個聲音嚇了我一跳。 || be startled by=被…嚇一跳 || I was startled ___ a loud noise. || by|with|of || be startled by = 被…嚇到。 || 我被一聲巨響嚇了一跳。
statistical || adj. || 統計的 || We need statistical data. || 我們需要統計資料。 || statistical data=統計資料 || The report is based ___ statistical data. || on|in|at || be based on = 以…為基礎。 || 這份報告以統計數據為依據。
steer || v. || 駕駛；引導 || He steered the boat to the shore. || 他把船駛向岸邊。 || steer clear of=避開… || You should steer clear ___ people who lie. || of|from|with || steer clear of = 避開…。 || 你應該遠離說謊的人。
stereotype || n. || 刻板印象 || It's a stereotype that girls are bad at math. || 女生數學不好是一種刻板印象。 || gender stereotype=性別刻板印象 || It is a stereotype ___ boys don't cry. || that|what|which || It is a stereotype that… = …是刻板印象。 || 男生不哭是一種刻板印象。
stew || n./v. || 燉菜；燉 || My mom made beef stew. || 我媽做了燉牛肉。 || beef stew=燉牛肉 || Let the beef stew cook slowly ___ two hours. || for|in|since || for + 一段時間。 || 讓燉牛肉慢慢煮兩個小時。
stimulate || v. || 刺激 || The government wants to stimulate the economy. || 政府想刺激經濟。 || stimulate growth=刺激成長 || Good teachers stimulate students ___ think for themselves. || to|for|of || stimulate sb to V = 激勵某人做…。 || 好老師激勵學生自己思考。
stimulus || n. || 刺激（物） || The new policy was a stimulus to the economy. || 新政策刺激了經濟。 || economic stimulus=經濟刺激 || The tax cut was a stimulus ___ the economy. || to|for|of || a stimulus to = 對…的刺激。 || 減稅是對經濟的一種刺激。
stink || v./n. || 發臭 || The garbage stinks. || 垃圾好臭。 || stink of=有…的臭味 || The room stinks ___ old socks. || of|with|from || stink of = 有…的臭味。 || 房間裡有一股臭襪子的味道。
stock || n./v. || 存貨；股票 || The shop has many items in stock. || 這家店有很多存貨。 || in stock=有現貨; out of stock=缺貨 || Sorry, this size is out ___ stock. || of|from|off || out of stock = 缺貨。 || 抱歉，這個尺寸缺貨。
storage || n. || 儲存 || We need more storage space. || 我們需要更多儲存空間。 || storage space=儲存空間 || My phone doesn't have enough storage ___ more photos. || for|to|of || storage for = 容納…的空間。 || 我的手機沒有足夠的空間存更多照片。
straighten || v. || 弄直 || Straighten your back. || 把背挺直。 || straighten up=整理 || Please straighten up your room ___ your grandparents arrive. || before|after|since || 祖父母到「之前」整理，用 before。 || 請在祖父母到之前把房間整理好。
straightforward || adj. || 直接的；簡單的 || The instructions are straightforward. || 說明很簡單明瞭。 || straightforward answer=直截了當的回答 || The question is straightforward, ___ many students got it wrong. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 這個問題很簡單，但很多學生答錯了。
strain || n./v. || 壓力；拉傷 || He strained a muscle. || 他拉傷了肌肉。 || under strain=承受壓力 || She has been ___ a lot of strain recently. || under|below|over || under strain = 承受壓力。 || 她最近承受很大的壓力。
