# 學測 L5 18：strand – threshold
strand || v./n. || 使困住；一縷 || We were stranded at the airport. || 我們被困在機場。 || be stranded=被困住 || Hundreds of travelers were stranded at the airport ___ the typhoon. || because of|because|although || because of + 名詞片語。 || 數百名旅客因颱風被困在機場。
strap || n./v. || 帶子 || The strap of my bag broke. || 我包包的帶子斷了。 || shoulder strap=肩帶 || The strap ___ my bag broke this morning. || of|for|to || the strap of = …的帶子。 || 我包包的背帶今天早上斷了。
strategic || adj. || 戰略的 || The city is in a strategic location. || 這座城市位於戰略要地。 || strategic plan=戰略計畫 || Taiwan is ___ a strategic location in Asia. || in|on|to || in a location = 位於…位置。 || 台灣位於亞洲的戰略位置。
striking || adj. || 顯著的；引人注目的 || There is a striking difference. || 有顯著的差異。 || striking resemblance=驚人的相似 || There is a striking resemblance ___ the two sisters. || between|among|with || 兩者之間用 between。 || 這兩姊妹長得驚人地相似。
structural || adj. || 結構的 || The building has structural problems. || 這棟建築有結構問題。 || structural damage=結構損害 || The earthquake caused structural damage ___ many buildings. || to|for|with || damage to = 對…的損害。 || 地震對許多建築造成結構損害。
stumble || v. || 絆倒；偶然發現 || He stumbled on the stairs. || 他在樓梯上絆了一下。 || stumble upon=偶然發現 || I stumbled ___ an old photo while cleaning my room. || upon|to|for || stumble upon = 偶然發現。 || 我在打掃房間時偶然發現一張舊照片。
sturdy || adj. || 堅固的 || This table is very sturdy. || 這張桌子很堅固。 || sturdy shoes=耐穿的鞋 || You need sturdy shoes ___ hiking. || for|to|of || for + V-ing = 為了…。 || 你需要一雙耐穿的鞋去健行。
submit || v. || 提交 || Please submit your report by Friday. || 請在週五前繳交報告。 || submit an application=提交申請 || All reports must ___ submitted by Friday. || be|is|been || 助動詞 must 後接 be + p.p.。 || 所有報告必須在週五前繳交。
subsequent || adj. || 隨後的 || Subsequent events proved him right. || 後來的事證明他是對的。 || subsequent to=在…之後 || The storm and the subsequent floods ___ many homes. || destroyed|destroying|destroy || 過去發生的事用過去式；主詞為複合主詞。 || 暴風雨和隨後的洪水摧毀了許多房屋。
subsidy || n. || 補助金 || Farmers receive government subsidies. || 農民獲得政府補助。 || government subsidy=政府補助 || The government gives subsidies ___ families with young children. || to|at|with || give sth to sb = 給某人某物。 || 政府提供補助給有幼兒的家庭。
substantial || adj. || 大量的；重大的 || She received a substantial pay raise. || 她獲得大幅加薪。 || substantial amount=大量 || There has been a substantial increase ___ the number of tourists. || in|of|on || an increase in = …的增加。 || 觀光客人數大幅增加。
substitute || n./v. || 代替 || We had a substitute teacher today. || 今天我們有代課老師。 || substitute A for B=用 A 代替 B || You can substitute honey ___ sugar in this recipe. || for|to|with || substitute A for B = 用 A 代替 B。 || 這個食譜中你可以用蜂蜜代替糖。
subtle || adj. || 微妙的 || There is a subtle difference. || 有細微的差別。 || subtle difference=細微的差別 || The difference between the two colors is very subtle, ___ I can't see it. || so|but|because || 前因後果用 so。 || 這兩個顏色的差別非常細微，所以我看不出來。
suburban || adj. || 郊區的 || They live in a suburban area. || 他們住在郊區。 || suburban area=郊區 || Many families moved ___ suburban areas for more space. || to|at|on || move to = 搬到…。 || 許多家庭為了更大的空間搬到郊區。
successor || n. || 繼任者 || Who will be the successor to the president? || 誰會是總統的繼任者？ || successor to=…的繼任者 || The company has chosen a successor ___ the retiring CEO. || to|at|for || successor to = …的繼任者。 || 公司已選出即將退休的執行長的繼任者。
suite || n. || 套房 || We stayed in a hotel suite. || 我們住在飯店套房。 || hotel suite=飯店套房 || We booked a suite ___ a view of the ocean. || with|of|in || with + 特徵 = 有…的。 || 我們訂了一間有海景的套房。
superb || adj. || 極好的 || The food was superb. || 食物棒極了。 || superb performance=精湛的表演 || The singer gave a superb performance, ___ everyone loved. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，只能用 which。 || 那位歌手的表演精湛，大家都很喜歡。
superstition || n. || 迷信 || It's a superstition that 4 is unlucky. || 四是不吉利的數字是一種迷信。 || old superstition=古老的迷信 || There is a superstition ___ breaking a mirror brings bad luck. || that|what|which || that 引導同位語子句，說明 superstition 的內容。 || 有一種迷信說打破鏡子會帶來厄運。
supervise || v. || 監督 || Teachers supervise students during lunch. || 午餐時間老師監督學生。 || supervise children=看管孩子 || Young children must ___ supervised near the pool. || be|is|been || 助動詞 must 後接 be + p.p.。 || 幼童在泳池附近必須有人看管。
supervision || n. || 監督 || Children need adult supervision. || 孩子需要大人看管。 || under supervision=在監督下 || Children should swim only ___ adult supervision. || under|below|over || under supervision = 在監督之下。 || 孩子應該只在大人看管下游泳。
supervisor || n. || 主管；監督者 || I need to ask my supervisor. || 我需要問我的主管。 || direct supervisor=直屬主管 || I'll talk to my supervisor ___ I make a decision. || before|after|since || 做決定「之前」先談，用 before。 || 我做決定之前會先和主管談。
supposedly || adv. || 據說 || The movie is supposedly very good. || 據說這部電影很好看。 || supposedly=據稱 || The restaurant is supposedly the best ___ town. || in|on|at || the best in + 地方。 || 據說這家餐廳是鎮上最好的。
supreme || adj. || 最高的 || The Supreme Court made a decision. || 最高法院做出了判決。 || Supreme Court=最高法院 || The Supreme Court is the highest court ___ the country. || in|on|at || the highest in + 範圍。 || 最高法院是國內最高的法院。
surplus || n./adj. || 過剩；盈餘 || The country has a trade surplus. || 這個國家有貿易順差。 || trade surplus=貿易順差 || The farmers sold their surplus rice ___ a low price. || at|in|on || at a price = 以…價格。 || 農民以低價出售多餘的稻米。
surveillance || n. || 監視 || The police kept him under surveillance. || 警方對他進行監視。 || surveillance camera=監視器 || The thief was caught ___ a surveillance camera. || on|in|at || be caught on camera = 被攝影機拍到。 || 小偷被監視器拍到了。
suspend || v. || 暫停；停學 || He was suspended from school. || 他被停學了。 || be suspended from=被停… || The student was suspended ___ school for fighting. || from|of|at || be suspended from = 被…停權。 || 那個學生因打架被停學。
sustain || v. || 維持；遭受 || We can't sustain this speed. || 我們無法維持這個速度。 || sustain injuries=受傷 || He sustained serious injuries ___ the car accident. || in|on|at || in an accident = 在事故中。 || 他在車禍中受了重傷。
sustainable || adj. || 永續的 || We need sustainable energy. || 我們需要永續能源。 || sustainable development=永續發展 || We should find more sustainable ways ___ produce energy. || to|for|of || ways to V = 做…的方法。 || 我們應該找到更多永續的發電方式。
swap || v./n. || 交換 || Let's swap seats. || 我們換位子吧。 || swap A for B=用 A 換 B || I swapped my sandwich ___ his apple. || for|to|at || swap A for B = 用 A 換 B。 || 我用我的三明治換了他的蘋果。
symbolic || adj. || 象徵的 || The dove is symbolic of peace. || 鴿子象徵和平。 || be symbolic of=象徵… || The color red is symbolic ___ good luck in Chinese culture. || of|for|to || be symbolic of = 象徵…。 || 在中華文化中，紅色象徵好運。
symptom || n. || 症狀 || Fever is a symptom of the flu. || 發燒是流感的症狀。 || common symptom=常見症狀 || A high fever is one of the symptoms ___ the flu. || of|for|to || symptoms of = …的症狀。 || 高燒是流感的症狀之一。
syndrome || n. || 症候群 || He has Down syndrome. || 他有唐氏症。 || Down syndrome=唐氏症 || Children with Down syndrome ___ special care. || need|needs|needing || 主詞 Children 複數，動詞用原形。 || 有唐氏症的孩子需要特別照顧。
tackle || v./n. || 處理；擒抱 || We must tackle this problem. || 我們必須處理這個問題。 || tackle a problem=處理問題 || The government must take action ___ tackle air pollution. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 政府必須採取行動處理空氣汙染。
tactic || n. || 策略；戰術（常用 tactics） || The coach changed his tactics. || 教練改變了戰術。 || sales tactic=銷售手法 || The team changed its tactics ___ the second half. || in|on|at || in the second half = 在下半場。 || 球隊在下半場改變了戰術。
tangle || v./n. || 糾結 || My headphone cord is tangled. || 我的耳機線纏在一起了。 || in a tangle=亂成一團 || My hair gets tangled ___ the wind blows. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 風一吹我的頭髮就打結。
tempt || v. || 引誘 || The cake tempted me. || 那塊蛋糕誘惑著我。 || be tempted to V=很想要… || I was tempted ___ buy the expensive bag. || to|for|of || be tempted to V = 很想要…。 || 我很想買那個昂貴的包包。
temptation || n. || 誘惑 || It's hard to resist temptation. || 很難抗拒誘惑。 || resist temptation=抗拒誘惑 || I couldn't resist the temptation ___ eat another cookie. || to|for|of || the temptation to V = 做…的誘惑。 || 我無法抗拒再吃一塊餅乾的誘惑。
terminal || n./adj. || 航廈；末期的 || Our flight leaves from Terminal 2. || 我們的班機從第二航廈出發。 || airport terminal=機場航廈 || Please go ___ Terminal 1 for international flights. || to|at|in || go to = 前往…。 || 國際航班請前往第一航廈。
terrify || v. || 使驚嚇 || Spiders terrify me. || 蜘蛛讓我很害怕。 || be terrified of=非常害怕… || My little sister is terrified ___ the dark. || of|with|to || be terrified of = 非常害怕…。 || 我妹妹非常怕黑。
testify || v. || 作證 || She testified in court. || 她在法庭上作證。 || testify against=作證指控… || The witness agreed ___ testify in court. || to|for|of || agree to + 原形動詞。 || 證人同意出庭作證。
texture || n. || 質地 || This fabric has a soft texture. || 這種布料質地柔軟。 || smooth texture=光滑的質地 || I don't like the texture ___ this cake. || of|for|in || the texture of = …的質地。 || 我不喜歡這個蛋糕的口感。
theft || n. || 偷竊 || He reported the theft to the police. || 他向警方報案遭竊。 || car theft=偷車 || She reported the theft of her bike ___ the police. || to|for|at || report sth to sb = 向某人報告某事。 || 她向警方報案她的腳踏車被偷了。
theology || n. || 神學 || He studies theology. || 他研究神學。 || Christian theology=基督教神學 || He decided to study theology ___ university. || at|on|to || at university = 在大學。 || 他決定在大學讀神學。
theoretical || adj. || 理論上的 || The course is too theoretical. || 這門課太偏理論了。 || theoretical knowledge=理論知識 || The class is too theoretical; we need more practice ___ well. || as|so|too || as well = 也。 || 這門課太偏理論了，我們也需要更多實作。
therapist || n. || 治療師 || She sees a therapist every week. || 她每週去看心理治療師。 || physical therapist=物理治療師 || My mom works as a physical therapist ___ a hospital. || in|on|to || in a hospital = 在醫院裡。 || 我媽媽在醫院擔任物理治療師。
therapy || n. || 治療 || He needs physical therapy. || 他需要物理治療。 || physical therapy=物理治療 || After the accident, he needed months ___ therapy. || of|for|in || months of = 數個月的…。 || 意外發生後，他需要好幾個月的治療。
thereby || adv. || 因此；藉以 || He exercised daily, thereby improving his health. || 他每天運動，因此改善了健康。 || thereby=從而 || She studied hard, thereby ___ the exam with a high score. || passing|passed|pass || thereby + V-ing 表結果。 || 她努力讀書，因而高分通過考試。
thesis || n. || 論文 || She is writing her master's thesis. || 她正在寫碩士論文。 || master's thesis=碩士論文 || She is writing her thesis ___ climate change. || on|in|at || a thesis on = 關於…的論文。 || 她正在寫關於氣候變遷的論文。
thigh || n. || 大腿 || He hurt his thigh. || 他的大腿受傷了。 || thigh muscle=大腿肌肉 || He injured his thigh ___ playing soccer. || while|during|since || while + V-ing = 當…的時候。 || 他踢足球時大腿受傷了。
threshold || n. || 門檻 || She has a low pain threshold. || 她很怕痛。 || pain threshold=痛覺閾值 || We are on the threshold ___ a new era. || of|for|to || on the threshold of = 在…的開端。 || 我們正站在一個新時代的開端。
