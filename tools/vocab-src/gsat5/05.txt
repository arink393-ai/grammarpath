# 學測 L5 05：consecutive – customs
consecutive || adj. || 連續的 || It rained for five consecutive days. || 連續下了五天雨。 || consecutive days=連續幾天 || Our team has won the title ___ three consecutive years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 我們隊已經連續三年贏得冠軍。
consensus || n. || 共識 || The group reached a consensus. || 小組達成了共識。 || reach a consensus=達成共識 || There is a consensus ___ scientists that climate change is real. || among|between|along || among + 三者以上 = 在…之中。 || 科學家們有共識：氣候變遷是真實的。
consent || n./v. || 同意 || You need your parents' consent. || 你需要父母的同意。 || written consent=書面同意 || Students under 18 need their parents' consent ___ join the trip. || to|for|of || consent to V = 同意做…；此處不定詞修飾 consent。 || 未滿十八歲的學生需要父母同意才能參加旅行。
conservation || n. || 保育；保存 || Wildlife conservation is important. || 野生動物保育很重要。 || energy conservation=節約能源 || The group works ___ the conservation of sea turtles. || for|on|at || work for = 為…工作／努力。 || 這個團體致力於海龜的保育。
considerate || adj. || 體貼的 || It's very considerate of you. || 你真是體貼。 || considerate of=體貼… || It was very considerate ___ you to help the old lady. || of|for|to || It is + 形容詞(人的特質) + of sb + to V。 || 你幫那位老太太真是太體貼了。
constitutional || adj. || 憲法的 || Free speech is a constitutional right. || 言論自由是憲法賦予的權利。 || constitutional right=憲法權利 || Freedom of speech is a constitutional right ___ every citizen enjoys. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 enjoys 的受詞。 || 言論自由是每個公民享有的憲法權利。
constraint || n. || 限制 || We have time constraints. || 我們有時間上的限制。 || time constraint=時間限制 || ___ time constraints, we couldn't visit every museum. || Because of|Because|Although || because of + 名詞片語。 || 因為時間限制，我們無法參觀每間博物館。
consultation || n. || 諮詢 || You should have a consultation with a doctor. || 你應該諮詢醫生。 || in consultation with=與…商議 || The decision was made in consultation ___ the teachers. || with|to|for || in consultation with = 與…商議。 || 這個決定是與老師們商議後做出的。
consumption || n. || 消耗；消費 || Our energy consumption is too high. || 我們的能源消耗太高了。 || energy consumption=能源消耗 || We should reduce our consumption ___ plastic. || of|for|with || the consumption of = …的消耗。 || 我們應該減少塑膠的使用量。
contagious || adj. || 傳染性的 || The flu is very contagious. || 流感的傳染性很強。 || contagious disease=傳染病 || Stay home ___ you have a contagious disease. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你得了傳染病就待在家。
contaminate || v. || 汙染 || The river was contaminated by factory waste. || 河川被工廠廢水汙染。 || contaminated water=受汙染的水 || The water ___ contaminated by chemicals from the factory. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 水被工廠的化學物質汙染了。
contemplate || v. || 沉思；考慮 || He contemplated moving abroad. || 他考慮過搬到國外。 || contemplate V-ing=考慮做… || She is contemplating ___ abroad next year. || studying|to study|study || contemplate + V-ing = 考慮做…。 || 她正在考慮明年出國讀書。
contemporary || adj. || 當代的 || I like contemporary art. || 我喜歡當代藝術。 || contemporary art=當代藝術 || The museum is famous ___ its contemporary art. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 這間博物館以當代藝術聞名。
contempt || n. || 輕視 || He looked at me with contempt. || 他輕蔑地看著我。 || with contempt=輕蔑地 || She looked at the rude man ___ contempt. || with|by|in || with contempt = 輕蔑地。 || 她輕蔑地看著那個無禮的男人。
contend || v. || 競爭；主張 || Three teams are contending for the title. || 三支隊伍爭奪冠軍。 || contend with=應付…; contend for=爭奪… || Farmers have to contend ___ bad weather every year. || with|for|to || contend with = 應付…。 || 農夫每年都得應付惡劣的天氣。
continental || adj. || 大陸的 || We had a continental breakfast. || 我們吃了歐陸式早餐。 || continental breakfast=歐陸式早餐 || The hotel serves a continental breakfast ___ 7 to 10 a.m. || from|between|since || from A to B = 從 A 到 B。 || 飯店從早上七點到十點供應歐陸式早餐。
contractor || n. || 承包商 || The contractor built our new house. || 承包商蓋了我們的新房子。 || building contractor=營造商 || We hired a contractor ___ repair our roof. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們雇了一位承包商來修屋頂。
contradiction || n. || 矛盾 || There is a contradiction in his story. || 他的說法有矛盾。 || in contradiction to=與…矛盾 || There is a contradiction ___ what he says and what he does. || between|among|with || between A and B = A 和 B 之間。 || 他說的和做的之間有矛盾。
controversial || adj. || 有爭議的 || It's a controversial topic. || 這是一個有爭議的話題。 || controversial issue=爭議性議題 || The new law is ___ controversial that many people are protesting. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 新法爭議很大，許多人在抗議。
controversy || n. || 爭議 || The decision caused a lot of controversy. || 這個決定引起很大的爭議。 || cause controversy=引起爭議 || There has been a lot of controversy ___ the new rule. || over|of|with || controversy over = 關於…的爭議。 || 關於新規定一直有很多爭議。
conversion || n. || 轉換 || The conversion of the old factory into a museum took two years. || 把舊工廠改建成博物館花了兩年。 || conversion rate=兌換率 || The conversion of the old factory ___ a museum took two years. || into|onto|from || conversion of A into B = 把 A 轉換成 B。 || 把舊工廠改建成博物館花了兩年。
convert || v. || 轉換；改變信仰 || They converted the garage into a bedroom. || 他們把車庫改成臥室。 || convert A into B=把 A 轉換成 B || We converted the old garage ___ a study room. || into|onto|from || convert A into B = 把 A 改成 B。 || 我們把舊車庫改成了書房。
convict || v./n. || 判有罪；囚犯 || He was convicted of murder. || 他被判謀殺罪。 || be convicted of=被判…罪 || The man was convicted ___ stealing cars. || of|for|with || be convicted of = 被判…罪。 || 那名男子被判偷車罪。
conviction || n. || 信念；定罪 || She spoke with conviction. || 她說話充滿信念。 || with conviction=堅定地 || She spoke ___ such conviction that everyone believed her. || with|by|in || with conviction = 堅定地；such…that 結構。 || 她說得如此堅定，大家都相信了她。
coordinate || v. || 協調 || She coordinates the school events. || 她負責協調學校活動。 || coordinate with=與…協調 || We need someone ___ coordinate the school festival. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 修飾 someone。 || 我們需要一個人來協調校慶活動。
copyright || n. || 版權 || The book is protected by copyright. || 這本書受版權保護。 || copyright law=著作權法 || You can't use this song ___ breaking copyright laws. || without|with|by || without + V-ing = 不…。 || 你不能使用這首歌而不違反版權法。
core || n./adj. || 核心 || Honesty is a core value. || 誠實是核心價值。 || core value=核心價值 || Honesty is one of the core ___ of our school. || values|value|valuable || one of the + 複數名詞。 || 誠實是我們學校的核心價值之一。
corporate || adj. || 公司的 || He has a corporate job. || 他在大公司上班。 || corporate culture=企業文化 || Every company has ___ own corporate culture. || its|it's|their || 單數名詞 company 的所有格用 its。 || 每家公司都有自己的企業文化。
corporation || n. || 公司；企業 || He works for a large corporation. || 他在一家大企業工作。 || multinational corporation=跨國企業 || He has worked for the corporation ___ 2015. || since|for|from || since + 時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 他從 2015 年起就在這家企業工作。
correlation || n. || 關聯 || There is a correlation between sleep and health. || 睡眠和健康有關聯。 || correlation between=…之間的關聯 || Studies show a strong correlation ___ exercise and happiness. || between|among|with || between A and B = A 和 B 之間。 || 研究顯示運動和快樂之間有很強的關聯。
correspondent || n. || 特派員；記者 || She is a war correspondent. || 她是戰地記者。 || foreign correspondent=駐外記者 || The correspondent reported ___ the war for ten years. || on|in|at || report on = 報導…。 || 那位特派員報導這場戰爭長達十年。
corridor || n. || 走廊 || Don't run in the corridor. || 不要在走廊上奔跑。 || long corridor=長廊 || Students are not allowed ___ run in the corridor. || to|for|of || be allowed to + 原形動詞。 || 學生不准在走廊上奔跑。
corrupt || adj./v. || 腐敗的 || The official was corrupt. || 那位官員很腐敗。 || corrupt official=貪官 || The corrupt official ___ sent to prison last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那位貪官去年被送進監獄。
corruption || n. || 貪汙 || People are angry about corruption. || 人們對貪汙感到憤怒。 || fight corruption=打擊貪腐 || The mayor was accused ___ corruption. || of|for|with || be accused of = 被控…。 || 市長被控貪汙。
counsel || v./n. || 諮商；勸告 || Teachers counsel students about their future. || 老師為學生的未來提供建議。 || counsel sb=輔導某人 || She counsels students ___ have family problems. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 她輔導有家庭問題的學生。
counselor || n. || 輔導老師 || You can talk to the school counselor. || 你可以找學校的輔導老師談談。 || school counselor=學校輔導老師 || If you feel stressed, you can talk ___ the school counselor. || to|at|for || talk to sb = 和某人談。 || 如果你覺得有壓力，可以找學校輔導老師談談。
courteous || adj. || 有禮貌的 || The staff were courteous and helpful. || 員工有禮貌又熱心。 || courteous service=有禮的服務 || It is important to be courteous ___ customers. || to|for|of || be courteous to = 對…有禮貌。 || 對顧客有禮貌很重要。
coverage || n. || 報導；覆蓋範圍 || The news coverage of the event was huge. || 媒體對此事件大量報導。 || news coverage=新聞報導 || The typhoon got a lot of coverage ___ TV. || on|in|at || on TV = 在電視上。 || 這次颱風在電視上獲得大量報導。
credibility || n. || 可信度 || The scandal hurt his credibility. || 醜聞損害了他的可信度。 || lose credibility=失去公信力 || The politician lost his credibility ___ he lied to the public. || after|before|until || 說謊「之後」失去公信力，用 after。 || 那位政客對大眾說謊後失去了公信力。
creek || n. || 小溪 || The kids played in the creek. || 孩子們在小溪裡玩。 || small creek=小溪 || There is a small creek ___ the back of our house. || at|in|on || at the back of = 在…後面。 || 我們家後面有一條小溪。
cripple || v. || 使癱瘓；嚴重損害 || The strike crippled the city's transportation. || 罷工使城市交通癱瘓。 || be crippled by=因…而癱瘓 || The city was crippled ___ the heavy snow. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這座城市因大雪而陷入癱瘓。
criterion || n. || 標準（複數 criteria） || Price is one criterion for choosing a phone. || 價格是選手機的一個標準。 || main criterion=主要標準 || What is the main criterion ___ choosing the winner? || for|of|to || a criterion for = …的標準。 || 選出贏家的主要標準是什麼？
crucial || adj. || 關鍵的 || This is a crucial moment. || 這是關鍵時刻。 || be crucial to=對…至關重要 || Good sleep is crucial ___ students' learning. || to|of|with || be crucial to = 對…至關重要。 || 良好睡眠對學生的學習至關重要。
crude || adj. || 粗糙的；原油的 || The price of crude oil went up. || 原油價格上漲了。 || crude oil=原油 || The price of crude oil ___ risen sharply this month. || has|have|is || 主詞 The price 單數，用 has。 || 原油價格本月急遽上漲。
cruise || n./v. || 郵輪之旅 || We went on a cruise to Japan. || 我們搭郵輪去日本。 || go on a cruise=搭郵輪旅遊 || My parents went ___ a cruise for their anniversary. || on|in|at || go on a cruise = 搭郵輪旅遊。 || 我爸媽為了結婚紀念日去搭郵輪旅行。
crystal || n./adj. || 水晶；清澈的 || The water is crystal clear. || 水清澈透明。 || crystal clear=清澈透明 || The water in the lake is ___ crystal clear that you can see the fish. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 湖水清澈得可以看到魚。
cuisine || n. || 菜餚 || I love Japanese cuisine. || 我愛日本料理。 || local cuisine=當地料理 || Taiwan is famous ___ its local cuisine. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 台灣以當地美食聞名。
currency || n. || 貨幣 || The yen is Japan's currency. || 日圓是日本的貨幣。 || foreign currency=外幣 || You need to change your money ___ the local currency. || into|onto|from || change A into B = 把 A 換成 B。 || 你需要把錢換成當地貨幣。
curriculum || n. || 課程 || The school changed its curriculum. || 學校改了課程。 || school curriculum=學校課程 || Coding is now part ___ the school curriculum. || of|for|in || part of = …的一部分。 || 程式設計現在是學校課程的一部分。
custody || n. || 監護權；拘留 || The mother got custody of the children. || 母親取得了孩子的監護權。 || in custody=被拘留 || The suspect is now ___ police custody. || in|on|at || in custody = 被拘留。 || 嫌犯現在被警方拘留。
customs || n. || 海關 || We went through customs at the airport. || 我們在機場通過海關。 || go through customs=通關 || You have to go ___ customs after you land. || through|across|along || go through customs = 通過海關。 || 降落後你必須通過海關。
