# 學測 L5 14：prescribe – rear
prescribe || v. || 開藥方 || The doctor prescribed some medicine. || 醫生開了一些藥。 || prescribe medicine=開藥 || The doctor prescribed some medicine ___ my cough. || for|to|with || prescribe sth for + 症狀。 || 醫生開了一些治咳嗽的藥給我。
prescription || n. || 處方 || You need a prescription for this medicine. || 這種藥需要處方箋。 || prescription drug=處方藥 || You can't buy this medicine ___ a prescription. || without|with|by || without + 名詞 = 沒有…。 || 沒有處方箋你不能買這種藥。
presidency || n. || 總統任期 || During his presidency, the economy grew. || 他擔任總統期間經濟成長。 || during one's presidency=在某人任內 || The economy grew a lot ___ his presidency. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 他擔任總統期間，經濟大幅成長。
presidential || adj. || 總統的 || The presidential election is next year. || 總統大選在明年。 || presidential election=總統大選 || The presidential election will be held ___ January. || in|on|at || 月份前用 in。 || 總統大選將在一月舉行。
presumably || adv. || 大概 || Presumably, he forgot. || 他大概是忘了。 || presumably=推測起來 || He is not here; presumably he ___ the bus. || missed|misses|has missing || 推測過去發生的事用過去式。 || 他不在這裡，大概是錯過公車了。
presume || v. || 推測；假定 || I presume you are hungry. || 我猜你餓了。 || presume that=推定… || I presume ___ you have finished your homework. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 presume 的受詞。 || 我猜你已經做完作業了。
prevail || v. || 盛行；獲勝 || Justice will prevail. || 正義必勝。 || prevail over=戰勝… || In the end, good will prevail ___ evil. || over|above|on || prevail over = 戰勝…。 || 最後善會戰勝惡。
prey || n./v. || 獵物 || The lion caught its prey. || 獅子抓住了牠的獵物。 || fall prey to=成為…的受害者 || Old people often fall prey ___ phone scams. || to|for|of || fall prey to = 成為…的受害者。 || 老人常成為電話詐騙的受害者。
prior || adj. || 在先的 || Prior to the meeting, please read the report. || 會議前請先讀報告。 || prior to=在…之前 || Please arrive 30 minutes prior ___ the exam. || to|for|than || prior to = 在…之前。 || 請在考試前三十分鐘到達。
proclaim || v. || 宣布 || The king proclaimed a holiday. || 國王宣布放假一天。 || proclaim independence=宣布獨立 || The country proclaimed its independence ___ 1945. || in|on|at || 年份前用 in。 || 這個國家在 1945 年宣布獨立。
productivity || n. || 生產力 || Good sleep improves productivity. || 良好的睡眠能提升生產力。 || increase productivity=提高生產力 || Taking short breaks can increase your productivity ___ work. || at|in|on || at work = 在工作上。 || 短暫休息可以提高你的工作效率。
profile || n. || 簡介；側面 || Update your profile picture. || 更新你的大頭貼。 || profile picture=大頭貼 || You can change your profile picture ___ the settings page. || on|at|to || on a page = 在…頁面上。 || 你可以在設定頁面更換大頭貼。
profound || adj. || 深刻的 || The book had a profound effect on me. || 這本書對我有深遠的影響。 || profound effect=深遠的影響 || Her death had a profound effect ___ the whole family. || on|to|for || have an effect on = 對…有影響。 || 她的去世對全家人有深遠的影響。
progressive || adj. || 進步的；漸進的 || She has progressive ideas. || 她有進步的思想。 || progressive tense=進行式 || The progressive tense is used ___ talk about actions in progress. || to|for|of || be used to + 原形動詞 = 被用來…。 || 進行式用來談論正在進行的動作。
prohibit || v. || 禁止 || Smoking is prohibited here. || 這裡禁止吸菸。 || prohibit sb from=禁止某人… || Students are prohibited ___ using phones during the exam. || from|to|of || prohibit sb from V-ing = 禁止某人做…。 || 考試期間禁止學生使用手機。
projection || n. || 預測；投影 || Sales projections for next year are good. || 明年的銷售預測很不錯。 || sales projection=銷售預測 || The projections ___ next year look positive. || for|of|to || projections for = 對…的預測。 || 明年的預測看起來很樂觀。
prolong || v. || 延長 || Exercise can prolong your life. || 運動能延長壽命。 || prolong life=延長壽命 || Regular exercise can help prolong your life, ___ it also makes you happier. || and|or|so || 補充說明用 and。 || 規律運動有助於延長壽命，而且也能讓你更快樂。
prone || adj. || 易於…的 || He is prone to colds. || 他容易感冒。 || be prone to=易於… || Children are more prone ___ accidents. || to|for|of || be prone to = 易於…。 || 兒童比較容易發生意外。
propaganda || n. || 宣傳 || The government used propaganda. || 政府利用宣傳手段。 || political propaganda=政治宣傳 || Don't believe everything you read; some of it is propaganda, ___ you should be careful. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 別相信你讀到的一切，有些是政治宣傳，所以要小心。
prophet || n. || 先知 || He was seen as a prophet. || 他被視為先知。 || false prophet=假先知 || The old man was seen ___ a prophet by the villagers. || as|like|for || be seen as = 被視為…。 || 那位老人被村民視為先知。
proportion || n. || 比例 || A large proportion of students passed. || 大部分學生都及格了。 || in proportion to=與…成比例 || A large proportion of the students ___ smartphones. || own|owns|owning || a proportion of + 複數名詞，動詞用複數。 || 大部分學生都有智慧型手機。
prosecution || n. || 起訴；檢方 || The prosecution presented new evidence. || 檢方提出了新證據。 || face prosecution=面臨起訴 || People who drive drunk may face prosecution ___ the law. || under|below|over || under the law = 依據法律。 || 酒駕的人可能依法遭到起訴。
prospect || n. || 前景；可能性 || The job has good prospects. || 這份工作前景看好。 || job prospects=就業前景 || The prospect ___ going abroad excites her. || of|for|to || the prospect of = …的可能性。 || 出國的可能性讓她很興奮。
province || n. || 省 || Canada has ten provinces. || 加拿大有十個省。 || province=省份 || Canada is divided ___ ten provinces and three territories. || into|onto|from || be divided into = 被分成…。 || 加拿大分成十個省和三個地區。
provision || n. || 提供；條款 || The provision of clean water is important. || 提供乾淨的水很重要。 || make provision for=為…做準備 || The provision of free lunches ___ helped many poor children. || has|have|is || 主詞 The provision 單數，用 has。 || 提供免費午餐幫助了許多貧窮的孩子。
provoke || v. || 激怒；引起 || Don't provoke the dog. || 不要激怒那隻狗。 || provoke anger=激起憤怒 || His comment provoked anger ___ many people. || among|between|along || among + 三者以上 = 在…之中。 || 他的評論在許多人之中引起憤怒。
pulse || n. || 脈搏 || The nurse checked my pulse. || 護理師量了我的脈搏。 || take one's pulse=量脈搏 || The nurse took my pulse ___ checked my temperature. || and|or|but || 兩個並列的動作用 and。 || 護理師量了我的脈搏，還量了體溫。
purchase || v./n. || 購買 || You can purchase tickets online. || 你可以在網路上購票。 || make a purchase=購物 || Tickets can ___ purchased online. || be|is|been || 助動詞 can 後接 be + p.p.。 || 門票可以在網路上購買。
pyramid || n. || 金字塔 || The pyramids are in Egypt. || 金字塔在埃及。 || Egyptian pyramid=埃及金字塔 || The pyramids ___ built thousands of years ago. || were|was|are || 被動語態；pyramids 複數用 were + p.p.。 || 金字塔是在數千年前建造的。
qualify || v. || 有資格；合格 || She qualified for the final. || 她取得決賽資格。 || qualify for=取得…的資格 || Our team qualified ___ the national finals. || for|to|with || qualify for = 取得…的資格。 || 我們隊取得了全國決賽的資格。
quest || n. || 探求；追尋 || Humans are on a quest for knowledge. || 人類在追求知識。 || quest for=追求… || The scientist spent his life ___ a quest for the truth. || on|in|at || on a quest = 在追尋中。 || 那位科學家一生都在追尋真理。
questionnaire || n. || 問卷 || Please fill out the questionnaire. || 請填寫問卷。 || fill out a questionnaire=填問卷 || Please fill ___ the questionnaire and return it to me. || out|up|off || fill out = 填寫（表格）。 || 請填寫問卷並交回給我。
quiver || v./n. || 顫抖 || Her voice quivered. || 她的聲音在顫抖。 || quiver with=因…而顫抖 || Her lips quivered ___ fear. || with|by|on || quiver with + 情緒 = 因…而顫抖。 || 她的嘴唇因恐懼而顫抖。
quota || n. || 配額 || Each country has a quota. || 每個國家都有配額。 || meet a quota=達到配額 || The salesman failed ___ meet his monthly quota. || to|for|of || fail to + 原形動詞。 || 那位業務員沒能達到每月業績配額。
racism || n. || 種族歧視 || Racism is still a problem. || 種族歧視仍是個問題。 || fight racism=對抗種族歧視 || We must all stand up ___ racism. || against|to|with || stand up against = 起身對抗…。 || 我們都必須起身對抗種族歧視。
rack || n. || 架子 || Put your bike in the bike rack. || 把你的腳踏車停在車架上。 || bike rack=腳踏車架 || Hang your coat ___ the rack by the door. || on|in|at || 掛在架子上用 on。 || 把你的外套掛在門邊的架子上。
radiation || n. || 輻射 || Too much radiation is harmful. || 過多的輻射有害。 || radiation exposure=輻射曝露 || Workers must be protected ___ radiation. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 工作人員必須受到保護以免受輻射傷害。
radical || adj. || 激進的；根本的 || They made radical changes. || 他們做了徹底的改變。 || radical change=徹底的改變 || The new manager made radical changes ___ the company. || to|for|with || changes to = 對…的改變。 || 新經理對公司做了徹底的改變。
ragged || adj. || 破爛的 || The boy wore ragged clothes. || 那個男孩穿著破爛的衣服。 || ragged clothes=破爛衣服 || The boy ___ ragged clothes was begging on the street. || in|at|on || in + 衣服 = 穿著…。 || 那個穿著破爛衣服的男孩在街上乞討。
raid || n./v. || 突襲 || The police raided the house. || 警方突襲了那棟房子。 || police raid=警方突襲 || The police carried out a raid ___ the building at midnight. || on|in|at || a raid on = 對…的突襲。 || 警方在午夜突襲了那棟大樓。
rail || n. || 欄杆；鐵路 || Hold on to the rail. || 抓緊欄杆。 || by rail=搭火車 || The goods were sent ___ rail. || by|on|with || by rail = 經由鐵路。 || 貨物經由鐵路運送。
rally || n./v. || 集會；團結 || Thousands of people joined the rally. || 數千人參加了集會。 || hold a rally=舉行集會 || Thousands of people took part ___ the rally. || in|on|at || take part in = 參加…。 || 數千人參加了這場集會。
ranch || n. || 大牧場 || They own a cattle ranch. || 他們擁有一座養牛場。 || cattle ranch=養牛場 || My uncle has worked on the ranch ___ he was young. || since|for|from || since + 子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 我叔叔從年輕起就在牧場工作。
random || adj. || 隨機的 || The winners were chosen at random. || 得獎者是隨機選出的。 || at random=隨機地 || The teacher chose three students ___ random. || at|in|by || at random = 隨機地。 || 老師隨機選了三位學生。
ratio || n. || 比例 || The ratio of boys to girls is 1 to 2. || 男生與女生的比例是一比二。 || ratio of A to B=A 與 B 的比例 || The ratio of students ___ teachers is 20 to 1. || to|with|for || the ratio of A to B = A 與 B 的比例。 || 學生與老師的比例是二十比一。
rational || adj. || 理性的 || Let's make a rational decision. || 讓我們做出理性的決定。 || rational decision=理性的決定 || It is hard to stay rational ___ you are angry. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 生氣時很難保持理性。
rattle || v./n. || 發出嘎嘎聲；搖鈴 || The windows rattled in the wind. || 窗戶在風中嘎嘎作響。 || baby rattle=嬰兒搖鈴 || The baby laughed ___ he shook the rattle. || as|which|what || as = 當…的時候。 || 寶寶搖著搖鈴時笑了。
realism || n. || 現實主義；寫實 || The painting is famous for its realism. || 這幅畫以寫實聞名。 || sense of realism=真實感 || The movie is praised ___ its realism. || for|of|with || praise sth for = 因…稱讚。 || 這部電影因寫實而受到讚揚。
realm || n. || 領域；王國 || This is beyond the realm of science. || 這超出了科學的範疇。 || in the realm of=在…領域 || Such an idea is still ___ the realm of science fiction. || in|on|at || in the realm of = 在…領域中。 || 這樣的想法仍屬於科幻的範疇。
rear || n./adj./v. || 後面；養育 || The exit is at the rear of the bus. || 出口在公車後方。 || rear seat=後座 || Please exit ___ the rear door. || through|across|along || through = 穿過…。 || 請從後門下車。
