# 學測 L5 11：legacy – molecule
legacy || n. || 遺產；傳承 || The artist left a great legacy. || 那位藝術家留下了偉大的遺產。 || leave a legacy=留下遺產 || The legacy ___ he left behind is still important today. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 left 的受詞。 || 他留下的遺產至今仍然很重要。
legendary || adj. || 傳奇的 || He is a legendary singer. || 他是傳奇歌手。 || legendary figure=傳奇人物 || The legendary player retired ___ the age of 40. || at|in|on || at the age of = 在…歲時。 || 那位傳奇球員在四十歲時退休。
legislation || n. || 立法；法規 || New legislation was passed to protect animals. || 新法規通過以保護動物。 || pass legislation=通過法規 || New legislation ___ passed last year to protect workers. || was|were|is || legislation 不可數，用單數；被動 was + p.p.。 || 去年通過了保護勞工的新法規。
legislative || adj. || 立法的 || The Legislative Yuan makes laws. || 立法院負責制定法律。 || legislative body=立法機構 || The Legislative Yuan is responsible ___ making laws. || for|of|to || be responsible for = 負責…。 || 立法院負責制定法律。
legitimate || adj. || 合法的；正當的 || That's a legitimate question. || 那是個合理的問題。 || legitimate reason=正當理由 || You can't miss school ___ a legitimate reason. || without|with|by || without + 名詞 = 沒有…。 || 沒有正當理由不能缺課。
lest || conj. || 以免 || Speak quietly lest you wake the baby. || 說話小聲點，以免吵醒寶寶。 || lest=以免 || Walk carefully lest you ___ on the ice. || slip|slips|slipped || lest + 主詞 + (should) + 原形動詞。 || 小心走路，以免在冰上滑倒。
liability || n. || 責任；負擔 || The company accepted liability for the accident. || 公司承擔了事故的責任。 || accept liability=承擔責任 || The company denied liability ___ the accident. || for|of|to || liability for = 對…的責任。 || 公司否認對事故負有責任。
likelihood || n. || 可能性 || There is little likelihood of rain today. || 今天下雨的可能性很小。 || in all likelihood=十之八九 || ___ all likelihood, the game will be canceled. || In|On|At || in all likelihood = 十之八九。 || 比賽十之八九會被取消。
likewise || adv. || 同樣地 || He worked hard, and his sister did likewise. || 他努力工作，他妹妹也一樣。 || do likewise=照做 || She left early, and I did likewise ___ I was tired too. || because|although|unless || 表示原因用 because。 || 她提早離開，我也一樣，因為我也累了。
loop || n. || 環；迴圈 || The road makes a loop around the lake. || 這條路環繞湖泊一圈。 || in the loop=知情 || Please keep me ___ the loop about the plan. || in|on|at || keep sb in the loop = 讓某人隨時知情。 || 請讓我隨時知道計畫的最新消息。
lounge || n. || 休息室 || We waited in the airport lounge. || 我們在機場貴賓室等候。 || airport lounge=機場貴賓室 || Passengers can rest ___ the lounge before boarding. || in|on|to || 在室內用 in。 || 旅客登機前可以在休息室休息。
lump || n. || 塊；腫塊 || I found a lump on my neck. || 我在脖子上摸到一個腫塊。 || a lump of sugar=一塊方糖 || Would you like a lump ___ sugar in your tea? || of|for|in || a lump of = 一塊…。 || 你的茶要加一塊方糖嗎？
mainstream || n./adj. || 主流 || Her music is not mainstream. || 她的音樂不是主流。 || mainstream media=主流媒體 || His ideas are far ___ the mainstream. || from|of|to || far from = 遠離…。 || 他的想法和主流相距甚遠。
maintenance || n. || 維修；保養 || The elevator is under maintenance. || 電梯正在維修中。 || under maintenance=維修中 || The elevator is ___ maintenance today. || under|below|of || under maintenance = 維修中。 || 電梯今天在維修中。
mammal || n. || 哺乳動物 || Whales are mammals. || 鯨魚是哺乳動物。 || marine mammal=海洋哺乳動物 || Whales are mammals, ___ they live in the sea. || although|because|so || 前後語意轉折，用 although（雖然）。 || 雖然鯨魚住在海裡，但牠們是哺乳動物。
mandate || n./v. || 授權；強制 || The law mandates that children wear seat belts. || 法律規定兒童必須繫安全帶。 || mandate that=強制規定… || The new rule mandates ___ all students wear helmets. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 mandates 的受詞。 || 新規定強制所有學生戴安全帽。
manifest || v./adj. || 顯示；明顯的 || His anger manifested itself in violence. || 他的憤怒以暴力的形式表現出來。 || manifest itself=顯現 || Stress can manifest ___ in many ways. || itself|it|its || 主詞與受詞相同時用反身代名詞。 || 壓力會以許多方式表現出來。
manipulate || v. || 操控 || He tried to manipulate his friends. || 他試圖操控朋友。 || manipulate sb into=操控某人做… || Don't let anyone manipulate you ___ doing something wrong. || into|onto|from || manipulate sb into V-ing = 操控某人做…。 || 別讓任何人操控你去做錯事。
mansion || n. || 豪宅 || The singer lives in a mansion. || 那位歌手住在豪宅裡。 || luxury mansion=豪宅 || The singer lives in a mansion ___ has ten bedrooms. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當主詞。 || 那位歌手住在有十間臥室的豪宅裡。
marine || adj. || 海洋的 || We should protect marine life. || 我們應該保護海洋生物。 || marine life=海洋生物 || Plastic waste is a threat ___ marine life. || to|for|of || a threat to = 對…的威脅。 || 塑膠垃圾對海洋生物是一大威脅。
masculine || adj. || 男性的；陽剛的 || He has a masculine voice. || 他有陽剛的嗓音。 || masculine traits=男性特質 || Some people think pink is not ___ masculine color. || a|an|the || masculine 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 有些人認為粉紅色不是陽剛的顏色。
massage || n./v. || 按摩 || I need a massage. || 我需要按摩。 || get a massage=去按摩 || A massage can help you relax ___ a long day. || after|before|since || after + 名詞 = 在…之後。 || 按摩能幫助你在漫長的一天後放鬆。
massive || adj. || 巨大的 || The earthquake was massive. || 那場地震規模很大。 || massive amount=大量 || The typhoon caused massive damage ___ the farms. || to|for|with || damage to = 對…的損害。 || 颱風對農場造成巨大損害。
masterpiece || n. || 傑作 || The Mona Lisa is a masterpiece. || 《蒙娜麗莎》是一幅傑作。 || literary masterpiece=文學傑作 || The Mona Lisa is a masterpiece ___ by Leonardo da Vinci. || painted|painting|paints || 過去分詞片語表被動，修飾 masterpiece。 || 《蒙娜麗莎》是達文西畫的傑作。
mattress || n. || 床墊 || This mattress is too soft. || 這張床墊太軟了。 || soft mattress=軟床墊 || This mattress is ___ soft for me to sleep well. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不能…。 || 這張床墊太軟，我睡不好。
meantime || n. || 同時；其間 || In the meantime, please wait here. || 在此期間，請在這裡等候。 || in the meantime=在此期間 || The doctor will see you soon. ___ the meantime, please take a seat. || In|On|At || in the meantime = 在此期間。 || 醫生很快就會看你，在此期間請先坐下。
mechanism || n. || 機制 || The body has a defense mechanism. || 身體有防禦機制。 || defense mechanism=防禦機制 || Scientists are studying the mechanism ___ which the virus spreads. || by|with|for || by which = 藉由…的方式。 || 科學家正在研究病毒傳播的機制。
medication || n. || 藥物 || Take this medication twice a day. || 這個藥一天吃兩次。 || take medication=服藥 || He has been ___ medication for his heart problem. || on|in|at || be on medication = 正在服藥。 || 他一直在服用治療心臟問題的藥物。
mentor || n. || 導師 || She was my mentor at work. || 她是我工作上的導師。 || mentor program=導師制度 || Every new teacher is given a mentor ___ guide them. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 每位新老師都會分配一位導師來指導他們。
merge || v. || 合併 || The two companies merged. || 兩家公司合併了。 || merge with=與…合併 || The small company merged ___ a larger one. || with|to|for || merge with = 與…合併。 || 那家小公司與一家較大的公司合併。
metaphor || n. || 隱喻 || "Life is a journey" is a metaphor. || 「人生是一趟旅程」是一個隱喻。 || use a metaphor=使用隱喻 || The poet uses the sea as a metaphor ___ freedom. || for|at|to || a metaphor for = …的隱喻。 || 詩人用大海作為自由的隱喻。
metropolitan || adj. || 大都市的 || The Metropolitan Museum is in New York. || 大都會博物館位於紐約。 || metropolitan area=大都會區 || More than seven million people live in the Taipei metropolitan ___. || area|areas|are || the metropolitan area = 都會區（單數）。 || 超過七百萬人住在台北都會區。
midst || n. || 中間 || In the midst of the storm, the power went out. || 暴風雨中停電了。 || in the midst of=在…之中 || ___ the midst of the meeting, my phone rang. || In|On|At || in the midst of = 在…之中。 || 開會開到一半，我的手機響了。
migration || n. || 遷徙；移居 || Bird migration happens every autumn. || 鳥類每年秋天遷徙。 || bird migration=鳥類遷徙 || Bird migration usually happens ___ autumn. || in|on|at || 季節前用 in。 || 鳥類遷徙通常在秋天發生。
milestone || n. || 里程碑 || Graduation is a milestone in life. || 畢業是人生的里程碑。 || important milestone=重要里程碑 || Getting into college is an important milestone ___ many young people. || for|of|with || for + 對象 = 對…而言。 || 考上大學對許多年輕人是重要的里程碑。
miniature || adj./n. || 小型的；縮圖 || He builds miniature trains. || 他製作迷你火車模型。 || in miniature=小規模的 || He has collected miniature cars ___ he was a child. || since|for|from || since + 子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 他從小就收集迷你汽車。
minimal || adj. || 最小的 || The damage was minimal. || 損害很輕微。 || minimal effort=最少的努力 || The storm caused only minimal damage, ___ was lucky. || which|what|that || 非限定關係子句中 which 可指前面整句。 || 暴風雨只造成輕微損害，這很幸運。
minimize || v. || 減到最少 || We must minimize the risk. || 我們必須把風險降到最低。 || minimize the risk=降低風險 || Wear a helmet ___ minimize the risk of injury. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 戴安全帽以降低受傷的風險。
mint || n. || 薄荷 || I like mint tea. || 我喜歡薄荷茶。 || mint tea=薄荷茶 || Would you like some mint tea ___ coffee? || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 你要薄荷茶還是咖啡？
missionary || n. || 傳教士 || The missionary built a school. || 那位傳教士蓋了一所學校。 || Christian missionary=基督教傳教士 || The hospital was built by a missionary ___ came from Canada. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 這間醫院是由一位來自加拿大的傳教士建立的。
moan || v./n. || 呻吟；抱怨 || He moaned in pain. || 他痛苦地呻吟。 || moan about=抱怨… || Stop moaning ___ the weather! || about|to|with || moan about = 抱怨…。 || 別再抱怨天氣了！
mock || v./adj. || 嘲笑；模擬的 || Don't mock other people. || 不要嘲笑別人。 || mock exam=模擬考 || We have a mock exam ___ Friday. || on|in|at || 星期前用 on。 || 我們星期五有模擬考。
mode || n. || 模式 || Put your phone in silent mode. || 把手機調成靜音模式。 || silent mode=靜音模式 || Please switch your phone ___ silent mode. || to|at|of || switch to = 切換到…。 || 請把手機切換成靜音模式。
modify || v. || 修改 || We modified the plan. || 我們修改了計畫。 || modify a plan=修改計畫 || The plan ___ to be modified because of the rain. || had|has|have || 過去事件；had to = 必須（過去）。 || 因為下雨，計畫必須修改。
molecule || n. || 分子 || Water molecules are very small. || 水分子非常小。 || water molecule=水分子 || A water molecule is made ___ of hydrogen and oxygen. || up|out|down || be made up of = 由…組成。 || 水分子由氫和氧組成。
