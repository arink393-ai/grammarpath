# 學測 L5 08：eternal – gasp
eternal || adj. || 永恆的 || They promised eternal love. || 他們許下永恆的愛。 || eternal love=永恆的愛 || Many people dream of eternal youth, ___ no one can stay young forever. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 許多人夢想永保青春，但沒有人能永遠年輕。
ethic || n. || 倫理；道德（常用 ethics） || She has a strong work ethic. || 她有很強的職業道德。 || work ethic=職業道德 || Medical ethics ___ an important subject for doctors. || is|are|be || ethics 作學科名稱時視為單數。 || 醫學倫理對醫生來說是重要的科目。
ethical || adj. || 道德的 || Is it ethical to test on animals? || 用動物做實驗合乎道德嗎？ || ethical issue=道德議題 || Is it ethical ___ use animals for testing? || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 用動物做實驗合乎道德嗎？
evolution || n. || 演化 || Darwin studied evolution. || 達爾文研究演化。 || theory of evolution=演化論 || Darwin is famous ___ his theory of evolution. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 達爾文以演化論聞名。
evolve || v. || 演化；發展 || Humans evolved from apes. || 人類從猿類演化而來。 || evolve from=由…演化而來 || Birds evolved ___ dinosaurs. || from|of|by || evolve from = 由…演化而來。 || 鳥類是由恐龍演化而來的。
exaggeration || n. || 誇大 || It's no exaggeration to say he is a genius. || 說他是天才一點也不誇張。 || no exaggeration=一點也不誇張 || It is no exaggeration ___ say that she saved my life. || to|for|of || It is no exaggeration to V = 說…一點也不誇張。 || 說她救了我一命一點也不誇張。
exceed || v. || 超過 || Don't exceed the speed limit. || 不要超速。 || exceed the speed limit=超速 || The cost of the trip exceeded our budget ___ 5,000 dollars. || by|with|in || by + 數量 = 差距…。 || 這趟旅行的花費超出預算五千元。
exceptional || adj. || 傑出的；例外的 || She is an exceptional student. || 她是個傑出的學生。 || exceptional talent=傑出天賦 || He has exceptional talent ___ music. || for|to|of || talent for = 對…的天賦。 || 他有傑出的音樂天賦。
excessive || adj. || 過度的 || Excessive drinking is bad for you. || 過度飲酒對你有害。 || excessive use=過度使用 || Excessive use of phones can harm ___ eyes. || your|you|yours || 名詞 eyes 前用所有格形容詞。 || 過度使用手機會傷害你的眼睛。
exclaim || v. || 驚叫 || "Wow!" she exclaimed. || 「哇！」她驚叫道。 || exclaim in surprise=驚訝地大叫 || "It's beautiful!" she exclaimed ___ she saw the sea. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 她看到大海時驚呼：「好美！」
exclude || v. || 排除 || He was excluded from the team. || 他被排除在隊伍之外。 || exclude A from B=把 A 排除在 B 之外 || Nobody should be excluded ___ the activity. || from|of|by || exclude A from B = 把 A 排除在 B 之外。 || 不應該有人被排除在活動之外。
exclusive || adj. || 獨有的；高級的 || This is an exclusive interview. || 這是獨家專訪。 || exclusive interview=獨家專訪 || This offer is exclusive ___ our members. || to|for|with || be exclusive to = 僅限…。 || 這個優惠僅限會員。
execute || v. || 執行；處決 || The plan was executed perfectly. || 計畫被完美執行。 || execute a plan=執行計畫 || The plan ___ executed perfectly by the team. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個計畫被團隊完美執行了。
execution || n. || 執行；死刑 || The execution of the plan took a month. || 執行計畫花了一個月。 || stay of execution=緩刑 || The idea was good, ___ the execution was poor. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反，用 but。 || 這個點子很好，但執行得很差。
executive || n./adj. || 主管；行政的 || She is a senior executive. || 她是一位高階主管。 || chief executive=執行長 || She became the youngest executive ___ the company. || in|on|to || the youngest + in + 團體。 || 她成為公司裡最年輕的主管。
exile || n./v. || 流放；流亡 || The king lived in exile. || 國王流亡在外。 || in exile=流亡 || The writer lived ___ exile for twenty years. || in|on|at || in exile = 流亡中。 || 那位作家流亡了二十年。

expedition || n. || 遠征；探險 || They went on an expedition to the South Pole. || 他們前往南極探險。 || go on an expedition=去探險 || They went ___ an expedition to the Amazon. || on|in|at || go on an expedition = 去探險。 || 他們到亞馬遜探險。

explicit || adj. || 明確的 || The instructions were explicit. || 說明很明確。 || explicit instructions=明確的指示 || The teacher gave us explicit instructions ___ how to do the project. || on|in|at || instructions on = 關於…的指示。 || 老師給了我們如何做專題的明確指示。
exploit || v. || 剝削；利用 || The workers were exploited. || 工人遭到剝削。 || exploit resources=開發資源 || Children should never be exploited ___ cheap labor. || as|to|from || as = 作為…。 || 兒童絕不應被剝削作為廉價勞工。
exploration || n. || 探索 || Space exploration is expensive. || 太空探索很昂貴。 || space exploration=太空探索 || Space exploration has taught us a lot ___ the universe. || about|to|for || about = 關於…。 || 太空探索讓我們對宇宙有更多了解。
extension || n. || 延長；分機 || He asked for an extension. || 他要求延期。 || phone extension=電話分機 || Can I ask ___ an extension on my homework? || for|to|of || ask for = 要求…。 || 我可以要求延後交作業嗎？
extensive || adj. || 廣泛的 || She has extensive knowledge of history. || 她對歷史有廣泛的知識。 || extensive research=廣泛的研究 || The typhoon caused extensive damage ___ the island. || to|for|with || damage to = 對…的損害。 || 颱風對這座島造成大面積的損害。
exterior || n./adj. || 外部 || The exterior of the house is white. || 房子的外觀是白色的。 || exterior wall=外牆 || The exterior of the house ___ painted last month. || was|were|is || 被動語態；主詞單數，過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這棟房子的外觀上個月粉刷過。
external || adj. || 外部的 || This medicine is for external use only. || 這種藥僅供外用。 || external use=外用 || This cream is for external use only, ___ don't eat it. || so|but|or || 前因後果用 so。 || 這種乳膏僅供外用，所以不要吃。
extinct || adj. || 絕種的 || Dinosaurs are extinct. || 恐龍已經絕種了。 || become extinct=絕種 || Many animals may become extinct ___ we do not protect them. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果我們不保護牠們，許多動物可能會絕種。
extraordinary || adj. || 非凡的 || She has an extraordinary voice. || 她有非凡的嗓音。 || extraordinary talent=非凡的才能 || It was an extraordinary experience ___ I will never forget. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 forget 的受詞。 || 那是一次我永遠不會忘記的非凡經歷。
fabric || n. || 布料；結構 || This fabric is soft. || 這塊布料很柔軟。 || cotton fabric=棉布 || This shirt is made ___ a soft cotton fabric. || of|by|at || be made of = 由…製成（看得出原料）。 || 這件襯衫是用柔軟的棉布做的。
fabulous || adj. || 極好的 || We had a fabulous time. || 我們玩得很開心。 || fabulous view=絕佳的景色 || What a fabulous view ___ is! || it|this|that's || What + a + 形容詞 + 名詞 + 主詞 + 動詞！ || 這景色真是太棒了！
facilitate || v. || 促進；使容易 || Technology can facilitate learning. || 科技能促進學習。 || facilitate communication=促進溝通 || The new app was designed ___ facilitate communication between teachers and parents. || to|for|of || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 這個新應用程式是為了促進師生家長溝通而設計的。
