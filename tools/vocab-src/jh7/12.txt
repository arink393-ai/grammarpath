# 七年級 12：trip – zoo
trip || n. || 旅行 || Have a nice trip! || 祝你旅途愉快！ || take a trip=去旅行; school trip=校外教學 || We are going on a trip ___ Hualien. || to|for|at || a trip to + 地點 = 去…的旅行。 || 我們要去花蓮旅行。
try || v. || 嘗試 || Try again. || 再試一次。 || try on=試穿; try one's best=盡力 || Can I try ___ this dress? || on|in|at || try on = 試穿。 || 我可以試穿這件洋裝嗎？
turn || v./n. || 轉；輪到 || Turn left at the corner. || 在轉角左轉。 || turn on=打開; turn off=關掉; your turn=輪到你 || It's your turn ___ sing. || to|for|of || It's your turn to V = 輪到你做…。 || 輪到你唱了。
umbrella || n. || 雨傘 || Take an umbrella with you. || 帶把傘。 || open an umbrella=撐傘 || Don't forget ___ umbrella. It's raining. || your|you|yours || 名詞 umbrella 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 別忘了你的傘，下雨了。
uncle || n. || 叔叔；舅舅 || My uncle is a doctor. || 我叔叔是醫生。 || visit my uncle=去找叔叔 || My uncle ___ in Tainan. || works|work|working || My uncle 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 我叔叔在台南工作。
up || adv. || 向上 || Stand up, please. || 請站起來。 || stand up=站起來; get up=起床; look up=查 || Stand up ___ say your name. || and|but|or || 連接兩個動作用 and。 || 站起來，然後說出你的名字。
use || v. || 使用 || Can I use your pen? || 我可以用你的筆嗎？ || use a computer=用電腦; be used to=習慣於 || Can I use ___ computer? || your|you|yours || 名詞 computer 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 我可以用你的電腦嗎？
vegetable || n. || 蔬菜 || Eat more vegetables. || 多吃蔬菜。 || fresh vegetables=新鮮蔬菜 || How ___ vegetables do you eat every day? || many|much|any || vegetables 可數複數，用 How many。 || 你每天吃多少蔬菜？
visit || v. || 拜訪；參觀 || We visit Grandma every Sunday. || 我們每個星期天去看奶奶。 || visit a museum=參觀博物館 || We visit our grandma ___ Sundays. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我們每個星期天去看奶奶。
wait || v. || 等 || Please wait a minute. || 請等一下。 || wait for=等待; wait a minute=等一下 || I'm waiting ___ the bus. || for|to|at || wait for = 等待…。 || 我在等公車。
wake || v. || 醒來；叫醒 || Wake up! It's seven o'clock. || 起床！七點了。 || wake up=醒來; wake sb up=叫醒某人 || Please wake me up ___ six. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 請在六點叫醒我。
walk || v./n. || 走路；散步 || I walk to school. || 我走路上學。 || take a walk=散步; walk the dog=遛狗 || Let's take a walk ___ the park. || in|on|at || 在公園裡用 in the park。 || 我們去公園散步吧。
wall || n. || 牆 || There is a map on the wall. || 牆上有一張地圖。 || on the wall=在牆上 || There ___ two pictures on the wall. || are|is|be || two pictures 複數，There are。 || 牆上有兩幅畫。
want || v. || 想要 || I want a new bike. || 我想要一台新腳踏車。 || want to V=想要做… || I want ___ a doctor. || to be|be|being || want to + 原形動詞。 || 我想當醫生。
warm || adj. || 溫暖的 || It's warm today. || 今天很暖和。 || keep warm=保暖; warm weather=溫暖的天氣 || It's warm ___ spring. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 春天很溫暖。
wash || v. || 洗 || Wash your hands before dinner. || 晚餐前洗手。 || wash hands=洗手; wash the dishes=洗碗 || Wash your hands ___ you eat. || before|after|until || 吃東西「之前」洗手，用 before。 || 吃東西前要洗手。
watch || v./n. || 看；手錶 || I watch TV every night. || 我每天晚上看電視。 || watch TV=看電視; watch out=小心 || My brother ___ watching TV in the living room. || is|are|does || 現在進行式 be + V-ing；My brother 用 is。 || 我弟弟正在客廳看電視。
water || n. || 水 || Drink more water. || 多喝水。 || a glass of water=一杯水; drink water=喝水 || How ___ water do you drink a day? || much|many|long || water 不可數，用 How much。 || 你一天喝多少水？
way || n. || 路；方法 || Can you tell me the way to the station? || 你可以告訴我去車站的路嗎？ || on the way=在路上; by the way=順帶一提 || Can you tell me the way ___ the library? || to|for|of || the way to + 地點 = 去某地的路。 || 你可以告訴我去圖書館的路嗎？
wear || v. || 穿；戴 || Students wear uniforms at school. || 學生在學校穿制服。 || wear glasses=戴眼鏡; wear a uniform=穿制服 || She ___ wearing a red dress today. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；She 用 is。 || 她今天穿著紅色洋裝。
weather || n. || 天氣 || How is the weather today? || 今天天氣如何？ || good weather=好天氣; weather report=氣象報告 || How ___ the weather today? || is|are|does || the weather 單數，用 is。 || 今天天氣怎麼樣？
week || n. || 週 || There are seven days in a week. || 一週有七天。 || this week=這週; next week=下週 || I go swimming twice ___ week. || a|an|the || 每週幾次用 a week：twice a week。 || 我一週去游泳兩次。
weekend || n. || 週末 || What do you do on weekends? || 你週末都做什麼？ || on weekends=週末時; this weekend=這個週末 || What ___ you do on weekends? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你週末都做什麼？
well || adv. || 很好地 || She sings well. || 她唱得很好。 || do well=表現好; as well=也 || He speaks English very well, ___ he can't speak Japanese. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 他英文說得很好，但不會說日文。
west || n. || 西方 || The sun sets in the west. || 太陽從西邊落下。 || in the west=在西方 || The sun sets ___ the west. || in|on|at || 在某個方位用 in。 || 太陽從西邊落下。
wet || adj. || 濕的 || My shoes are wet. || 我的鞋子濕了。 || get wet=弄濕; wet paint=油漆未乾 || My shoes are wet. Please put ___ in the sun. || them|it|they || shoes 複數，受格用 them。 || 我的鞋子濕了，請把它們放在太陽下。
white || adj./n. || 白色（的） || Snow is white. || 雪是白色的。 || white shirt=白襯衫; black and white=黑白 || The cat is white, and ___ eyes are blue. || its|it's|it || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 那隻貓是白色的，牠的眼睛是藍色的。
wife || n. || 妻子 || His wife is a teacher. || 他的妻子是老師。 || husband and wife=夫妻 || Mr. Chen and ___ wife are teachers. || his|he|him || 名詞 wife 前用所有格形容詞 his。 || 陳先生和他的太太都是老師。
win || v. || 贏 || I hope our team will win. || 我希望我們隊會贏。 || win a game=贏得比賽; win a prize=得獎 || I hope our team ___ win the game tomorrow. || will|is|did || tomorrow 是未來，用 will + 原形動詞。 || 我希望我們隊明天會贏得比賽。
window || n. || 窗戶 || Please open the window. || 請打開窗戶。 || open the window=開窗; window seat=靠窗座位 || Please close the window. It's ___ cold. || too|to|two || too + 形容詞 = 太…。 || 請關窗，太冷了。
winter || n. || 冬天 || It's cold in winter. || 冬天很冷。 || winter vacation=寒假 || It's cold ___ winter. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 冬天很冷。
wish || v./n. || 希望；願望 || Make a wish! || 許個願吧！ || make a wish=許願; best wishes=最好的祝福 || Make a wish ___ blow out the candles. || and|but|or || 連接兩個動作用 and。 || 許個願，然後吹熄蠟燭。
woman || n. || 女人 || The woman is my aunt. || 那位女士是我阿姨。 || women=女人（複數） || The woman ___ long hair is my teacher. || with|in|on || with + 外表特徵 = 有著…的。 || 那位長頭髮的女士是我的老師。
word || n. || 字；話 || How do you spell this word? || 這個字怎麼拼？ || in other words=換句話說; new word=生字 || How do you spell this word? ___ me show you. || Let|Lets|Letting || Let me + 原形動詞 = 讓我…。 || 這個字怎麼拼？我示範給你看。
work || v./n. || 工作 || My parents work hard. || 我的父母工作很努力。 || go to work=去上班; work hard=努力工作 || My mom goes ___ work at eight. || to|at|for || go to work = 去上班。 || 我媽媽八點去上班。
write || v. || 寫 || Write your name here. || 在這裡寫你的名字。 || write down=寫下; write to sb=寫信給某人 || I'm writing a letter ___ my grandma. || to|for|at || write to sb = 寫信給某人。 || 我正在寫信給我奶奶。
yard || n. || 院子 || The kids are playing in the yard. || 孩子們在院子裡玩。 || front yard=前院 || The dog is playing ___ the yard. || in|on|at || 在院子裡用 in the yard。 || 狗正在院子裡玩。
year || n. || 年 || I am twelve years old. || 我十二歲。 || this year=今年; every year=每年 || My sister is two years older ___ me. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 我姊姊比我大兩歲。
yellow || adj./n. || 黃色（的） || Bananas are yellow. || 香蕉是黃色的。 || yellow light=黃燈 || ___ color is the banana? It's yellow. || What|How|Which of || 問顏色用 What color。 || 香蕉是什麼顏色？黃色。
yesterday || adv./n. || 昨天 || I was sick yesterday. || 我昨天生病了。 || the day before yesterday=前天 || Where ___ you yesterday? || were|are|was || yesterday 是過去，主詞 you 用 were。 || 你昨天在哪裡？
young || adj. || 年輕的 || My mom looks young. || 我媽媽看起來很年輕。 || young people=年輕人 || My mom looks young. She ___ forty years old. || is|are|has || 表示年齡用 be 動詞；She 用 is。 || 我媽媽看起來很年輕，她四十歲。
zebra || n. || 斑馬 || A zebra has black and white stripes. || 斑馬有黑白條紋。 || zebra crossing=斑馬線 || A zebra ___ black and white stripes. || has|have|is || A zebra 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 斑馬有黑白相間的條紋。
zoo || n. || 動物園 || Let's go to the zoo. || 我們去動物園吧。 || go to the zoo=去動物園 || We can see pandas ___ the zoo. || at|on|of || at the zoo = 在動物園。 || 我們在動物園可以看到貓熊。
