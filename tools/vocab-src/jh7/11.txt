# 七年級 11：swim – tree
swim || v. || 游泳 || I can swim very well. || 我很會游泳。 || go swimming=去游泳; swim in the sea=在海裡游泳 || Let's go swimming ___ Saturday. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我們星期六去游泳吧。
T-shirt || n. || T 恤 || I like this T-shirt. || 我喜歡這件 T 恤。 || white T-shirt=白 T 恤 || How much ___ these T-shirts? || are|is|does || these T-shirts 複數，用 are。 || 這些 T 恤多少錢？
table || n. || 桌子 || The food is on the table. || 食物在桌上。 || on the table=在桌上; set the table=擺餐具 || Your keys are ___ the table. || on|in|at || 在桌子「上」用 on。 || 你的鑰匙在桌上。
tail || n. || 尾巴 || The dog is wagging its tail. || 那隻狗在搖尾巴。 || wag its tail=搖尾巴 || The monkey has a long tail, and ___ uses it to climb. || it|its|it's || 主詞用主格 it。 || 猴子有一條長尾巴，牠用尾巴來爬樹。
Taiwan || n. || 台灣 || I live in Taiwan. || 我住在台灣。 || in Taiwan=在台灣 || Taiwan is ___ island. || an|a|the || island 以母音音開頭，用 an。 || 台灣是一座島嶼。
take || v. || 拿；帶；搭乘；花費 || I take the bus to school. || 我搭公車上學。 || take a bus=搭公車; take a photo=拍照 || It takes me ten minutes ___ walk to school. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V = 某人花…時間做某事。 || 我走路上學要花十分鐘。
talk || v. || 說話；談話 || Don't talk in class. || 上課不要說話。 || talk to=跟…說話; talk about=談論 || What are you talking ___? || about|to|for || talk about = 談論…。 || 你們在聊什麼？
tall || adj. || 高的 || My brother is very tall. || 我哥哥很高。 || tall building=高樓 || How tall ___ you? || are|is|do || 主詞 you 的 be 動詞用 are。 || 你多高？
taxi || n. || 計程車 || Let's take a taxi. || 我們搭計程車吧。 || take a taxi=搭計程車; by taxi=搭計程車 || We are late. Let's go ___ taxi. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 我們遲到了，搭計程車去吧。
tea || n. || 茶 || Would you like some tea? || 你要喝點茶嗎？ || a cup of tea=一杯茶; green tea=綠茶 || I'd like a cup ___ tea. || of|for|with || a cup of + 飲料 = 一杯…。 || 我想要一杯茶。
teacher || n. || 老師 || My teacher is very kind. || 我的老師很親切。 || English teacher=英文老師 || Our English teacher ___ from Canada. || is|are|am || Our English teacher 單數，用 is。 || 我們的英文老師來自加拿大。
team || n. || 隊伍 || Our team won the game. || 我們隊贏了比賽。 || basketball team=籃球隊; on the team=在隊上 || There are five players ___ our team. || on|at|of || 在隊上用 on the team。 || 我們隊上有五名球員。
telephone || n. || 電話 || The telephone is ringing. || 電話響了。 || telephone number=電話號碼; on the telephone=在講電話 || Can you answer the telephone ___ me? || for|to|of || 替某人做某事用 for。 || 你可以幫我接電話嗎？
television || n. || 電視 || I watch television after dinner. || 我晚餐後看電視。 || watch television=看電視; on television=在電視上 || Don't watch television ___ long. || too|so|to || too long = 太久。 || 不要看電視太久。
tell || v. || 告訴；講述 || Tell me a story. || 講個故事給我聽。 || tell a story=講故事; tell the truth=說實話 || Please tell ___ the truth. || me|I|my || tell 後面的人用受格 me。 || 請告訴我實話。
test || n. || 考試 || We have a math test today. || 我們今天有數學考試。 || take a test=考試; pass the test=考試及格 || We have an English test ___ Friday. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我們星期五有英文考試。
thank || v. || 感謝 || Thank you for your help. || 謝謝你的幫忙。 || thank you for=謝謝你… || Thank you ___ helping me. || for|to|of || thank sb for + 名詞/V-ing = 謝謝某人…。 || 謝謝你幫助我。
then || adv. || 然後；那時 || Wash your hands, then eat. || 先洗手，然後吃東西。 || and then=然後; by then=到那時 || First wash your hands, ___ then eat your lunch. || and|but|or || and then = 然後。 || 先洗手，然後吃午餐。
thin || adj. || 瘦的；薄的 || The cat is too thin. || 那隻貓太瘦了。 || thin book=薄的書 || The dog is thin because it ___ eat much. || doesn't|don't|isn't || it 第三人稱單數，一般動詞否定用 doesn't。 || 那隻狗很瘦，因為牠吃不多。
thing || n. || 事情；東西 || There are many things on the desk. || 書桌上有很多東西。 || many things=很多事; the same thing=同樣的事 || There ___ many things in my bag. || are|is|be || many things 複數，There are。 || 我的包包裡有很多東西。
think || v. || 想；認為 || I think it will rain. || 我認為會下雨。 || think about=考慮; think of=想到 || What do you think ___ this movie? || of|at|to || think of = 認為…怎麼樣。 || 你覺得這部電影怎麼樣？
tie || n./v. || 領帶；綁 || Dad wears a tie to work. || 爸爸打領帶上班。 || wear a tie=打領帶; tie shoes=綁鞋帶 || He is wearing a tie ___ a white shirt. || and|but|or || 兩件都有穿，用 and 連接。 || 他打著領帶，穿著白襯衫。
tiger || n. || 老虎 || The tiger is sleeping. || 老虎在睡覺。 || baby tiger=小老虎 || The tiger is ___ than the cat. || bigger|big|biggest || 兩者比較用比較級 bigger（big 雙寫 g 加 er）。 || 老虎比貓大。
time || n. || 時間 || What time is it? || 現在幾點？ || what time=幾點; on time=準時; have a good time=玩得愉快 || What time ___ it now? || is|are|does || 問時間：What time is it? || 現在幾點？
tired || adj. || 累的 || I'm tired after school. || 我放學後很累。 || feel tired=覺得累; be tired of=厭倦 || ___ you tired now? || Are|Do|Is || 形容詞 tired 的問句用 be 動詞；主詞 you 用 Are。 || 你現在累嗎？
today || adv./n. || 今天 || What day is it today? || 今天星期幾？ || today's news=今天的新聞 || What day ___ it today? || is|are|does || 問星期幾：What day is it today? || 今天星期幾？
toe || n. || 腳趾 || I hurt my toe. || 我弄傷了我的腳趾。 || big toe=大拇趾 || We have ten toes ___ our feet. || on|in|at || 腳趾長在腳「上」用 on。 || 我們的腳上有十根腳趾。
tomato || n. || 番茄 || I don't like tomatoes. || 我不喜歡番茄。 || tomato soup=番茄湯; tomato juice=番茄汁 || Tomatoes ___ red. || are|is|am || Tomatoes 複數，用 are。 || 番茄是紅色的。
tomorrow || adv./n. || 明天 || See you tomorrow. || 明天見。 || see you tomorrow=明天見; tomorrow morning=明天早上 || We ___ going to visit Grandma tomorrow. || are|will|do || be going to + 原形動詞；主詞 We 用 are。 || 我們明天要去看奶奶。
tonight || adv./n. || 今晚 || What are you doing tonight? || 你今晚要做什麼？ || tonight's dinner=今晚的晚餐 || What ___ you going to do tonight? || are|do|is || be going to 的問句：What are you going to do? || 你今晚打算做什麼？
too || adv. || 也；太 || I like dogs, too. || 我也喜歡狗。 || me too=我也是; too much=太多 || The soup is too hot. Wait ___ a minute. || for|to|at || wait for a minute = 等一下。 || 湯太燙了，等一下。
tool || n. || 工具 || A computer is a useful tool. || 電腦是很有用的工具。 || useful tool=有用的工具 || A computer is ___ useful tool. || a|an|the || useful 以子音音 /j/ 開頭，用 a。 || 電腦是一個很有用的工具。
top || n. || 頂端 || We climbed to the top of the mountain. || 我們爬到了山頂。 || on top of=在…上面; the top of=…的頂端 || There is a bird on the top ___ the tree. || of|in|at || the top of = …的頂端。 || 樹頂上有一隻鳥。
town || n. || 城鎮 || I live in a small town. || 我住在一個小鎮。 || small town=小鎮; downtown=市中心 || My grandparents live ___ a small town. || in|on|at || 住在城鎮用 in。 || 我的祖父母住在一個小鎮。
toy || n. || 玩具 || The kids are playing with their toys. || 孩子們在玩他們的玩具。 || toy car=玩具車; toy store=玩具店 || These toys are ___. Don't touch them. || mine|my|me || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 mine。 || 這些玩具是我的，別碰它們。
tree || n. || 樹 || There is a big tree in front of my house. || 我家前面有一棵大樹。 || climb a tree=爬樹; plant trees=種樹 || There ___ many trees in the park. || are|is|be || many trees 複數，There are。 || 公園裡有很多樹。
