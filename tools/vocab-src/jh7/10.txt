# 七年級 10：see – talk
see || v. || 看見 || I can see the mountain from here. || 我從這裡看得到那座山。 || see you=再見; see a doctor=看醫生 || Can you see the bird ___ the tree? || in|on|at || 鳥在樹「上」（樹枝間）用 in the tree。 || 你看得到樹上的鳥嗎？
sell || v. || 賣 || They sell fruit at the market. || 他們在市場賣水果。 || sell out=賣完 || Does the store sell milk? Yes, ___ does. || it|they|its || the store 用代名詞 it；用 Does 問就用 does 答。 || 那家店有賣牛奶嗎？有。
send || v. || 寄；送 || Send me a message. || 傳訊息給我。 || send a letter=寄信; send sb sth=寄某物給某人 || Please send ___ an e-mail. || me|I|my || send 後面的人用受格 me。 || 請寄一封電子郵件給我。
ship || n. || 船 || The ship is very big. || 那艘船非常大。 || by ship=搭船 || The ship ___ leaving the port now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The ship 用 is。 || 那艘船現在正要離港。
shirt || n. || 襯衫 || He is wearing a white shirt. || 他穿著白襯衫。 || T-shirt=T 恤; white shirt=白襯衫 || This shirt is too big ___ me. || for|to|of || too + 形容詞 + for sb = 對某人來說太…。 || 這件襯衫對我來說太大了。
shoes || n. || 鞋子 || These shoes are new. || 這雙鞋是新的。 || a pair of shoes=一雙鞋; put on shoes=穿鞋 || These shoes ___ too small for me. || are|is|am || shoes 複數，用 are。 || 這雙鞋對我來說太小了。
shop || n./v. || 商店；購物 || My mom likes to shop. || 我媽媽喜歡購物。 || go shopping=去購物; shop for=選購 || This shop ___ open until ten at night. || is|are|does || This shop 單數，be 動詞用 is。 || 這家店營業到晚上十點。
short || adj. || 短的；矮的 || My brother is short. || 我弟弟個子矮。 || short hair=短髮 || She has short hair, ___ her sister has long hair. || but|so|or || 前後對比用 but。 || 她是短髮，但她姊姊是長髮。
show || v./n. || 給…看；展示；節目 || Show me your picture. || 給我看你的畫。 || TV show=電視節目; show sb sth=給某人看某物 || Can you show ___ the way to the station? || me|I|my || show 後面的人用受格 me。 || 你可以告訴我去車站的路嗎？
sick || adj. || 生病的 || I'm sick today. || 我今天生病了。 || get sick=生病; feel sick=覺得不舒服 || He is sick, ___ he can't go to school. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 他生病了，所以不能去上學。
side || n. || 邊；側 || Walk on the right side of the road. || 走在路的右側。 || on the left side=在左邊; side by side=並肩 || Walk ___ the left side of the road. || on|in|at || 在…的一側用 on。 || 走在路的左側。
sing || v. || 唱歌 || She can sing very well. || 她很會唱歌。 || sing a song=唱歌 || Listen! Mia ___ singing in her room. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；Mia 用 is。 || 你聽！米亞正在她房間裡唱歌。
sister || n. || 姊妹 || My sister is a nurse. || 我姊姊是護理師。 || big sister=姊姊; little sister=妹妹 || My sister and I ___ in the same school. || are|is|am || 主詞 My sister and I 複數，用 are。 || 我妹妹和我在同一所學校。
sit || v. || 坐 || Please sit down. || 請坐下。 || sit down=坐下; sit on=坐在…上 || Can I sit ___ you? || next to|next|near of || next to = 在…旁邊。 || 我可以坐你旁邊嗎？
size || n. || 尺寸 || What size do you wear? || 你穿什麼尺寸？ || the same size=相同尺寸; large size=大號 || Do you have this shirt in a smaller size? Yes, we ___. || do|are|have || 用 Do you 問，就用 do 回答。 || 這件襯衫有小一點的尺寸嗎？有。
skirt || n. || 裙子 || She is wearing a long skirt. || 她穿著一件長裙。 || long skirt=長裙 || How much ___ this skirt? || is|are|does || this skirt 單數，用 is。 || 這件裙子多少錢？
sky || n. || 天空 || The sky is blue. || 天空是藍色的。 || in the sky=在天空中 || There are many stars ___ the sky. || in|on|at || 在天空中用 in the sky。 || 天空中有很多星星。
sleep || v./n. || 睡覺 || I sleep eight hours a night. || 我一晚睡八小時。 || go to sleep=去睡覺; sleep well=睡得好 || Don't make noise. The baby ___ sleeping. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The baby 用 is。 || 別吵，寶寶在睡覺。
slim || adj. || 苗條的 || She is tall and slim. || 她又高又瘦。 || stay slim=保持苗條 || She is tall ___ slim. || and|but|or || 兩個相似的特質用 and 連接。 || 她又高又苗條。
slow || adj. || 慢的 || The bus is very slow. || 公車很慢。 || slow down=放慢速度 || The turtle is slow, ___ it never stops. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 烏龜很慢，但牠從不停下來。
small || adj. || 小的 || My room is small. || 我的房間很小。 || small town=小鎮 || This shirt is too small ___ me. || for|of|to || too + 形容詞 + for sb = 對某人來說太…。 || 這件襯衫對我來說太小了。
smile || v./n. || 微笑 || She always smiles at me. || 她總是對我微笑。 || smile at=對…微笑; big smile=燦爛的笑容 || She always smiles ___ me. || at|to|for || smile at sb = 對某人微笑。 || 她總是對我微笑。
snake || n. || 蛇 || I'm afraid of snakes. || 我怕蛇。 || be afraid of snakes=怕蛇 || I'm afraid ___ snakes. || of|at|for || be afraid of = 害怕…。 || 我怕蛇。
snow || n./v. || 雪；下雪 || It snows in winter here. || 這裡冬天會下雪。 || play in the snow=玩雪 || Does it snow ___ Taiwan? || in|on|at || 在某個國家、地區用 in。 || 台灣會下雪嗎？
soccer || n. || 足球 || Let's play soccer after school. || 我們放學後去踢足球吧。 || play soccer=踢足球; soccer team=足球隊 || The boys ___ playing soccer now. || are|is|does || 現在進行式；The boys 複數用 are。 || 男孩們現在正在踢足球。
socks || n. || 襪子 || I need a pair of socks. || 我需要一雙襪子。 || a pair of socks=一雙襪子 || Where ___ my socks? || are|is|am || my socks 複數，用 are。 || 我的襪子在哪裡？
sofa || n. || 沙發 || The cat is sleeping on the sofa. || 貓在沙發上睡覺。 || on the sofa=在沙發上 || Dad is sitting ___ the sofa. || on|in|at || 在沙發「上」用 on。 || 爸爸正坐在沙發上。
son || n. || 兒子 || They have two sons. || 他們有兩個兒子。 || only son=獨子 || Mr. Lin has a son. ___ son is a doctor. || His|He|Him || 名詞 son 前用所有格形容詞 His。 || 林先生有一個兒子，他的兒子是醫生。
song || n. || 歌曲 || This song is very popular. || 這首歌很流行。 || sing a song=唱歌; favorite song=最喜歡的歌 || What's your favorite song? My favorite song ___ "Happy." || is|are|am || My favorite song 單數，用 is。 || 你最喜歡的歌是什麼？是〈Happy〉。
soon || adv. || 很快地；不久 || See you soon. || 待會見。 || as soon as=一…就…; soon after=不久之後 || Summer vacation is coming soon. I ___ very excited. || am|is|are || 主詞 I 用 am。 || 暑假快到了，我好興奮。
sorry || adj. || 抱歉的 || I'm sorry. I'm late. || 對不起，我遲到了。 || I'm sorry=對不起; feel sorry for=為…感到難過 || I'm sorry ___ being late. || for|of|to || be sorry for + 名詞/V-ing = 為…感到抱歉。 || 很抱歉我遲到了。
soup || n. || 湯 || This soup is hot. || 這湯很燙。 || a bowl of soup=一碗湯; chicken soup=雞湯 || Would you like a bowl ___ soup? || of|for|with || a bowl of = 一碗…。 || 你要來一碗湯嗎？
speak || v. || 說（語言）；講話 || Can you speak English? || 你會說英文嗎？ || speak English=說英文; speak to=跟…說話 || Can I speak ___ Amy, please? || to|at|for || speak to sb = 跟某人說話（電話用語）。 || 請問艾咪在嗎？（我可以跟艾咪說話嗎？）
spring || n. || 春天 || Flowers bloom in spring. || 春天花開。 || in spring=在春天 || It's warm ___ spring. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 春天很溫暖。
star || n. || 星星；明星 || There are many stars in the sky. || 天空有很多星星。 || movie star=電影明星; shooting star=流星 || He is a big movie star, ___ he is very kind. || and|so|or || 連接兩個並列的敘述用 and。 || 他是大明星，而且人很好。
stay || v. || 停留；保持 || Stay at home. It's raining. || 待在家，外面在下雨。 || stay at home=待在家; stay up=熬夜 || Don't stay ___ late. || up|in|on || stay up late = 熬夜。 || 不要熬夜。
stop || v. || 停止 || Stop talking, please. || 請停止講話。 || stop + V-ing=停止做…; bus stop=公車站 || Please stop ___. The baby is sleeping. || talking|talk|to talking || stop + V-ing = 停止做某事。 || 請別說話了，寶寶在睡覺。
student || n. || 學生 || I am a junior high school student. || 我是國中生。 || good student=好學生 || There ___ thirty students in our class. || are|is|be || thirty students 複數，There are。 || 我們班有三十個學生。
study || v. || 讀書；學習 || I study English every day. || 我每天讀英文。 || study for=為…讀書; study hard=用功讀書 || He is studying ___ the test. || for|to|at || study for = 為…而讀書。 || 他正在為考試讀書。
summer || n. || 夏天 || It's hot in summer. || 夏天很熱。 || summer vacation=暑假 || We go swimming ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 我們夏天去游泳。
sun || n. || 太陽 || The sun is shining. || 太陽正照耀著。 || in the sun=在陽光下; the sun rises=太陽升起 || The sun ___ in the east. || rises|rise|rising || The sun 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 太陽從東方升起。
sunny || adj. || 晴朗的 || It's sunny today. || 今天是晴天。 || sunny day=晴天 || It's sunny today. Let's ___ to the beach. || go|goes|going || Let's 後面接原形動詞。 || 今天天氣晴朗，我們去海邊吧。
sure || adj. || 確定的 || Are you sure? || 你確定嗎？ || be sure=確定; make sure=確認 || Can you help me? Sure, I ___. || can|do|am || 用 Can you 問，就用 can 回答。 || 你可以幫我嗎？當然可以。
sweater || n. || 毛衣 || Wear a sweater. It's cold. || 穿件毛衣，天氣很冷。 || wear a sweater=穿毛衣 || Your sweater is nice. Where did you buy ___? || it|them|its || your sweater 單數，受格用 it。 || 你的毛衣很好看，你在哪裡買的？
