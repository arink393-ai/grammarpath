# 七年級 06：hotel – left
hotel || n. || 旅館；飯店 || We stayed at a nice hotel. || 我們住在一間不錯的飯店。 || stay at a hotel=住飯店 || Is there a hotel ___ here? || near|nearly|nearby of || near + 地點 = 在…附近。 || 這附近有旅館嗎？
hour || n. || 小時 || I sleep eight hours a night. || 我一晚睡八小時。 || an hour=一小時; for hours=好幾個小時 || I study English for ___ hour every day. || an|a|one of || hour 的 h 不發音，以母音音開頭，用 an。 || 我每天讀一小時英文。
house || n. || 房子 || My house is near the park. || 我家在公園附近。 || big house=大房子; at my house=在我家 || There ___ three bedrooms in my house. || are|is|be || three bedrooms 複數，There are。 || 我家有三間臥室。
hungry || adj. || 餓的 || I'm hungry. Let's eat. || 我餓了，我們吃飯吧。 || feel hungry=覺得餓 || I'm hungry. Let's ___ lunch. || eat|eats|eating || Let's 後面接原形動詞。 || 我餓了，我們吃午餐吧。
ice || n. || 冰 || Put some ice in my drink. || 在我的飲料裡加些冰塊。 || ice cube=冰塊 || There isn't ___ ice in the fridge. || any|some|many || 否定句用 any；ice 不可數。 || 冰箱裡沒有冰塊了。
ice cream || n. || 冰淇淋 || I love chocolate ice cream. || 我愛巧克力冰淇淋。 || eat ice cream=吃冰淇淋 || Would you like ___ ice cream? || some|many|a few || 請別人吃東西用 some；ice cream 不可數。 || 你要來點冰淇淋嗎？
idea || n. || 主意；想法 || That's a good idea. || 那是個好主意。 || good idea=好主意; have no idea=不知道 || That's ___ great idea! || a|an|the || great 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 那真是個好主意！
jacket || n. || 夾克；外套 || Take your jacket with you. || 帶著你的外套。 || put on a jacket=穿上外套 || Put ___ your jacket. It's windy. || on|in|at || put on = 穿上。 || 穿上你的外套，風很大。
jeans || n. || 牛仔褲 || I like to wear jeans. || 我喜歡穿牛仔褲。 || a pair of jeans=一條牛仔褲 || These jeans ___ too long for me. || are|is|am || jeans 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 這條牛仔褲對我來說太長了。
job || n. || 工作 || My dad has a new job. || 我爸爸有新工作。 || good job=做得好; find a job=找工作 || What ___ your mother's job? || is|are|does || your mother's job 單數，用 is。 || 你媽媽的工作是什麼？
joke || n. || 笑話 || He tells funny jokes. || 他講好笑的笑話。 || tell a joke=講笑話; make a joke=開玩笑 || Tell ___ a joke, please. || us|we|our || tell 後面的人用受格 us。 || 請講個笑話給我們聽。
juice || n. || 果汁 || I drink orange juice every morning. || 我每天早上喝柳橙汁。 || orange juice=柳橙汁; a glass of juice=一杯果汁 || Is there ___ juice in the fridge? || any|many|a || 疑問句用 any；juice 不可數。 || 冰箱裡有果汁嗎？
jump || v. || 跳 || The cat can jump very high. || 那隻貓可以跳得很高。 || jump rope=跳繩; jump into=跳進 || Look! The kids ___ jumping rope. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；The kids 複數用 are。 || 你看！孩子們在跳繩。
just || adv. || 只是；剛剛 || I'm just kidding. || 我只是開玩笑的。 || just a moment=稍等一下; just now=剛才 || It's just ___ joke. Don't be angry. || a|an|the || joke 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 這只是個玩笑，別生氣。
keep || v. || 保持；保存 || Keep the door open. || 讓門開著。 || keep quiet=保持安靜; keep going=繼續 || Please keep ___ in the library. || quiet|quietly|quite || keep + 形容詞 = 保持…（的狀態）。 || 在圖書館請保持安靜。
key || n. || 鑰匙 || I lost my key. || 我弄丟了我的鑰匙。 || car key=車鑰匙; the key to=…的關鍵 || Where ___ my keys? || are|is|am || my keys 複數，用 are。 || 我的鑰匙在哪裡？
kick || v. || 踢 || Don't kick the chair. || 不要踢椅子。 || kick a ball=踢球 || Don't kick the door. You ___ hurt your foot. || will|are|do || 表示將會發生的結果用 will + 原形動詞。 || 別踢門，你會傷到腳。
kid || n. || 小孩 || The kids are playing outside. || 孩子們在外面玩。 || kid brother=小弟弟 || The kids ___ in the park now. || are|is|be || The kids 複數，用 are。 || 孩子們現在在公園裡。
kind || adj./n. || 仁慈的；種類 || She is kind to everyone. || 她對每個人都很親切。 || be kind to=對…親切; a kind of=一種 || She is always kind ___ animals. || to|for|with || be kind to = 對…親切。 || 她對動物總是很仁慈。
king || n. || 國王 || The king lives in a castle. || 國王住在城堡裡。 || the king of=…之王 || The lion is the king ___ the jungle. || of|in|for || the king of = …之王。 || 獅子是叢林之王。
kiss || v./n. || 親吻 || Mom kisses me goodnight. || 媽媽親我道晚安。 || kiss goodbye=吻別 || Mom kisses ___ goodnight every night. || me|I|my || kisses 後面接受詞，用受格 me。 || 媽媽每天晚上親我道晚安。
kitchen || n. || 廚房 || Mom is cooking in the kitchen. || 媽媽在廚房煮飯。 || in the kitchen=在廚房 || Mom ___ cooking in the kitchen. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；Mom 用 is。 || 媽媽正在廚房煮飯。
kite || n. || 風箏 || Let's fly a kite. || 我們去放風箏吧。 || fly a kite=放風箏 || It's windy. Let's ___ a kite. || fly|flies|flying || Let's 後面接原形動詞。 || 風很大，我們去放風箏吧。
knee || n. || 膝蓋 || I hurt my knee. || 我的膝蓋受傷了。 || on one's knees=跪著 || I hurt my knee, ___ I can't play basketball today. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 我膝蓋受傷了，所以今天不能打籃球。
knife || n. || 刀子 || Be careful with the knife. || 使用刀子要小心。 || knife and fork=刀叉 || Be careful ___ the knife. || with|of|at || be careful with = 小心使用…。 || 使用刀子要小心。
know || v. || 知道；認識 || I know the answer. || 我知道答案。 || know about=知道…的事; you know=你知道的 || ___ you know his name? || Do|Are|Is || 一般動詞 know 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 Do。 || 你知道他的名字嗎？
lake || n. || 湖 || We swim in the lake in summer. || 我們夏天在湖裡游泳。 || Sun Moon Lake=日月潭 || Sun Moon Lake is ___ Nantou. || in|on|at || 在某個地區裡面用 in。 || 日月潭在南投。
lamp || n. || 燈 || Turn on the lamp, please. || 請打開檯燈。 || desk lamp=檯燈 || Please turn ___ the lamp. It's dark. || on|in|up || turn on = 打開（電器）。 || 請打開燈，好暗。
large || adj. || 大的 || I'd like a large Coke. || 我要一杯大杯可樂。 || large size=大號 || This shirt is too large ___ me. || for|of|at || too + 形容詞 + for sb = 對某人來說太…。 || 這件襯衫對我來說太大了。
last || adj. || 最後的；上一個 || I saw him last week. || 我上週看到他。 || last week=上週; at last=終於 || I ___ at home last Sunday. || was|am|were || last Sunday 表示過去，主詞 I 用 was。 || 我上週日在家。
late || adj./adv. || 遲的；晚的 || Don't be late for school. || 上學不要遲到。 || be late for=…遲到; late at night=深夜 || Don't be late ___ school. || for|to|at || be late for = …遲到。 || 上學不要遲到。
later || adv. || 稍後 || See you later. || 待會見。 || see you later=待會見; two days later=兩天後 || I'll call you later. I ___ busy now. || am|is|are || 主詞 I 用 am。 || 我晚點打給你，我現在很忙。
laugh || v. || 笑 || Don't laugh at him. || 不要嘲笑他。 || laugh at=嘲笑; laugh out loud=大笑 || Don't laugh ___ other people. || at|to|for || laugh at = 嘲笑。 || 不要嘲笑別人。
lazy || adj. || 懶惰的 || Don't be lazy. || 不要懶惰。 || lazy cat=懶貓 || My cat is lazy. It sleeps all day ___. || long|longer|longly || all day long = 一整天。 || 我的貓很懶，整天睡覺。
learn || v. || 學習 || I learn English at school. || 我在學校學英文。 || learn to V=學習做…; learn from=向…學習 || I want to learn ___ swim. || how to|what to|to how || learn how to V = 學習如何做…。 || 我想學游泳。
leave || v. || 離開 || I leave home at seven. || 我七點出門。 || leave for=前往; leave home=離開家 || The train leaves ___ Taipei at nine. || for|to|at || leave for + 目的地 = 出發前往…。 || 火車九點出發前往台北。
left || adj./n. || 左邊（的） || Turn left at the corner. || 在轉角左轉。 || turn left=左轉; on the left=在左邊 || The bank is ___ your left. || on|in|at || 在左邊用 on your left。 || 銀行在你的左邊。
