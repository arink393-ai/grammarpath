# 七年級 09：pet – sea
pet || n. || 寵物 || Do you have a pet? || 你有養寵物嗎？ || pet dog=寵物狗; pet shop=寵物店 || ___ you have any pets? || Do|Are|Does || 一般動詞 have 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 Do。 || 你有養寵物嗎？
photo || n. || 照片 || Let's take a photo. || 我們來拍張照吧。 || take a photo=拍照; family photo=全家福 || Can you take a photo ___ us? || of|for|to || take a photo of sb = 幫某人拍照。 || 你可以幫我們拍張照嗎？
piano || n. || 鋼琴 || She plays the piano every day. || 她每天彈鋼琴。 || play the piano=彈鋼琴; piano lesson=鋼琴課 || She plays ___ piano very well. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 她鋼琴彈得很好。
pick || v. || 挑選；摘；撿 || Pick a card. || 選一張牌。 || pick up=撿起、接人; pick flowers=摘花 || Please pick ___ the trash. || up|on|out || pick up = 撿起來。 || 請把垃圾撿起來。
picture || n. || 圖畫；照片 || Look at this picture. || 看這張照片。 || take a picture=拍照; draw a picture=畫畫 || Look ___ this picture. || at|to|for || look at = 看…。 || 看這張照片。
pie || n. || 派 || Mom makes an apple pie. || 媽媽做了一個蘋果派。 || apple pie=蘋果派; a piece of pie=一塊派 || Would you like a piece ___ pie? || of|for|in || a piece of = 一塊…。 || 你要來一塊派嗎？
pig || n. || 豬 || The pig is eating. || 豬在吃東西。 || pig out=大吃大喝 || There ___ three pigs on the farm. || are|is|be || three pigs 複數，There are。 || 農場裡有三隻豬。
pink || adj./n. || 粉紅色（的） || She likes pink. || 她喜歡粉紅色。 || pink dress=粉紅色洋裝 || Her bag is pink, and ___ shoes are pink too. || her|she|hers || 名詞 shoes 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 她的包包是粉紅色的，鞋子也是。
pizza || n. || 披薩 || Let's order a pizza. || 我們點一個披薩吧。 || a slice of pizza=一片披薩 || Let's ___ a pizza for dinner. || order|orders|ordering || Let's 後面接原形動詞。 || 我們晚餐點披薩吧。
place || n. || 地方 || This is a nice place. || 這是個好地方。 || take place=舉行; in place=在適當位置 || Taipei is a nice place ___ visit. || to|for|of || a place to V = 一個可以做…的地方。 || 台北是個值得造訪的好地方。
plan || n./v. || 計畫 || What are your plans for the weekend? || 你週末有什麼計畫？ || make a plan=訂計畫; plan to V=計畫做… || We plan ___ a party for Mom. || to have|having|have || plan to + 原形動詞。 || 我們計畫為媽媽辦一場派對。
plant || n./v. || 植物；種植 || Water the plants every day. || 每天替植物澆水。 || water the plants=澆花; plant trees=種樹 || Don't forget ___ water the plants. || to|for|of || forget to V = 忘記要去做…。 || 別忘了替植物澆水。
plate || n. || 盤子 || Put the cake on the plate. || 把蛋糕放在盤子上。 || a plate of=一盤 || There is some rice ___ the plate. || on|in|at || 在盤子「上」用 on。 || 盤子上有一些飯。
play || v. || 玩；打（球）；演奏 || Let's play soccer. || 我們去踢足球吧。 || play basketball=打籃球; play the piano=彈鋼琴 || My brother is playing ___ his friends. || with|to|at || play with = 和…玩。 || 我弟弟正在和他的朋友玩。
playground || n. || 操場；遊樂場 || The kids are on the playground. || 孩子們在操場上。 || on the playground=在操場上 || The students are playing ___ the playground. || on|in|at || 在操場上用 on the playground。 || 學生們正在操場上玩。
please || adv. || 請 || Please sit down. || 請坐。 || yes, please=好的，麻煩了 || Please ___ quiet. || be|are|is || Please + 原形動詞；be quiet = 安靜。 || 請安靜。
pool || n. || 游泳池 || We swim in the pool. || 我們在游泳池游泳。 || swimming pool=游泳池 || There ___ a big pool in our school. || is|are|be || a big pool 單數，There is。 || 我們學校有一座大游泳池。
poor || adj. || 貧窮的；可憐的 || The poor dog is hungry. || 那隻可憐的狗很餓。 || poor people=窮人; poor thing=可憐的東西 || The poor cat is cold. Let's help ___. || it|them|its || 動物單數用 it；help 後接受格 it。 || 那隻可憐的貓很冷，我們幫幫牠吧。
price || n. || 價格 || What's the price of this bag? || 這個包包多少錢？ || the price of=…的價格; low price=低價 || What's the price ___ this T-shirt? || of|in|at || the price of + 物品 = 某物的價格。 || 這件 T 恤的價格是多少？
pull || v. || 拉 || Pull the door. || 拉門。 || pull out=拔出 || Don't pull the cat's tail, ___ it will bite you. || or|and|so || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 別拉貓的尾巴，否則牠會咬你。
push || v. || 推 || Push the door to open it. || 推門把它打開。 || push the button=按按鈕 || Don't push. Wait ___ line. || in|on|at || wait in line = 排隊。 || 不要推，請排隊。
put || v. || 放 || Put your bag on the chair. || 把你的包包放在椅子上。 || put on=穿上; put away=收好 || Put your books ___ your bag. || in|at|to || 放進某物「裡」用 in。 || 把你的書放進書包裡。
queen || n. || 皇后；女王 || The queen lives in a palace. || 女王住在宮殿裡。 || the Queen of England=英國女王 || The queen ___ a beautiful crown. || has|have|having || The queen 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 女王有一頂美麗的皇冠。
quick || adj. || 快速的 || Let's have a quick lunch. || 我們快速吃個午餐吧。 || quick question=小問題; be quick=快一點 || Be quick, ___ we will be late. || or|and|but || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 快一點，不然我們會遲到。
quiet || adj. || 安靜的 || Please be quiet in class. || 上課請安靜。 || keep quiet=保持安靜 || The baby is sleeping. ___ be quiet. || Please|Pleases|Pleasing || 客氣的祈使句：Please + 原形動詞。 || 寶寶在睡覺，請安靜。
rabbit || n. || 兔子 || The rabbit has long ears. || 兔子有長耳朵。 || white rabbit=白兔 || The rabbit ___ eating a carrot. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The rabbit 用 is。 || 兔子正在吃紅蘿蔔。
race || n. || 比賽（競速） || He won the race. || 他贏得了賽跑。 || run a race=賽跑; boat race=划船比賽 || Who is the ___ runner in the race? || fastest|faster|fast || 三者以上比較用最高級 the fastest。 || 誰是這場賽跑中最快的跑者？
radio || n. || 收音機；廣播 || My grandpa listens to the radio. || 我爺爺聽廣播。 || listen to the radio=聽廣播; on the radio=在廣播上 || My grandpa listens ___ the radio every morning. || to|at|for || listen to = 聽…。 || 我爺爺每天早上聽廣播。
rain || n./v. || 雨；下雨 || It rains a lot in June. || 六月常下雨。 || heavy rain=大雨; in the rain=在雨中 || It rains a lot ___ spring. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 春天常下雨。
rainy || adj. || 下雨的 || It's a rainy day. || 今天是雨天。 || rainy day=雨天; rainy season=雨季 || It's rainy today, ___ take an umbrella. || so|but|because || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 今天下雨，所以帶把傘吧。
read || v. || 讀；閱讀 || I read books every night. || 我每天晚上看書。 || read a book=看書; read aloud=朗讀 || She ___ reading a book now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；She 用 is。 || 她現在正在看書。
ready || adj. || 準備好的 || Are you ready? || 你準備好了嗎？ || be ready for=為…準備好; get ready=做好準備 || Are you ready ___ the test? || for|to|at || be ready for + 名詞 = 為…準備好。 || 你準備好考試了嗎？
real || adj. || 真的；真實的 || Is this a real flower? || 這是真的花嗎？ || real life=現實生活 || Is this story real? No, ___ isn't. || it|this|its || 回答時用代名詞 it 代替 this story。 || 這個故事是真的嗎？不是。
red || adj./n. || 紅色（的） || Stop at the red light. || 紅燈要停。 || red light=紅燈; red bean=紅豆 || Stop when the light is red, ___ go when it is green. || and|or|so || 連接兩個指令用 and。 || 紅燈停，綠燈行。
rice || n. || 米飯 || We eat rice every day. || 我們每天吃飯。 || fried rice=炒飯; a bowl of rice=一碗飯 || I want two bowls ___ rice. || of|for|with || a bowl of = 一碗…。 || 我要兩碗飯。
ride || v. || 騎；搭乘 || I ride my bike to school. || 我騎腳踏車上學。 || ride a bike=騎腳踏車; ride a horse=騎馬 || My sister rides ___ bike to school. || her|she|hers || 名詞 bike 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 我姊姊騎她的腳踏車上學。
right || adj./n. || 右邊的；對的 || Turn right at the corner. || 在轉角右轉。 || turn right=右轉; that's right=沒錯; all right=好的 || The post office is ___ your right. || on|in|at || 在右邊用 on your right。 || 郵局在你的右邊。
ring || n./v. || 戒指；（鈴）響 || The phone is ringing. || 電話響了。 || ring the bell=按鈴; wedding ring=結婚戒指 || The phone is ringing. Can you answer ___? || it|them|its || the phone 單數，受格用 it。 || 電話響了，你可以接嗎？
river || n. || 河 || We swim in the river. || 我們在河裡游泳。 || along the river=沿著河 || Don't swim in the river. It's ___ dangerous. || too|to|two || too + 形容詞 = 太…。 || 不要在河裡游泳，太危險了。
road || n. || 道路 || Be careful when you cross the road. || 過馬路時要小心。 || cross the road=過馬路; on the road=在路上 || There are many cars ___ the road. || on|in|at || 在路上用 on the road。 || 路上有很多車。
robot || n. || 機器人 || The robot can talk. || 這個機器人會說話。 || robot arm=機器手臂 || This robot can ___ the floor. || clean|cleans|cleaning || can 後面接原形動詞。 || 這個機器人會掃地。
rock || n. || 岩石；搖滾樂 || He likes rock music. || 他喜歡搖滾樂。 || rock music=搖滾樂; rock band=搖滾樂團 || There are many rocks ___ the beach. || on|in|of || 在海灘上用 on the beach。 || 海灘上有很多岩石。
room || n. || 房間 || This is my room. || 這是我的房間。 || living room=客廳; clean one's room=打掃房間 || Please clean ___ room. || your|you|yours || 名詞 room 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 請打掃你的房間。
rose || n. || 玫瑰 || He gave her a red rose. || 他送她一朵紅玫瑰。 || red rose=紅玫瑰 || These roses ___ beautiful. || are|is|am || These roses 複數，用 are。 || 這些玫瑰好漂亮。
round || adj. || 圓的 || The Earth is round. || 地球是圓的。 || round table=圓桌 || The moon is round ___ the Moon Festival. || at|in|on || at（在…時候）+ 節日：at the Moon Festival。 || 中秋節的月亮是圓的。
ruler || n. || 尺 || Can I borrow your ruler? || 我可以借你的尺嗎？ || use a ruler=用尺 || Draw a line ___ a ruler. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 用尺畫一條線。
run || v. || 跑 || He runs every morning. || 他每天早上跑步。 || run fast=跑得快; run away=逃跑 || Don't run ___ the classroom. || in|on|at || 在教室裡面用 in。 || 不要在教室裡奔跑。
sad || adj. || 悲傷的 || Why are you sad? || 你為什麼難過？ || feel sad=覺得難過 || Don't be sad. Everything will be ___. || fine|finely|fined || be 動詞後面接形容詞 fine。 || 別難過，一切都會沒事的。
salad || n. || 沙拉 || I have a salad for lunch. || 我午餐吃沙拉。 || fruit salad=水果沙拉 || Would you like ___ salad? || some|many|a few || 請別人吃東西用 some。 || 你要來些沙拉嗎？
salt || n. || 鹽 || Don't put too much salt in the soup. || 湯裡別放太多鹽。 || a little salt=一點鹽 || Can you pass ___ the salt? || me|I|my || pass sb sth：pass 後面的人用受格 me。 || 你可以把鹽遞給我嗎？
same || adj. || 相同的 || We are in the same class. || 我們在同一班。 || the same as=和…一樣 || Your bag is the same ___ mine. || as|like|to || the same as = 和…一樣。 || 你的包包和我的一樣。
sandwich || n. || 三明治 || I made a sandwich for lunch. || 我做了一個三明治當午餐。 || ham sandwich=火腿三明治 || I'd like ___ egg sandwich. || an|a|the || egg 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我要一個蛋三明治。
save || v. || 節省；拯救 || Turn off the lights to save energy. || 關燈來節省能源。 || save money=存錢; save water=省水 || We should save water ___ energy. || and|but|or || 兩者都要，用 and 連接。 || 我們應該節約用水和能源。
say || v. || 說 || What did you say? || 你說什麼？ || say hello=打招呼; say goodbye=道別 || Say goodbye ___ your friends. || to|for|at || say goodbye to sb = 向某人道別。 || 跟你的朋友說再見。
school || n. || 學校 || I go to school at seven. || 我七點去上學。 || go to school=上學; after school=放學後 || We don't go to school ___ Sundays. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我們星期天不用上學。
sea || n. || 海 || Fish live in the sea. || 魚住在海裡。 || by the sea=在海邊; sea water=海水 || There are many fish ___ the sea. || in|on|at || 在海裡用 in the sea。 || 海裡有很多魚。
