# 七年級 02：belt – cheap
belt || n. || 皮帶；腰帶 || This belt is too long. || 這條皮帶太長了。 || seat belt=安全帶; wear a belt=繫皮帶 || Please fasten ___ seat belt. || your|you|yours || 名詞 seat belt 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 請繫好你的安全帶。
bench || n. || 長椅 || Let's sit on the bench. || 我們坐在長椅上吧。 || sit on a bench=坐在長椅上; park bench=公園長椅 || Two old men ___ sitting on the bench. || are|is|does || 現在進行式；Two old men 複數用 are。 || 兩位老先生正坐在長椅上。
big || adj. || 大的 || Your house is very big. || 你的房子很大。 || big city=大城市; a big fan of=…的忠實粉絲 || This box is too big ___ me. || for|of|to || too + 形容詞 + for sb = 對某人來說太…。 || 這個箱子對我來說太大了。
bird || n. || 鳥 || A bird is singing in the tree. || 一隻鳥在樹上唱歌。 || early bird=早起的人; bird's nest=鳥巢 || Look! The birds ___ flying in the sky. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；The birds 複數用 are。 || 你看！鳥兒在天空飛。
birthday || n. || 生日 || Happy birthday to you! || 祝你生日快樂！ || birthday party=生日派對; birthday cake=生日蛋糕 || My birthday is ___ May 5. || on|in|at || 特定的日期前面用 on。 || 我的生日是五月五日。
bite || v./n. || 咬 || The dog didn't bite me. || 那隻狗沒有咬我。 || take a bite=咬一口; mosquito bite=蚊子咬的包 || Don't worry. My dog ___ bite. || doesn't|don't|isn't || My dog 第三人稱單數，否定用 doesn't + 原形。 || 別擔心，我的狗不會咬人。
black || adj./n. || 黑色（的） || She has long black hair. || 她有一頭黑色長髮。 || black and white=黑白; black tea=紅茶 || My cat is black, and ___ eyes are yellow. || its|it's|it || 貓用 it；所有格是 its（不加撇號）。 || 我的貓是黑色的，牠的眼睛是黃色的。
blow || v. || 吹 || The wind is blowing hard. || 風吹得很大。 || blow out=吹熄; blow one's nose=擤鼻涕 || The wind ___ blowing hard today. || is|are|does || 現在進行式 be + V-ing；The wind 單數用 is。 || 今天風吹得很大。
blue || adj./n. || 藍色（的） || The sky is blue today. || 今天天空是藍色的。 || light blue=淺藍色; dark blue=深藍色 || ___ color is the sky? It's blue. || What|Which|How || 問顏色用 What color。 || 天空是什麼顏色？藍色。
boat || n. || 船 || We take a boat to the island. || 我們搭船去那個島。 || by boat=搭船; take a boat=搭船 || We go to Green Island ___ boat. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by（中間不加冠詞）。 || 我們搭船去綠島。
body || n. || 身體 || Exercise is good for your body. || 運動對你的身體有益。 || body parts=身體部位; mind and body=身心 || Water is good for ___ body. || your|you|yours || 名詞 body 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 水對你的身體有益。
book || n. || 書 || I read a book every week. || 我每週讀一本書。 || read a book=看書; comic book=漫畫書 || These books ___ very interesting. || are|is|am || These books 複數，用 are。 || 這些書很有趣。
bored || adj. || 感到無聊的 || I am bored. Let's go out. || 我好無聊，我們出去吧。 || feel bored=覺得無聊; bored with=對…感到厭煩 || The students look bored ___ the long speech. || with|at|in || be/look bored with = 對…感到厭煩。 || 學生們對冗長的演講顯得很無聊。
born || adj. || 出生的 || I was born in Taichung. || 我在台中出生。 || be born in=出生於 || I was born ___ 2013. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 我在 2013 年出生。
boss || n. || 老闆；上司 || My boss is very kind. || 我的老闆很親切。 || the boss of=…的老闆 || The boss ___ to work at eight every day. || comes|come|coming || The boss 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 老闆每天八點來上班。
both || pron./adj. || 兩者都 || Both of my parents are teachers. || 我的父母都是老師。 || both A and B=A 和 B 都; both of=…兩者都 || Both Tom and Amy ___ good at math. || are|is|am || both A and B 當主詞視為複數，用 are。 || 湯姆和艾咪數學都很好。
bowl || n. || 碗 || I want a bowl of rice. || 我要一碗飯。 || a bowl of=一碗; rice bowl=飯碗 || There ___ two bowls on the table. || are|is|be || two bowls 複數，There are。 || 桌上有兩個碗。
box || n. || 箱子；盒子 || What's in the box? || 盒子裡有什麼？ || lunch box=便當盒; a box of=一盒 || What's in the box? Let's open ___. || it|them|its || the box 是單數物品，受格用 it。 || 盒子裡有什麼？我們打開它吧。
boy || n. || 男孩 || The boy is my brother. || 那個男孩是我弟弟。 || little boy=小男孩; boys and girls=男孩女孩們 || Two boys ___ playing soccer. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；Two boys 複數用 are。 || 兩個男孩正在踢足球。
brave || adj. || 勇敢的 || The brave boy saved the cat. || 那個勇敢的男孩救了貓。 || be brave=要勇敢 || Be brave and ___ afraid. || don't be|not be|doesn't be || 否定祈使句：Don't + 原形（be）。 || 勇敢一點，別害怕。
bread || n. || 麵包 || I have bread and milk for breakfast. || 我早餐吃麵包配牛奶。 || a piece of bread=一片麵包 || I want two ___ of bread. || pieces|piece|breads || bread 不可數，用 two pieces of bread。 || 我要兩片麵包。
break || v./n. || 打破；休息 || Don't break the glass. || 別打破玻璃杯。 || take a break=休息一下; break the rules=違反規則 || Let's ___ a break. || take|takes|taking || Let's 後面接原形動詞；take a break = 休息一下。 || 我們休息一下吧。
breakfast || n. || 早餐 || I eat breakfast at seven. || 我七點吃早餐。 || have breakfast=吃早餐; breakfast shop=早餐店 || What do you usually eat ___ breakfast? || for|of|in || 「早餐吃什麼」用 for breakfast。 || 你早餐通常吃什麼？
bring || v. || 帶來 || Please bring your textbook tomorrow. || 明天請帶課本來。 || bring sth to=把某物帶到…; bring back=帶回來 || Don't forget ___ bring your umbrella. || to|for|at || forget to V = 忘記要去做某事。 || 別忘了帶雨傘。
brother || n. || 兄弟 || My brother is two years older than me. || 我哥哥比我大兩歲。 || big brother=哥哥; little brother=弟弟 || How many brothers ___ you have? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你有幾個兄弟？
brown || adj./n. || 棕色（的） || The bear is brown. || 那隻熊是棕色的。 || brown hair=棕色頭髮; brown sugar=黑糖 || Her eyes are brown, and ___ hair is long. || her|she|hers || 名詞 hair 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 她的眼睛是棕色的，頭髮很長。
bug || n. || 蟲子 || There is a bug on your shirt. || 你的襯衫上有一隻蟲。 || catch bugs=抓蟲 || There ___ a bug on the wall. || is|are|be || a bug 單數，There is。 || 牆上有一隻蟲。
build || v. || 建造 || They build a new school here. || 他們在這裡蓋一所新學校。 || build a house=蓋房子 || They ___ going to build a new library here. || are|is|will || be going to + 原形動詞；They 用 are。 || 他們要在這裡蓋一座新圖書館。
bus || n. || 公車 || I take the bus to school. || 我搭公車上學。 || take a bus=搭公車; bus stop=公車站 || Tom goes to school ___ bus. || by|on|in || 搭乘交通工具用 by（中間不加冠詞）。 || 湯姆搭公車上學。
busy || adj. || 忙碌的 || My dad is always busy. || 我爸爸總是很忙。 || be busy with=忙於…; busy street=熱鬧的街道 || Are you busy now? No, I ___. || 'm not|don't|amn't || 用 Are you 問，否定回答用 I'm not。 || 你現在忙嗎？不忙。
buy || v. || 買 || I want to buy a new bag. || 我想買一個新包包。 || buy sth for sb=買某物給某人 || My mom wants ___ buy a cake for me. || to|for|at || want to + 原形動詞 = 想要做…。 || 我媽媽想買一個蛋糕給我。
cake || n. || 蛋糕 || We eat cake at birthday parties. || 我們在生日派對上吃蛋糕。 || a piece of cake=一塊蛋糕、小事一樁; birthday cake=生日蛋糕 || Would you like ___ cake? || some|many|a few || 請別人吃東西用 some；cake（指蛋糕的量）不可數。 || 你要來點蛋糕嗎？
call || v./n. || 打電話；叫 || Please call me later. || 請晚點打電話給我。 || call sb=打電話給某人; phone call=通電話 || Please call ___ tonight. || me|I|my || call 後面接受詞，用受格 me。 || 今晚請打電話給我。
camp || n./v. || 營隊；露營 || We go to a summer camp every year. || 我們每年去參加夏令營。 || summer camp=夏令營; go camping=去露營 || We are going ___ camping this weekend. || to go|go|going || be going to + 原形動詞：go camping = 去露營。 || 我們這週末要去露營。
candy || n. || 糖果 || Too much candy is bad for your teeth. || 吃太多糖果對牙齒不好。 || a piece of candy=一顆糖果 || Don't eat ___ much candy. || too|very|so || too much = 太多（帶有負面意思）。 || 別吃太多糖果。
cap || n. || 帽子（有帽舌的） || He is wearing a blue cap. || 他戴著一頂藍色的帽子。 || baseball cap=棒球帽 || Is this your cap? Yes, it's ___. || mine|my|me || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 mine。 || 這是你的帽子嗎？是，是我的。
car || n. || 汽車 || My father drives a car to work. || 我爸爸開車上班。 || by car=開車; drive a car=開車 || There ___ many cars on the street. || are|is|be || many cars 複數，There are。 || 街上有很多車子。
card || n. || 卡片 || I made a birthday card for Mom. || 我做了一張生日卡給媽媽。 || birthday card=生日卡; play cards=玩紙牌 || This card is ___ you. Happy birthday! || for|to|of || 「給某人的」用 for sb。 || 這張卡片是給你的，生日快樂！
care || v./n. || 在乎；照顧 || I care about you. || 我在乎你。 || take care of=照顧; care about=在乎 || Please take care ___ my cat. || of|for|to || take care of = 照顧。 || 請幫我照顧我的貓。
carry || v. || 攜帶；搬運 || Can you carry this box for me? || 你能幫我搬這個箱子嗎？ || carry a bag=提包包 || He ___ carrying a heavy box. || is|are|does || 現在進行式 be + V-ing；He 用 is。 || 他正在搬一個很重的箱子。
case || n. || 盒子；情況 || Put your glasses in the case. || 把你的眼鏡放進盒子裡。 || pencil case=鉛筆盒; in case=以防萬一 || There are five pens in ___ pencil case. || my|me|mine || 名詞 pencil case 前用所有格形容詞 my。 || 我的鉛筆盒裡有五支筆。
cat || n. || 貓 || My cat likes to sleep on the sofa. || 我的貓喜歡睡在沙發上。 || feed the cat=餵貓; pet cat=寵物貓 || My cat ___ fish. || likes|like|liking || My cat 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 我的貓喜歡吃魚。
catch || v. || 接住；抓住；趕上 || Catch the ball! || 接住球！ || catch a cold=感冒; catch the bus=趕上公車 || Hurry up, ___ you can't catch the bus. || or|and|but || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 快一點，否則你會趕不上公車。
chair || n. || 椅子 || Please sit on the chair. || 請坐在椅子上。 || sit on a chair=坐在椅子上 || There ___ four chairs in the kitchen. || are|is|be || four chairs 複數，There are。 || 廚房裡有四張椅子。
cheap || adj. || 便宜的 || This T-shirt is cheap. || 這件 T 恤很便宜。 || cheap price=便宜的價格 || These shoes are cheap. How ___ are they? || much|many|long || 問價錢用 How much。 || 這雙鞋很便宜，多少錢？
