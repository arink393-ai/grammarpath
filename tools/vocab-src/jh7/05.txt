# 七年級 05：front – hot dog
front || n. || 前面 || There is a tree in front of my house. || 我家前面有一棵樹。 || in front of=在…前面; front door=前門 || A bus is stopping in front ___ the school. || of|to|at || in front of = 在…前面。 || 一輛公車停在學校前面。
fruit || n. || 水果 || Eat more fruit and vegetables. || 多吃蔬菜水果。 || fresh fruit=新鮮水果; fruit juice=果汁 || What fruit ___ you like? || do|are|does || 一般動詞 like 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你喜歡什麼水果？
full || adj. || 飽的；滿的 || I'm full. I can't eat any more. || 我飽了，吃不下了。 || be full of=充滿…; full moon=滿月 || The box is full ___ toys. || of|with|in || be full of = 裝滿…。 || 箱子裡裝滿了玩具。
fun || n. || 樂趣 || Swimming is fun. || 游泳很好玩。 || have fun=玩得開心; for fun=為了好玩 || We had a lot of fun ___ the party. || at|on|in || 在派對上用 at the party。 || 我們在派對上玩得很開心。
funny || adj. || 好笑的；有趣的 || The movie is very funny. || 這部電影很好笑。 || funny story=好笑的故事 || The story is ___ funny that everyone laughs. || so|such|very || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 這個故事好笑到大家都笑了。
game || n. || 遊戲；比賽 || Let's play a game. || 我們來玩遊戲吧。 || play games=玩遊戲; video game=電玩 || Don't play video games ___ late. || too|so|very || too late = 太晚。 || 別玩電動玩到太晚。
garden || n. || 花園 || My grandma has a beautiful garden. || 我奶奶有一座美麗的花園。 || in the garden=在花園裡 || There ___ many flowers in the garden. || are|is|be || many flowers 複數，There are。 || 花園裡有很多花。
gate || n. || 大門 || Meet me at the school gate. || 在學校大門跟我碰面。 || school gate=校門 || Let's meet ___ the school gate at seven. || at|on|in || 在某個地點（點）用 at。 || 我們七點在校門口碰面。
get || v. || 得到；到達；變得 || I get up at six every day. || 我每天六點起床。 || get up=起床; get home=到家 || What time do you get up? I get up ___ six. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 你幾點起床？我六點起床。
ghost || n. || 鬼 || Do you believe in ghosts? || 你相信有鬼嗎？ || ghost story=鬼故事 || ___ you believe in ghosts? || Do|Are|Does || 一般動詞 believe 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 Do。 || 你相信有鬼嗎？
giant || n./adj. || 巨人；巨大的 || The giant is very tall. || 那個巨人非常高。 || giant panda=大貓熊 || There ___ a giant tree in the park. || is|are|be || a giant tree 單數，There is。 || 公園裡有一棵巨大的樹。
gift || n. || 禮物 || This gift is for you. || 這份禮物是給你的。 || birthday gift=生日禮物 || This gift is ___ you. || for|of|to || 「給某人的」用 for sb。 || 這份禮物是給你的。
girl || n. || 女孩 || The girl with long hair is my sister. || 那個長頭髮的女孩是我姊姊。 || little girl=小女孩 || The girls ___ singing in the classroom. || are|is|does || 現在進行式；The girls 複數用 are。 || 女孩們正在教室裡唱歌。
give || v. || 給 || Give me the book, please. || 請把書給我。 || give up=放棄; give sb sth=給某人某物 || Please give ___ a pen. || me|I|my || give 後面的人用受格 me。 || 請給我一支筆。
glad || adj. || 高興的 || I'm glad to see you. || 很高興見到你。 || be glad to V=很高興做… || I'm glad ___ meet you. || to|for|at || be glad to + 原形動詞。 || 很高興認識你。
glass || n. || 玻璃杯；玻璃 || I drink a glass of water every morning. || 我每天早上喝一杯水。 || a glass of=一杯 || Can I have a glass ___ water? || of|for|with || a glass of + 飲料 = 一杯…。 || 可以給我一杯水嗎？
glove || n. || 手套 || Wear your gloves. It's cold. || 戴上你的手套，天氣很冷。 || a pair of gloves=一雙手套 || I need a new pair ___ gloves. || of|for|to || a pair of = 一雙。 || 我需要一雙新手套。
go || v. || 去 || Let's go to the park. || 我們去公園吧。 || go to school=上學; go home=回家 || My sister goes to school ___ bike. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 我姊姊騎腳踏車上學。
god || n. || 神 || Oh my god! || 我的天啊！ || thank God=謝天謝地 || People pray to the gods ___ the temple. || in|at|on || 在寺廟裡面用 in。 || 人們在廟裡向神明祈禱。
good || adj. || 好的 || Good morning! || 早安！ || be good at=擅長; good job=做得好 || She is good ___ math. || at|in|for || be good at = 擅長…。 || 她擅長數學。
grandfather || n. || 祖父；外公 || My grandfather is seventy years old. || 我爺爺七十歲。 || visit grandfather=去看爺爺 || My grandfather ___ walking in the park now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；My grandfather 用 is。 || 我爺爺現在正在公園散步。
grandmother || n. || 祖母；外婆 || My grandmother cooks very well. || 我奶奶很會做菜。 || grandma's house=奶奶家 || My grandmother ___ in Hualien. || lives|live|living || My grandmother 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 我奶奶住在花蓮。
grass || n. || 草 || The cows eat grass. || 牛吃草。 || on the grass=在草地上; keep off the grass=勿踐踏草坪 || Please keep ___ the grass. || off|of|out || keep off = 遠離、不要踩。 || 請勿踐踏草地。
gray || adj./n. || 灰色（的） || The sky is gray today. || 今天天空灰灰的。 || gray hair=白頭髮 || My grandpa has gray hair, ___ he is still strong. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 我爺爺頭髮灰白，但身體還是很硬朗。
great || adj. || 很棒的；偉大的 || That's a great idea! || 那是個很棒的主意！ || great idea=好主意; have a great time=玩得很開心 || We had a great time ___ the beach. || at|in|on || at the beach = 在海邊。 || 我們在海邊玩得很開心。
green || adj./n. || 綠色（的） || The grass is green. || 草是綠色的。 || green tea=綠茶; green light=綠燈 || Go when the light turns green, ___ stop when it's red. || and|or|so || 連接兩個指令用 and。 || 綠燈時前進，紅燈時停下。
group || n. || 群；組 || We work in groups of four. || 我們四人一組合作。 || in groups=分組; a group of=一群 || We work in groups ___ four. || of|in|for || groups of four = 四人一組。 || 我們四人一組合作。
grow || v. || 成長；種植 || My dad grows vegetables in the yard. || 我爸爸在院子裡種菜。 || grow up=長大; grow vegetables=種菜 || What do you want to be when you grow ___? || up|in|out || grow up = 長大。 || 你長大後想做什麼？
guess || v. || 猜 || Guess what I have in my hand. || 猜猜我手裡有什麼。 || guess what=你猜怎麼著; make a guess=猜一猜 || Can you guess how old ___? || I am|am I|I is || 間接問句用直述句語序：how old I am。 || 你猜得到我幾歲嗎？
guitar || n. || 吉他 || He plays the guitar well. || 他吉他彈得很好。 || play the guitar=彈吉他 || Can you play ___ guitar? || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 你會彈吉他嗎？
guy || n. || 傢伙；人 || He is a nice guy. || 他是個好人。 || nice guy=好人; you guys=你們大家 || Hi, you guys! How ___ you? || are|is|do || you guys（你們）用 are。 || 嗨，大家好嗎？
hair || n. || 頭髮 || She has long hair. || 她有一頭長髮。 || long hair=長髮; wash one's hair=洗頭 || Her hair ___ long and black. || is|are|am || hair（頭髮整體）不可數，用 is。 || 她的頭髮又長又黑。
ham || n. || 火腿 || I want a ham sandwich. || 我要一個火腿三明治。 || ham and eggs=火腿蛋 || I'd like a ham sandwich ___ a cup of milk. || and|or|but || 兩樣東西都要，用 and 連接。 || 我要一個火腿三明治和一杯牛奶。
hamburger || n. || 漢堡 || I eat a hamburger for lunch. || 我午餐吃漢堡。 || hamburger and fries=漢堡配薯條 || How much ___ the hamburger? || is|are|do || the hamburger 單數，用 is。 || 這個漢堡多少錢？
hand || n. || 手 || Raise your hand. || 舉起你的手。 || raise one's hand=舉手; wash hands=洗手 || Wash your hands ___ you eat. || before|after|until || 吃東西「之前」洗手，用 before。 || 吃東西前要洗手。
happy || adj. || 快樂的 || I'm happy today. || 我今天很開心。 || Happy birthday=生日快樂; be happy with=對…滿意 || Why ___ you so happy today? || are|do|is || 主詞 you 的 be 動詞用 are。 || 你今天為什麼這麼開心？
hard || adj./adv. || 困難的；努力地 || He studies hard. || 他很用功。 || work hard=努力工作; hard work=辛苦的工作 || She studies hard, ___ she gets good grades. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 她很用功，所以成績很好。
hat || n. || 帽子 || Put on your hat. It's sunny. || 戴上你的帽子，太陽很大。 || put on a hat=戴帽子 || Whose hat is this? It's ___. || his|he|him || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 his。 || 這是誰的帽子？是他的。
hate || v. || 討厭 || I hate rainy days. || 我討厭下雨天。 || hate to V=討厭做… || My brother hates ___ the dishes. || washing|wash|washes || hate 後面可接動名詞 V-ing（或 to V）。 || 我弟弟討厭洗碗。
head || n. || 頭 || My head hurts. || 我的頭好痛。 || shake one's head=搖頭; head to=前往 || Put your hat ___ your head. || on|in|at || 戴在頭「上」用 on。 || 把帽子戴在頭上。
hear || v. || 聽見 || Can you hear me? || 你聽得到我說話嗎？ || hear from=收到…的消息; hear about=聽說 || I can't hear ___. Please speak louder. || you|your|yours || hear 後面接受詞，用受格 you。 || 我聽不到你說話，請大聲一點。
heart || n. || 心；心臟 || She has a kind heart. || 她有一顆善良的心。 || by heart=背誦、記住; with all my heart=全心全意 || Learn the words ___ heart. || by|in|with || learn by heart = 背起來、記住。 || 把這些單字背起來。
help || v./n. || 幫助 || Can you help me? || 你可以幫我嗎？ || help sb with sth=幫某人做某事; ask for help=求助 || Can you help me ___ my homework? || with|for|at || help sb with sth = 幫某人做某事。 || 你可以幫我寫功課嗎？
hen || n. || 母雞 || The hen lays an egg every day. || 那隻母雞每天下一顆蛋。 || hen and chicks=母雞和小雞 || There ___ five hens on the farm. || are|is|be || five hens 複數，There are。 || 農場上有五隻母雞。
high || adj. || 高的 || The mountain is very high. || 那座山很高。 || high school=高中; high price=高價 || The mountain is very high. Let's climb ___ together. || it|them|its || the mountain 單數物品，受格用 it。 || 那座山很高，我們一起去爬吧。
hill || n. || 小山；丘陵 || Let's walk up the hill. || 我們走上山丘吧。 || up the hill=上山丘; on the hill=在山丘上 || There ___ a small house on the hill. || is|are|be || a small house 單數，There is。 || 山丘上有一間小房子。
hobby || n. || 嗜好 || My hobby is reading. || 我的嗜好是閱讀。 || have a hobby=有嗜好 || What ___ your hobbies? || are|is|do || hobbies 複數，用 are。 || 你的嗜好是什麼？
home || n./adv. || 家；在家 || I go home at five. || 我五點回家。 || go home=回家; at home=在家 || I stay ___ home on weekends. || at|in|on || at home = 在家。 || 我週末待在家。
homework || n. || 功課 || I do my homework after dinner. || 我晚餐後寫功課。 || do homework=寫功課; finish homework=寫完功課 || Tom ___ his homework every night. || does|do|doing || Tom 第三人稱單數，do 改成 does。 || 湯姆每天晚上寫功課。
honey || n. || 蜂蜜 || Bees make honey. || 蜜蜂釀蜂蜜。 || sweet as honey=甜如蜜 || There isn't ___ honey in the jar. || any|some|many || 否定句用 any；honey 不可數。 || 罐子裡沒有蜂蜜了。
horse || n. || 馬 || He can ride a horse. || 他會騎馬。 || ride a horse=騎馬 || Can you ___ a horse? || ride|rides|riding || can 後面接原形動詞。 || 你會騎馬嗎？
hot || adj. || 熱的；辣的 || It's very hot in summer. || 夏天很熱。 || hot water=熱水; hot and spicy=又熱又辣 || It's hot today. Let's go ___. || swimming|swim|to swimming || go + V-ing：go swimming = 去游泳。 || 今天很熱，我們去游泳吧。
hot dog || n. || 熱狗 || I'd like a hot dog, please. || 請給我一份熱狗。 || hot dog stand=熱狗攤 || I want two hot dogs ___ a Coke. || and|or|but || 兩樣都要，用 and 連接。 || 我要兩份熱狗和一杯可樂。
