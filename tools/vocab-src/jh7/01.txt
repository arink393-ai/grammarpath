# 七年級 01：a few – below
a few || phr. || 一些；幾個 || I have a few friends in my class. || 我在班上有幾個朋友。 || a few days=幾天; a few minutes=幾分鐘 || There are a few ___ on the table. || apples|apple|an apple || a few 後面接可數名詞複數，用 apples。 || 桌上有幾顆蘋果。
a lot || phr. || 很多；非常 || Thank you a lot. || 非常謝謝你。 || a lot of=很多的; like sth a lot=非常喜歡 || He ___ a lot of books. || has|have|having || He 是第三人稱單數，動詞用 has。 || 他有很多書。
about || prep. || 關於；大約 || This story is about a brave boy. || 這個故事是關於一個勇敢的男孩。 || talk about=談論; think about=考慮 || What ___ you talking about? || are|is|do || 現在進行式 be + V-ing，主詞 you 用 are。 || 你們在談論什麼？
above || prep. || 在…上方 || The clock is above the door. || 時鐘在門的上方。 || above the door=門的上方; above all=最重要的是 || There ___ a picture above the bed. || is|are|be || a picture 是單數，There is。 || 床的上方有一幅畫。
actor || n. || 男演員 || The actor is very famous. || 那位演員很有名。 || movie actor=電影演員; famous actor=有名的演員 || My uncle is ___ actor. || an|a|the || actor 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我的叔叔是一位演員。
after || prep. || 在…之後 || I do my homework after dinner. || 我晚餐後寫功課。 || after school=放學後; after class=下課後 || We play basketball after school ___ Fridays. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我們星期五放學後打籃球。
afternoon || n. || 下午 || I have English class in the afternoon. || 我下午有英文課。 || in the afternoon=在下午; this afternoon=今天下午 || I have art class ___ the afternoon. || in|on|at || in the morning / afternoon / evening 用 in。 || 我下午有美術課。
again || adv. || 再一次 || Please say it again. || 請再說一次。 || again and again=一再地; try again=再試一次 || Please ___ it again. || say|says|saying || 祈使句用原形動詞開頭。 || 請再說一次。
age || n. || 年齡 || What is your age? || 你幾歲？ || at the age of=在…歲時; the same age=同年齡 || Amy and I are the same age. We ___ twelve. || are|is|am || 主詞 We 用 are。 || 艾咪和我同年，我們十二歲。
ago || adv. || 以前 || I met him two years ago. || 我兩年前認識他。 || two days ago=兩天前; long ago=很久以前 || I ___ in Tainan three years ago. || was|am|is || ago 表示過去，be 動詞用過去式 was。 || 我三年前在台南。
air || n. || 空氣 || The air in the mountains is fresh. || 山上的空氣很新鮮。 || fresh air=新鮮空氣; in the air=在空中 || The air ___ very clean here. || is|are|am || air 是不可數名詞，視為單數，用 is。 || 這裡的空氣很乾淨。
along || prep. || 沿著 || We walk along the river. || 我們沿著河邊走。 || walk along=沿著走; along the street=沿著街道 || Let's ___ along the beach. || walk|walks|walking || Let's 後面接原形動詞。 || 我們沿著海灘走吧。
also || adv. || 也 || I like dogs. I also like cats. || 我喜歡狗，我也喜歡貓。 || also like=也喜歡 || She likes music. She also ___ the piano. || plays|play|playing || She 是第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 她喜歡音樂，她也彈鋼琴。
angry || adj. || 生氣的 || Mom is angry with me. || 媽媽在生我的氣。 || angry with sb=生某人的氣; get angry=生氣 || Why ___ your mom angry? || is|are|does || angry 是形容詞，問句用 be 動詞；your mom 用 is。 || 你媽媽為什麼生氣？
animal || n. || 動物 || The panda is my favorite animal. || 貓熊是我最喜歡的動物。 || wild animal=野生動物; farm animal=農場動物 || How many animals ___ there at the zoo? || are|is|do || There be 句型問數量：How many animals are there…? || 動物園裡有多少動物？
answer || n./v. || 答案；回答 || Do you know the answer? || 你知道答案嗎？ || answer the phone=接電話; the answer to=…的答案 || ___ you know the answer? || Do|Are|Does || 一般動詞 know 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 Do。 || 你知道答案嗎？
ant || n. || 螞蟻 || An ant is very small. || 螞蟻很小。 || a line of ants=一排螞蟻 || There ___ many ants on the cake. || are|is|be || many ants 是複數，There are。 || 蛋糕上有很多螞蟻。
apple || n. || 蘋果 || An apple a day keeps the doctor away. || 一天一蘋果，醫生遠離我。 || apple juice=蘋果汁; apple pie=蘋果派 || I eat ___ apple every day. || an|a|two || apple 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我每天吃一顆蘋果。
arm || n. || 手臂 || He hurt his arm. || 他的手臂受傷了。 || arm in arm=手挽著手; long arms=長手臂 || Raise ___ arms, class. || your|you|yours || 名詞 arms 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 同學們，舉起你們的手臂。
art || n. || 藝術；美術 || Art is my favorite subject. || 美術是我最喜歡的科目。 || art class=美術課; art museum=美術館 || We ___ art class on Monday. || have|has|having || 主詞 We 用 have。 || 我們星期一有美術課。
ask || v. || 問；要求 || Can I ask you a question? || 我可以問你一個問題嗎？ || ask a question=問問題; ask for help=求助 || Can I ask ___ a question? || you|your|yours || ask 後面接受詞，you 的受格還是 you。 || 我可以問你一個問題嗎？
aunt || n. || 阿姨；姑姑；嬸嬸 || My aunt lives in Kaohsiung. || 我阿姨住在高雄。 || visit my aunt=去找阿姨 || My aunt ___ a nurse. || is|are|am || My aunt 是單數，用 is。 || 我阿姨是護理師。
away || adv. || 離開；在遠處 || Go away! || 走開！ || go away=走開; far away=遙遠; run away=逃跑 || The park is two kilometers away ___ my home. || from|of|to || A is ... away from B = A 離 B 有…遠。 || 公園離我家兩公里遠。
baby || n. || 嬰兒 || The baby is sleeping. || 嬰兒正在睡覺。 || baby sister=小妹妹; have a baby=生小孩 || Be quiet. The baby ___ sleeping. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The baby 單數用 is。 || 安靜，寶寶正在睡覺。
back || adv./n. || 回來；背部 || I will be back soon. || 我很快就回來。 || come back=回來; at the back of=在…後面 || Please come back ___ five o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 請在五點回來。
bad || adj. || 壞的；不好的 || Smoking is bad for your health. || 抽菸對你的健康不好。 || bad for=對…不好; not bad=還不錯 || Eating too much candy is bad ___ your teeth. || for|to|at || be bad for = 對…有害。 || 吃太多糖果對牙齒不好。
bag || n. || 袋子；包包 || This is my school bag. || 這是我的書包。 || school bag=書包; paper bag=紙袋 || Whose bag is this? It's ___. || mine|my|me || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 mine。 || 這是誰的包包？是我的。
banana || n. || 香蕉 || Monkeys like bananas. || 猴子喜歡香蕉。 || a bunch of bananas=一串香蕉 || How ___ bananas do you want? || many|much|any || bananas 可數複數，用 How many。 || 你要幾根香蕉？
band || n. || 樂團 || My brother plays in a band. || 我哥哥在樂團裡演奏。 || school band=學校樂隊; rock band=搖滾樂團 || There ___ five people in the band. || are|is|am || five people 複數，There are。 || 樂團裡有五個人。
bank || n. || 銀行 || The bank is next to the park. || 銀行在公園旁邊。 || go to the bank=去銀行; bank account=銀行帳戶 || The bank is next ___ the post office. || to|of|at || next to = 在…旁邊。 || 銀行在郵局旁邊。
basketball || n. || 籃球 || Let's play basketball after school. || 我們放學後去打籃球吧。 || play basketball=打籃球; basketball team=籃球隊 || He can ___ basketball well. || play|plays|playing || can 後面接原形動詞。 || 他籃球打得很好。
bat || n. || 蝙蝠；球棒 || A bat can fly at night. || 蝙蝠會在晚上飛。 || baseball bat=棒球棒 || Bats ___ at night. || fly|flies|flying || 主詞 Bats 是複數，動詞用原形。 || 蝙蝠在晚上飛。
bath || n. || 洗澡 || I take a bath every night. || 我每天晚上洗澡。 || take a bath=泡澡、洗澡; bath towel=浴巾 || My little brother ___ a bath every night. || takes|take|taking || 第三人稱單數動詞加 s。 || 我弟弟每天晚上洗澡。
bathroom || n. || 浴室；廁所 || Where is the bathroom? || 廁所在哪裡？ || go to the bathroom=上廁所; clean the bathroom=打掃浴室 || ___ is the bathroom? It's on the second floor. || Where|What|Who || 問地點用 Where。 || 廁所在哪裡？在二樓。
beach || n. || 海灘 || We play on the beach in summer. || 我們夏天在海灘玩。 || on the beach=在海灘上; go to the beach=去海邊 || Many people are swimming ___ the beach. || at|in|of || at the beach = 在海邊。 || 很多人在海邊游泳。
bean || n. || 豆子 || I don't like green beans. || 我不喜歡四季豆。 || green bean=四季豆; red bean=紅豆 || Do you like red beans? No, I ___. || don't|am not|doesn't || 用 Do 問，就用 don't 回答。 || 你喜歡紅豆嗎？不，我不喜歡。
bear || n. || 熊 || The bear is big and strong. || 那隻熊又大又壯。 || black bear=黑熊; teddy bear=泰迪熊 || Look! The bear ___ eating honey. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The bear 單數用 is。 || 你看！那隻熊正在吃蜂蜜。
beautiful || adj. || 美麗的 || What a beautiful day! || 多美好的一天！ || beautiful flower=美麗的花; look beautiful=看起來很美 || Your dress is ___ beautiful. || very|much|many || 形容詞前面加 very 表示「很」。 || 你的洋裝很漂亮。
bed || n. || 床 || It's time for bed. || 該睡覺了。 || go to bed=上床睡覺; make the bed=整理床鋪 || I go to bed ___ ten o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 我十點上床睡覺。
bedroom || n. || 臥室 || My bedroom is small but clean. || 我的臥室小但很乾淨。 || in my bedroom=在我的房間 || There ___ two beds in my bedroom. || are|is|be || two beds 複數，There are。 || 我的房間裡有兩張床。
bee || n. || 蜜蜂 || Bees make honey. || 蜜蜂釀蜂蜜。 || busy as a bee=非常忙碌 || A bee ___ honey. || makes|make|making || A bee 單數，動詞加 s。 || 蜜蜂釀蜂蜜。
beef || n. || 牛肉 || I want beef noodles. || 我要牛肉麵。 || beef noodles=牛肉麵; beef steak=牛排 || Do you eat beef? Yes, I ___. || do|am|does || 用 Do 問，就用 do 回答。 || 你吃牛肉嗎？吃。
begin || v. || 開始 || The class begins at eight. || 八點開始上課。 || begin to V=開始做…; begin with=以…開始 || Class begins ___ eight o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 八點開始上課。
bell || n. || 鈴；鐘 || The bell is ringing. || 鐘聲響了。 || ring the bell=按鈴; school bell=學校鐘聲 || Listen! The bell ___ ringing. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The bell 用 is。 || 你聽！鐘聲響了。
below || prep. || 在…下方 || Write your name below the line. || 把你的名字寫在線的下面。 || below zero=零度以下; see below=見下方 || ___ your name below the line. || Write|Writes|Writing || 祈使句用原形動詞開頭。 || 把你的名字寫在線的下面。
