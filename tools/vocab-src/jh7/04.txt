# 七年級 04：drink – frog
drink || v./n. || 喝；飲料 || I drink milk every morning. || 我每天早上喝牛奶。 || drink water=喝水; something to drink=喝的東西 || Would you like something ___ drink? || to|for|of || something to V = 可以做…的東西（something to drink = 喝的東西）。 || 你想喝點什麼嗎？
drive || v. || 開車 || My mom drives me to school. || 我媽媽開車送我上學。 || drive a car=開車; drive sb to=開車送某人到… || My father drives ___ to school every day. || us|we|our || drives 後面接受詞，用受格 us。 || 我爸爸每天開車送我們上學。
drum || n. || 鼓 || He plays the drums in the band. || 他在樂團打鼓。 || play the drums=打鼓 || He plays ___ drums very well. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 他鼓打得很好。
dry || adj. || 乾的 || The clothes are dry now. || 衣服現在乾了。 || dry season=乾季; keep sth dry=保持乾燥 || Are your clothes dry? Yes, ___ are. || they|it|them || clothes 複數，代名詞主格用 they。 || 你的衣服乾了嗎？乾了。
duck || n. || 鴨子 || The ducks are swimming in the pond. || 鴨子在池塘裡游泳。 || roast duck=烤鴨 || The ducks ___ swimming in the pond. || are|is|does || 現在進行式；The ducks 複數用 are。 || 鴨子正在池塘裡游泳。
each || adj./pron. || 每一；每個 || Each student has a book. || 每個學生都有一本書。 || each other=彼此; each day=每一天 || Each student ___ a computer. || has|have|having || each + 單數名詞，動詞用單數 has。 || 每個學生都有一台電腦。
ear || n. || 耳朵 || Rabbits have long ears. || 兔子有長耳朵。 || ear to ear=笑得合不攏嘴 || A rabbit ___ long ears. || has|have|is || A rabbit 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 兔子有長耳朵。
early || adj./adv. || 早的；早地 || I get up early every day. || 我每天早起。 || get up early=早起; early morning=清晨 || I get up early ___ Monday. || on|in|at || 星期幾前面用 on。 || 我星期一很早起床。
earth || n. || 地球 || We live on the Earth. || 我們住在地球上。 || on earth=在地球上、究竟 || We must take care ___ the Earth. || of|for|to || take care of = 照顧、愛護。 || 我們必須愛護地球。
east || n. || 東方 || The sun rises in the east. || 太陽從東方升起。 || in the east=在東方; east coast=東岸 || The sun rises ___ the east. || in|on|at || 在某個方位用 in：in the east。 || 太陽從東邊升起。
easy || adj. || 簡單的 || The test is easy. || 考試很簡單。 || easy to V=容易做…; take it easy=放輕鬆 || This question is easy ___ me. || for|of|at || 「對某人來說」用 for sb。 || 這個問題對我來說很簡單。
eat || v. || 吃 || I eat breakfast at seven. || 我七點吃早餐。 || eat out=外食; eat breakfast=吃早餐 || What do you want ___ eat? || to|for|at || want to + 原形動詞。 || 你想吃什麼？
egg || n. || 蛋 || I have an egg for breakfast. || 我早餐吃一顆蛋。 || fried egg=荷包蛋; boiled egg=水煮蛋 || I eat ___ egg every day. || an|a|two || egg 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我每天吃一顆蛋。
end || n./v. || 結束；末端 || The movie ends at nine. || 電影九點結束。 || at the end of=在…的最後; in the end=最後 || The show ends ___ ten o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 表演十點結束。
English || n./adj. || 英文；英國的 || I study English every day. || 我每天讀英文。 || speak English=說英文; English class=英文課 || ___ you speak English? || Can|Are|Is || 助動詞 Can 問能力，後接原形動詞 speak。 || 你會說英文嗎？
enjoy || v. || 享受；喜愛 || I enjoy reading books. || 我喜歡看書。 || enjoy oneself=玩得愉快; enjoy + V-ing=喜歡做… || I enjoy ___ to music. || listening|listen|to listen || enjoy 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我喜歡聽音樂。
evening || n. || 傍晚；晚上 || I watch TV in the evening. || 我晚上看電視。 || in the evening=在晚上; this evening=今晚 || We have dinner ___ the evening. || in|on|at || in the morning / afternoon / evening。 || 我們在晚上吃晚餐。
every || adj. || 每一 || I go jogging every morning. || 我每天早上去慢跑。 || every day=每天; every week=每週 || Every student ___ a uniform. || wears|wear|wearing || every + 單數名詞，動詞用單數加 s。 || 每個學生都穿制服。
eye || n. || 眼睛 || Close your eyes. || 閉上你的眼睛。 || close your eyes=閉上眼睛; open your eyes=張開眼睛 || She has big eyes, and ___ eyes are brown. || her|she|hers || 名詞 eyes 前用所有格形容詞 her。 || 她有大眼睛，她的眼睛是棕色的。
face || n. || 臉 || Wash your face. || 洗你的臉。 || wash one's face=洗臉; face to face=面對面 || Wash your face ___ brush your teeth. || and|but|or || 連接兩個動作用 and。 || 洗臉然後刷牙。
fall || n./v. || 秋天；落下 || Leaves fall in autumn. || 秋天樹葉會落下。 || fall down=跌倒; in the fall=在秋天 || The leaves fall from the trees ___ autumn. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 秋天樹葉從樹上落下。
family || n. || 家庭；家人 || There are four people in my family. || 我家有四個人。 || family member=家人; family photo=全家福 || There ___ five people in my family. || are|is|be || five people 複數，There are。 || 我家有五個人。
fan || n. || 迷；電風扇 || I am a big fan of Jay Chou. || 我是周杰倫的大粉絲。 || big fan=忠實粉絲; electric fan=電風扇 || She is a big fan ___ baseball. || of|for|to || be a fan of = 是…的迷。 || 她是個大棒球迷。
far || adj./adv. || 遠的；遠地 || My home is far from school. || 我家離學校很遠。 || far from=離…很遠; how far=多遠 || My home is far ___ school. || from|of|to || be far from = 離…很遠。 || 我家離學校很遠。
farm || n. || 農場 || My grandpa lives on a farm. || 我爺爺住在農場。 || on a farm=在農場; farm animal=農場動物 || My uncle works ___ a farm. || on|of|to || 在農場用 on a farm。 || 我叔叔在農場工作。
fast || adj./adv. || 快的；快地 || He runs very fast. || 他跑得很快。 || fast food=速食; run fast=跑得快 || He can run very fast, ___ he can't swim. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 他跑得很快，但不會游泳。
fat || adj. || 胖的 || My cat is fat. || 我的貓很胖。 || get fat=變胖 || Don't eat too much, ___ you will get fat. || or|and|so || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 別吃太多，不然你會變胖。
father || n. || 父親 || My father is a doctor. || 我爸爸是醫生。 || father and son=父子; Father's Day=父親節 || My father ___ to work by bus. || goes|go|going || My father 第三人稱單數，go 加 es。 || 我爸爸搭公車上班。
feed || v. || 餵 || I feed my dog every morning. || 我每天早上餵狗。 || feed the cat=餵貓 || Don't feed the animals ___ the zoo. || at|on|of || at the zoo = 在動物園。 || 不要在動物園餵動物。
feel || v. || 覺得；感覺 || I feel happy today. || 我今天覺得很開心。 || feel happy=覺得開心; feel like + V-ing=想要做… || How ___ you feel today? || do|are|is || 一般動詞 feel 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你今天感覺怎麼樣？
few || adj. || 很少的 || Few students like tests. || 很少學生喜歡考試。 || a few=一些; very few=極少的 || There are few ___ in the park today. || people|person|peoples || few 後面接可數複數名詞；people 是 person 的複數。 || 今天公園裡人很少。
find || v. || 找到；發現 || I can't find my key. || 我找不到我的鑰匙。 || find out=查明; find sth=找到某物 || I can't find my keys. Where ___ they? || are|is|do || keys 複數，用 are。 || 我找不到我的鑰匙，它們在哪裡？
fine || adj. || 好的；晴朗的 || I'm fine, thank you. || 我很好，謝謝。 || fine day=晴朗的日子 || How are you? I'm fine, ___ you. || thank|thanks to|thanking || 回答問候：I'm fine, thank you. || 你好嗎？我很好，謝謝你。
fire || n. || 火 || Don't play with fire. || 別玩火。 || on fire=著火; fire station=消防局 || Don't play ___ fire. || with|at|on || play with = 玩弄…。 || 別玩火。
fish || n. || 魚 || There are many fish in the river. || 河裡有很多魚。 || go fishing=去釣魚; fish and chips=炸魚薯條 || Let's have fish ___ dinner tonight. || for|of|to || 「晚餐吃…」用 for dinner。 || 我們今晚晚餐吃魚吧。
floor || n. || 地板；樓層 || My room is on the second floor. || 我的房間在二樓。 || on the floor=在地板上; first floor=一樓 || The library is ___ the fifth floor. || on|in|at || 在第幾樓用 on。 || 圖書館在五樓。
flower || n. || 花 || These flowers are beautiful. || 這些花很漂亮。 || flower shop=花店; a bunch of flowers=一束花 || These flowers ___ for you. || are|is|am || These flowers 複數，用 are。 || 這些花是給你的。
fly || v. || 飛 || Birds can fly. || 鳥會飛。 || fly a kite=放風箏; fly to=飛往… || Let's go ___ fly a kite. || and|but|or || go and V = 去做某事。 || 我們去放風箏吧。
food || n. || 食物 || Taiwanese food is delicious. || 台灣的食物很好吃。 || fast food=速食; junk food=垃圾食物 || The food here ___ very good. || is|are|am || food 不可數，視為單數，用 is。 || 這裡的食物很好吃。
foot || n. || 腳 || I go to school on foot. || 我走路上學。 || on foot=步行; feet=腳（複數） || My foot hurts. I can't walk ___. || well|good|nice || 修飾動詞 walk 用副詞 well。 || 我的腳很痛，沒辦法好好走路。
fork || n. || 叉子 || We eat steak with a knife and fork. || 我們用刀叉吃牛排。 || knife and fork=刀叉 || Can I have ___ fork, please? || a|an|some || fork 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 可以給我一支叉子嗎？
free || adj. || 自由的；免費的；有空的 || Are you free tonight? || 你今晚有空嗎？ || free time=空閒時間; for free=免費 || What do you do in your free time? I ___ comic books. || read|reads|reading || 主詞 I 用原形動詞。 || 你空閒時做什麼？我看漫畫。
fresh || adj. || 新鮮的 || The fish is very fresh. || 這魚很新鮮。 || fresh air=新鮮空氣; fresh fruit=新鮮水果 || The fruit here ___ always fresh. || is|are|am || fruit 當集合名詞視為單數，用 is。 || 這裡的水果總是很新鮮。
friend || n. || 朋友 || She is my best friend. || 她是我最好的朋友。 || best friend=最好的朋友; make friends=交朋友 || Tom and Ben are my friends. I play with ___ every day. || them|they|their || with 後面用受格 them。 || 湯姆和班是我的朋友，我每天和他們玩。
frog || n. || 青蛙 || The frog can jump very far. || 青蛙可以跳得很遠。 || frog jump=蛙跳 || The frog ___ jumping into the water. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The frog 用 is。 || 青蛙正跳進水裡。
