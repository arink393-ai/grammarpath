# 七年級 03：chicken – dress
chicken || n. || 雞；雞肉 || I like fried chicken. || 我喜歡炸雞。 || fried chicken=炸雞; chicken soup=雞湯 || Would you like chicken ___ fish? || or|and|but || 二選一的問句用 or。 || 你想要雞肉還是魚？
child || n. || 小孩 || The child is playing in the park. || 那個小孩在公園玩。 || only child=獨生子女; as a child=小時候 || The child ___ playing with a ball. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The child 單數用 is。 || 那個小孩正在玩球。
city || n. || 城市 || Taipei is a big city. || 台北是個大城市。 || big city=大城市; city life=都市生活 || Taichung is a city ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在某個國家、地區「裡面」用 in。 || 台中是台灣的一座城市。
class || n. || 班級；課 || We have English class today. || 我們今天有英文課。 || in class=上課中; class leader=班長 || There ___ thirty students in my class. || are|is|be || thirty students 複數，There are。 || 我們班有三十個學生。
classmate || n. || 同學 || Amy is my classmate. || 艾咪是我的同學。 || new classmate=新同學 || Amy and I ___ classmates. || are|is|am || 主詞 Amy and I 複數，用 are。 || 艾咪和我是同學。
classroom || n. || 教室 || Our classroom is on the third floor. || 我們的教室在三樓。 || in the classroom=在教室裡 || Our classroom is ___ the third floor. || on|in|at || 在第幾樓用 on。 || 我們的教室在三樓。
clean || adj./v. || 乾淨的；打掃 || We clean the classroom every day. || 我們每天打掃教室。 || clean up=清理乾淨; keep sth clean=保持…乾淨 || Look! They ___ cleaning the classroom. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；They 用 are。 || 你看！他們正在打掃教室。
climb || v. || 爬 || Let's climb the mountain. || 我們去爬山吧。 || climb a mountain=爬山; climb a tree=爬樹 || My cat likes ___ climb trees. || to|for|at || like to V = 喜歡做某事。 || 我的貓喜歡爬樹。
clock || n. || 時鐘 || The clock on the wall is slow. || 牆上的時鐘慢了。 || alarm clock=鬧鐘 || There ___ a clock on the wall. || is|are|be || a clock 單數，There is。 || 牆上有一個時鐘。
close || v. || 關上 || Please close the door. || 請關門。 || close the door=關門; close your eyes=閉上眼睛 || ___ close the window. It's cold. || Please|Pleases|Pleasing || 客氣的祈使句：Please + 原形動詞。 || 請關窗，好冷。
clothes || n. || 衣服 || I need some new clothes. || 我需要一些新衣服。 || put on clothes=穿上衣服; wash clothes=洗衣服 || Your clothes ___ dirty. || are|is|am || clothes 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 你的衣服很髒。
cloud || n. || 雲 || There are many clouds in the sky. || 天空中有很多雲。 || white cloud=白雲; dark cloud=烏雲 || There ___ no clouds in the sky today. || are|is|be || no clouds 複數，There are。 || 今天天空沒有雲。
club || n. || 社團；俱樂部 || I am in the music club. || 我參加音樂社。 || join a club=參加社團; club activity=社團活動 || Which club ___ you in? || are|do|is || 主詞 you 的 be 動詞用 are。 || 你參加哪個社團？
coat || n. || 外套；大衣 || Put on your coat. It's cold. || 穿上你的外套，天氣很冷。 || put on a coat=穿上外套; winter coat=冬季大衣 || Put ___ your coat before you go out. || on|in|at || put on = 穿上（衣物）。 || 出門前穿上外套。
cold || adj./n. || 冷的；感冒 || It's cold today. || 今天很冷。 || catch a cold=感冒; have a cold=感冒 || ___ is very cold today. || It|This|He || 描述天氣用 It 當主詞。 || 今天很冷。
color || n. || 顏色 || What color is your bike? || 你的腳踏車是什麼顏色？ || favorite color=最喜歡的顏色 || ___ color do you like? || What|How|Who || 問顏色用 What color。 || 你喜歡什麼顏色？
come || v. || 來 || Come here, please. || 請過來。 || come back=回來; come in=進來; come from=來自 || Where ___ you come from? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你來自哪裡？
comic || n. || 漫畫 || He likes to read comics. || 他喜歡看漫畫。 || comic book=漫畫書 || My brother reads comics every day, ___ he doesn't read other books. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 我弟弟每天看漫畫，但不看其他書。
computer || n. || 電腦 || I use the computer to do homework. || 我用電腦寫功課。 || computer game=電腦遊戲; use a computer=用電腦 || Is this your computer? No, it's ___. || my sister's|my sister|my sisters || 表示「我姊姊的」用所有格 's。 || 這是你的電腦嗎？不，是我姊姊的。
cook || v./n. || 煮；廚師 || My mom cooks dinner every day. || 我媽媽每天煮晚餐。 || cook dinner=煮晚餐; good cook=很會做菜的人 || Dad is a good cook. ___ food is delicious. || His|He|Him || 名詞 food 前用所有格形容詞 His。 || 爸爸很會做菜，他做的菜很好吃。
cookie || n. || 餅乾 || She made some cookies for us. || 她為我們做了一些餅乾。 || chocolate cookie=巧克力餅乾 || Can I have ___ cookies? || some|much|a || 請求時用 some；cookies 可數複數。 || 我可以吃一些餅乾嗎？
cool || adj. || 涼爽的；酷的 || It's cool in the evening. || 傍晚很涼爽。 || cool weather=涼爽的天氣; look cool=看起來很酷 || Your new shoes look ___ cool! || so|such|much || so + 形容詞 = 好…。 || 你的新鞋看起來好酷！
couch || n. || 長沙發 || Dad is sleeping on the couch. || 爸爸在沙發上睡覺。 || on the couch=在沙發上; couch potato=成天看電視的人 || The cat is sleeping ___ the couch. || on|in|at || 在沙發「上」用 on。 || 貓在沙發上睡覺。
cow || n. || 母牛；乳牛 || Cows give us milk. || 母牛提供我們牛奶。 || milk a cow=擠牛奶 || There ___ ten cows on the farm. || are|is|be || ten cows 複數，There are。 || 農場上有十頭牛。
crazy || adj. || 瘋狂的；著迷的 || He is crazy about baseball. || 他非常著迷棒球。 || be crazy about=非常著迷; go crazy=發瘋 || My sister is crazy ___ K-pop. || about|at|of || be crazy about = 對…非常著迷。 || 我姊姊非常迷韓國流行音樂。
cry || v. || 哭 || The baby is crying. || 嬰兒在哭。 || cry for=哭著要; stop crying=別哭了 || Why ___ the baby crying? || is|does|are || 現在進行式問句：Why is + 主詞 + V-ing？ || 寶寶為什麼在哭？
cup || n. || 杯子 || I want a cup of coffee. || 我想要一杯咖啡。 || a cup of tea=一杯茶 || I drink two cups ___ tea every day. || of|for|in || a cup of + 飲料 = 一杯…。 || 我每天喝兩杯茶。
cut || v. || 切；剪 || Please cut the cake. || 請切蛋糕。 || cut the cake=切蛋糕; cut hair=剪頭髮 || Be careful. Don't cut ___. || yourself|you|your || 主詞和受詞是同一人時用反身代名詞 yourself。 || 小心，別割到你自己。
cute || adj. || 可愛的 || What a cute puppy! || 好可愛的小狗！ || so cute=好可愛 || Your puppy is ___ cute! || so|such|much || so + 形容詞 = 好…。 || 你的小狗好可愛！
dance || v./n. || 跳舞 || Let's dance together. || 我們一起跳舞吧。 || dance class=舞蹈課; dance to the music=隨音樂起舞 || She ___ dancing on the stage now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；She 用 is。 || 她現在正在舞台上跳舞。
dark || adj. || 黑暗的；深色的 || It's getting dark. || 天色漸漸暗了。 || in the dark=在黑暗中; dark blue=深藍色 || It gets dark early ___ winter. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 冬天天黑得早。
day || n. || 天；白天 || Have a nice day! || 祝你有美好的一天！ || every day=每天; one day=有一天 || There are seven days ___ a week. || in|on|at || 「在…之中」用 in：seven days in a week。 || 一週有七天。
desk || n. || 書桌 || My book is on the desk. || 我的書在桌上。 || at the desk=在書桌前 || There ___ some books on my desk. || are|is|be || some books 複數，There are。 || 我的書桌上有一些書。
die || v. || 死亡 || The flowers died because of no water. || 那些花因為沒有水而枯死了。 || die of=死於（疾病）; plants die=植物枯死 || Water the plants, ___ they will die. || or|and|but || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 替植物澆水，否則它們會枯死。
dinner || n. || 晚餐 || We have dinner at six. || 我們六點吃晚餐。 || have dinner=吃晚餐; after dinner=晚餐後 || What ___ we having for dinner, Mom? || are|do|is || 現在進行式；主詞 we 用 are。 || 媽，我們晚餐吃什麼？
dirty || adj. || 髒的 || Your hands are dirty. || 你的手很髒。 || dirty clothes=髒衣服; get dirty=弄髒 || Your shoes are dirty. Please clean ___. || them|it|they || shoes 是複數，受格用 them。 || 你的鞋子髒了，請把它們擦乾淨。
dish || n. || 盤子；菜餚 || I wash the dishes after dinner. || 我晚餐後洗碗。 || wash the dishes=洗碗; side dish=配菜 || It's your turn ___ wash the dishes. || to|for|of || It's your turn to V = 輪到你做…。 || 輪到你洗碗了。
dog || n. || 狗 || My dog likes to run. || 我的狗喜歡跑步。 || walk the dog=遛狗; hot dog=熱狗 || I walk ___ dog every morning. || my|me|mine || 名詞 dog 前用所有格形容詞 my。 || 我每天早上遛我的狗。
doll || n. || 洋娃娃 || The little girl has many dolls. || 那個小女孩有很多洋娃娃。 || play with dolls=玩洋娃娃 || The girl is playing ___ her doll. || with|at|to || play with = 和…玩。 || 女孩正在玩她的洋娃娃。
dollar || n. || 元（貨幣） || This pen costs ten dollars. || 這支筆十元。 || NT dollar=新台幣 || These pens are two dollars each. How ___ pens do you want? || many|much|long || pens 可數複數，問數量用 How many。 || 這些筆每支兩元，你要幾支？
door || n. || 門 || Please open the door. || 請開門。 || open the door=開門; next door=隔壁 || Someone is knocking ___ the door. || at|to|in || knock at/on the door = 敲門；選 at。 || 有人在敲門。
down || adv. || 向下 || Please sit down. || 請坐下。 || sit down=坐下; go down=下去 || Sit down, ___ you? || will|do|are || 祈使句的附加問句用 will you。 || 請坐下，好嗎？
draw || v. || 畫 || I like to draw animals. || 我喜歡畫動物。 || draw a picture=畫畫 || Can you draw a cat ___ me? || for|of|to || 為某人做某事用 for。 || 你可以幫我畫一隻貓嗎？
dream || n./v. || 夢；夢想 || My dream is to be a teacher. || 我的夢想是當老師。 || have a dream=做夢; dream of=夢想 || My dream ___ to be a singer. || is|are|am || My dream 單數，用 is。 || 我的夢想是當歌手。
dress || n. || 洋裝 || She is wearing a red dress. || 她穿著一件紅色洋裝。 || wear a dress=穿洋裝 || I like your dress. Where did you buy ___? || it|them|its || your dress 單數物品，受格用 it。 || 我喜歡你的洋裝，你在哪裡買的？
