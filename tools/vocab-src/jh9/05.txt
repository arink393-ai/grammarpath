# 九年級 05：diary – enemy
diary || n. || 日記 || I write in my diary every night. || 我每天晚上寫日記。 || keep a diary=寫日記 || I have kept a diary ___ I was ten. || since|for|when || since + 過去時間子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 我從十歲起就開始寫日記。
diet || n. || 飲食；節食 || You need a healthy diet. || 你需要健康的飲食。 || go on a diet=節食; balanced diet=均衡飲食 || She is ___ a diet, so she doesn't eat cake. || on|in|at || be on a diet = 在節食。 || 她在節食，所以不吃蛋糕。
difference || n. || 差異 || What's the difference between them? || 它們有什麼不同？ || make a difference=產生影響 || What's the difference ___ a frog and a toad? || between|among|from || 兩者之間用 between。 || 青蛙和蟾蜍有什麼不同？
difficulty || n. || 困難 || I have difficulty sleeping. || 我難以入睡。 || have difficulty V-ing=做…有困難 || I have difficulty ___ English. || speaking|to speak|speak || have difficulty + V-ing = 做…有困難。 || 我說英文有困難。
diligent || adj. || 勤勉的 || She is a diligent student. || 她是個勤勉的學生。 || diligent worker=勤奮的工人 || She is diligent, ___ she always gets good grades. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 她很勤勉，所以成績總是很好。
dinosaur || n. || 恐龍 || Dinosaurs lived millions of years ago. || 恐龍生活在數百萬年前。 || dinosaur bones=恐龍骨頭 || Dinosaurs ___ on Earth millions of years ago. || lived|live|have lived || millions of years ago 表示過去，用過去式。 || 恐龍在數百萬年前生活在地球上。
diplomat || n. || 外交官 || Her father is a diplomat. || 她的父親是外交官。 || become a diplomat=成為外交官 || She wants to become a diplomat ___ she can travel around the world. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 為了（使…能夠）。 || 她想當外交官，這樣就能環遊世界。
direct || adj./v. || 直接的；指導 || Is there a direct flight to Paris? || 有直飛巴黎的班機嗎？ || direct flight=直飛班機 || Is there a direct flight ___ Tokyo? || to|for|at || a flight to + 目的地。 || 有直飛東京的班機嗎？
direction || n. || 方向 || Which direction is the station? || 車站在哪個方向？ || in the direction of=朝…方向; sense of direction=方向感 || They walked in the direction ___ the park. || of|to|at || in the direction of = 朝…的方向。 || 他們朝公園的方向走去。
disappear || v. || 消失 || The cat disappeared into the dark. || 貓消失在黑暗中。 || disappear from=從…消失 || The sun disappeared ___ the clouds. || behind|beside|between of || behind = 在…後面。 || 太陽消失在雲層後面。
discover || v. || 發現 || Columbus discovered America. || 哥倫布發現了美洲。 || discover a secret=發現祕密 || The island ___ discovered in 1600. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那座島在 1600 年被發現。
discuss || v. || 討論 || Let's discuss the plan. || 我們來討論這個計畫吧。 || discuss sth with sb=和某人討論某事 || We discussed the problem ___ our teacher. || with|to|about || discuss sth with sb = 和某人討論某事。 || 我們和老師討論了這個問題。
discussion || n. || 討論 || We had a discussion about the trip. || 我們討論了這次旅行。 || have a discussion=討論 || We had a long discussion ___ the school trip. || about|on to|for || a discussion about = 關於…的討論。 || 我們針對校外教學討論了很久。
dishonest || adj. || 不誠實的 || It is dishonest to cheat. || 作弊是不誠實的。 || dishonest behavior=不誠實的行為 || It is dishonest ___ copy other people's homework. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 抄別人的作業是不誠實的。
distance || n. || 距離 || What's the distance between Taipei and Tainan? || 台北到台南的距離是多少？ || in the distance=在遠處 || We could see a mountain ___ the distance. || in|at|on || in the distance = 在遠處。 || 我們可以看到遠處的一座山。
distant || adj. || 遙遠的 || She lives in a distant village. || 她住在一個偏遠的村莊。 || distant future=遙遠的未來 || Maybe people will live on Mars in the distant future, ___ they? || won't|will|don't || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：will → won't。 || 也許人們在遙遠的未來會住在火星上，不是嗎？
divide || v. || 分開；劃分 || Divide the cake into four pieces. || 把蛋糕分成四塊。 || divide A into B=把 A 分成 B || The class was divided ___ five groups. || into|in|to be || divide A into B = 把 A 分成 B。 || 全班被分成五組。
dizzy || adj. || 頭暈的 || I feel dizzy. || 我覺得頭暈。 || feel dizzy=頭暈 || I felt dizzy ___ I stood up too fast. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 我站起來太快時覺得頭暈。
dodge ball || n. || 躲避球 || We played dodge ball in PE class. || 我們在體育課玩躲避球。 || play dodge ball=玩躲避球 || We ___ dodge ball in PE class yesterday. || played|play|have played || yesterday 是過去，用過去式。 || 我們昨天在體育課玩躲避球。
dolphin || n. || 海豚 || Dolphins are very smart. || 海豚非常聰明。 || dolphin watching=賞海豚 || Dolphins are ___ smarter than many other animals. || much|many|more || 比較級前面用 much 加強語氣。 || 海豚比許多其他動物聰明得多。
donkey || n. || 驢子 || The donkey is carrying a heavy bag. || 那隻驢子馱著一個重袋子。 || as stubborn as a donkey=像驢一樣固執 || The donkey ___ used to carry goods in the past. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 過去驢子被用來載貨。
double || adj./v. || 雙倍的 || I'd like a double room. || 我要一間雙人房。 || double room=雙人房 || The price of eggs has doubled ___ last year. || since|for|from || since + 過去時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 雞蛋價格從去年起翻了一倍。
doubt || n./v. || 懷疑 || There is no doubt that he is right. || 毫無疑問他是對的。 || no doubt=無疑地; without doubt=毫無疑問 || There is no doubt ___ she will win. || that|what|which || There is no doubt that… = 毫無疑問…。 || 毫無疑問她會贏。
doughnut || n. || 甜甜圈 || I had a doughnut for breakfast. || 我早餐吃了一個甜甜圈。 || chocolate doughnut=巧克力甜甜圈 || How many doughnuts ___ in the box? || are there|there are|is there || 疑問句的 There be 要倒裝：are there…? || 盒子裡有幾個甜甜圈？
downstairs || adv. || 在樓下 || Mom is downstairs in the kitchen. || 媽媽在樓下的廚房。 || go downstairs=下樓 || Dinner is ready. Come downstairs, ___ you? || will|do|are || 祈使句的附加問句用 will you。 || 晚餐好了，下樓來好嗎？
downtown || adv./n. || 在市中心 || Let's go downtown. || 我們去市中心吧。 || go downtown=去市區 || The new mall ___ built downtown last year. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那家新購物中心去年在市中心建成。
dragon || n. || 龍 || The dragon is a symbol of power in China. || 龍在中國是權力的象徵。 || Dragon Boat Festival=端午節 || People race dragon boats ___ the Dragon Boat Festival. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 人們在端午節期間划龍舟。
drama || n. || 戲劇 || I like Korean dramas. || 我喜歡韓劇。 || TV drama=電視劇 || She has watched this drama ___ times. || three|third|thrice of || 次數：three times = 三次。 || 這部劇她已經看了三次。
dresser || n. || 梳妝台；五斗櫃 || My socks are in the dresser. || 我的襪子在五斗櫃裡。 || in the dresser=在五斗櫃裡 || The mirror ___ the dresser is broken. || on|in|at || 在梳妝台上用 on。 || 梳妝台上的鏡子破了。
drugstore || n. || 藥局 || I bought some medicine at the drugstore. || 我在藥局買了一些藥。 || go to the drugstore=去藥局 || Is there a drugstore ___ here? || near|nearly|nearby of || near + 地點 = 在…附近。 || 這附近有藥局嗎？
dryer || n. || 烘乾機；吹風機 || Put the clothes in the dryer. || 把衣服放進烘乾機。 || hair dryer=吹風機 || Can I use your hair dryer? Mine ___ broken. || is|are|has || Mine（= my hair dryer）單數，用 is。 || 我可以用你的吹風機嗎？我的壞了。
dumb || adj. || 啞的；愚笨的 || That was a dumb question. || 那是個蠢問題。 || dumb question=蠢問題 || There are no dumb questions, ___ don't be afraid to ask. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 沒有蠢問題，所以別怕發問。
dumpling || n. || 水餃 || We eat dumplings on Chinese New Year's Eve. || 我們除夕吃水餃。 || make dumplings=包水餃 || We made dumplings ___ New Year's Eve. || on|in|at || 特定的日子用 on。 || 我們在除夕包水餃。
duty || n. || 責任；職責 || It's our duty to protect the Earth. || 保護地球是我們的責任。 || on duty=值班中; duty-free=免稅 || It's our duty ___ the Earth. || to protect|protect|protecting || It is + 名詞 + to V。 || 保護地球是我們的責任。
eagle || n. || 老鷹 || The eagle flew high in the sky. || 老鷹在天空高飛。 || bald eagle=白頭海鵰 || An eagle can see much ___ than humans. || farther|far|farthest || 兩者比較用比較級 farther。 || 老鷹可以看得比人類遠得多。
earn || v. || 賺 || He earns money by teaching. || 他靠教書賺錢。 || earn money=賺錢; earn a living=謀生 || He earns money ___ delivering newspapers. || by|with|from of || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 他靠送報紙賺錢。
earrings || n. || 耳環 || She is wearing gold earrings. || 她戴著金耳環。 || a pair of earrings=一副耳環 || These earrings ___ a gift from my mom. || were|was|is || These earrings 複數，用 were。 || 這副耳環是我媽媽送的禮物。
ease || n. || 輕鬆；容易 || He finished the test with ease. || 他輕鬆地完成了考試。 || with ease=輕鬆地; at ease=自在 || She solved the problem ___ ease. || with|by|in || with ease = 輕鬆地。 || 她輕鬆地解決了問題。
edge || n. || 邊緣 || Don't stand at the edge of the cliff. || 不要站在懸崖邊。 || on the edge of=在…的邊緣 || The cup is on the edge ___ the table. || of|in|at || on the edge of = 在…的邊緣。 || 杯子在桌子的邊緣。
education || n. || 教育 || Education is important. || 教育很重要。 || higher education=高等教育 || Everyone should have the chance ___ a good education. || to get|getting|get || have the chance to + 原形動詞。 || 每個人都應該有機會接受良好的教育。
effort || n. || 努力 || Learning English takes effort. || 學英文需要努力。 || make an effort=努力 || He made a great effort ___ the exam. || to pass|passing|pass || make an effort to + 原形動詞。 || 他非常努力要通過考試。
elder || adj. || 年長的 || My elder sister is a nurse. || 我姊姊是護理師。 || elder brother=哥哥 || My elder brother is two years older ___ me. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 我哥哥比我大兩歲。
elect || v. || 選舉 || We elected Amy as class leader. || 我們選艾咪當班長。 || elect sb as=選某人當… || Amy ___ elected class leader last week. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 艾咪上週被選為班長。
electric || adj. || 電的 || I want an electric scooter. || 我想要一台電動機車。 || electric car=電動車; electric fan=電風扇 || Electric cars are ___ for the environment. || better|good|best || 比較級 better（good 的比較級）。 || 電動車對環境比較好。
embarrass || v. || 使尷尬 || Don't embarrass me in front of my friends. || 別在我朋友面前讓我難堪。 || embarrassed=感到尷尬的 || I was ___ embarrassed that my face turned red. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…；此句 embarrassed 由 embarrass 變化而來。 || 我尷尬到臉都紅了。
emotion || n. || 情緒 || She hid her emotions. || 她隱藏了自己的情緒。 || show emotion=表露情緒 || Music can express emotions ___ words cannot. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（emotions）。 || 音樂可以表達文字無法表達的情感。
emphasize || v. || 強調 || The teacher emphasized the importance of reading. || 老師強調閱讀的重要性。 || emphasize that=強調… || The teacher emphasized the importance ___ reading. || of|for|on || the importance of = …的重要性。 || 老師強調閱讀的重要性。
employ || v. || 雇用 || The company employs 500 workers. || 這家公司雇用五百名員工。 || be employed by=受雇於… || She ___ employed by a big company. || is|are|has || 被動語態；She 用 is + p.p.。 || 她受雇於一家大公司。
empty || adj. || 空的 || The box is empty. || 箱子是空的。 || empty room=空房間; empty-handed=空手 || The classroom was empty ___ the students went home. || after|before|during || after + 子句 = 在…之後。 || 學生回家後教室就空了。
enemy || n. || 敵人 || Laziness is your biggest enemy. || 懶惰是你最大的敵人。 || make enemies=樹敵 || The soldiers were attacked ___ the enemy. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 士兵們遭到敵人攻擊。
