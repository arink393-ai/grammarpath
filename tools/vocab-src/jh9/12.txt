# 九年級 12：princess – rub
princess || n. || 公主 || The princess lived in a castle. || 公主住在城堡裡。 || fairy-tale princess=童話公主 || The princess was saved ___ a brave knight. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 公主被一位勇敢的騎士救了。
principal || n. || 校長 || The principal gave a speech. || 校長發表了演說。 || school principal=校長 || The principal ___ speaking to the students now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The principal 用 is。 || 校長現在正在對學生說話。
principle || n. || 原則 || He has strong principles. || 他很有原則。 || in principle=原則上 || In principle, I agree ___ you, but I have some questions. || with|to|at || agree with sb = 同意某人。 || 原則上我同意你，但我有一些問題。
print || v. || 列印；印刷 || Please print this page. || 請印出這一頁。 || print out=印出 || The books ___ printed in Taiwan. || were|was|are being || 被動語態；The books 複數，過去用 were。 || 這些書是在台灣印的。
printer || n. || 印表機 || The printer is out of paper. || 印表機沒紙了。 || printer ink=墨水匣 || The printer is out ___ paper. || of|from|with || out of = 用完…。 || 印表機沒紙了。
private || adj. || 私人的 || This is a private room. || 這是私人房間。 || private school=私立學校 || This beach is private, ___ we can't swim here. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這是私人海灘，所以我們不能在這裡游泳。
produce || v. || 生產 || This factory produces cars. || 這間工廠生產汽車。 || produce food=生產食物 || These cars ___ produced in Japan. || are|is|be || 被動語態；These cars 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 這些車是在日本生產的。
production || n. || 生產；製作 || The production of cars has increased. || 汽車產量增加了。 || mass production=大量生產 || The production ___ rice has decreased this year. || of|for|in || the production of = …的產量。 || 今年稻米的產量減少了。
professor || n. || 教授 || She is a professor at a university. || 她是大學教授。 || university professor=大學教授 || The professor ___ taught here for twenty years. || has|have|is || 現在完成式；The professor 用 has。 || 那位教授在這裡教了二十年。
progress || n. || 進步 || You have made great progress. || 你進步很多。 || make progress=進步 || You have made great progress ___ English. || in|on|at || make progress in = 在…方面進步。 || 你的英文進步很多。
project || n. || 專題；計畫 || We are working on a science project. || 我們正在做一個科學專題。 || school project=學校專題 || We ___ working on a group project now. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；主詞 We 用 are。 || 我們現在正在做分組專題。
promise || v./n. || 承諾 || I promise I will come. || 我保證我會來。 || keep a promise=遵守承諾; make a promise=許下承諾 || He promised ___ me with my homework. || to help|helping|help || promise to + 原形動詞。 || 他答應要幫我寫功課。
pronounce || v. || 發音 || How do you pronounce this word? || 這個字怎麼唸？ || pronounce a word=唸一個字 || How ___ you pronounce this word? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 這個字怎麼唸？
protect || v. || 保護 || We should protect the environment. || 我們應該保護環境。 || protect A from B=保護 A 免於 B || Sunglasses protect your eyes ___ the sun. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 太陽眼鏡保護你的眼睛不受陽光傷害。
provide || v. || 提供 || The school provides lunch. || 學校提供午餐。 || provide sb with sth=提供某人某物 || The hotel provides guests ___ free breakfast. || with|for|to || provide sb with sth = 提供某人某物。 || 這家飯店提供房客免費早餐。
pump || n./v. || 抽水機；打氣 || Use a pump to fill the tire. || 用打氣筒幫輪胎打氣。 || air pump=打氣筒 || The water ___ pumped from the well. || is|are|be || 被動語態；water 不可數用 is + p.p.。 || 水是從井裡抽上來的。
punish || v. || 處罰 || He was punished for being late. || 他因為遲到被處罰。 || punish sb for sth=因某事處罰某人 || He was punished ___ cheating on the test. || for|of|with || punish sb for + V-ing = 因…處罰某人。 || 他因為考試作弊被處罰。
purpose || n. || 目的 || What is the purpose of your visit? || 你來訪的目的是什麼？ || on purpose=故意地 || He broke the vase ___ purpose. || on|in|by || on purpose = 故意地。 || 他故意打破了花瓶。
purse || n. || 錢包（女用） || She lost her purse. || 她弄丟了她的錢包。 || leather purse=皮包 || She found her purse ___ the sofa. || under|below of|beneath of || under = 在…下面。 || 她在沙發底下找到了她的錢包。
puzzle || n. || 謎題；拼圖 || I like doing puzzles. || 我喜歡玩拼圖。 || jigsaw puzzle=拼圖 || It took me two hours ___ finish the puzzle. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 我花了兩個小時完成這個拼圖。
quit || v. || 放棄；辭職；戒 || He quit smoking. || 他戒菸了。 || quit + V-ing=停止做… || My dad quit ___ last year. || smoking|to smoke|smoke || quit + V-ing = 戒掉、停止做…。 || 我爸爸去年戒菸了。
railroad || n. || 鐵路 || The railroad goes along the coast. || 鐵路沿著海岸延伸。 || railroad station=火車站 || The railroad ___ built one hundred years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這條鐵路建於一百年前。
railway || n. || 鐵路 || The railway station is near here. || 火車站在這附近。 || railway station=火車站 || Is the railway station far ___ here? || from|of|to || far from = 離…很遠。 || 火車站離這裡遠嗎？
raincoat || n. || 雨衣 || Wear a raincoat when it rains. || 下雨時穿雨衣。 || put on a raincoat=穿上雨衣 || Put on your raincoat ___ you'll get wet. || or|and|but || 祈使句 + or = 否則…。 || 穿上雨衣，不然你會淋濕。
rare || adj. || 稀有的 || This bird is very rare. || 這種鳥非常稀有。 || rare animal=稀有動物 || It is rare ___ see snow in Taipei. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 在台北很少看到雪。
rather || adv. || 寧願；相當 || I'd rather stay home. || 我寧願待在家。 || would rather=寧願; rather than=而不是 || I would rather stay home ___ go out. || than|then|to || would rather A than B = 寧願 A 也不要 B。 || 我寧願待在家也不要出門。
realize || v. || 了解；實現 || I didn't realize it was so late. || 我沒發現已經這麼晚了。 || realize one's dream=實現夢想 || I didn't realize ___ it was so late. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 realize 的受詞。 || 我沒發現已經這麼晚了。
receive || v. || 收到 || I received a letter from my friend. || 我收到朋友寄來的信。 || receive a letter=收到信 || I received a gift ___ my aunt. || from|to|of || receive sth from sb = 從某人那裡收到某物。 || 我收到阿姨送的禮物。
record || n./v. || 紀錄；錄製 || She broke the world record. || 她打破了世界紀錄。 || world record=世界紀錄; break a record=破紀錄 || This song ___ recorded in 2020. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這首歌是 2020 年錄製的。
recover || v. || 康復；恢復 || He has recovered from his illness. || 他已經從病中康復了。 || recover from=從…中康復 || He has recovered ___ the flu. || from|of|with || recover from = 從…中康復。 || 他已經從流感中康復了。
rectangle || n. || 長方形 || A rectangle has four sides. || 長方形有四個邊。 || rectangle shape=長方形 || A rectangle ___ four right angles. || has|have|is || A rectangle 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 長方形有四個直角。
recycle || v. || 回收 || We recycle bottles and paper. || 我們回收瓶子和紙。 || recycle bottles=回收瓶子 || Plastic bottles can ___ recycled. || be|is|are || 助動詞 can 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 塑膠瓶可以回收。
refuse || v. || 拒絕 || She refused to help me. || 她拒絕幫我。 || refuse to V=拒絕做… || He refused ___ the money. || to take|taking|take || refuse to + 原形動詞。 || 他拒絕收下那筆錢。
regret || v. || 後悔 || I regret not studying harder. || 我後悔沒有更用功。 || regret + V-ing=後悔做了… || I regret ___ so much time on games. || spending|to spend|spend || regret + V-ing = 後悔做了…（過去的事）。 || 我後悔花了那麼多時間在遊戲上。
regular || adj. || 規律的；普通的 || Regular exercise is good for you. || 規律運動對你有益。 || regular exercise=規律運動 || Regular exercise ___ good for your health. || is|are|be || Regular exercise 單數（不可數），用 is。 || 規律運動對健康有益。
reject || v. || 拒絕 || My idea was rejected. || 我的想法被否決了。 || reject an offer=拒絕提議 || My idea ___ rejected by the team. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我的想法被團隊否決了。
remind || v. || 提醒 || Remind me to call Mom. || 提醒我打電話給媽媽。 || remind sb to V=提醒某人做…; remind sb of=使某人想起… || This song reminds me ___ my childhood. || of|to|for || remind sb of = 使某人想起…。 || 這首歌讓我想起童年。
rent || v./n. || 租 || We rent an apartment. || 我們租了一間公寓。 || rent a car=租車; pay the rent=付房租 || We rented a car ___ our trip to Hualien. || for|to|at || for + 名詞 = 為了…。 || 我們為了去花蓮旅行租了一輛車。
repair || v. || 修理 || My bike needs to be repaired. || 我的腳踏車需要修理。 || repair a car=修車 || My bike needs ___ repaired. || to be|being|be || need to be + p.p. = 需要被…。 || 我的腳踏車需要修理。
respect || v./n. || 尊重 || We should respect our parents. || 我們應該尊重父母。 || show respect=表示尊敬 || We should respect ___ elders. || our|us|ours || 名詞 elders 前用所有格形容詞 our。 || 我們應該尊敬長輩。
responsible || adj. || 負責任的 || You are responsible for your actions. || 你要為你的行為負責。 || be responsible for=對…負責 || Who is responsible ___ this mess? || for|to|of || be responsible for = 對…負責。 || 誰要為這一團亂負責？
result || n. || 結果 || The result of the test was good. || 考試結果很好。 || as a result=結果 || She studied hard. As ___ result, she passed the exam. || a|an|the || as a result = 結果。 || 她很用功，結果通過了考試。
return || v./n. || 歸還；返回 || Please return the book tomorrow. || 請明天還書。 || return to=回到…; return sth to sb=把某物還給某人 || Please return the book ___ the library. || to|for|at || return sth to… = 把某物歸還給…。 || 請把書還給圖書館。
review || v./n. || 複習；評論 || Let's review the lesson. || 我們來複習這一課。 || review for a test=為考試複習 || You should review your notes ___ the test. || before|after|since || 考試「之前」複習，用 before。 || 你應該在考試前複習筆記。
revise || v. || 修改 || Please revise your essay. || 請修改你的作文。 || revise an essay=修改作文 || The essay ___ revised three times. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這篇作文修改了三次。
rob || v. || 搶劫 || The bank was robbed. || 那家銀行被搶了。 || rob sb of sth=搶奪某人的某物 || The man robbed the woman ___ her purse. || of|from|with || rob sb of sth = 搶奪某人的某物。 || 那個男人搶了那位女士的錢包。
role || n. || 角色 || She played the role of the queen. || 她飾演女王的角色。 || play a role=扮演角色; role model=榜樣 || Parents play an important role ___ children's lives. || in|on|at || play a role in = 在…中扮演角色。 || 父母在孩子的生命中扮演重要的角色。
roller skate || n. || 直排輪；溜冰鞋 || I want a pair of roller skates. || 我想要一雙直排輪。 || go roller skating=去溜直排輪 || Wear a helmet when you ___ roller skating. || go|goes|going || 主詞 you，動詞用原形。 || 溜直排輪時要戴安全帽。
roof || n. || 屋頂 || There is a cat on the roof. || 屋頂上有一隻貓。 || on the roof=在屋頂上 || There is a cat ___ the roof. || on|in|at || 在屋頂上用 on。 || 屋頂上有一隻貓。
rub || v. || 摩擦；揉 || Don't rub your eyes. || 不要揉眼睛。 || rub one's eyes=揉眼睛 || Don't rub your eyes ___ your dirty hands. || with|by|in || 用某物用 with。 || 不要用髒手揉眼睛。
