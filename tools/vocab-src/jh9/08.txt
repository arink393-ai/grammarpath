# 九年級 08：hop – kitten
hop || v. || 單腳跳；（兔子）跳 || The rabbit hopped away. || 兔子跳走了。 || hop on=跳上（車） || The rabbit hopped ___ the garden. || into|of|at || hop into = 跳進…。 || 兔子跳進了花園。
horrible || adj. || 可怕的 || The weather was horrible. || 天氣糟透了。 || horrible dream=可怕的夢 || I had a horrible dream ___ night. || last|yesterday|this || last night = 昨晚。 || 我昨晚做了一個可怕的夢。
host || n./v. || 主人；主持人；主辦 || Taiwan hosted the Universiade in 2017. || 台灣在 2017 年主辦了世大運。 || host a party=辦派對 || The Universiade ___ hosted by Taipei in 2017. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 世大運在 2017 年由台北主辦。
housework || n. || 家事 || I help my mom with the housework. || 我幫媽媽做家事。 || do housework=做家事 || I help my mom ___ the housework. || with|for|to || help sb with sth = 幫某人做某事。 || 我幫媽媽做家事。
hug || v./n. || 擁抱 || She gave me a big hug. || 她給了我一個大大的擁抱。 || give sb a hug=擁抱某人 || She gave ___ a big hug. || me|I|my || gave 後面的人用受格 me。 || 她給了我一個大大的擁抱。
human || n./adj. || 人類 || Humans need water to live. || 人類需要水才能生存。 || human being=人類 || Humans need water ___ live. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 人類需要水才能生存。
humble || adj. || 謙虛的 || She is humble about her success. || 她對自己的成功很謙虛。 || humble person=謙虛的人 || Although he is famous, he is still humble, ___ he? || isn't|doesn't|is || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || 雖然他很有名，他仍然很謙虛，不是嗎？
humid || adj. || 潮濕的 || Taiwan is hot and humid in summer. || 台灣夏天又熱又潮濕。 || hot and humid=悶熱 || It's hot and humid ___ summer in Taiwan. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 台灣的夏天又熱又潮濕。
humor || n. || 幽默 || He has a good sense of humor. || 他很有幽默感。 || sense of humor=幽默感 || My teacher has a good sense ___ humor. || of|for|in || a sense of humor = 幽默感。 || 我的老師很有幽默感。
humorous || adj. || 幽默的 || The speech was humorous. || 那場演講很幽默。 || humorous story=幽默的故事 || He is the ___ humorous person I know. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 他是我認識最幽默的人。
hunger || n. || 飢餓 || Many children die of hunger. || 很多孩子死於飢餓。 || die of hunger=餓死 || Many people in the world suffer ___ hunger. || from|of|with || suffer from = 受…之苦。 || 世界上很多人受飢餓之苦。
hunter || n. || 獵人 || The hunter went into the forest. || 獵人走進了森林。 || lion hunter=獵獅人 || The deer ___ caught by the hunter. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那隻鹿被獵人抓到了。
ignore || v. || 忽視 || Don't ignore me. || 不要不理我。 || ignore sb=忽視某人 || The warning ___ ignored by many people. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個警告被很多人忽視了。
ill || adj. || 生病的 || He is ill in bed. || 他臥病在床。 || fall ill=生病 || He has been ill ___ last week. || since|for|from || since + 過去時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 他從上週起就生病了。
imagine || v. || 想像 || Can you imagine living on the moon? || 你能想像住在月球上嗎？ || imagine + V-ing=想像做… || Can you imagine ___ on the moon? || living|to live|live || imagine 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 你能想像住在月球上嗎？
impolite || adj. || 無禮的 || It's impolite to talk with your mouth full. || 嘴裡塞滿食物說話是不禮貌的。 || impolite behavior=無禮的行為 || It's impolite ___ talk with food in your mouth. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 嘴裡有食物時說話是不禮貌的。
importance || n. || 重要性 || Don't forget the importance of sleep. || 別忘了睡眠的重要性。 || the importance of=…的重要性 || The teacher talked about the importance ___ reading. || of|for|to || the importance of = …的重要性。 || 老師談到閱讀的重要性。
impossible || adj. || 不可能的 || Nothing is impossible. || 沒有不可能的事。 || impossible mission=不可能的任務 || It is impossible ___ finish the work in one day. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 一天之內完成這項工作是不可能的。
improve || v. || 改善 || I want to improve my English. || 我想改善我的英文。 || improve one's English=提升英文程度 || Reading more can help you improve ___ English. || your|you|yours || 名詞 English 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 多閱讀可以幫助你提升英文。
include || v. || 包括 || The price includes breakfast. || 價格包含早餐。 || including=包括 || Breakfast ___ included in the price. || is|are|be || 被動語態；Breakfast 用 is + p.p.。 || 價格包含早餐。
income || n. || 收入 || His income is not high. || 他的收入不高。 || high income=高收入 || Her income is ___ than mine. || higher|high|highest || 兩者比較用比較級 higher。 || 她的收入比我高。
increase || v./n. || 增加 || The price has increased. || 價格上漲了。 || increase by=增加了… || The number of cars ___ increased a lot. || has|have|is || The number of… 視為單數，用 has + p.p.。 || 汽車的數量增加了很多。
independent || adj. || 獨立的 || She is very independent. || 她很獨立。 || be independent of=不依賴… || Students should learn to be independent ___ their parents. || of|from|with || be independent of = 不依賴…。 || 學生應該學會不依賴父母。
indicate || v. || 指出；表示 || The sign indicates the way to the exit. || 這個標誌指出往出口的路。 || indicate that=表示… || The study indicates ___ exercise is good for the brain. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 indicate 的受詞。 || 研究顯示運動對大腦有益。
influence || n./v. || 影響 || Parents have a great influence on their children. || 父母對孩子有很大的影響。 || have an influence on=對…有影響 || Parents have a strong influence ___ their children. || on|in|at || have an influence on = 對…有影響。 || 父母對孩子有很大的影響。
information || n. || 資訊 || I need more information. || 我需要更多資訊。 || a piece of information=一則資訊 || Can you give me ___ information about the trip? || some|a|many || information 不可數，用 some（不能用 a 或 many）。 || 你可以給我一些關於這趟旅行的資訊嗎？
ink || n. || 墨水 || My pen has run out of ink. || 我的筆沒水了。 || run out of ink=沒墨水 || My pen has run out ___ ink. || of|from|with || run out of = 用完…。 || 我的筆沒水了。
insist || v. || 堅持 || He insisted on paying. || 他堅持要付錢。 || insist on=堅持… || He insisted ___ paying for dinner. || on|in|to || insist on + V-ing = 堅持做…。 || 他堅持要付晚餐錢。
inspire || v. || 啟發；激勵 || Her story inspired me. || 她的故事啟發了我。 || inspire sb to V=激勵某人做… || Her story inspired me ___ harder. || to study|study|studying || inspire sb to + 原形動詞。 || 她的故事激勵我更用功。
instant || adj. || 立即的；即溶的 || I had instant noodles for lunch. || 我午餐吃泡麵。 || instant noodles=泡麵; instant coffee=即溶咖啡 || Eating too many instant noodles ___ bad for your health. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 吃太多泡麵對健康不好。
instrument || n. || 樂器；儀器 || Can you play any instruments? || 你會演奏任何樂器嗎？ || musical instrument=樂器 || Can you play any musical instruments? Yes, I can play ___ piano. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 你會演奏樂器嗎？會，我會彈鋼琴。
intelligent || adj. || 聰明的 || Dolphins are intelligent animals. || 海豚是聰明的動物。 || intelligent student=聰明的學生 || Dolphins are ___ intelligent than dogs. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 海豚比狗更聰明。
international || adj. || 國際的 || English is an international language. || 英文是一種國際語言。 || international airport=國際機場 || English is ___ international language. || an|a|the || international 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 英文是一種國際語言。
interrupt || v. || 打斷 || Don't interrupt me when I'm talking. || 我說話時不要打斷我。 || interrupt sb=打斷某人 || Sorry ___ interrupt, but can I ask a question? || to|for|of || Sorry to + 原形動詞。 || 抱歉打斷一下，我可以問個問題嗎？
introduce || v. || 介紹 || Let me introduce myself. || 讓我自我介紹。 || introduce oneself=自我介紹; introduce A to B=把 A 介紹給 B || Let me introduce my friend ___ you. || to|for|with || introduce A to B = 把 A 介紹給 B。 || 讓我把我的朋友介紹給你。
invent || v. || 發明 || Who invented the telephone? || 誰發明了電話？ || invent a machine=發明機器 || The telephone ___ invented by Bell. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 電話是貝爾發明的。
invitation || n. || 邀請 || Thank you for your invitation. || 謝謝你的邀請。 || accept an invitation=接受邀請 || Thank you ___ your kind invitation. || for|to|of || thank you for + 名詞。 || 謝謝你的盛情邀請。
iron || n./v. || 鐵；熨斗；熨燙 || My mom irons my shirts. || 我媽媽幫我燙襯衫。 || iron clothes=燙衣服 || My shirt ___ ironed by my mom this morning. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我的襯衫今天早上被媽媽燙好了。
jam || n. || 果醬；堵塞 || I like strawberry jam on my toast. || 我喜歡在吐司上塗草莓果醬。 || traffic jam=塞車 || We were late because we were stuck ___ a traffic jam. || in|on|at || be stuck in = 困在…之中。 || 我們遲到了，因為塞在車陣中。
jazz || n. || 爵士樂 || My dad likes jazz music. || 我爸爸喜歡爵士樂。 || jazz music=爵士樂 || My dad enjoys ___ to jazz. || listening|to listen|listen || enjoy 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我爸爸喜歡聽爵士樂。
jealous || adj. || 嫉妒的 || She is jealous of her sister. || 她嫉妒她的妹妹。 || be jealous of=嫉妒… || Don't be jealous ___ other people. || of|with|to || be jealous of = 嫉妒…。 || 不要嫉妒別人。
jeep || n. || 吉普車 || We drove a jeep in the mountains. || 我們在山裡開吉普車。 || drive a jeep=開吉普車 || We went up the mountain ___ jeep. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 我們搭吉普車上山。
journalist || n. || 記者 || She wants to be a journalist. || 她想當記者。 || news journalist=新聞記者 || The journalist asked the mayor ___ questions. || some|any|a || 肯定句用 some。 || 那位記者問了市長一些問題。
judge || n./v. || 法官；評審；判斷 || Don't judge a book by its cover. || 不要以貌取人。 || judge by=根據…判斷 || Don't judge people ___ their looks. || by|with|from || judge by = 根據…判斷。 || 不要以外表評斷人。
kangaroo || n. || 袋鼠 || Kangaroos live in Australia. || 袋鼠住在澳洲。 || baby kangaroo=小袋鼠 || Kangaroos can be found ___ Australia. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 在澳洲可以看到袋鼠。
ketchup || n. || 番茄醬 || I like ketchup with my fries. || 我喜歡薯條沾番茄醬。 || tomato ketchup=番茄醬 || Would you like some ketchup ___ your fries? || with|to|for || with = 搭配…。 || 你的薯條要搭配番茄醬嗎？
kilometer || n. || 公里 || The school is two kilometers away. || 學校在兩公里外。 || ten kilometers=十公里 || The school is two kilometers away ___ my home. || from|of|to || A is … away from B = A 離 B 有…遠。 || 學校離我家兩公里。
kindergarten || n. || 幼稚園 || My little sister goes to kindergarten. || 我妹妹讀幼稚園。 || go to kindergarten=上幼稚園 || My sister has gone to kindergarten ___ last year. || since|for|from || since + 過去時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 我妹妹從去年開始上幼稚園。
kingdom || n. || 王國 || The king ruled the kingdom for 50 years. || 國王統治這個王國五十年。 || the animal kingdom=動物界 || The kingdom ___ ruled by a wise king. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那個王國由一位賢明的國王統治。
kitten || n. || 小貓 || The kitten is playing with a ball. || 小貓在玩球。 || cute kitten=可愛的小貓 || The kitten ___ playing with a ball of yarn. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The kitten 用 is。 || 小貓正在玩毛線球。
