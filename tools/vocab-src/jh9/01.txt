# 九年級 01：absent – ball
absent || adj. || 缺席的 || Tom was absent from school yesterday. || 湯姆昨天沒來上學。 || be absent from=缺席… || Tom was absent ___ school because he was sick. || from|of|at || be absent from = 缺席…。 || 湯姆因為生病沒來上學。
accept || v. || 接受 || She accepted my invitation. || 她接受了我的邀請。 || accept a gift=收下禮物; accept an invitation=接受邀請 || The invitation ___ accepted by everyone. || was|were|is being || 被動語態；The invitation 單數，過去用 was。 || 大家都接受了這個邀請。
accident || n. || 意外；事故 || He was hurt in a car accident. || 他在車禍中受傷。 || car accident=車禍; by accident=意外地 || He was hurt ___ a car accident last year. || in|on|by || 在意外事故中用 in。 || 他去年在一場車禍中受傷。
active || adj. || 活躍的；主動的 || She is an active student in class. || 她在課堂上是個積極的學生。 || be active in=積極參與… || She is active ___ school activities. || in|on|at || be active in = 積極參與…。 || 她積極參與學校活動。
activity || n. || 活動 || We have many activities at school. || 我們在學校有很多活動。 || outdoor activity=戶外活動; club activity=社團活動 || Which activity ___ you like best? || do|are|does || 一般動詞 like 的疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你最喜歡哪個活動？
add || v. || 加 || Add some sugar to the tea. || 在茶裡加點糖。 || add A to B=把 A 加到 B; add up=加總 || Add some salt ___ the soup. || to|in|with || add A to B = 把 A 加進 B。 || 在湯裡加一些鹽。
address || n. || 地址 || What's your address? || 你的地址是什麼？ || e-mail address=電子郵件地址 || Can you tell me ___ your address is? || what|which|where || 間接問句：what + 主詞 + 動詞。 || 你可以告訴我你的地址是什麼嗎？
admire || v. || 欽佩；欣賞 || I admire my father very much. || 我非常欽佩我爸爸。 || admire sb for sth=因某事欽佩某人 || I admire her ___ her courage. || for|of|to || admire sb for sth = 因某事欽佩某人。 || 我欽佩她的勇氣。
adult || n. || 成人 || Tickets for adults are 200 dollars. || 成人票兩百元。 || become an adult=成為大人 || Children must be with ___ adult. || an|a|the more || adult 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 兒童必須有成人陪同。
advertisement || n. || 廣告 || I saw an advertisement for a new phone. || 我看到一則新手機的廣告。 || TV advertisement=電視廣告 || I saw an advertisement ___ a new phone on TV. || for|of|to || an advertisement for = …的廣告。 || 我在電視上看到一則新手機的廣告。
advice || n. || 建議 || Can you give me some advice? || 你可以給我一些建議嗎？ || give advice=給建議; take one's advice=接受某人的建議 || Can you give me ___ advice? || some|an|many || advice 不可數，用 some。 || 你可以給我一些建議嗎？
advise || v. || 建議；勸告 || The doctor advised me to rest. || 醫生建議我休息。 || advise sb to V=建議某人做… || The doctor advised me ___ more water. || to drink|drink|drinking || advise sb to + 原形動詞。 || 醫生建議我多喝水。
affect || v. || 影響 || The weather affects my mood. || 天氣會影響我的心情。 || affect sb=影響某人 || Lack of sleep can affect your health ___ your grades. || and|but|or || 兩者都會受影響，用 and。 || 睡眠不足會影響你的健康和成績。
against || prep. || 反對；靠著 || Many people are against the plan. || 很多人反對這個計畫。 || against the wall=靠著牆; be against=反對 || Are you for ___ against the plan? || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 你贊成還是反對這個計畫？
aim || n./v. || 目標；瞄準 || My aim is to become a doctor. || 我的目標是成為醫生。 || aim at=瞄準; aim to V=打算做… || My aim is ___ a doctor. || to become|become|became || be 動詞 + to V 當補語。 || 我的目標是成為醫生。
air conditioner || n. || 冷氣機 || Turn on the air conditioner. It's hot. || 開冷氣，好熱。 || turn on the air conditioner=開冷氣 || Please turn off the air conditioner ___ you leave. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 離開時請關冷氣。
airlines || n. || 航空公司 || Which airlines are you flying with? || 你搭哪家航空公司？ || China Airlines=中華航空 || She works ___ a flight attendant for China Airlines. || as|like|for || work as + 職業 = 擔任…。 || 她在中華航空擔任空服員。
alarm || n. || 警報；鬧鐘 || The fire alarm went off. || 火災警報響了。 || alarm clock=鬧鐘; fire alarm=火災警報 || My alarm clock didn't ring, so I ___ late for school. || was|am|were || 過去的事，主詞 I 用 was。 || 我的鬧鐘沒響，所以我上學遲到了。
album || n. || 相簿；專輯 || This is my photo album. || 這是我的相簿。 || photo album=相簿; new album=新專輯 || Her new album ___ released last week. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 她的新專輯上週發行了。
alike || adj. || 相像的 || The twins look alike. || 那對雙胞胎長得很像。 || look alike=看起來很像 || The two sisters look alike, ___ they are very different in personality. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 那兩姊妹長得很像，但個性很不同。
alive || adj. || 活著的 || The fish is still alive. || 那條魚還活著。 || stay alive=活下來 || The fish is still alive, ___ it? || isn't|doesn't|wasn't || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || 那條魚還活著，不是嗎？
alone || adj./adv. || 單獨的；獨自 || She lives alone. || 她一個人住。 || live alone=獨居; leave me alone=別管我 || She has lived alone ___ five years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 她一個人住已經五年了。
aloud || adv. || 大聲地；出聲地 || Please read the text aloud. || 請把課文唸出聲。 || read aloud=朗讀 || The teacher asked me ___ the poem aloud. || to read|read|reading || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 老師要我把詩唸出來。
alphabet || n. || 字母表 || There are 26 letters in the English alphabet. || 英文字母有 26 個。 || the alphabet=字母表 || How many letters ___ there in the English alphabet? || are|is|do || There be 問數量：How many… are there? || 英文字母有幾個？
altogether || adv. || 總共；完全 || There are fifty students altogether. || 總共有五十個學生。 || altogether=總共 || How much is it altogether? It ___ 300 dollars. || is|are|has || It 單數，用 is。 || 總共多少錢？三百元。
ambulance || n. || 救護車 || Call an ambulance! || 快叫救護車！ || call an ambulance=叫救護車 || The injured man ___ taken to the hospital by ambulance. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那個受傷的人被救護車送到醫院。
among || prep. || 在…之中（三者以上） || She is the tallest among her friends. || 她是朋友之中最高的。 || among them=在他們之中 || Divide the cake among the four children, ___ you? || will|do|are || 祈使句的附加問句用 will you。 || 把蛋糕分給四個孩子，好嗎？
amount || n. || 數量 || He spent a large amount of money. || 他花了一大筆錢。 || a large amount of=大量的 || A large amount of water ___ wasted every day. || is|are|be || a large amount of + 不可數名詞，動詞用單數；被動 is + p.p.。 || 每天有大量的水被浪費。
ancient || adj. || 古代的 || We visited an ancient temple. || 我們參觀了一座古廟。 || ancient history=古代史; ancient times=古代 || This ancient temple ___ built more than 300 years ago. || was|is|has || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這座古廟建於三百多年前。
angel || n. || 天使 || She sings like an angel. || 她唱歌像天使一樣。 || little angel=小天使 || You are ___ angel! Thank you so much. || an|a|the || angel 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 你真是個天使！太謝謝你了。
anger || n. || 憤怒 || He shouted in anger. || 他憤怒地大叫。 || in anger=生氣地 || He left the room ___ anger. || in|with|by || in anger = 憤怒地。 || 他憤怒地離開房間。
ankle || n. || 腳踝 || I hurt my ankle when I was running. || 我跑步時扭傷了腳踝。 || twist one's ankle=扭傷腳踝 || I hurt my ankle ___ I was playing basketball. || while|during|for || while + 過去進行式，表示當時正在做的事。 || 我在打籃球時扭傷了腳踝。
anywhere || adv. || 任何地方 || I can't find my key anywhere. || 我到處都找不到我的鑰匙。 || anywhere else=其他任何地方 || You can sit anywhere you ___. || like|likes|liked it || 名詞子句 anywhere (that) you like；主詞 you 用原形。 || 你想坐哪裡都可以。
apologize || v. || 道歉 || You should apologize to her. || 你應該向她道歉。 || apologize to sb for sth=為某事向某人道歉 || You should apologize ___ her for being late. || to|for|at || apologize to sb = 向某人道歉。 || 你應該為遲到向她道歉。
appreciate || v. || 感激；欣賞 || I really appreciate your help. || 我真的很感激你的幫忙。 || appreciate it=感激 || I would appreciate it if you ___ help me. || could|can|will be || 禮貌的請求：I would appreciate it if you could… || 如果你能幫我，我會很感激。
argue || v. || 爭吵；爭論 || Don't argue with your sister. || 不要跟你姊姊吵架。 || argue with sb=和某人爭吵; argue about=爭論… || They often argue ___ money. || about|to|with || argue about + 事 = 為…爭吵。 || 他們常為錢吵架。
armchair || n. || 扶手椅 || Grandpa is sitting in the armchair. || 爺爺坐在扶手椅上。 || in an armchair=坐在扶手椅上 || Grandpa is sleeping ___ the armchair. || in|on|at || 坐在有扶手的椅子裡用 in。 || 爺爺在扶手椅上睡覺。
army || n. || 軍隊 || My brother is in the army. || 我哥哥在當兵。 || join the army=從軍 || My cousin joined the army ___ he graduated. || after|during|while of || after + 子句 = 在…之後。 || 我表哥畢業後就從軍了。
arrange || v. || 安排；整理 || Can you arrange a meeting for us? || 你可以幫我們安排會議嗎？ || arrange a meeting=安排會議 || The books are arranged ___ size. || by|with|in || arrange by… = 依照…排列。 || 這些書是依照大小排列的。
artist || n. || 藝術家 || Van Gogh is a famous artist. || 梵谷是一位有名的藝術家。 || famous artist=有名的藝術家 || Van Gogh was a famous artist ___ painted Sunflowers. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾「人」用 who。 || 梵谷是畫了〈向日葵〉的著名藝術家。
asleep || adj. || 睡著的 || The baby is asleep. || 寶寶睡著了。 || fall asleep=睡著; fast asleep=熟睡 || I fell asleep ___ I was watching TV. || while|during|until || while + 進行中的動作 = 在…的時候。 || 我看電視時睡著了。
assistant || n. || 助理 || She is the manager's assistant. || 她是經理的助理。 || shop assistant=店員 || The assistant helped me ___ the right size. || find|finding|found || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 那位助理幫我找到了合適的尺寸。
assume || v. || 假定；以為 || I assume you are hungry. || 我猜你餓了。 || assume that=假定… || I assumed ___ you knew the answer. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 assume 的受詞。 || 我以為你知道答案。
attention || n. || 注意 || Pay attention to the teacher. || 注意聽老師說。 || pay attention to=注意… || Please pay attention ___ the traffic lights. || to|at|for || pay attention to = 注意…。 || 請注意紅綠燈。
available || adj. || 可用的；有空的 || Is this seat available? || 這個座位可以坐嗎？ || available for=可供…使用 || Tickets are available ___ the front desk. || at|in|on || 在某個地點（櫃台）用 at。 || 票可以在前台取得。
avoid || v. || 避免 || Avoid eating too much sugar. || 避免吃太多糖。 || avoid + V-ing=避免做… || You should avoid ___ junk food. || eating|to eat|eat || avoid 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 你應該避免吃垃圾食物。
baby sitter || n. || 保母 || My aunt is a baby sitter. || 我阿姨是保母。 || hire a baby sitter=請保母 || The baby sitter takes care ___ the baby when the parents are at work. || of|for|to || take care of = 照顧。 || 父母上班時，保母照顧寶寶。
backpack || n. || 背包 || I carry my books in my backpack. || 我把書放在背包裡。 || school backpack=書包 || Whose backpack is this? It's ___. || hers|her|she || 後面沒有名詞，用所有格代名詞 hers。 || 這是誰的背包？是她的。
backward || adv. || 向後 || Don't walk backward. || 不要倒著走。 || backward and forward=前前後後 || He fell backward ___ he was walking on the ice. || while|during|since || while + 進行中的動作。 || 他在冰上走路時往後跌倒了。
ball || n. || 球 || Throw me the ball. || 把球丟給我。 || play ball=玩球; kick a ball=踢球 || The ball ___ kicked into the goal by Tom. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 球被湯姆踢進了球門。
