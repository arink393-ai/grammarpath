# 九年級 07：frank – honesty
frank || adj. || 坦白的 || To be frank, I don't like it. || 坦白說，我不喜歡。 || to be frank=坦白說 || To be frank, I don't think the plan will ___. || work|works|working || will 後面接原形動詞。 || 坦白說，我不認為這個計畫會成功。
freedom || n. || 自由 || Everyone wants freedom. || 每個人都想要自由。 || freedom of speech=言論自由 || People fought ___ freedom. || for|to|with || fight for = 為…奮鬥。 || 人們為自由而戰。
freezer || n. || 冷凍庫 || Put the ice cream in the freezer. || 把冰淇淋放進冷凍庫。 || in the freezer=在冷凍庫裡 || The ice cream ___ kept in the freezer. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The ice cream 用 is + p.p.。 || 冰淇淋放在冷凍庫裡。
freezing || adj. || 極冷的 || It's freezing outside. || 外面冷死了。 || freezing cold=極冷 || It's freezing outside, ___ wear a warm coat. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 外面冷死了，所以穿件暖和的大衣。
friendship || n. || 友誼 || Our friendship is very important to me. || 我們的友誼對我很重要。 || true friendship=真正的友誼 || Friendship is one of ___ important things in life. || the most|more|most || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。 || 友誼是人生中最重要的事之一。
frighten || v. || 使害怕 || The loud noise frightened the baby. || 巨大的聲響嚇到了寶寶。 || frightened=感到害怕的 || The baby ___ frightened by the loud noise. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 寶寶被巨大的聲響嚇到了。
frisbee || n. || 飛盤 || Let's play frisbee in the park. || 我們去公園玩飛盤吧。 || throw a frisbee=丟飛盤 || The dog caught the frisbee ___ its mouth. || in|on|by || 用嘴巴咬住用 in its mouth。 || 狗用嘴巴接住了飛盤。
furniture || n. || 家具 || We bought some new furniture. || 我們買了一些新家具。 || a piece of furniture=一件家具 || We bought three pieces ___ furniture. || of|for|in || furniture 不可數，用 pieces of furniture。 || 我們買了三件家具。
gain || v. || 獲得；增加 || He gained weight during the holiday. || 他假期間變胖了。 || gain weight=增加體重; gain experience=獲得經驗 || You can gain a lot of experience ___ working part-time. || by|with|at || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 你可以藉由打工獲得很多經驗。
garage || n. || 車庫 || The car is in the garage. || 車子在車庫裡。 || in the garage=在車庫裡 || My dad parks his car ___ the garage. || in|on|at || 在車庫裡用 in。 || 我爸爸把車停在車庫裡。
gather || v. || 聚集 || People gathered in the park. || 人們聚集在公園裡。 || gather together=聚在一起 || Families gather together ___ Chinese New Year. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 農曆新年期間家人團聚。
general || adj. || 一般的；普遍的 || In general, I like my school. || 大致上我喜歡我的學校。 || in general=一般來說 || In general, students ___ summer vacation. || love|loves|loving || students 複數，動詞用原形。 || 一般來說，學生都喜歡暑假。
generous || adj. || 慷慨的 || It was generous of you to share your lunch. || 你願意分享午餐真是大方。 || be generous with=在…方面大方 || It was generous ___ you to share your lunch. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你願意分享午餐真是大方。
genius || n. || 天才 || Einstein was a genius. || 愛因斯坦是個天才。 || genius=天才 || Einstein was a genius ___ changed science. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾「人」用 who。 || 愛因斯坦是一位改變了科學的天才。
gentle || adj. || 溫和的 || She is gentle with animals. || 她對動物很溫柔。 || be gentle with=對…溫柔 || Be gentle ___ the baby. || with|to be|at || be gentle with = 對…溫柔。 || 對寶寶要溫柔。
gentleman || n. || 紳士 || He is a real gentleman. || 他是個真正的紳士。 || ladies and gentlemen=各位先生女士 || A gentleman always lets ladies go ___. || first|firstly|the first || go first = 先走。 || 紳士總是讓女士先走。
geography || n. || 地理 || I'm good at geography. || 我地理很好。 || geography class=地理課 || I'm good ___ geography. || at|of|to || be good at = 擅長…。 || 我地理很好。
gesture || n. || 手勢 || He made a friendly gesture. || 他做了一個友善的手勢。 || make a gesture=做手勢 || Some gestures have different meanings ___ different countries. || in|on|at || 在…國家裡用 in。 || 有些手勢在不同國家有不同的意思。
goal || n. || 目標；球門 || My goal is to pass the exam. || 我的目標是通過考試。 || set a goal=設定目標; score a goal=得分 || My goal is ___ a good senior high school. || to enter|enter|entered || be 動詞 + to V 當補語。 || 我的目標是考上一所好高中。
gold || n. || 金 || She has a gold ring. || 她有一枚金戒指。 || gold medal=金牌 || The ring is made ___ gold. || of|at|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這枚戒指是金子做的。
golden || adj. || 金色的；珍貴的 || She has golden hair. || 她有一頭金髮。 || golden chance=千載難逢的機會 || This is a golden chance ___ you to learn. || for|to|of || a chance for sb to V = 讓某人…的機會。 || 這是你學習的大好機會。
golf || n. || 高爾夫球 || My uncle plays golf on weekends. || 我叔叔週末打高爾夫。 || play golf=打高爾夫球 || My uncle has played golf ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 我叔叔打高爾夫已經十年了。
goodness || n. || 善良 || My goodness! || 我的天啊！ || my goodness=天啊; out of goodness=出於善意 || She helped the old man out ___ goodness. || of|from|for || out of + 名詞 = 出於…。 || 她出於善意幫助那位老人。
government || n. || 政府 || The government built a new park. || 政府建了一座新公園。 || local government=地方政府 || The new park ___ built by the government. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這座新公園是政府建的。
granddaughter || n. || 孫女 || She has two granddaughters. || 她有兩個孫女。 || granddaughter=孫女 || My grandma loves her granddaughter very ___. || much|many|more || very much = 非常。 || 我奶奶非常愛她的孫女。
grandson || n. || 孫子 || He is proud of his grandson. || 他以他的孫子為榮。 || grandson=孫子 || Grandpa is proud ___ his grandson. || of|for|at || be proud of = 以…為榮。 || 爺爺以他的孫子為榮。
greedy || adj. || 貪心的 || Don't be greedy. || 別貪心。 || greedy for=渴求… || The greedy man wanted ___ money. || more|much|most || 比較級 more = 更多。 || 那個貪心的人想要更多錢。
greet || v. || 問候；迎接 || She greeted me with a smile. || 她微笑著和我打招呼。 || greet sb=向某人打招呼 || She greeted us ___ a big smile. || with|by|in || with + 名詞 = 帶著…。 || 她帶著燦爛的笑容迎接我們。
guard || n./v. || 警衛；守衛 || The guard stopped us at the gate. || 警衛在門口攔住了我們。 || security guard=保全 || The building ___ guarded day and night. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The building 用 is + p.p.。 || 這棟大樓日夜都有人看守。
guest || n. || 客人 || We have guests tonight. || 我們今晚有客人。 || guest room=客房 || Be polite ___ our guests. || to|for|with || be polite to = 對…有禮貌。 || 對我們的客人要有禮貌。
guide || n./v. || 導遊；引導 || The guide showed us around the museum. || 導遊帶我們參觀博物館。 || tour guide=導遊 || The tour guide showed us ___ the old town. || around|to|with || show sb around… = 帶某人參觀…。 || 導遊帶我們參觀老城區。
gun || n. || 槍 || The police officer has a gun. || 那位警察有一把槍。 || water gun=水槍 || Guns are not allowed ___ this building. || in|on|at || 在建築物裡用 in。 || 這棟大樓內禁止攜帶槍械。
hair dresser || n. || 美髮師 || My aunt is a hair dresser. || 我阿姨是美髮師。 || hair dresser=美髮師 || I had my hair ___ by a hair dresser. || cut|cutting|to cut || have + 物 + p.p. = 請人把某物…。 || 我請美髮師剪了頭髮。
haircut || n. || 理髮；髮型 || I need a haircut. || 我需要剪頭髮。 || get a haircut=剪頭髮 || I didn't recognize him ___ his new haircut. || with|by|in || with + 特徵 = 有著…。 || 他剪了新髮型，我沒認出他。
hall || n. || 大廳；會堂 || The concert is in the city hall. || 音樂會在市政廳舉行。 || city hall=市政府; dining hall=餐廳 || The meeting will be held ___ the school hall. || in|on|of || 在大廳裡用 in。 || 會議將在學校禮堂舉行。
hammer || n. || 鐵鎚 || He hit the nail with a hammer. || 他用鐵鎚敲釘子。 || hammer a nail=釘釘子 || He hit the nail ___ a hammer. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 他用鐵鎚敲釘子。
handkerchief || n. || 手帕 || He wiped his face with a handkerchief. || 他用手帕擦臉。 || paper handkerchief=面紙 || She always carries a handkerchief ___ her bag. || in|on|at || 放在包包裡用 in。 || 她總是在包包裡放一條手帕。
handle || v./n. || 處理；把手 || I can handle it. || 我可以處理。 || handle a problem=處理問題; door handle=門把 || This problem is ___ difficult for me to handle. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而無法…。 || 這個問題對我來說太難處理了。
hanger || n. || 衣架 || Put your coat on a hanger. || 把你的外套掛在衣架上。 || coat hanger=衣架 || Hang your shirts ___ hangers. || on|in|at || 掛在衣架上用 on。 || 把你的襯衫掛在衣架上。
hardly || adv. || 幾乎不 || I can hardly hear you. || 我幾乎聽不到你說話。 || hardly ever=幾乎從不 || I can hardly believe it, ___ I? || can|can't|do || hardly 是否定意味，附加問句用肯定 can I。 || 我簡直不敢相信，對吧？
heater || n. || 暖氣機 || Turn on the heater. It's cold. || 開暖氣，好冷。 || electric heater=電暖器 || Turn off the heater ___ you go to sleep. || before|after|until || 睡覺「之前」關掉，用 before。 || 睡覺前把暖氣關掉。
helicopter || n. || 直升機 || The helicopter landed on the roof. || 直升機降落在屋頂上。 || by helicopter=搭直升機 || The injured man was taken to the hospital ___ helicopter. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 那名傷者被直升機送往醫院。
hero || n. || 英雄 || He is my hero. || 他是我的英雄。 || national hero=民族英雄 || He became a hero ___ he saved a child from the fire. || because|so|but || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 他成為英雄，因為他從火場救出一個孩子。
highway || n. || 公路 || We drove on the highway. || 我們開上高速公路。 || on the highway=在公路上 || There was a car accident ___ the highway. || on|in|at || 在公路上用 on。 || 公路上發生了一場車禍。
hip || n. || 臀部 || She put her hands on her hips. || 她雙手叉腰。 || hip hop=嘻哈 || My grandma hurt her hip when she ___ down. || fell|falls|has fallen || 描述過去的事用過去式 fell。 || 我奶奶跌倒時傷到了臀部。
hippo || n. || 河馬 || Hippos spend a lot of time in water. || 河馬花很多時間待在水裡。 || baby hippo=小河馬 || Hippos spend a lot of time ___ water. || in|on|at || 在水裡用 in。 || 河馬花很多時間待在水裡。
hire || v. || 雇用 || The company hired ten new workers. || 公司雇用了十名新員工。 || hire sb=雇用某人 || Ten new workers ___ hired last month. || were|was|are || 被動語態；Ten new workers 複數，過去用 were。 || 上個月雇用了十名新員工。
hole || n. || 洞 || There is a hole in my sock. || 我的襪子破了一個洞。 || dig a hole=挖洞 || There is a hole ___ my sock. || in|on|at || 在…裡面（破洞）用 in。 || 我的襪子破了一個洞。
homesick || adj. || 想家的 || She felt homesick when she studied abroad. || 她出國讀書時很想家。 || feel homesick=想家 || She felt homesick ___ she studied abroad. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 她出國讀書時很想家。
honesty || n. || 誠實 || Honesty is the best policy. || 誠實為上策。 || honesty=誠實 || Honesty is ___ best policy. || the|a|an || 最高級前面加 the。 || 誠實為上策。
