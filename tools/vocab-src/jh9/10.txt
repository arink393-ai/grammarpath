# 九年級 10：microwave – owner
microwave || n. || 微波爐 || Heat the food in the microwave. || 用微波爐加熱食物。 || microwave oven=微波爐 || Don't put metal ___ the microwave. || in|on|at || 放進微波爐裡用 in。 || 不要把金屬放進微波爐。
midnight || n. || 午夜 || He studied until midnight. || 他讀書讀到半夜。 || at midnight=在午夜 || He studied ___ midnight. || until|since|for || until = 直到。 || 他讀書讀到半夜。
minor || adj. || 較小的；次要的 || It's just a minor problem. || 這只是個小問題。 || minor mistake=小錯誤 || It's just a minor problem, ___ don't worry. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這只是個小問題，所以別擔心。
minus || prep. || 減 || Ten minus three is seven. || 十減三等於七。 || plus and minus=加減 || Ten minus three ___ seven. || is|are|be || 數學算式視為單數，用 is。 || 十減三等於七。
mirror || n. || 鏡子 || She looked at herself in the mirror. || 她看著鏡子裡的自己。 || look in the mirror=照鏡子 || She looked at ___ in the mirror. || herself|her|she || 主詞與受詞同一人用反身代名詞 herself。 || 她看著鏡子裡的自己。
mix || v. || 混合 || Mix the flour with the eggs. || 把麵粉和蛋混合。 || mix A with B=把 A 和 B 混合 || Mix the flour ___ the milk. || with|to|by || mix A with B = 把 A 和 B 混合。 || 把麵粉和牛奶混合。
model || n. || 模型；模特兒 || He likes to build model planes. || 他喜歡做模型飛機。 || model plane=模型飛機; role model=榜樣 || My sister is a role model ___ me. || for|to|of || a role model for sb = 某人的榜樣。 || 我姊姊是我的榜樣。
monster || n. || 怪物 || The kids are afraid of the monster. || 孩子們怕那個怪物。 || sea monster=海怪 || The movie is about a monster ___ lives in the sea. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a monster）。 || 這部電影是關於一隻住在海裡的怪物。
mosquito || n. || 蚊子 || I was bitten by a mosquito. || 我被蚊子咬了。 || mosquito bite=蚊子咬的包 || I ___ bitten by mosquitoes last night. || was|am|were || 被動語態；過去主詞 I 用 was。 || 我昨晚被蚊子咬了。
motion || n. || 動作；運動 || The car is in motion. || 車子正在移動。 || in motion=在運動中; slow motion=慢動作 || Don't get off the bus ___ it is in motion. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 公車行駛時不要下車。
movement || n. || 動作；運動 || His movements are very quick. || 他的動作很快。 || body movement=肢體動作 || Dancers practice their movements ___ and over again. || over|again|more || over and over again = 一再地。 || 舞者一次又一次地練習動作。
MRT || n. || 捷運 || I take the MRT to school. || 我搭捷運上學。 || take the MRT=搭捷運; MRT station=捷運站 || It takes me thirty minutes ___ get to school by MRT. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 我搭捷運上學要花三十分鐘。
musician || n. || 音樂家 || Mozart was a great musician. || 莫札特是一位偉大的音樂家。 || famous musician=有名的音樂家 || Mozart was one of the ___ musicians in history. || greatest|greater|great || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。 || 莫札特是史上最偉大的音樂家之一。
napkin || n. || 餐巾 || Put the napkin on your lap. || 把餐巾放在你的腿上。 || paper napkin=紙巾 || Could you pass me a napkin, ___? || please|pleased|pleasing || 客氣的請求加 please。 || 可以遞給我一張餐巾嗎？麻煩了。
narrow || adj. || 狹窄的 || The street is very narrow. || 這條街很窄。 || narrow road=窄路 || This street is ___ narrow for two cars. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + for… = 對…來說太…。 || 這條街太窄，容不下兩輛車。
nation || n. || 國家 || The whole nation celebrated. || 全國都在慶祝。 || the whole nation=全國 || The United Nations ___ founded in 1945. || was|is|were || 組織名稱視為單數；被動 was + p.p.。 || 聯合國成立於 1945 年。
natural || adj. || 自然的；天然的 || Natural resources are limited. || 天然資源是有限的。 || natural resources=天然資源 || It's natural ___ feel nervous before an exam. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 考試前感到緊張是很自然的。
naughty || adj. || 頑皮的 || The naughty boy broke the window. || 那個頑皮的男孩打破了窗戶。 || naughty child=調皮的孩子 || The window ___ broken by the naughty boy. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 窗戶被那個頑皮的男孩打破了。
nearly || adv. || 幾乎 || It's nearly ten o'clock. || 快十點了。 || nearly all=幾乎全部 || Nearly all the students ___ the test. || passed|pass|passes || 描述過去的事用過去式 passed。 || 幾乎所有學生都通過了考試。
necessary || adj. || 必要的 || Water is necessary for life. || 水是生命所必需的。 || if necessary=必要的話 || It is necessary ___ you to sleep eight hours. || for|of|to || It is + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 你有必要睡八個小時。
necklace || n. || 項鍊 || She is wearing a gold necklace. || 她戴著一條金項鍊。 || pearl necklace=珍珠項鍊 || This necklace was given to me ___ my grandma. || by|from|with || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這條項鍊是奶奶送給我的。
needle || n. || 針 || She is sewing with a needle. || 她正在用針縫東西。 || needle and thread=針線 || She is sewing a button ___ a needle. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 她正在用針縫鈕扣。
negative || adj. || 負面的；否定的 || Don't be so negative. || 別那麼負面。 || negative thinking=負面思考 || Try not ___ negative. || to be|be|being || try not to + 原形動詞 = 盡量不要…。 || 盡量不要那麼負面。
neither || adj./pron. || 兩者都不 || Neither of them came. || 他們兩個都沒來。 || neither A nor B=既不是 A 也不是 B || Neither Tom nor Amy ___ at home. || is|are|be || neither A nor B，動詞與 B（Amy）一致，用 is。 || 湯姆和艾咪都不在家。
nephew || n. || 姪子；外甥 || My nephew is five years old. || 我的姪子五歲。 || nephew and niece=姪子和姪女 || My nephew is ___ than my niece. || younger|young|youngest || 兩者比較用比較級 younger。 || 我的姪子比我的姪女年紀小。
nervous || adj. || 緊張的 || I'm nervous about the exam. || 我對考試感到緊張。 || be nervous about=對…感到緊張 || I'm nervous ___ the speech contest. || about|of|at with || be nervous about = 對…感到緊張。 || 我對演講比賽感到緊張。
nest || n. || 巢 || The bird built a nest in the tree. || 鳥在樹上築了一個巢。 || bird's nest=鳥巢 || The bird built a nest ___ the tree. || in|on|at || 在樹上（枝葉間）用 in。 || 鳥在樹上築了一個巢。
net || n. || 網 || The fisherman caught fish with a net. || 漁夫用網捕魚。 || fishing net=漁網 || The fish ___ caught in a net. || were|was|is || 被動語態；fish 在此指多條魚，用 were。 || 魚被網子捕到了。
nice-looking || adj. || 好看的 || He is a nice-looking young man. || 他是一位好看的年輕人。 || nice-looking=好看的 || The nice-looking man ___ is standing there is my uncle. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾「人」用 who。 || 站在那裡的那位帥哥是我叔叔。
niece || n. || 姪女；外甥女 || My niece loves drawing. || 我的姪女喜歡畫畫。 || niece=姪女 || My niece loves ___ pictures. || drawing|draw|draws || love 後面可接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我的姪女喜歡畫畫。
nod || v. || 點頭 || He nodded his head. || 他點點頭。 || nod one's head=點頭 || She nodded ___ agreement. || in|with|for || in agreement = 表示同意。 || 她點頭表示同意。
none || pron. || 沒有一個 || None of the students were late. || 沒有一個學生遲到。 || none of=…中沒有一個 || None ___ my friends like horror movies. || of|in|from || none of = …中沒有一個。 || 我的朋友中沒有一個喜歡恐怖片。
nor || conj. || 也不 || Neither he nor I am going. || 他和我都不去。 || neither… nor…=既不…也不… || I don't like coffee, nor ___ I like tea. || do|am|does || nor 放句首要倒裝：nor + 助動詞 + 主詞。 || 我不喜歡咖啡，也不喜歡茶。
novel || n. || 小說 || I'm reading a novel. || 我正在讀一本小說。 || love novel=愛情小說 || This novel ___ written by a famous writer. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這本小說是一位有名的作家寫的。
nut || n. || 堅果 || Squirrels like nuts. || 松鼠喜歡堅果。 || peanut=花生 || Some people are allergic ___ nuts. || to|with|of || be allergic to = 對…過敏。 || 有些人對堅果過敏。
obey || v. || 服從；遵守 || You must obey the rules. || 你必須遵守規則。 || obey the law=守法 || Students must obey the school rules, ___ they will be punished. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 學生必須遵守校規，否則會受處罰。
object || n. || 物體；目標 || What is that object in the sky? || 天空中那個物體是什麼？ || flying object=飛行物體 || There ___ a strange object in the sky last night. || was|were|is || 過去的 There be；a strange object 單數用 was。 || 昨晚天空中有一個奇怪的物體。
ocean || n. || 海洋 || The Pacific Ocean is very big. || 太平洋非常大。 || the Pacific Ocean=太平洋 || The Pacific Ocean is the ___ ocean in the world. || largest|larger|large || 三者以上比較用最高級 the largest。 || 太平洋是世界上最大的海洋。
offer || v./n. || 提供 || He offered me a job. || 他提供我一份工作。 || offer sb sth=提供某人某物 || She offered ___ me with my homework. || to help|helping|help || offer to + 原形動詞 = 主動提出要做…。 || 她主動提出要幫我寫功課。
omit || v. || 省略 || You can omit this sentence. || 你可以省略這個句子。 || omit a word=省略一個字 || This word can ___ omitted. || be|is|been || 助動詞 can 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 這個字可以省略。
oneself || pron. || 自己 || It's important to believe in oneself. || 相信自己很重要。 || by oneself=獨自; enjoy oneself=玩得愉快 || It's important to take care of oneself, ___ it? || isn't|doesn't|is || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || 照顧好自己是很重要的，不是嗎？
onion || n. || 洋蔥 || Cutting onions makes me cry. || 切洋蔥會讓我流淚。 || onion rings=洋蔥圈 || Cutting onions always ___ me cry. || makes|make|making || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，動詞加 s。 || 切洋蔥總是讓我流淚。
operation || n. || 手術；運作 || My grandpa had an operation. || 我爺爺動了手術。 || have an operation=動手術 || My grandpa had ___ operation last week. || an|a|the || operation 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 我爺爺上週動了手術。
opinion || n. || 意見 || In my opinion, he is right. || 依我看，他是對的。 || in my opinion=依我看 || ___ my opinion, reading is more fun than watching TV. || In|On|At || in my opinion = 依我看。 || 依我看，閱讀比看電視有趣。
ordinary || adj. || 普通的 || It was just an ordinary day. || 那只是普通的一天。 || ordinary people=一般人 || It was ___ ordinary day. || an|a|the || ordinary 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 那是普通的一天。
oven || n. || 烤箱 || Bake the cake in the oven. || 把蛋糕放進烤箱烤。 || in the oven=在烤箱裡 || Bake the bread in the oven ___ twenty minutes. || for|in|since || for + 一段時間。 || 把麵包放進烤箱烤二十分鐘。
over-weight || adj. || 過重的 || He is a little over-weight. || 他有點過重。 || be over-weight=體重過重 || He is over-weight ___ he eats too much junk food. || because|so|but || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 他體重過重，因為他吃太多垃圾食物。
overpass || n. || 天橋 || Use the overpass to cross the road. || 走天橋過馬路。 || pedestrian overpass=行人天橋 || Please use the overpass ___ crossing the road. || instead of|because of|in spite || instead of + V-ing = 而不是…。 || 請走天橋，而不是直接穿越馬路。
overseas || adv./adj. || 在海外 || She works overseas. || 她在海外工作。 || study overseas=海外留學 || My cousin has lived overseas ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 我表哥住在海外已經十年了。
owner || n. || 擁有者 || Who is the owner of this car? || 這輛車的車主是誰？ || the owner of=…的擁有者 || The dog followed ___ owner everywhere. || its|it's|their || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 那隻狗到處跟著牠的主人。
