# 九年級 11：ox – prince
ox || n. || 公牛 || The ox is very strong. || 那頭公牛非常強壯。 || oxen=公牛（複數） || In the past, farmers used an ox ___ plow the fields. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 過去農夫用牛來耕田。
pain || n. || 疼痛 || I have a pain in my back. || 我背痛。 || feel pain=感到疼痛; no pain, no gain=不勞則無獲 || No pain, no ___. || gain|gains|gained || 諺語：No pain, no gain.（名詞 gain）。 || 不勞則無獲。
painful || adj. || 疼痛的；痛苦的 || The injury was painful. || 那個傷很痛。 || painful memory=痛苦的回憶 || It was painful ___ say goodbye to my friends. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 和朋友道別是很痛苦的。
painter || n. || 畫家 || Van Gogh was a Dutch painter. || 梵谷是荷蘭畫家。 || famous painter=有名的畫家 || The painting ___ painted by a famous painter. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這幅畫是一位有名的畫家畫的。
pajamas || n. || 睡衣 || He is still in his pajamas. || 他還穿著睡衣。 || a pair of pajamas=一套睡衣 || My pajamas ___ blue and white. || are|is|am || pajamas 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 我的睡衣是藍白色的。
pale || adj. || 蒼白的 || You look pale. Are you sick? || 你臉色蒼白，你生病了嗎？ || look pale=臉色蒼白 || You look pale. You ___ go see a doctor. || should|must not|don't || 給建議用 should + 原形動詞。 || 你臉色蒼白，應該去看醫生。
pan || n. || 平底鍋 || Fry the eggs in a pan. || 用平底鍋煎蛋。 || frying pan=平底鍋 || Heat some oil ___ the pan. || in|on|at of || 在鍋子裡用 in。 || 在平底鍋裡熱一些油。
panda || n. || 貓熊 || Pandas eat bamboo. || 貓熊吃竹子。 || giant panda=大貓熊 || Pandas ___ found only in China. || are|is|be || 被動語態；Pandas 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 貓熊只在中國被發現（野生）。
pardon || n./v. || 原諒；再說一次 || Pardon me? || 你說什麼？（請再說一次） || I beg your pardon=請再說一次 || Pardon me, could you say that ___? || again|ago|back || say that again = 再說一次。 || 不好意思，您可以再說一次嗎？
parking lot || n. || 停車場 || The parking lot is full. || 停車場滿了。 || parking lot=停車場 || There are no empty spaces ___ the parking lot. || in|on|at of || 在停車場裡用 in。 || 停車場裡沒有空位了。
parrot || n. || 鸚鵡 || The parrot can say "hello." || 那隻鸚鵡會說「哈囉」。 || talking parrot=會說話的鸚鵡 || The parrot can ___ "hello" and "goodbye." || say|says|saying || 助動詞 can 後面接原形動詞。 || 那隻鸚鵡會說「哈囉」和「再見」。
partner || n. || 夥伴 || Work with your partner. || 和你的夥伴一起做。 || business partner=事業夥伴 || Please work ___ your partner. || with|to|at || work with sb = 和某人合作。 || 請和你的夥伴一起做。
passenger || n. || 乘客 || The bus has thirty passengers. || 公車上有三十名乘客。 || passenger seat=副駕駛座 || All passengers ___ asked to wear seat belts. || are|is|be || 被動語態；All passengers 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 請所有乘客繫好安全帶。
path || n. || 小路 || Follow the path to the lake. || 沿著小路走到湖邊。 || walk along the path=沿著小徑走 || We walked ___ the path to the lake. || along|across of|through of || walk along = 沿著…走。 || 我們沿著小路走到湖邊。
patient || adj./n. || 有耐心的；病人 || Be patient with children. || 對孩子要有耐心。 || be patient with=對…有耐心 || Be patient ___ your little brother. || with|to|for || be patient with = 對…有耐心。 || 對你弟弟要有耐心。
pattern || n. || 圖案；模式 || I like the pattern on your shirt. || 我喜歡你襯衫上的圖案。 || sentence pattern=句型 || I like the pattern ___ your dress. || on|in|at || 衣服表面上的圖案用 on。 || 我喜歡你洋裝上的圖案。
pause || v./n. || 暫停 || She paused for a moment. || 她停頓了一下。 || pause for a moment=暫停一下 || Let's pause ___ a moment and think. || for|in|at || for a moment = 一會兒。 || 我們暫停一下想一想。
peace || n. || 和平 || Everyone wants peace. || 每個人都想要和平。 || world peace=世界和平; in peace=平靜地 || We all hope ___ world peace. || for|to|of || hope for = 希望得到…。 || 我們都希望世界和平。
peaceful || adj. || 和平的；寧靜的 || The village is quiet and peaceful. || 那個村莊安靜又寧靜。 || peaceful life=寧靜的生活 || The village is ___ peaceful than the city. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 那個村莊比城市寧靜。
pepper || n. || 胡椒 || Add some salt and pepper. || 加點鹽和胡椒。 || salt and pepper=鹽和胡椒; green pepper=青椒 || Add some salt ___ pepper to the soup. || and|or|but || 兩者都加，用 and。 || 在湯裡加一些鹽和胡椒。
perfect || adj. || 完美的 || Nobody is perfect. || 沒有人是完美的。 || perfect score=滿分 || Nobody is perfect, ___ don't be too hard on yourself. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 沒有人是完美的，所以別對自己太嚴苛。
period || n. || 期間；節（課） || We have seven periods a day. || 我們一天有七節課。 || class period=課堂時間 || During that period, he lived ___ Japan. || in|on|at || 住在國家用 in。 || 在那段期間，他住在日本。
personal || adj. || 個人的 || This is my personal opinion. || 這是我個人的意見。 || personal information=個人資料 || Don't share your personal information ___ strangers online. || with|to|for || share sth with sb = 和某人分享某物。 || 不要在網路上和陌生人分享你的個人資料。
physics || n. || 物理學 || Physics is my favorite subject. || 物理是我最喜歡的科目。 || physics class=物理課 || Physics ___ difficult for many students. || is|are|be || physics 是學科名稱，視為單數，用 is。 || 物理對很多學生來說很難。
pigeon || n. || 鴿子 || There are many pigeons in the park. || 公園裡有很多鴿子。 || feed pigeons=餵鴿子 || Please don't feed the pigeons ___ the park. || in|on|at of || 在公園裡用 in。 || 請不要在公園裡餵鴿子。
pile || n. || 一堆 || There is a pile of books on my desk. || 我桌上有一堆書。 || a pile of=一堆… || There is a pile ___ clothes on the bed. || of|with|in || a pile of = 一堆…。 || 床上有一堆衣服。
pillow || n. || 枕頭 || This pillow is soft. || 這個枕頭很軟。 || pillow fight=枕頭大戰 || This pillow is ___ than that one. || softer|soft|softest || 兩者比較用比較級 softer。 || 這個枕頭比那個軟。
pineapple || n. || 鳳梨 || Pineapple cakes are famous in Taiwan. || 鳳梨酥在台灣很有名。 || pineapple cake=鳳梨酥 || Pineapple cakes are famous ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 鳳梨酥在台灣很有名。
plain || adj. || 樸素的；簡單的 || She wears plain clothes. || 她穿著樸素。 || plain water=白開水 || I'd like a glass of plain water, ___ ice. || with no|no with|without no || with no ice = 不加冰。 || 我要一杯白開水，不加冰。
platform || n. || 月台 || The train leaves from Platform 3. || 火車從三號月台出發。 || Platform 3=第三月台 || Please wait for the train ___ Platform 2. || on|in|at of || 在月台上用 on。 || 請在第二月台候車。
pleasant || adj. || 令人愉快的 || We had a pleasant trip. || 我們有一趟愉快的旅程。 || pleasant weather=宜人的天氣 || The weather is ___ pleasant today. || very|much|many || 形容詞前面加 very 表示「很」。 || 今天天氣很宜人。
pleased || adj. || 高興的 || I'm pleased to meet you. || 很高興認識你。 || be pleased with=對…感到滿意 || The teacher was pleased ___ our work. || with|of|at to || be pleased with = 對…感到滿意。 || 老師對我們的作品很滿意。
plus || prep. || 加 || Two plus three is five. || 二加三等於五。 || plus=加 || Two plus three ___ five. || is|are|be || 數學算式視為單數，用 is。 || 二加三等於五。
poem || n. || 詩 || She wrote a poem about the sea. || 她寫了一首關於大海的詩。 || write a poem=寫詩 || This poem ___ written by Li Bai. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這首詩是李白寫的。
poison || n. || 毒藥 || Some mushrooms contain poison. || 有些蘑菇含有毒素。 || rat poison=老鼠藥 || Keep the poison away ___ children. || from|of|to || keep A away from B = 讓 A 遠離 B。 || 讓毒藥遠離兒童。
pollute || v. || 污染 || Factories pollute the river. || 工廠污染了河川。 || pollute the air=污染空氣 || The river ___ polluted by the factory. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The river 用 is + p.p.。 || 這條河被工廠污染了。
pollution || n. || 污染 || Air pollution is a big problem. || 空氣污染是個大問題。 || air pollution=空氣污染 || Air pollution is ___ serious problem in many cities. || a|an|much || serious 以子音開頭，可數單數名詞 problem 前用 a。 || 空氣污染在很多城市是個嚴重的問題。
pop music || n. || 流行音樂 || Teenagers like pop music. || 青少年喜歡流行音樂。 || listen to pop music=聽流行音樂 || Pop music is popular ___ teenagers. || with|to|for || be popular with = 受…歡迎。 || 流行音樂很受青少年歡迎。
population || n. || 人口 || Taiwan has a population of 23 million. || 台灣有兩千三百萬人口。 || large population=人口眾多 || What is the population ___ Taiwan? || of|in|for || the population of = …的人口。 || 台灣的人口是多少？
position || n. || 位置；職位 || What position do you play? || 你打什麼位置？ || in position=在適當位置 || He applied ___ the position of manager. || for|to|with || apply for = 申請…。 || 他應徵了經理的職位。
positive || adj. || 正面的；積極的 || Stay positive! || 保持正面！ || positive attitude=積極的態度 || A positive attitude can help you ___. || succeed|success|succeeding || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 積極的態度可以幫助你成功。
potato || n. || 馬鈴薯 || I like mashed potatoes. || 我喜歡馬鈴薯泥。 || sweet potato=地瓜; mashed potatoes=馬鈴薯泥 || Sweet potatoes ___ good for your health. || are|is|be || Sweet potatoes 複數，用 are。 || 地瓜對健康有益。
powder || n. || 粉 || Add some milk powder. || 加一些奶粉。 || milk powder=奶粉 || Mix the powder ___ water. || with|to|by || mix A with B = 把 A 和 B 混合。 || 把粉和水混合。
praise || v./n. || 稱讚 || The teacher praised her. || 老師稱讚了她。 || praise sb for sth=因某事稱讚某人 || The teacher praised her ___ her hard work. || for|of|to || praise sb for sth = 因某事稱讚某人。 || 老師稱讚她很努力。
precious || adj. || 珍貴的 || Time is precious. || 時間是寶貴的。 || precious memories=珍貴的回憶 || Time is more precious ___ money. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 時間比金錢更寶貴。
president || n. || 總統；總裁 || The president gave a speech. || 總統發表了演講。 || vice president=副總統 || The president ___ elected four years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 總統是四年前選出來的。
pressure || n. || 壓力 || Students are under a lot of pressure. || 學生承受很大的壓力。 || under pressure=在壓力下 || Many students are ___ pressure before exams. || under|below|with || under pressure = 在壓力之下。 || 很多學生在考試前承受壓力。
priest || n. || 牧師；神父 || The priest is kind. || 那位神父很親切。 || Catholic priest=天主教神父 || The priest ___ is talking to the children is very kind. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾「人」用 who。 || 正在和孩子們說話的那位神父非常親切。
primary || adj. || 主要的；初級的 || The primary goal is to learn. || 主要目標是學習。 || primary school=小學 || My little sister goes ___ primary school. || to|at|in || go to school = 上學。 || 我妹妹讀小學。
prince || n. || 王子 || The prince married Cinderella. || 王子和灰姑娘結婚了。 || Prince Charming=白馬王子 || The prince fell ___ love with Cinderella. || in|on|at || fall in love with = 愛上…。 || 王子愛上了灰姑娘。
