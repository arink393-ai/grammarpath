# 九年級 04：control – diamond
control || v./n. || 控制 || You need to control your anger. || 你需要控制你的怒氣。 || out of control=失控; control oneself=克制自己 || The fire was out ___ control. || of|from|in || out of control = 失控。 || 火勢失控了。
convenience store || n. || 便利商店 || There is a convenience store on every corner. || 每個街角都有便利商店。 || convenience store=便利商店 || You can pay your bills ___ a convenience store. || at|on|to || 在某個地點（店）用 at。 || 你可以在便利商店繳帳單。
conversation || n. || 對話 || We had a long conversation. || 我們聊了很久。 || have a conversation=交談 || I had a conversation ___ a foreigner in English. || with|to|for || have a conversation with sb = 和某人交談。 || 我用英文和一位外國人交談。
corn || n. || 玉米 || I like corn soup. || 我喜歡玉米湯。 || corn soup=玉米濃湯 || Corn ___ grown in many parts of Taiwan. || is|are|be || 被動語態；corn 不可數用 is + p.p.。 || 台灣許多地方都種玉米。
cotton || n. || 棉花 || This shirt is made of cotton. || 這件襯衫是棉做的。 || cotton shirt=棉衫 || This T-shirt is made ___ cotton. || of|at|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這件 T 恤是棉做的。
cough || v./n. || 咳嗽 || He has a bad cough. || 他咳得很嚴重。 || have a cough=咳嗽 || He has been coughing ___ three days. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成進行式。 || 他已經咳了三天。
couple || n. || 一對；夫妻 || They are a happy couple. || 他們是一對幸福的夫妻。 || a couple of=幾個; married couple=夫妻 || I'll be back in a couple ___ minutes. || of|for|with || a couple of = 幾個。 || 我幾分鐘後回來。
courage || n. || 勇氣 || It takes courage to say sorry. || 說抱歉需要勇氣。 || have the courage to V=有勇氣做… || She had the courage ___ the truth. || to tell|telling|tell || have the courage to + 原形動詞。 || 她有勇氣說出真相。
court || n. || 球場；法院 || We play basketball on the court. || 我們在球場上打籃球。 || basketball court=籃球場 || The students are playing basketball ___ the court. || on|in|at || 在球場上用 on the court。 || 學生們正在球場上打籃球。
cowboy || n. || 牛仔 || The cowboy rode a horse. || 牛仔騎著一匹馬。 || cowboy hat=牛仔帽 || The cowboy ___ a big hat is my uncle. || with|of|on || with + 外表特徵 = 戴著…的。 || 戴著大帽子的那個牛仔是我叔叔。
crab || n. || 螃蟹 || Crabs walk sideways. || 螃蟹橫著走。 || crab meat=蟹肉 || Crabs ___ sideways. || walk|walks|walking || Crabs 複數，動詞用原形。 || 螃蟹橫著走。
crayon || n. || 蠟筆 || The kids are drawing with crayons. || 孩子們在用蠟筆畫畫。 || a box of crayons=一盒蠟筆 || The kids are drawing ___ crayons. || with|by|at || 用某個工具用 with。 || 孩子們正在用蠟筆畫畫。
cream || n. || 奶油；乳霜 || Would you like cream in your coffee? || 你的咖啡要加奶油嗎？ || ice cream=冰淇淋; sun cream=防曬乳 || Put some sun cream on ___ you go to the beach. || before|after|until || 去海邊「之前」擦防曬，用 before。 || 去海邊之前擦點防曬乳。
create || v. || 創造 || The artist created a beautiful painting. || 那位藝術家創作了一幅美麗的畫。 || create a new style=創造新風格 || This app ___ created by a high school student. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個 app 是一位高中生做的。
credit card || n. || 信用卡 || Can I pay by credit card? || 我可以刷卡嗎？ || pay by credit card=刷卡 || Can I pay ___ credit card? || by|with|in || pay by credit card = 用信用卡付款。 || 我可以用信用卡付款嗎？
crime || n. || 犯罪 || Stealing is a crime. || 偷竊是犯罪。 || commit a crime=犯罪 || The police are trying to stop crime ___ this area. || in|on|at || 在某個地區用 in。 || 警方正努力遏止這個地區的犯罪。
crowd || n. || 人群 || There was a big crowd at the concert. || 演唱會上人山人海。 || a crowd of=一群… || The singer was surrounded ___ a crowd of fans. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 那位歌手被一群粉絲團團圍住。
crowded || adj. || 擁擠的 || The MRT is very crowded in the morning. || 早上捷運非常擁擠。 || be crowded with=擠滿… || The night market was crowded ___ people. || with|of|by || be crowded with = 擠滿…。 || 夜市擠滿了人。
cruel || adj. || 殘忍的 || It's cruel to hurt animals. || 傷害動物是殘忍的。 || be cruel to=對…殘忍 || Don't be cruel ___ animals. || to|for|with || be cruel to = 對…殘忍。 || 不要殘忍對待動物。
culture || n. || 文化 || I'm interested in Japanese culture. || 我對日本文化有興趣。 || Taiwanese culture=台灣文化 || Food is an important part ___ culture. || of|in|for || part of = …的一部分。 || 食物是文化中重要的一部分。
cure || v./n. || 治癒；療法 || The doctor cured my illness. || 醫生治好了我的病。 || cure for=…的療法 || There is no cure ___ the common cold. || for|of|to || a cure for = …的療法。 || 一般感冒沒有特效藥。
curious || adj. || 好奇的 || Children are curious about everything. || 小孩對每件事都很好奇。 || be curious about=對…好奇 || Children are curious ___ everything. || about|of|at || be curious about = 對…好奇。 || 小孩對每件事都很好奇。
current || adj. || 目前的 || What is your current address? || 你目前的地址是什麼？ || current events=時事 || We talked about current events ___ class. || in|on|at || in class = 上課時。 || 我們在課堂上討論時事。
curtain || n. || 窗簾 || Please close the curtains. || 請拉上窗簾。 || close the curtains=拉上窗簾 || Open the curtains ___ let the sunshine in. || and|but|or || 連接兩個動作用 and。 || 拉開窗簾，讓陽光照進來。
curve || n. || 曲線；彎道 || Drive slowly on the curve. || 在彎道上要開慢一點。 || sharp curve=急轉彎 || There is a sharp curve ahead, so you should drive ___. || slowly|slow|slower than || 修飾動詞 drive 用副詞 slowly。 || 前面有急轉彎，所以你應該開慢一點。
custom || n. || 習俗 || It's a custom to eat mooncakes on Moon Festival. || 中秋節吃月餅是一種習俗。 || local custom=當地習俗 || Every country has ___ own customs. || its|it's|their own || country 單數用 its。 || 每個國家都有自己的習俗。
customer || n. || 顧客 || The customer is always right. || 顧客永遠是對的。 || regular customer=常客 || The store was full ___ customers. || of|to|by || be full of = 充滿…。 || 店裡擠滿了顧客。
daily || adj./adv. || 每日的 || Exercise is part of my daily life. || 運動是我日常生活的一部分。 || daily life=日常生活 || Exercise has become part ___ my daily life. || of|for|in || part of = …的一部分。 || 運動已成為我日常生活的一部分。
damage || v./n. || 損害 || The typhoon damaged many houses. || 颱風損壞了很多房子。 || cause damage=造成損害 || Many houses ___ damaged by the typhoon. || were|was|are || 被動語態；Many houses 複數，過去用 were。 || 很多房子被颱風損壞了。
danger || n. || 危險 || The sign means "Danger!" || 這個標誌的意思是「危險！」 || in danger=處於危險中; out of danger=脫離險境 || Many animals are ___ danger because of pollution. || in|at|on || in danger = 處於危險中。 || 很多動物因為污染而處於危險中。
dawn || n. || 黎明 || We got up at dawn. || 我們天一亮就起床了。 || at dawn=黎明時 || The farmers start working ___ dawn. || at|in|on || at dawn = 在黎明時。 || 農夫們天一亮就開始工作。
deaf || adj. || 聾的 || The old man is a little deaf. || 那位老先生有點重聽。 || go deaf=變聾 || Speak louder. My grandpa is a little deaf, ___ he? || isn't|doesn't|is || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || 說大聲點，我爺爺有點重聽，不是嗎？
debate || n./v. || 辯論 || We had a debate in English class. || 我們在英文課上辯論。 || debate about=就…辯論 || The students debated ___ whether homework should be banned. || about|of|to || debate about = 就…辯論。 || 學生們辯論是否應該取消作業。
decision || n. || 決定 || It's a difficult decision. || 這是一個困難的決定。 || make a decision=做決定 || You have to make a decision ___ Friday. || by|until|since || by + 時間 = 在…之前（截止）。 || 你必須在星期五之前做決定。
decorate || v. || 裝飾 || We decorated the tree with lights. || 我們用燈裝飾樹。 || decorate A with B=用 B 裝飾 A || We decorated the classroom ___ balloons. || with|by|of || decorate A with B = 用 B 裝飾 A。 || 我們用氣球佈置教室。
decrease || v./n. || 減少 || The number of students is decreasing. || 學生人數正在減少。 || decrease by=減少了… || The number of babies ___ decreased in recent years. || has|have|is || The number of… 視為單數，用 has + p.p.。 || 近年來嬰兒數量減少了。
deer || n. || 鹿 || There are many deer in the park. || 公園裡有很多鹿。 || a herd of deer=一群鹿 || There ___ many deer in Nara Park. || are|is|be || deer 單複數同形；many deer 是複數，用 are。 || 奈良公園裡有很多鹿。
degree || n. || 度；學位 || It's 30 degrees today. || 今天三十度。 || degrees Celsius=攝氏度; college degree=大學學位 || The temperature dropped ___ five degrees last night. || by|in|with || drop by + 數量 = 下降了…。 || 昨晚氣溫下降了五度。
deliver || v. || 遞送 || The mailman delivers letters every day. || 郵差每天送信。 || deliver a package=送包裹 || The package ___ delivered yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 包裹昨天送達了。
department || n. || 部門；系 || She works in the sales department. || 她在業務部工作。 || department store=百貨公司 || My sister studies in the English department ___ a university. || of|to|for || the department of… = …的系。 || 我姊姊在一所大學的英文系讀書。
depend || v. || 依靠；視…而定 || It depends on the weather. || 那要看天氣而定。 || depend on=依靠、視…而定 || You can depend ___ me. || on|in|at || depend on = 依靠…。 || 你可以依靠我。
describe || v. || 描述 || Can you describe the man? || 你能描述那個人嗎？ || describe sth to sb=向某人描述某事 || Can you describe ___ the man looked like? || what|how|which || describe what sb looked like = 描述某人長什麼樣子。 || 你能描述那個人長什麼樣子嗎？
desert || n. || 沙漠 || Camels live in the desert. || 駱駝住在沙漠裡。 || the Sahara Desert=撒哈拉沙漠 || It is very hot and dry ___ the desert. || in|on|at || 在沙漠裡用 in。 || 沙漠裡又熱又乾。
design || v./n. || 設計 || She designed this dress. || 這件洋裝是她設計的。 || fashion design=時裝設計 || This building ___ designed by a famous architect. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這棟建築是由一位知名建築師設計的。
desire || n./v. || 渴望 || She has a strong desire to succeed. || 她有強烈的成功渴望。 || desire to V=渴望做… || He has a strong desire ___ a doctor. || to become|becoming|become || desire to + 原形動詞。 || 他非常渴望成為醫生。
dessert || n. || 甜點 || What's for dessert? || 甜點是什麼？ || for dessert=作為甜點 || We had ice cream ___ dessert. || for|as for|with || for dessert = 作為甜點。 || 我們吃冰淇淋當甜點。
detect || v. || 偵測；察覺 || The machine can detect smoke. || 這台機器能偵測煙霧。 || detect a problem=發現問題 || The smoke ___ detected by the alarm. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 警報器偵測到了煙霧。
develop || v. || 發展 || Taiwan has developed quickly. || 台灣發展得很快。 || develop a habit=養成習慣; developing country=開發中國家 || Reading helps you develop ___ imagination. || your|you|yours || 名詞 imagination 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 閱讀幫助你培養想像力。
dial || v. || 撥（號） || Dial 119 in an emergency. || 緊急時撥 119。 || dial a number=撥號 || Dial 119 ___ you see a fire. || if|so|but || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果看到火災就撥 119。
diamond || n. || 鑽石 || She has a diamond ring. || 她有一枚鑽石戒指。 || diamond ring=鑽戒 || Diamonds are ___ than gold. || more expensive|expensiver|most expensive || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 鑽石比黃金還貴。
