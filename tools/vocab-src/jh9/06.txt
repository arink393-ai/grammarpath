# 九年級 06：energetic – forward
energetic || adj. || 精力充沛的 || Kids are always energetic. || 小孩總是精力充沛。 || energetic kids=活力十足的孩子 || She is ___ energetic that she never gets tired. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 她精力充沛到從不覺得累。
energy || n. || 能量；精力 || Turn off the lights to save energy. || 關燈來節省能源。 || save energy=節省能源; full of energy=精力充沛 || Solar energy ___ used to make electricity. || is|are|be || 被動語態；energy 不可數，用 is + p.p.。 || 太陽能被用來發電。
engine || n. || 引擎 || The car's engine is broken. || 這台車的引擎壞了。 || start the engine=發動引擎 || The engine ___ fixed by a mechanic yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 引擎昨天被一位技師修好了。
entrance || n. || 入口 || Meet me at the entrance. || 在入口跟我碰面。 || main entrance=大門; entrance exam=入學考試 || Let's meet ___ the entrance of the museum. || at|on|in of || 在入口（地點）用 at。 || 我們在博物館入口碰面吧。
environment || n. || 環境 || We should protect the environment. || 我們應該保護環境。 || protect the environment=保護環境 || Everyone should ___ the environment. || protect|protects|protecting || 助動詞 should 後面接原形動詞。 || 每個人都應該保護環境。
envy || v./n. || 羨慕 || I envy your long holiday. || 我羨慕你有長假。 || with envy=羨慕地 || I envy you ___ you have such a long holiday. || because|so|but || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 我羨慕你，因為你有這麼長的假期。
equal || adj. || 相等的；平等的 || All people are equal. || 人人平等。 || be equal to=等於… || One kilometer is equal ___ 1,000 meters. || to|with|for || be equal to = 等於…。 || 一公里等於一千公尺。
especially || adv. || 特別地 || I love fruit, especially mangoes. || 我愛水果，尤其是芒果。 || especially=尤其 || It's very hot here, especially ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 這裡很熱，尤其是夏天。
event || n. || 事件；活動 || The school sports day is a big event. || 學校運動會是一件大事。 || sports event=運動賽事 || The event will be held ___ May 5. || on|in|at || 特定日期前面用 on。 || 這個活動將在五月五日舉行。
everywhere || adv. || 到處 || I looked everywhere for my key. || 我到處找我的鑰匙。 || everywhere=到處 || I have looked everywhere ___ my phone. || for|at|to || look for = 尋找。 || 我到處都找過我的手機了。
evil || adj. || 邪惡的 || The evil queen wanted to hurt Snow White. || 邪惡的皇后想傷害白雪公主。 || evil spirit=惡靈 || In the story, the evil queen ___ jealous of Snow White. || was|were|is being || 過去的故事用過去式；the queen 單數用 was。 || 故事裡，邪惡的皇后嫉妒白雪公主。
exam || n. || 考試 || I have an exam tomorrow. || 我明天有考試。 || take an exam=參加考試; pass an exam=考試及格 || I have to study ___ the final exam. || for|to|at || study for = 為…而讀書。 || 我必須為期末考念書。
excite || v. || 使興奮 || The news excited everyone. || 這個消息讓大家很興奮。 || excite sb=使某人興奮 || The good news excited us, ___ we started to cheer. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 好消息讓我們很興奮，所以我們開始歡呼。
exist || v. || 存在 || Do ghosts exist? || 鬼真的存在嗎？ || exist on=靠…生存 || Do you think ghosts really exist? I don't think ___. || so|it|that || I don't think so. = 我不這麼認為。 || 你認為鬼真的存在嗎？我不這麼認為。
exit || n. || 出口 || Where is the exit? || 出口在哪裡？ || emergency exit=緊急出口 || Take Exit 2 ___ you want to go to the museum. || if|so|but || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你要去博物館就走二號出口。
express || v. || 表達 || It's hard to express my feelings. || 很難表達我的感受。 || express oneself=表達自己 || Music helps people express ___ feelings. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 feelings 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 音樂幫助人們表達他們的感受。
extra || adj. || 額外的 || Do you have an extra pen? || 你有多的筆嗎？ || extra money=額外的錢 || Do you have ___ extra pen? || an|a|the || extra 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 你有多的筆嗎？
fair || adj. || 公平的 || That's not fair! || 那不公平！ || be fair to=對…公平 || The teacher is fair ___ all students. || to|for|with at || be fair to = 對…公平。 || 老師對所有學生都很公平。
false || adj. || 錯誤的；假的 || Is the sentence true or false? || 這個句子是對還是錯？ || true or false=是非題 || Is this sentence true ___ false? || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 這個句子是對還是錯？
fancy || adj. || 花俏的；高檔的 || We ate at a fancy restaurant. || 我們在一家高級餐廳吃飯。 || fancy dress=化妝服 || This restaurant is ___ fancy for us. || too|to|so that || too + 形容詞 = 太…。 || 這家餐廳對我們來說太高檔了。
fantastic || adj. || 極好的 || The show was fantastic! || 這場表演太棒了！ || fantastic idea=好點子 || We had a fantastic time ___ the beach. || at|in|on of || at the beach = 在海邊。 || 我們在海邊玩得非常開心。
fashionable || adj. || 時髦的 || She always wears fashionable clothes. || 她總是穿時髦的衣服。 || fashionable clothes=時髦的衣服 || Her clothes are ___ fashionable than mine. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 她的衣服比我的時髦。
faucet || n. || 水龍頭 || Turn off the faucet. || 關上水龍頭。 || turn on the faucet=打開水龍頭 || Turn off the faucet ___ you brush your teeth. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 刷牙時要關上水龍頭。
fault || n. || 過錯 || It's not your fault. || 那不是你的錯。 || it's one's fault=是某人的錯 || It's not ___ fault. Don't blame me. || my|me|mine || 名詞 fault 前用所有格形容詞 my。 || 那不是我的錯，別怪我。
fear || n. || 恐懼 || She has a fear of heights. || 她有懼高症。 || fear of=害怕…; in fear=害怕地 || He has a fear ___ dogs. || of|for|about || a fear of = 對…的恐懼。 || 他怕狗。
fee || n. || 費用 || The entrance fee is 100 dollars. || 入場費一百元。 || entrance fee=入場費; school fees=學費 || The entrance fee ___ 100 dollars for students. || is|are|has || The entrance fee 單數，用 is。 || 學生的入場費是一百元。
feeling || n. || 感覺；感情 || I know how you feel. I have the same feeling. || 我懂你的感受，我也有同感。 || hurt one's feelings=傷害某人的感情 || I have a feeling ___ it will rain today. || that|what|which || a feeling that… = 有…的預感；that 引導同位語子句。 || 我有預感今天會下雨。
female || adj./n. || 女性（的） || Most of the nurses are female. || 大部分的護理師是女性。 || female student=女學生 || There are more female ___ than male students in our class. || students|student|studying || more + 複數名詞 + than。 || 我們班的女學生比男學生多。
fence || n. || 籬笆；圍欄 || The dog jumped over the fence. || 狗跳過了圍欄。 || over the fence=越過圍欄 || The dog jumped ___ the fence. || over|under|through || jump over = 跳過（上方）。 || 狗跳過了圍欄。
film || n. || 電影；底片 || Have you seen this film? || 你看過這部電影嗎？ || watch a film=看電影 || Have you ___ this film? || seen|saw|see || 現在完成式 have + p.p.（see 的 p.p. 是 seen）。 || 你看過這部電影嗎？
final || adj./n. || 最後的；決賽 || This is the final question. || 這是最後一題。 || final exam=期末考; finals=決賽 || Our team made it ___ the finals. || to|at|in || make it to = 進入、到達。 || 我們隊打進了決賽。
fit || v./adj. || 適合；健康的 || These shoes don't fit me. || 這雙鞋不合我的腳。 || keep fit=保持健康 || I exercise every day ___ keep fit. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我每天運動以保持健康。
flag || n. || 旗子 || The flag is red, blue and white. || 旗子是紅、藍、白色的。 || national flag=國旗 || The national flag ___ raised every morning at school. || is|are|be || 被動語態；習慣性用現在式 is + p.p.。 || 學校每天早上升國旗。
flashlight || n. || 手電筒 || Bring a flashlight when you go camping. || 去露營時帶一支手電筒。 || turn on a flashlight=打開手電筒 || You'll need a flashlight ___ it gets dark. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 天黑時你會需要手電筒。
flat tire || n. || 爆胎 || My bike has a flat tire. || 我的腳踏車爆胎了。 || get a flat tire=爆胎 || We were late because we ___ a flat tire. || had|have|has || 過去的事用過去式 had。 || 我們遲到了，因為爆胎。
flight || n. || 班機；飛行 || Our flight leaves at nine. || 我們的班機九點起飛。 || direct flight=直飛班機 || Our flight ___ delayed because of the storm. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我們的班機因暴風雨延誤了。
flour || n. || 麵粉 || Bread is made from flour. || 麵包是用麵粉做的。 || a bag of flour=一袋麵粉 || Bread is made ___ flour. || from|of|by || be made from = 由…製成（看不出原料）。 || 麵包是用麵粉做的。
flu || n. || 流行性感冒 || I caught the flu last week. || 我上週得了流感。 || catch the flu=得流感; flu shot=流感疫苗 || He has been sick with the flu ___ Monday. || since|for|from || since + 過去時間點，搭配現在完成式。 || 他從星期一起就得了流感。
flute || n. || 長笛 || She plays the flute in the band. || 她在樂團吹長笛。 || play the flute=吹長笛 || She has played ___ flute for three years. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 她吹長笛已經三年了。
focus || v./n. || 專注；焦點 || Focus on your work. || 專心工作。 || focus on=專注於… || Please focus ___ your studies. || on|in|at || focus on = 專注於…。 || 請專心在學業上。
fog || n. || 霧 || The fog was so thick that we couldn't see. || 霧濃到我們看不見。 || thick fog=濃霧 || The fog was ___ thick that the flights were canceled. || so|such|very || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 霧濃到班機都取消了。
foggy || adj. || 有霧的 || It's foggy this morning. || 今天早上有霧。 || foggy day=霧天 || It was foggy, ___ the driver drove slowly. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 起霧了，所以司機開得很慢。
foolish || adj. || 愚蠢的 || It was foolish of me to believe him. || 我相信他真是太傻了。 || foolish mistake=愚蠢的錯誤 || It was foolish ___ me to believe him. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 我相信他真是太傻了。
football || n. || 足球；美式足球 || Football is popular in Europe. || 足球在歐洲很受歡迎。 || play football=踢足球 || Football is the ___ popular sport in Europe. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 足球是歐洲最受歡迎的運動。
forest || n. || 森林 || Many animals live in the forest. || 很多動物住在森林裡。 || rain forest=雨林 || Many trees in the forest ___ cut down every year. || are|is|be || 被動語態；Many trees 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 森林裡每年都有很多樹被砍伐。
forgive || v. || 原諒 || Please forgive me. || 請原諒我。 || forgive sb for sth=原諒某人做了某事 || Please forgive me ___ being late. || for|of|to || forgive sb for + V-ing = 原諒某人做了某事。 || 請原諒我遲到。
form || n./v. || 表格；形式；形成 || Please fill out this form. || 請填寫這張表格。 || fill out a form=填表 || Please fill ___ this form. || out|up|off || fill out = 填寫（表格）。 || 請填寫這張表格。
formal || adj. || 正式的 || Wear formal clothes to the party. || 穿正式服裝去參加派對。 || formal dress=正式服裝 || You should wear formal clothes ___ a job interview. || for|to|at of || for + 場合 = 為了…。 || 面試時你應該穿正式服裝。
former || adj. || 以前的；前者 || He is a former teacher. || 他以前是老師。 || former president=前總統 || The former president ___ visiting our school tomorrow. || is|are|was || 現在進行式表示已安排的未來；主詞單數用 is。 || 前總統明天要來訪我們學校。
forward || adv. || 向前 || Please step forward. || 請向前一步。 || look forward to=期待… || I'm looking forward ___ seeing you. || to|for|at || look forward to + V-ing = 期待…。 || 我很期待見到你。
