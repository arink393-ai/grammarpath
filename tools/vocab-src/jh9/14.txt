# 九年級 14：slender – survive
slender || adj. || 苗條的 || She is tall and slender. || 她又高又苗條。 || slender figure=苗條的身材 || The model is ___ slender that she looks like a stick. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那位模特兒瘦到像根竹竿。
slice || n. || 片 || Can I have a slice of pizza? || 我可以吃一片披薩嗎？ || a slice of=一片… || Can I have a slice ___ bread? || of|for|in || a slice of = 一片…。 || 我可以吃一片麵包嗎？
slippers || n. || 拖鞋 || Put on your slippers. || 穿上你的拖鞋。 || a pair of slippers=一雙拖鞋 || My slippers ___ under the bed. || are|is|be || slippers 複數，用 are。 || 我的拖鞋在床底下。
snail || n. || 蝸牛 || The snail moves slowly. || 蝸牛移動得很慢。 || at a snail's pace=慢吞吞地 || A snail moves ___ slowly than a turtle. || more|most|much || 副詞 slowly 的比較級用 more slowly。 || 蝸牛移動得比烏龜還慢。
sneakers || n. || 運動鞋 || I wear sneakers to school. || 我穿運動鞋上學。 || a pair of sneakers=一雙運動鞋 || These sneakers ___ very comfortable. || are|is|be || sneakers 複數，用 are。 || 這雙運動鞋非常舒服。
sneaky || adj. || 鬼鬼祟祟的 || The cat is sneaky. || 那隻貓鬼鬼祟祟的。 || sneaky smile=奸笑 || The sneaky fox tried ___ the chickens. || to steal|stealing|steal || try to + 原形動詞。 || 那隻狡猾的狐狸試圖偷雞。
snowman || n. || 雪人 || The kids made a snowman. || 孩子們堆了一個雪人。 || make a snowman=堆雪人 || The kids made a snowman ___ the snow. || in|on|at || 在雪中用 in the snow。 || 孩子們在雪地裡堆了一個雪人。
soap || n. || 肥皂 || Wash your hands with soap. || 用肥皂洗手。 || a bar of soap=一塊肥皂 || Wash your hands ___ soap and water. || with|by|in || 用某物用 with。 || 用肥皂和水洗手。
social || adj. || 社會的；社交的 || I use social media every day. || 我每天用社群媒體。 || social media=社群媒體 || Don't spend too much time ___ social media. || on|in|at || spend time on + 名詞。 || 別花太多時間在社群媒體上。
society || n. || 社會 || We live in a modern society. || 我們生活在現代社會。 || modern society=現代社會 || Everyone ___ a role to play in society. || has|have|having || everyone 視為單數，用 has。 || 每個人在社會中都有要扮演的角色。
soda || n. || 汽水 || I'd like a can of soda. || 我要一罐汽水。 || a can of soda=一罐汽水 || Drinking too much soda ___ bad for your teeth. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 喝太多汽水對牙齒不好。
soft drink || n. || 清涼飲料 || Soft drinks have a lot of sugar. || 清涼飲料含有很多糖。 || soft drink=汽水、清涼飲料 || Soft drinks contain a lot ___ sugar. || of|with|for || a lot of = 很多的。 || 清涼飲料含有很多糖。
softball || n. || 壘球 || We play softball in PE class. || 我們在體育課打壘球。 || play softball=打壘球 || Softball is ___ to baseball. || similar|same|like || be similar to = 和…相似。 || 壘球和棒球很像。
solve || v. || 解決 || Can you solve this problem? || 你能解決這個問題嗎？ || solve a problem=解決問題 || The problem ___ solved by the team. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個問題被團隊解決了。
soul || n. || 靈魂 || Music is food for the soul. || 音樂是靈魂的食糧。 || body and soul=全心全意 || Music is food ___ the soul. || for|of|to || food for… = …的食糧。 || 音樂是靈魂的食糧。
sour || adj. || 酸的 || The lemon tastes sour. || 檸檬嚐起來很酸。 || sweet and sour=酸甜 || The lemons are ___ sour to eat. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而無法…。 || 檸檬太酸了，沒辦法吃。
soy-sauce || n. || 醬油 || Add some soy-sauce to the noodles. || 在麵裡加一點醬油。 || soy sauce=醬油 || Don't put too ___ soy-sauce in the soup. || much|many|few || soy-sauce 不可數，用 too much。 || 湯裡別加太多醬油。
spaghetti || n. || 義大利麵 || I had spaghetti for dinner. || 我晚餐吃義大利麵。 || spaghetti with meatballs=肉丸義大利麵 || I had spaghetti ___ dinner. || for|at|in || 「晚餐吃…」用 for dinner。 || 我晚餐吃義大利麵。
speaker || n. || 演講者；喇叭 || The speaker talked about the environment. || 演講者談到環境議題。 || native speaker=母語人士 || The speaker ___ is on stage is a famous writer. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾「人」用 who。 || 台上的那位演講者是一位知名作家。
speech || n. || 演講 || She gave a speech in English. || 她用英文發表了演講。 || give a speech=發表演講; speech contest=演講比賽 || She won first prize ___ the speech contest. || in|on|at of || win a prize in + 比賽。 || 她在演講比賽中得到第一名。
speed || n. || 速度 || The car was going at high speed. || 那輛車以高速行駛。 || at high speed=高速地; speed limit=速限 || The car was going ___ high speed. || at|in|with || at high speed = 高速地。 || 那輛車以高速行駛。
spirit || n. || 精神 || The team has a great spirit. || 這支隊伍很有團隊精神。 || team spirit=團隊精神 || We need team spirit ___ win the game. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們需要團隊精神來贏得比賽。
spot || n./v. || 地點；斑點；發現 || This is a good spot for a picnic. || 這是野餐的好地點。 || on the spot=當場; tourist spot=觀光景點 || This is a popular tourist spot ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 這是台灣一個熱門的觀光景點。
spread || v. || 散布；傳播 || The news spread quickly. || 消息傳得很快。 || spread news=散播消息 || The flu is spreading ___ in the school. || quickly|quick|quicker than || 修飾動詞用副詞 quickly。 || 流感在學校裡迅速傳播。
state || n. || 州；狀態 || California is a state in the U.S. || 加州是美國的一個州。 || the United States=美國 || There ___ fifty states in the United States. || are|is|be || fifty states 複數，There are。 || 美國有五十個州。
stationery || n. || 文具 || I bought some stationery. || 我買了一些文具。 || stationery store=文具店 || I bought some stationery ___ the bookstore. || at|on|to || 在某個地點（店）用 at。 || 我在書店買了一些文具。
steal || v. || 偷 || It is wrong to steal. || 偷竊是不對的。 || steal sth from sb=從某人那裡偷某物 || It's wrong ___ steal other people's things. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 偷別人的東西是不對的。
steam || n. || 蒸氣 || Steam came out of the pot. || 蒸氣從鍋子冒出來。 || steamed bun=饅頭; steam engine=蒸汽機 || Steam came out ___ the pot. || of|from in|to || come out of = 從…出來。 || 蒸氣從鍋子冒出來。
step || n. || 步伐；階梯 || Watch your step. || 小心腳步。 || step by step=一步一步地 || Learn English step ___ step. || by|to|after || step by step = 一步一步地。 || 一步一步學英文。
stingy || adj. || 小氣的 || Don't be stingy. || 別小氣。 || stingy person=小氣鬼 || He is so stingy ___ he never buys anyone a drink. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 他小氣到從不請任何人喝飲料。
stomachache || n. || 胃痛 || I have a stomachache. || 我胃痛。 || have a stomachache=胃痛 || I have a stomachache ___ I ate too much. || because|so|but || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 我胃痛，因為我吃太多了。
stone || n. || 石頭 || The house is made of stone. || 這房子是石頭蓋的。 || stone wall=石牆 || The old bridge ___ made of stone. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The old bridge 用 is + p.p.。 || 那座老橋是用石頭建的。
storm || n. || 暴風雨 || A storm is coming. || 暴風雨要來了。 || heavy storm=強烈暴風雨 || The flight was canceled ___ the storm. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞 the storm，用 because of。 || 班機因為暴風雨取消了。
stormy || adj. || 暴風雨的 || It was a stormy night. || 那是一個暴風雨的夜晚。 || stormy weather=暴風雨天氣 || It was stormy, ___ we stayed at home. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 外面在下暴風雨，所以我們待在家。
stove || n. || 爐子 || Mom is cooking on the stove. || 媽媽在爐子上煮東西。 || gas stove=瓦斯爐 || Don't touch the stove. It's ___ hot. || too|so that|much || too + 形容詞 = 太…。 || 別碰爐子，太燙了。
straw || n. || 吸管；稻草 || Can I have a straw? || 可以給我一根吸管嗎？ || plastic straw=塑膠吸管 || Many stores don't give out plastic straws ___. || anymore|any more than|no more || not… anymore = 不再…。 || 很多店已經不再提供塑膠吸管了。
stream || n. || 小溪 || Fish swim in the stream. || 魚在小溪裡游。 || mountain stream=山澗 || There ___ many fish in the stream. || are|is|be || many fish 複數，There are。 || 小溪裡有很多魚。
strike || v./n. || 打擊；罷工 || The workers are on strike. || 工人們在罷工。 || on strike=罷工中 || The workers have been on strike ___ a week. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 工人們已經罷工一週了。
style || n. || 風格 || I like your style. || 我喜歡你的風格。 || in style=時髦; hair style=髮型 || This dress is ___ style this year. || in|on|at || in style = 流行、時髦。 || 這款洋裝今年很流行。
subway || n. || 地鐵 || We took the subway to the museum. || 我們搭地鐵去博物館。 || take the subway=搭地鐵 || We went to the museum ___ subway. || by|on|with || 搭乘交通工具用 by。 || 我們搭地鐵去博物館。
succeed || v. || 成功 || If you work hard, you will succeed. || 如果你努力，你就會成功。 || succeed in=在…方面成功 || She succeeded ___ passing the exam. || in|on|at to || succeed in + V-ing = 成功做到…。 || 她成功通過了考試。
success || n. || 成功 || Hard work is the key to success. || 努力是成功的關鍵。 || the key to success=成功的關鍵 || Hard work is the key ___ success. || to|of|for || the key to = …的關鍵。 || 努力是成功的關鍵。
such || adj. || 如此的 || It was such a nice day. || 那是如此美好的一天。 || such a/an + 形容詞 + 名詞=如此…的… || It was such ___ nice day that we went on a picnic. || a|an|the || such a + 形容詞 + 單數名詞 + that…。 || 天氣如此好，所以我們去野餐了。
sudden || adj. || 突然的 || There was a sudden change in the weather. || 天氣突然變了。 || all of a sudden=突然地 || All of a sudden, the lights ___ off. || went|go|goes || 描述過去發生的事用過去式 went。 || 突然間，燈熄了。
suggest || v. || 建議 || I suggest going by bus. || 我建議搭公車去。 || suggest + V-ing=建議做… || I suggest ___ a taxi. || taking|to take|take || suggest 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我建議搭計程車。
suit || n./v. || 西裝；適合 || He wore a black suit. || 他穿了一套黑西裝。 || suit sb=適合某人 || This dress suits ___ very well. || you|your|yours || suit 後面接受詞，用受格 you。 || 這件洋裝非常適合你。
super || adj. || 超級的 || The movie was super exciting. || 那部電影超刺激的。 || super hero=超級英雄 || The movie was super exciting. I want to see it ___. || again|ago|back || 再一次用 again。 || 那部電影超刺激的，我想再看一次。
supper || n. || 晚餐 || We have supper at six. || 我們六點吃晚餐。 || have supper=吃晚餐 || We have supper ___ six o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 我們六點吃晚餐。
support || v./n. || 支持 || My parents support my dream. || 我的父母支持我的夢想。 || support sb=支持某人 || Thank you ___ your support. || for|to|of || thank you for + 名詞。 || 謝謝你的支持。
survive || v. || 存活 || Only two people survived the accident. || 只有兩個人在事故中倖存。 || survive an accident=從意外中生還 || Plants cannot survive ___ water. || without|with|within || without = 沒有…。 || 植物沒有水無法存活。
