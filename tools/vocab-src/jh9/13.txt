# 九年級 13：rubber – sleepy
rubber || n. || 橡膠；橡皮擦 || Tires are made of rubber. || 輪胎是用橡膠做的。 || rubber band=橡皮筋 || Tires are made ___ rubber. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 輪胎是用橡膠做的。
rude || adj. || 無禮的 || It's rude to interrupt people. || 打斷別人說話很沒禮貌。 || be rude to=對…無禮 || Don't be rude ___ your teacher. || to|for|with || be rude to = 對…無禮。 || 不要對你的老師無禮。
ruin || v. || 毀壞 || The rain ruined our picnic. || 雨毀了我們的野餐。 || ruin a plan=毀了計畫 || Our picnic ___ ruined by the rain. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我們的野餐被雨毀了。
rush || v./n. || 衝；匆忙 || Don't rush. We have time. || 別急，我們有時間。 || in a rush=匆忙地; rush hour=尖峰時間 || The MRT is crowded ___ rush hour. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 尖峰時間捷運很擁擠。
safety || n. || 安全 || Safety comes first. || 安全第一。 || for safety=為了安全; safety belt=安全帶 || For your safety, please wear a helmet ___ you ride a scooter. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 為了你的安全，騎機車時請戴安全帽。
sailor || n. || 水手 || The sailor has been at sea for a month. || 那名水手已經出海一個月了。 || sailor=水手 || The sailor has been at sea ___ two months. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 那名水手已經出海兩個月了。
sample || n. || 樣品；試吃品 || Can I try a free sample? || 我可以試吃看看嗎？ || free sample=免費試用品 || The store gave ___ some free samples. || us|we|our || gave 後面的人用受格 us。 || 那家店給了我們一些免費試吃品。
sand || n. || 沙 || The kids are playing in the sand. || 孩子們在沙子裡玩。 || sand castle=沙堡 || The kids built a castle ___ sand. || of|from|by || be built of / made of = 用…蓋成。 || 孩子們用沙子蓋了一座城堡。
satisfy || v. || 使滿意 || The food didn't satisfy him. || 這食物沒有讓他滿意。 || be satisfied with=對…滿意 || Are you satisfied ___ the result? || with|of|at || be satisfied with = 對…感到滿意。 || 你對結果滿意嗎？
saucer || n. || 茶碟；碟子 || She put the cup on the saucer. || 她把杯子放在茶碟上。 || cup and saucer=杯碟; flying saucer=飛碟 || Put the cup ___ the saucer. || on|in|at || 放在碟子上用 on。 || 把杯子放在茶碟上。
scarf || n. || 圍巾 || Wear a scarf. It's cold. || 戴條圍巾，好冷。 || wear a scarf=戴圍巾 || She wore a scarf ___ keep warm. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她圍著圍巾保暖。
scene || n. || 場景；現場 || This is my favorite scene in the movie. || 這是我最喜歡的電影場景。 || the scene of=…的現場 || The police arrived ___ the scene quickly. || at|in|to || arrive at + 地點 = 到達…。 || 警察很快就抵達現場。
scenery || n. || 風景 || The scenery in Hualien is beautiful. || 花蓮的風景很美。 || beautiful scenery=美麗的風景 || The scenery here ___ beautiful. || is|are|be || scenery 不可數，用 is。 || 這裡的風景很美。
scientist || n. || 科學家 || She wants to be a scientist. || 她想當科學家。 || famous scientist=有名的科學家 || The scientist ___ invented this machine is from Taiwan. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾「人」當主詞用 who。 || 發明這台機器的科學家來自台灣。
score || n./v. || 分數；得分 || What's the score? || 比數是多少？ || get a high score=得高分 || She got a high score ___ the English test. || on|in|at || 在考試上得分用 on the test。 || 她英文考試得了高分。
seafood || n. || 海鮮 || I love seafood. || 我愛海鮮。 || seafood restaurant=海鮮餐廳 || Some people are allergic ___ seafood. || to|with|of || be allergic to = 對…過敏。 || 有些人對海鮮過敏。
search || v./n. || 搜尋 || I searched the Internet for information. || 我上網搜尋資料。 || search for=搜尋… || They searched ___ the lost dog all day. || for|to|at || search for = 搜尋…。 || 他們整天都在找那隻走失的狗。
secondary || adj. || 次要的；中等的 || Money is secondary to health. || 金錢比健康次要。 || secondary school=中學 || Money is secondary ___ health. || to|than|for || be secondary to = 比…次要。 || 金錢比健康次要。
secret || n. || 祕密 || Can you keep a secret? || 你能保守祕密嗎？ || keep a secret=保守祕密; in secret=祕密地 || Please keep it ___ secret. || a|an|the || keep it a secret = 保守這個祕密。 || 請保守這個祕密。
section || n. || 部分；區 || The book has three sections. || 這本書有三個部分。 || non-smoking section=非吸菸區 || Please read the first section ___ the article. || of|in|for || the first section of = …的第一部分。 || 請讀文章的第一部分。
seek || v. || 尋求 || You should seek help. || 你應該尋求協助。 || seek help=尋求協助; hide and seek=捉迷藏 || If you have problems, don't be afraid ___ seek help. || to|for|of || be afraid to + 原形動詞 = 不敢做…。 || 如果你有問題，別害怕尋求協助。
seem || v. || 似乎 || You seem tired. || 你似乎很累。 || seem to V=似乎… || He seems ___ happy today. || to be|being|be || seem to + 原形動詞。 || 他今天似乎很開心。
select || v. || 挑選 || Select the correct answer. || 選出正確答案。 || select from=從…中挑選 || She was selected ___ the class leader. || as|for|to || be selected as = 被選為…。 || 她被選為班長。
selfish || adj. || 自私的 || Don't be selfish. || 別自私。 || selfish person=自私的人 || It was selfish ___ him to eat all the cake. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 他把蛋糕全吃光真是自私。
semester || n. || 學期 || The new semester starts in September. || 新學期九月開始。 || this semester=這學期 || The new semester starts ___ September. || in|on|at || 月份前面用 in。 || 新學期九月開始。
sense || n. || 感覺；意義 || He has a good sense of direction. || 他方向感很好。 || make sense=有道理; sense of humor=幽默感 || What you said makes ___ sense. || no|not|none || make no sense = 沒道理。 || 你說的話沒道理。
servant || n. || 僕人 || The king had many servants. || 國王有很多僕人。 || servant=僕人 || The servants ___ told to clean the castle. || were|was|are || 被動語態；The servants 複數，過去用 were。 || 僕人們被吩咐打掃城堡。
serve || v. || 服務；供應 || Breakfast is served from 7 to 10. || 早餐從七點供應到十點。 || serve food=上菜; serve customers=服務客人 || Breakfast is served ___ 7 to 10 a.m. || from|between|since || from A to B = 從 A 到 B。 || 早餐從上午七點供應到十點。
shark || n. || 鯊魚 || Sharks live in the ocean. || 鯊魚住在海洋裡。 || shark attack=鯊魚攻擊 || The swimmer was attacked ___ a shark. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 那名泳客被鯊魚攻擊了。
sheet || n. || 床單；一張 || Please give me a sheet of paper. || 請給我一張紙。 || a sheet of paper=一張紙; bed sheet=床單 || Please give me a sheet ___ paper. || of|for|in || a sheet of paper = 一張紙。 || 請給我一張紙。
shelf || n. || 架子 || Put the books on the shelf. || 把書放在架子上。 || shelves=架子（複數）; bookshelf=書架 || Put the books back ___ the shelf. || on|in|at || 放在架子上用 on。 || 把書放回架子上。
shoot || v. || 射擊；投（籃）；拍攝 || He shoots the ball well. || 他投籃很準。 || shoot a movie=拍電影 || He likes to shoot hoops with his friends ___ school. || after|during|while || after school = 放學後。 || 他喜歡放學後和朋友投籃。
shore || n. || 岸 || We walked along the shore. || 我們沿著岸邊散步。 || on the shore=在岸上 || We walked ___ the shore at sunset. || along|across|through || walk along = 沿著…走。 || 我們在日落時沿著岸邊散步。
shrimp || n. || 蝦子 || I like fried shrimp. || 我喜歡炸蝦。 || fried shrimp=炸蝦 || I'm allergic ___ shrimp. || to|with|of || be allergic to = 對…過敏。 || 我對蝦子過敏。
shut || v. || 關閉 || Shut the door, please. || 請關門。 || shut down=關閉; shut up=閉嘴 || Please shut the window ___ you leave. || before|after|until || 離開「之前」關窗，用 before。 || 離開前請關窗。
silence || n. || 安靜；沉默 || There was silence in the room. || 房間裡一片寂靜。 || in silence=安靜地 || The students listened ___ silence. || in|with|by || in silence = 安靜地。 || 學生們安靜地聽著。
silent || adj. || 安靜的 || Please be silent in the library. || 在圖書館請保持安靜。 || keep silent=保持沉默 || The letter "k" in "knife" is silent, ___ it? || isn't|doesn't|is || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || knife 的 k 不發音，對吧？
silly || adj. || 愚蠢的 || Don't be silly. || 別傻了。 || silly question=傻問題 || That was a silly mistake, ___ it? || wasn't|didn't|was || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：was → wasn't。 || 那是個愚蠢的錯誤，不是嗎？
silver || n. || 銀 || She won a silver medal. || 她得了銀牌。 || silver medal=銀牌 || She won ___ silver medal in the race. || a|an|the more || silver 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 她在比賽中得了銀牌。
similar || adj. || 相似的 || Our ideas are similar. || 我們的想法很相似。 || be similar to=和…相似 || My bag is similar ___ yours. || to|with|as || be similar to = 和…相似。 || 我的包包和你的很像。
sincere || adj. || 真誠的 || Please accept my sincere apology. || 請接受我誠摯的道歉。 || sincere apology=誠摯的道歉 || She is always sincere ___ her friends. || with|to be|at || be sincere with = 對…真誠。 || 她對朋友總是很真誠。
single || adj. || 單一的；單身的 || She is still single. || 她還是單身。 || single room=單人房; every single day=每一天 || I'd like a single room ___ two nights. || for|in|at || for + 一段時間。 || 我要一間單人房，住兩晚。
sink || n./v. || 水槽；下沉 || Put the dishes in the sink. || 把碗盤放進水槽。 || kitchen sink=廚房水槽 || The dishes are ___ the sink. || in|on|at || 在水槽裡用 in。 || 碗盤在水槽裡。
skate || v. || 溜冰 || Can you skate? || 你會溜冰嗎？ || ice skate=溜冰 || It's dangerous ___ skate on thin ice. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 在薄冰上溜冰很危險。
ski || v. || 滑雪 || We went skiing in Japan. || 我們去日本滑雪。 || go skiing=去滑雪 || Have you ever ___ skiing? || been|went|go || 現在完成式問經驗：Have you ever been + V-ing? || 你滑過雪嗎？
skill || n. || 技能 || Reading is an important skill. || 閱讀是一項重要的技能。 || language skills=語言能力 || Reading is ___ important skill. || an|a|the || important 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 閱讀是一項重要的技能。
skillful || adj. || 熟練的 || He is a skillful driver. || 他是個技術熟練的駕駛。 || be skillful at=擅長… || He is skillful ___ playing the guitar. || at|in|for || be skillful at = 擅長…。 || 他很擅長彈吉他。
skin || n. || 皮膚 || She has soft skin. || 她的皮膚很柔嫩。 || dry skin=乾性皮膚 || Sunscreen protects your skin ___ the sun. || from|of|with || protect A from B = 保護 A 免於 B。 || 防曬乳保護你的皮膚免受陽光傷害。
skinny || adj. || 很瘦的 || The dog is very skinny. || 那隻狗非常瘦。 || skinny jeans=緊身牛仔褲 || The skinny dog ___ fed by a kind girl yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那隻瘦巴巴的狗昨天被一個好心的女孩餵了。
sleepy || adj. || 想睡的 || I'm sleepy. I want to go to bed. || 我好睏，想去睡覺。 || feel sleepy=覺得睏 || I felt sleepy ___ I stayed up late. || because|so|but || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 我覺得很睏，因為我熬夜了。
