# 九年級 09：koala – method
koala || n. || 無尾熊 || Koalas sleep about 20 hours a day. || 無尾熊一天睡約二十小時。 || koala bear=無尾熊 || Koalas sleep about twenty hours ___ day. || a|an|the || 每天用 a day。 || 無尾熊一天睡約二十小時。
lack || n./v. || 缺乏 || Lack of sleep is bad for you. || 睡眠不足對你不好。 || lack of=缺乏… || Lack ___ sleep can make you sick. || of|for|in || lack of = 缺乏…。 || 睡眠不足會讓你生病。
lady || n. || 女士 || The lady in red is my teacher. || 穿紅衣的那位女士是我的老師。 || ladies and gentlemen=各位先生女士 || The lady ___ is talking to Mom is my aunt. || who|which|whom it || 關係代名詞修飾「人」當主詞用 who。 || 正在和媽媽說話的那位女士是我阿姨。
lamb || n. || 小羊；羊肉 || The lamb is following its mother. || 小羊跟著牠的媽媽。 || roast lamb=烤羊肉 || The lamb is following ___ mother. || its|it's|their || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 小羊跟著牠的媽媽。
lantern || n. || 燈籠 || We carry lanterns at the Lantern Festival. || 我們在元宵節提燈籠。 || paper lantern=紙燈籠; sky lantern=天燈 || The lanterns were ___ beautiful that we took many photos. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 燈籠美到我們拍了好多照片。
latest || adj. || 最新的 || This is the latest phone. || 這是最新款的手機。 || the latest news=最新消息 || Have you heard the latest news? No, I ___. || haven't|didn't|don't || 用 Have you 問，否定回答用 haven't。 || 你聽說最新消息了嗎？還沒。
latter || adj./n. || 後者 || I prefer the latter. || 我比較喜歡後者。 || the former and the latter=前者與後者 || I have a cat and a dog. The latter ___ very noisy. || is|are|has || the latter 指單一的狗，用 is。 || 我有一隻貓和一隻狗，後者（狗）很吵。
law || n. || 法律 || You must obey the law. || 你必須遵守法律。 || break the law=犯法; obey the law=守法 || Everyone must obey the law, ___ they will be punished. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 每個人都必須守法，否則會受罰。
lay || v. || 放置；下蛋 || The hen lays an egg every day. || 那隻母雞每天下一顆蛋。 || lay eggs=下蛋 || The hen lays an egg ___ day. || every|each of|all || every + 單數名詞 = 每一…。 || 那隻母雞每天下一顆蛋。
leaf || n. || 葉子 || A leaf fell from the tree. || 一片葉子從樹上掉下來。 || leaves=葉子（複數） || A leaf fell ___ my head. || on|in|at || 落在頭上用 on。 || 一片葉子掉在我頭上。
lettuce || n. || 萵苣 || I want a salad with lettuce and tomatoes. || 我要一份有萵苣和番茄的沙拉。 || a head of lettuce=一顆萵苣 || There isn't ___ lettuce in the fridge. || any|some|many || 否定句用 any；lettuce 不可數。 || 冰箱裡沒有萵苣了。
lick || v. || 舔 || The dog licked my hand. || 那隻狗舔了我的手。 || lick one's lips=舔嘴唇 || The cat is licking ___ paws. || its|it's|it || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 貓在舔牠的爪子。
lid || n. || 蓋子 || Put the lid on the pot. || 把蓋子蓋在鍋子上。 || pot lid=鍋蓋 || Put the lid ___ the box. || on|in|at || 蓋在上面用 on。 || 把蓋子蓋在箱子上。
lift || v. || 舉起 || Can you help me lift this box? || 你可以幫我抬這個箱子嗎？ || lift up=舉起; give sb a lift=載某人一程 || This box is too heavy for me ___ lift. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 這個箱子太重了，我抬不起來。
lightning || n. || 閃電 || The tree was hit by lightning. || 那棵樹被閃電擊中。 || thunder and lightning=雷電 || The tree was hit ___ lightning last night. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 那棵樹昨晚被閃電擊中。
likely || adj. || 可能的 || It is likely to rain tomorrow. || 明天可能會下雨。 || be likely to V=可能… || It is likely ___ rain this afternoon. || to|for|that to || be likely to + 原形動詞。 || 今天下午可能會下雨。
limit || n./v. || 限制 || There is a time limit. || 有時間限制。 || speed limit=速限; time limit=時間限制 || The speed limit ___ this road is 50 km/h. || on|in|at || the speed limit on a road = 某條路的速限。 || 這條路的速限是時速五十公里。
link || n./v. || 連結 || Click the link to see more. || 點擊連結看更多。 || link A to B=把 A 和 B 連結 || The two cities ___ linked by a bridge. || are|is|be || 被動語態；The two cities 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 這兩座城市由一座橋相連。
liquid || n. || 液體 || Water is a liquid. || 水是液體。 || liquid soap=洗手乳 || Liquids are not allowed ___ your carry-on bag. || in|on|at || 在包包裡用 in。 || 隨身行李中不可攜帶液體。
liter || n. || 公升 || I drink two liters of water a day. || 我一天喝兩公升的水。 || a liter of=一公升… || I drink two liters ___ water a day. || of|for|in || a liter of = 一公升…。 || 我一天喝兩公升的水。
loaf || n. || 一條（麵包） || I bought a loaf of bread. || 我買了一條麵包。 || a loaf of bread=一條麵包 || I bought a loaf ___ bread for breakfast. || of|for|in || a loaf of bread = 一條麵包。 || 我買了一條麵包當早餐。
local || adj. || 當地的 || Let's try the local food. || 我們來嘗嘗當地的食物吧。 || local food=當地食物; local people=當地人 || You should try the local food ___ you travel. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 旅行時你應該嘗嘗當地的食物。
lock || v./n. || 鎖 || Don't forget to lock the door. || 別忘了鎖門。 || lock the door=鎖門 || The door ___ locked when I got home. || was|is|were || 過去的狀態/被動 was + p.p.。 || 我到家時門是鎖著的。
locker || n. || 置物櫃 || Put your bag in the locker. || 把你的包包放在置物櫃裡。 || school locker=學校置物櫃 || Every student ___ a locker at school. || has|have|having || every student 視為單數，用 has。 || 每個學生在學校都有一個置物櫃。
loser || n. || 失敗者；輸家 || Don't be a bad loser. || 輸了不要不服氣。 || good loser=輸得起的人 || The loser shook hands ___ the winner. || with|to|at || shake hands with = 和…握手。 || 輸的人和贏的人握手。
ma'am || n. || 女士（尊稱） || Can I help you, ma'am? || 女士，需要幫忙嗎？ || yes, ma'am=是的，女士 || Excuse me, ma'am. ___ this your bag? || Is|Are|Does || this your bag 單數，用 Is。 || 女士，不好意思，這是您的包包嗎？
magazine || n. || 雜誌 || I read a fashion magazine. || 我看一本時尚雜誌。 || fashion magazine=時尚雜誌 || This magazine ___ published every month. || is|are|be || 被動語態；This magazine 用 is + p.p.。 || 這本雜誌每月出刊。
magician || n. || 魔術師 || The magician pulled a rabbit out of a hat. || 魔術師從帽子裡變出一隻兔子。 || famous magician=有名的魔術師 || The magician pulled a rabbit out ___ his hat. || of|from|in || out of = 從…裡面出來。 || 魔術師從他的帽子裡變出一隻兔子。
major || adj./n. || 主要的；主修 || Traffic is a major problem in big cities. || 交通是大城市的主要問題。 || major in=主修… || My sister majors ___ English at college. || in|on|at || major in = 主修…。 || 我姊姊在大學主修英文。
male || adj./n. || 男性（的） || There are more male students than female students. || 男學生比女學生多。 || male student=男學生 || Most of the male students ___ basketball. || play|plays|playing || Most of the male students 複數，動詞用原形。 || 大部分男學生都打籃球。
mall || n. || 購物中心 || Let's go shopping at the mall. || 我們去購物中心逛街吧。 || shopping mall=購物中心 || Let's meet ___ the mall at two. || at|on|in of || 在某個地點用 at。 || 我們兩點在購物中心碰面吧。
manager || n. || 經理 || The manager is in a meeting. || 經理在開會。 || store manager=店長 || I'd like to speak ___ the manager. || to|at|for || speak to sb = 跟某人說話。 || 我想跟經理談談。
mango || n. || 芒果 || Mango ice is popular in summer. || 芒果冰在夏天很受歡迎。 || mango ice=芒果冰 || Mango ice is very popular ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 芒果冰在夏天非常受歡迎。
manner || n. || 方式；禮貌（複數） || He has good manners. || 他很有禮貌。 || table manners=餐桌禮儀; good manners=禮貌 || It's bad manners ___ talk with your mouth full. || to|for|of || It is + 名詞 + to V。 || 嘴裡塞滿東西說話是不禮貌的。
marry || v. || 和…結婚 || Will you marry me? || 你願意嫁給我嗎？ || marry sb=和某人結婚 || She married a doctor ___ 2020. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 她在 2020 年嫁給一位醫生。
marvelous || adj. || 極好的 || We had a marvelous time. || 我們度過了非常美好的時光。 || marvelous idea=絕妙的點子 || The view from the top was marvelous, ___ it? || wasn't|didn't|was || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：was → wasn't。 || 從山頂看的景色棒極了，不是嗎？
mass || n./adj. || 大量；大眾的 || Masses of people came to the concert. || 大批人潮來參加演唱會。 || mass media=大眾傳播媒體 || Masses ___ people came to the concert. || of|for|in || masses of = 大量的…。 || 大批人潮來參加演唱會。
master || n./v. || 大師；精通 || He is a master of kung fu. || 他是一位功夫大師。 || master a skill=精通一項技能 || It takes years ___ master a language. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 精通一種語言需要好幾年。
mat || n. || 墊子 || Wipe your feet on the mat. || 在墊子上擦擦你的腳。 || yoga mat=瑜伽墊; door mat=門墊 || Please wipe your feet ___ the mat. || on|in|at || 在墊子上用 on。 || 請在墊子上擦擦腳。
match || n./v. || 比賽；火柴；搭配 || The shirt matches your pants. || 這件襯衫和你的褲子很搭。 || tennis match=網球比賽; match with=和…搭配 || Does this tie match ___ my shirt? || with|to|for || match with = 和…搭配。 || 這條領帶和我的襯衫搭嗎？
maximum || n./adj. || 最大量 || The maximum speed is 100 km/h. || 最高速限是時速一百公里。 || at the maximum=最多 || The elevator can carry a maximum ___ ten people. || of|for|in || a maximum of = 最多…。 || 這台電梯最多可載十個人。
meaning || n. || 意義 || What's the meaning of this word? || 這個字是什麼意思？ || the meaning of=…的意思 || What's the meaning ___ this word? || of|for|to || the meaning of = …的意思。 || 這個字是什麼意思？
measure || v. || 測量 || Measure the length of the table. || 量一下桌子的長度。 || measure the height=量身高 || The nurse measured my height ___ weight. || and|but|or || 兩者都量，用 and。 || 護理師量了我的身高和體重。
mechanic || n. || 技工 || The mechanic fixed my car. || 技工修好了我的車。 || car mechanic=汽車技師 || My car ___ fixed by the mechanic yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我的車昨天被技工修好了。
memory || n. || 記憶；回憶 || I have a good memory. || 我記性很好。 || happy memories=快樂的回憶 || I have many happy memories ___ my childhood. || of|for|in || memories of = …的回憶。 || 我有許多童年的快樂回憶。
men's room || n. || 男廁 || The men's room is on the left. || 男廁在左邊。 || men's room=男廁 || Excuse me, ___ is the men's room? || where|what|which || 問地點用 where。 || 不好意思，男廁在哪裡？
message || n. || 訊息 || I'll send you a message. || 我會傳訊息給你。 || leave a message=留言 || Can I leave a message ___ Mr. Lin? || for|to|with || leave a message for sb = 留言給某人。 || 我可以留言給林先生嗎？
metal || n. || 金屬 || This box is made of metal. || 這個盒子是金屬做的。 || heavy metal=重金屬 || This box is made ___ metal. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這個盒子是金屬做的。
meter || n. || 公尺 || The pool is 50 meters long. || 這座泳池五十公尺長。 || 100 meters=一百公尺 || How ___ is the pool? It's 50 meters long. || long|much|many || 問長度用 How long。 || 這座泳池多長？五十公尺。
method || n. || 方法 || This is a good method of learning English. || 這是學英文的好方法。 || teaching method=教學方法 || This is a good method ___ learning new words. || of|to|with || a method of + V-ing = …的方法。 || 這是背新單字的好方法。
