# 九年級 03：captain – contract
captain || n. || 隊長；船長 || He is the captain of our team. || 他是我們隊的隊長。 || team captain=隊長; ship captain=船長 || He was chosen ___ the captain of the team. || as|for|to || be chosen as = 被選為…。 || 他被選為隊長。
careless || adj. || 粗心的 || Don't be careless. || 不要粗心。 || careless mistake=粗心的錯誤 || It was careless ___ you to leave the door open. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你沒關門真是粗心。
carpet || n. || 地毯 || There is a red carpet on the floor. || 地板上有一塊紅地毯。 || red carpet=紅毯 || The carpet ___ cleaned last week. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 地毯上週清洗過了。
carrot || n. || 紅蘿蔔 || Rabbits like carrots. || 兔子喜歡紅蘿蔔。 || carrot juice=紅蘿蔔汁 || Carrots are good ___ your eyes. || for|to|at || be good for = 對…有益。 || 紅蘿蔔對你的眼睛有益。
cartoon || n. || 卡通 || My brother likes watching cartoons. || 我弟弟喜歡看卡通。 || cartoon character=卡通人物 || My little brother enjoys ___ cartoons. || watching|to watch|watch || enjoy 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我弟弟喜歡看卡通。
cash || n. || 現金 || Do you pay in cash or by card? || 你付現還是刷卡？ || pay in cash=付現金 || Can I pay ___ cash? || in|by|with of || pay in cash = 用現金付款。 || 我可以付現金嗎？
cause || v./n. || 導致；原因 || Smoking can cause cancer. || 抽菸會導致癌症。 || cause a problem=造成問題; the cause of=…的原因 || The accident ___ caused by a drunk driver. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那場事故是一名酒駕司機造成的。
ceiling || n. || 天花板 || There is a lamp on the ceiling. || 天花板上有一盞燈。 || on the ceiling=在天花板上 || A spider is walking ___ the ceiling. || on|in|at || 在天花板表面上用 on。 || 一隻蜘蛛在天花板上爬。
central || adj. || 中央的 || The hotel is in central Taipei. || 這間飯店在台北市中心。 || central Taiwan=中台灣 || Taichung is ___ central Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在某個地區用 in。 || 台中位於台灣中部。
century || n. || 世紀 || We live in the 21st century. || 我們生活在二十一世紀。 || in the 21st century=在二十一世紀 || This temple was built ___ the 18th century. || in|on|at || 世紀前面用 in。 || 這座廟建於十八世紀。
cereal || n. || 穀片 || I eat cereal with milk for breakfast. || 我早餐吃穀片配牛奶。 || a bowl of cereal=一碗穀片 || I have a bowl ___ cereal every morning. || of|for|with || a bowl of = 一碗…。 || 我每天早上吃一碗穀片。
certain || adj. || 確定的；某個 || I'm certain he will come. || 我確定他會來。 || be certain of=確定…; a certain person=某個人 || Are you certain ___ the answer? || of|at|with || be certain of = 確定…。 || 你確定答案嗎？
channel || n. || 頻道 || What channel is the game on? || 比賽在哪一台播？ || change the channel=轉台 || The game is on Channel 5 ___ eight o'clock. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 比賽八點在第五台播出。
character || n. || 角色；性格 || Who is your favorite character in the story? || 故事中你最喜歡的角色是誰？ || main character=主角 || The main character of the story is a boy ___ loves music. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾「人」用 who。 || 故事的主角是一個熱愛音樂的男孩。
charge || v./n. || 收費；充電 || My phone needs to be charged. || 我的手機需要充電。 || free of charge=免費; in charge of=負責… || Who is in charge ___ this project? || of|for|to || in charge of = 負責…。 || 誰負責這個專案？
chart || n. || 圖表 || Look at the chart on page 10. || 看第十頁的圖表。 || pie chart=圓餅圖 || The chart shows ___ many students like sports. || how|what|which || 名詞子句 how many… = 有多少…。 || 這張圖表顯示有多少學生喜歡運動。
chase || v. || 追逐 || The cat is chasing a mouse. || 貓在追一隻老鼠。 || chase after=追趕 || The thief was chased ___ the police. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 小偷被警察追捕。
chemistry || n. || 化學 || Chemistry is difficult for me. || 化學對我來說很難。 || chemistry class=化學課 || I find chemistry ___ than physics. || easier|easy|easiest || 兩者比較用比較級 easier。 || 我覺得化學比物理簡單。
chess || n. || 西洋棋 || Do you know how to play chess? || 你會下西洋棋嗎？ || play chess=下棋 || Do you know how ___ chess? || to play|play|playing || how to + 原形動詞 = 如何做…。 || 你會下西洋棋嗎？
childhood || n. || 童年 || I had a happy childhood. || 我有一個快樂的童年。 || in one's childhood=在童年時 || I lived in the country ___ my childhood. || during|while|when || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 我童年時住在鄉下。
childish || adj. || 幼稚的 || Don't be so childish. || 別這麼幼稚。 || childish behavior=幼稚的行為 || It's childish ___ you to cry over a toy. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你為了一個玩具哭真是幼稚。
childlike || adj. || 天真的 || She has a childlike smile. || 她有天真的笑容。 || childlike wonder=赤子之心 || Grandpa has a childlike heart, ___ he loves to play games with us. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 爺爺有一顆赤子之心，所以喜歡跟我們玩遊戲。
chin || n. || 下巴 || He has a beard on his chin. || 他下巴有鬍子。 || chin up=打起精神 || Keep your chin up. Things will get ___. || better|good|best || get + 比較級 = 變得更…。 || 打起精神，事情會好轉的。
chubby || adj. || 圓胖的 || The baby has chubby cheeks. || 寶寶有胖嘟嘟的臉頰。 || chubby cheeks=胖嘟嘟的臉頰 || The chubby baby is ___ cute that everyone loves him. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that… = 如此…以致於…。 || 那個胖嘟嘟的寶寶可愛到大家都愛他。
classical || adj. || 古典的 || I like classical music. || 我喜歡古典音樂。 || classical music=古典音樂 || Listening to classical music helps me ___. || relax|relaxing|relaxes || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 聽古典音樂幫助我放鬆。
clever || adj. || 聰明的 || The fox is very clever. || 狐狸很聰明。 || clever idea=聰明的點子 || That's a clever idea! Who thought ___ it? || of|about to|for || think of = 想到。 || 真是個聰明的點子！是誰想到的？
climate || n. || 氣候 || Taiwan has a warm climate. || 台灣氣候溫暖。 || climate change=氣候變遷 || Climate change is ___ serious problem. || a|an|the more || serious 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 氣候變遷是一個嚴重的問題。
closet || n. || 衣櫥 || Hang your clothes in the closet. || 把你的衣服掛在衣櫥裡。 || in the closet=在衣櫥裡 || Put your coat ___ the closet. || in|on|at || 放進衣櫥裡用 in。 || 把你的外套放進衣櫥。
coach || n. || 教練 || Our coach is very strict. || 我們的教練很嚴格。 || basketball coach=籃球教練 || Our coach makes us ___ every day. || practice|to practice|practicing || make sb + 原形動詞（使役動詞）。 || 我們的教練要我們每天練習。
coast || n. || 海岸 || We drove along the east coast. || 我們沿著東海岸開車。 || east coast=東海岸; along the coast=沿著海岸 || Hualien is ___ the east coast of Taiwan. || on|in|at of || 位在海岸上用 on the coast。 || 花蓮在台灣的東海岸。
cockroach || n. || 蟑螂 || I'm afraid of cockroaches. || 我怕蟑螂。 || kill a cockroach=打蟑螂 || A cockroach ___ found in the kitchen last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 昨晚在廚房發現了一隻蟑螂。
coin || n. || 硬幣 || I have some coins in my pocket. || 我口袋裡有一些硬幣。 || ten-dollar coin=十元硬幣 || How many coins ___ you have in your pocket? || do|are|does || 一般動詞疑問句，主詞 you 用 do。 || 你口袋裡有幾枚硬幣？
cola || n. || 可樂 || I'd like a cola, please. || 請給我一杯可樂。 || diet cola=健怡可樂 || Drinking too much cola is bad ___ your health. || for|to|at || be bad for = 對…有害。 || 喝太多可樂對健康不好。
college || n. || 大學；學院 || My sister is in college. || 我姊姊在讀大學。 || go to college=上大學 || My brother wants to study science ___ college. || in|on|at of || in college = 在大學裡。 || 我哥哥想在大學念理科。
colorful || adj. || 多彩的 || The garden is full of colorful flowers. || 花園裡開滿了五顏六色的花。 || colorful flowers=色彩繽紛的花 || The garden is full ___ colorful flowers. || of|with|in || be full of = 充滿…。 || 花園裡開滿了色彩繽紛的花。
command || n./v. || 命令 || The soldiers followed the command. || 士兵們服從命令。 || give a command=下命令 || The dog was trained to follow commands ___ its owner. || from|of|to || commands from sb = 來自某人的命令。 || 那隻狗被訓練服從主人的命令。
comment || n./v. || 評論 || Do you have any comments? || 你有任何意見嗎？ || make a comment=發表評論; no comment=無可奉告 || Please leave your comments ___ the box. || in|on|at || 放進箱子裡用 in。 || 請把你的意見放進箱子裡。
company || n. || 公司 || My father works for a big company. || 我爸爸在一家大公司工作。 || work for a company=在公司上班 || My father has worked for this company ___ ten years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 我爸爸在這家公司工作十年了。
compare || v. || 比較 || Don't compare yourself with others. || 不要拿自己跟別人比。 || compare A with B=比較 A 和 B || Don't compare yourself ___ others. || with|to be|at || compare A with B = 拿 A 和 B 比較。 || 不要拿自己跟別人比。
complain || v. || 抱怨 || Stop complaining about the food. || 別再抱怨食物了。 || complain about=抱怨… || He always complains ___ the weather. || about|of to|for || complain about = 抱怨…。 || 他總是抱怨天氣。
complete || v./adj. || 完成；完整的 || Please complete the form. || 請填完這張表格。 || complete a task=完成任務 || The work must ___ completed by Friday. || be|is|been || 助動詞 must 後面接原形 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 這項工作必須在星期五前完成。
concern || n./v. || 擔心；關心 || Thank you for your concern. || 謝謝你的關心。 || be concerned about=擔心… || Parents are concerned ___ their children's health. || about|of|to || be concerned about = 擔心…。 || 父母擔心孩子的健康。
confident || adj. || 有信心的 || She is confident about the test. || 她對考試很有信心。 || be confident of=對…有信心 || I'm confident ___ I will pass the test. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句：be confident that… || 我有信心會通過考試。
confuse || v. || 使困惑 || The question confused me. || 這個問題把我搞糊塗了。 || confuse A with B=把 A 和 B 搞混 || I always confuse him ___ his twin brother. || with|to|for of || confuse A with B = 把 A 和 B 搞混。 || 我總是把他和他的雙胞胎弟弟搞混。
congratulation || n. || 恭喜 || Congratulations on your success! || 恭喜你成功！ || congratulations on=恭喜… || Congratulations ___ winning the contest! || on|for|at || congratulations on + 名詞/V-ing = 恭喜…。 || 恭喜你贏得比賽！
consider || v. || 考慮；認為 || I'm considering studying abroad. || 我在考慮出國讀書。 || consider + V-ing=考慮做… || I'm considering ___ a new phone. || buying|to buy|buy || consider 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我在考慮買一支新手機。
considerate || adj. || 體貼的 || She is very considerate of others. || 她很體貼別人。 || be considerate of=體貼… || It's considerate ___ you to help the old lady. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你幫助那位老太太真是體貼。
contact lens || n. || 隱形眼鏡 || She wears contact lenses. || 她戴隱形眼鏡。 || wear contact lenses=戴隱形眼鏡 || You should take out your contact lenses ___ you go to bed. || before|after|until || 睡覺「之前」拿下來，用 before。 || 你睡覺前應該把隱形眼鏡拿下來。
continue || v. || 繼續 || Please continue reading. || 請繼續讀。 || continue to V=繼續做… || Although it was raining, they continued ___ the game. || to play|played|play || continue to + 原形動詞（或 V-ing）。 || 雖然在下雨，他們繼續比賽。
contract || n. || 合約 || He signed a contract with the team. || 他和球隊簽了約。 || sign a contract=簽約 || The contract ___ signed by both sides last week. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這份合約上週由雙方簽署了。
