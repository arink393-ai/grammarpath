# 九年級 16：underpass – youth
underpass || n. || 地下道 || Use the underpass to cross the road. || 走地下道過馬路。 || pedestrian underpass=行人地下道 || It's safer to use the underpass ___ to cross the road directly. || than|then|that || 比較級 safer + than。 || 走地下道比直接穿越馬路安全。
underwear || n. || 內衣 || Pack enough underwear for the trip. || 旅行時要帶足夠的內衣。 || clean underwear=乾淨的內衣 || Don't forget ___ enough underwear. || to pack|packing|pack || forget to V = 忘記要去做。 || 別忘了帶足夠的內衣。
unique || adj. || 獨特的 || Everyone is unique. || 每個人都是獨一無二的。 || unique style=獨特的風格 || Everyone is unique ___ their own way. || in|on|at || in one's own way = 以自己的方式。 || 每個人都有自己獨特之處。
universe || n. || 宇宙 || The universe is very big. || 宇宙非常大。 || in the universe=在宇宙中 || Are we alone ___ the universe? || in|on|at || 在宇宙中用 in。 || 我們在宇宙中是孤單的嗎？
university || n. || 大學 || My brother goes to university. || 我哥哥讀大學。 || go to university=上大學 || My sister studies English ___ a university. || at|in|on || 在某所大學用 at。 || 我姊姊在一所大學讀英文。
upon || prep. || 在…之上 || Once upon a time, there was a king. || 從前從前，有一位國王。 || once upon a time=從前 || Once upon a time, there ___ a little girl. || was|were|is || 過去的 There be；a little girl 單數用 was。 || 從前從前，有一個小女孩。
upper || adj. || 上面的 || Put it on the upper shelf. || 把它放在上層的架子上。 || upper floor=樓上 || The books on the upper shelf ___ hard to reach. || are|is|be || The books 複數，用 are。 || 上層架子上的書很難拿到。
upstairs || adv. || 在樓上 || My room is upstairs. || 我的房間在樓上。 || go upstairs=上樓 || My room is upstairs, ___ my brother's room is downstairs. || and|so|or || 連接兩個並列的句子用 and（對比）。 || 我的房間在樓上，而我哥哥的房間在樓下。
usual || adj. || 平常的 || He came late as usual. || 他照例又遲到了。 || as usual=照常 || She got up at six ___ usual. || as|like|for || as usual = 照常。 || 她照常六點起床。
valley || n. || 山谷 || There is a river in the valley. || 山谷裡有一條河。 || in the valley=在山谷裡 || The village is ___ the valley. || in|on|at || 在山谷裡用 in。 || 那個村莊在山谷裡。
valuable || adj. || 貴重的；有價值的 || This ring is very valuable. || 這枚戒指非常貴重。 || valuable experience=寶貴的經驗 || This was a valuable experience ___ me. || for|to|of || 「對某人來說」用 for sb。 || 這對我來說是一次寶貴的經驗。
value || n. || 價值 || The value of friendship is great. || 友誼的價值很大。 || the value of=…的價值 || Don't forget the value ___ time. || of|for|to || the value of = …的價值。 || 別忘了時間的價值。
vendor || n. || 攤販 || The vendor sells fruit on the street. || 那位攤販在街上賣水果。 || street vendor=路邊攤販 || The vendor ___ fruit every morning at the market. || sells|sell|selling || The vendor 第三人稱單數，動詞加 s。 || 那位攤販每天早上在市場賣水果。
vest || n. || 背心 || He wore a vest over his shirt. || 他在襯衫外面穿了一件背心。 || life vest=救生衣 || You must wear a life vest ___ you go on the boat. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 上船時你必須穿救生衣。
victory || n. || 勝利 || Our team celebrated the victory. || 我們隊慶祝勝利。 || win a victory=獲勝 || The fans cheered ___ the team's victory. || for|to|at || cheer for = 為…歡呼。 || 球迷們為球隊的勝利歡呼。
village || n. || 村莊 || My grandparents live in a small village. || 我的祖父母住在一個小村莊。 || small village=小村莊 || My grandparents have lived in the village ___ fifty years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成式。 || 我的祖父母住在那個村莊五十年了。
vinegar || n. || 醋 || Add some vinegar to the soup. || 在湯裡加一點醋。 || black vinegar=烏醋 || Add a little vinegar ___ the noodles. || to|in|for || add A to B = 把 A 加到 B。 || 在麵裡加一點醋。
vocabulary || n. || 字彙 || Reading helps you build your vocabulary. || 閱讀幫助你增加字彙量。 || build vocabulary=增加字彙 || Reading can help you ___ your vocabulary. || build|builds|building || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 閱讀可以幫助你增加字彙量。
volleyball || n. || 排球 || We play volleyball in PE class. || 我們在體育課打排球。 || play volleyball=打排球 || There ___ six players on a volleyball team. || are|is|be || six players 複數，There are。 || 一支排球隊有六名球員。
vote || v./n. || 投票 || Who did you vote for? || 你投給誰？ || vote for=投票給… || Who did you vote ___ in the class election? || for|to|at || vote for = 投票給…。 || 班級選舉你投給誰？
waist || n. || 腰 || She wore a belt around her waist. || 她在腰上繫了一條皮帶。 || waist size=腰圍 || She tied a ribbon ___ her waist. || around|across|along || around = 環繞…。 || 她在腰間繫了一條緞帶。
war || n. || 戰爭 || Nobody wants war. || 沒有人想要戰爭。 || at war=處於戰爭中; World War II=第二次世界大戰 || Many people died ___ the war. || during|while|when || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 很多人在戰爭期間喪生。
waste || v./n. || 浪費 || Don't waste food. || 不要浪費食物。 || waste time=浪費時間; a waste of=…的浪費 || It's a waste ___ time to argue. || of|for|to || a waste of = …的浪費。 || 爭吵是浪費時間。
waterfalls || n. || 瀑布 || The waterfalls are beautiful. || 那些瀑布很美。 || Niagara Falls=尼加拉瀑布 || The waterfalls ___ one of the most popular tourist spots here. || are|is|be || 主詞 The waterfalls 複數，用 are。 || 這些瀑布是這裡最熱門的景點之一。
wedding || n. || 婚禮 || We went to my cousin's wedding. || 我們去參加表姊的婚禮。 || wedding dress=婚紗 || The wedding will ___ held in a church. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 婚禮將在教堂舉行。
weekday || n. || 平日 || I go to school on weekdays. || 我平日去上學。 || on weekdays=平日 || The museum is less crowded ___ weekdays. || on|in|at || on weekdays = 在平日。 || 博物館平日比較不擁擠。
weight || n. || 重量；體重 || What's your weight? || 你體重多少？ || lose weight=減重; gain weight=增重 || He is trying ___ lose weight. || to|for|of || try to + 原形動詞。 || 他正在努力減重。
whale || n. || 鯨魚 || The whale is the biggest animal in the world. || 鯨魚是世界上最大的動物。 || blue whale=藍鯨 || The blue whale is ___ biggest animal on Earth. || the|a|an || 最高級前面加 the。 || 藍鯨是地球上最大的動物。
wheel || n. || 輪子 || A bicycle has two wheels. || 腳踏車有兩個輪子。 || steering wheel=方向盤 || A car ___ four wheels. || has|have|is || A car 第三人稱單數，用 has。 || 汽車有四個輪子。
whole || adj. || 全部的 || I ate the whole cake. || 我吃掉了整個蛋糕。 || the whole day=一整天 || It rained the whole day, ___ we stayed home. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 下了一整天雨，所以我們待在家。
wild || adj. || 野生的 || Wild animals live in the forest. || 野生動物住在森林裡。 || wild animal=野生動物 || Wild animals should ___ protected. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 野生動物應該受到保護。
wing || n. || 翅膀 || The bird spread its wings. || 那隻鳥張開了翅膀。 || spread one's wings=展翅 || The bird hurt ___ wing. || its|it's|it || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 那隻鳥傷了牠的翅膀。
winner || n. || 優勝者 || The winner will get a prize. || 優勝者將獲得獎品。 || the winner of=…的優勝者 || The winner ___ the contest will get a trip to Japan. || of|in|for || the winner of = …的優勝者。 || 比賽的優勝者將獲得日本之旅。
wok || n. || 炒菜鍋 || Mom cooks with a wok. || 媽媽用炒菜鍋煮菜。 || Chinese wok=中式炒鍋 || Heat the oil ___ the wok. || in|on|at || 在鍋子裡用 in。 || 在炒鍋裡熱油。
wolf || n. || 狼 || The wolf howled at the moon. || 狼對著月亮嚎叫。 || wolves=狼（複數） || The boy who cried wolf ___ not believed by the villagers. || was|were|is || 被動語態；The boy 單數，過去用 was。 || 放羊的孩子不被村民相信。
women's room || n. || 女廁 || The women's room is on the right. || 女廁在右邊。 || women's room=女廁 || The women's room is ___ your right. || on|in|at || on your right = 在你的右邊。 || 女廁在你的右邊。
wood || n. || 木頭 || The table is made of wood. || 這張桌子是木頭做的。 || made of wood=木製的 || The table is made ___ wood. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這張桌子是木頭做的。
woods || n. || 樹林 || We went for a walk in the woods. || 我們去樹林裡散步。 || in the woods=在樹林裡 || Don't get lost ___ the woods. || in|on|at || 在樹林裡用 in。 || 別在樹林裡迷路了。
worm || n. || 蟲 || The early bird catches the worm. || 早起的鳥兒有蟲吃。 || earthworm=蚯蚓 || The early bird ___ the worm. || catches|catch|catching || The early bird 第三人稱單數，動詞加 es。 || 早起的鳥兒有蟲吃。
wound || n./v. || 傷口；使受傷 || He was wounded in the war. || 他在戰爭中受傷了。 || wound=傷口 || The soldier ___ wounded in the battle. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那名士兵在戰鬥中受傷了。
wrist || n. || 手腕 || She wears a watch on her wrist. || 她手腕上戴著一支錶。 || on one's wrist=在手腕上 || She wears a watch ___ her wrist. || on|in|at || 戴在手腕上用 on。 || 她手腕上戴著一支錶。
yell || v. || 大叫 || Don't yell at me. || 不要對我大吼。 || yell at=對…吼叫; yell for help=大聲呼救 || Don't yell ___ your little brother. || at|to|on || yell at sb = 對某人吼叫。 || 不要對你弟弟大吼。
youth || n. || 青春；年輕人 || In his youth, he was a good athlete. || 他年輕時是個好運動員。 || in one's youth=年輕時 || In my youth, I ___ to play basketball every day. || used|use|was used || used to + 原形動詞 = 過去常常…。 || 我年輕時每天都打籃球。
