# 學測 L3 17：slope – substance
slope || n. || 斜坡 || The car went down the slope. || 車子開下斜坡。 || steep slope=陡坡 || The children slid down the grassy slope ___ laughing. || while|during|since || while + V-ing（分詞構句）= 一邊…。 || 孩子們一邊笑一邊滑下長滿草的斜坡。
snap || v./n. || 啪地折斷；快照 || The branch snapped. || 樹枝啪地一聲斷了。 || snap one's fingers=彈指 || The branch snapped ___ the weight of the snow. || under|below|with || under the weight of = 在…的重壓下。 || 樹枝在積雪的重壓下啪地斷了。
solid || adj. || 固體的；堅固的 || Ice is solid water. || 冰是固態的水。 || solid food=固體食物 || When water freezes, it becomes ___ solid. || a|an|the || become a solid = 變成固體。 || 水結冰時就變成固體。
someday || adv. || 將來有一天 || I want to visit Paris someday. || 我希望有一天能去巴黎。 || someday=有一天 || I hope I ___ travel around the world someday. || can|could have|am || I hope + 子句；can + 原形動詞。 || 我希望有一天能環遊世界。
somehow || adv. || 不知怎麼地 || Somehow I lost my keys. || 不知怎麼地，我弄丟了鑰匙。 || somehow=不知怎地 || Somehow, the cat managed ___ out of the house. || to get|getting|get || manage to + 原形動詞 = 設法做到…。 || 不知怎麼地，那隻貓設法跑出了屋子。
sometime || adv. || 某個時候 || Let's have lunch sometime. || 找個時間一起吃午餐吧。 || sometime next week=下週某個時候 || Let's meet sometime ___ week. || next|the next of|later || next week = 下週。 || 我們下週找個時間見面吧。
sorrow || n. || 悲傷 || She felt deep sorrow. || 她感到深深的悲傷。 || in sorrow=悲傷地 || He tried to hide his sorrow ___ a smile. || with|by|in || with + 名詞 = 用…。 || 他試著用微笑掩飾悲傷。
spaghetti || n. || 義大利麵 || I had spaghetti for lunch. || 我午餐吃義大利麵。 || spaghetti with tomato sauce=番茄義大利麵 || This restaurant serves the ___ spaghetti in town. || best|better|good || 最高級 the best（in town 表範圍）。 || 這家餐廳的義大利麵是鎮上最好吃的。
specific || adj. || 具體的；特定的 || Can you be more specific? || 你可以說得更具體一點嗎？ || specific reason=具體的理由 || Please give me a more specific answer ___ I can understand. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 請給我更具體的答案，這樣我才能理解。
spice || n. || 香料 || Indian food uses a lot of spices. || 印度料理使用很多香料。 || add spice to=增添趣味 || Too many spices can cover the natural taste ___ the food. || of|for|to || the taste of = …的味道。 || 太多香料會掩蓋食物的天然味道。
spill || v./n. || 灑出 || I spilled coffee on my shirt. || 我把咖啡灑在襯衫上。 || spill the beans=洩漏祕密 || I spilled coffee on my shirt, ___ I had to change it. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 我把咖啡灑在襯衫上，所以得換一件。
spin || v./n. || 旋轉 || The dancer spins on stage. || 舞者在台上旋轉。 || spin around=轉圈 || The Earth spins ___ its axis. || on|in|at || spin on its axis = 繞地軸自轉。 || 地球繞著地軸自轉。
spinach || n. || 菠菜 || Spinach is good for you. || 菠菜對你有益。 || fresh spinach=新鮮菠菜 || Spinach is rich ___ iron. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 菠菜富含鐵質。
spit || v. || 吐（口水） || Don't spit on the street. || 不要在街上吐痰。 || spit out=吐出 || People who spit on the street should ___ fined. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 在街上吐痰的人應該被罰款。
spite || n. || 惡意；儘管 || In spite of the rain, we went out. || 儘管下雨，我們還是出去了。 || in spite of=儘管 || In spite ___ the heavy rain, the game continued. || of|for|to || in spite of + 名詞 = 儘管…。 || 儘管下著大雨，比賽仍繼續進行。
splash || v./n. || 濺起 || The kids splashed water on each other. || 孩子們互相潑水。 || make a splash=引起轟動 || The car splashed water ___ me as it drove past. || on|at|to || splash sth on sb = 把某物濺到某人身上。 || 那輛車經過時把水濺到我身上。
spoil || v. || 寵壞；破壞 || Don't spoil your children. || 不要寵壞你的孩子。 || spoil the fun=掃興 || The rain spoiled our picnic, ___ we had to go home early. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 雨破壞了我們的野餐，所以我們得早點回家。
spray || v./n. || 噴灑 || Spray some water on the plants. || 在植物上噴一些水。 || hair spray=髮膠 || The farmer sprayed water ___ the plants. || on|in|at || spray A on B = 把 A 噴在 B 上。 || 農夫在植物上噴水。
spy || n./v. || 間諜 || He was a spy during the war. || 他在戰爭期間是個間諜。 || spy movie=間諜片 || The spy ___ caught while he was stealing secret files. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那名間諜在偷機密文件時被抓到了。
squeeze || v. || 擠壓 || Squeeze the lemon. || 擠檸檬汁。 || squeeze out=擠出 || Squeeze some lemon juice ___ the fish. || onto|of|at || squeeze A onto B = 把 A 擠到 B 上。 || 在魚上擠一些檸檬汁。
squirrel || n. || 松鼠 || A squirrel ran up the tree. || 一隻松鼠跑上了樹。 || squirrel=松鼠 || Squirrels collect nuts ___ they can eat them in winter. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 松鼠收集堅果，這樣冬天就有東西吃。
stable || adj. || 穩定的 || He has a stable job. || 他有一份穩定的工作。 || stable job=穩定的工作 || Make sure the ladder is stable ___ you climb it. || before|after|since || 爬「之前」確認，用 before。 || 爬梯子之前確認它是穩固的。
stadium || n. || 體育場 || The game was held in a big stadium. || 比賽在一座大型體育場舉行。 || baseball stadium=棒球場 || The stadium can hold ___ to 50,000 people. || up|over|of || up to = 多達…。 || 這座體育場最多可容納五萬人。
staff || n. || 員工（總稱） || The hotel staff are very friendly. || 飯店員工都很友善。 || staff member=員工 || The hotel staff helped us carry our bags ___ our room. || to|at|in || carry sth to + 地點 = 把某物搬到…。 || 飯店員工幫我們把行李搬到房間。
stale || adj. || 不新鮮的 || The bread is stale. || 麵包不新鮮了。 || stale bread=不新鮮的麵包 || The bread became stale ___ it was left out for three days. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 麵包變得不新鮮了，因為放在外面三天了。
stare || v. || 凝視 || Don't stare at people. || 不要盯著別人看。 || stare at=盯著看 || It's rude to stare ___ strangers. || at|to|on || stare at = 盯著…看。 || 盯著陌生人看是不禮貌的。
starve || v. || 挨餓 || Many people starved during the war. || 戰爭期間很多人挨餓。 || starve to death=餓死 || Many people starved ___ the war. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 戰爭期間很多人挨餓。
statue || n. || 雕像 || There is a statue in the park. || 公園裡有一座雕像。 || the Statue of Liberty=自由女神像 || The statue ___ made of bronze more than 100 years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這座雕像是一百多年前用青銅製成的。
steady || adj. || 穩定的 || He walked at a steady pace. || 他以穩定的步伐行走。 || slow and steady=穩紮穩打 || Slow and steady wins the ___. || race|races|racing || 諺語：Slow and steady wins the race.（穩紮穩打才能贏）。 || 穩紮穩打才能贏。
steal || v. || 偷 || It's wrong to steal. || 偷竊是不對的。 || steal sth from sb=從某人那裡偷東西 || Someone tried to steal my bike, but the police ___ him. || caught|catch|catches || 描述過去的事用過去式 caught。 || 有人想偷我的腳踏車，但被警察抓到了。
steam || n./v. || 蒸氣；蒸 || Steam rose from the pot. || 蒸氣從鍋子升起。 || steamed buns=包子、饅頭 || Steam is produced ___ water boils. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 水沸騰時會產生蒸氣。
steep || adj. || 陡峭的 || The mountain path is steep. || 山路很陡。 || steep hill=陡坡 || The hill was ___ steep for the old man to climb. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 那座山坡太陡，老先生爬不上去。
sticky || adj. || 黏的 || My hands are sticky. || 我的手黏黏的。 || sticky rice=糯米 || My hands got sticky ___ I ate the candy. || after|before|until || 吃糖「之後」手變黏，用 after。 || 我吃了糖之後手黏黏的。
stiff || adj. || 僵硬的 || My neck is stiff. || 我的脖子很僵硬。 || stiff neck=落枕 || My shoulders feel stiff ___ sitting at the computer all day. || after|before|since || after + V-ing = 在…之後。 || 在電腦前坐了一整天後，我的肩膀很僵硬。
sting || v./n. || 叮；螫 || Bees sting when they feel danger. || 蜜蜂感到危險時會螫人。 || bee sting=蜂螫 || Be careful. Bees can sting you ___ you touch their nest. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 小心，如果你碰到蜂巢，蜜蜂可能會螫你。
stir || v. || 攪拌 || Stir the soup. || 攪拌湯。 || stir-fry=快炒 || Stir the soup slowly ___ it doesn't burn. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便（不會）…。 || 慢慢攪拌湯，才不會燒焦。
stitch || n./v. || 縫線；針 || He needed five stitches. || 他縫了五針。 || a stitch in time saves nine=及時一針省九針 || A stitch in time ___ nine. || saves|save|saving || 諺語；主詞 A stitch 單數，動詞加 s。 || 及時一針省九針（小洞不補，大洞吃苦）。
stomach || n. || 胃 || My stomach hurts. || 我的胃好痛。 || stomachache=胃痛 || Don't take this medicine on an empty stomach, ___ it may hurt your stomach. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 不要空腹吃這個藥，否則可能會傷胃。
stool || n. || 凳子 || He sat on a stool. || 他坐在凳子上。 || bar stool=吧台椅 || He stood ___ a stool to reach the top shelf. || on|in|at || 站在凳子上用 on。 || 他站在凳子上拿最上層的架子。
stormy || adj. || 暴風雨的 || It was a stormy night. || 那是一個暴風雨的夜晚。 || stormy weather=暴風雨天氣 || All flights were canceled ___ the stormy weather. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 所有航班因暴風雨天氣取消。
stove || n. || 爐子 || Mom is cooking on the stove. || 媽媽在爐子上煮菜。 || gas stove=瓦斯爐 || Don't leave the kitchen ___ something is cooking on the stove. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 爐子上在煮東西時不要離開廚房。
strategy || n. || 策略 || We need a new strategy. || 我們需要新的策略。 || learning strategy=學習策略 || The coach explained the strategy ___ the game. || before|after|since || 比賽「之前」解說，用 before。 || 教練在比賽前說明了策略。
straw || n. || 吸管；稻草 || Can I have a straw? || 可以給我一根吸管嗎？ || plastic straw=塑膠吸管; the last straw=最後一根稻草 || Many restaurants no longer give out plastic straws ___ protect the environment. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 許多餐廳為了保護環境不再提供塑膠吸管。
strength || n. || 力量；優點 || He doesn't have the strength to lift it. || 他沒有力氣舉起它。 || strengths and weaknesses=優缺點 || Everyone has ___ own strengths and weaknesses. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 strengths 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 每個人都有自己的優缺點。
strip || n./v. || 條；剝去 || Cut the paper into strips. || 把紙剪成長條。 || a strip of=一條… || Cut the paper ___ long strips. || into|in|to || cut A into B = 把 A 切成 B。 || 把紙剪成長條。
structure || n. || 結構 || The structure of the building is strong. || 這棟建築的結構很堅固。 || sentence structure=句子結構 || The structure of the bridge ___ checked every year. || is|are|be || 被動語態；The structure 單數用 is + p.p.。 || 這座橋的結構每年都會檢查。
stubborn || adj. || 固執的 || He is as stubborn as a mule. || 他像騾子一樣固執。 || stubborn person=固執的人 || He is too stubborn ___ listen to anyone. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不會…。 || 他太固執了，不聽任何人的話。
studio || n. || 工作室；攝影棚 || The singer recorded a song in the studio. || 那位歌手在錄音室錄了一首歌。 || recording studio=錄音室 || The new album ___ recorded in a studio in Tokyo. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 新專輯是在東京的一間錄音室錄製的。
stuff || n./v. || 東西；塞 || I have a lot of stuff to do. || 我有很多事要做。 || stuffed animal=填充玩偶 || There is too much stuff ___ my room. || in|on|at || 在房間裡用 in。 || 我的房間裡東西太多了。
substance || n. || 物質 || Water is a liquid substance. || 水是一種液態物質。 || harmful substance=有害物質 || Some substances ___ in cigarettes can cause cancer. || found|finding|finds || 過去分詞 found 表被動（在…中被發現的）。 || 香菸中含有的某些物質會致癌。
