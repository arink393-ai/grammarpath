# 學測 L3 13：pancake – portrait
pancake || n. || 鬆餅 || I had pancakes for breakfast. || 我早餐吃了鬆餅。 || pancake with syrup=淋糖漿的鬆餅 || She put some honey ___ her pancakes. || on|in|at || 淋在鬆餅上用 on。 || 她在鬆餅上淋了一些蜂蜜。
panic || n./v. || 驚慌 || Don't panic! || 別驚慌！ || in a panic=驚慌地 || People ran out of the building ___ a panic. || in|with|on || in a panic = 驚慌地。 || 人們驚慌地跑出大樓。
parade || n. || 遊行 || We watched the parade on the street. || 我們在街上看遊行。 || hold a parade=舉行遊行 || Thousands of people watched the parade, ___ lasted for three hours. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 數千人觀看了這場持續三小時的遊行。
paradise || n. || 天堂 || This island is a paradise. || 這座島是個天堂。 || shopping paradise=購物天堂 || Taipei is a paradise ___ food lovers. || for|to|of || a paradise for sb = 某人的天堂。 || 台北是美食愛好者的天堂。
parcel || n. || 包裹 || I received a parcel from my aunt. || 我收到阿姨寄來的包裹。 || send a parcel=寄包裹 || The parcel ___ delivered yesterday afternoon. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 包裹昨天下午送達了。
parrot || n. || 鸚鵡 || The parrot can say "hello." || 那隻鸚鵡會說「哈囉」。 || talking parrot=會說話的鸚鵡 || The parrot can repeat everything ___ it hears. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾 everything。 || 那隻鸚鵡能重複牠聽到的每一句話。
passage || n. || 一段文字；通道 || Read the passage and answer the questions. || 閱讀文章並回答問題。 || reading passage=閱讀文章 || Read the passage carefully ___ answering the questions. || before|after|until || 回答問題「之前」仔細閱讀，用 before。 || 回答問題前仔細閱讀文章。
passenger || n. || 乘客 || All passengers must wear seat belts. || 所有乘客都必須繫安全帶。 || passenger seat=副駕駛座 || All passengers are required ___ their seat belts. || to fasten|fastening|fasten || be required to + 原形動詞。 || 所有乘客都必須繫好安全帶。
passion || n. || 熱情 || She has a passion for music. || 她對音樂充滿熱情。 || have a passion for=對…有熱情 || He has a strong passion ___ cooking. || for|of|to || a passion for = 對…的熱情。 || 他對烹飪非常熱衷。
passport || n. || 護照 || Don't forget your passport. || 別忘了你的護照。 || apply for a passport=申請護照 || You need a passport ___ you travel abroad. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 你出國旅行時需要護照。
pat || v./n. || 輕拍 || He patted me on the back. || 他拍拍我的背。 || pat sb on the back=拍某人的背（讚許） || The coach patted him ___ the shoulder. || on|in|at || pat sb on + 身體部位 = 輕拍某人的…。 || 教練拍了拍他的肩膀。
patience || n. || 耐心 || Teaching requires patience. || 教學需要耐心。 || lose patience=失去耐心 || She has a lot of patience ___ young children. || with|to|for || patience with sb = 對某人有耐心。 || 她對小孩很有耐心。
pause || n./v. || 暫停 || She paused before answering. || 她回答前停頓了一下。 || without a pause=毫不停頓 || He paused for a moment ___ answering the question. || before|after|until || 回答「之前」停頓，用 before。 || 他在回答問題之前停頓了一下。
pea || n. || 豌豆 || I don't like peas. || 我不喜歡豌豆。 || green peas=青豆; like two peas in a pod=長得一模一樣 || The twins are like two peas ___ a pod. || in|on|at || like two peas in a pod = 一模一樣。 || 那對雙胞胎長得一模一樣。
peanut || n. || 花生 || He is allergic to peanuts. || 他對花生過敏。 || peanut butter=花生醬 || Some people are allergic ___ peanuts. || to|with|of || be allergic to = 對…過敏。 || 有些人對花生過敏。
pearl || n. || 珍珠 || She is wearing a pearl necklace. || 她戴著珍珠項鍊。 || pearl milk tea=珍珠奶茶 || Pearl milk tea is a drink ___ was invented in Taiwan. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a drink）。 || 珍珠奶茶是一種在台灣發明的飲料。
peel || v./n. || 剝皮 || Peel the orange before eating it. || 吃柳丁前先剝皮。 || banana peel=香蕉皮 || Peel the potatoes ___ you cook them. || before|after|since || 煮「之前」先削皮，用 before。 || 煮馬鈴薯之前先削皮。
penguin || n. || 企鵝 || Penguins can't fly. || 企鵝不會飛。 || emperor penguin=皇帝企鵝 || Penguins can't fly, ___ they can swim very well. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 企鵝不會飛，但很會游泳。
penny || n. || 一分錢 || I don't have a penny. || 我身無分文。 || a penny saved is a penny earned=省一分是一分 || A penny saved is a penny ___. || earned|earn|earning || 諺語，過去分詞 earned 表被動；與 saved 對應。 || 省一分就是賺一分。
pepper || n. || 胡椒；甜椒 || Add some salt and pepper. || 加點鹽和胡椒。 || black pepper=黑胡椒; green pepper=青椒 || Would you like some pepper ___ your soup? || in|on|at || 加在湯裡用 in。 || 你的湯要加點胡椒嗎？
perform || v. || 表演；執行 || The band performed on stage. || 樂團在舞台上表演。 || perform well=表現良好 || The students performed ___ well that the audience clapped for a long time. || so|such|too || so + 副詞 + that…。 || 學生們表演得非常好，觀眾鼓掌了很久。
performance || n. || 表演；表現 || Her performance was amazing. || 她的表演很精彩。 || give a performance=演出 || Everyone was impressed ___ her performance. || by|with|at || be impressed by = 對…印象深刻。 || 大家都對她的表演印象深刻。
permission || n. || 許可 || You need permission to enter. || 你需要許可才能進入。 || ask for permission=請求許可 || You need to ask for permission ___ you use the lab. || before|after|since || 使用「之前」申請許可，用 before。 || 使用實驗室之前你需要申請許可。
permit || v./n. || 允許；許可證 || Smoking is not permitted here. || 這裡禁止吸菸。 || work permit=工作許可證 || Students are not permitted ___ phones during the exam. || to use|using|use || be permitted to + 原形動詞。 || 考試期間學生不准使用手機。
persuade || v. || 說服 || She persuaded me to join the club. || 她說服我加入社團。 || persuade sb to V=說服某人做… || My friend persuaded me ___ the English club. || to join|joining|join || persuade sb to + 原形動詞。 || 我朋友說服我加入英文社。
photographer || n. || 攝影師 || He is a professional photographer. || 他是職業攝影師。 || wedding photographer=婚禮攝影師 || The photographer asked everyone ___. || to smile|smiling|smile || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 攝影師請大家微笑。
pigeon || n. || 鴿子 || Pigeons are in the park. || 公園裡有鴿子。 || homing pigeon=信鴿 || In the past, pigeons ___ used to carry messages. || were|was|are || 被動語態；pigeons 複數，過去用 were。 || 過去鴿子被用來傳遞訊息。
pile || n./v. || 一堆 || There is a pile of books on the desk. || 桌上有一堆書。 || a pile of=一堆… || A pile of dirty dishes ___ in the sink. || is|are|be || a pile of + 複數名詞當主詞，看 pile 用 is。 || 水槽裡有一堆髒碗盤。
pill || n. || 藥丸 || Take two pills after meals. || 飯後吃兩顆藥丸。 || sleeping pill=安眠藥 || Take one pill three times ___ day. || a|an|the || 每天幾次用 a day。 || 一天三次，每次一顆。
pilot || n. || 飛行員 || She wants to be a pilot. || 她想當飛行員。 || airline pilot=航空公司機師 || The pilot, ___ has flown for 20 years, landed the plane safely. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 飛行資歷二十年的機師安全地降落了飛機。
pine || n. || 松樹 || There are many pine trees on the mountain. || 山上有很多松樹。 || pine tree=松樹 || The mountain is covered ___ pine trees. || with|by|of || be covered with = 覆蓋著…。 || 山上覆蓋著松樹。
pineapple || n. || 鳳梨 || Pineapple cakes are a popular gift. || 鳳梨酥是受歡迎的伴手禮。 || pineapple cake=鳳梨酥 || Pineapple cakes are one of the most popular gifts ___ tourists buy in Taiwan. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 buy 的受詞。 || 鳳梨酥是遊客在台灣最常買的伴手禮之一。
pint || n. || 品脫 || He drank a pint of milk. || 他喝了一品脫的牛奶。 || a pint of=一品脫… || A pint ___ about half a liter. || is|are|be || A pint 單數，用 is。 || 一品脫大約是半公升。
pit || n. || 坑；果核 || Don't swallow the cherry pit. || 別吞下櫻桃籽。 || fire pit=火坑 || The workers dug a deep pit ___ the ground. || in|on|at || 在地面「裡面」挖坑用 in。 || 工人在地上挖了一個深坑。
pitch || n./v. || 投（球）；音調 || He pitched the ball fast. || 他快速地投出球。 || pitch a tent=搭帳篷 || We pitched our tent ___ the lake. || near|nearly|nearby of || near + 地點 = 在…附近。 || 我們在湖邊搭了帳篷。
pity || n./v. || 可惜；同情 || It's a pity that you can't come. || 可惜你不能來。 || what a pity=真可惜; feel pity for=同情 || It's a pity ___ you missed the concert. || that|what|which || It's a pity that… = …真可惜。 || 你錯過了演唱會真可惜。
plastic || n./adj. || 塑膠 || Plastic bags are bad for the environment. || 塑膠袋對環境有害。 || plastic bag=塑膠袋 || Plastic bags take hundreds of years ___ break down. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V（此處主詞為 plastic bags）。 || 塑膠袋需要數百年才能分解。
playful || adj. || 愛玩的 || The puppy is very playful. || 那隻小狗很愛玩。 || playful puppy=頑皮的小狗 || The puppy is ___ playful that it never sits still. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那隻小狗愛玩到從不安靜坐著。
plenty || pron. || 大量 || We have plenty of time. || 我們有充足的時間。 || plenty of=大量的… || There is plenty ___ food for everyone. || of|for|with || plenty of = 大量的…。 || 食物很多，足夠每個人吃。
plug || n./v. || 插頭；插上 || Plug in the computer. || 把電腦插上電。 || plug in=插電 || Don't forget to plug in the charger ___ you go to bed. || before|after|since || 睡覺「之前」插上充電器，用 before。 || 睡覺前別忘了插上充電器。
pole || n. || 竿；極 || The flag is on the pole. || 旗子在旗竿上。 || North Pole=北極; flag pole=旗竿 || Polar bears live near the North Pole, ___ is very cold. || which|where|what || 非限定關係子句修飾地點並當主詞，用 which。 || 北極熊住在非常寒冷的北極附近。
political || adj. || 政治的 || They talked about political issues. || 他們談論政治議題。 || political party=政黨 || There are many political parties ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 台灣有許多政黨。
politician || n. || 政治人物 || The politician gave a speech. || 那位政治人物發表了演說。 || local politician=地方政治人物 || The politician promised ___ taxes. || to lower|lowering|lower || promise to + 原形動詞。 || 那位政治人物承諾要降稅。
politics || n. || 政治 || I'm not interested in politics. || 我對政治不感興趣。 || talk politics=談論政治 || Politics ___ a topic that many people avoid at dinner. || is|are|be || politics 視為單數，用 is。 || 政治是很多人在飯桌上避談的話題。
poll || n. || 民意調查 || The poll shows most people agree. || 民調顯示大多數人同意。 || opinion poll=民意調查 || According ___ the poll, most people support the plan. || to|with|for || according to = 根據…。 || 根據民調，大多數人支持這個計畫。
pollute || v. || 污染 || Factories pollute the air. || 工廠污染空氣。 || pollute the river=污染河川 || The river ___ polluted by waste from the factory. || has been|have been|is being be || 現在完成式被動；The river 單數用 has been。 || 這條河已經被工廠的廢水污染了。
pollution || n. || 污染 || Air pollution is getting worse. || 空氣污染越來越嚴重。 || air pollution=空氣污染 || Air pollution is getting ___ in many big cities. || worse|bad|worst || get + 比較級 = 變得更…。 || 許多大城市的空氣污染越來越嚴重。
porcelain || n. || 瓷器 || This vase is made of porcelain. || 這個花瓶是瓷做的。 || Chinese porcelain=中國瓷器 || This porcelain vase ___ over 500 years old. || is|are|has || This porcelain vase 單數，用 is。 || 這個瓷花瓶有五百多年歷史。
portion || n. || 一份；部分 || The portions here are big. || 這裡的份量很大。 || a portion of=一部分… || A large portion of the money ___ given to charity. || was|were|is being || a portion of + 不可數名詞，動詞用單數；過去被動用 was。 || 那筆錢的一大部分被捐給了慈善機構。
portrait || n. || 肖像 || She painted a portrait of her mother. || 她畫了一幅媽媽的肖像。 || self-portrait=自畫像 || The portrait ___ painted by a famous artist. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這幅肖像是由一位知名藝術家畫的。
