# 學測 L3 03：budget – client
budget || n./v. || 預算 || We have a small budget for the trip. || 我們這趟旅行的預算不多。 || on a budget=預算有限; budget for=為…編預算 || We need to travel ___ a tight budget this year. || on|in|with || on a budget = 在有限預算下。 || 我們今年需要在預算吃緊的情況下旅行。
buffalo || n. || 水牛 || The farmer uses a buffalo to plow the field. || 農夫用水牛犁田。 || water buffalo=水牛 || In the past, buffaloes ___ used to plow rice fields. || were|was|are || 被動語態；buffaloes 複數，過去用 were。 || 過去水牛被用來犁稻田。
buffet || n. || 自助餐 || The hotel offers a breakfast buffet. || 飯店提供自助早餐。 || buffet dinner=自助晚餐 || At a buffet, you can eat as much as you ___. || like|liked|to like || as much as you like = 你想吃多少就吃多少。 || 在自助餐，你想吃多少就吃多少。
bulb || n. || 燈泡 || The bulb in the kitchen is broken. || 廚房的燈泡壞了。 || light bulb=燈泡 || The light bulb ___ by Edison in 1879. || was improved|improved|has improved || 被動語態：燈泡「被」改良，過去用 was + p.p.。 || 電燈泡在 1879 年由愛迪生改良。
bull || n. || 公牛 || The bull is very strong. || 那頭公牛非常強壯。 || bull market=牛市 || The bull ran ___ the red cloth. || toward|to forward|at of || run toward = 朝…跑去。 || 那頭公牛朝紅布衝過去。
bullet || n. || 子彈 || The bullet hit the wall. || 子彈打中了牆壁。 || bullet train=子彈列車 || The bullet train, ___ travels at 300 km/h, is very convenient. || which|who|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 時速三百公里的子彈列車非常方便。
bump || v./n. || 撞；碰 || I bumped my head on the door. || 我的頭撞到門。 || bump into=撞到、巧遇 || I bumped ___ an old friend at the station yesterday. || into|on|to || bump into = 巧遇、撞到。 || 我昨天在車站巧遇一位老朋友。
bunch || n. || 一串；一束 || He gave her a bunch of flowers. || 他送她一束花。 || a bunch of=一串／束… || A bunch of bananas ___ on the table. || is|are|be || a bunch of + 複數名詞當主詞，看 bunch，用單數 is。 || 桌上有一串香蕉。
bundle || n. || 捆 || She carried a bundle of clothes. || 她提著一捆衣服。 || a bundle of=一捆… || A bundle of old newspapers ___ left at the door. || was|were|is being || a bundle of 當主詞看 bundle，過去被動用 was + p.p.。 || 門口放著一捆舊報紙。
bury || v. || 埋葬；埋 || Dogs like to bury bones in the yard. || 狗喜歡把骨頭埋在院子裡。 || bury treasure=埋寶藏 || The pirates decided to bury the treasure ___ nobody could find it. || where|which|what || 關係副詞 where 引導地點子句。 || 海盜們決定把寶藏埋在沒人找得到的地方。
bush || n. || 灌木 || The cat is hiding in the bush. || 貓躲在灌木叢裡。 || beat around the bush=拐彎抹角 || Stop beating around the bush and tell me ___ happened. || what|which|that || 名詞子句 what happened = 發生了什麼事。 || 別拐彎抹角了，告訴我發生了什麼事。
buzz || n./v. || 嗡嗡聲 || I heard the buzz of a bee. || 我聽到蜜蜂的嗡嗡聲。 || buzz around=嗡嗡地飛來飛去 || A bee ___ buzzing around the flowers. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；A bee 用 is。 || 一隻蜜蜂在花叢間嗡嗡地飛。
cabin || n. || 小木屋；船艙 || We stayed in a cabin in the mountains. || 我們住在山上的小木屋。 || log cabin=小木屋 || The cabin ___ we stayed in had no electricity. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當受詞。 || 我們住的那間小木屋沒有電。
cable || n. || 電纜 || The cable is broken. || 電纜斷了。 || cable car=纜車; cable TV=有線電視 || Taking the cable car is the ___ way to get to the top. || easiest|easier|easy || 三者以上比較用最高級 the easiest。 || 搭纜車是上山頂最容易的方式。
cafeteria || n. || 自助餐廳；學生餐廳 || We eat lunch in the cafeteria. || 我們在學生餐廳吃午餐。 || school cafeteria=學生餐廳 || The cafeteria is ___ crowded at noon that it's hard to find a seat. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 中午學生餐廳擠到很難找到位子。
campus || n. || 校園 || The university has a beautiful campus. || 這所大學有美麗的校園。 || on campus=在校園內 || Smoking is not allowed ___ campus. || on|in|at of || on campus = 在校園內。 || 校園內禁止吸菸。
canyon || n. || 峽谷 || The Grand Canyon is in the U.S. || 大峽谷在美國。 || the Grand Canyon=大峽谷 || The Grand Canyon, ___ is over 400 kilometers long, attracts millions of visitors. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 長達四百多公里的大峽谷吸引了數百萬名遊客。
capable || adj. || 有能力的 || She is capable of doing the job. || 她有能力做這份工作。 || be capable of=有能力… || He is capable ___ solving difficult problems. || of|to|for || be capable of + V-ing = 有能力做…。 || 他有能力解決困難的問題。
captain || n. || 隊長；船長 || He was chosen as captain of the team. || 他被選為隊長。 || team captain=隊長 || The captain ordered everyone ___ the ship. || to leave|leaving|leave || order sb to + 原形動詞。 || 船長命令所有人離開船。
capture || v./n. || 捕獲；捕捉 || The police captured the thief. || 警方抓到了小偷。 || capture the moment=捕捉瞬間 || The photo captured the moment ___ the team won. || when|which|what || 關係副詞 when 修飾時間名詞 the moment。 || 這張照片捕捉到球隊獲勝的那一刻。
career || n. || 職業；生涯 || She had a long career as a teacher. || 她擔任教職很長一段時間。 || career choice=職業選擇 || He is considering a career ___ medicine. || in|of|at || a career in + 領域 = 在…領域的職業。 || 他正在考慮從事醫學相關的職業。
carpenter || n. || 木匠 || The carpenter made a wooden table. || 木匠做了一張木桌。 || skilled carpenter=熟練的木匠 || The table ___ made by a carpenter in our village. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這張桌子是我們村裡一位木匠做的。
carpet || n. || 地毯 || The carpet is soft. || 地毯很柔軟。 || red carpet=紅地毯 || The movie stars walked ___ the red carpet. || on|in|at || walk on = 走在…上面。 || 電影明星們走上紅地毯。
carriage || n. || 馬車；車廂 || The princess rode in a carriage. || 公主坐在馬車裡。 || horse carriage=馬車 || Cinderella went to the ball ___ a golden carriage. || in|on|by || 坐在馬車裡用 in。 || 灰姑娘坐著金色的馬車去參加舞會。
cart || n. || 手推車 || Put the groceries in the cart. || 把雜貨放進推車裡。 || shopping cart=購物推車 || He pushed a shopping cart ___ with food. || filled|filling|fills || 過去分詞 filled 表被動（被裝滿的推車）。 || 他推著一台裝滿食物的購物車。
cast || v./n. || 投；演員陣容 || The movie has a great cast. || 這部電影卡司很強。 || cast a shadow=投下陰影 || The tall tree cast a long shadow ___ the ground. || on|in|at || 投射在地面上用 on。 || 高大的樹在地上投下長長的影子。
casual || adj. || 休閒的；隨意的 || We wear casual clothes on Fridays. || 我們星期五穿便服。 || casual clothes=便服 || You can wear casual clothes ___ you like. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你喜歡，可以穿便服。
cattle || n. || 牛（總稱） || The farmer raises cattle. || 那位農夫養牛。 || dairy cattle=乳牛 || Cattle ___ raised on this farm for milk. || are|is|was || cattle 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 這座農場養牛是為了產牛奶。
cave || n. || 洞穴 || Bats live in caves. || 蝙蝠住在洞穴裡。 || cave man=穴居人 || The cave ___ we explored was dark and cold. || that|who|where || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 explored 的受詞。 || 我們探險的那個洞穴又暗又冷。
champion || n. || 冠軍 || She is the world champion. || 她是世界冠軍。 || world champion=世界冠軍 || The champion was given a gold medal ___ the end of the game. || at|in|on || at the end of = 在…結束時。 || 比賽結束時，冠軍獲頒一面金牌。
charm || n./v. || 魅力 || The old town has its own charm. || 這座老城有它獨特的魅力。 || lucky charm=幸運符 || Everyone was charmed ___ her smile. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 大家都被她的笑容迷住了。
chat || v./n. || 聊天 || We chatted for hours. || 我們聊了好幾個小時。 || chat with=和…聊天 || We chatted ___ hours last night. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 我們昨晚聊了好幾個小時。
cheek || n. || 臉頰 || She kissed him on the cheek. || 她親了他的臉頰。 || rosy cheeks=紅潤的臉頰 || She kissed her baby ___ the cheek. || on|in|at || kiss sb on the cheek = 親某人的臉頰。 || 她親了寶寶的臉頰。
cheerful || adj. || 愉快的 || She is always cheerful. || 她總是很開朗。 || cheerful smile=開朗的笑容 || No matter ___ happens, she stays cheerful. || what|which|how || no matter what = 無論發生什麼事。 || 無論發生什麼事，她都保持開朗。
cherry || n. || 櫻桃 || Cherry blossoms bloom in spring. || 櫻花在春天盛開。 || cherry blossom=櫻花 || Many people go to Japan ___ see the cherry blossoms. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 很多人去日本賞櫻。
chest || n. || 胸部；箱子 || He has a pain in his chest. || 他胸口痛。 || treasure chest=寶箱; chest pain=胸痛 || The treasure chest was found ___ the bottom of the sea. || at|in|on || at the bottom of = 在…的底部。 || 寶箱在海底被發現。
chill || n./v. || 寒意；冷卻 || There is a chill in the air. || 空氣中有股寒意。 || chill out=放鬆 || Put the drinks in the fridge ___ chill them. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 把飲料放進冰箱冰涼。
chilly || adj. || 寒冷的 || It's chilly this morning. || 今天早上很冷。 || chilly weather=寒冷的天氣 || It's chilly outside, so you'd better ___ a jacket. || wear|to wear|wearing || had better + 原形動詞 = 最好…。 || 外面很冷，你最好穿件外套。
chimney || n. || 煙囪 || Smoke came out of the chimney. || 煙從煙囪冒出來。 || factory chimney=工廠煙囪 || Santa Claus is said ___ down the chimney. || to come|coming|come || be said to + 原形動詞 = 據說…。 || 據說聖誕老人會從煙囪下來。
chin || n. || 下巴 || He rested his chin on his hand. || 他用手托著下巴。 || keep one's chin up=打起精神 || Keep your chin up. Things ___ better soon. || will get|get|got || 表示將會發生用 will + 原形動詞。 || 打起精神，事情很快就會好轉。
chip || n. || 碎片；洋芋片；晶片 || I like potato chips. || 我喜歡洋芋片。 || potato chips=洋芋片; computer chip=電腦晶片 || Taiwan is famous ___ making computer chips. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 台灣以製造電腦晶片聞名。
chop || v. || 砍；切 || Chop the onions into small pieces. || 把洋蔥切成小塊。 || chop down=砍倒; chopsticks=筷子 || Chop the carrots ___ small pieces. || into|in|to || chop A into B = 把 A 切成 B。 || 把紅蘿蔔切成小塊。
cigarette || n. || 香菸 || Smoking cigarettes is bad for your health. || 抽菸對你的健康有害。 || a pack of cigarettes=一包菸 || Smoking cigarettes ___ harmful to your health. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 抽菸有害健康。
cinema || n. || 電影院 || Let's go to the cinema tonight. || 我們今晚去看電影吧。 || go to the cinema=去看電影 || The new cinema, ___ opened last month, has ten screens. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 上個月開幕的新電影院有十個影廳。
circus || n. || 馬戲團 || We went to the circus last weekend. || 我們上週末去看馬戲團表演。 || circus clown=馬戲團小丑 || The children were excited ___ the circus. || about|of|with to || be excited about = 對…感到興奮。 || 孩子們對馬戲團感到很興奮。
citizen || n. || 公民；市民 || Every citizen has the right to vote. || 每個公民都有投票權。 || senior citizen=年長者 || Every citizen ___ the right to vote. || has|have|having || every + 單數名詞，用 has。 || 每位公民都有投票的權利。
civil || adj. || 公民的；民間的 || They fought for civil rights. || 他們為民權而奮鬥。 || civil rights=公民權; civil war=內戰 || The American Civil War ended ___ 1865. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 美國南北戰爭在 1865 年結束。
clay || n. || 黏土 || The kids made animals out of clay. || 孩子們用黏土捏動物。 || clay pot=陶罐 || This pot ___ made of clay two hundred years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個罐子是兩百年前用黏土做的。
cleaner || n. || 清潔工；清潔劑 || The cleaner comes every Monday. || 清潔工每週一來。 || vacuum cleaner=吸塵器 || The office is cleaned ___ a cleaner every evening. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 辦公室每天晚上由清潔工打掃。
client || n. || 客戶 || The lawyer met her client. || 律師和她的客戶見面。 || regular client=老客戶 || The lawyer, ___ clients are mostly companies, is very busy. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位律師的客戶多半是公司，所以他非常忙。
