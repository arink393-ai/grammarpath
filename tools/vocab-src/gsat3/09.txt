# 學測 L3 09：homesick – jewel
homesick || adj. || 想家的 || She felt homesick when she studied abroad. || 她出國念書時很想家。 || feel homesick=想家 || She felt homesick ___ her first month in London. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 她在倫敦的第一個月很想家。
hometown || n. || 家鄉 || My hometown is Tainan. || 我的家鄉是台南。 || return to one's hometown=返鄉 || Tainan, ___ is my hometown, is famous for its food. || which|where|that || 非限定關係子句修飾物並當主詞，用 which。 || 我的家鄉台南以美食聞名。
honesty || n. || 誠實 || Honesty is the best policy. || 誠實為上策。 || with honesty=誠實地 || She is respected ___ her honesty. || for|of|with || be respected for = 因…受到尊敬。 || 她因誠實而受到尊敬。
honor || n./v. || 榮譽；尊敬 || It's an honor to meet you. || 能見到您是我的榮幸。 || in honor of=為了紀念… || The statue was built in honor ___ the hero. || of|for|to || in honor of = 為了紀念…。 || 這座雕像是為了紀念那位英雄而建的。
hopeful || adj. || 抱有希望的 || We are hopeful about the future. || 我們對未來充滿希望。 || be hopeful about=對…抱有希望 || The doctors are hopeful ___ he will recover soon. || that|what|which || be hopeful that… = 希望…。 || 醫生們希望他能很快康復。
horn || n. || 角；喇叭 || The driver honked his horn. || 司機按了喇叭。 || honk the horn=按喇叭 || Drivers should not honk their horns ___ necessary. || unless|if|because || unless = 除非。 || 除非必要，駕駛不應按喇叭。
horrible || adj. || 可怕的 || I had a horrible dream last night. || 我昨晚做了一個可怕的夢。 || horrible experience=可怕的經歷 || The accident was ___ horrible that I couldn't sleep. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那場意外可怕到我睡不著。
horror || n. || 恐怖 || I don't like horror movies. || 我不喜歡恐怖片。 || horror movie=恐怖片; in horror=驚恐地 || She watched ___ horror as the car crashed. || in|on|by || in horror = 驚恐地。 || 她驚恐地看著那輛車撞毀。
hourly || adj./adv. || 每小時的 || Buses leave hourly. || 公車每小時一班。 || hourly pay=時薪 || The buses leave hourly, so you don't have to wait ___ long. || too|to|so that || too + 形容詞 = 太…。 || 公車每小時一班，所以你不必等太久。
housekeeper || n. || 管家 || The housekeeper cleans the house every day. || 管家每天打掃房子。 || hotel housekeeper=飯店房務員 || The housekeeper, ___ has worked here for ten years, knows everything about the house. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 在這裡工作了十年的管家對這間房子瞭若指掌。
hug || v./n. || 擁抱 || She hugged her mother. || 她擁抱了她的媽媽。 || give sb a hug=擁抱某人 || The two friends hugged each other ___ they met at the airport. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 兩個朋友在機場見面時相擁。
hum || v. || 哼歌 || She hummed a song while cooking. || 她邊煮飯邊哼歌。 || hum a tune=哼曲子 || She hummed a song ___ she was cooking dinner. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 她煮晚餐時哼著歌。
humid || adj. || 潮濕的 || Taiwan is humid in summer. || 台灣夏天很潮濕。 || hot and humid=悶熱 || It's so humid today that my clothes won't ___. || dry|dried|drying || will 後面接原形動詞。 || 今天濕到我的衣服乾不了。
humor || n. || 幽默 || He has a good sense of humor. || 他很有幽默感。 || sense of humor=幽默感 || A sense of humor can help you ___ stress. || deal with|dealing with|dealt with || help sb + 原形動詞。 || 幽默感能幫助你應付壓力。
humorous || adj. || 幽默的 || He told a humorous story. || 他說了一個幽默的故事。 || humorous story=幽默故事 || He is the ___ humorous teacher in our school. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 他是我們學校最幽默的老師。
hunger || n. || 飢餓 || Many people in the world suffer from hunger. || 世界上很多人受飢餓之苦。 || die of hunger=餓死 || Millions of children still suffer ___ hunger. || from|of|with || suffer from = 受…之苦。 || 數百萬名兒童仍受飢餓之苦。
hut || n. || 小屋 || They lived in a small hut. || 他們住在一間小屋裡。 || wooden hut=小木屋 || The hut ___ we stayed in last night had no electricity. || that|what|where || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 stayed in 的受詞。 || 我們昨晚住的小屋沒有電。
icy || adj. || 結冰的 || The road is icy. Drive carefully. || 路面結冰了，小心開車。 || icy road=結冰的路面 || Drive slowly, ___ the roads are icy. || for|so|but || for（連接詞）= 因為（補充理由）。 || 開慢一點，因為路面結冰了。
imagination || n. || 想像力 || Children have a lot of imagination. || 小孩有豐富的想像力。 || use one's imagination=發揮想像力 || Reading novels can help you develop ___ imagination. || your|you|yours || 名詞 imagination 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 閱讀小說可以幫助你培養想像力。
immediate || adj. || 立即的 || We need an immediate answer. || 我們需要立即的答覆。 || immediate action=立即行動 || The problem requires ___ immediate solution. || an|a|the more || immediate 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 這個問題需要立即解決。
import || n./v. || 進口 || Taiwan imports a lot of oil. || 台灣進口大量石油。 || import from=從…進口 || Most of the oil in Taiwan ___ imported from other countries. || is|are|be || 被動語態；oil 不可數用 is + p.p.。 || 台灣大部分的石油是從其他國家進口的。
impress || v. || 使印象深刻 || Her speech impressed everyone. || 她的演講讓大家印象深刻。 || be impressed by=對…印象深刻 || We were impressed ___ her excellent speech. || by|to|at || be impressed by = 對…印象深刻。 || 我們對她出色的演講印象深刻。
indoor || adj. || 室內的 || The school has an indoor pool. || 這所學校有室內游泳池。 || indoor activities=室內活動 || On rainy days, we can do some indoor activities ___ going outside. || instead of|because of|in spite || instead of + V-ing = 而不是…。 || 雨天時我們可以做些室內活動，而不是出門。
indoors || adv. || 在室內 || Let's stay indoors today. || 我們今天待在室內吧。 || stay indoors=待在室內 || Because of the typhoon, everyone was told ___ indoors. || to stay|staying|stay || be told to + 原形動詞。 || 因為颱風，大家被告知要待在室內。
industrial || adj. || 工業的 || Kaohsiung is an industrial city. || 高雄是一座工業城市。 || industrial area=工業區 || The river was polluted ___ industrial waste. || by|at|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 這條河被工業廢水污染了。
inferior || adj. || 較差的 || This product is inferior to that one. || 這個產品比那個差。 || be inferior to=比…差 || Cheap products are often inferior ___ expensive ones in quality. || to|than|from || be inferior to = 比…差（不用 than）。 || 便宜的產品品質往往比昂貴的差。
inform || v. || 通知 || Please inform us of any changes. || 如有任何變動請通知我們。 || inform sb of sth=通知某人某事 || Please inform me ___ the time of the meeting. || of|to|with || inform sb of sth = 通知某人某事。 || 請通知我會議時間。
information || n. || 資訊 || I need more information. || 我需要更多資訊。 || a piece of information=一則資訊 || The information ___ on the website is not correct. || given|giving|gives || 過去分詞 given 表被動（被提供的資訊）。 || 網站上提供的資訊不正確。
injury || n. || 傷害 || He had a serious injury. || 他受了重傷。 || serious injury=重傷 || He couldn't play in the game ___ a leg injury. || because of|because|although || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 他因為腿傷無法參加比賽。
inn || n. || 小旅館 || We stayed at a small inn. || 我們住在一間小旅館。 || country inn=鄉村旅館 || The inn ___ we stayed at was run by an old couple. || which|where|what || 關係代名詞 which 當介系詞 at 的受詞；用 where 就不能再加 at。 || 我們住的那間旅館由一對老夫婦經營。
inner || adj. || 內部的；內心的 || Listen to your inner voice. || 傾聽你內心的聲音。 || inner peace=內心平靜 || Meditation can help you find inner peace ___ you feel stressed. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 當你感到壓力時，冥想能幫助你找到內心平靜。
innocent || adj. || 無辜的；天真的 || He is innocent. || 他是無辜的。 || be innocent of=對…無罪 || The man was found innocent ___ the crime. || of|for|with || be innocent of = 對…是無辜的。 || 那名男子被判無罪。
inspect || v. || 檢查 || The officer inspected our bags. || 官員檢查了我們的包包。 || inspect carefully=仔細檢查 || All bags must ___ inspected before boarding. || be|is|been || must 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 登機前所有包包都必須接受檢查。
inspector || n. || 檢查員；督察 || The inspector checked the building. || 檢查員檢查了那棟大樓。 || police inspector=警察督察 || The inspector found that the building ___ safe. || was not|were not|is being || 主要子句過去式，名詞子句也用過去式；building 單數用 was。 || 檢查員發現那棟大樓不安全。
intelligent || adj. || 聰明的 || Dolphins are intelligent animals. || 海豚是聰明的動物。 || intelligent machine=智慧機器 || Dolphins are more intelligent ___ many other animals. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 海豚比許多其他動物聰明。
interrupt || v. || 打斷 || Don't interrupt me while I'm talking. || 我說話時不要打斷我。 || interrupt sb=打斷某人 || I'm sorry ___ interrupt, but there's a phone call for you. || to|for|of || be sorry to + 原形動詞。 || 抱歉打斷一下，有你的電話。
invent || v. || 發明 || Who invented the light bulb? || 誰發明了電燈泡？ || invent a machine=發明機器 || The telephone ___ invented by Alexander Graham Bell. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 電話是由貝爾發明的。
inventor || n. || 發明家 || Edison was a great inventor. || 愛迪生是一位偉大的發明家。 || famous inventor=著名發明家 || Edison was an inventor ___ inventions changed the world. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 愛迪生是一位發明改變了世界的發明家。
investigate || v. || 調查 || The police are investigating the case. || 警方正在調查這個案子。 || investigate a case=調查案件 || The case is still ___ investigated by the police. || being|been|be || 現在進行式被動：is being + p.p.。 || 這個案子仍在接受警方調查。
invitation || n. || 邀請 || Thank you for the invitation. || 謝謝你的邀請。 || accept an invitation=接受邀請 || She turned ___ the invitation because she was busy. || down|off|up || turn down = 拒絕。 || 她因為很忙而婉拒了邀請。
ivory || n. || 象牙 || Ivory trade is illegal. || 象牙交易是違法的。 || ivory tower=象牙塔 || Many elephants ___ killed for their ivory every year. || are|is|be || 被動語態；elephants 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 每年都有很多大象因象牙而被殺。
jail || n. || 監獄 || He was sent to jail. || 他被送進監獄。 || in jail=在坐牢 || He was sent to jail ___ stealing a car. || for|of|with || for + V-ing = 因為…。 || 他因偷車被送進監獄。
jar || n. || 罐子 || She keeps cookies in a jar. || 她把餅乾放在罐子裡。 || a jar of=一罐… || Can you open this jar ___ me? || for|to|with || 替某人做某事用 for。 || 你可以幫我打開這個罐子嗎？
jaw || n. || 下巴；顎 || He hurt his jaw in the fight. || 他在打架中傷了下巴。 || jaw-dropping=令人瞠目結舌的 || His jaw dropped ___ he saw the price. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 他看到價格時嚇得下巴都掉了。
jazz || n. || 爵士樂 || He loves jazz music. || 他熱愛爵士樂。 || jazz band=爵士樂團 || Jazz music, ___ started in America, is popular around the world. || which|what|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 起源於美國的爵士樂風靡全世界。
jealous || adj. || 嫉妒的 || He is jealous of his brother. || 他嫉妒他的哥哥。 || be jealous of=嫉妒… || Don't be jealous ___ other people's success. || of|to|with || be jealous of = 嫉妒…。 || 不要嫉妒別人的成功。
jeep || n. || 吉普車 || We drove a jeep through the mountains. || 我們開吉普車穿越山區。 || drive a jeep=開吉普車 || A jeep is ___ for driving on mountain roads than a normal car. || better|good|best || 比較級 better + than。 || 吉普車比一般汽車更適合開山路。
jelly || n. || 果凍；果醬 || The kids love jelly. || 孩子們很愛果凍。 || peanut butter and jelly=花生醬果醬三明治 || I'd like some jelly ___ my toast. || on|in|at || 塗在吐司上用 on。 || 我的吐司要塗一些果醬。
jet || n. || 噴射機 || The jet flew across the sky. || 噴射機飛越天空。 || jet lag=時差 || I had bad jet lag ___ flying to New York. || after|before|until || after + V-ing = 在…之後。 || 飛到紐約後，我有嚴重的時差。
jewel || n. || 寶石 || The crown is covered with jewels. || 王冠上鑲滿了寶石。 || precious jewel=珍貴的寶石 || The crown is decorated ___ jewels. || with|by|of || be decorated with = 用…裝飾。 || 王冠上裝飾著寶石。
