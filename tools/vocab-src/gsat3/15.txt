# 學測 L3 15：relax – scarce
relax || v. || 放鬆 || Relax and take a deep breath. || 放輕鬆，深呼吸。 || relax oneself=放鬆 || I like to relax ___ listening to music after school. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 我喜歡放學後聽音樂放鬆。
release || v./n. || 釋放；發行 || The singer released a new album. || 那位歌手發行了新專輯。 || release a new album=發行新專輯 || The new album ___ released last Friday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 新專輯上週五發行了。
reliable || adj. || 可靠的 || He is a reliable friend. || 他是個可靠的朋友。 || reliable source=可靠的來源 || You should only use information from reliable sources, ___ you may be misled. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 你應該只使用可靠來源的資訊，否則可能會被誤導。
relief || n. || 寬慰；減輕 || What a relief! || 真是鬆了一口氣！ || to one's relief=令某人鬆一口氣的是 || ___ my relief, the test was easier than I expected. || To|For|With || to one's relief = 令某人鬆一口氣的是。 || 讓我鬆了一口氣的是，考試比預期簡單。
religious || adj. || 宗教的 || She is very religious. || 她很虔誠。 || religious belief=宗教信仰 || People have the freedom to choose ___ religious beliefs. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 beliefs 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 人們有選擇宗教信仰的自由。
rely || v. || 依賴 || You can rely on me. || 你可以依靠我。 || rely on=依靠… || Many students rely ___ their phones too much. || on|in|to || rely on = 依賴…。 || 很多學生太依賴手機。
remain || v. || 保持；剩下 || Please remain seated. || 請保持坐著。 || remain calm=保持冷靜 || Please remain ___ until the plane stops completely. || seated|seating|to seat || remain + 過去分詞（形容詞），表示狀態。 || 飛機完全停止前請保持坐著。
remind || v. || 提醒 || Remind me to buy milk. || 提醒我買牛奶。 || remind sb to V=提醒某人做…; remind sb of=使某人想起 || Please remind me ___ my homework tomorrow. || to bring|bringing|bring || remind sb to + 原形動詞。 || 明天請提醒我帶作業。
remote || adj. || 遙遠的；偏遠的 || They live in a remote village. || 他們住在一個偏遠的村莊。 || remote control=遙控器; remote area=偏遠地區 || Many teachers are needed in remote areas ___ there are few schools. || where|which|that || 關係副詞 where 修飾地點名詞 areas。 || 學校很少的偏遠地區需要很多老師。
represent || v. || 代表 || She represented our school in the contest. || 她代表我們學校參加比賽。 || represent sb=代表某人 || She represented our school ___ the speech contest last year. || in|on|for || in + 比賽 = 在…比賽中。 || 她去年代表我們學校參加演講比賽。
representative || n./adj. || 代表 || He is a representative of the class. || 他是班級代表。 || class representative=班代 || Each class will send a representative ___ the meeting. || to|for|at || send sb to + 地點/活動 = 派某人去…。 || 每班都會派一名代表出席會議。
republic || n. || 共和國 || France is a republic. || 法國是共和國。 || the Republic of China=中華民國 || The Republic of China ___ founded in 1912. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 中華民國成立於 1912 年。
request || n./v. || 請求 || I have a request. || 我有一個請求。 || at the request of=應…的請求; make a request=提出請求 || The meeting was canceled at the request ___ the manager. || of|for|to || at the request of = 應…的要求。 || 會議應經理的要求取消了。
reserve || v./n. || 預訂；保留 || I reserved a table for two. || 我訂了一張兩人桌。 || reserve a table=訂位 || I'd like to reserve a table ___ four at seven o'clock. || for|of|to || a table for + 人數 = …人桌。 || 我想預訂七點的四人桌。
resist || v. || 抵抗；忍住 || I can't resist chocolate. || 我無法抗拒巧克力。 || resist + V-ing=忍住不做… || I couldn't resist ___ the last piece of cake. || eating|to eat|eat || resist 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 我忍不住吃了最後一塊蛋糕。
resource || n. || 資源 || Water is a precious resource. || 水是珍貴的資源。 || natural resources=天然資源 || We should use natural resources wisely ___ they will run out. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 我們應該明智地使用天然資源，否則會用光。
response || n. || 回應 || I haven't received a response yet. || 我還沒收到回覆。 || in response to=回應… || ___ response to the complaints, the company changed its policy. || In|On|At || in response to = 回應…。 || 為了回應投訴，公司改變了政策。
responsibility || n. || 責任 || It's your responsibility to clean your room. || 打掃你的房間是你的責任。 || take responsibility for=對…負責 || You should take responsibility ___ your own actions. || for|of|to || take responsibility for = 對…負責。 || 你應該為自己的行為負責。
restrict || v. || 限制 || The school restricts phone use. || 學校限制手機使用。 || restrict to=限制在… || Phone use is restricted ___ break time at our school. || to|in|for || be restricted to = 被限制在…。 || 我們學校只准在下課時間使用手機。
reveal || v. || 揭露 || The report revealed the truth. || 這份報告揭露了真相。 || reveal a secret=揭露祕密 || The study revealed ___ many teenagers don't get enough sleep. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 reveal 的受詞。 || 研究顯示許多青少年睡眠不足。
ribbon || n. || 緞帶 || She tied her hair with a red ribbon. || 她用紅緞帶綁頭髮。 || tie a ribbon=繫緞帶 || The gift was wrapped in paper and tied ___ a ribbon. || with|by|of || tie A with B = 用 B 綁住 A。 || 禮物用紙包好並繫上緞帶。
rid || v./adj. || 擺脫 || I want to get rid of these old clothes. || 我想丟掉這些舊衣服。 || get rid of=擺脫、丟掉 || It's hard to get rid ___ bad habits. || of|off|from || get rid of = 擺脫…。 || 壞習慣很難擺脫。
ripe || adj. || 成熟的 || The bananas are ripe. || 香蕉熟了。 || ripe fruit=成熟的水果 || These mangoes aren't ripe yet, so don't eat ___. || them|it|they || mangoes 複數，受格用 them。 || 這些芒果還沒熟，所以別吃。
risk || n./v. || 風險 || Smoking increases the risk of cancer. || 抽菸會增加罹癌風險。 || at risk=處於危險中; take a risk=冒險 || Smoking increases the risk ___ heart disease. || of|for|to || the risk of = …的風險。 || 抽菸會增加心臟病的風險。
roar || v./n. || 吼叫 || The lion roared loudly. || 獅子大聲吼叫。 || roar with laughter=哄堂大笑 || The audience roared ___ laughter at the joke. || with|in|by || roar with laughter = 哄堂大笑。 || 觀眾聽了笑話哄堂大笑。
roast || v./adj. || 烘烤 || We roasted marshmallows over the fire. || 我們在火上烤棉花糖。 || roast chicken=烤雞 || The chicken ___ roasting in the oven now. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The chicken 用 is。 || 雞肉現在正在烤箱裡烤。
rob || v. || 搶劫 || Two men robbed the bank. || 兩名男子搶了銀行。 || rob sb of sth=搶奪某人的某物 || The thief robbed the old woman ___ her bag. || of|from|with || rob sb of sth = 搶奪某人的某物。 || 小偷搶了那位老太太的包包。
robbery || n. || 搶劫案 || There was a robbery at the bank. || 銀行發生了搶案。 || bank robbery=銀行搶案 || The robbery happened ___ midnight. || at|in|on || at midnight = 在午夜。 || 搶案發生在午夜。
robe || n. || 長袍 || He wore a bath robe. || 他穿著浴袍。 || bath robe=浴袍 || The judge was dressed ___ a black robe. || in|with|on || be dressed in = 穿著…。 || 法官穿著一件黑色長袍。
rocket || n. || 火箭 || The rocket was launched into space. || 火箭被發射到太空。 || launch a rocket=發射火箭 || The rocket ___ launched successfully yesterday. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 火箭昨天成功發射了。
romantic || adj. || 浪漫的 || It was a romantic dinner. || 那是一頓浪漫的晚餐。 || romantic movie=愛情片 || Paris is often called the ___ romantic city in the world. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 巴黎常被稱為世界上最浪漫的城市。
rot || v./n. || 腐爛 || The apples began to rot. || 蘋果開始腐爛。 || rot away=爛掉 || The fruit will rot ___ you don't put it in the fridge. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果你不把水果放進冰箱，它會爛掉。
rotten || adj. || 腐爛的 || The eggs are rotten. || 這些蛋壞掉了。 || rotten apple=爛蘋果 || Throw away the rotten fruit ___ it makes the other fruit go bad. || before|after|since || 在其他水果變壞「之前」丟掉，用 before。 || 在其他水果被傳染之前把爛掉的丟掉。
rough || adj. || 粗糙的；艱難的 || His hands are rough. || 他的手很粗糙。 || rough day=難熬的一天 || I've had a rough day, so I want to go to bed ___. || early|earlier than|earliest || 副詞 early 修飾 go to bed。 || 我今天過得很辛苦，所以想早點睡。
roughly || adv. || 大約 || There were roughly 100 people. || 大約有一百個人。 || roughly speaking=大略地說 || It takes roughly two hours ___ get to Taichung by car. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 開車到台中大約要兩小時。
routine || n. || 例行公事 || Exercise is part of my daily routine. || 運動是我每天的例行公事。 || daily routine=日常作息 || Brushing my teeth is part ___ my morning routine. || of|in|for || part of = …的一部分。 || 刷牙是我早晨例行公事的一部分。
rug || n. || 小地毯 || There is a rug on the floor. || 地上鋪著一塊地毯。 || soft rug=柔軟的地毯 || The cat is sleeping ___ the rug. || on|in|at || 在地毯上用 on。 || 貓在地毯上睡覺。
rumor || n. || 謠言 || Don't believe rumors. || 不要相信謠言。 || spread rumors=散播謠言 || There is a rumor ___ the school will close. || that|what|which || a rumor that… = 有…的傳言（同位語子句）。 || 有傳言說學校要關了。
rush || v./n. || 衝；匆忙 || Don't rush. Take your time. || 別急，慢慢來。 || in a rush=匆忙地; rush hour=尖峰時段 || I left home in a rush and forgot ___ my keys. || to take|taking|take || forget to V = 忘記要做（沒做）。 || 我匆忙出門，忘了帶鑰匙。
rust || n./v. || 鏽 || The old car is covered in rust. || 那輛舊車佈滿了鏽。 || rust away=鏽蝕 || The iron gate has rusted ___ it was left in the rain. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 鐵門生鏽了，因為它被雨淋。
sack || n. || 大袋子 || He carried a sack of rice. || 他扛著一袋米。 || a sack of=一袋… || He carried a heavy sack ___ potatoes on his back. || of|with|for || a sack of = 一袋…。 || 他背著一大袋馬鈴薯。
sake || n. || 緣故 || Do it for your own sake. || 為了你自己去做吧。 || for the sake of=為了… || He quit smoking for the sake ___ his health. || of|for|to || for the sake of = 為了…。 || 他為了健康而戒菸。
salary || n. || 薪水 || She gets a good salary. || 她的薪水很好。 || high salary=高薪 || His salary is ___ higher than mine. || much|many|very || 比較級前面用 much 加強語氣。 || 他的薪水比我高得多。
satisfactory || adj. || 令人滿意的 || The result was satisfactory. || 結果令人滿意。 || satisfactory result=令人滿意的結果 || The service at the hotel was not satisfactory, ___ we complained. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 飯店的服務令人不滿意，所以我們投訴了。
sauce || n. || 醬汁 || Would you like some sauce? || 你要一些醬汁嗎？ || soy sauce=醬油; tomato sauce=番茄醬 || The noodles taste better ___ this sauce. || with|by|of || with = 搭配…。 || 麵搭配這個醬汁更好吃。
saucer || n. || 茶碟 || Put the cup on the saucer. || 把杯子放在茶碟上。 || flying saucer=飛碟 || Some people claim they ___ a flying saucer. || have seen|has seen|seeing || 現在完成式表示經驗；主詞 they 用 have。 || 有些人聲稱自己看過飛碟。
sausage || n. || 香腸 || I'd like a sausage. || 我要一根香腸。 || grilled sausage=烤香腸 || Grilled sausages are one of the most popular snacks ___ night markets. || at|on|of || at night markets = 在夜市。 || 烤香腸是夜市最受歡迎的小吃之一。
saving || n. || 儲蓄 || She used her savings to travel. || 她用存款去旅行。 || savings account=儲蓄帳戶 || He spent all his savings ___ a new car. || on|in|for || spend money on = 把錢花在…。 || 他把所有存款都花在一輛新車上。
scale || n. || 規模；秤 || The project is on a large scale. || 這個計畫規模很大。 || on a large scale=大規模地 || The festival was held ___ a large scale this year. || on|in|at || on a large scale = 大規模地。 || 今年的節慶大規模舉辦。
scarce || adj. || 稀少的 || Water is scarce in the desert. || 沙漠裡水很稀少。 || scarce resources=稀有資源 || Food was scarce ___ the war. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 戰爭期間食物很短缺。
