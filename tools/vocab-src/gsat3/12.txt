# 學測 L3 12：motor – palm
motor || n. || 馬達 || The boat has a small motor. || 這艘船有一個小馬達。 || electric motor=電動馬達 || The motor stopped working ___ it was too old. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 馬達因為太舊而停止運轉。
multiply || v. || 乘；增加 || Multiply 3 by 4. || 三乘以四。 || multiply A by B=A 乘以 B || If you multiply 5 ___ 6, you get 30. || by|with|to || multiply A by B = A 乘以 B。 || 五乘以六等於三十。
murder || n./v. || 謀殺 || He was arrested for murder. || 他因謀殺被捕。 || commit murder=犯下謀殺 || The man was arrested ___ murder. || for|of|with || arrest sb for + 罪名。 || 那名男子因謀殺被捕。
muscle || n. || 肌肉 || Exercise builds muscle. || 運動能鍛鍊肌肉。 || build muscle=增肌 || Lifting weights helps you build ___ muscles. || your|you|yours || 名詞 muscles 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 舉重能幫助你鍛鍊肌肉。
mushroom || n. || 蘑菇 || I like mushroom soup. || 我喜歡蘑菇湯。 || mushroom soup=蘑菇湯 || Some wild mushrooms are poisonous, so you should never eat ___ you don't know. || ones|one|them || ones 代替前面的複數名詞 mushrooms。 || 有些野生蘑菇有毒，所以不認識的絕對不要吃。
mystery || n. || 謎；神祕 || How he escaped is still a mystery. || 他怎麼逃走的仍是個謎。 || mystery novel=推理小說 || ___ the house was built is still a mystery. || How|What|Which || 名詞子句 How… 當主詞 = …如何…。 || 那間房子是怎麼蓋的至今仍是個謎。
naked || adj. || 赤裸的 || You can see the stars with the naked eye. || 你可以用肉眼看到星星。 || the naked eye=肉眼 || Some stars can be seen ___ the naked eye. || with|by|in || with the naked eye = 用肉眼。 || 有些星星用肉眼就看得到。
nap || n./v. || 小睡 || I take a nap after lunch. || 我午餐後小睡一下。 || take a nap=小睡 || Taking a short nap ___ help you feel more energetic. || can|cans|could to || 助動詞 can 後接原形動詞。 || 小睡片刻能讓你更有精神。
napkin || n. || 餐巾 || Put the napkin on your lap. || 把餐巾放在腿上。 || paper napkin=紙巾 || Could you pass me a napkin? I spilled ___ juice. || my|me|mine || 名詞 juice 前用所有格形容詞 my。 || 可以遞給我一張餐巾嗎？我把果汁灑了。
native || adj./n. || 本地的；本國的 || English is her native language. || 英文是她的母語。 || native speaker=母語人士 || She speaks English ___ a native speaker. || like|such|so || like = 像…一樣（介系詞）。 || 她說英文就像母語人士一樣。
navy || n. || 海軍 || My uncle is in the navy. || 我叔叔在海軍服役。 || navy blue=海軍藍 || He joined the navy ___ he graduated from high school. || after|before|since || 畢業「之後」加入，用 after。 || 他高中畢業後加入了海軍。
neat || adj. || 整潔的 || Her room is always neat. || 她的房間總是很整潔。 || neat and tidy=整整齊齊 || Keep your desk neat ___ you can find things easily. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 保持書桌整潔，才能輕鬆找到東西。
necessity || n. || 必需品 || Water is a necessity of life. || 水是生活必需品。 || daily necessities=日用品 || Food, water, and shelter ___ basic necessities of life. || are|is|be || 主詞為三樣東西（複數），用 are。 || 食物、水和住所是生活的基本必需品。
necktie || n. || 領帶 || He wore a red necktie. || 他打著紅色領帶。 || wear a necktie=打領帶 || He bought a necktie ___ his father for Father's Day. || for|to|with || 為某人買用 for。 || 他為父親節買了一條領帶給爸爸。
neighborhood || n. || 鄰近地區 || We live in a quiet neighborhood. || 我們住在一個安靜的社區。 || in the neighborhood=在附近 || Is there a park ___ the neighborhood? || in|on|at || in the neighborhood = 在附近。 || 附近有公園嗎？
nest || n./v. || 巢 || The bird built a nest in the tree. || 鳥在樹上築巢。 || bird's nest=鳥巢 || The birds built their nest ___ small sticks. || with|by|at || with + 材料 = 用…。 || 鳥用小樹枝築巢。
nickname || n. || 綽號 || His nickname is "Little Tiger." || 他的綽號是「小老虎」。 || give sb a nickname=給某人取綽號 || He got the nickname "Speedy" ___ he runs so fast. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 他因為跑得很快而得到「飛毛腿」的綽號。
normal || adj. || 正常的 || It's normal to feel nervous. || 感到緊張是正常的。 || back to normal=恢復正常 || It's normal ___ feel nervous before an exam. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 考試前感到緊張是正常的。
novelist || n. || 小說家 || She is a famous novelist. || 她是一位知名小說家。 || best-selling novelist=暢銷小說家 || The novelist ___ books have been translated into 20 languages is from Taiwan. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位作品被翻譯成二十種語言的小說家來自台灣。
nun || n. || 修女 || The nun works at the hospital. || 那位修女在醫院工作。 || Catholic nun=天主教修女 || The nun has devoted her life ___ helping the poor. || to|for|with || devote oneself/one's life to + V-ing = 致力於…。 || 那位修女一生致力於幫助窮人。
oak || n. || 橡樹 || The table is made of oak. || 這張桌子是橡木做的。 || oak tree=橡樹 || The old oak tree ___ been standing here for over 200 years. || has|have|is || 現在完成進行式；The old oak tree 用 has。 || 這棵老橡樹已經在這裡矗立超過兩百年了。
observe || v. || 觀察；遵守 || Scientists observe the stars. || 科學家觀察星星。 || observe the rules=遵守規則 || The scientist observed the birds ___ a long time. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 那位科學家觀察這些鳥很長一段時間。
occasion || n. || 場合；時刻 || A wedding is a special occasion. || 婚禮是一個特別的場合。 || on special occasions=在特別的場合 || We only use these plates ___ special occasions. || on|in|at || on + occasion = 在…場合。 || 我們只在特殊場合才用這些盤子。
odd || adj. || 奇怪的；奇數的 || That's an odd question. || 那是個奇怪的問題。 || odd number=奇數 || It's odd ___ he didn't call me back. || that|what|which || It is odd that… = …很奇怪。 || 奇怪的是他沒回我電話。
omit || v. || 省略 || You can omit this word. || 你可以省略這個字。 || omit a word=省略一個字 || In this sentence, the word "that" can ___ omitted. || be|is|been || can 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 這個句子裡的 that 可以省略。
ongoing || adj. || 進行中的 || The investigation is ongoing. || 調查仍在進行中。 || ongoing project=進行中的計畫 || The ongoing project will ___ finished by next month. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 這個進行中的專案將在下個月完成。
onion || n. || 洋蔥 || Cutting onions makes me cry. || 切洋蔥讓我流淚。 || green onion=蔥 || Cutting onions always ___ my eyes water. || makes|make|making || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，動詞加 s。 || 切洋蔥總是讓我眼睛流淚。
onto || prep. || 到…之上 || The cat jumped onto the table. || 貓跳到了桌子上。 || onto=到…上 || The cat jumped onto the table and knocked ___ a cup. || over|off of|down to || knock over = 打翻。 || 貓跳上桌子打翻了一個杯子。
operation || n. || 手術；運作 || He had an operation on his knee. || 他的膝蓋動了手術。 || have an operation=動手術 || He had an operation ___ his heart last month. || on|in|at || an operation on + 身體部位 = …的手術。 || 他上個月動了心臟手術。
opportunity || n. || 機會 || This is a great opportunity for you. || 這對你來說是個大好機會。 || take the opportunity to V=利用機會做… || I took the opportunity ___ my English when I was in the U.S. || to practice|practicing|practice || the opportunity to + 原形動詞。 || 我在美國時趁機練習英文。
optimistic || adj. || 樂觀的 || She is optimistic about the future. || 她對未來很樂觀。 || be optimistic about=對…樂觀 || He remains optimistic ___ the result. || about|of|with || be optimistic about = 對…樂觀。 || 他對結果仍然很樂觀。
oral || adj. || 口頭的；口腔的 || We have an oral test tomorrow. || 我們明天有口試。 || oral test=口試; oral health=口腔健康 || Brushing your teeth twice a day is important for ___ oral health. || your|you|yours || 名詞 oral health 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 一天刷兩次牙對口腔健康很重要。
organic || adj. || 有機的 || She only buys organic vegetables. || 她只買有機蔬菜。 || organic food=有機食品 || Organic vegetables are grown ___ using chemicals. || without|with|within || without + V-ing = 不…。 || 有機蔬菜的種植不使用化學藥劑。
organize || v. || 組織；籌劃 || She organized a party for her friend. || 她為朋友籌辦了一場派對。 || organize an event=籌辦活動 || The event ___ organized by the student council. || was|be|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這個活動是由學生會籌辦的。
original || adj./n. || 原本的；原創的 || This is the original painting. || 這是原畫。 || original idea=原創點子 || The movie is not as good as the original ___. || novel|novels|novelist || 題目句中已有 original，答案為被修飾的名詞 novel。 || 這部電影沒有原著小說好看。
outdoor || adj. || 戶外的 || I like outdoor activities. || 我喜歡戶外活動。 || outdoor activities=戶外活動 || Hiking is one of my favorite outdoor ___. || activities|activity|active || one of + 複數名詞。 || 健行是我最喜歡的戶外活動之一。
outdoors || adv. || 在戶外 || The kids like playing outdoors. || 孩子們喜歡在戶外玩。 || play outdoors=在戶外玩 || Children should spend more time playing outdoors ___ of staying at home. || instead|because|in spite || instead of = 而不是…。 || 孩子們應該花更多時間在戶外玩，而不是待在家裡。
outer || adj. || 外部的 || The astronaut went into outer space. || 太空人進入外太空。 || outer space=外太空 || Nobody knows ___ there is life in outer space. || whether|what|which || whether 引導名詞子句（是否）。 || 沒有人知道外太空是否有生命。
outline || n./v. || 大綱 || Write an outline before writing the essay. || 寫作文之前先寫大綱。 || make an outline=擬大綱 || You should make an outline ___ you start writing. || before|after|since || 開始寫「之前」擬大綱，用 before。 || 開始寫作之前你應該先擬大綱。
oven || n. || 烤箱 || Bake the cake in the oven. || 把蛋糕放進烤箱烤。 || microwave oven=微波爐 || Bake the cookies in the oven ___ fifteen minutes. || for|in|since || for + 一段時間。 || 把餅乾放進烤箱烤十五分鐘。
overseas || adv./adj. || 在海外 || He works overseas. || 他在海外工作。 || study overseas=海外留學 || Many students dream of ___ overseas. || studying|to study|study || 介系詞 of 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 許多學生夢想出國留學。
owe || v. || 欠 || I owe you 100 dollars. || 我欠你一百元。 || owe sb sth=欠某人某物; owe A to B=把 A 歸功於 B || I owe my success ___ my parents. || to|for|with || owe A to B = 把 A 歸功於 B。 || 我把我的成功歸功於我的父母。
owl || n. || 貓頭鷹 || Owls hunt at night. || 貓頭鷹在夜間獵食。 || night owl=夜貓子 || I'm a night owl. I usually go to bed ___ 2 a.m. || at|on|in || 幾點鐘前面用 at。 || 我是夜貓子，通常凌晨兩點才睡。
ownership || n. || 所有權 || The ownership of the land is unclear. || 這塊地的所有權不明。 || home ownership=擁有房屋 || The ownership of the house was passed ___ his son. || to|for|with || pass A to B = 把 A 傳給 B。 || 房子的所有權傳給了他的兒子。
ox || n. || 公牛 || The ox pulled the cart. || 公牛拉著車。 || as strong as an ox=力大如牛 || He is as strong ___ an ox. || as|like|than || as + 形容詞 + as = 和…一樣。 || 他力大如牛。
pad || n. || 墊子；便條本 || Write it down on the note pad. || 把它寫在便條本上。 || note pad=便條本; knee pad=護膝 || Wear knee pads ___ protect your knees when skating. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 溜冰時要戴護膝保護膝蓋。
painter || n. || 畫家 || Van Gogh was a Dutch painter. || 梵谷是荷蘭畫家。 || famous painter=知名畫家 || The painter, ___ works are now very valuable, was poor during his life. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 這位畫家的作品如今非常珍貴，但他生前很窮。
pal || n. || 朋友 || She is my pen pal. || 她是我的筆友。 || pen pal=筆友 || I have been writing to my pen pal in Canada ___ three years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成進行式。 || 我和加拿大的筆友通信已經三年了。
palace || n. || 宮殿 || The king lived in a palace. || 國王住在宮殿裡。 || royal palace=皇宮 || The National Palace Museum is famous ___ its Chinese art. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 故宮博物院以中國藝術品聞名。
palm || n. || 手掌；棕櫚 || She read my palm. || 她幫我看手相。 || palm tree=棕櫚樹 || There are many palm trees ___ the beach. || on|in|of || 在海灘上用 on the beach。 || 海灘上有很多棕櫚樹。
