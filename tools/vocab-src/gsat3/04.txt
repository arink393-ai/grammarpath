# 學測 L3 04：clinic – crew
clinic || n. || 診所 || I went to the clinic because I had a cold. || 我因為感冒去了診所。 || dental clinic=牙醫診所 || The clinic ___ I usually go to is near my home. || that|who|where || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 go to 的受詞。 || 我常去的那家診所在我家附近。
clip || n./v. || 夾子；剪輯 || I watched a short video clip. || 我看了一段短片。 || paper clip=迴紋針; video clip=影片片段 || Use a paper clip ___ the pages together. || to hold|holding|hold || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 用迴紋針把這些頁夾在一起。
closet || n. || 衣櫥 || Hang your coat in the closet. || 把外套掛在衣櫥裡。 || walk-in closet=更衣間 || The closet is full ___ clothes she never wears. || of|with|by || be full of = 充滿…。 || 衣櫥裡塞滿了她從來不穿的衣服。
clothe || v. || 給…穿衣 || The charity clothes and feeds poor children. || 這個慈善團體供貧童吃穿。 || clothe oneself=穿衣 || The charity provides food and clothes ___ poor families. || for|to|with || provide sth for sb = 為某人提供某物。 || 這個慈善團體為貧困家庭提供食物和衣服。
clown || n. || 小丑 || The clown made the kids laugh. || 小丑逗得孩子們哈哈大笑。 || circus clown=馬戲團小丑 || The clown made the children ___. || laugh|to laugh|laughing || make + 受詞 + 原形動詞（使役動詞）。 || 小丑讓孩子們哈哈大笑。
clue || n. || 線索 || The police found an important clue. || 警方找到了一條重要的線索。 || have no clue=毫無頭緒 || The detective found a clue ___ led him to the thief. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a clue）。 || 那位偵探找到一條帶他找到小偷的線索。
coach || n./v. || 教練 || The coach trains the team every day. || 教練每天訓練隊伍。 || basketball coach=籃球教練 || The team, ___ by a famous coach, won the championship. || trained|training|trains || 過去分詞 trained 表被動（由…訓練）。 || 由一位名教練訓練的這支隊伍贏得了冠軍。
cock || n. || 公雞 || The cock crows every morning. || 公雞每天早上啼叫。 || cock=公雞 || The cock crows early ___ the morning. || in|on|at || in the morning = 在早上。 || 公雞一早就啼叫。
cocktail || n. || 雞尾酒 || They served cocktails at the party. || 派對上供應雞尾酒。 || cocktail party=雞尾酒會 || Children are not allowed ___ cocktails. || to drink|drinking|drink || be allowed to + 原形動詞。 || 兒童不准喝雞尾酒。
coconut || n. || 椰子 || I drank coconut water on the beach. || 我在海灘上喝椰子水。 || coconut milk=椰奶 || Coconut trees grow well in places ___ the weather is hot. || where|which|that || 關係副詞 where 修飾地點名詞 places。 || 椰子樹在天氣炎熱的地方生長良好。
collar || n. || 衣領；項圈 || The dog has a red collar. || 那隻狗戴著紅色項圈。 || shirt collar=襯衫領子 || The dog ___ collar is red belongs to my neighbor. || whose|which|who || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 戴著紅色項圈的那隻狗是我鄰居的。
collection || n. || 收藏 || He has a large collection of stamps. || 他有大量的郵票收藏。 || a collection of=一批…的收藏 || The museum has a collection of paintings ___ are over 300 years old. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（paintings）。 || 這間博物館收藏了一批超過三百年歷史的畫作。
colony || n. || 殖民地；群落 || Hong Kong was once a British colony. || 香港曾是英國的殖民地。 || a colony of ants=一群螞蟻 || Hong Kong ___ a British colony for more than 150 years. || was|is|has || 描述過去的事實用過去式 was。 || 香港曾是英國的殖民地超過一百五十年。
colorful || adj. || 色彩繽紛的 || The market is full of colorful fruits. || 市場裡滿是五顏六色的水果。 || colorful life=多采多姿的生活 || The garden, ___ with colorful flowers, attracts many visitors. || filled|filling|fills || 過去分詞 filled 表被動（被花填滿）。 || 開滿繽紛花朵的花園吸引了許多遊客。
column || n. || 專欄；柱子 || She writes a column for the newspaper. || 她為報紙寫專欄。 || newspaper column=報紙專欄 || The article appeared in the column ___ she writes every week. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 writes 的受詞。 || 那篇文章刊登在她每週寫的專欄裡。
comfort || n./v. || 安慰；舒適 || She comforted her friend. || 她安慰了她的朋友。 || take comfort in=從…得到安慰; comfort zone=舒適圈 || Her kind words comforted me ___ I was feeling down. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 我心情低落時，她溫暖的話安慰了我。
comma || n. || 逗號 || Put a comma after "however." || 在 however 後面加一個逗號。 || use a comma=用逗號 || A comma should be used ___ the two clauses. || between|among|in || 兩者之間用 between。 || 兩個子句之間應該用逗號。
committee || n. || 委員會 || She is on the school committee. || 她是學校委員會的成員。 || committee member=委員 || The committee made its final decision ___ a long discussion. || after|during|while || after + 名詞 = 在…之後。 || 委員會經過長時間討論後做出了最後決定。
communicate || v. || 溝通 || We communicate by e-mail. || 我們用電子郵件溝通。 || communicate with=和…溝通 || It's important to communicate ___ your parents. || with|to|for || communicate with sb = 和某人溝通。 || 和父母溝通很重要。
comparison || n. || 比較 || In comparison with last year, sales are up. || 和去年相比，銷售量上升了。 || in comparison with=和…相比 || In comparison ___ other cities, Taipei is very safe. || with|to be|of || in comparison with = 和…相比。 || 和其他城市相比，台北非常安全。
compete || v. || 競爭；參賽 || Ten teams will compete in the contest. || 十支隊伍將參加比賽。 || compete with=和…競爭; compete for=爭奪… || Many students compete ___ a few spots in the top school. || for|with|to || compete for = 爭奪…。 || 許多學生爭奪頂尖學校少數的名額。
complain || v. || 抱怨 || He always complains about the food. || 他總是抱怨食物。 || complain about=抱怨… || Instead of complaining, you should try ___ the problem. || to solve|solving to|solve || try to + 原形動詞。 || 與其抱怨，你應該試著解決問題。
complaint || n. || 抱怨；投訴 || We received many complaints about the noise. || 我們收到很多關於噪音的投訴。 || make a complaint=投訴 || The hotel received a complaint ___ the noisy guests. || about|to|for || a complaint about = 對…的抱怨。 || 飯店收到了一則關於吵鬧房客的投訴。
concert || n. || 演唱會；音樂會 || We went to a concert last night. || 我們昨晚去聽演唱會。 || rock concert=搖滾演唱會 || All the tickets for the concert ___ sold out in five minutes. || were|was|are || 被動語態；tickets 複數，過去用 were。 || 演唱會的票在五分鐘內全部售罄。
conclusion || n. || 結論 || What is your conclusion? || 你的結論是什麼？ || in conclusion=總之; come to a conclusion=得出結論 || ___ conclusion, exercise is good for both body and mind. || In|On|At || in conclusion = 總之。 || 總之，運動對身心都有益。
cone || n. || 圓錐；甜筒 || I'd like an ice cream cone. || 我要一支甜筒冰淇淋。 || ice cream cone=甜筒; traffic cone=交通錐 || The road was blocked ___ traffic cones. || with|by|of || be blocked with = 被…擋住。 || 道路被交通錐擋住了。
confirm || v. || 確認 || Please confirm your booking. || 請確認你的預約。 || confirm a reservation=確認預約 || Please call us to confirm ___ you will come. || whether|what|which || whether 引導名詞子句（是否）。 || 請打電話確認你是否會來。
confuse || v. || 使困惑 || The instructions confused me. || 這些說明讓我很困惑。 || confuse A with B=把 A 和 B 搞混 || People often confuse the word "affect" ___ "effect." || with|to|and || confuse A with B = 把 A 和 B 搞混。 || 人們常把 affect 和 effect 搞混。
connect || v. || 連接 || The bridge connects the two cities. || 這座橋連接兩座城市。 || connect A with B=連接 A 和 B || The two cities are connected ___ a high-speed railway. || by|with|from || 被動語態中表示方式／手段用 by。 || 這兩座城市以高鐵相連。
conscious || adj. || 有意識的；察覺的 || He was conscious of the danger. || 他察覺到了危險。 || be conscious of=意識到… || People are becoming more conscious ___ their health. || of|to|for || be conscious of = 意識到…。 || 人們越來越注意自己的健康。
considerable || adj. || 相當大的 || It cost a considerable amount of money. || 那花了相當大的一筆錢。 || considerable amount=相當大的數量 || The project took a considerable amount ___ time. || of|in|for || a considerable amount of = 相當多的…。 || 這個專案花了相當多的時間。
constant || adj. || 持續的 || The baby needs constant care. || 嬰兒需要持續的照顧。 || constant noise=持續的噪音 || The constant noise from the construction site made it hard ___ sleep. || to|for|of || make it + 形容詞 + to V。 || 工地持續的噪音讓人難以入睡。
continent || n. || 大陸；洲 || Asia is the largest continent. || 亞洲是最大的洲。 || the seven continents=七大洲 || Asia is the largest continent ___ the world. || in|of|on || 最高級 + in the world。 || 亞洲是世界上最大的洲。
controller || n. || 控制器；管理員 || He picked up the game controller. || 他拿起了遊戲手把。 || game controller=遊戲手把; air traffic controller=航管員 || An air traffic controller must stay focused ___ all times. || at|in|on || at all times = 隨時。 || 航管員必須隨時保持專注。
cooker || n. || 炊具；鍋 || We cook rice in a rice cooker. || 我們用電鍋煮飯。 || rice cooker=電鍋 || The rice cooker, ___ my grandma bought 30 years ago, still works. || which|who|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 奶奶三十年前買的電鍋到現在還能用。
costly || adj. || 昂貴的；代價高的 || The mistake was costly. || 這個錯誤代價很高。 || costly mistake=代價高昂的錯誤 || Repairing the old car would be ___ costly than buying a new one. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 修那輛舊車會比買新車還貴。
cotton || n. || 棉花 || This shirt is made of cotton. || 這件襯衫是棉質的。 || cotton candy=棉花糖 || Cotton clothes are more comfortable ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 棉質衣服在夏天比較舒服。
cough || n./v. || 咳嗽 || He has had a cough for a week. || 他已經咳嗽一週了。 || cough medicine=咳嗽藥 || She has been coughing ___ last Monday. || since|for|from || since + 過去時間點，搭配現在完成進行式。 || 她從上週一就一直在咳嗽。
countable || adj. || 可數的 || "Apple" is a countable noun. || apple 是可數名詞。 || countable noun=可數名詞 || 'Apple' is a countable noun, but 'water' ___. || is not|does not|are not || 省略重複的 countable noun，be 動詞否定用 is not。 || apple 是可數名詞，但 water 不是。
county || n. || 縣 || Hualien is the largest county in Taiwan. || 花蓮是台灣最大的縣。 || Hualien County=花蓮縣 || Hualien is the largest county ___ Taiwan. || in|of|on || 最高級 + in + 地方。 || 花蓮是台灣面積最大的縣。
crab || n. || 螃蟹 || We ate crab for dinner. || 我們晚餐吃螃蟹。 || crab meat=蟹肉 || Crabs walk sideways, ___ is quite interesting. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句 which 指代整個前句。 || 螃蟹橫著走，這一點很有趣。
cradle || n. || 搖籃 || The baby is sleeping in the cradle. || 寶寶在搖籃裡睡覺。 || from the cradle to the grave=從生到死 || The baby fell asleep ___ his mother was rocking the cradle. || while|during|for || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 媽媽搖著搖籃時，寶寶睡著了。
crane || n. || 起重機；鶴 || A crane is lifting the steel. || 起重機正在吊起鋼材。 || tower crane=塔式起重機 || The heavy steel was lifted ___ a crane. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者（工具）用 by。 || 沉重的鋼材由起重機吊起。
crash || n./v. || 撞擊；墜毀 || The car crashed into a tree. || 那輛車撞上了一棵樹。 || car crash=車禍; crash into=撞上… || The car crashed ___ a tree last night. || into|in|to || crash into = 撞上…。 || 那輛車昨晚撞上了一棵樹。
crawl || v. || 爬行 || The baby is learning to crawl. || 寶寶正在學爬。 || crawl on the floor=在地上爬 || Babies usually learn ___ crawl before they walk. || to|for|of || learn to + 原形動詞。 || 嬰兒通常先學會爬才會走路。
creative || adj. || 有創意的 || She is a creative designer. || 她是個有創意的設計師。 || creative thinking=創意思考 || The more you read, the ___ creative you become. || more|most|much || the + 比較級…, the + 比較級… = 越…就越…。 || 你讀得越多，就變得越有創意。
creator || n. || 創造者 || He is the creator of the famous cartoon. || 他是那部知名卡通的創作者。 || the creator of=…的創造者 || The creator ___ the game is only twenty years old. || of|for|to || the creator of = …的創造者。 || 這款遊戲的創作者只有二十歲。
creature || n. || 生物 || The ocean is full of strange creatures. || 海洋裡充滿奇特的生物。 || living creature=生物; sea creature=海洋生物 || Many sea creatures ___ threatened by plastic pollution. || are|is|be || 被動語態；Many sea creatures 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 許多海洋生物受到塑膠污染的威脅。
credit || n./v. || 信用；功勞 || She deserves credit for the success. || 這次成功她功不可沒。 || credit card=信用卡; give sb credit=歸功於某人 || You can pay ___ credit card. || by|with|in || pay by credit card = 用信用卡付款。 || 你可以用信用卡付款。
crew || n. || 全體船員／機組人員 || The crew worked hard to save the ship. || 全體船員努力拯救船隻。 || flight crew=機組人員 || The crew worked all night ___ repair the ship. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 船員們整夜工作以修理船隻。
