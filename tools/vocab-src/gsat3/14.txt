# 學測 L3 14：poster – regret
poster || n. || 海報 || There is a poster on the wall. || 牆上有一張海報。 || movie poster=電影海報 || The students made posters ___ the school festival. || for|to|of || for + 名詞 = 為了…。 || 學生們為校慶做了海報。
pottery || n. || 陶器 || She is learning to make pottery. || 她在學做陶藝。 || pottery class=陶藝課 || Yingge is famous ___ its pottery. || for|of|with || be famous for = 以…聞名。 || 鶯歌以陶瓷聞名。
pour || v. || 倒；傾盆而下 || Pour the milk into the cup. || 把牛奶倒進杯子裡。 || pour into=倒入; it's pouring=下大雨 || Pour the water ___ the glass slowly. || into|of|at || pour A into B = 把 A 倒入 B。 || 慢慢地把水倒進杯子裡。
poverty || n. || 貧窮 || Many people live in poverty. || 許多人生活在貧窮中。 || live in poverty=生活貧困 || Millions of children still live ___ poverty. || in|on|at || live in poverty = 生活在貧困中。 || 數百萬名兒童仍生活在貧困中。
powder || n. || 粉 || Add some milk powder. || 加一些奶粉。 || milk powder=奶粉; baking powder=泡打粉 || Mix the powder ___ warm water. || with|to|by || mix A with B = 把 A 和 B 混合。 || 把粉和溫水混合。
practical || adj. || 實用的 || This is a practical gift. || 這是一份實用的禮物。 || practical skills=實用技能 || Learning to cook is more practical ___ learning to play video games. || than|then|as || 比較級 + than。 || 學做菜比學打電動更實用。
precious || adj. || 珍貴的 || Time is precious. || 時間是寶貴的。 || precious stone=寶石 || Nothing is ___ precious than health. || more|most|much || 比較級 more + than。 || 沒有什麼比健康更珍貴。
preparation || n. || 準備 || The preparation took two weeks. || 準備工作花了兩週。 || in preparation for=為…做準備 || The students are studying hard in preparation ___ the exam. || for|of|to || in preparation for = 為…做準備。 || 學生們正在為考試努力準備。
presence || n. || 出席；存在 || Thank you for your presence. || 謝謝你的蒞臨。 || in the presence of=在…面前 || Please don't smoke in the presence ___ children. || of|for|with || in the presence of = 在…面前。 || 請不要在孩子面前抽菸。
pretend || v. || 假裝 || He pretended to be asleep. || 他假裝睡著了。 || pretend to V=假裝做… || He pretended ___ asleep when his mom came in. || to be|being|be || pretend to + 原形動詞。 || 媽媽進來時他假裝睡著了。
prevent || v. || 預防；阻止 || Washing hands can prevent disease. || 洗手可以預防疾病。 || prevent A from V-ing=阻止 A 做… || The heavy rain prevented us ___ going out. || from|of|to || prevent sb from + V-ing = 阻止某人做…。 || 大雨讓我們無法出門。
previous || adj. || 先前的 || I met him on a previous trip. || 我在之前的一次旅行中認識他。 || previous experience=先前的經驗 || No previous experience is required ___ this job. || for|of|to || required for = 對…有要求。 || 這份工作不需要相關經驗。
probable || adj. || 可能的 || It is probable that it will rain. || 很可能會下雨。 || highly probable=極有可能 || It is probable ___ the flight will be delayed. || that|what|which || It is probable that… = …很可能。 || 班機很可能會延誤。
process || n./v. || 過程 || Learning a language is a long process. || 學習語言是一個漫長的過程。 || in the process of=在…的過程中 || The new library is still ___ the process of being built. || in|on|at || in the process of = 在…的過程中。 || 新圖書館仍在興建中。
producer || n. || 製作人；生產者 || He is a movie producer. || 他是電影製作人。 || film producer=電影製片 || Taiwan is one of the biggest producers ___ computer chips. || of|for|in || the producer of = …的生產者。 || 台灣是電腦晶片最大的生產者之一。
product || n. || 產品 || This product is made in Taiwan. || 這個產品是台灣製造的。 || new product=新產品 || The new product ___ be available next month. || will|is|has || will + 原形動詞 be。 || 新產品將在下個月上市。
professor || n. || 教授 || She is a professor of history. || 她是歷史教授。 || university professor=大學教授 || The professor ___ class I took last year is retiring. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 我去年修的那門課的教授要退休了。
profit || n. || 利潤 || The company made a big profit. || 這家公司賺了大筆利潤。 || make a profit=獲利 || The company made a profit ___ ten million dollars last year. || of|for|in || a profit of + 金額 = …的利潤。 || 這家公司去年獲利一千萬元。
promote || v. || 促進；升職 || She was promoted to manager. || 她升為經理。 || be promoted to=升為… || She was promoted ___ manager last month. || to|as|for || be promoted to = 升職為…。 || 她上個月升為經理。
pronounce || v. || 發音 || How do you pronounce this word? || 這個字怎麼唸？ || pronounce correctly=正確發音 || This word is difficult ___ pronounce. || to|for|of || 形容詞 + to V（難以…）。 || 這個字很難唸。
proof || n. || 證明；證據 || Do you have any proof? || 你有任何證據嗎？ || proof of=…的證據 || You need to show proof ___ age to buy this. || of|for|to || proof of = …的證明。 || 你需要出示年齡證明才能購買。
property || n. || 財產 || This house is my property. || 這間房子是我的財產。 || private property=私人財產 || This house is private property, so you ___ enter without permission. || must not|must|have to || must not = 禁止、不可以。 || 這間房子是私人財產，所以未經許可不得進入。
protection || n. || 保護 || Wear a helmet for protection. || 戴安全帽以保護自己。 || protection from=免於…的保護 || Sunscreen provides protection ___ the sun. || from|of|with || protection from = 免於…的保護。 || 防曬乳能保護你免受陽光傷害。
pub || n. || 酒吧 || They went to a pub after work. || 他們下班後去酒吧。 || local pub=當地酒吧 || Young people under 18 ___ not allowed to enter the pub. || are|is|do || 被動語態；Young people 複數用 are。 || 未滿十八歲的年輕人不准進入酒吧。
pump || n./v. || 抽水機；打氣筒 || Use the pump to fill the tire. || 用打氣筒幫輪胎打氣。 || pump up=打氣 || He used a pump ___ fill the tire with air. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他用打氣筒幫輪胎打氣。
punch || v./n. || 用拳頭打 || He punched the wall in anger. || 他生氣地捶牆。 || punch sb=揍某人 || He was so angry ___ he punched the wall. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 他氣到捶牆。
puppet || n. || 木偶 || The kids watched a puppet show. || 孩子們看了一場偶戲。 || puppet show=木偶戲 || Taiwanese glove puppetry is a traditional art ___ has a long history. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（art）。 || 台灣布袋戲是一種有悠久歷史的傳統藝術。
pure || adj. || 純的 || This is pure gold. || 這是純金。 || pure water=純水 || This necklace is made of pure gold, ___ it is very expensive. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這條項鍊是純金打造的，所以非常昂貴。
purse || n. || 錢包 || She lost her purse. || 她弄丟了錢包。 || leather purse=皮製錢包 || She realized that she ___ her purse on the bus. || had left|has left|leaves || 「發現」之前已發生，用過去完成式 had + p.p.。 || 她發現自己把錢包忘在公車上了。
queer || adj. || 古怪的 || That's a queer idea. || 那是個古怪的點子。 || queer feeling=奇怪的感覺 || I had a queer feeling that someone ___ watching me. || was|were|is || 主要子句過去式，名詞子句也用過去進行式；someone 用 was。 || 我有種奇怪的感覺，好像有人在看我。
quit || v. || 停止；辭職 || He quit his job last month. || 他上個月辭職了。 || quit smoking=戒菸 || My uncle quit ___ ten years ago. || smoking|to smoke|smoke || quit + V-ing = 戒掉、停止做…。 || 我叔叔十年前就戒菸了。
quote || v./n. || 引用 || He quoted a famous saying. || 他引用了一句名言。 || quote from=引用自… || The speaker quoted ___ a famous poem. || from|of|with || quote from = 引用自…。 || 演講者引用了一首知名的詩。
racial || adj. || 種族的 || Racial discrimination is wrong. || 種族歧視是錯的。 || racial discrimination=種族歧視 || Racial discrimination should not ___ tolerated in any society. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 任何社會都不應容忍種族歧視。
rag || n. || 破布 || Clean the table with a rag. || 用抹布擦桌子。 || in rags=衣衫襤褸 || Clean the window ___ a wet rag. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 用濕抹布擦窗戶。
rank || n./v. || 等級；排名 || The team ranks first in the league. || 這支隊伍在聯盟中排名第一。 || rank first=排名第一 || Taiwan ranks among the safest countries ___ the world. || in|at|on || 最高級 + in the world。 || 台灣名列世界上最安全的國家之一。
rate || n. || 比率；速度 || The birth rate is falling. || 出生率正在下降。 || birth rate=出生率; at a rate of=以…的速度 || The birth rate in Taiwan ___ been falling for years. || has|have|is || 現在完成進行式；The birth rate 單數用 has。 || 台灣的出生率多年來持續下降。
raw || adj. || 生的 || Do you eat raw fish? || 你吃生魚片嗎？ || raw fish=生魚; raw material=原料 || Raw meat should ___ kept in the fridge. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 生肉應該放在冰箱保存。
ray || n. || 光線 || A ray of sunlight came through the window. || 一道陽光從窗戶照進來。 || X-ray=X 光 || A ray of hope appeared ___ everything seemed lost. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 就在一切似乎都完了的時候，出現了一線希望。
razor || n. || 刮鬍刀 || He shaves with a razor. || 他用刮鬍刀刮鬍子。 || electric razor=電動刮鬍刀 || Be careful ___ you use a razor. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 使用刮鬍刀時要小心。
react || v. || 反應 || How did she react to the news? || 她對那個消息有什麼反應？ || react to=對…做出反應 || How did he react ___ the bad news? || to|for|with || react to = 對…做出反應。 || 他對那個壞消息有什麼反應？
reaction || n. || 反應 || What was his reaction? || 他的反應是什麼？ || reaction to=對…的反應 || Her reaction ___ the news surprised everyone. || to|for|of || reaction to = 對…的反應。 || 她對這個消息的反應讓大家都很驚訝。
reasonable || adj. || 合理的 || The price is reasonable. || 價格很合理。 || reasonable price=合理的價格 || The price of the hotel is ___ reasonable than I expected. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 這間飯店的價格比我預期的合理。
receipt || n. || 收據 || Please keep your receipt. || 請保留你的收據。 || keep the receipt=保留收據 || You can't return the item ___ a receipt. || without|with|within || without = 沒有…。 || 沒有收據就不能退貨。
receiver || n. || 接收者；話筒 || She picked up the receiver. || 她拿起話筒。 || telephone receiver=電話聽筒 || She picked up the receiver ___ said hello. || and|but|or || 連接兩個先後動作用 and。 || 她拿起話筒說了聲哈囉。
recognize || v. || 認出 || I didn't recognize you. || 我沒認出你。 || recognize sb=認出某人 || I didn't recognize him ___ he had changed so much. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 我沒認出他，因為他變了很多。
recorder || n. || 錄音機；直笛 || She plays the recorder. || 她吹直笛。 || voice recorder=錄音筆 || Most students in Taiwan learn to play ___ recorder in elementary school. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 台灣大多數學生在小學學吹直笛。
rectangle || n. || 長方形 || A rectangle has four right angles. || 長方形有四個直角。 || rectangle shape=長方形 || The table is ___ the shape of a rectangle. || in|on|at || in the shape of = 呈…的形狀。 || 這張桌子是長方形的。
reduce || v. || 減少 || We should reduce waste. || 我們應該減少浪費。 || reduce waste=減少浪費 || We should reduce the amount of plastic ___ we use every day. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 use 的受詞。 || 我們應該減少每天使用的塑膠量。
regional || adj. || 地區的 || This is a regional festival. || 這是一個地方性的節慶。 || regional food=地方特色食物 || Each part of Taiwan has ___ own regional food. || its|it's|their || Each part 單數，所有格用 its。 || 台灣每個地方都有自己的地方美食。
regret || v./n. || 後悔 || I regret saying that. || 我後悔說了那句話。 || regret + V-ing=後悔做了… || I regret not ___ harder when I was young. || studying|to study|study || regret + (not) V-ing = 後悔（沒）做過…。 || 我後悔年輕時沒有更用功。
