# 學測 L3 18：subtract – tide
subtract || v. || 減去 || Subtract 5 from 10. || 十減五。 || subtract A from B=從 B 減去 A || If you subtract 3 ___ 10, you get 7. || from|of|by || subtract A from B = 從 B 減去 A。 || 十減三等於七。
suburb || n. || 郊區 || They live in the suburbs. || 他們住在郊區。 || in the suburbs=在郊區 || Many people move to the suburbs ___ houses are cheaper there. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 很多人搬到郊區，因為那裡的房子比較便宜。
suck || v. || 吸 || The baby is sucking its thumb. || 寶寶在吸吮大拇指。 || suck up=吸起 || Babies often suck ___ thumbs. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 thumbs 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 嬰兒常吸吮自己的大拇指。
suffer || v. || 受苦；罹患 || He suffers from headaches. || 他受頭痛所苦。 || suffer from=罹患…；受…之苦 || Many people suffer ___ allergies in spring. || from|of|with || suffer from = 受…之苦。 || 很多人在春天受過敏所苦。
sufficient || adj. || 足夠的 || We have sufficient food. || 我們有足夠的食物。 || sufficient time=足夠的時間 || Is one hour sufficient ___ the test? || for|to|of || be sufficient for + 名詞 = 足以應付…。 || 一小時足夠寫完這份考試嗎？
suicide || n. || 自殺 || Suicide prevention is important. || 預防自殺很重要。 || suicide prevention=自殺防治 || The school holds talks on suicide prevention ___ students know where to get help. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 學校舉辦自殺防治講座，讓學生知道可以向哪裡求助。
sum || n. || 總數；金額 || He paid a large sum of money. || 他付了一大筆錢。 || a large sum of=一大筆… || A large sum of money ___ donated to the hospital last year. || was|were|is || a sum of money 視為單數，過去被動用 was + p.p.。 || 去年有一大筆錢捐給了醫院。
summary || n. || 摘要 || Write a summary of the story. || 寫下這個故事的摘要。 || in summary=總之 || Write a summary ___ the article in 100 words. || of|for|to || a summary of = …的摘要。 || 用一百字寫出這篇文章的摘要。
summit || n. || 山頂；高峰會 || They reached the summit of the mountain. || 他們登上了山頂。 || reach the summit=登頂 || It took them five hours ___ reach the summit. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 他們花了五小時才登上山頂。
superior || adj. || 較優的 || This product is superior to that one. || 這個產品比那個好。 || be superior to=優於… || This new phone is superior ___ the old one in many ways. || to|than|from || be superior to = 優於…（不用 than）。 || 這支新手機在很多方面都優於舊的。
surround || v. || 包圍 || The house is surrounded by trees. || 房子被樹木環繞。 || be surrounded by=被…包圍 || The village is surrounded ___ mountains. || by|with|from || be surrounded by = 被…包圍。 || 那個村莊被群山環繞。
survey || n./v. || 調查 || The survey shows that most students like sports. || 調查顯示大多數學生喜歡運動。 || do a survey=做調查 || According ___ the survey, most teenagers sleep less than seven hours. || to|with|for || according to = 根據…。 || 根據調查，大多數青少年睡不到七小時。
survivor || n. || 倖存者 || He was the only survivor. || 他是唯一的倖存者。 || the only survivor=唯一的倖存者 || The survivors ___ taken to the hospital right away. || were|was|are || 被動語態；survivors 複數，過去用 were。 || 倖存者立刻被送往醫院。
suspect || v./n. || 懷疑；嫌疑犯 || The police arrested the suspect. || 警方逮捕了嫌疑犯。 || suspect sb of=懷疑某人… || The man was suspected ___ stealing the car. || of|for|with || be suspected of = 被懷疑…。 || 那名男子涉嫌偷車。
suspicion || n. || 懷疑 || He was arrested on suspicion of murder. || 他因涉嫌謀殺被捕。 || on suspicion of=涉嫌… || The man was arrested ___ suspicion of stealing. || on|in|with || on suspicion of = 涉嫌…。 || 那名男子因涉嫌竊盜被捕。
swan || n. || 天鵝 || Two swans are swimming in the lake. || 兩隻天鵝在湖裡游泳。 || black swan=黑天鵝 || The ugly duckling grew up ___ became a beautiful swan. || and|but|or || 連接兩個先後動作用 and。 || 醜小鴨長大後變成了一隻美麗的天鵝。
swear || v. || 發誓；咒罵 || I swear I didn't do it. || 我發誓不是我做的。 || swear to God=對天發誓 || I swear ___ I will never lie to you again. || that|what|which || swear that… = 發誓…。 || 我發誓再也不會對你說謊。
sweat || n./v. || 汗 || He was covered in sweat. || 他渾身是汗。 || no sweat=小事一樁 || After running, he was covered ___ sweat. || in|of|by || be covered in = 覆滿…。 || 跑完步後他渾身是汗。
swell || v. || 腫脹 || My ankle began to swell. || 我的腳踝開始腫起來。 || swell up=腫起 || Her ankle started to swell ___ she twisted it. || after|before|since || 扭到「之後」開始腫，用 after。 || 她扭到腳踝後，腳踝開始腫起來。
swift || adj. || 迅速的 || The swift runner won the race. || 那位跑得飛快的選手贏得比賽。 || swift action=迅速的行動 || Thanks to the swift action of the firefighters, no one ___ hurt. || was|were|is || 被動語態；no one 單數，過去用 was。 || 多虧消防員迅速行動，沒有人受傷。
sword || n. || 劍 || The knight drew his sword. || 騎士拔出了劍。 || the pen is mightier than the sword=文勝於武 || The pen is mightier ___ the sword. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 筆勝於劍。
tablet || n. || 平板；藥片 || I read e-books on my tablet. || 我用平板看電子書。 || tablet computer=平板電腦 || Take two tablets ___ day after meals. || a|an|the || 每天用 a day。 || 每天飯後服用兩片。
tag || n. || 標籤 || Check the price tag. || 看看價格標籤。 || price tag=價格標籤; name tag=名牌 || Please wear your name tag ___ the event. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 活動期間請佩戴名牌。
tailor || n. || 裁縫師 || The tailor made a suit for him. || 裁縫師為他做了一套西裝。 || tailor-made=量身訂做的 || He had his suit ___ by a tailor. || made|make|making || have + 物 + p.p. = 請人把某物…（使役）。 || 他請裁縫師幫他做了一套西裝。
talent || n. || 天賦 || She has a talent for music. || 她有音樂天賦。 || have a talent for=有…的天分 || He has a natural talent ___ drawing. || for|of|in || a talent for = …的天分。 || 他有繪畫的天分。
talkative || adj. || 健談的 || She is very talkative. || 她非常健談。 || talkative person=健談的人 || She is the ___ talkative person in our family. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 她是我們家最健談的人。
tame || adj./v. || 溫馴的；馴服 || The horse is tame. || 那匹馬很溫馴。 || tame animal=溫馴的動物 || It took a long time ___ tame the wild horse. || to|for|of || It takes + 時間 + to V。 || 馴服那匹野馬花了很長的時間。
tangerine || n. || 橘子 || We eat tangerines during Chinese New Year. || 我們在農曆新年吃橘子。 || tangerine=橘子 || Tangerines are a symbol ___ good luck in Chinese culture. || of|for|to || a symbol of = …的象徵。 || 在中華文化中，橘子象徵好運。
tank || n. || 槽；坦克 || The fish tank needs cleaning. || 魚缸需要清理了。 || fish tank=魚缸; gas tank=油箱 || The fish tank needs ___ every week. || cleaning|clean|to cleaning || need + V-ing = 需要被…（被動意味）。 || 魚缸每週都需要清理。
tap || v./n. || 輕敲；水龍頭 || She tapped me on the shoulder. || 她輕拍我的肩膀。 || tap water=自來水 || Someone tapped me ___ the shoulder. || on|in|at || tap sb on + 身體部位。 || 有人輕拍了我的肩膀。
tasty || adj. || 美味的 || The food here is tasty. || 這裡的食物很好吃。 || tasty food=美味的食物 || This is the ___ tasty cake I've ever eaten. || most|more|much || 最高級 the most + 形容詞。 || 這是我吃過最好吃的蛋糕。
tease || v. || 取笑 || Don't tease your little sister. || 不要取笑你妹妹。 || tease sb about=拿…取笑某人 || He was teased ___ his big ears when he was young. || about|for|at || tease sb about sth = 拿某事取笑某人。 || 他小時候因為耳朵大被取笑。
technical || adj. || 技術的 || There was a technical problem. || 出現了技術上的問題。 || technical problem=技術問題 || The flight was delayed ___ a technical problem. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 班機因技術問題延誤。
technique || n. || 技巧 || She learned a new painting technique. || 她學了一種新的繪畫技巧。 || learning technique=學習技巧 || Using mind maps is a useful technique ___ learning new words. || for|to|of || a technique for + V-ing = …的技巧。 || 使用心智圖是學習新單字的實用技巧。
teenage || adj. || 青少年的 || She has two teenage children. || 她有兩個十幾歲的孩子。 || teenage years=青少年時期 || Many teenage students feel stressed ___ exams. || about|with|of || feel stressed about = 為…感到壓力。 || 許多青少年學生對考試感到壓力。
temper || n. || 脾氣 || He has a bad temper. || 他脾氣很壞。 || lose one's temper=發脾氣 || Try not ___ your temper when you are angry. || to lose|losing|lose || try not to + 原形動詞。 || 生氣時試著不要發脾氣。
temporary || adj. || 暫時的 || This is only a temporary job. || 這只是一份臨時工作。 || temporary job=臨時工作 || The road is closed ___ a temporary basis. || on|in|at || on a temporary basis = 暫時地。 || 這條路暫時封閉。
tend || v. || 傾向 || People tend to eat more in winter. || 人們在冬天往往吃得比較多。 || tend to V=傾向於… || Teenagers tend ___ stay up late. || to|for|in || tend to + 原形動詞。 || 青少年往往熬夜。
tender || adj. || 嫩的；溫柔的 || The steak is tender. || 牛排很嫩。 || tender meat=嫩肉 || The meat is so tender ___ it melts in your mouth. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這肉嫩到入口即化。
tent || n. || 帳篷 || We slept in a tent. || 我們睡在帳篷裡。 || put up a tent=搭帳篷 || We put up our tent ___ it got dark. || before|after|since || 天黑「之前」搭好，用 before。 || 我們在天黑前搭好了帳篷。
terrific || adj. || 極好的 || That's a terrific idea! || 那真是個好主意！ || terrific job=做得很好 || You did a terrific job, ___ you? || didn't|weren't|did || 肯定句（did）的附加問句用否定 didn't。 || 你做得很棒，不是嗎？
territory || n. || 領土；地盤 || Animals protect their territory. || 動物保護自己的地盤。 || enemy territory=敵方領土 || Dogs often mark ___ territory. || their|them|theirs || 名詞 territory 前用所有格形容詞 their。 || 狗常會標記自己的地盤。
thankful || adj. || 感謝的 || I'm thankful for your help. || 我很感謝你的幫忙。 || be thankful for=感謝… || We should be thankful ___ what we have. || for|to|of || be thankful for = 對…心存感謝。 || 我們應該對擁有的一切心存感激。
theory || n. || 理論 || Einstein's theory changed science. || 愛因斯坦的理論改變了科學。 || in theory=理論上 || ___ theory, the plan should work. || In|On|At || in theory = 理論上。 || 理論上，這個計畫應該可行。
thirst || n. || 口渴 || I drank water to quench my thirst. || 我喝水解渴。 || thirst for knowledge=求知慾 || She has a strong thirst ___ knowledge. || for|of|to || a thirst for = 對…的渴望。 || 她有強烈的求知慾。
thread || n. || 線 || She sewed the button with a needle and thread. || 她用針線縫鈕扣。 || needle and thread=針線 || She sewed the button ___ a needle and thread. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 她用針線縫鈕扣。
threat || n. || 威脅 || Climate change is a threat to the world. || 氣候變遷是對世界的威脅。 || a threat to=對…的威脅 || Plastic pollution is a serious threat ___ sea animals. || to|for|of || a threat to = 對…的威脅。 || 塑膠污染對海洋動物是嚴重的威脅。
threaten || v. || 威脅 || The storm threatened the town. || 暴風雨威脅著小鎮。 || threaten to V=威脅要做… || Many animals are threatened ___ climate change. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 許多動物受到氣候變遷的威脅。
thumb || n. || 拇指 || He gave me a thumbs-up. || 他對我比了個讚。 || thumbs-up=讚; rule of thumb=經驗法則 || As a rule of thumb, you should drink eight glasses of water ___ day. || a|an|the || 每天用 a day。 || 根據經驗法則，你每天應該喝八杯水。
tide || n. || 潮汐 || The tide is coming in. || 漲潮了。 || high tide=漲潮; low tide=退潮 || You can walk to the island ___ the tide is low. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 退潮時你可以走到那座小島。
