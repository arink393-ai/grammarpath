# 學測 L3 20：violence – zone
violence || n. || 暴力 || Violence is never the answer. || 暴力永遠不是解決之道。 || domestic violence=家庭暴力 || Violence should never ___ used to solve problems. || be|is|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 永遠不應該用暴力解決問題。
violent || adj. || 暴力的；猛烈的 || The movie is too violent for kids. || 這部電影對小孩來說太暴力了。 || violent storm=猛烈的暴風雨 || The movie is too violent ___ children to watch. || for|of|to || too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 這部電影太暴力，不適合小孩觀看。
violet || n./adj. || 紫羅蘭；紫色 || She wore a violet dress. || 她穿了一件紫色洋裝。 || violet flowers=紫羅蘭 || Roses are red, violets ___ blue. || are|is|be || violets 複數，用 are。 || 玫瑰是紅的，紫羅蘭是藍的。
visible || adj. || 看得見的 || The mountain is visible from here. || 從這裡看得見那座山。 || clearly visible=清楚可見 || On a clear day, Taipei 101 is visible ___ far away. || from|of|at || from far away = 從遠處。 || 天氣晴朗時，從很遠的地方就能看到台北 101。
vision || n. || 視力；願景 || She has good vision. || 她的視力很好。 || poor vision=視力不佳 || Looking at screens for too long can damage ___ vision. || your|you|yours || 名詞 vision 前用所有格形容詞 your。 || 盯著螢幕太久會傷害你的視力。
vitamin || n. || 維他命 || Oranges are rich in vitamin C. || 柳橙富含維他命 C。 || vitamin C=維他命 C || Fruits and vegetables are rich ___ vitamins. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 蔬菜水果富含維他命。
vivid || adj. || 生動的；鮮明的 || She has a vivid imagination. || 她的想像力很豐富。 || vivid memory=鮮明的記憶 || I still have vivid memories ___ my first day at school. || of|for|in || memories of = …的回憶。 || 我對上學第一天仍有鮮明的記憶。
vocabulary || n. || 字彙 || Reading helps you build your vocabulary. || 閱讀幫助你增加字彙量。 || build vocabulary=累積字彙 || Reading English novels is a good way ___ improve your vocabulary. || to|for|of || a way to V = 做…的方法。 || 閱讀英文小說是增加字彙量的好方法。
volleyball || n. || 排球 || We play volleyball in PE class. || 我們在體育課打排球。 || play volleyball=打排球 || Beach volleyball is a sport ___ is played on sand. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a sport）。 || 沙灘排球是一項在沙灘上進行的運動。
volume || n. || 音量；冊 || Please turn down the volume. || 請把音量調小。 || turn up the volume=調高音量 || Could you turn ___ the volume? It's too loud. || down|off|out || turn down = 調低（音量）。 || 你可以把音量調小嗎？太大聲了。
voter || n. || 選民 || Voters went to the polls. || 選民前往投票。 || young voters=年輕選民 || Young voters ___ encouraged to vote in the election. || are|is|be || 被動語態；voters 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 年輕選民被鼓勵在選舉中投票。
wage || n. || 工資 || The minimum wage has gone up. || 基本工資調漲了。 || minimum wage=基本工資 || The minimum wage in Taiwan ___ increased this year. || has|have|be || 現在完成式；The minimum wage 單數用 has。 || 台灣的基本工資今年調漲了。
wagon || n. || 馬車；貨車 || The farmer loaded the wagon with hay. || 農夫把乾草裝上貨車。 || horse-drawn wagon=馬車 || The wagon was pulled ___ two horses. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 那輛貨車由兩匹馬拉著。
wander || v. || 漫步；閒逛 || We wandered around the old town. || 我們在老城區閒逛。 || wander around=閒逛 || We wandered around the old town ___ the whole afternoon. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 我們在老城區閒逛了一整個下午。
warmth || n. || 溫暖 || I felt the warmth of the sun. || 我感受到陽光的溫暖。 || the warmth of=…的溫暖 || I will never forget the warmth ___ the people there. || of|for|in || the warmth of = …的溫暖。 || 我永遠不會忘記那裡的人的溫暖。
warn || v. || 警告 || The teacher warned us not to be late. || 老師警告我們不要遲到。 || warn sb not to V=警告某人不要… || The doctor warned him ___ smoke. || not to|to not|not || warn sb not to + 原形動詞。 || 醫生警告他不要抽菸。
waterfall || n. || 瀑布 || The waterfall is very beautiful. || 那座瀑布很美。 || Niagara Falls=尼加拉瀑布 || We hiked for two hours ___ see the waterfall. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們健行了兩小時去看瀑布。
wax || n. || 蠟 || Candles are made of wax. || 蠟燭是用蠟做的。 || candle wax=蠟燭蠟 || Candles are made ___ wax. || of|by|at || be made of = 由…製成。 || 蠟燭是用蠟做的。
weaken || v. || 使變弱 || The illness weakened him. || 那場病讓他變虛弱。 || weaken one's health=削弱健康 || Lack of sleep can weaken your immune system, ___ you may get sick easily. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 睡眠不足會削弱免疫力，所以你可能容易生病。
wealthy || adj. || 富有的 || He comes from a wealthy family. || 他出身富裕家庭。 || wealthy family=富裕家庭 || He is wealthy, ___ he isn't happy. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 他很有錢，但並不快樂。
weapon || n. || 武器 || Weapons are not allowed on the plane. || 飛機上禁止攜帶武器。 || nuclear weapon=核武 || Weapons of any kind ___ not allowed on the plane. || are|is|be || Weapons 複數，用 are。 || 飛機上禁止攜帶任何武器。
weave || v. || 編織 || She weaves baskets from bamboo. || 她用竹子編籃子。 || weave a basket=編籃子 || The women in the village weave baskets ___ bamboo. || from|by|at || weave A from B = 用 B 編織 A。 || 村裡的婦女用竹子編籃子。
web || n. || 網；網路 || The spider spun a web. || 蜘蛛織了一張網。 || spider web=蜘蛛網; the Web=網際網路 || You can find a lot of information ___ the Web. || on|in|at || on the Web = 在網路上。 || 你可以在網路上找到很多資訊。
wed || v. || 結婚 || They wed in the spring. || 他們在春天結婚。 || newly wed=新婚 || The couple will wed ___ June. || in|on|at || 月份前面用 in。 || 這對情侶將在六月結婚。
weed || n./v. || 雜草；除草 || Grandpa is pulling weeds in the garden. || 爺爺在花園拔雜草。 || pull weeds=拔草 || The garden is full ___ weeds. || of|with|by || be full of = 充滿…。 || 花園裡長滿了雜草。
weekly || adj./adv. || 每週的 || We have a weekly meeting. || 我們每週開一次會。 || weekly meeting=週會 || We have a weekly meeting ___ Monday mornings. || on|in|at || 特定某天的早上用 on。 || 我們每週一早上開週會。
weep || v. || 哭泣 || It is natural to weep when you are sad. || 難過時哭泣是很自然的。 || weep for joy=喜極而泣 || The mother began to weep ___ joy when she saw her son. || for|at|in || weep for joy = 喜極而泣。 || 那位母親看到兒子時喜極而泣。
wheat || n. || 小麥 || Bread is made from wheat. || 麵包是用小麥做的。 || wheat flour=麵粉 || Bread and noodles are made ___ wheat. || from|at|by || be made from = 由…製成（看不出原料）。 || 麵包和麵條是用小麥做的。
whip || v./n. || 鞭打；攪打 || Whip the cream until it is thick. || 把鮮奶油打到濃稠。 || whipped cream=鮮奶油 || Whip the cream ___ it becomes thick. || until|since|while || until = 直到…。 || 把鮮奶油打到變濃稠為止。
whistle || n./v. || 口哨；吹口哨 || The referee blew the whistle. || 裁判吹了哨子。 || blow a whistle=吹哨 || The game ended ___ the referee blew the whistle. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 裁判吹哨時比賽就結束了。
wicked || adj. || 邪惡的 || The wicked witch put a spell on the princess. || 邪惡的女巫對公主下了咒。 || wicked witch=邪惡的女巫 || The princess ___ put under a spell by the wicked witch. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 公主被邪惡的女巫下了咒。
widen || v. || 加寬 || They are widening the road. || 他們正在拓寬道路。 || widen the gap=擴大差距 || The road ___ widened last year to reduce traffic jams. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這條路去年拓寬以減少塞車。
wipe || v. || 擦 || Wipe the table, please. || 請擦桌子。 || wipe off=擦掉 || Wipe the table ___ a wet cloth. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 用濕布擦桌子。
wisdom || n. || 智慧 || My grandpa is full of wisdom. || 我爺爺充滿智慧。 || words of wisdom=智慧之言 || Wisdom comes ___ experience. || from|of|with || come from = 來自…。 || 智慧來自經驗。
wrap || v. || 包裝 || She wrapped the gift in red paper. || 她用紅紙包禮物。 || wrap up=包好 || She wrapped the gift ___ colorful paper. || in|at|by || wrap A in B = 用 B 包 A。 || 她用彩色紙包禮物。
wrist || n. || 手腕 || She wears a watch on her wrist. || 她手腕上戴著一支錶。 || sprain one's wrist=扭傷手腕 || He sprained his wrist ___ playing tennis. || while|during|since || while + V-ing（分詞構句）= 在…的時候。 || 他在打網球時扭傷了手腕。
yearly || adj./adv. || 每年的 || We have a yearly health check. || 我們每年做一次健康檢查。 || yearly checkup=年度健檢 || The festival is held yearly ___ October. || in|on|at || 月份前面用 in。 || 這個節慶每年十月舉辦。
yell || v. || 大叫 || Don't yell at me. || 不要對我大吼。 || yell at=對…吼叫 || The coach yelled ___ the players to run faster. || at|of|on || yell at sb = 對某人大吼。 || 教練對球員大吼要他們跑快一點。
yolk || n. || 蛋黃 || I don't like egg yolk. || 我不喜歡蛋黃。 || egg yolk=蛋黃 || Separate the yolk ___ the egg white. || from|of|with || separate A from B = 把 A 和 B 分開。 || 把蛋黃和蛋白分開。
youngster || n. || 年輕人 || The youngsters are playing basketball. || 年輕人在打籃球。 || youngster=年輕人 || This program is designed ___ youngsters who want to learn coding. || for|to|with || be designed for = 為…設計。 || 這個課程是為想學寫程式的年輕人設計的。
zipper || n. || 拉鍊 || The zipper on my jacket is broken. || 我外套的拉鍊壞了。 || zip up=拉上拉鍊 || The zipper on my bag ___ broken yesterday. || got|get|gets || 描述過去的事用過去式 got（got broken = 壞掉）。 || 我包包的拉鍊昨天壞了。
zone || n. || 區域 || This is a no-parking zone. || 這是禁止停車區。 || time zone=時區; school zone=學校區域 || Drivers must slow down ___ school zones. || in|on|at || 在某區域內用 in。 || 駕駛在學校區域必須減速。
