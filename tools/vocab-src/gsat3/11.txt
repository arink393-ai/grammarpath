# 學測 L3 11：lord – moth
lord || n. || 君主；上帝 || The lord lived in a castle. || 領主住在城堡裡。 || Lord=主（上帝） || The lord, ___ owned all the land, was very rich. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 擁有所有土地的領主非常富有。
loser || n. || 失敗者；輸家 || Don't be a bad loser. || 輸了別不服氣。 || good loser=輸得起的人 || A good loser congratulates the winner ___ complaining. || instead of|because of|in spite || instead of + V-ing = 而不是…。 || 輸得起的人會恭喜贏家，而不是抱怨。
lover || n. || 愛好者；情人 || He is a music lover. || 他是個音樂愛好者。 || music lover=音樂愛好者; nature lover=大自然愛好者 || As a nature lover, she spends most weekends ___ in the mountains. || hiking|to hike|hike || spend + 時間 + V-ing。 || 身為大自然愛好者，她大部分的週末都在山上健行。
luggage || n. || 行李 || How much luggage do you have? || 你有多少行李？ || a piece of luggage=一件行李 || How ___ luggage are you taking? || much|many|a few || luggage 不可數，用 How much。 || 你要帶多少行李？
lung || n. || 肺 || Smoking damages your lungs. || 抽菸會傷害你的肺。 || lung cancer=肺癌 || Smoking is one of the main causes ___ lung cancer. || of|for|to || the cause of = …的原因。 || 抽菸是肺癌的主要原因之一。
magical || adj. || 魔法的；神奇的 || It was a magical night. || 那是神奇的一夜。 || magical power=魔力 || The fireworks made the night ___ magical. || so|such|very much || make + 受詞 + so + 形容詞 = 使…如此…。 || 煙火讓那一夜如此神奇。
magician || n. || 魔術師 || The magician made the coin disappear. || 魔術師讓硬幣消失了。 || famous magician=知名魔術師 || The magician made the rabbit ___. || disappear|to disappear|disappearing || make + 受詞 + 原形動詞（使役動詞）。 || 魔術師讓兔子消失了。
magnet || n. || 磁鐵 || The magnet attracts metal. || 磁鐵吸引金屬。 || fridge magnet=冰箱磁鐵 || Magnets can attract objects ___ are made of iron. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（objects）。 || 磁鐵可以吸引鐵製的物品。
maid || n. || 女僕；女傭 || The maid cleans the rooms every day. || 女傭每天打掃房間。 || house maid=女傭 || The rooms ___ cleaned by the maid every morning. || are|is|be || 被動語態；The rooms 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 房間每天早上由女傭打掃。
majority || n. || 大多數 || The majority of students agreed. || 大多數學生同意。 || the majority of=大多數的… || The majority of the students ___ against the new rule. || are|is|be || the majority of + 複數名詞，動詞用複數 are。 || 大多數學生反對新規定。
mall || n. || 購物中心 || Let's go to the mall. || 我們去購物中心吧。 || shopping mall=購物中心 || The new mall, ___ opened last month, is always crowded. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 上個月開幕的新購物中心總是擠滿人。
marble || n. || 大理石；彈珠 || The floor is made of marble. || 地板是大理石做的。 || marble floor=大理石地板 || The statue is carved ___ white marble. || from|by|with || carve A from B = 用 B 雕刻成 A。 || 這座雕像是用白色大理石雕成的。
march || v./n. || 行進 || The soldiers marched down the street. || 士兵們沿著街道行進。 || march on=向前行進 || The students marched ___ the street to protest. || down|to down|at || march down the street = 沿著街道行進。 || 學生們沿著街道遊行抗議。
marker || n. || 麥克筆；標記 || Write your name with a marker. || 用麥克筆寫你的名字。 || permanent marker=油性麥克筆 || Please write your answers ___ a black marker. || with|by|in || 用某工具寫用 with。 || 請用黑色麥克筆寫下答案。
marvelous || adj. || 很棒的 || We had a marvelous time. || 我們度過了非常美好的時光。 || marvelous view=絕佳的景色 || The view from the top of the mountain was ___ marvelous that we took many photos. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 山頂的景色美到我們拍了好多照片。
mathematical || adj. || 數學的 || She has great mathematical skills. || 她數學能力很強。 || mathematical problem=數學題 || He is good at solving mathematical problems ___ a young age. || at|in|on || at a young age = 在年紀很小時。 || 他年紀很小就擅長解數學題。
mayor || n. || 市長 || The mayor gave a speech. || 市長發表了演說。 || city mayor=市長 || The mayor promised ___ more parks in the city. || to build|building|build || promise to + 原形動詞。 || 市長承諾在市區興建更多公園。
meadow || n. || 草地 || Cows are eating grass in the meadow. || 牛在草地上吃草。 || green meadow=綠色草地 || We had a picnic ___ a meadow full of flowers. || in|on|at || 在草地（區域內）用 in。 || 我們在開滿花的草地上野餐。
meaningful || adj. || 有意義的 || It was a meaningful experience. || 那是一次有意義的經驗。 || meaningful life=有意義的人生 || Volunteering is a meaningful way ___ help others. || to|for|of || a way to V = 做…的方式。 || 當志工是幫助他人的有意義方式。
meanwhile || adv. || 同時 || I'll cook dinner. Meanwhile, you can set the table. || 我來煮晚餐，同時你可以擺餐具。 || in the meanwhile=同時 || Dad was cooking. Meanwhile, Mom ___ the table. || was setting|sets|has set || 過去同時進行的動作，用過去進行式。 || 爸爸在煮飯，同時媽媽在擺餐具。
medal || n. || 獎牌 || She won a gold medal. || 她贏得一面金牌。 || gold medal=金牌 || She was awarded a gold medal ___ her excellent performance. || for|of|with || be awarded sth for = 因…獲頒某物。 || 她因表現傑出而獲頒金牌。
medium || adj./n. || 中等的；媒介 || I'd like a medium Coke. || 我要中杯可樂。 || medium size=中號; mass media=大眾媒體 || I'd like my steak cooked ___ medium. || to|at|in || cook sth to medium = 煮到五分熟。 || 我的牛排要五分熟。
melon || n. || 甜瓜 || This melon is very sweet. || 這顆甜瓜很甜。 || watermelon=西瓜 || This melon is ___ sweeter than the one we bought last week. || much|more|very || 比較級前面用 much 加強語氣。 || 這顆甜瓜比上週買的甜得多。
melt || v. || 融化 || The ice is melting. || 冰正在融化。 || melt away=融化消失 || The snow will melt ___ the sun comes out. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 太陽出來時雪就會融化。
mend || v. || 修補 || Can you mend my shirt? || 你可以幫我補襯衫嗎？ || mend a relationship=修補關係 || My grandma helped me mend ___ torn jeans. || my|me|mine || 名詞 jeans 前用所有格形容詞 my。 || 我奶奶幫我補好破掉的牛仔褲。
mental || adj. || 心理的；精神的 || Mental health is as important as physical health. || 心理健康和身體健康一樣重要。 || mental health=心理健康 || Mental health is as important ___ physical health. || as|than|like || as + 形容詞 + as = 和…一樣。 || 心理健康和身體健康一樣重要。
merry || adj. || 歡樂的 || Merry Christmas! || 聖誕快樂！ || Merry Christmas=聖誕快樂 || We wish you a merry Christmas ___ a happy New Year. || and|or|but || 兩個祝福並列用 and。 || 祝你聖誕快樂、新年快樂。
mess || n./v. || 混亂 || Your room is a mess. || 你的房間一團亂。 || make a mess=弄亂; in a mess=一團糟 || Clean up the mess ___ your mom comes home. || before|after|since || 媽媽回家「之前」清理，用 before。 || 在媽媽回家前把這團亂收拾好。
microphone || n. || 麥克風 || Please speak into the microphone. || 請對著麥克風說話。 || microphone=麥克風 || Speak into the microphone so that everyone ___ hear you. || can|could have|to || so that + 主詞 + can = 以便能夠…。 || 對著麥克風說話，這樣大家才聽得到你。
microwave || n./v. || 微波爐 || Heat the soup in the microwave. || 用微波爐熱湯。 || microwave oven=微波爐 || Never put metal ___ a microwave. || in|on|at || 放進微波爐裡用 in。 || 絕對不要把金屬放進微波爐。
mighty || adj. || 強大的 || The mighty river flows to the sea. || 那條大河流向大海。 || mighty king=強大的國王 || The pen is mightier ___ the sword. || than|then|that || 比較級 + than。 || 筆勝於劍（文勝於武）。
minus || prep. || 減 || Ten minus four is six. || 十減四等於六。 || minus=減 || The temperature dropped to minus five degrees ___ night. || at|in|on || at night = 在夜間。 || 夜間氣溫降到零下五度。
miracle || n. || 奇蹟 || It's a miracle that he survived. || 他能活下來真是個奇蹟。 || work miracles=創造奇蹟 || It's a miracle ___ nobody was hurt in the accident. || that|what|which || It is a miracle that… = …真是奇蹟。 || 那場意外中沒有人受傷真是奇蹟。
misery || n. || 痛苦；悲慘 || The war caused a lot of misery. || 戰爭造成許多苦難。 || live in misery=過著悲慘的生活 || Many people lived ___ misery during the war. || in|with|by || live in misery = 過著悲慘的生活。 || 戰爭期間許多人過著悲慘的生活。
missile || n. || 飛彈 || The missile hit the target. || 飛彈擊中了目標。 || missile attack=飛彈攻擊 || The missile ___ launched from a ship. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 飛彈是從一艘船上發射的。
missing || adj. || 失蹤的；遺失的 || My wallet is missing. || 我的皮夾不見了。 || missing person=失蹤人口 || The missing child ___ found by the police last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 失蹤的孩子昨晚被警方找到了。
mission || n. || 任務 || Their mission is to help poor children. || 他們的任務是幫助貧童。 || mission impossible=不可能的任務 || The astronauts successfully completed their mission ___ space. || in|on|at || in space = 在太空中。 || 太空人成功完成了在太空中的任務。
mist || n. || 薄霧 || The mountain is covered in mist. || 山被薄霧籠罩著。 || morning mist=晨霧 || The mountain was covered ___ mist in the morning. || in|of|by || be covered in = 覆滿…。 || 早上山被薄霧籠罩著。
mob || n. || 暴民；人群 || An angry mob gathered outside. || 一群憤怒的民眾聚集在外面。 || angry mob=憤怒的群眾 || The singer was surrounded ___ a mob of fans. || by|with|of || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 那位歌手被一大群粉絲包圍。
mobile || adj. || 可移動的 || Mobile phones are very useful. || 手機很有用。 || mobile phone=手機 || Mobile phones have changed the way people ___. || communicate|communicates|communicating || the way (that) + 子句；people 複數用原形。 || 手機改變了人們溝通的方式。
moist || adj. || 潮濕的 || Keep the soil moist. || 保持土壤濕潤。 || moist soil=濕潤的土壤 || Keep the soil moist ___ the plant can grow well. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 以便…。 || 保持土壤濕潤，植物才能長得好。
moisture || n. || 濕氣 || Plants need moisture. || 植物需要水分。 || absorb moisture=吸收濕氣 || This cream helps your skin keep ___ moisture. || its|it's|their || skin 單數，所有格用 its。 || 這款乳霜幫助皮膚保持水分。
monk || n. || 僧侶 || The monk lives in a temple. || 那位僧侶住在寺廟裡。 || Buddhist monk=佛教僧侶 || The monk, ___ has lived in the temple for 30 years, is very wise. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 在寺廟住了三十年的僧侶非常有智慧。
monster || n. || 怪物 || The kids are afraid of monsters. || 孩子們怕怪物。 || sea monster=海怪 || The movie is about a monster ___ lives under the bed. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a monster）。 || 這部電影是關於一個住在床底下的怪物。
monthly || adj./adv. || 每月的 || We have a monthly meeting. || 我們每個月開一次會。 || monthly salary=月薪 || The club holds a meeting monthly, usually ___ the first Friday of the month. || on|in|at || 特定的日子前面用 on。 || 社團每月開一次會，通常在每月第一個週五。
moral || adj./n. || 道德的；寓意 || What is the moral of the story? || 這個故事的寓意是什麼？ || the moral of the story=故事的寓意 || The moral ___ this story is that honesty is the best policy. || of|for|to || the moral of = …的寓意。 || 這個故事的寓意是誠實為上策。
mosquito || n. || 蚊子 || I was bitten by a mosquito. || 我被蚊子咬了。 || mosquito bite=蚊子叮咬 || Dengue fever is spread ___ mosquitoes. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 登革熱是由蚊子傳播的。
mostly || adv. || 大部分地 || The students are mostly girls. || 學生大部分是女生。 || mostly=大多 || The students in this class are mostly girls, ___ the teacher is a man. || but|so|or || 前後對比用 but。 || 這班的學生大多是女生，但老師是男性。
motel || n. || 汽車旅館 || We stayed at a motel for the night. || 我們在汽車旅館住了一晚。 || cheap motel=便宜的汽車旅館 || We stayed at a motel ___ the highway. || near|nearly|nearby of || near + 地點 = 在…附近。 || 我們住在公路附近的一間汽車旅館。
moth || n. || 蛾 || A moth flew toward the light. || 一隻蛾飛向燈光。 || like a moth to a flame=飛蛾撲火 || Moths are attracted ___ light. || to|by|with || be attracted to = 被…吸引。 || 蛾會被光吸引。
