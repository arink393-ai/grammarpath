# 學測 L3 08：gesture – holy
gesture || n./v. || 手勢；姿態 || He made a friendly gesture. || 他做了一個友善的手勢。 || make a gesture=做手勢 || The same gesture can have different meanings ___ different cultures. || in|on|at || 在…文化中用 in。 || 同樣的手勢在不同文化中可能有不同意義。
glance || v./n. || 瞥見 || She glanced at her watch. || 她瞥了一眼手錶。 || at a glance=一眼就; glance at=瞥一眼 || She glanced ___ her phone during the meeting. || at|to|on || glance at = 瞥一眼。 || 開會時她瞥了一眼手機。
global || adj. || 全球的 || Global warming is a serious problem. || 全球暖化是個嚴重的問題。 || global warming=全球暖化 || Global warming is caused mainly ___ human activities. || by|with|from || 被動語態中動作執行者用 by。 || 全球暖化主要是由人類活動造成的。
glory || n. || 光榮 || The team returned home in glory. || 隊伍光榮返鄉。 || in glory=光榮地 || The soldiers fought for the glory ___ their country. || of|for|to || the glory of = …的榮耀。 || 士兵們為國家的榮耀而戰。
glow || v./n. || 發光 || The stars glow in the night sky. || 星星在夜空中閃耀。 || glow in the dark=在黑暗中發光 || These stickers glow ___ the dark. || in|at|on || glow in the dark = 在黑暗中發光。 || 這些貼紙在黑暗中會發光。
golf || n. || 高爾夫球 || He plays golf every weekend. || 他每個週末打高爾夫。 || play golf=打高爾夫 || He has been playing golf ___ over twenty years. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間，搭配現在完成進行式。 || 他打高爾夫已經超過二十年了。
gossip || n./v. || 八卦；閒聊 || Don't believe the gossip. || 別相信那些八卦。 || gossip about=說…的閒話 || It's not nice to gossip ___ other people. || about|to|with || gossip about = 說…的閒話。 || 說別人的閒話是不好的。
governor || n. || 州長；首長 || The governor gave a speech. || 州長發表了演說。 || state governor=州長 || The governor, ___ was elected last year, promised to improve education. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 去年當選的州長承諾要改善教育。
grab || v. || 抓取 || He grabbed my arm. || 他抓住我的手臂。 || grab a bite=隨便吃點東西 || Let's grab something to eat ___ the movie starts. || before|after|since || 電影開始「之前」吃點東西，用 before。 || 電影開始前我們隨便吃點東西吧。
graduate || v./n. || 畢業；畢業生 || She graduated from college last year. || 她去年大學畢業。 || graduate from=從…畢業 || She graduated ___ National Taiwan University. || from|of|at || graduate from = 從…畢業。 || 她畢業於台灣大學。
grasp || v./n. || 抓住；理解 || I couldn't grasp the meaning. || 我沒辦法理解它的意思。 || grasp the meaning=理解意思 || It took me a long time ___ grasp the idea. || to|for|of || It takes sb + 時間 + to V。 || 我花了很久才理解這個概念。
grasshopper || n. || 蚱蜢 || A grasshopper jumped onto my arm. || 一隻蚱蜢跳到我的手臂上。 || grasshopper=蚱蜢 || The ant and the grasshopper is a story ___ teaches us to work hard. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a story）。 || 《螞蟻與蚱蜢》是一個教我們要努力工作的故事。
grassy || adj. || 長滿草的 || We had a picnic on a grassy hill. || 我們在長滿草的山坡上野餐。 || grassy field=草地 || The kids are running ___ the grassy field. || on|in|at || 在草地上用 on。 || 孩子們在草地上奔跑。
greedy || adj. || 貪心的 || Don't be greedy. || 別貪心。 || greedy for=貪求… || The greedy man wanted more money ___ he already had a lot. || although|because|so || although = 雖然（讓步）。 || 雖然那個貪心的人已經很有錢，他還想要更多。
greenhouse || n. || 溫室 || The plants grow in a greenhouse. || 這些植物在溫室裡生長。 || greenhouse effect=溫室效應 || The greenhouse effect ___ making the Earth warmer. || is|are|does || 現在進行式；The greenhouse effect 用 is。 || 溫室效應正讓地球變暖。
grin || v./n. || 露齒而笑 || He grinned at me. || 他對我咧嘴一笑。 || grin from ear to ear=笑得合不攏嘴 || He grinned ___ ear to ear when he heard the good news. || from|at|of || grin from ear to ear = 笑得合不攏嘴。 || 聽到好消息時，他笑得合不攏嘴。
grocery || n. || 雜貨 || I need to buy some groceries. || 我需要買一些雜貨。 || grocery store=雜貨店 || I stopped by the grocery store ___ my way home. || on|in|at || on one's way home = 在回家路上。 || 我在回家的路上順道去了雜貨店。
guidance || n. || 指導 || Thank you for your guidance. || 謝謝你的指導。 || under the guidance of=在…的指導下 || The students did the experiment ___ the guidance of their teacher. || under|below|with || under the guidance of = 在…的指導下。 || 學生們在老師的指導下做實驗。
gum || n. || 口香糖；牙齦 || Chewing gum is not allowed in class. || 上課禁止嚼口香糖。 || chewing gum=口香糖 || Chewing gum ___ not allowed in the MRT. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 捷運內禁止嚼口香糖。
hairdresser || n. || 美髮師 || I went to the hairdresser yesterday. || 我昨天去美髮院。 || hairdresser's=美髮院 || I had my hair ___ by a hairdresser yesterday. || cut|cutting|to cut || have + 物 + p.p. = 請人把某物…（使役）。 || 我昨天請美髮師剪了頭髮。
hallway || n. || 走廊 || Don't run in the hallway. || 不要在走廊上奔跑。 || in the hallway=在走廊 || The students are not allowed ___ in the hallway. || to run|running|run || be allowed to + 原形動詞。 || 學生不准在走廊上奔跑。
hammer || n./v. || 鐵鎚 || He used a hammer to fix the chair. || 他用鐵鎚修椅子。 || hammer a nail=釘釘子 || He hit the nail ___ a hammer. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 他用鐵鎚敲釘子。
handful || n. || 一把；少數 || She ate a handful of nuts. || 她吃了一把堅果。 || a handful of=一把；少數的 || Only a handful of students ___ the answer. || knew|knows|know to || 描述過去的事用過去式 knew。 || 只有少數學生知道答案。
handkerchief || n. || 手帕 || He wiped his tears with a handkerchief. || 他用手帕擦眼淚。 || cotton handkerchief=棉手帕 || She gave him a handkerchief ___ wipe his tears. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 她給他一條手帕擦眼淚。
handy || adj. || 方便的；手巧的 || A phone is handy for taking photos. || 手機拍照很方便。 || come in handy=派上用場 || A flashlight will come in handy ___ there's a blackout. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 如果停電，手電筒就會派上用場。
hanger || n. || 衣架 || Put your shirt on a hanger. || 把襯衫掛在衣架上。 || coat hanger=衣架 || Hang your coat ___ a hanger so it won't get wrinkled. || on|in|at || 掛在衣架上用 on。 || 把外套掛在衣架上才不會皺。
harbor || n. || 港口 || The ship sailed into the harbor. || 船駛進了港口。 || Kaohsiung Harbor=高雄港 || Kaohsiung Harbor is one of the ___ ports in Taiwan. || busiest|busier|busy || one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。 || 高雄港是台灣最繁忙的港口之一。
harm || n./v. || 傷害 || Smoking does harm to your health. || 抽菸對健康有害。 || do harm to=對…有害 || Too much sugar can do harm ___ your body. || to|for|with || do harm to = 對…有害。 || 太多糖會傷害你的身體。
harmful || adj. || 有害的 || Smoking is harmful to your health. || 抽菸有害健康。 || be harmful to=對…有害 || Plastic is harmful ___ sea animals. || to|for|with || be harmful to = 對…有害。 || 塑膠對海洋動物有害。
harvest || n./v. || 收成 || The farmers harvest rice in autumn. || 農夫們在秋天收割稻米。 || harvest season=收穫季節 || The rice ___ harvested twice a year in southern Taiwan. || is|are|was being || 被動語態；rice 不可數用 is + p.p.。 || 在南台灣，稻米一年收成兩次。
hasty || adj. || 匆忙的；草率的 || Don't make a hasty decision. || 別做草率的決定。 || hasty decision=倉促的決定 || You shouldn't make a hasty decision ___ you have all the facts. || before|after|since || 在掌握事實「之前」不要做決定，用 before。 || 在掌握所有事實之前，你不應該草率做決定。
hatch || v. || 孵化 || The eggs will hatch soon. || 這些蛋很快就會孵化。 || hatch eggs=孵蛋 || The chicks will hatch ___ about 21 days. || in|after of|since || in + 一段時間 = 在…之後（未來）。 || 小雞大約二十一天後會孵出來。
hateful || adj. || 可恨的 || That was a hateful thing to say. || 說那種話真可惡。 || hateful words=惡毒的話 || It was hateful ___ him to say such things. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 他說那種話真可惡。
hay || n. || 乾草 || The horses are eating hay. || 馬在吃乾草。 || make hay while the sun shines=把握時機 || Make hay while the sun ___. || shines|shine|is shining to || 諺語，while 子句中 the sun 單數用 shines。 || 把握時機（趁太陽出來時曬乾草）。
headline || n. || 標題 || The news made headlines. || 這則新聞登上頭條。 || make headlines=成為頭條 || The story made headlines ___ over the world. || all|every|whole || all over the world = 全世界。 || 這則報導登上了全球頭條。
headquarters || n. || 總部 || The company's headquarters is in Taipei. || 這家公司的總部在台北。 || company headquarters=公司總部 || The company moved its headquarters ___ Taipei last year. || to|at|in of || move A to + 地點 = 把 A 搬到…。 || 這家公司去年把總部遷到台北。
heal || v. || 痊癒 || The wound will heal in a week. || 傷口一週內會癒合。 || heal up=痊癒 || Time heals all wounds, ___ it? || doesn't|isn't|does || 肯定句（一般動詞 heals）的附加問句用 doesn't。 || 時間能治癒一切傷痛，不是嗎？
heap || n. || 一堆 || There is a heap of clothes on the bed. || 床上有一堆衣服。 || a heap of=一堆… || There is a heap ___ dirty clothes in the corner. || of|with|in || a heap of = 一堆…。 || 角落裡有一堆髒衣服。
heater || n. || 暖氣機 || Turn on the heater. It's cold. || 開暖氣，好冷。 || electric heater=電暖器 || Don't forget to turn off the heater ___ you leave. || before|after|since || 離開「之前」關掉，用 before。 || 離開前別忘了關暖氣。
heel || n. || 腳跟；鞋跟 || She is wearing high heels. || 她穿著高跟鞋。 || high heels=高跟鞋 || Walking in high heels for a long time ___ hurt your feet. || can|cans|could to || 助動詞 can 後接原形動詞。 || 穿高跟鞋走太久會傷腳。
hell || n. || 地獄 || The traffic was hell this morning. || 今天早上交通糟透了。 || go to hell=下地獄 || The traffic jam was ___ bad that it felt like hell. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 塞車塞到像在地獄一樣。
helmet || n. || 安全帽 || Always wear a helmet when you ride a scooter. || 騎機車時一定要戴安全帽。 || wear a helmet=戴安全帽 || You are required ___ a helmet when riding a scooter. || to wear|wearing|wear || be required to + 原形動詞。 || 騎機車時規定要戴安全帽。
hesitate || v. || 猶豫 || Don't hesitate to ask for help. || 需要幫忙時別猶豫。 || hesitate to V=猶豫是否做… || If you have any questions, don't hesitate ___ ask me. || to|for|of || hesitate to + 原形動詞。 || 如果有任何問題，儘管問我。
hint || n. || 暗示；提示 || Can you give me a hint? || 你能給我一點提示嗎？ || give a hint=給提示; drop a hint=暗示 || If you can't guess the answer, I'll give you ___ hint. || a|an|the more || hint 以子音開頭，用 a。 || 如果你猜不出答案，我會給你一個提示。
hire || v. || 雇用 || The company hired five new workers. || 公司雇用了五名新員工。 || hire sb=雇用某人 || The company is going to hire someone ___ can speak Japanese. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 公司打算雇用會說日文的人。
historian || n. || 歷史學家 || The historian wrote a book about Taiwan. || 那位歷史學家寫了一本關於台灣的書。 || famous historian=知名史學家 || Historians believe that the temple ___ built in the 17th century. || was|is|has || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 歷史學家相信這座廟建於十七世紀。
historic || adj. || 歷史性的；有歷史意義的 || This is a historic building. || 這是一棟具有歷史意義的建築。 || historic site=古蹟 || Tainan is a city ___ many historic sites. || with|of|in || with = 擁有…。 || 台南是一座擁有許多古蹟的城市。
holder || n. || 持有者；架子 || Every ticket holder can enter. || 每位持票者都能進場。 || cup holder=杯架; record holder=紀錄保持人 || Only ticket holders ___ allowed to enter the concert hall. || are|is|be || 被動語態；ticket holders 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 只有持票者才能進入音樂廳。
hollow || adj. || 中空的 || The tree is hollow inside. || 這棵樹裡面是空的。 || hollow tree=中空的樹 || The owl lives in a hollow tree ___ is 100 years old. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a hollow tree）。 || 那隻貓頭鷹住在一棵百年的中空樹裡。
holy || adj. || 神聖的 || This is a holy place. || 這是一個神聖的地方。 || holy place=聖地 || You should be quiet ___ you are in a holy place. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 你在神聖的地方時應該保持安靜。
