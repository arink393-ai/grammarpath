# 學測 L3 07：faint – geography
faint || adj./v. || 微弱的；昏倒 || She fainted because of the heat. || 她因為太熱而昏倒了。 || faint light=微弱的光 || She fainted ___ standing in the sun for too long. || after|before|until || after + V-ing = 在…之後。 || 她在太陽下站太久後昏倒了。
fairly || adv. || 相當地；公平地 || The test was fairly easy. || 考試相當簡單。 || fairly good=相當好 || The teacher treats all students fairly, ___ everyone respects her. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 老師公平對待所有學生，所以大家都尊敬她。
fairy || n. || 仙子 || Cinderella's fairy godmother helped her. || 灰姑娘的仙女教母幫助了她。 || fairy tale=童話故事 || Fairy tales are stories ___ often have happy endings. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（stories）。 || 童話故事通常是有圓滿結局的故事。
faith || n. || 信心；信仰 || I have faith in you. || 我對你有信心。 || have faith in=對…有信心 || Never lose faith ___ yourself. || in|on|to || have/lose faith in = 對…有／失去信心。 || 永遠不要對自己失去信心。
fake || adj./n. || 假的 || This bag is fake. || 這個包包是假貨。 || fake news=假新聞 || Many people can't tell the difference ___ fake news and real news. || between|among|from || 兩者之間用 between。 || 很多人分辨不出假新聞和真新聞。
familiar || adj. || 熟悉的 || The song sounds familiar. || 這首歌聽起來很耳熟。 || be familiar with=熟悉… || Are you familiar ___ this area? || with|to|of || be familiar with = 熟悉…。 || 你熟悉這個地區嗎？
fancy || adj. || 花俏的；高級的 || We had dinner at a fancy restaurant. || 我們在一家高級餐廳吃晚餐。 || fancy dress=化妝舞會服裝 || The restaurant was ___ fancy that we felt a little nervous. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那家餐廳高級到讓我們有點緊張。
fare || n. || 車資 || The bus fare is 15 dollars. || 公車車資是十五元。 || bus fare=公車票價 || Students can take the MRT at half fare ___ they show their student ID. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 學生出示學生證就能以半價搭捷運。
farther || adv./adj. || 更遠地 || I can't walk any farther. || 我走不動了。 || farther away=更遠 || The station is farther ___ than I thought. || away|off from|out of || farther away = 更遠。 || 車站比我想的還遠。
fashionable || adj. || 時髦的 || She always wears fashionable clothes. || 她總是穿時髦的衣服。 || fashionable clothes=時髦的衣服 || These shoes are the ___ fashionable ones in the shop. || most|more|much || 三者以上比較用最高級 the most。 || 這雙鞋是店裡最時髦的。
faucet || n. || 水龍頭 || Turn off the faucet. || 關上水龍頭。 || leaky faucet=漏水的水龍頭 || Remember to turn off the faucet ___ you are brushing your teeth. || while|during|for || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 刷牙時記得關水龍頭。
fearful || adj. || 害怕的 || The child was fearful of the dark. || 那個孩子怕黑。 || be fearful of=害怕… || The villagers were fearful ___ another earthquake. || of|about to|with || be fearful of = 害怕…。 || 村民們害怕再發生地震。
feather || n. || 羽毛 || The bird has colorful feathers. || 那隻鳥有彩色的羽毛。 || birds of a feather=物以類聚 || Birds of a feather flock ___. || together|each other|with || 諺語：Birds of a feather flock together.（物以類聚） || 物以類聚。
fence || n. || 籬笆 || The dog jumped over the fence. || 狗跳過了籬笆。 || build a fence=築圍籬 || The garden is surrounded ___ a wooden fence. || by|with|from || be surrounded by = 被…圍繞。 || 花園被一道木籬笆圍繞著。
fighter || n. || 戰士；鬥士 || He is a brave fighter. || 他是個勇敢的鬥士。 || fire fighter=消防員 || The fire fighters worked hard ___ put out the fire. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 消防員努力撲滅大火。
firework || n. || 煙火 || We watched fireworks on New Year's Eve. || 我們在跨年夜看煙火。 || set off fireworks=放煙火 || Thousands of people gathered ___ watch the fireworks at Taipei 101. || to|for|so as || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 數千人聚集在台北 101 觀賞煙火。
fist || n. || 拳頭 || He made a fist. || 他握緊拳頭。 || make a fist=握拳 || He hit the table with his fist ___ anger. || in|with|by || in anger = 生氣地。 || 他生氣地用拳頭捶桌子。
flame || n. || 火焰 || The house was in flames. || 那棟房子陷入火海。 || in flames=燃燒中 || The whole building was ___ flames when the fire fighters arrived. || in|on|at || in flames = 燃燒著。 || 消防員抵達時，整棟大樓已陷入火海。
flash || n./v. || 閃光；閃爍 || There was a flash of lightning. || 一道閃電劃過。 || in a flash=一瞬間; flash drive=隨身碟 || The lights flashed ___ and off. || on|in|up || on and off = 時亮時滅。 || 燈光一閃一閃的。
flashlight || n. || 手電筒 || Take a flashlight with you. || 帶著手電筒。 || flashlight=手電筒 || You'd better bring a flashlight ___ case the lights go out. || in|for|with || in case = 以防萬一。 || 你最好帶支手電筒以防停電。
flavor || n. || 口味 || What flavor of ice cream do you like? || 你喜歡什麼口味的冰淇淋？ || strawberry flavor=草莓口味 || Which flavor do you prefer, chocolate ___ vanilla? || or|and|but || 二選一用 or。 || 你比較喜歡哪個口味，巧克力還是香草？
flesh || n. || 肉；果肉 || The flesh of the fruit is sweet. || 這種水果的果肉很甜。 || flesh and blood=血肉之軀；親骨肉 || The flesh of the mango is soft ___ sweet. || and|but|or || 兩個優點並列用 and。 || 芒果的果肉又軟又甜。
float || v. || 漂浮 || Wood floats on water. || 木頭浮在水上。 || float on water=浮在水上 || Leaves ___ floating on the surface of the lake. || are|is|do || 現在進行式；Leaves 複數用 are。 || 葉子漂浮在湖面上。
flock || n. || 一群（鳥、羊） || A flock of birds flew over us. || 一群鳥從我們頭頂飛過。 || a flock of=一群… || A flock of sheep ___ eating grass on the hill. || is|are|be || a flock of + 複數名詞當主詞，看 flock 用 is。 || 一群羊在山坡上吃草。
flood || n./v. || 洪水；淹沒 || The heavy rain caused a flood. || 大雨造成了水災。 || flash flood=山洪暴發 || The streets ___ flooded after the heavy rain. || were|was|are || 被動語態；streets 複數，過去用 were。 || 大雨過後街道淹水了。
flour || n. || 麵粉 || Mix the flour with milk. || 把麵粉和牛奶混合。 || a bag of flour=一袋麵粉 || Bread is made ___ flour, water, and yeast. || from|of|by || be made from = 由…製成（看不出原料）。 || 麵包是用麵粉、水和酵母做成的。
flute || n. || 長笛 || She plays the flute. || 她吹長笛。 || play the flute=吹長笛 || She has played the flute ___ she was seven. || since|for|from || since + 過去時間子句，搭配現在完成式。 || 她從七歲起就吹長笛。
foggy || adj. || 有霧的 || It's foggy this morning. || 今天早上有霧。 || foggy day=霧天 || It was ___ foggy that the airport was closed. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 霧大到機場關閉了。
fold || v./n. || 摺疊 || Fold the paper in half. || 把紙對折。 || fold in half=對折; fold one's arms=交叉雙臂 || Fold your clothes neatly ___ putting them in the drawer. || before|after|since || 放進去「之前」先摺好，用 before。 || 放進抽屜之前把衣服摺整齊。
follower || n. || 追隨者；粉絲 || The singer has millions of followers. || 那位歌手有數百萬名追蹤者。 || followers on social media=社群追蹤者 || The influencer, ___ has over a million followers, posts videos every day. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位擁有百萬追蹤者的網紅每天都發影片。
fond || adj. || 喜愛的 || She is fond of music. || 她喜愛音樂。 || be fond of=喜愛… || My grandpa is fond ___ telling old stories. || of|with|for || be fond of = 喜愛…。 || 我爺爺很喜歡講古早的故事。
forever || adv. || 永遠 || I will remember this forever. || 我會永遠記得這件事。 || forever young=永遠年輕 || Nothing lasts forever, ___ we should enjoy every moment. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 沒有什麼是永恆的，所以我們應該珍惜每一刻。
fortune || n. || 財富；運氣 || He made a fortune in business. || 他做生意發了大財。 || make a fortune=發大財; good fortune=好運 || He made a fortune ___ selling phones online. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 他靠在網路上賣手機發了財。
fountain || n. || 噴泉 || The kids are playing near the fountain. || 孩子們在噴泉附近玩。 || water fountain=噴泉；飲水機 || There is a big fountain ___ the middle of the park. || in|on|at of || in the middle of = 在…的中央。 || 公園中央有一座大噴泉。
frank || adj. || 坦白的 || To be frank, I don't agree. || 坦白說，我不同意。 || to be frank=坦白說 || To be frank ___ you, I don't like your idea. || with|to|for || be frank with sb = 對某人坦白。 || 坦白跟你說，我不喜歡你的點子。
freeze || v. || 結冰；冷凍 || Water freezes at 0°C. || 水在攝氏零度結冰。 || freeze to death=凍死 || Water freezes ___ 0°C. || at|in|on || 在某個溫度用 at。 || 水在攝氏零度結冰。
freezer || n. || 冷凍庫 || Put the meat in the freezer. || 把肉放進冷凍庫。 || in the freezer=在冷凍庫 || Meat can be kept in the freezer ___ several months. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 肉可以在冷凍庫保存好幾個月。
frequent || adj. || 頻繁的 || Typhoons are frequent in summer. || 夏天常有颱風。 || frequent visitor=常客 || Earthquakes are frequent in Taiwan ___ it lies on a plate boundary. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 台灣地震頻繁，因為它位在板塊交界處。
fright || n. || 驚嚇 || The noise gave me a fright. || 那個聲音嚇了我一跳。 || give sb a fright=嚇某人一跳 || She screamed ___ fright when she saw the snake. || in|with|by || in fright = 驚嚇地。 || 她看到蛇時嚇得尖叫。
frighten || v. || 使驚嚇 || The loud noise frightened the baby. || 巨響嚇到了寶寶。 || be frightened of=害怕… || The little girl was frightened ___ the big dog. || of|with|about to || be frightened of = 害怕…。 || 小女孩很怕那隻大狗。
fuel || n. || 燃料 || The car is running out of fuel. || 這輛車快沒油了。 || fossil fuel=化石燃料 || We should use less fossil fuel ___ protect the environment. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們應該少用化石燃料以保護環境。
fund || n./v. || 資金；基金 || We need funds for the project. || 我們需要這個計畫的資金。 || raise funds=募款 || The students held a concert ___ raise funds for the poor. || to|for|so as || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 學生們舉辦音樂會為窮人募款。
fur || n. || 毛皮 || The cat has soft fur. || 那隻貓的毛很柔軟。 || fur coat=皮草大衣 || The cat ___ fur is white is very friendly. || whose|which|that || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那隻白毛的貓非常親人。
gallon || n. || 加侖 || The car uses one gallon of gas every 30 miles. || 這輛車每三十英里用一加侖油。 || a gallon of=一加侖… || A gallon is equal ___ about 3.8 liters. || to|with|as || be equal to = 等於…。 || 一加侖約等於三點八公升。
gamble || v./n. || 賭博 || He lost all his money gambling. || 他賭博輸光了所有的錢。 || gamble away=賭輸掉 || He lost all his money ___ gambling. || by|with|in of || by + V-ing = 因為…。 || 他因為賭博輸光了所有的錢。
gang || n. || 幫派；一夥人 || The police arrested the gang members. || 警方逮捕了幫派成員。 || gang member=幫派分子 || The gang members ___ arrested by the police last night. || were|was|are || 被動語態；members 複數，過去用 were。 || 幫派成員昨晚被警方逮捕。
gap || n. || 差距；縫隙 || There is a big gap between the rich and the poor. || 貧富差距很大。 || generation gap=代溝; the gap between=…之間的差距 || The generation gap ___ parents and children is common. || between|among|in || the gap between A and B = A 與 B 之間的差距。 || 父母與子女之間的代溝很常見。
garage || n. || 車庫 || He parked the car in the garage. || 他把車停在車庫裡。 || garage sale=車庫拍賣 || The car ___ in the garage now. || is|are|does || 描述現在的位置，The car 用 is。 || 車子現在在車庫裡。
gasoline || n. || 汽油 || The price of gasoline has gone up. || 汽油價格上漲了。 || gasoline price=油價 || The price of gasoline ___ gone up again. || has|have|is || 現在完成式；The price 單數用 has。 || 汽油價格又上漲了。
geography || n. || 地理 || I'm interested in geography. || 我對地理有興趣。 || world geography=世界地理 || Knowing geography helps you understand ___ countries are different. || why|what|which || 名詞子句 why… = 為什麼…。 || 懂地理能幫助你理解為什麼各國不同。
