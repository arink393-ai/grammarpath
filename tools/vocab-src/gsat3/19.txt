# 學測 L3 19：tight – victim
tight || adj./adv. || 緊的 || These shoes are too tight. || 這雙鞋太緊了。 || tight schedule=緊湊的行程 || These jeans are too tight ___ me. || for|to|with || too + 形容詞 + for sb = 對某人來說太…。 || 這條牛仔褲對我來說太緊了。
tighten || v. || 使變緊 || Tighten your seat belt. || 繫緊你的安全帶。 || tighten one's belt=勒緊褲帶（節省） || Please tighten your seat belt ___ the plane takes off. || before|after|since || 起飛「之前」繫緊，用 before。 || 飛機起飛前請繫緊安全帶。
timber || n. || 木材 || The house is made of timber. || 這房子是用木材蓋的。 || timber house=木造房屋 || The old house ___ built of timber over 100 years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那棟老房子是一百多年前用木材蓋的。
tobacco || n. || 菸草 || Tobacco is harmful to your health. || 菸草有害健康。 || tobacco company=菸草公司 || Tobacco contains a substance ___ can cause cancer. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a substance）。 || 菸草含有一種會致癌的物質。
ton || n. || 噸 || The truck weighs ten tons. || 這輛卡車重十噸。 || tons of=大量的 || I have tons ___ homework to do tonight. || of|for|with || tons of = 大量的…。 || 我今晚有一大堆功課要做。
toss || v. || 扔 || He tossed the ball to me. || 他把球扔給我。 || toss a coin=擲硬幣 || Let's toss a coin ___ decide who goes first. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們擲硬幣決定誰先。
tough || adj. || 困難的；堅韌的 || It was a tough decision. || 那是個艱難的決定。 || tough question=難題 || When the going gets tough, the tough get ___. || going|go|to go || 諺語：get going = 開始行動。 || 情況越艱難，強者越要行動。
tourism || n. || 觀光業 || Tourism is important to Taiwan. || 觀光業對台灣很重要。 || tourism industry=觀光產業 || Tourism brings a lot of money ___ the country. || to|for|at || bring A to B = 為 B 帶來 A。 || 觀光業為國家帶來大量收入。
tourist || n. || 觀光客 || Many tourists visit Taipei 101. || 很多觀光客參觀台北 101。 || tourist attraction=觀光景點 || Taipei 101 is one of the most popular tourist ___ in Taiwan. || attractions|attraction|attractive || one of + 複數名詞。 || 台北 101 是台灣最熱門的觀光景點之一。
tow || v. || 拖；拉 || The car was towed away. || 那輛車被拖走了。 || tow truck=拖吊車 || My car ___ towed away because I parked in the wrong place. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 我的車被拖走了，因為我停錯地方。
tower || n. || 塔 || The Eiffel Tower is in Paris. || 艾菲爾鐵塔在巴黎。 || clock tower=鐘塔 || The Eiffel Tower, ___ is over 300 meters tall, is a symbol of Paris. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 高三百多公尺的艾菲爾鐵塔是巴黎的象徵。
trace || n./v. || 痕跡；追溯 || The thief left no trace. || 小偷沒留下任何痕跡。 || without a trace=無影無蹤 || The ship disappeared ___ a trace. || without|with|within || without a trace = 無影無蹤。 || 那艘船消失得無影無蹤。
trader || n. || 商人 || He is a stock trader. || 他是股票交易員。 || stock trader=股票交易員 || In the past, traders ___ goods along the Silk Road. || carried|carry|have carried || in the past 表示過去，用過去式。 || 過去商人沿著絲路運送貨物。
trail || n. || 小徑 || We hiked along the mountain trail. || 我們沿著山間小徑健行。 || hiking trail=步道 || We followed the trail ___ the top of the mountain. || to|at|in || to + 地點 = 到…。 || 我們沿著小徑到山頂。
transport || n./v. || 運輸 || Public transport is convenient in Taipei. || 台北的大眾運輸很方便。 || public transport=大眾運輸 || The goods will ___ transported by ship. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 這批貨物將以船運送。
traveler || n. || 旅行者 || The hotel is popular with young travelers. || 這間旅館很受年輕旅客歡迎。 || solo traveler=獨自旅行者 || The hostel is popular ___ young travelers. || with|to|for || be popular with = 受…歡迎。 || 這間青年旅館很受年輕旅客歡迎。
tray || n. || 托盤 || The waiter carried a tray of drinks. || 服務生端著一盤飲料。 || a tray of=一托盤… || The waiter carried a tray ___ drinks to our table. || of|with|for || a tray of = 一托盤…。 || 服務生端著一托盤飲料到我們桌邊。
trend || n. || 趨勢 || This is the latest fashion trend. || 這是最新的時尚趨勢。 || latest trend=最新趨勢 || There is a growing trend ___ online shopping. || toward|of|at || a trend toward = 朝…的趨勢。 || 網路購物有日益增長的趨勢。
tribe || n. || 部落 || The tribe lives in the mountains. || 那個部落住在山裡。 || indigenous tribe=原住民部落 || There are 16 officially recognized indigenous tribes ___ Taiwan. || in|on|at || 在國家用 in。 || 台灣有十六個官方認定的原住民族。
tricky || adj. || 棘手的 || This is a tricky question. || 這是個棘手的問題。 || tricky question=刁鑽的問題 || The question was ___ tricky that nobody could answer it. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這個問題刁鑽到沒有人答得出來。
troop || n. || 部隊 || The troops arrived in the city. || 部隊抵達了城市。 || troops=部隊 || The troops ___ sent to help after the earthquake. || were|was|are || 被動語態；troops 複數，過去用 were。 || 地震後部隊被派去協助救災。
tropical || adj. || 熱帶的 || Mangoes are tropical fruits. || 芒果是熱帶水果。 || tropical fruit=熱帶水果 || Southern Taiwan has a tropical climate, ___ is hot all year round. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 南台灣屬熱帶氣候，全年炎熱。
trumpet || n. || 小喇叭 || He plays the trumpet. || 他吹小喇叭。 || play the trumpet=吹小喇叭 || He has been playing ___ trumpet for five years. || the|a|an || 演奏樂器時，樂器前加 the。 || 他吹小喇叭已經五年了。
trunk || n. || 樹幹；後車廂；象鼻 || Put the bags in the trunk. || 把袋子放進後車廂。 || tree trunk=樹幹 || An elephant uses ___ trunk to pick up food. || its|it's|their || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 大象用牠的鼻子撿食物。
truthful || adj. || 誠實的 || Please be truthful with me. || 請對我誠實。 || truthful answer=誠實的回答 || Always be truthful ___ your parents. || with|to be|at || be truthful with = 對…誠實。 || 對父母要永遠誠實。
tub || n. || 浴缸；桶 || She is relaxing in the hot tub. || 她在熱水浴缸裡放鬆。 || hot tub=按摩浴缸 || He filled the tub ___ hot water. || with|of|by || fill A with B = 用 B 裝滿 A。 || 他把浴缸裝滿熱水。
tug || v./n. || 用力拉 || The child tugged at his mother's sleeve. || 孩子拉了拉媽媽的袖子。 || tug of war=拔河 || We played tug of war ___ the school sports day. || on|in|at || on + 特定的日子。 || 我們在學校運動會上玩拔河。
tune || n./v. || 曲調；調音 || She hummed a tune. || 她哼了一段曲子。 || in tune=音準; stay tuned=請繼續收看 || Stay tuned ___ more news after the break. || for|to|with || stay tuned for = 繼續收看…。 || 廣告後請繼續收看更多新聞。
tunnel || n. || 隧道 || The train went through a tunnel. || 火車穿過了一條隧道。 || go through a tunnel=穿過隧道 || The Hsuehshan Tunnel, ___ is about 13 kilometers long, connects Taipei and Yilan. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 長約十三公里的雪山隧道連接台北和宜蘭。
tutor || n. || 家教 || I have a math tutor. || 我有數學家教。 || private tutor=家教 || My parents hired a tutor ___ help me with English. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我父母請了家教幫我補英文。
twin || n. || 雙胞胎 || She has a twin sister. || 她有一個雙胞胎姊妹。 || twin brother=雙胞胎兄弟 || The twins look so much alike ___ I can't tell them apart. || that|what|which || so + 副詞 + that…。 || 那對雙胞胎長得太像了，我分不出來。
twist || v./n. || 扭轉；扭傷 || I twisted my ankle. || 我扭傷了腳踝。 || twist one's ankle=扭傷腳踝 || I twisted my ankle ___ I was playing basketball. || while|during|since || while + 子句 = 當…的時候。 || 我打籃球時扭傷了腳踝。
underlying || adj. || 潛在的；根本的 || We need to find the underlying cause. || 我們需要找出根本原因。 || underlying cause=根本原因 || Lack of sleep may be the underlying cause ___ your headaches. || of|for|to || the cause of = …的原因。 || 睡眠不足可能是你頭痛的根本原因。
underwear || n. || 內衣 || Pack enough underwear for the trip. || 旅行時帶足夠的內衣。 || clean underwear=乾淨的內衣 || Don't forget ___ enough underwear for the trip. || to pack|packing|pack || forget to V = 忘記要做（還沒做）。 || 別忘了帶足夠的內衣去旅行。
union || n. || 聯盟；工會 || The workers joined the union. || 工人們加入了工會。 || European Union=歐盟 || The European Union ___ made up of 27 countries. || is|are|be || The European Union 視為單數；be made up of = 由…組成。 || 歐盟由二十七個國家組成。
unique || adj. || 獨特的 || Every person is unique. || 每個人都是獨一無二的。 || unique style=獨特風格 || Taiwan has a unique culture ___ mixes many traditions. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a unique culture）。 || 台灣有融合多種傳統的獨特文化。
unite || v. || 團結；聯合 || The people united to fight. || 人民團結起來奮戰。 || united=團結的 || United we stand, divided we ___. || fall|falls|fell || 諺語，主詞 we 用原形 fall。 || 團結則立，分裂則亡。
unity || n. || 團結 || Unity is strength. || 團結就是力量。 || national unity=國家團結 || Unity is strength, ___ we should work together. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 團結就是力量，所以我們應該合作。
urban || adj. || 都市的 || More people live in urban areas now. || 現在更多人住在都市地區。 || urban area=都市區 || More and more people are moving ___ urban areas. || to|in|at || move to = 搬到…。 || 越來越多人搬到都市地區。
vacant || adj. || 空著的 || Is this seat vacant? || 這個座位空著嗎？ || vacant seat=空位 || There are no vacant rooms at the hotel, ___ we have to find another one. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 這家旅館沒有空房，所以我們得另找一家。
van || n. || 廂型車 || They moved the furniture in a van. || 他們用廂型車搬家具。 || moving van=搬家車 || They rented a van ___ move their furniture. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 他們租了一輛廂型車搬家具。
vanish || v. || 消失 || The magician made the coin vanish. || 魔術師讓硬幣消失了。 || vanish into thin air=消失得無影無蹤 || The rabbit vanished ___ thin air. || into|in|to || vanish into thin air = 憑空消失。 || 兔子憑空消失了。
variety || n. || 多樣；種類 || The store sells a variety of fruits. || 這家店賣各式各樣的水果。 || a variety of=各式各樣的 || The night market offers a variety ___ snacks. || of|for|in || a variety of = 各式各樣的…。 || 夜市提供各式各樣的小吃。
various || adj. || 各種的 || There are various ways to learn English. || 學英文有各種方法。 || various kinds of=各種… || Students ___ various countries study at this school. || from|of|in || from + 地方 = 來自…。 || 來自各國的學生在這所學校就讀。
vary || v. || 不同；變化 || Prices vary from store to store. || 價格因店而異。 || vary from A to B=因 A 到 B 而異 || Prices vary ___ store to store. || from|between|in || vary from A to B = 從 A 到 B 各不相同。 || 價格因店而異。
vase || n. || 花瓶 || Put the flowers in the vase. || 把花插進花瓶裡。 || flower vase=花瓶 || The vase ___ broken by the cat last night. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 花瓶昨晚被貓打破了。
vehicle || n. || 車輛 || No vehicles are allowed in this area. || 這區禁止車輛進入。 || electric vehicle=電動車 || Electric vehicles are becoming ___ popular. || more and more|most and most|much and much || 比較級 and 比較級 = 越來越…。 || 電動車越來越受歡迎。
verse || n. || 詩句；韻文 || She read a verse from the poem. || 她朗讀了詩中的一節。 || in verse=用韻文寫的 || The first verse of the song ___ written by the singer herself. || was|were|is being || 被動語態；The first verse 單數，過去用 was。 || 這首歌的第一段是歌手自己寫的。
vest || n. || 背心 || He wore a vest over his shirt. || 他在襯衫外穿了一件背心。 || life vest=救生衣 || Everyone on the boat must wear a life vest ___ safety. || for|to|of || for + 名詞 = 為了…。 || 為了安全，船上每個人都必須穿救生衣。
