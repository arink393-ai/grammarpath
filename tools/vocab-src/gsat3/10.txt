# 學測 L3 10：jewelry – loose
jewelry || n. || 珠寶（總稱） || She likes to wear jewelry. || 她喜歡戴珠寶。 || a piece of jewelry=一件珠寶 || All the jewelry in the shop ___ very expensive. || is|are|be || jewelry 不可數，用 is。 || 店裡所有的珠寶都非常昂貴。
journey || n./v. || 旅程 || The journey took three hours. || 這段旅程花了三個小時。 || a long journey=長途旅程 || The journey from Taipei to Kaohsiung ___ about two hours by high-speed rail. || takes|take|taking || The journey 單數，動詞加 s。 || 從台北到高雄搭高鐵的旅程約需兩小時。
joyful || adj. || 快樂的 || It was a joyful day. || 那是快樂的一天。 || joyful news=喜訊 || The wedding was a joyful event ___ everyone enjoyed. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當 enjoyed 的受詞。 || 婚禮是一場大家都很享受的歡樂盛事。
juicy || adj. || 多汁的 || The peach is sweet and juicy. || 這顆桃子又甜又多汁。 || juicy steak=多汁的牛排 || The steak was ___ juicy that everyone loved it. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 牛排多汁到大家都很愛。
jungle || n. || 叢林 || Tigers live in the jungle. || 老虎住在叢林裡。 || the law of the jungle=弱肉強食 || Many kinds of animals can be found ___ the jungle. || in|on|at || 在叢林裡用 in。 || 叢林裡可以找到很多種動物。
junior || adj./n. || 年紀較小的；低年級的 || He is two years my junior. || 他比我小兩歲。 || junior high school=國中 || She is three years junior ___ me. || to|than|from || be junior to = 比…年輕／資淺（不用 than）。 || 她比我小三歲。
junk || n. || 垃圾；廢物 || Don't eat junk food. || 不要吃垃圾食物。 || junk food=垃圾食物; junk mail=垃圾郵件 || Eating too much junk food ___ bad for your health. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 吃太多垃圾食物對健康不好。
kangaroo || n. || 袋鼠 || Kangaroos carry their babies in pouches. || 袋鼠把寶寶放在育兒袋裡。 || baby kangaroo=小袋鼠 || Kangaroos, ___ are found only in Australia, can jump very far. || which|who|what || 非限定關係子句修飾動物，用 which。 || 只在澳洲出現的袋鼠能跳得很遠。
keyboard || n. || 鍵盤 || I spilled coffee on my keyboard. || 我把咖啡灑在鍵盤上。 || computer keyboard=電腦鍵盤 || He typed the report ___ the keyboard very quickly. || on|in|at || 在鍵盤上打字用 on。 || 他在鍵盤上很快地打完報告。
kidney || n. || 腎臟 || Drinking enough water is good for your kidneys. || 喝足夠的水對腎臟有益。 || kidney disease=腎臟病 || Drinking enough water helps your kidneys ___ well. || work|working|to working || help sb/sth + 原形動詞。 || 喝足夠的水有助於腎臟正常運作。
kilometer || n. || 公里 || The station is two kilometers away. || 車站在兩公里外。 || kilometers per hour=每小時公里數 || The school is about three kilometers away ___ my house. || from|of|to || A is … away from B。 || 學校離我家大約三公里。
kindergarten || n. || 幼稚園 || My sister goes to kindergarten. || 我妹妹讀幼稚園。 || go to kindergarten=上幼稚園 || Children usually go to kindergarten ___ they are four or five. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 孩子們通常在四、五歲時上幼稚園。
kingdom || n. || 王國 || The king ruled the kingdom for 40 years. || 國王統治這個王國四十年。 || the United Kingdom=英國 || The United Kingdom ___ made up of four countries. || is|are|be || 國名視為單數；be made up of = 由…組成。 || 英國由四個國家組成。
kit || n. || 一套用具 || Do you have a first-aid kit? || 你有急救箱嗎？ || first-aid kit=急救箱; tool kit=工具組 || Every car should have a first-aid kit ___ case of emergency. || in|for|with || in case of = 以防…。 || 每輛車都應該備有急救箱以防萬一。
knight || n. || 騎士 || The knight saved the princess. || 騎士救了公主。 || brave knight=勇敢的騎士 || The princess ___ saved by a brave knight. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 公主被一位勇敢的騎士救了。
knit || v. || 編織 || My grandma is knitting a sweater. || 我奶奶在織毛衣。 || knit a sweater=織毛衣 || My grandma knitted this scarf ___ me. || for|to|with || 為某人做某事用 for。 || 我奶奶為我織了這條圍巾。
knot || n./v. || 結 || Tie a knot in the rope. || 在繩子上打個結。 || tie a knot=打結 || He tied the two ropes together ___ a strong knot. || with|by|at || with + 名詞 = 用…。 || 他用一個牢固的結把兩條繩子綁在一起。
koala || n. || 無尾熊 || Koalas sleep most of the day. || 無尾熊大部分時間都在睡覺。 || koala bear=無尾熊 || Koalas sleep up ___ 20 hours a day. || to|for|at || up to = 多達…。 || 無尾熊一天睡多達二十小時。
label || n./v. || 標籤 || Read the label before you buy it. || 購買前先看標籤。 || price label=價格標籤 || Always read the label ___ taking any medicine. || before|after|since || 吃藥「之前」看標籤，用 before。 || 服用任何藥物之前一定要看標籤。
lace || n. || 蕾絲；鞋帶 || Her dress is made of lace. || 她的洋裝是蕾絲做的。 || shoe lace=鞋帶 || Your shoe laces are untied. You'd better ___ them. || tie|to tie|tying || had better + 原形動詞 = 最好…。 || 你的鞋帶鬆了，你最好綁一下。
ladder || n. || 梯子 || He climbed the ladder to fix the roof. || 他爬上梯子修屋頂。 || climb a ladder=爬梯子 || Be careful ___ you climb the ladder. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 爬梯子時要小心。
lately || adv. || 最近 || Have you seen Tom lately? || 你最近有見到湯姆嗎？ || lately=最近 || I ___ been very busy lately. || have|had|am || lately 常與現在完成式連用；主詞 I 用 have。 || 我最近非常忙。
laughter || n. || 笑聲 || The room was full of laughter. || 房間裡充滿了笑聲。 || burst into laughter=突然大笑 || Laughter is the best medicine, ___ it? || isn't|doesn't|is || 肯定句的附加問句用否定：is → isn't。 || 笑是最好的良藥，不是嗎？
laundry || n. || 待洗的衣物；洗衣店 || I do the laundry on Sundays. || 我星期天洗衣服。 || do the laundry=洗衣服 || It's your turn ___ do the laundry today. || to|for|of || It's one's turn to V。 || 今天輪到你洗衣服。
lawn || n. || 草坪 || Dad is mowing the lawn. || 爸爸在除草。 || mow the lawn=割草 || Please keep ___ the lawn. || off|of|away || keep off = 遠離、勿踐踏。 || 請勿踐踏草坪。
leak || n./v. || 漏；洩漏 || The roof leaks when it rains. || 下雨時屋頂會漏水。 || gas leak=瓦斯外洩 || The roof started to leak ___ the heavy rain. || during|while|since || during + 名詞 = 在…期間。 || 大雨期間屋頂開始漏水。
leap || v./n. || 跳躍 || The cat leaped onto the table. || 貓跳上了桌子。 || leap year=閏年; a leap of faith=信念的躍進 || There ___ 366 days in a leap year. || are|is|be || 366 days 複數，There are。 || 閏年有三百六十六天。
learning || n. || 學習；學問 || Learning never stops. || 學習永無止境。 || lifelong learning=終身學習 || Online learning ___ becoming more and more popular. || is|are|be || learning 不可數，用 is。 || 線上學習越來越受歡迎。
leather || n. || 皮革 || This bag is made of leather. || 這個包包是皮革做的。 || leather jacket=皮夾克 || These shoes are made ___ real leather. || of|by|at || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這雙鞋是真皮做的。
leisure || n. || 空閒；休閒 || What do you do in your leisure time? || 你空閒時做什麼？ || leisure time=空閒時間 || I like reading ___ my leisure time. || in|on|at || in one's leisure time = 在空閒時間。 || 我喜歡在空閒時間閱讀。
lemonade || n. || 檸檬汁 || I'd like a glass of lemonade. || 我要一杯檸檬汁。 || a glass of lemonade=一杯檸檬汁 || I'd like a glass of lemonade ___ no ice, please. || with|by|of || with no ice = 去冰。 || 我要一杯檸檬汁，去冰，謝謝。
leopard || n. || 豹 || A leopard can run very fast. || 豹可以跑得很快。 || snow leopard=雪豹 || A leopard can't change ___ spots. || its|it's|their || 諺語：A leopard can't change its spots.（本性難移）；動物用 its。 || 本性難移。
lettuce || n. || 萵苣 || Put some lettuce in the sandwich. || 在三明治裡放一些萵苣。 || a head of lettuce=一顆萵苣 || Wash the lettuce carefully ___ eating it. || before|after|until || 吃「之前」洗乾淨，用 before。 || 吃萵苣之前要仔細清洗。
liberty || n. || 自由 || They fought for liberty. || 他們為自由而戰。 || the Statue of Liberty=自由女神像 || The Statue of Liberty ___ given to the U.S. by France. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 自由女神像是法國送給美國的。
lick || v. || 舔 || The dog licked my face. || 狗舔了我的臉。 || lick one's lips=舔嘴唇 || The cat licked ___ paws after eating. || its|it's|it || 動物用 it；所有格是 its。 || 貓吃完東西後舔了舔牠的爪子。
lifetime || n. || 一生 || This is a chance of a lifetime. || 這是千載難逢的機會。 || once in a lifetime=一生一次 || Seeing the Northern Lights was a once-in-a-lifetime experience ___ me. || for|to|of || 「對某人來說」用 for sb。 || 看到北極光對我來說是一生一次的體驗。
lighthouse || n. || 燈塔 || The lighthouse guides ships at night. || 燈塔在夜間引導船隻。 || lighthouse keeper=燈塔看守人 || The lighthouse, ___ was built in 1880, is still in use. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 建於 1880 年的燈塔至今仍在使用。
lightning || n. || 閃電 || The tree was struck by lightning. || 那棵樹被閃電擊中。 || thunder and lightning=雷電 || It's dangerous to stand under a tree ___ there is lightning. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 打雷閃電時站在樹下很危險。
lily || n. || 百合 || The lily is a symbol of purity. || 百合是純潔的象徵。 || white lily=白百合 || The lily is a flower ___ is often used at weddings. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a flower）。 || 百合是常用於婚禮的花。
limb || n. || 肢體；樹枝 || He broke a limb in the accident. || 他在事故中斷了一條肢體。 || life and limb=生命安全 || He broke a limb ___ he fell off the ladder. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 他從梯子上摔下來時摔斷了一條肢體。
litter || n./v. || 垃圾；亂丟 || Don't litter in the park. || 不要在公園亂丟垃圾。 || litter box=貓砂盆 || People who litter in public places should ___ fined. || be|being|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 在公共場所亂丟垃圾的人應該被罰款。
lively || adj. || 活潑的；熱鬧的 || The night market is very lively. || 夜市很熱鬧。 || lively discussion=熱烈的討論 || The class had a lively discussion ___ climate change. || about|at|with || a discussion about = 關於…的討論。 || 全班熱烈討論氣候變遷。
loaf || n. || 一條（麵包） || I bought a loaf of bread. || 我買了一條麵包。 || a loaf of bread=一條麵包 || Can you pick up a loaf ___ bread on your way home? || of|for|in || a loaf of bread = 一條麵包。 || 你回家路上可以順便買一條麵包嗎？
lobby || n. || 大廳 || Let's meet in the hotel lobby. || 我們在飯店大廳碰面吧。 || hotel lobby=飯店大廳 || I'll wait for you ___ the lobby. || in|on|of || 在大廳裡用 in。 || 我會在大廳等你。
locate || v. || 位於；找出 || The hotel is located near the station. || 飯店位於車站附近。 || be located in=位於… || Taipei 101 ___ located in the Xinyi District. || is|are|was being || 被動語態 be located；Taipei 101 用 is。 || 台北 101 位於信義區。
location || n. || 位置 || The hotel has a great location. || 這間飯店的位置很好。 || convenient location=便利的位置 || The location of the new school ___ not been decided yet. || has|have|is || 現在完成式被動；The location 單數用 has。 || 新學校的位置還沒決定。
lock || v./n. || 鎖 || Don't forget to lock the door. || 別忘了鎖門。 || lock the door=鎖門 || I found that the door ___ locked when I got home. || was|is|were || 主要子句過去式，名詞子句也用過去式；door 單數用 was。 || 我到家時發現門是鎖著的。
log || n./v. || 原木；登入 || He put another log on the fire. || 他往火裡添了一根木頭。 || log in=登入; log out=登出 || You need a password ___ log in to the system. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 你需要密碼才能登入系統。
lollipop || n. || 棒棒糖 || The kid is licking a lollipop. || 那個小孩在舔棒棒糖。 || lollipop=棒棒糖 || The doctor gave the child a lollipop ___ he was so brave. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 醫生給那個孩子一支棒棒糖，因為他很勇敢。
loose || adj. || 鬆的 || These pants are too loose. || 這條褲子太鬆了。 || loose clothes=寬鬆的衣服 || These pants are too loose ___ me. || for|to|with || too + 形容詞 + for sb = 對某人來說太…。 || 這條褲子對我來說太鬆了。
