# 學測 L3 05：cricket – dose
cricket || n. || 蟋蟀；板球 || I can hear crickets at night. || 我晚上聽得到蟋蟀叫。 || play cricket=打板球 || Cricket is a sport ___ is very popular in India. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a sport）。 || 板球是一項在印度非常受歡迎的運動。
criminal || n./adj. || 罪犯；犯罪的 || The criminal was sent to prison. || 那名罪犯被送進監獄。 || criminal record=犯罪紀錄 || The criminal ___ by the police last night. || was caught|caught|has caught || 被動語態：罪犯「被」抓，過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那名罪犯昨晚被警方逮捕。
crispy || adj. || 酥脆的 || I like crispy fried chicken. || 我喜歡酥脆的炸雞。 || crispy skin=酥脆的外皮 || The chicken is crispy on the outside ___ juicy on the inside. || and|but|or || 兩個優點並列用 and。 || 這雞肉外酥內多汁。
crop || n. || 農作物 || Rice is the main crop here. || 稻米是這裡的主要作物。 || crop failure=歉收 || The crops ___ damaged by the typhoon last month. || were|was|are || 被動語態；crops 複數，過去用 were。 || 農作物上個月被颱風損壞了。
crown || n./v. || 王冠；加冕 || The king wore a gold crown. || 國王戴著金王冠。 || crown prince=王儲 || The queen was crowned ___ 1953. || in|on|at || 年份前面用 in。 || 女王在 1953 年加冕。
cruel || adj. || 殘忍的 || It's cruel to keep animals in small cages. || 把動物關在小籠子裡很殘忍。 || be cruel to=對…殘忍 || It is cruel ___ people to hurt animals for fun. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 人們為了好玩而傷害動物是殘忍的。
cupboard || n. || 櫥櫃 || The cups are in the cupboard. || 杯子在櫥櫃裡。 || kitchen cupboard=廚房櫥櫃 || Put the plates back in the cupboard ___ you've washed them. || after|before|until || 洗「完」之後放回去，用 after。 || 碗盤洗好之後放回櫥櫃。
dairy || n./adj. || 乳製品；乳品的 || Milk and cheese are dairy products. || 牛奶和起司是乳製品。 || dairy products=乳製品; dairy farm=酪農場 || Some people can't eat dairy products ___ they are allergic to milk. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 有些人不能吃乳製品，因為他們對牛奶過敏。
dam || n. || 水壩 || The dam provides water for the city. || 這座水壩為城市供水。 || build a dam=建水壩 || The dam, ___ was built in 1970, supplies electricity to the area. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 這座建於 1970 年的水壩為該地區供電。
dare || v./aux. || 敢 || He didn't dare to say a word. || 他一個字都不敢說。 || dare to V=敢做…; how dare you=你怎麼敢 || I don't dare ___ my teacher that question. || to ask|asking|asked || dare to + 原形動詞（肯定句中 dare 當一般動詞）。 || 我不敢問老師那個問題。
darling || n. || 親愛的 || Good night, darling. || 晚安，親愛的。 || my darling=我親愛的 || Come here, darling, and let me ___ you a hug. || give|to give|giving || let + 受詞 + 原形動詞。 || 過來，親愛的，讓我抱抱你。
dash || v./n. || 衝；破折號 || He dashed out of the room. || 他衝出房間。 || make a dash=衝刺; dash off=急忙離開 || She dashed to the station, ___ the train had already left. || but|so|or || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 她衝到車站，但火車已經開走了。
database || n. || 資料庫 || The information is stored in a database. || 資訊儲存在資料庫裡。 || online database=線上資料庫 || All the students' records ___ kept in the database. || are|is|be || 被動語態；records 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 所有學生的紀錄都存在資料庫裡。
dawn || n. || 黎明 || We got up at dawn to see the sunrise. || 我們天一亮就起床看日出。 || at dawn=黎明時; from dawn to dusk=從早到晚 || The farmers work ___ dawn to dusk. || from|since|between || from dawn to dusk = 從黎明到黃昏。 || 農夫們從早工作到晚。
dealer || n. || 商人；經銷商 || He is a car dealer. || 他是汽車經銷商。 || car dealer=汽車經銷商 || The car dealer tried to persuade me ___ a new car. || to buy|buying|buy || persuade sb to + 原形動詞。 || 汽車業務試著說服我買新車。
decade || n. || 十年 || The city has changed a lot in the past decade. || 這座城市在過去十年變化很大。 || for decades=數十年來 || Prices ___ risen a lot over the past decade. || have|has|had || 現在完成式；Prices 複數用 have。 || 過去十年物價上漲很多。
deck || n. || 甲板 || We stood on the deck of the ship. || 我們站在船的甲板上。 || on deck=在甲板上 || The passengers enjoyed the sunset ___ the deck. || on|in|at of || 在甲板上用 on。 || 乘客們在甲板上欣賞夕陽。
decorate || v. || 裝飾 || We decorated the tree with lights. || 我們用燈裝飾樹。 || decorate A with B=用 B 裝飾 A || The hall ___ decorated with flowers for the wedding. || was|is being|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 大廳為了婚禮用鮮花裝飾。
decrease || v./n. || 減少 || The number of students has decreased. || 學生人數減少了。 || decrease by=減少了… || The birth rate in Taiwan ___ been decreasing for years. || has|have|is || 現在完成進行式；The birth rate 單數用 has。 || 台灣的出生率多年來持續下降。
deed || n. || 行為；事蹟 || He did a good deed today. || 他今天做了一件好事。 || good deed=善行 || Doing one good deed a day ___ make the world a better place. || can|cans|canning || 助動詞 can 後接原形動詞。 || 每天做一件好事就能讓世界更美好。
deepen || v. || 加深 || The friendship deepened over the years. || 這份友誼隨著歲月加深。 || deepen understanding=加深了解 || Traveling can deepen your understanding ___ other cultures. || of|to|with || understanding of = 對…的了解。 || 旅行可以加深你對其他文化的了解。
definition || n. || 定義 || Look up the definition of the word. || 查一下這個字的定義。 || by definition=按照定義 || What is the definition ___ "happiness" to you? || of|for|to || the definition of = …的定義。 || 對你來說，「幸福」的定義是什麼？
democracy || n. || 民主 || Taiwan is a democracy. || 台灣是民主國家。 || freedom and democracy=自由與民主 || In a democracy, people ___ the right to choose their leaders. || have|has|having || people 複數，用 have。 || 在民主國家，人民有權選擇領導者。
democratic || adj. || 民主的 || Taiwan has a democratic government. || 台灣有民主政府。 || democratic country=民主國家 || Taiwan became a democratic country ___ the 1990s. || in|on|at || 年代前面用 in。 || 台灣在 1990 年代成為民主國家。
deposit || n./v. || 存款；押金 || I need to pay a deposit for the apartment. || 我要付公寓的押金。 || pay a deposit=付訂金 || You will get your deposit back ___ you return the key. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 你歸還鑰匙時就會拿回押金。
designer || n. || 設計師 || She wants to be a fashion designer. || 她想成為時裝設計師。 || fashion designer=時裝設計師 || The designer ___ dresses are sold worldwide is from Taiwan. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位作品行銷全球的設計師來自台灣。
desirable || adj. || 值得擁有的；理想的 || This is a desirable job. || 這是一份令人嚮往的工作。 || highly desirable=非常理想 || It is desirable ___ everyone to learn a second language. || for|of|to || It is + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 每個人都學第二語言是理想的。
desire || n./v. || 渴望 || She has a strong desire to travel. || 她非常渴望旅行。 || desire for=對…的渴望; desire to V=渴望做… || He has a strong desire ___ a doctor. || to become|becoming|of becoming to || a desire to + 原形動詞。 || 他非常渴望成為醫生。
dessert || n. || 甜點 || What would you like for dessert? || 你甜點想吃什麼？ || for dessert=作為甜點 || We had ice cream ___ dessert. || for|as for|with || for dessert = 作為甜點。 || 我們吃冰淇淋當甜點。
destroy || v. || 破壞 || The fire destroyed the old house. || 大火燒毀了那棟老房子。 || destroy the environment=破壞環境 || The old temple ___ destroyed in the earthquake. || was|is|has || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那座古廟在地震中被毀。
detect || v. || 察覺；偵測 || The machine can detect metal. || 這台機器能偵測金屬。 || detect a problem=發現問題 || The smoke alarm ___ detect even a small amount of smoke. || can|cans|could to || 助動詞 can 後接原形動詞。 || 煙霧警報器即使是少量的煙也能偵測到。
determine || v. || 決定；確定 || Your attitude determines your success. || 你的態度決定你的成功。 || be determined to V=下定決心做… || She is determined ___ the exam this year. || to pass|passing|pass || be determined to + 原形動詞。 || 她下定決心今年要通過考試。
devil || n. || 魔鬼 || Speak of the devil! || 說曹操，曹操到！ || speak of the devil=說曹操曹操到 || "Speak of the devil!" said Amy ___ Tom walked into the room. || as|so|unless || as = 當…的時候（同時發生）。 || 湯姆走進房間時，艾咪說：「說曹操，曹操到！」
dim || adj./v. || 昏暗的 || The light is too dim to read by. || 燈光太暗，沒辦法看書。 || dim light=昏暗的燈光 || The light is ___ dim for me to read. || too|so|very || too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 燈光太暗了，我沒辦法看書。
dime || n. || 一角硬幣 || A dime is worth ten cents. || 一角硬幣值十分錢。 || a dime a dozen=多得是 || A dime ___ worth ten cents. || is|are|be || A dime 單數，用 is。 || 一角硬幣值十分錢。
dine || v. || 用餐 || We dined at a famous restaurant. || 我們在一家有名的餐廳用餐。 || dine out=外出用餐 || We decided to dine ___ tonight instead of cooking. || out|in of|off || dine out = 外出用餐。 || 我們決定今晚外出用餐，不自己煮。
dinosaur || n. || 恐龍 || Dinosaurs died out millions of years ago. || 恐龍在數百萬年前滅絕。 || dinosaur fossil=恐龍化石 || Dinosaurs ___ out about 66 million years ago. || died|dead|have died || 過去的事用過去式；die out = 滅絕。 || 恐龍大約在六千六百萬年前滅絕。
dip || v./n. || 浸；蘸 || Dip the fries in ketchup. || 把薯條沾番茄醬。 || dip into=浸入 || Dip the bread ___ the soup before eating it. || into|onto|at || dip A into B = 把 A 浸入 B。 || 吃之前把麵包沾湯。
dirt || n. || 泥土；灰塵 || The kids came home covered in dirt. || 孩子們滿身泥巴回家。 || dirt road=泥土路 || His shoes were covered ___ dirt. || in|of|by || be covered in = 覆滿…。 || 他的鞋子沾滿了泥土。
discount || n. || 折扣 || Students get a 20% discount. || 學生享八折優惠。 || get a discount=打折 || Students can get a 10% discount ___ they show their ID. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 學生出示證件可享九折。
dishonest || adj. || 不誠實的 || It is dishonest to cheat on a test. || 考試作弊是不誠實的。 || dishonest behavior=不誠實的行為 || A dishonest person is someone ___ often tells lies. || who|which|what || 關係代名詞修飾人，用 who。 || 不誠實的人是常常說謊的人。
disk || n. || 磁碟；光碟 || Save the file on the disk. || 把檔案存在磁碟上。 || hard disk=硬碟 || Please save the file ___ the disk before you turn off the computer. || on|at|of || 存在磁碟上用 on。 || 關電腦之前請把檔案存到磁碟裡。
dislike || v./n. || 不喜歡 || I dislike waiting in line. || 我不喜歡排隊。 || dislike + V-ing=不喜歡做… || Many students dislike ___ up early. || getting|to getting|get || dislike 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 很多學生不喜歡早起。
ditch || n. || 水溝 || The car ended up in a ditch. || 那輛車最後掉進水溝裡。 || drainage ditch=排水溝 || The car slid off the road and ___ into a ditch. || fell|fall|falls || 過去發生的事用過去式 fell。 || 那輛車滑出道路，掉進了水溝。
dive || v./n. || 潛水；跳水 || He dived into the pool. || 他跳進泳池。 || scuba dive=水肺潛水 || It's dangerous ___ dive into shallow water. || to|for|of || It is + 形容詞 + to V。 || 跳進淺水裡是很危險的。
dizzy || adj. || 頭暈的 || I feel dizzy. || 我頭暈。 || feel dizzy=頭暈 || I felt dizzy after ___ on the ride. || going|go|went || 介系詞 after 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 坐完那個遊樂設施後我覺得頭暈。
dock || n. || 碼頭 || The ship is at the dock. || 船停在碼頭。 || at the dock=在碼頭 || The ship ___ at the dock for repairs now. || is|are|does || 描述現在的狀態，The ship 用 is。 || 那艘船現在停在碼頭維修。
dolphin || n. || 海豚 || Dolphins are intelligent animals. || 海豚是聰明的動物。 || dolphin watching=賞鯨豚 || Dolphins, ___ are very smart, can communicate with each other. || which|who|what || 非限定關係子句修飾動物，用 which。 || 非常聰明的海豚能夠互相溝通。
donkey || n. || 驢子 || The donkey carried the bags up the hill. || 驢子把袋子馱上山。 || as stubborn as a donkey=非常固執 || He is as stubborn ___ a donkey. || as|like|than || as + 形容詞 + as = 和…一樣。 || 他跟驢子一樣固執。
dose || n. || 劑量 || Take one dose of medicine every six hours. || 每六小時服用一劑藥。 || a dose of=一劑… || Don't take more than the recommended dose, ___ it may be harmful. || or|and|but || 語意「否則」用 or。 || 不要超過建議劑量，否則可能有害。
