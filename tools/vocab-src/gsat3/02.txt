# 學測 L3 02：backward – bud
backward || adv. || 向後 || He took a step backward. || 他往後退了一步。 || backward and forward=來來回回 || Try walking backward, and you ___ how hard it is. || will see|see|saw || 祈使句 + and + will + 原形動詞 = 如果…就會…。 || 試著倒著走，你就會知道有多難。
bacon || n. || 培根 || I had bacon and eggs for breakfast. || 我早餐吃了培根和蛋。 || bacon and eggs=培根蛋 || The smell of bacon ___ me hungry every morning. || makes|make|making || 主詞 The smell 單數，動詞加 s；make + 受詞 + 形容詞。 || 培根的香味每天早上都讓我肚子餓。
bacteria || n. || 細菌 || Wash your hands to kill bacteria. || 洗手可以殺死細菌。 || harmful bacteria=有害細菌 || Some bacteria ___ harmful, but others are helpful. || are|is|be || bacteria 是複數（單數 bacterium），用 are。 || 有些細菌是有害的，但有些是有益的。
badly || adv. || 嚴重地；很差地 || He was badly hurt in the accident. || 他在意外中受了重傷。 || badly hurt=重傷; need sth badly=非常需要 || The house ___ badly damaged in the earthquake. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那間房子在地震中嚴重受損。
baggage || n. || 行李 || Please check your baggage here. || 請在這裡托運行李。 || baggage claim=行李提領處 || How much baggage ___ you allowed to bring on the plane? || are|is|do || 被動 be allowed to；主詞 you 用 are。 || 你可以帶多少行李上飛機？
bait || n./v. || 誘餌 || He used worms as bait. || 他用蟲當魚餌。 || fish bait=魚餌 || Worms are often used ___ bait for fishing. || as|like|for being || be used as = 被用作…。 || 蟲常被用來當釣魚的餌。
bamboo || n. || 竹子 || Pandas eat bamboo. || 貓熊吃竹子。 || bamboo shoot=竹筍 || This basket is made ___ bamboo. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…製成（看得出材料）。 || 這個籃子是用竹子做的。
bang || n./v. || 砰的一聲；猛擊 || He banged the door shut. || 他砰地一聲把門關上。 || with a bang=砰的一聲 || The door closed ___ a loud bang. || with|in|by || with a bang = 伴隨砰的一聲。 || 門砰的一聲關上了。
banker || n. || 銀行家 || Her father is a banker. || 她父親是銀行家。 || investment banker=投資銀行家 || The banker ___ I met yesterday gave me some good advice. || whom|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當受詞，用 whom（或 who/that）。 || 我昨天遇到的那位銀行家給了我一些好建議。
bare || adj. || 赤裸的；光禿的 || Don't walk in bare feet. || 不要光著腳走路。 || bare feet=赤腳; with bare hands=徒手 || He caught the fish ___ his bare hands. || with|by|in || with one's bare hands = 徒手。 || 他徒手抓到了那條魚。
barely || adv. || 幾乎不；勉強 || I could barely hear him. || 我幾乎聽不到他說話。 || barely enough=勉強夠 || She barely passed the exam, ___ she? || did|didn't|was || barely 帶否定意味，附加問句用肯定 did she。 || 她勉強通過考試，是吧？
barn || n. || 穀倉 || The farmer keeps hay in the barn. || 農夫把乾草放在穀倉裡。 || red barn=紅色穀倉 || The cows ___ kept in the barn during winter. || are|is|be || 被動語態；The cows 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 冬天時牛被關在穀倉裡。
barrel || n. || 桶 || The price of oil is 80 dollars a barrel. || 油價是每桶八十美元。 || a barrel of=一桶… || Oil is sold ___ the barrel. || by|in|with || by the + 單位 = 以…計算。 || 石油是以桶計價販售的。
basement || n. || 地下室 || We keep old things in the basement. || 我們把舊東西放在地下室。 || in the basement=在地下室 || The basement was flooded ___ the heavy rain. || because of|because|so || 後面接名詞片語，用 because of。 || 地下室因為大雨淹水了。
bay || n. || 海灣 || The boats are in the bay. || 船停在海灣裡。 || Tokyo Bay=東京灣 || The hotel has a beautiful view ___ the bay. || of|at|to || a view of = …的景色。 || 這家飯店可以欣賞到海灣的美景。
bead || n. || 珠子 || She made a necklace of beads. || 她用珠子做了一條項鍊。 || glass beads=玻璃珠 || The necklace is made ___ colorful beads. || of|from|by || be made of = 由…製成。 || 這條項鍊是用彩色珠子做的。
beam || n./v. || 光束；橫樑 || A beam of light came through the window. || 一道光從窗戶照了進來。 || a beam of light=一道光 || A beam of sunlight ___ through the window this morning. || shone|shines|shining || this morning 已過去，用過去式 shone。 || 今天早上一道陽光從窗戶照進來。
beast || n. || 野獸 || Beauty and the Beast is my favorite movie. || 《美女與野獸》是我最喜歡的電影。 || wild beast=野獸 || The beast, ___ was actually a prince, fell in love with Belle. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾（擬人化的）人，用 who。 || 那隻野獸其實是一位王子，他愛上了貝兒。
beetle || n. || 甲蟲 || The beetle is black and shiny. || 那隻甲蟲又黑又亮。 || beetle=甲蟲 || There are more than 300,000 kinds of beetles ___ the world. || in|on|at || 最多 in the world = 世界上。 || 世界上有超過三十萬種甲蟲。
beneath || prep. || 在…之下 || The cat is sleeping beneath the table. || 貓在桌子底下睡覺。 || beneath the surface=在表面之下 || Many fish live beneath the surface ___ the sea. || of|in|on || the surface of = …的表面。 || 許多魚生活在海面之下。
benefit || n./v. || 好處；受益 || Regular exercise has many benefits. || 規律運動有很多好處。 || benefit from=從…中受益 || Students can benefit ___ reading English newspapers. || from|of|for || benefit from = 從…中受益。 || 學生可以從閱讀英文報紙中受益。
berry || n. || 莓果 || Berries are rich in vitamins. || 莓果富含維他命。 || strawberry=草莓; blueberry=藍莓 || Berries are rich ___ vitamin C. || in|of|with || be rich in = 富含…。 || 莓果富含維他命 C。
besides || adv./prep. || 此外；除了…之外（還） || Besides English, he speaks Japanese. || 除了英文，他還會說日文。 || besides that=除此之外 || Besides ___ the piano, she also plays the violin. || playing|play|to play || 介系詞 besides 後面接動名詞 V-ing。 || 除了彈鋼琴，她也拉小提琴。
bet || v./n. || 打賭 || I bet you can't do it. || 我打賭你做不到。 || I bet=我敢說; make a bet=打賭 || I bet you ___ win the game if you practice hard. || will|would have|had || I bet + 子句；未來的事用 will。 || 我敢說只要你努力練習就會贏。
bind || v. || 綁；約束 || The contract binds both sides. || 這份合約對雙方都有約束力。 || bind together=綁在一起 || Please use this string to bind the papers together ___ they won't get lost. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 為了（使…不會）。 || 請用這條繩子把文件綁在一起，這樣才不會弄丟。
bitter || adj. || 苦的；痛苦的 || The medicine tastes bitter. || 這藥嚐起來很苦。 || bitter taste=苦味; bitter experience=痛苦的經驗 || No matter how bitter the medicine is, you ___ take it. || must|must to|musting || must + 原形動詞 = 必須。 || 不論藥有多苦，你都必須吃。
bleed || v. || 流血 || Your nose is bleeding. || 你流鼻血了。 || bleed to death=失血而死 || His knee was bleeding ___ he fell off his bike. || after|before|until || 摔車「之後」流血，用 after。 || 他從腳踏車摔下來後，膝蓋流血了。
bless || v. || 保佑；祝福 || God bless you. || 上帝保佑你。 || be blessed with=有幸擁有… || She is blessed ___ a beautiful voice. || with|by|of || be blessed with = 有幸擁有…。 || 她天生擁有美妙的嗓音。
bloody || adj. || 流血的 || He had a bloody nose. || 他流鼻血了。 || bloody nose=流鼻血; bloody war=血腥的戰爭 || The bloody war ___ for five years. || lasted|lasts|has lasted since || 描述過去已結束的事用過去式 lasted。 || 那場血腥的戰爭持續了五年。
blouse || n. || 女用襯衫 || She is wearing a white blouse. || 她穿著一件白色女襯衫。 || silk blouse=絲質女襯衫 || The blouse ___ she bought yesterday is too small. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當受詞。 || 她昨天買的那件女襯衫太小了。
bold || adj. || 大膽的；粗體的 || It was a bold decision. || 那是一個大膽的決定。 || bold move=大膽的舉動; in bold=用粗體 || It was bold ___ her to quit her job and travel. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 她辭職去旅行真是大膽。
bomb || n./v. || 炸彈 || A bomb exploded in the city. || 一顆炸彈在城裡爆炸。 || atomic bomb=原子彈 || The city ___ bombed many times during the war. || was|is|has || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這座城市在戰爭期間被轟炸了很多次。
bookcase || n. || 書櫃 || The bookcase is full of novels. || 書櫃裡擺滿了小說。 || in the bookcase=在書櫃裡 || The bookcase is so heavy ___ it takes three people to move it. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這個書櫃重到要三個人才搬得動。
boot || n. || 靴子 || She is wearing a pair of boots. || 她穿著一雙靴子。 || rain boots=雨鞋 || These boots ___ made in Italy. || were|was|is || 被動語態；These boots 複數，過去用 were。 || 這雙靴子是義大利製的。
bore || v. || 使厭煩 || The long speech bored everyone. || 冗長的演講讓大家都很無聊。 || bored=感到無聊的; boring=令人無聊的 || The students were bored ___ the long lecture. || with|of|at || be bored with = 對…感到厭煩。 || 學生們對冗長的講課感到厭煩。
bowling || n. || 保齡球 || We went bowling last weekend. || 我們上週末去打保齡球。 || go bowling=去打保齡球 || Bowling is a sport ___ both kids and adults can enjoy. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a sport）。 || 保齡球是大人小孩都能享受的運動。
brake || n./v. || 煞車 || The driver hit the brakes. || 司機踩了煞車。 || hit the brakes=踩煞車 || If the driver ___ hit the brakes, there would have been an accident. || hadn't|didn't|hasn't || 與過去事實相反的假設：If + had (not) + p.p.。 || 如果司機沒踩煞車，就會發生車禍了。
brass || n. || 黃銅 || The door handle is made of brass. || 門把是黃銅做的。 || brass band=銅管樂隊 || He plays the trumpet in a brass band, ___ practices every Friday. || which|who|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物（樂隊），用 which。 || 他在一個銅管樂隊吹小號，那個樂隊每週五練習。
bravery || n. || 勇敢 || He was praised for his bravery. || 他因勇敢而受到讚揚。 || act of bravery=英勇的行為 || The firefighter was praised ___ his bravery. || for|of|with || be praised for = 因…受到讚揚。 || 那位消防員因勇敢而受到讚揚。
breast || n. || 胸部 || She held the baby to her breast. || 她把寶寶抱在胸前。 || chicken breast=雞胸肉 || Chicken breast is ___ in fat than other parts. || lower|low|lowest || 比較級 lower + than。 || 雞胸肉的脂肪比其他部位低。
breath || n. || 呼吸；氣息 || Take a deep breath. || 深呼吸。 || take a deep breath=深呼吸; out of breath=上氣不接下氣 || After running up the stairs, I was out ___ breath. || of|from|off || out of breath = 上氣不接下氣。 || 跑上樓梯後，我上氣不接下氣。
breathe || v. || 呼吸 || Breathe in slowly. || 慢慢吸氣。 || breathe in=吸氣; breathe out=吐氣 || It is hard to breathe ___ the air is polluted. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 空氣受污染時很難呼吸。
breeze || n. || 微風 || A cool breeze came from the sea. || 一陣涼爽的微風從海上吹來。 || sea breeze=海風 || We enjoyed the cool breeze ___ sitting on the beach. || while|during|since || while + V-ing（分詞構句）= 在…的時候。 || 我們坐在海灘上時享受著涼爽的微風。
brick || n. || 磚塊 || The wall is made of red bricks. || 這面牆是紅磚砌的。 || brick wall=磚牆 || The old house, ___ was built of bricks, is over 100 years old. || which|that|who || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 那棟用磚塊蓋的老房子已經超過一百年了。
bride || n. || 新娘 || The bride looked beautiful. || 新娘看起來很美。 || bride and groom=新娘與新郎 || The bride and groom ___ married in a church last Sunday. || got|get|have got || 描述過去的事用過去式 got。 || 新娘和新郎上週日在教堂結婚。
broadcast || n./v. || 廣播；播出 || The game will be broadcast live. || 比賽將會現場直播。 || live broadcast=現場直播 || The concert will be broadcast live ___ TV. || on|in|at || on TV = 在電視上。 || 這場演唱會將在電視上現場直播。
brunch || n. || 早午餐 || We had brunch at a café. || 我們在一家咖啡廳吃早午餐。 || Sunday brunch=週日早午餐 || Brunch is a meal ___ combines breakfast and lunch. || that|what|who || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物（a meal）。 || 早午餐是結合早餐和午餐的一餐。
bubble || n./v. || 泡泡 || The kids are blowing bubbles. || 孩子們在吹泡泡。 || bubble tea=珍珠奶茶; blow bubbles=吹泡泡 || Bubble tea, ___ was invented in Taiwan, is now popular worldwide. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 珍珠奶茶起源於台灣，現在風靡全世界。
bucket || n. || 桶子 || He carried a bucket of water. || 他提著一桶水。 || a bucket of=一桶… || Fill the bucket ___ water and bring it here. || with|of|by || fill A with B = 用 B 裝滿 A。 || 把桶子裝滿水拿過來。
bud || n./v. || 芽；花苞 || The trees are in bud. || 樹正在發芽。 || in bud=含苞待放 || In spring, the trees begin ___ new buds. || to grow|growing to|grow || begin to + 原形動詞。 || 春天時，樹開始長出新芽。
