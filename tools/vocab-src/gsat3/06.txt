# 學測 L3 06：doubtful – fade
doubtful || adj. || 懷疑的；不確定的 || I'm doubtful about his story. || 我對他的說法有所懷疑。 || be doubtful about=對…懷疑 || It's doubtful ___ he will come on time. || whether|what|which || It is doubtful whether… = …是不確定的。 || 他是否會準時來還很難說。
doughnut || n. || 甜甜圈 || I had a doughnut and coffee. || 我吃了一個甜甜圈配咖啡。 || chocolate doughnut=巧克力甜甜圈 || The doughnut shop, ___ opened last week, is always crowded. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 上週開幕的甜甜圈店總是擠滿了人。
downtown || adv./n. || 在市中心 || Let's go downtown for dinner. || 我們去市中心吃晚餐吧。 || go downtown=去市區 || Living downtown is convenient, ___ it's also expensive. || but|so|because || 前後語意相反用 but。 || 住在市中心很方便，但也很貴。
drag || v. || 拖；拉 || He dragged the heavy box across the floor. || 他把重箱子拖過地板。 || drag on=拖延 || The meeting dragged ___ for three hours. || on|in|off || drag on = 拖延、冗長地持續。 || 會議拖了三個小時。
dragonfly || n. || 蜻蜓 || A dragonfly landed on the water. || 一隻蜻蜓停在水面上。 || dragonfly=蜻蜓 || Dragonflies can be seen near ponds ___ summer. || in|on|at || 季節前面用 in。 || 夏天在池塘附近可以看到蜻蜓。
drain || v./n. || 排水；排水管 || The sink won't drain. || 水槽排不出水。 || drain away=流走; down the drain=付諸東流 || The water in the sink won't drain ___ something is blocking the pipe. || because|although|so || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 水槽的水排不掉，因為有東西堵住了水管。
dramatic || adj. || 戲劇性的；劇烈的 || There was a dramatic change in the weather. || 天氣發生了劇烈的變化。 || dramatic change=劇烈變化 || The city has seen dramatic changes ___ the past ten years. || over|since|by || over the past + 時間 = 在過去…期間。 || 這座城市在過去十年發生了劇烈的變化。
drip || v./n. || 滴下 || Water is dripping from the ceiling. || 水從天花板滴下來。 || drip dry=晾乾 || Water ___ dripping from the ceiling since last night. || has been|is|was || since + 過去時間點，用現在完成進行式。 || 從昨晚起水就一直從天花板滴下來。
drown || v. || 淹死 || He almost drowned in the river. || 他差點在河裡溺水。 || drown in=溺死於… || Children should never swim alone, or they ___ drown. || might|mights|to might || 助動詞 might 後接原形動詞。 || 小孩絕不該獨自游泳，否則可能會溺水。
drugstore || n. || 藥局 || I bought some cold medicine at the drugstore. || 我在藥局買了感冒藥。 || drugstore=藥局 || The drugstore ___ open 24 hours a day. || is|are|does || The drugstore 單數，用 is。 || 這家藥局二十四小時營業。
drunk || adj. || 喝醉的 || He was drunk last night. || 他昨晚喝醉了。 || drunk driving=酒駕; get drunk=喝醉 || Drunk driving is dangerous, ___ it is also illegal. || and|but|or || 兩個並列的敘述用 and。 || 酒駕很危險，而且也是違法的。
dumb || adj. || 啞的；愚蠢的 || That was a dumb idea. || 那是個蠢主意。 || dumb question=蠢問題 || It was dumb ___ me to forget my passport. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 我忘了帶護照真是太蠢了。
dump || v./n. || 傾倒；垃圾場 || Don't dump trash in the river. || 不要把垃圾倒進河裡。 || garbage dump=垃圾場 || People who dump trash in the river should ___ fined. || be|being|been || should 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 往河裡倒垃圾的人應該被罰款。
dumpling || n. || 水餃 || We made dumplings together. || 我們一起包水餃。 || make dumplings=包水餃 || Dumplings are a food ___ Taiwanese people often eat during holidays. || that|who|what || 關係代名詞 that 修飾物並當受詞。 || 水餃是台灣人在節日常吃的食物。
dust || n./v. || 灰塵 || The old books are covered with dust. || 那些舊書積滿了灰塵。 || dust off=撢去灰塵 || The furniture was covered ___ dust. || with|of|by || be covered with = 覆蓋著…。 || 家具上積滿了灰塵。
eager || adj. || 渴望的 || She is eager to learn. || 她渴望學習。 || be eager to V=渴望做… || The students are eager ___ the results of the exam. || to know|knowing|for knowing || be eager to + 原形動詞。 || 學生們渴望知道考試結果。
echo || n./v. || 回音 || We heard the echo of our voices in the cave. || 我們在洞穴裡聽到自己聲音的回音。 || echo back=回響 || Our voices echoed ___ the empty hall. || through|across|along of || through = 穿過（內部）。 || 我們的聲音在空蕩的大廳裡迴盪。
edit || v. || 編輯 || She edits the school newspaper. || 她編輯校刊。 || edit a video=剪輯影片 || The video ___ edited by a professional editor. || was|is being|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 這支影片由專業剪輯師剪輯。
editor || n. || 編輯 || She is the editor of a magazine. || 她是一本雜誌的編輯。 || chief editor=總編輯 || The editor asked the writer ___ the article. || to revise|revising|revise || ask sb to + 原形動詞。 || 編輯要求作者修改那篇文章。
educate || v. || 教育 || Parents should educate their children well. || 父母應該好好教育孩子。 || well-educated=受過良好教育的 || The program aims to educate people ___ the importance of recycling. || about|with|for || educate sb about sth = 教育某人關於某事。 || 這個計畫旨在教育民眾回收的重要性。
educational || adj. || 教育的；有教育意義的 || This is an educational TV program. || 這是一個有教育意義的電視節目。 || educational toys=益智玩具 || The trip was both fun ___ educational. || and|or|but || both A and B = A 和 B 都…。 || 這趟旅行既好玩又有教育意義。
efficient || adj. || 有效率的 || She is an efficient worker. || 她是個有效率的員工。 || energy-efficient=節能的 || This new method is ___ efficient than the old one. || more|most|much || 多音節形容詞的比較級用 more。 || 這個新方法比舊的更有效率。
elbow || n. || 手肘 || He hurt his elbow playing tennis. || 他打網球時傷了手肘。 || elbow room=活動空間 || He hurt his elbow ___ playing tennis. || while|during|since || while + V-ing（分詞構句）= 在…的時候。 || 他在打網球時傷了手肘。
elderly || adj. || 年長的 || We should help the elderly. || 我們應該幫助年長者。 || the elderly=年長者 || The elderly ___ given priority seats on the MRT. || are|is|be || the + 形容詞 = 一群人（複數），用 are。 || 捷運上有博愛座提供給年長者。
elect || v. || 選舉 || We elected a new class leader. || 我們選出新的班長。 || elect sb as=選某人為… || She ___ elected president of the student council last week. || was|is|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 她上週當選學生會會長。
election || n. || 選舉 || The election will be held in January. || 選舉將於一月舉行。 || presidential election=總統大選 || The election will ___ held next January. || be|is|been || will 後面接 be；被動 be + p.p.。 || 選舉將於明年一月舉行。
electricity || n. || 電 || The storm cut off the electricity. || 暴風雨讓電力中斷了。 || save electricity=節約用電 || We should save electricity ___ turning off the lights. || by|with|for || by + V-ing = 藉由…。 || 我們應該藉由關燈來節約用電。
electronic || adj. || 電子的 || I bought some electronic products. || 我買了一些電子產品。 || electronic devices=電子裝置 || Please turn off all electronic devices ___ the plane takes off. || before|after|since || 起飛「之前」關閉，用 before。 || 飛機起飛前請關閉所有電子裝置。
element || n. || 元素；要素 || Water is an important element of life. || 水是生命的重要元素。 || key element=關鍵要素 || Hard work is an important element ___ success. || of|in|to || an element of = …的要素。 || 努力是成功的重要要素。
elevator || n. || 電梯 || Let's take the elevator. || 我們搭電梯吧。 || take the elevator=搭電梯 || In case of fire, don't use the elevator. Use the stairs ___. || instead|instead of|rather || instead（副詞）= 改為、取而代之。 || 發生火災時不要搭電梯，改走樓梯。
emergency || n. || 緊急情況 || In an emergency, call 119. || 緊急時撥 119。 || in an emergency=在緊急時; emergency exit=緊急出口 || In ___ emergency, stay calm and call for help. || an|a|the || emergency 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 遇到緊急情況時，保持冷靜並求救。
emotional || adj. || 情緒的；激動的 || She became emotional at the wedding. || 她在婚禮上情緒激動。 || emotional support=情感支持 || She got emotional ___ she saw her family again. || when|what|which || when 引導時間子句。 || 她再見到家人時情緒激動。
emperor || n. || 皇帝 || The emperor ruled for 60 years. || 那位皇帝統治了六十年。 || the Roman emperor=羅馬皇帝 || The emperor, ___ ruled China for 60 years, was very powerful. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 那位統治中國六十年的皇帝權力非常大。
enable || v. || 使能夠 || The Internet enables us to learn anywhere. || 網路讓我們能隨處學習。 || enable sb to V=使某人能夠做… || Technology enables people ___ from home. || to work|working|work || enable sb to + 原形動詞。 || 科技讓人們能在家工作。
energetic || adj. || 精力充沛的 || The kids are energetic. || 孩子們精力充沛。 || energetic person=活力充沛的人 || She is ___ energetic that she exercises every morning before work. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 她精力充沛到每天早上上班前都會運動。
enjoyable || adj. || 令人愉快的 || We had an enjoyable evening. || 我們度過了一個愉快的夜晚。 || enjoyable experience=愉快的經驗 || It was ___ enjoyable trip. || an|a|the || enjoyable 以母音開頭，用 an。 || 那是一趟愉快的旅行。
entry || n. || 進入；參賽作品 || No entry! || 禁止進入！ || no entry=禁止進入; entry fee=入場費 || The contest received more than 500 entries, ___ were from high school students. || most of which|most of them which|which most || 非限定關係子句 most of which 修飾前面的 entries。 || 這項比賽收到五百多件作品，其中大多數來自高中生。
envy || n./v. || 羨慕 || I envy your talent. || 我羨慕你的才華。 || be green with envy=非常羨慕 || Everyone looked at her new car ___ envy. || with|in|by || with envy = 羨慕地。 || 大家都羨慕地看著她的新車。
erase || v. || 擦掉；刪除 || Please erase the board. || 請擦黑板。 || erase a file=刪除檔案 || The file ___ erased by mistake. || was|is being|were || 被動語態；過去用 was + p.p.。 || 那個檔案被不小心刪除了。
excellence || n. || 卓越 || The school is known for its excellence in teaching. || 這所學校以教學卓越聞名。 || academic excellence=學術卓越 || The school is known ___ its excellence in science. || for|as|with || be known for = 以…聞名。 || 這所學校以科學教育卓越著稱。
exchange || n./v. || 交換 || We exchanged gifts at Christmas. || 我們在聖誕節交換禮物。 || exchange student=交換學生; in exchange for=作為…的交換 || I'd like to exchange this shirt ___ a larger size. || for|with|to || exchange A for B = 用 A 換 B。 || 我想把這件襯衫換成大一號的。
exhibition || n. || 展覽 || We went to an art exhibition. || 我們去看了一場藝術展覽。 || art exhibition=藝術展覽 || The exhibition, ___ lasts for three months, attracts many visitors. || which|that|where || 非限定關係子句修飾物，用 which。 || 這個為期三個月的展覽吸引了許多參觀者。
expectation || n. || 期望 || The movie didn't meet my expectations. || 這部電影沒有達到我的期望。 || meet one's expectations=達到期望 || The results were far beyond our expectations, ___ made us very happy. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句 which 指代整個前句。 || 結果遠超出我們的預期，這讓我們非常開心。
experiment || n./v. || 實驗 || We did an experiment in science class. || 我們在自然課做了一個實驗。 || do an experiment=做實驗 || The scientists are doing experiments ___ find a cure for the disease. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 科學家們正在做實驗以找出這種疾病的療法。
explode || v. || 爆炸 || The bomb exploded. || 炸彈爆炸了。 || explode with anger=勃然大怒 || The gas tank exploded ___ a loud bang. || with|in|by || with a loud bang = 伴隨巨大的轟聲。 || 瓦斯桶轟的一聲爆炸了。
explore || v. || 探索 || Let's explore the old town. || 我們去探索老城區吧。 || explore the world=探索世界 || We explored the old town ___ the whole afternoon. || for|since|in || for + 一段時間。 || 我們花了一整個下午探索老城區。
export || n./v. || 出口 || Taiwan exports computer chips. || 台灣出口電腦晶片。 || export to=出口到… || Taiwanese computer chips ___ exported all over the world. || are|is|be || 被動語態；chips 複數用 are + p.p.。 || 台灣的電腦晶片外銷全世界。
expressive || adj. || 富表現力的 || She has expressive eyes. || 她有一雙會說話的眼睛。 || expressive face=表情豐富的臉 || Her face is so expressive ___ you can always tell how she feels. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 她的表情豐富到你總能看出她的感受。
extreme || adj./n. || 極端的 || The weather here can be extreme. || 這裡的天氣可能很極端。 || extreme weather=極端天氣 || Extreme weather ___ becoming more common because of climate change. || is|are|be || weather 不可數，用 is；現在進行式。 || 由於氣候變遷，極端天氣越來越常見。
fade || v. || 褪色；逐漸消失 || The color of the shirt has faded. || 這件襯衫褪色了。 || fade away=逐漸消失 || The sound of the music slowly faded ___. || away|off|out of || fade away = 逐漸消失。 || 音樂聲慢慢地消失了。
