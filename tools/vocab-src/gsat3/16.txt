# 學測 L3 16：scarf – slippery
scarf || n. || 圍巾 || She wore a red scarf. || 她圍著一條紅圍巾。 || wool scarf=羊毛圍巾 || My grandma knitted this scarf ___ me. || for|to|with || 為某人做某事用 for。 || 這條圍巾是我奶奶為我織的。
scary || adj. || 可怕的 || The movie was really scary. || 那部電影真的很可怕。 || scary movie=恐怖片 || The movie was ___ scary that I couldn't sleep. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那部電影恐怖到我睡不著。
scatter || v. || 散布 || The wind scattered the leaves. || 風把樹葉吹散了。 || scatter seeds=撒種子 || Toys were scattered ___ over the floor. || all|every|whole || all over = 到處。 || 玩具散落一地。
scholar || n. || 學者 || He is a famous scholar. || 他是一位知名的學者。 || famous scholar=知名學者 || The scholar, ___ books are widely read, gave a speech at our school. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位著作廣為流傳的學者到我們學校演講。
scholarship || n. || 獎學金 || She won a scholarship to study abroad. || 她獲得出國留學的獎學金。 || win a scholarship=獲得獎學金 || She was awarded a scholarship ___ her excellent grades. || for|of|with || be awarded sth for = 因…獲得某物。 || 她因成績優異獲得獎學金。
scientific || adj. || 科學的 || Scientific research takes time. || 科學研究需要時間。 || scientific research=科學研究 || Scientific research has shown ___ exercise improves memory. || that|what|which || that 引導名詞子句當 shown 的受詞。 || 科學研究顯示運動能增進記憶力。
scientist || n. || 科學家 || She wants to be a scientist. || 她想成為科學家。 || famous scientist=知名科學家 || The scientist ___ discovered the vaccine won the Nobel Prize. || who|which|whose || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 發現這種疫苗的科學家獲得了諾貝爾獎。
scissors || n. || 剪刀 || Can I use your scissors? || 我可以用你的剪刀嗎？ || a pair of scissors=一把剪刀 || These scissors ___ very sharp. Be careful. || are|is|be || scissors 永遠是複數，用 are。 || 這把剪刀很鋒利，要小心。
scout || n. || 童子軍；偵察員 || He is a boy scout. || 他是童子軍。 || boy scout=童子軍 || The scouts learned how ___ a fire. || to make|making|make || how to + 原形動詞。 || 童子軍學會了如何生火。
scream || v./n. || 尖叫 || She screamed when she saw the snake. || 她看到蛇時尖叫。 || scream for help=尖叫求救 || She screamed ___ help when she fell into the river. || for|to|with || scream for help = 尖叫求救。 || 她掉進河裡時尖叫求救。
screw || n./v. || 螺絲 || Use a screwdriver to tighten the screw. || 用螺絲起子把螺絲鎖緊。 || screwdriver=螺絲起子 || He tightened the screws ___ a screwdriver. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 他用螺絲起子把螺絲鎖緊。
scrub || v. || 刷洗 || Scrub the floor clean. || 把地板刷乾淨。 || scrub the floor=刷地板 || She scrubbed the pot until it was ___. || clean|cleanly|cleaning || be 動詞後面接形容詞 clean。 || 她把鍋子刷到乾乾淨淨。
seal || n./v. || 海豹；封印 || Seals live in cold water. || 海豹生活在冷水裡。 || seal a letter=封信 || The letter was sealed ___ the envelope was mailed. || before|after|until || 寄出「之前」封好，用 before。 || 信在寄出前就封好了。
security || n. || 安全；保全 || The airport has tight security. || 機場的安檢很嚴格。 || security check=安全檢查; security guard=保全 || All passengers must go through a security check ___ boarding. || before|after|since || 登機「之前」安檢，用 before。 || 所有乘客登機前都必須通過安檢。
semester || n. || 學期 || The new semester starts in September. || 新學期九月開始。 || this semester=這學期 || I'm taking five classes ___ semester. || this|these|that of || this + 單數名詞 semester。 || 我這學期修五門課。
senior || adj./n. || 年長的；高年級的 || She is a senior in high school. || 她是高三生。 || senior citizen=年長者 || She is two years senior ___ me. || to|than|from || be senior to = 比…年長／資深（不用 than）。 || 她比我大兩歲。
sensible || adj. || 明智的 || That's a sensible decision. || 那是個明智的決定。 || sensible choice=明智的選擇 || It was sensible ___ you to save some money. || of|for|to || It is + 描述人的形容詞 + of sb + to V。 || 你存些錢是明智的。
separation || n. || 分離 || The separation from his family made him sad. || 和家人分開讓他很難過。 || separation from=與…分離 || The separation of trash ___ recycling is required in Taiwan. || for|of|to || for + 名詞 = 為了…。 || 在台灣，垃圾分類回收是規定。
sexual || adj. || 性的 || Sexual harassment is a crime. || 性騷擾是犯罪。 || sexual harassment=性騷擾 || Students should learn how ___ protect themselves from sexual harassment. || to|for|of || how to + 原形動詞。 || 學生應該學習如何保護自己免於性騷擾。
sexy || adj. || 性感的 || She wore a sexy dress. || 她穿了一件性感的洋裝。 || sexy voice=性感的嗓音 || The singer is famous for her sexy voice ___ beautiful dance moves. || and|or|but || 兩者並列用 and。 || 那位歌手以性感的嗓音和優美的舞姿聞名。
shadow || n. || 影子 || The tree cast a long shadow. || 樹投下長長的影子。 || in the shadow of=在…的陰影下 || Children like to play with their shadows ___ the sun. || in|on|at || in the sun = 在陽光下。 || 孩子們喜歡在陽光下玩自己的影子。
shallow || adj. || 淺的 || The river is shallow here. || 這裡的河很淺。 || shallow water=淺水 || The water is too shallow ___ dive into. || to|for|of || too + 形容詞 + to V = 太…而不能…。 || 水太淺，不能跳水。
shampoo || n./v. || 洗髮精 || I need to buy some shampoo. || 我需要買洗髮精。 || a bottle of shampoo=一瓶洗髮精 || I ran out ___ shampoo this morning. || of|from|with || run out of = 用完…。 || 我今天早上洗髮精用完了。
shepherd || n. || 牧羊人 || The shepherd looks after the sheep. || 牧羊人照顧羊群。 || German shepherd=德國牧羊犬 || The shepherd, ___ lives in the mountains, has 200 sheep. || who|which|whom || 非限定關係子句修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 住在山上的那位牧羊人有兩百隻羊。
shiny || adj. || 閃亮的 || She has shiny black hair. || 她有一頭亮麗的黑髮。 || shiny shoes=亮晶晶的鞋子 || He polished his shoes until they were ___ shiny as a mirror. || as|so|too || as + 形容詞 + as = 和…一樣。 || 他把鞋子擦到像鏡子一樣亮。
shorten || v. || 縮短 || Can you shorten these pants? || 你可以把這條褲子改短嗎？ || shorten the time=縮短時間 || The new MRT line will shorten the travel time ___ half. || by|in|to || by + 數量 = 減少了…。 || 新捷運線將縮短一半的交通時間。
shortly || adv. || 不久 || The train will arrive shortly. || 火車即將到站。 || shortly after=不久之後 || Shortly ___ the movie started, the power went out. || after|before|since || shortly after = 在…之後不久。 || 電影開始後不久就停電了。
shovel || n./v. || 鏟子 || He used a shovel to dig a hole. || 他用鏟子挖洞。 || snow shovel=雪鏟 || He dug a hole in the garden ___ a shovel. || with|by|in || 用某個工具用 with。 || 他用鏟子在花園裡挖了一個洞。
shrimp || n. || 蝦 || I love fried shrimp. || 我愛炸蝦。 || shrimp fried rice=蝦仁炒飯 || I'm allergic to shrimp, so I can't eat anything ___ shrimp in it. || with|of|by || with = 含有…。 || 我對蝦過敏，所以不能吃任何含蝦的東西。
shrink || v. || 縮水 || Wool sweaters may shrink in hot water. || 羊毛衣在熱水中可能會縮水。 || shrink in the wash=洗後縮水 || Wool sweaters may shrink ___ you wash them in hot water. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句。 || 羊毛衣如果用熱水洗可能會縮水。
sigh || v./n. || 嘆氣 || She sighed with relief. || 她鬆了一口氣。 || sigh with relief=鬆一口氣 || She sighed ___ relief when she heard the news. || with|in|by || sigh with relief = 如釋重負地嘆氣。 || 她聽到消息時鬆了一口氣。
signal || n./v. || 信號 || There is no signal here. || 這裡沒有訊號。 || phone signal=手機訊號 || There is no phone signal ___ the mountains. || in|on|at || 在山區裡用 in。 || 山區裡沒有手機訊號。
significant || adj. || 重要的；顯著的 || There has been a significant change. || 已經有了顯著的變化。 || significant change=顯著的變化 || There has been a significant increase ___ the number of tourists. || in|of|to || an increase in = …的增加。 || 觀光客的人數顯著增加。
silk || n. || 絲 || This scarf is made of silk. || 這條圍巾是絲做的。 || silk shirt=絲質襯衫 || Silk ___ first made in China thousands of years ago. || was|is|were || 被動語態；silk 不可數，過去用 was + p.p.。 || 絲綢在數千年前首先在中國製造。
similarity || n. || 相似處 || There are many similarities between the two. || 兩者有很多相似之處。 || similarity between=…之間的相似處 || There are many similarities ___ Chinese and Japanese food. || between|among|with || 兩者之間用 between。 || 中式和日式料理有很多相似之處。
sin || n. || 罪 || Lying is a sin. || 說謊是一種罪。 || commit a sin=犯罪 || It's a sin ___ waste food. || to|for|of || It is + 名詞 + to V。 || 浪費食物是一種罪過。
sincere || adj. || 真誠的 || Please accept my sincere apology. || 請接受我誠摯的歉意。 || sincere thanks=誠摯的感謝 || She is always sincere ___ her friends. || with|to be|at || be sincere with = 對…真誠。 || 她對朋友總是很真誠。
sink || n./v. || 水槽；下沉 || Put the dishes in the sink. || 把碗盤放進水槽。 || kitchen sink=廚房水槽 || The ship began to sink ___ it hit a rock. || after|before|until || 撞到石頭「之後」開始下沉，用 after。 || 那艘船撞到岩石後開始下沉。
sip || v./n. || 啜飲 || She sipped her tea slowly. || 她慢慢地啜飲著茶。 || take a sip=喝一小口 || She took a sip of tea ___ answering the question. || before|after|since || 回答「之前」喝一口，用 before。 || 她在回答問題之前喝了一小口茶。
situation || n. || 情況 || The situation is getting worse. || 情況越來越糟。 || in this situation=在這種情況下 || What would you do if you ___ in my situation? || were|are|have been || 與現在事實相反的假設：If + 過去式（be 用 were）。 || 如果你處在我的情況，你會怎麼做？
skate || v./n. || 溜冰 || Can you skate? || 你會溜冰嗎？ || ice skate=溜冰 || We went to the ice rink ___ skate last weekend. || to|for|so || 不定詞 to V 表示目的。 || 我們上週末去溜冰場溜冰。
ski || v./n. || 滑雪 || We go to Japan to ski every winter. || 我們每年冬天去日本滑雪。 || ski resort=滑雪勝地 || It's my first time to ski, so I'm a little ___. || nervous|nervously|nerve || be 動詞後面接形容詞 nervous。 || 這是我第一次滑雪，所以有點緊張。
skillful || adj. || 熟練的 || He is a skillful driver. || 他是個熟練的駕駛。 || be skillful at=擅長… || She is skillful ___ making cakes. || at|in to|for || be skillful at = 擅長…。 || 她很擅長做蛋糕。
skinny || adj. || 很瘦的 || The dog is very skinny. || 那隻狗非常瘦。 || skinny jeans=緊身牛仔褲 || The dog was so skinny ___ you could see its bones. || that|what|which || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 那隻狗瘦到看得見骨頭。
skip || v. || 跳過；略過 || Don't skip breakfast. || 不要不吃早餐。 || skip class=翹課; skip breakfast=不吃早餐 || You shouldn't skip breakfast ___ it's the most important meal of the day. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 你不應該不吃早餐，因為那是一天中最重要的一餐。
slave || n. || 奴隸 || Don't be a slave to your phone. || 別當手機的奴隸。 || slave to=…的奴隸 || Many people have become slaves ___ their phones. || to|of|for || a slave to = …的奴隸。 || 很多人已經成為手機的奴隸。
sleeve || n. || 袖子 || The shirt has long sleeves. || 這件襯衫是長袖的。 || long sleeves=長袖; roll up one's sleeves=捲起袖子（準備做事） || Let's roll up our sleeves and get ___ work. || to|at|for || get to work = 開始工作。 || 我們捲起袖子開始工作吧。
slender || adj. || 苗條的 || She is tall and slender. || 她又高又苗條。 || slender figure=苗條的身材 || She is slender ___ she eats a lot. || although|because|so || although = 雖然（讓步）。 || 雖然她吃很多，她還是很苗條。
slice || n./v. || 片 || Can I have a slice of cake? || 我可以吃一片蛋糕嗎？ || a slice of=一片… || Cut the bread ___ thin slices. || into|in|to || cut A into B = 把 A 切成 B。 || 把麵包切成薄片。
slippery || adj. || 滑的 || The floor is slippery. || 地板很滑。 || slippery road=濕滑的道路 || Be careful. The floor is slippery ___ it's wet. || because|so|although || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 小心，地板濕了所以很滑。
