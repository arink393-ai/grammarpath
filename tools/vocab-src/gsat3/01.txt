# 學測 L3 01：aboard – background
aboard || prep./adv. || 在（船、車、機）上 || All passengers are now aboard the ship. || 所有乘客都已上船。 || go aboard=上船（機）; welcome aboard=歡迎搭乘 || By the time we arrived, all the passengers ___ aboard the plane. || had gone|have gone|go || 過去某時之前已完成的動作，用過去完成式 had + p.p.。 || 我們到達時，所有乘客都已經登機了。
acceptable || adj. || 可接受的 || Your excuse is not acceptable. || 你的藉口是不能接受的。 || socially acceptable=社會可接受的 || Is it acceptable ___ students to use phones in class? || for|of|to || It is + 形容詞 + for sb + to V。 || 學生上課使用手機是可以接受的嗎？
accurate || adj. || 準確的 || The weather report was accurate this time. || 這次的氣象預報很準確。 || accurate information=正確的資訊 || The information ___ in the report is accurate. || provided|providing|provides || 過去分詞 provided 修飾名詞，表被動（被提供的資訊）。 || 報告中提供的資訊是準確的。
ache || n./v. || 疼痛 || My legs ache after the long hike. || 長途健行後我的腿很痠痛。 || headache=頭痛; aches and pains=各種痠痛 || My legs still ache ___ the long hike yesterday. || from|of|with || ache from = 因…而痠痛。 || 我的腿還因為昨天的長途健行而痠痛。
additional || adj. || 額外的 || There is an additional charge for delivery. || 送貨要額外收費。 || additional cost=額外費用 || There is an additional fee ___ you want to check in early. || if|unless|though || if 引導條件子句「如果…」。 || 如果你要提早入住，需額外付費。
admire || v. || 欽佩；欣賞 || I admire her courage. || 我欽佩她的勇氣。 || admire sb for sth=因某事欽佩某人 || She is admired ___ her kindness by everyone. || for|of|with || be admired for = 因…而受到欽佩。 || 她因為善良而受到大家的欽佩。
advanced || adj. || 進階的；先進的 || This is an advanced English class. || 這是進階英文課。 || advanced technology=先進科技 || Taiwan is famous for its advanced technology, ___ is used all over the world. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句（逗號後）修飾物，用 which，不可用 that。 || 台灣以其先進的科技聞名，這些科技被世界各地使用。
advantage || n. || 優勢；好處 || Speaking two languages is an advantage. || 會說兩種語言是一種優勢。 || take advantage of=利用; have an advantage over=勝過 || You should take advantage ___ this chance. || of|for|to || take advantage of = 利用…。 || 你應該好好利用這個機會。
adventure || n. || 冒險 || The trip was a great adventure. || 這趟旅行是一次很棒的冒險。 || adventure story=冒險故事 || The movie is about a boy ___ goes on an adventure in space. || who|whom|which || 關係代名詞修飾人並當主詞，用 who。 || 這部電影是關於一個到太空冒險的男孩。
advise || v. || 建議；勸告 || The doctor advised me to rest. || 醫生建議我休息。 || advise sb to V=建議某人做… || I was advised ___ more vegetables by the doctor. || to eat|eating|eat || 被動：be advised to + 原形動詞。 || 醫生建議我多吃蔬菜。
adviser || n. || 顧問 || She is my academic adviser. || 她是我的學業導師。 || financial adviser=理財顧問 || The adviser, ___ advice I followed, helped me choose a college. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位顧問幫我選了大學，我聽從了他的建議。
afford || v. || 負擔得起 || I can't afford a new car. || 我買不起新車。 || can afford to V=負擔得起做… || We can't afford ___ such an expensive house. || to buy|buying|buy || afford to + 原形動詞。 || 我們買不起這麼貴的房子。
agriculture || n. || 農業 || Agriculture is important to the country. || 農業對這個國家很重要。 || modern agriculture=現代農業 || Many young people are no longer interested ___ agriculture. || in|on|at || be interested in = 對…感興趣。 || 許多年輕人已不再對農業感興趣。
airline || n. || 航空公司 || Which airline are you flying with? || 你搭哪家航空公司？ || airline ticket=機票 || The airline announced that all flights ___ canceled because of the typhoon. || were|was|be || 主要子句過去式，名詞子句也用過去式；flights 複數被動用 were。 || 航空公司宣布所有航班因颱風取消。
alley || n. || 巷子 || The restaurant is in a small alley. || 這家餐廳在一條小巷子裡。 || narrow alley=窄巷 || Walking alone in a dark alley ___ dangerous at night. || is|are|be || 動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。 || 晚上獨自走在暗巷裡很危險。
almond || n. || 杏仁 || Almonds are good for your health. || 杏仁對健康有益。 || almond milk=杏仁奶 || Eating a handful of almonds every day ___ lower the risk of heart disease. || may|mays|maying || 助動詞 may 後接原形動詞；may 本身沒有變化。 || 每天吃一把杏仁可能降低心臟病的風險。
alphabet || n. || 字母表 || There are 26 letters in the English alphabet. || 英文字母有 26 個。 || the Greek alphabet=希臘字母 || Children usually learn the alphabet ___ they learn to read. || before|after|since || 先學字母「之前」才會閱讀，用 before。 || 孩子們通常在學會閱讀之前先學字母。
ambassador || n. || 大使 || He was the ambassador to Japan. || 他曾任駐日大使。 || ambassador to=駐…大使 || The ambassador, ___ arrived yesterday, will meet the president today. || who|which|that || 非限定關係子句修飾人，用 who（不可用 that）。 || 那位大使昨天抵達，今天將與總統會面。
ambition || n. || 抱負；野心 || Her ambition is to become a pilot. || 她的抱負是成為飛行員。 || have an ambition to V=有做…的抱負 || He has always had the ambition ___ his own company. || to start|of starting to|starting || the ambition to + 原形動詞。 || 他一直有自己創業的抱負。
ambulance || n. || 救護車 || Call an ambulance right away! || 馬上叫救護車！ || call an ambulance=叫救護車 || Had the ambulance arrived earlier, the man ___ survived. || might have|may|might has || 與過去事實相反的假設：主要子句用 might/would have + p.p.。 || 如果救護車早點到，那名男子可能就活下來了。
angel || n. || 天使 || She is an angel to the poor children. || 她是那些貧童的天使。 || guardian angel=守護天使 || She looked after the orphans as if she ___ their guardian angel. || were|is|has been || as if + 假設語氣，be 動詞一律用 were。 || 她照顧那些孤兒，彷彿她是他們的守護天使。
anxious || adj. || 焦慮的；渴望的 || She was anxious about her exam results. || 她對考試結果感到焦慮。 || be anxious about=為…焦慮; be anxious to V=渴望做… || He is anxious ___ his parents again. || to see|seeing|for seeing || be anxious to + 原形動詞 = 渴望做…。 || 他渴望再見到他的父母。
anyhow || adv. || 無論如何 || It was raining, but we went out anyhow. || 下著雨，但我們無論如何還是出去了。 || anyhow=無論如何 || The tickets were expensive, but we bought them anyhow ___ we really wanted to see the show. || because|although|unless || 後面是原因，用 because。 || 票很貴，但我們還是買了，因為我們真的很想看這場表演。
apart || adv. || 分開；相距 || The two towns are ten miles apart. || 那兩個小鎮相距十英里。 || apart from=除了…以外; fall apart=散架 || Apart ___ English, she also speaks French. || from|of|with || apart from = 除了…之外。 || 除了英文，她也會說法文。
apologize || v. || 道歉 || You should apologize to her. || 你應該向她道歉。 || apologize to sb for sth=為某事向某人道歉 || He apologized to me ___ breaking my phone. || for|of|to || apologize for + V-ing = 為…道歉。 || 他為弄壞我的手機向我道歉。
appeal || n./v. || 吸引；呼籲 || The idea appeals to young people. || 這個點子吸引年輕人。 || appeal to=吸引…、向…呼籲 || The new game appeals ___ teenagers all over the world. || to|for|with || appeal to = 吸引…。 || 這款新遊戲吸引了世界各地的青少年。
approve || v. || 贊成；批准 || My parents don't approve of my plan. || 我父母不贊成我的計畫。 || approve of=贊成… || My parents didn't approve ___ my going abroad alone. || of|to|for || approve of = 贊成…。 || 我父母不贊成我獨自出國。
apron || n. || 圍裙 || Mom wears an apron when she cooks. || 媽媽煮飯時穿著圍裙。 || wear an apron=穿圍裙 || Put on an apron ___ your clothes won't get dirty. || so that|such that|so as || so that = 為了（使…不會）。 || 穿上圍裙，這樣衣服就不會弄髒。
armed || adj. || 武裝的 || The robbers were armed with guns. || 搶匪持有槍械。 || armed forces=武裝部隊; be armed with=配備… || The soldiers were armed ___ modern weapons. || with|by|of || be armed with = 配備…。 || 士兵們配備了現代化武器。
arrest || v./n. || 逮捕 || The police arrested the thief. || 警方逮捕了小偷。 || under arrest=被捕; arrest sb for=因…逮捕某人 || The man was arrested ___ stealing a car. || for|of|with || arrest sb for + V-ing = 因…逮捕某人。 || 那名男子因偷車被捕。
ash || n. || 灰；灰燼 || The house was burned to ashes. || 那間房子被燒成灰燼。 || burn to ashes=燒成灰 || The volcano covered the town ___ ash. || with|by|of || cover A with B = 用 B 覆蓋 A。 || 火山灰覆蓋了整個小鎮。
aside || adv. || 在旁邊；撇開 || Put your phone aside and listen. || 把手機放一邊，專心聽。 || put aside=放一邊、存下; aside from=除了 || She puts aside some money every month ___ she can travel abroad. || so that|such that|in order || so that = 為了（使…能夠）。 || 她每個月存一些錢，這樣就能出國旅行。
assist || v. || 協助 || He assisted me with my homework. || 他協助我做功課。 || assist sb with/in=協助某人… || The nurse assisted the doctor ___ the operation. || with|to|for || assist sb with sth = 協助某人某事。 || 護理師協助醫生進行手術。
assistant || n. || 助理 || She works as a teaching assistant. || 她擔任助教。 || shop assistant=店員; teaching assistant=助教 || The assistant, ___ name is Amy, is very helpful. || whose|who|which || 所有格關係代名詞 whose + 名詞。 || 那位名叫艾咪的助理非常幫得上忙。
assume || v. || 假定；以為 || I assumed you knew the answer. || 我以為你知道答案。 || assume that=假定… || Let's assume that everyone ___ on time tomorrow. || will arrive|arrived|arriving || 名詞子句講未來的事用 will + 原形動詞。 || 我們假設明天每個人都會準時到。
athlete || n. || 運動員 || She is a professional athlete. || 她是職業運動員。 || professional athlete=職業運動員 || The athlete, ___ trained for years, finally won a gold medal. || having|has|to have || 分詞構句（完成式）表示先發生的動作。 || 那位運動員訓練多年後，終於贏得金牌。
attitude || n. || 態度 || He has a positive attitude toward life. || 他對人生抱持積極的態度。 || attitude toward=對…的態度 || Your attitude ___ learning is more important than your talent. || toward|with|of || attitude toward = 對…的態度。 || 你對學習的態度比天分更重要。
attract || v. || 吸引 || The festival attracts many tourists. || 這個節慶吸引許多觀光客。 || attract attention=吸引注意 || The night market attracts thousands of tourists, ___ makes it very crowded. || which|that|what || 非限定關係子句 which 指代前面整個句子。 || 夜市吸引成千上萬的觀光客，使得那裡非常擁擠。
attractive || adj. || 吸引人的 || The offer is very attractive. || 這個提議很吸引人。 || attractive price=吸引人的價格 || The job is ___ attractive that many people applied for it. || so|such|too || so + 形容詞 + that…。 || 這份工作吸引人到很多人都去應徵。
audience || n. || 觀眾 || The audience clapped loudly. || 觀眾大聲鼓掌。 || a large audience=大批觀眾 || The singer bowed to the audience ___ the end of the show. || at|in|on || at the end of = 在…結束時。 || 表演結束時，歌手向觀眾鞠躬。
automatic || adj. || 自動的 || The door is automatic. || 這扇門是自動的。 || automatic door=自動門 || The door is automatic, so you don't need ___ it. || to push|pushing|push || need to + 原形動詞。 || 這扇門是自動的，所以你不需要推。
automobile || n. || 汽車 || The automobile changed the way people travel. || 汽車改變了人們旅行的方式。 || automobile industry=汽車工業 || Since the automobile ___ invented, people's lives have changed a lot. || was|has been|is || since 子句敘述過去的時間點，用過去式 was invented。 || 自從汽車發明以來，人們的生活改變了很多。
avenue || n. || 大道 || The museum is on Fifth Avenue. || 博物館在第五大道上。 || Fifth Avenue=第五大道 || The shop is located ___ Park Avenue. || on|in|of || 在某條街道上用 on。 || 那家店位於公園大道上。
awake || adj./v. || 醒著的 || I was awake all night. || 我整晚沒睡。 || stay awake=保持清醒; wide awake=完全清醒 || The noise kept me awake ___ night. || all|every|whole || all night = 一整晚。 || 噪音讓我整晚睡不著。
awaken || v. || 喚醒；使意識到 || The news awakened people to the danger. || 這則新聞讓人們意識到危險。 || awaken sb to sth=使某人意識到… || The movie awakened people ___ the problem of climate change. || to|for|of || awaken sb to sth = 使某人意識到…。 || 這部電影讓人們意識到氣候變遷的問題。
award || n./v. || 獎；頒發 || She won an award for her novel. || 她的小說得了獎。 || win an award=得獎 || He was awarded a prize ___ his excellent work. || for|of|with || be awarded (a prize) for = 因…獲獎。 || 他因出色的作品而獲獎。
aware || adj. || 意識到的 || Are you aware of the danger? || 你意識到危險了嗎？ || be aware of=意識到… || Many people are not aware ___ how much sugar is in soft drinks. || of|to|with || be aware of = 意識到…。 || 很多人沒有意識到汽水裡有多少糖。
awful || adj. || 可怕的；糟糕的 || The food tasted awful. || 這食物難吃極了。 || feel awful=覺得很糟 || The food tasted awful, ___ we didn't finish it. || so|but|or || 前面是原因、後面是結果，用 so。 || 食物難吃極了，所以我們沒吃完。
awkward || adj. || 尷尬的；笨拙的 || There was an awkward silence. || 出現了一陣尷尬的沉默。 || awkward situation=尷尬的處境 || I felt awkward ___ I met my ex-classmate at the party. || when|which|what || when 引導時間子句。 || 在派對上遇到以前的同學時，我覺得很尷尬。
background || n. || 背景 || She comes from a musical background. || 她出身音樂世家。 || family background=家庭背景 || Students ___ different backgrounds study together here. || from|to|at || from different backgrounds = 來自不同背景。 || 來自不同背景的學生在這裡一起學習。
