# zh
## 選時態的關鍵：看時間線索
選擇時態時，先找句子裡的<b>時間線索</b>，再決定用哪一個時態。
表：時間線索 → 時態
| 時間線索 | 時態 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| every day, usually, always, often | 現在簡單式 | She <b>walks</b> to school every day. |
| now, right now, Look!, at the moment | 現在進行式 | Look! He <b>is running</b>. |
| yesterday, last…, …ago, in 2010 | 過去簡單式 | I <b>saw</b> him yesterday. |
| at 8 last night, when + 過去式 | 過去進行式 | I <b>was sleeping</b> when you called. |
| ever, never, already, yet, just, for, since | 現在完成式 | I <b>have</b> already <b>eaten</b>. |
| for / since + 強調持續 | 現在完成進行式 | It <b>has been raining</b> since noon. |
| by the time + 過去式, before + 過去式 | 過去完成式 | The film <b>had started</b> when we arrived. |
| tomorrow, next…, soon, tonight | 未來式 | I <b>will call</b> you tomorrow. |
| by + 未來時間 | 未來完成式 | I <b>will have finished</b> by 5. |

## 十二時態總表
表：英文十二時態（以 play 為例）
| | 簡單式 | 進行式 | 完成式 | 完成進行式 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 現在 | play / plays | am / is / are playing | have / has played | have / has been playing |
| 過去 | played | was / were playing | had played | had been playing |
| 未來 | will play | will be playing | will have played | will have been playing |
註：簡單式＝事實、習慣；進行式＝正在進行；完成式＝到某時已完成；完成進行式＝到某時已持續多久。

## 最常犯的三種錯
| ✗ 錯誤 | ✓ 正確 | 規則 |
|---|---|---|
| I have seen him yesterday. | I <b>saw</b> him yesterday. | 明確過去時間 → 過去式 |
| She works right now. | She<b>'s working</b> right now. | right now → 進行式 |
| I live here since 2015. | I<b>'ve lived</b> here since 2015. | since → 完成式 |

# mc
Q: My brother ___ his homework right now. || 我弟弟現在正在寫功課。
A: does | is doing* | has done
W: right now 表示正在進行，用現在進行式。
Q: We ___ to Taitung last summer. || 我們去年夏天去了台東。
A: have gone | went* | go
W: last summer 是明確過去時間，用過去簡單式。
Q: She ___ in this company since 2018. || 她從 2018 年就在這家公司工作。
A: works | has worked* | worked
W: since 2018 表示持續到現在，用現在完成式。
Q: When I got to school, the class ___. || 我到學校時，課已經開始了。
A: has started | had started* | starts
W: 比過去另一件事更早發生，用過去完成式。

# err
S: I [have met|met] her two years ago. || 我兩年前認識她的。
W: 有 ago 這種明確過去時間，用過去簡單式。
S: Listen! The baby [cries|is crying]. || 聽！寶寶在哭。
W: Listen! 表示正在發生，用現在進行式。
S: He [is knowing|has known] me for ten years. || 他認識我十年了。
W: 有 for ten years 持續到現在，而且 know 是狀態動詞，用現在完成式。

# mk

- since 2015 持續到現在，用現在完成式 I've lived。

# mc
Q: She usually ___ the bus to school. || 她通常搭公車上學。
A: takes* | is taking | has taken
W: usually 表示習慣，用現在簡單式。
Q: I ___ my homework when my friend called. || 朋友打來時我正在寫功課。
A: do | was doing* | have done
W: 被打斷的進行中動作，用過去進行式。
Q: They ___ in Taipei since 2010. || 他們從 2010 年就住在台北。
A: live | have lived* | lived
W: since 表示持續到現在，用現在完成式。
Q: By next year, I ___ from college. || 到明年，我就已經大學畢業了。
A: will graduate | will have graduated* | graduated
W: by + 未來時間，用未來完成式。
# err
S: Look! It [snows|is snowing]! || 看！下雪了！
W: Look! 表示正在發生，用現在進行式。
S: I [have gone|went] to Kenting last summer. || 我去年夏天去了墾丁。
W: last summer 是明確過去，用過去簡單式。
S: She [works|has worked] here for ten years. || 她在這裡工作十年了。
W: for ten years 持續到現在，用現在完成式。
S: When we arrived, the show [already started|had already started]. || 我們到的時候，表演已經開始了。
W: 比「到達」更早發生，用過去完成式。
