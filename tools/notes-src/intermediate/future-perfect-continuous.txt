# zh
## 未來完成進行式：到未來某時已經持續了多久
公式：<b>will have been + V-ing</b>。表示一個動作到了<b>未來某個時間點時，已經持續了一段時間</b>（可能還會繼續）。
<span class="en">By June, I <b>will have been working</b> here for five years.</span>（到六月，我就在這裡工作滿五年了。）
表：完成進行式家族
| 時態 | 公式 | 持續到 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 現在完成進行式 | have been V-ing | 現在 | I <b>have been waiting</b> for 20 minutes. |
| 過去完成進行式 | had been V-ing | 過去某時 | I <b>had been waiting</b> for 20 minutes when she came. |
| 未來完成進行式 | will have been V-ing | 未來某時 | By 3 p.m., I <b>will have been waiting</b> for an hour. |

## 必備元素
未來完成進行式通常同時有兩個時間資訊：
- 未來的時間點：by + 時間、by the time + 現在式、next year…
- 持續多久：for + 一段時間
<span class="en"><b>By the time</b> he retires, he <b>will have been teaching</b> <b>for 40 years</b>.</span>

## 未來完成式 vs. 未來完成進行式
| 未來完成式 | 未來完成進行式 |
|---|---|
| 強調完成、數量 | 強調持續的時間長度 |
| By June, I <b>will have read</b> 20 books. | By June, I <b>will have been reading</b> for 6 months. |
| 狀態動詞用這個 | ✗ will have been knowing |
> 狀態動詞（know, own, believe）不用進行式：By then, she <b>will have known</b> him for ten years.

## 常見錯誤
| ✗ 錯誤 | ✓ 正確 |
|---|---|
| By June, I will work here for five years. | By June, I <b>will have been working</b> here for five years. |
| They will have been drive for hours. | They will have been <b>driving</b> for hours. |
| will has been | will <b>have</b> been（will 後一律用原形 have） |

# mc
Q: By next month, we ___ in this house for ten years. || 到下個月，我們就在這間房子住滿十年了。
A: will live | will have been living* | have been living
W: 到未來某時已持續多久，用 will have been V-ing。
Q: By 5 p.m., she will have been ___ for eight hours. || 到下午五點，她就已經工作了八小時。
A: work | working* | worked
W: will have been 後面接 V-ing。
Q: By the time he arrives, I ___ for two hours. || 等他到的時候，我就已經等了兩個小時。
A: will have been waiting* | will wait | am waiting
W: by the time + 現在式；主要子句用未來完成進行式。
Q: By then, I ___ her for twenty years. || 到那時，我就認識她二十年了。
A: will have been knowing | will have known* | will know
W: know 是狀態動詞，用未來完成式。

# err
S: Next year, he [will teach|will have been teaching] here for 30 years. || 明年他就在這裡教書滿三十年了。
W: 有 for 30 years 表示到未來某時已持續多久，要用未來完成進行式。
S: By noon, the kids will have been [swim|swimming] for three hours. || 到中午，孩子們就已經游了三個小時。
W: will have been 後面接 V-ing。
S: She [will has|will have] been studying for six hours by midnight. || 到午夜她就讀了六個小時了。
W: will 後面一律用原形 have。
