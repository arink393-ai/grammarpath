# zh
## 從屬連接詞是什麼
對等連接詞（and, but, or）連接「地位相等」的兩句；<b>從屬連接詞</b>（when, because, so that…）帶出一個「附屬子句」，這個子句不能單獨成句，要靠主句才完整。
表：四大類從屬連接詞
| 類別 | 常用字 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 時間 | when, while, as soon as, until, before, after | <b>As soon as</b> I got home, I fell asleep. |
| 原因 | because, since, as | <b>Since</b> it was late, we went home. |
| 結果 | so + 形/副 + that | It was <b>so</b> cold <b>that</b> the lake froze. |
| 目的 | so that + 主詞 + can/would | I saved money <b>so that</b> I could travel. |

## 子句的位置與逗號
附屬子句放<b>句首</b>→後面加逗號；放<b>句尾</b>→不加逗號。
| 位置 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 子句在前 | <b>Before</b> you leave, turn off the lights. |
| 子句在後 | Turn off the lights <b>before</b> you leave. |

## 時間子句用「現在式」表未來
講未來的事，when／as soon as／until／before／after 帶出的時間子句要用<b>現在式</b>，不用 will（主句才有 will）。
| ✗ 錯誤 | ✓ 正確 |
|---|---|
| I'll call when I will arrive. | I'll call <b>when</b> I <b>arrive</b>. |
| Wait until the rain will stop. | Wait <b>until</b> the rain <b>stops</b>. |
> 主句可以有 will，但時間子句不行。

## because vs. because of
because 後面接<b>子句</b>（主詞 + 動詞）；because of 後面接<b>名詞</b>。
| because + 子句 | because of + 名詞 |
|---|---|
| because it <b>was</b> raining | because of <b>the rain</b> |

## so … that（結果）vs. so that（目的）
so + 形容詞/副詞 + that ＝「如此…以致於」（結果）；so that + 主詞 + can/could ＝「為了」（目的）。兩者差一個字，意思不同。
<span class="en">It was <b>so</b> loud <b>that</b> I couldn't think.</span>（結果）
<span class="en">I spoke clearly <b>so that</b> everyone could follow.</span>（目的）

# mc
Q: ___ I finish my homework, I'll watch TV. || 我一寫完功課就去看電視。
A: Until | As soon as* | Because
W: 「一…就…」用時間連接詞 as soon as。
Q: We cancelled the picnic ___ it was raining hard. || 因為雨下得很大，我們取消了野餐。
A: so | because* | so that
W: 表原因、後接子句，用 because。
Q: It was so cold ___ the lake froze. || 天氣冷到湖面結冰。
A: so | that* | because
W: so + 形容詞 + that 表「如此…以致於」。
Q: Please wait ___ I come back. || 請等到我回來。
A: so that | until* | because
W: 「直到」用時間連接詞 until。

Q: ___ you've finished, you can leave. || 既然你做完了，就可以走了。
A: Until | Since* | So that
W: 「既然」表原因，用 Since。
Q: He whispered ___ nobody could hear him. || 他小聲說話，為了不讓別人聽見。
A: because | so that* | until
W: 表目的用 so that + 主詞 + could。
Q: ___ I was cooking, the phone rang. || 我在煮飯的時候，電話響了。
A: While* | Until | Because
W: 兩件事同時進行，用 while。
Q: The soup was so salty ___ nobody ate it. || 湯鹹到沒人吃。
A: because | that* | so
W: so + 形容詞 + that 表結果。
