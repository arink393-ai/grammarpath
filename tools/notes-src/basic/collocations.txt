# zh
## 什麼是搭配詞？
搭配詞（collocation）是英文母語者習慣放在一起的字。中文說「拍照」，英文是 <span class="en"><b>take</b> a photo</span>，不是 make 或 do。這些沒有一定的邏輯，最好整組背起來。
表：have 的搭配
| 類別 | 搭配詞 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 吃喝 | have breakfast / lunch / dinner | 吃早餐／午餐／晚餐 |
| 吃喝 | have a drink / a snack / coffee | 喝一杯／吃點心／喝咖啡 |
| 經驗 | have fun / a good time | 玩得開心 |
| 活動 | have a party / a meeting / a class | 開派對／開會／上課 |
| 休息 | have a rest / a break / a nap | 休息／小睡 |
| 清潔 | have a shower / a bath | 洗澡（英式） |
| 其他 | have a look / a try / a chat | 看一下／試試看／聊天 |

表：take 的搭配
| 搭配詞 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| take a photo / picture | 拍照 |
| take a shower / bath | 洗澡（美式） |
| take a bus / taxi / train | 搭公車／計程車／火車 |
| take a break / rest | 休息 |
| take medicine | 吃藥 |
| take a test / an exam | 參加考試 |
| take notes | 做筆記 |
| take care of | 照顧 |
| take part in | 參加 |
| take a walk | 散步 |
| take time | 花時間 |

表：get 的搭配
| 搭配詞 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| get up | 起床 |
| get dressed | 穿好衣服 |
| get home / to school | 到家／到學校 |
| get married | 結婚 |
| get lost | 迷路 |
| get sick / better / angry | 生病／好轉／生氣 |
| get a job / a letter | 得到工作／收到信 |
| get on / off the bus | 上／下公車 |
註：get + 形容詞 = 「變得…」：It's getting dark.

## make vs. do
| make（製造、產生） | do（做事、工作） |
|---|---|
| make a cake, make a mistake | do homework, do the dishes |
| make friends, make noise | do exercise, do your best |
| make a decision, make money | do the laundry, do a favor |

# mc
Q: Let's ___ a photo in front of the temple. || 我們在廟前拍張照吧。
A: do | make | take*
W: 拍照是 take a photo。
Q: What time do you usually ___ lunch? || 你通常幾點吃午餐？
A: take | have* | get
W: 吃三餐用 have。
Q: Be careful not to ___ lost in the city. || 小心別在城裡迷路。
A: get* | take | have
W: 迷路是 get lost。
Q: I always ___ my homework after dinner. || 我總是晚餐後寫功課。
A: make | do* | take
W: 做作業是 do homework。

# err
S: Don't forget to [eat|take] your medicine. || 別忘了吃藥。
W: 吃藥的英文是 take medicine。
S: I [did|made] a mistake on the test. || 我在考試中犯了一個錯。
W: 犯錯是 make a mistake。
S: We [made|had] a great time at the beach. || 我們在海邊玩得很開心。
W: 玩得開心是 have a great time。
