# zh
## 反身代名詞：動作回到自己身上
當主詞和受詞是<b>同一個人</b>時，受詞要用反身代名詞（-self / -selves）。<span class="en">I cut <b>myself</b>.</span>（我割到自己）
表：反身代名詞
| 主詞 | 反身代名詞 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I | myself | I taught <b>myself</b> to cook. |
| you（單數） | yourself | Help <b>yourself</b>! |
| he | himself | He hurt <b>himself</b>. |
| she | herself | She looked at <b>herself</b>. |
| it | itself | The cat cleaned <b>itself</b>. |
| we | ourselves | We enjoyed <b>ourselves</b>. |
| you（複數） | yourselves | Take care of <b>yourselves</b>. |
| they | themselves | They introduced <b>themselves</b>. |
註：單數 -self，複數 -selves。注意 himself、themselves，沒有 hisself、theirselves 這種字。

## 三種用法
| 用法 | 說明 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 當受詞 | 主詞＝受詞 | She blamed <b>herself</b>. |
| 強調 | 「親自」，可省略 | I made this cake <b>myself</b>. |
| by oneself | 獨自、靠自己 | He lives <b>by himself</b>. |

## 常見慣用語
| 片語 | 意思 |
|---|---|
| enjoy oneself | 玩得開心 |
| help oneself (to…) | 自己取用 |
| teach oneself | 自學 |
| make oneself at home | 別客氣、當自己家 |
| talk to oneself | 自言自語 |
> 有些動詞中文會說「自己」，英文卻不用反身：relax、feel、concentrate。I feel tired.（✗ I feel myself tired）

# mc
Q: The children enjoyed ___ at the party. || 孩子們在派對上玩得很開心。
A: them | themselves* | theirselves
W: 主詞是 The children（複數），用 themselves。
Q: Be careful! Don't cut ___. || 小心！別割到你自己。
A: you | yourself* | yourselves
W: 對一個人說話，用 yourself。
Q: She lives by ___. || 她一個人住。
A: her | herself* | hers
W: by oneself 表示「獨自」。
Q: Please help ___ to some cake. || 請自己拿蛋糕吃。
A: yourself* | you | your
W: help oneself to… 是「自行取用」。
