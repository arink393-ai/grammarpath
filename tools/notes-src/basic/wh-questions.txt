# zh
## 疑問詞問句：問「什麼、哪裡、誰…」
Yes/No 問句只能回答是或不是；想問細節，就要用疑問詞（Wh- words）開頭。
表：常用疑問詞
| 疑問詞 | 問什麼 | 例句 | 回答範例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | 什麼 | What is your name? | My name is Ken. |
| Who | 誰 | Who is your teacher? | Ms. Lin. |
| Where | 哪裡 | Where do you live? | In Taichung. |
| When | 何時 | When is your birthday? | It's in May. |
| Why | 為什麼 | Why are you late? | Because I missed the bus. |
| How | 如何、怎樣 | How do you go to school? | By bus. |
| Which | 哪一個（有選項） | Which color do you like? | Blue. |
| Whose | 誰的 | Whose bag is this? | It's Amy's. |

## 公式：疑問詞放最前面，後面接問句語序
表：兩種 Wh- 問句
| 句子裡有 | 公式 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be 動詞 | Wh- + am/is/are + 主詞 …? | Where <b>are</b> you? |
| 一般動詞 | Wh- + do/does + 主詞 + 原形動詞…? | What <b>does</b> she <b>like</b>? |
| 助動詞 | Wh- + can/will… + 主詞 + 原形動詞…? | When <b>can</b> you <b>come</b>? |
註：用了 does，後面動詞要用原形，-s 已經到 does 身上了。

## How 的家族
| 問法 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| How old | 幾歲 | How old are you? |
| How many | 多少（可數） | How many brothers do you have? |
| How much | 多少（不可數、價錢） | How much is it? |
| How long | 多久、多長 | How long does it take? |
| How often | 多常 | How often do you swim? |
| How far | 多遠 | How far is the station? |

## 問主詞時不用 do
疑問詞本身就是主詞時，直接接動詞：<span class="en"><b>Who</b> lives here?</span>（✗ Who does live here?）<span class="en"><b>What</b> happened?</span>

# mc
Q: ___ do you usually have lunch? — At school. || 你通常在哪裡吃午餐？在學校。
A: When | Where* | What
W: 回答是地點，問「哪裡」用 Where。
Q: What ___ your brother do on weekends? || 你哥哥週末都做什麼？
A: do | does* | is
W: 主詞 your brother 單數，助動詞用 does。
Q: ___ is your favorite singer? || 你最喜歡的歌手是誰？
A: Who* | Whose | Which
W: 問「誰」用 Who。
Q: ___ often do you go to the gym? || 你多常去健身房？
A: What | How* | Why
W: 問頻率用 How often。

# err
S: Where [you|do you] work? || 你在哪裡工作？
W: 一般動詞的問句要有助動詞 do。
S: What time does the movie [starts|start]? || 電影幾點開始？
W: 有 does 時，動詞用原形 start。
S: Who [does have|has] a pen? || 誰有筆？
W: Who 是主詞，直接接動詞，不加 does。

# mc
Q: ___ is your birthday? — It's on June 3. || 你的生日是什麼時候？六月三號。
A: Where | When* | Who
W: 問時間用 When。
Q: ___ do you like better, tea or coffee? || 茶和咖啡你比較喜歡哪個？
A: What | Which* | Who
W: 有固定選項時用 Which。
Q: Why ___ he crying? || 他為什麼在哭？
A: does | is* | do
W: 進行式的問句用 be 動詞 is。
Q: How ___ is it from here to the station? || 從這裡到車站多遠？
A: long | far* | many
W: 問距離用 How far。
