# zh
## Be 動詞過去式 was / were
現在的 am/is 過去變成 <b>was</b>；are 過去變成 <b>were</b>。用來說過去「是什麼、怎麼樣、在哪裡」。
表：was / were 配對
| 主詞 | 過去 be |
|---|---|
| I / he / she / it / 單數名詞 | was |
| you / we / they / 複數名詞 | were |
## 否定與問句
否定：was/were + not（wasn't, weren't）。問句：把 was/were 移到主詞前面。
| 肯定 | 否定 | 問句 |
|---|---|---|
| She was here. | She wasn't here. | Was she here? |
| They were late. | They weren't late. | Were they late? |
## There was / were（過去「有」）
單數用 There was，複數用 There were。
> ✗ They was happy. → ✓ They <b>were</b> happy.（複數配 were）
# mc
Q: I ___ really tired last night. || 我昨晚真的很累。
A: was* | were | am
W: I → was。
Q: ___ your parents at home? || 你爸媽在家嗎？
A: Was | Were* | Did
W: parents 複數，問句用 Were。
Q: There ___ many stars in the sky. || 天空有很多星星。
A: was | were* | is
W: many stars 複數 → There were。
# err
S: We [was|were] in the same team. || 我們在同一隊。
W: we 複數配 were。
S: He [weren't|wasn't] at the meeting. || 他沒在會議上。
W: he 的過去否定用 wasn't。
S: [Was|Were] you happy with the gift? || 你喜歡那個禮物嗎？
W: you 的問句用 Were。
