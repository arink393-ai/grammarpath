# zh
## was / were：過去的「是、在」
be 動詞的過去式只有兩個：<b>was</b> 和 <b>were</b>。用來描述過去的狀態、身分或位置。
表：be 動詞現在式與過去式對照
| 主詞 | 現在式 | 過去式 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | <b>was</b> |
| he / she / it / 單數名詞 | is | <b>was</b> |
| you / we / they / 複數名詞 | are | <b>were</b> |
註：記法：am、is → was；are → were。

## 句型
| 句型 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 肯定 | I <b>was</b> tired yesterday. |
| 否定 | They <b>weren't</b> at home last night. |
| 疑問 | <b>Was</b> she sick? — Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't. |
| Wh- 問句 | Where <b>were</b> you last weekend? |

## 常搭配的過去時間詞
| 時間詞 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| yesterday | 昨天 |
| last night / week / year | 昨晚、上週、去年 |
| ... ago | …以前（two days ago） |
| in 2020 | 在 2020 年 |
| this morning（已過去時） | 今天早上 |

## There was / There were
「過去有」用 There was（單數、不可數）／ There were（複數）：<span class="en">There <b>was</b> a cat in the garden. / There <b>were</b> many people at the party.</span>
> you 不論單複數，都用 were：You <b>were</b> right.

# mc
Q: We ___ at the beach last Sunday. || 我們上週日在海邊。
A: was | were* | are
W: 主詞 We 配 were；last Sunday 是過去。
Q: The test ___ very hard yesterday. || 昨天的考試很難。
A: was* | were | is
W: The test 單數，過去式用 was。
Q: ___ you busy last night? || 你昨晚忙嗎？
A: Was | Were* | Did
W: you 一律配 were；問句把 Were 放前面。
Q: There ___ two dogs in the yard this morning. || 今天早上院子裡有兩隻狗。
A: was | were* | is
W: two dogs 是複數，用 There were。

# err
S: My grandparents [was|were] farmers. || 我的祖父母以前是農夫。
W: My grandparents 是複數，要用 were。
S: [Did|Was] he at school yesterday? || 他昨天在學校嗎？
W: 句子裡沒有一般動詞，問句直接用 be 動詞 Was。
S: I [wasn't not|wasn't] hungry. || 我那時不餓。
W: wasn't 已經包含 not，不能再加一次。
