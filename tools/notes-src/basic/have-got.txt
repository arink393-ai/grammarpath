# zh
## have / has：擁有
have 表示「有、擁有」，也可以說身體特徵、家人、生病等。主詞是第三人稱單數時要用 <b>has</b>。
表：have 的用法
| 用途 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 擁有物品 | I <b>have</b> a new bike. |
| 家人、朋友 | She <b>has</b> two brothers. |
| 外表特徵 | He <b>has</b> short hair. |
| 生病 | I <b>have</b> a cold. |

## have got：英式口語的「有」
<b>have got / has got</b> 意思和 have 一樣，英式英文和口語很常用。
| | have | have got |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定 | She <b>has</b> a cat. | She<b>'s got</b> a cat. |
| 否定 | She <b>doesn't have</b> a cat. | She <b>hasn't got</b> a cat. |
| 疑問 | <b>Does</b> she <b>have</b> a cat? | <b>Has</b> she <b>got</b> a cat? |
| 簡答 | Yes, she does. | Yes, she has. |
註：have got 的否定和疑問直接用 have / has，不加 do / does；一般的 have 則要借助 do / does。

## 不要混用
兩種句型的助動詞不能混在一起：
- ✗ Do you have got…? → ✓ <span class="en"><b>Have</b> you <b>got</b>…?</span> 或 ✓ <span class="en"><b>Do</b> you <b>have</b>…?</span>
- ✗ I have got not… → ✓ <span class="en">I <b>haven't got</b>…</span>
> have got 只用在現在式。過去式說 had：I <b>had</b> a dog when I was young.（✗ I had got）

## have 當「吃、做」時不能換成 have got
<span class="en">have breakfast（吃早餐）, have a party（開派對）, have fun（玩得開心）</span>，這些是動作，可以用進行式 <span class="en">We're having lunch.</span>，但不能說 have got lunch。

Q: My sister ___ long hair. || 我姐姐留長頭髮。
A: have | has* | having
W: My sister 是第三人稱單數，用 has。
Q: ___ you got a pen? || 你有筆嗎？
A: Do | Have* | Are
W: have got 的問句把 Have 放前面，不用 Do。
Q: He ___ a car. He takes the bus. || 他沒有車，他搭公車。
A: hasn't got* | doesn't got | haven't got
W: 主詞 He 單數，have got 的否定是 hasn't got。
Q: Does your school ___ a swimming pool? || 你們學校有游泳池嗎？
A: has | have* | have got
W: 用了 Does，後面動詞回到原形 have。
