# zh
## have 和 have got：兩種「擁有」的說法
表示「擁有」時，have 和 have got 意思相同。have got 比較口語，英式英文很常用。
表：兩套說法
| 項目 | have | have got |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定 | I have a car. | I've got a car. |
| 第三人稱 | She has a dog. | She's got a dog. |
| 否定 | I don't have… | I haven't got… |
| 問句 | Do you have…? | Have you got…? |
## 兩套不要混用
問句和否定要整套一致，不能把兩種拌在一起。
> ✗ Do you have got a pen? → ✓ Do you have a pen? / Have you got a pen?
## 動作的 have 不能用 got
have breakfast、have a shower、have a break、have fun 這些是「動作」，只能用 have，不能換成 got。

# err
S: My dad [have|has] a motorcycle. || 我爸有一台機車。
W: 第三人稱單數 → has。
S: I [don't have got|haven't got] any money. || 我沒有錢。
W: have got 的否定是 haven't got（不要混用）。
S: She [has got|has] a shower every morning. || 她每天早上洗澡。
W: 動作 have a shower 不能用 got，用 has a shower。
