# zh
## 句型三：主詞＋及物動詞＋受詞
及物動詞（transitive verb）的動作必須「作用在某個東西或人身上」，所以後面一定要接<b>受詞</b>，句子才完整。只說 <span class="en">I bought.</span>（我買了）會讓人想問「買了什麼？」
受詞可以是：
表：受詞的種類
| 受詞種類 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 名詞 | She plays <b>the piano</b>. |
| 代名詞（受格） | I love <b>you</b>. |
| 動名詞 V-ing | He enjoys <b>reading</b>. |
| 不定詞 to V | I want <b>to go</b> home. |
| 名詞子句 | I know <b>that he is right</b>. |

## 後面直接接受詞、不加介系詞的動詞
這些動詞在中文翻譯時像是要加「關於、進入、和」，但英文不需要介系詞：
| 動詞 | ✗ 錯誤 | ✓ 正確 |
|---|---|---|
| discuss 討論 | discuss about the plan | discuss the plan |
| enter 進入 | enter into the room | enter the room |
| marry 和…結婚 | marry with him | marry him |
| reach 到達 | reach to the top | reach the top |
| attend 參加 | attend to the meeting | attend the meeting |
| answer 回答 | answer to the question | answer the question |
| approach 接近 | approach to the door | approach the door |

## 接 V-ing 還是 to V？
| 只接 V-ing | 只接 to V | 兩者皆可 |
|---|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, mind, practice, keep, avoid | want, hope, decide, plan, need, agree | like, love, start, begin |
| I <b>finished doing</b> my homework. | She <b>decided to study</b> abroad. | It <b>started to rain / raining</b>. |
> 詳細規則在進階課「Gerunds & Infinitives」。

# mc
Q: We discussed ___ in class. || 我們在課堂上討論了這個問題。
A: about the problem | the problem* | on the problem
W: discuss 是及物動詞，直接接受詞，不加 about。
Q: My brother enjoys ___ video games. || 我弟弟很喜歡打電動。
A: to play | playing* | play
W: enjoy 後面接動名詞 V-ing。
Q: She wants ___ a doctor. || 她想當醫生。
A: being | to be* | be
W: want 後面接不定詞 to V。
Q: The teacher entered ___. || 老師走進教室。
A: into the classroom | the classroom* | in the classroom
W: enter 是及物動詞，直接接 the classroom。

# err
S: She married [with a|a] doctor. || 她嫁給了一位醫生。
W: marry 直接接受詞，不加 with。
S: Please answer [to my|my] question. || 請回答我的問題。
W: answer 是及物動詞，後面直接接受詞。
S: I finished [to clean|cleaning] my room. || 我打掃完房間了。
W: finish 後面要接動名詞 V-ing。

# mk
- enjoy 後面要接動名詞：enjoy playing。
- discuss 是及物動詞，不加 about。
- enter 是及物動詞，不加 into。
