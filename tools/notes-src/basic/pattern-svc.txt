# zh
## 句型二 S + V + C：連綴動詞後面接補語
<b>連綴動詞</b>本身意思不完整，後面要接<b>補語</b>（名詞或形容詞）來描述<b>主詞</b>。
表：常見連綴動詞
| 類型 | 動詞 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be / 變成 | be, become, get, turn | She <b>became</b> a doctor. |
| 看似 | seem, appear, look | You <b>look</b> tired. |
| 感官 | feel, taste, smell, sound | The soup <b>tastes</b> good. |
## 補語用形容詞，不是副詞
連綴動詞後面接<b>形容詞</b>，因為它描述的是主詞（名詞），不是動作。
| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| She looks beautifully. | She looks <b>beautiful</b>. |
| It sounds greatly. | It sounds <b>great</b>. |
> 同一個動詞可能有兩種用法：<span class="en">She <b>looked</b> happy.</span>（連綴，+形容詞）vs <span class="en">She <b>looked</b> at me.</span>（一般動詞，+介系詞）。
# mc
Q: The music sounds ___. || 這音樂聽起來很棒。
A: wonderful* | wonderfully | wonder
W: 連綴動詞 sound + 形容詞 wonderful。
Q: He ___ angry when he heard it. || 他聽到時變得很生氣。
A: became* | became to | was become
W: become + 形容詞補語，不加 to。
Q: This milk ___ bad. Don't drink it. || 這牛奶聞起來壞了。
A: smells* | smells badly | is smell
W: smell 連綴動詞 + 形容詞 bad。
# err
S: You look [tiredly|tired] today. || 你今天看起來很累。
W: 連綴動詞 look 後接形容詞 tired。
S: The plan sounds [perfectly|perfect]. || 這計畫聽起來很完美。
W: sound 後接形容詞 perfect。
S: She became [to a star|a star]. || 她變成了明星。
W: become 後面直接接補語，不加 to。
