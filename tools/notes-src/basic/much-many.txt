# zh
## many 和 much：都是「很多」
英文的「很多」要看後面的名詞能不能數。
表：many / much / a lot of 比較
| 字 | 接什麼名詞 | 常用在 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| many | 複數可數名詞 | 肯定、否定、疑問句 | I have <b>many</b> friends. |
| much | 不可數名詞 | 否定句、疑問句 | I don't have <b>much</b> time. |
| a lot of / lots of | 兩者都可以 | 肯定句最自然 | She drinks <b>a lot of</b> water. |
註：much 在肯定句聽起來比較正式或生硬，日常說話多用 a lot of：I have <b>a lot of</b> homework.

## 問「多少」
| 問句 | 接 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| How many…? | 複數可數名詞 | <b>How many</b> students are there? |
| How much…? | 不可數名詞 | <b>How much</b> milk do you need? |
| How much…?（價錢） | — | <b>How much</b> is this shirt? |

## 「少」的說法
| | 可數 | 不可數 |
|---|---|---|
| 一些（正面） | a few friends | a little money |
| 很少（負面） | few friends | little money |
| 太多 | too many cars | too much sugar |
> a few / a little 是「有一些」；few / little 是「幾乎沒有」。

# mc
Q: How ___ water do you drink every day? || 你每天喝多少水？
A: many | much* | lot
W: water 不可數，用 How much。
Q: There are ___ cars on the road today. || 今天路上有很多車。
A: much | many* | a little
W: cars 是複數可數名詞，用 many。
Q: I don't have ___ free time this week. || 這週我沒什麼空閒時間。
A: many | much* | a few
W: time（時間）不可數，否定句用 much。
Q: Don't put ___ salt in the soup. || 湯裡別放太多鹽。
A: too many | too much* | too few
W: salt 不可數，用 too much。

# err
S: How [much|many] brothers do you have? || 你有幾個兄弟？
W: brothers 是可數名詞，用 How many。
S: She has [many|a lot of] money. || 她有很多錢。
W: money 不可數，不能用 many；肯定句用 a lot of。
S: We have [a few|a little] milk left. || 我們還剩一點牛奶。
W: milk 不可數，用 a little。
