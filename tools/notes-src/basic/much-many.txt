# zh
## many / much / a lot of：數量詞
說「多」時，要看名詞是可數還是不可數。
表：怎麼選
| 數量詞 | 配什麼 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| many | 可數複數 | many books |
| much | 不可數 | much time |
| a lot of / lots of | 兩者皆可 | a lot of books / water |
| a few | 可數複數 | a few apples |
| a little | 不可數 | a little milk |
## 問數量：How many / How much
可數用 <b>How many</b>，不可數用 <b>How much</b>。
> much 多用在否定句和問句；肯定句常用 a lot of：✓ I have a lot of friends.（比 many friends 更口語）
# mc
Q: How ___ sugar do you want? || 你要多少糖？
A: many | much* | few
W: sugar 不可數 → How much。
Q: There are ___ cars on the road. || 路上有很多車。
A: much | a lot of* | a little
W: cars 可數、肯定句 → a lot of。
Q: I have ___ time before the bus comes. || 公車來之前我有一點時間。
A: a few | a little* | many
W: time 不可數 → a little。
# err
S: She doesn't have [many|much] experience. || 她經驗不多。
W: experience 不可數 → much。
S: How [much|many] eggs do we need? || 我們需要幾顆蛋？
W: eggs 可數 → How many。
S: I read [much|many] books last month. || 我上個月讀了很多書。
W: books 可數 → many。
# mk
- books 可數，用 many。
- money 不可數，用 much。
- apples 可數，用 How many。
