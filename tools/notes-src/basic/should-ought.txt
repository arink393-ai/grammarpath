# zh
## 給建議：should / ought to
should 和 ought to 都表示「應該」，用來給建議、表達什麼是對的或合理的。意思幾乎一樣，should 比較常用；ought to 稍微正式一點。
表：should 與 ought to 句型
| 句型 | should | ought to |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定 | You <b>should</b> rest. | You <b>ought to</b> rest. |
| 否定 | You <b>shouldn't</b> worry. | You <b>ought not to</b> worry. |
| 疑問 | <b>Should</b> I call her? | （很少用於疑問句） |
註：should 後面直接接原形；ought 後面一定要有 to。

## 建議的強弱
表：從弱到強的建議
| 說法 | 強度 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| You could… | 最弱，提供選項 | You <b>could</b> take a taxi. |
| Why don't you…? | 溫和建議 | <b>Why don't you</b> ask the teacher? |
| You should… / ought to… | 一般建議 | You <b>should</b> see a doctor. |
| You had better… | 強烈，不做會有後果 | You<b>'d better</b> hurry, or you'll miss the bus. |
| You must… | 義務、命令 | You <b>must</b> wear a seat belt. |
> had better 後面也接原形動詞，否定是 had better not：You'd better not be late.

## 常見用法
| 用途 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 給別人建議 | You <b>should</b> drink more water. |
| 詢問意見 | What <b>should</b> I do? |
| 表示預期 | The bus <b>should</b> be here soon.（照理說應該快到了） |
| I think + should | I think you <b>should</b> apologize. |
| I don't think + should | I <b>don't think</b> you <b>should</b> go.（否定放前面更自然） |

Q: You look tired. You ___ go to bed early. || 你看起來很累，應該早點睡。
A: should* | should to | ought
W: should 後面直接接原形動詞。
Q: We ought ___ save water. || 我們應該節約用水。
A: to* | 不填 | for
W: ought 後面一定要接 to。
Q: You ___ eat so much candy. It's bad for your teeth. || 你不應該吃那麼多糖。
A: should | shouldn't* | ought
W: 建議「不要做」用 shouldn't。
Q: You'd better ___ now, or you'll be late. || 你最好現在就走，不然會遲到。
A: leave* | to leave | leaving
W: had better 後面接原形動詞。
