# zh
## should / ought to：給建議
should 和 ought to 都表「應該、最好」，用來給建議。
表：兩個說法
| 說法 | 結構 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| should | should + 原形 | You <b>should</b> rest. |
| ought to | ought <b>to</b> + 原形 | You <b>ought to</b> apologize. |
## 用法重點
| 規則 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| should 後接原形 | should <b>go</b>（✗ should to go） |
| 否定 | shouldn't |
| 徵詢 | <b>Should</b> I…? |
> should 也可表「推測、應該會」：<span class="en">The train <b>should</b> arrive at six.</span>（照理說六點到）
# mc
Q: You're sick. You ___ see a doctor. || 你生病了，應該看醫生。
A: should* | should to | shoulds
W: 建議用 should + 原形。
Q: You ___ eat so much junk food. || 你不該吃那麼多垃圾食物。
A: should | shouldn't* | don't should
W: 勸別做用 shouldn't。
Q: You ___ thank her for the gift. || 你應該謝謝她的禮物。
A: ought | ought to* | should to
W: ought to + 原形。
# err
S: You [should to|should] call your mom. || 你應該打給你媽。
W: should 後接原形，不加 to。
S: We [ought|ought to] save money. || 我們應該存錢。
W: ought 後面要有 to。
S: She [shoulds|should] exercise more. || 她應該多運動。
W: should 不隨主詞加 s。
