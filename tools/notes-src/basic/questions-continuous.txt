# zh
## 現在進行式的問句
現在進行式本身就有 be 動詞（am / is / are），所以問句只要把 <b>be 動詞移到主詞前面</b>，不需要 do / does。
表：現在進行式問句
| 類型 | 公式 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Yes/No 問句 | Am/Is/Are + 主詞 + V-ing…? | <b>Are</b> you <b>listening</b>? |
| Wh- 問句 | Wh- + am/is/are + 主詞 + V-ing…? | What <b>are</b> you <b>doing</b>? |
| 問主詞 | Who/What + is + V-ing…? | Who <b>is singing</b>? |

## 簡答
| 問句 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|---|---|---|
| Are you studying? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| Is she sleeping? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| Are they playing? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

## 常見錯誤
| ✗ 錯誤 | 問題 | ✓ 正確 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you working? | 進行式不用 do | <b>Are</b> you working? |
| What you are doing? | be 動詞沒移前面 | What <b>are you</b> doing? |
| Is he play football? | 少了 -ing | Is he <b>playing</b> football? |

## 也可以問「近期的計畫」
現在進行式可以問已經安排好的未來行程：<span class="en">What <b>are</b> you <b>doing</b> this weekend?</span>（你這週末要做什麼？）<span class="en"><b>Are</b> you <b>coming</b> to the party tonight?</span>
> 常見問法：Where are you going?（你要去哪？）What are you looking at?（你在看什麼？）

A: Do | Are* | Is
W: 進行式問句用 be 動詞；your parents 複數用 Are。
Q: What ___ she cooking? It smells good. || 她在煮什麼？好香。
A: does | is* | do
W: 進行式的 Wh- 問句：What + is + 主詞 + V-ing。
Q: Is it ___ outside? || 外面在下雨嗎？
A: rain | raining* | rains
W: 進行式要用 V-ing。
Q: Who ___ on the phone? || 誰在講電話？
A: is talking* | does talking | are talk
W: Who 當主詞，接 is + V-ing。
