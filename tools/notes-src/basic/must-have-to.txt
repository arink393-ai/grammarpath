# zh
## must / have to：必須
must 和 have to 都表「必須」。must 常是說話者覺得重要或規定；have to 常是外在要求。
表：肯定
| 說法 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| must | You <b>must</b> stop at a red light. |
| have to（he/she/it → has to） | She <b>has to</b> work on Saturday. |
## 否定意思完全不同！
| 說法 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| mustn't | 禁止（不可以） | You <b>mustn't</b> touch that. |
| don't have to | 不必（沒必要） | You <b>don't have to</b> pay. |
> must 沒有過去式，過去用 <b>had to</b>：<span class="en">I <b>had to</b> wait an hour.</span>
# mc
Q: You ___ wear a seatbelt in the car. || 你在車上必須繫安全帶。
A: must* | must to | have
W: 規定用 must + 原形。
Q: Tomorrow is a holiday, so I ___ get up early. || 明天放假，我不必早起。
A: mustn't | don't have to* | must not
W: 不必用 don't have to。
Q: My sister ___ wear glasses to read. || 我妹讀書必須戴眼鏡。
A: have to | has to* | must to
W: she → has to。
# err
S: You [must to|must] follow the rules. || 你必須遵守規則。
W: must 後接原形，不加 to。
S: You [mustn't|don't have to] come if you're busy. || 你忙的話不必來。
W: 「不必」用 don't have to（mustn't 是禁止）。
S: Yesterday I [must|had to] work late. || 我昨天必須加班。
W: must 無過去式，過去用 had to。
