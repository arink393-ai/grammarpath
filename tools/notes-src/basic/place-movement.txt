# zh
## 位置介系詞：在哪裡？
表：位置介系詞
| 介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| in | 在…裡面 | The cat is <b>in</b> the box. |
| on | 在…上面（接觸） | The book is <b>on</b> the desk. |
| under | 在…下面 | The ball is <b>under</b> the chair. |
| above / over | 在…上方（不接觸） | The lamp is <b>above</b> the table. |
| behind | 在…後面 | The garden is <b>behind</b> the house. |
| in front of | 在…前面 | The bus stopped <b>in front of</b> the school. |
| next to / beside | 在…旁邊 | Sit <b>next to</b> me. |
| between | 在（兩者）之間 | The bank is <b>between</b> the café and the park. |
| among | 在（三者以上）之中 | a house <b>among</b> the trees |
| across from / opposite | 在…對面 | The store is <b>across from</b> the station. |
| near | 在…附近 | I live <b>near</b> the school. |

## 移動介系詞：往哪裡去？
表：移動介系詞
| 介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| to | 到…（目的地） | We walked <b>to</b> the park. |
| into | 進入…裡面 | He jumped <b>into</b> the pool. |
| out of | 從…出來 | She ran <b>out of</b> the room. |
| onto | 到…上面 | The cat jumped <b>onto</b> the table. |
| across | 橫越（平面） | walk <b>across</b> the street |
| through | 穿過（立體空間） | drive <b>through</b> the tunnel |
| along | 沿著 | walk <b>along</b> the river |
| up / down | 往上／往下 | climb <b>up</b> the hill |
| past | 經過 | walk <b>past</b> the bank |
| around | 繞著、在…周圍 | run <b>around</b> the track |
| toward(s) | 朝向 | walk <b>toward</b> the door |

## in vs. into、on vs. onto
| 靜止的位置 | 移動的方向 |
|---|---|
| The fish is <b>in</b> the water. | The boy jumped <b>into</b> the water. |
| The cat is <b>on</b> the roof. | The cat climbed <b>onto</b> the roof. |
> go home / go there / go abroad / go downtown：home、there 是副詞，前面不加 to。

## across vs. through
across 是從平面的一邊到另一邊（馬路、河、橋）；through 是穿過有包圍感的空間（森林、隧道、人群、窗戶）。

# mc
Q: The children ran ___ the classroom when the bell rang. || 鐘一響，孩子們就跑出教室。
A: out of* | out | from in
W: 從裡面到外面用 out of。
Q: The train went ___ a long tunnel. || 火車穿過一條長隧道。
A: across | through* | along
W: 穿過有包圍感的空間（隧道）用 through。
Q: The post office is ___ the bank and the bakery. || 郵局在銀行和麵包店中間。
A: among | between* | across
W: 在「兩者之間」用 between。
Q: Let's go ___ now. It's late. || 我們現在回家吧，很晚了。
A: to home | home* | at home
W: home 在這裡是副詞，go home 不加 to。

# err
S: She jumped [in|into] the swimming pool. || 她跳進游泳池。
W: 表示「進入」的移動方向用 into。
S: We walked [through|across] the bridge. || 我們走過那座橋。
W: 從橋的一端到另一端（平面）用 across。
S: I went [to there|there] last year. || 我去年去過那裡。
W: there 是副詞，前面不加 to。

# mk
- 走進去是移動，用 into。
- arrive 不接 to；大城市用 arrive in。
- 「穿過」是 through；threw 是 throw 的過去式。
