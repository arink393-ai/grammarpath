# zh
## Yes/No 問句：用「是不是」回答
Yes/No 問句把 <b>be 動詞</b>或<b>助動詞</b>移到句首。
表：兩種結構
| 動詞類型 | 結構 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be 動詞 | Be + 主詞…? | <b>Are</b> you ready? |
| 一般動詞 | Do/Does + 主詞 + 原形…? | <b>Does</b> he play? |
| 情態助動詞 | Can/Will… + 主詞…? | <b>Can</b> you swim? |
## 簡答：用對應的助動詞
| 問句 | 簡答 |
|---|---|
| Do you…? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| Is she…? | Yes, she is. / No, she isn't. |
| Can you…? | Yes, I can. / No, I can't. |
> 有 Does/Did 的問句，主動詞要回<b>原形</b>：✗ Does he likes it? → ✓ Does he <b>like</b> it?
# mc
Q: ___ they students? || 他們是學生嗎？
A: Do | Does | Are*
W: be 動詞問句、they → Are。
Q: ___ your sister like music? || 你姐姐喜歡音樂嗎？
A: Do | Does* | Is
W: sister → Does + 原形。
Q: 'Do you have a pet?' — 'No, I ___.' || 不，我沒有。
A: don't* | doesn't | am not
W: 簡答用 don't。
# err
S: [You are|Are you] hungry? || 你餓了嗎？
W: be 動詞問句把 be 移到句首。
S: [Do|Does] she speak Japanese? || 她會說日文嗎？
W: she → Does。
S: Does he [plays|play] basketball? || 他打籃球嗎？
W: 有 does，動詞回原形 play。
