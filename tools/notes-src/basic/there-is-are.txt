# zh
## There is / There are：表示「有」
用 There is/are 說某地「有」某物。動詞的單複數要看<b>後面的名詞</b>。
表：怎麼選
| 後面的名詞 | 用 |
|---|---|
| 單數 | There is a cat. |
| 不可數 | There is some milk. |
| 複數 | There are two dogs. |
## 否定、問句、過去式
| 類型 | 現在 | 過去 |
|---|---|---|
| 否定 | There isn't / aren't | There wasn't / weren't |
| 問句 | Is / Are there…? | Was / Were there…? |
> 列舉多個東西時，動詞跟著<b>最靠近的第一個名詞</b>：<span class="en">There <b>is</b> a pen and two books. / There <b>are</b> two books and a pen.</span>
# mc
Q: ___ some milk in the glass. || 杯子裡有一些牛奶。
A: There is* | There are | They are
W: milk 不可數 → There is。
Q: ___ three books on the table. || 桌上有三本書。
A: There is | There are* | It is
W: three books 複數 → There are。
Q: ___ any eggs in the fridge? || 冰箱裡有蛋嗎？
A: Is there | Are there* | There are
W: eggs 複數、問句 → Are there。
# err
S: There [is|are] many people here. || 這裡有很多人。
W: many people 複數 → There are。
S: There [are|is] a dog in the garden. || 花園裡有一隻狗。
W: a dog 單數 → There is。
S: [There was|Was there] a party last night? || 昨晚有派對嗎？
W: 過去問句 → Was there…？
