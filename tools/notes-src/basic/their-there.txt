# zh
## their / there / they're：同音，意思完全不同
這三個字唸起來一樣，但用法不同。
表：三者比較
| 字 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| their | 他們的（+名詞） | <b>their</b> house, <b>their</b> dog |
| there | 那裡／存在 | over <b>there</b>; <b>There</b> is a cat. |
| they're | they are 的縮寫 | <b>They're</b> happy. |
## 判斷訣竅
| 問自己 | 答案 | 用 |
|---|---|---|
| 能換成 "they are" 嗎？ | 能 | they're |
| 指地點，或「有…」？ | 是 | there |
| 後面接名詞、表「他們的」？ | 是 | their |
> ✗ Their going home. → ✓ <b>They're</b> going home.（＝ They are going home）
# mc
Q: ___ books are on the desk. || 他們的書在桌上。
A: Their* | There | They're
W: 後接名詞 books、表「他們的」→ Their。
Q: ___ is a dog in the yard. || 院子裡有一隻狗。
A: Their | There* | They're
W: There is（存在）。
Q: ___ going to win the game. || 他們會贏這場比賽。
A: Their | There | They're*
W: ＝ They are → They're。
# err
S: [Their|They're] my classmates. || 他們是我的同學。
W: ＝ They are → They're。
S: Look over [their|there]! || 看那邊！
W: 指地點用 there。
S: [There|Their] car is red. || 他們的車是紅色的。
W: 後接名詞 car、表「他們的」→ Their。
# mk
- 能還原成 they are → they're。
- 指地點用 there。
- 表「他們的」+名詞用 their。
- 所有格用 their。
