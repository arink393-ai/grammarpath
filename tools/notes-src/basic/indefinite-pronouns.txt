# zh
## 不定代名詞：some- / any- / every- / no-
把 some-/any-/every-/no- 和 -one/-body（人）、-thing（物）、-where（地點）組合，就能泛指人、事、地。
表：組合
| 字首 | 人 | 物 | 地 |
|---|---|---|---|
| some- | someone | something | somewhere |
| any- | anyone | anything | anywhere |
| every- | everyone | everything | everywhere |
| no- | no one | nothing | nowhere |
## 兩個重點
| 重點 | 說明 |
|---|---|
| some- vs any- | 肯定用 some-；否定、問句用 any- |
| 當單數 | Everyone <b>is</b> here. / Nothing <b>is</b> easy. |
> no-、nothing 本身已是否定，<b>不再加 not</b>：✗ I don't have nothing → ✓ I have nothing / I don't have anything。邀請、請求的問句仍可用 some-：<span class="en">Would you like <b>something</b> to drink?</span>
# mc
Q: There isn't ___ in the fridge. || 冰箱裡什麼都沒有。
A: something | anything* | everything
W: 否定句用 any- → anything。
Q: ___ in the room was surprised. || 房間裡每個人都很驚訝。
A: Everyone* | Everyones | Every ones
W: Everyone 當單數主詞。
Q: Would you like ___ to eat? || 你想吃點什麼嗎？
A: anything | something* | nothing
W: 邀請的問句用 something。
# err
S: I don't know [nobody|anybody] here. || 我在這裡誰都不認識。
W: 否定句用 anybody（不和 not 並用）。
S: Everybody [are|is] welcome. || 歡迎每個人。
W: Everybody 當單數 → is。
S: Does anyone [knows|know] the way? || 有人知道路嗎？
W: 有 does，動詞回原形 know。
# mk
- 否定句用 anything；或用 nothing。
- 不定代名詞當單數 → is。
- 有 does，動詞回原形 know。
- 肯定句用 someone。
