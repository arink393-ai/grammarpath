# zh
## so 還是 such？
兩者都表「如此…以致於」，差別在後面接什麼：<b>so</b> 接形容詞／副詞，<b>such</b> 接名詞片語。
表：so vs. such
| 用 so | 用 such |
|---|---|
| so + 形容詞：so cold | such + a/an + 形容詞 + 單數名詞：such a cold day |
| so + 副詞：so fast | such + 形容詞 + 複數名詞：such nice people |
| so + many／much + 名詞：so many people | such + 形容詞 + 不可數名詞：such bad weather |

## 結果子句 that
that 帶出「結果」，口語中常省略：
<span class="en">It was <b>so</b> hot <b>that</b> we left. ＝ It was so hot we left.</span>

## 數量用 so，不用 such
| ✗ 錯誤 | ✓ 正確 |
|---|---|
| such many people | <b>so</b> many people |
| such much time | <b>so</b> much time |

## 快速判斷
後面第一個字是「形容詞／副詞」→ so；是「(a/an) 名詞片語」→ such。
| 例 | 選擇 |
|---|---|
| ___ tall | so（形容詞） |
| ___ a tall man | such（名詞片語） |
> 數量詞 many／much／few／little 前面一律用 so。

# mc
Q: The box was ___ heavy that I couldn't lift it. || 箱子重到我抬不起來。
A: such | so* | such a
W: 形容詞 heavy 前用 so。
Q: It was ___ exciting game that nobody sat down. || 比賽刺激到沒人坐下。
A: so | such an* | so an
W: 名詞片語 an exciting game 用 such an。
Q: They are ___ kind people that everyone trusts them. || 他們人好到大家都信任。
A: so | such* | such a
W: 複數名詞前用 such（不加 a）。
Q: There was ___ much traffic that we were late. || 車多到我們遲到了。
A: such | so* | such a
W: 數量 much 前用 so。

# err
S: It was [a so|such a] beautiful day that we went out. || 天氣好到我們出門了。
W: 名詞片語 a beautiful day 前用 such a。
S: She is [such|so] clever that she solved it fast. || 她聰明到很快就解開了。
W: 形容詞 clever 單用 so。
S: There were [such|so] many fans that the gate broke. || 球迷多到大門都被擠壞了。
W: many + 名詞用 so。

# mk
- 形容詞／副詞前用 so。
- (a/an) 名詞片語前用 such。
- 數量 many／much 前用 so，不用 such。
- that 帶出結果子句，可省略。
