# zh
## 主動 vs. 被動
<b>主動語態</b>強調「誰做了這件事」；<b>被動語態</b>強調「什麼東西被做了」，動作的執行者不重要、不知道、或不想說時就用被動。
公式：<b>be 動詞 + 過去分詞（p.p.）</b>（+ by + 執行者）
表：主動改被動
| | 主詞 | 動詞 | 受詞 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 主動 | Tom | broke | the window. |
| 被動 | The window | was broken | by Tom. |
註：步驟：① 受詞移到句首當主詞 ② 動詞改成 be + p.p.（時態不變） ③ 原主詞前加 by（可省略）。

## 各時態的被動式
表：被動語態時態總表（以 clean 為例）
| 時態 | 主動 | 被動 |
|---|---|---|
| 現在簡單式 | They clean the room. | The room <b>is cleaned</b>. |
| 現在進行式 | They are cleaning the room. | The room <b>is being cleaned</b>. |
| 過去簡單式 | They cleaned the room. | The room <b>was cleaned</b>. |
| 過去進行式 | They were cleaning the room. | The room <b>was being cleaned</b>. |
| 現在完成式 | They have cleaned the room. | The room <b>has been cleaned</b>. |
| 過去完成式 | They had cleaned the room. | The room <b>had been cleaned</b>. |
| 未來式 | They will clean the room. | The room <b>will be cleaned</b>. |
| 助動詞 | They must clean the room. | The room <b>must be cleaned</b>. |

## 什麼時候用被動？
| 情況 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 不知道誰做的 | My bike <b>was stolen</b> last night. |
| 執行者不重要 | English <b>is spoken</b> all over the world. |
| 新聞、科學、正式文章 | The museum <b>was built</b> in 1908. |
| 強調受影響的人事物 | The boy <b>was bitten</b> by a dog. |

## 不能用被動的動詞
<b>不及物動詞</b>沒有受詞，不能改被動：happen, arrive, die, appear, disappear, occur, rise, belong
<span class="en">The accident <b>happened</b> at night.</span>（✗ was happened）

## 雙受詞的被動
句型四有兩個受詞，可以各自當被動的主詞：
<span class="en">They gave him a prize. → He <b>was given</b> a prize. / A prize <b>was given</b> to him.</span>
> 不規則動詞的 p.p. 要熟：break → broken, give → given, write → written, steal → stolen, speak → spoken。

# mc
Q: This bridge ___ in 1990. || 這座橋建於 1990 年。
A: built | was built* | was build
W: 橋「被」建造，過去式被動：was built。
Q: The letters ___ every morning. || 信件每天早上都會被寄送。
A: deliver | are delivered* | are delivering
W: 信「被」寄送，現在簡單式被動：are + p.p.。
Q: The car is ___ right now. || 車子正在被修理。
A: repairing | being repaired* | repaired being
W: 現在進行式被動：is being + p.p.。
Q: What ___ at the party last night? || 昨晚派對上發生了什麼事？
A: was happened | happened* | was happening by
W: happen 是不及物動詞，不能用被動。

# err
S: The thief [caught|was caught] by the police. || 小偷被警察抓到了。
W: 小偷是「被」抓，要用 was caught。
S: The song was [wrote|written] by a teenager. || 這首歌是一個青少年寫的。
W: 被動要用過去分詞 written。
S: The homework must [finish|be finished] by Friday. || 作業必須在星期五前完成。
W: 助動詞的被動是 must be + p.p.。

# mk
