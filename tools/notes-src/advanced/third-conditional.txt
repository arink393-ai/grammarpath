# zh
## 第三條件句：與過去事實相反的假設
第三條件句用來假設<b>過去沒有發生</b>的事，常表達後悔或遺憾。因為是過去，結果已經無法改變。
公式：<b>If + had + p.p., would have + p.p.</b>
<span class="en">If I <b>had studied</b> harder, I <b>would have passed</b> the test.</span>（如果我當時更用功，就會考過了——但我沒有，所以沒考過。）
表：條件句家族
| 種類 | if 子句 | 主要子句 | 時間 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 第一 | 現在式 | will + V | 未來（可能） | If it rains, I'll stay home. |
| 第二 | 過去式 | would + V | 現在（相反） | If I had money, I'd buy it. |
| 第三 | had + p.p. | would have + p.p. | 過去（相反） | If I had known, I would have come. |

## 事實是相反的
第三條件句的肯定，事實是否定；否定，事實是肯定：
表：假設 vs. 事實
| 假設 | 事實 |
|---|---|
| If I <b>had left</b> earlier, I <b>wouldn't have missed</b> the bus. | I didn't leave early, so I missed the bus. |
| If she <b>hadn't helped</b> me, I <b>would have failed</b>. | She helped me, so I didn't fail. |

## 主要子句的變化
| 助動詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| would have p.p. | 就會… | I <b>would have called</b> you. |
| could have p.p. | 就能… | We <b>could have won</b>. |
| might have p.p. | 可能就會… | He <b>might have stayed</b>. |

## 混合條件句
過去的條件影響<b>現在</b>的結果：<b>If + had p.p., would + 原形</b>
<span class="en">If I <b>had taken</b> the medicine, I <b>would feel</b> better now.</span>（如果我當時有吃藥，現在就會好一點。）
> 倒裝寫法：<b>Had I known</b>, I would have told you.（= If I had known）

## 常見錯誤
| ✗ 錯誤 | ✓ 正確 |
|---|---|
| If I would have known | If I <b>had</b> known |
| I would helped | I would <b>have</b> helped |
| If we didn't miss the bus, … would have | If we <b>hadn't missed</b> the bus |

# mc
Q: If you ___ me, I would have picked you up. || 如果你有打給我，我就會去接你了。
A: called | had called* | would call
W: 與過去事實相反，if 子句用 had + p.p.。
Q: If it hadn't rained, we ___ the game. || 如果沒下雨，我們就會贏得比賽了。
A: would win | would have won* | had won
W: 主要子句用 would have + p.p.。
Q: ___ I known about the party, I would have gone. || 要是我早知道有派對，我就會去了。
A: Have | Had* | If
W: 倒裝寫法：Had I known = If I had known。
Q: If I had slept earlier last night, I ___ so tired now. || 如果我昨晚早點睡，現在就不會那麼累了。
A: wouldn't have been | wouldn't be* | won't be
W: 混合條件句：過去的條件 → 現在的結果，用 would + 原形。
