# zh
## 片語動詞：動詞＋小品詞＝新意思
片語動詞由<b>動詞＋副詞或介系詞</b>組成，意思常常和原本動詞差很多，要整組記：<span class="en">give（給）→ give <b>up</b>（放棄）</span>。
表：常見片語動詞
| 片語動詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| look after | 照顧 | She <b>looks after</b> her grandma. |
| look for | 尋找 | I'm <b>looking for</b> my keys. |
| look up | 查（字典） | <b>Look up</b> the word. |
| give up | 放棄 | Don't <b>give up</b>! |
| put on / take off | 穿上／脫下 | <b>Put on</b> your coat. |
| turn on / turn off | 打開／關掉（電器） | <b>Turn off</b> the light. |
| pick up | 撿起、接（人） | I'll <b>pick</b> you <b>up</b> at six. |
| find out | 發現、查出 | I <b>found out</b> the truth. |
| get up | 起床 | I <b>get up</b> at seven. |
| run out of | 用完 | We've <b>run out of</b> milk. |
| put off | 延期 | They <b>put off</b> the meeting. |
| get along with | 和…相處融洽 | I <b>get along with</b> my classmates. |
| put up with | 忍受 | I can't <b>put up with</b> the noise. |
| come up with | 想出 | She <b>came up with</b> a great idea. |

## 可分 vs. 不可分（最重要的考點）
表：受詞放哪裡？
| 類型 | 名詞受詞 | 代名詞受詞（it, them…） | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 可分（動詞＋副詞） | 中間或後面都可以 | <b>一定要放中間</b> | pick up, turn off, put on, give up, look up, throw away |
| | pick up <b>the box</b> / pick <b>the box</b> up | pick <b>it</b> up（✗ pick up it） | |
| 不可分（動詞＋介系詞） | 放後面 | 放後面 | look after, look for, get on, run into |
| | look after <b>the baby</b> | look after <b>her</b> | |
| 三字片語 | 放最後 | 放最後 | put up with, get along with, look forward to |
註：代名詞受詞遇到可分片語動詞，一定要夾在中間：Turn <b>it</b> off. / Put <b>them</b> on.

## 不及物的片語動詞
有些片語動詞後面不接受詞：<span class="en">The plane <b>took off</b>. / My car <b>broke down</b>. / Please <b>sit down</b>. / <b>Hurry up</b>!</span>
> 片語動詞多半比較口語。正式寫作可用單字替換：find out → discover、put off → postpone、give up → quit。

# mc
Q: This is your jacket. Please ___. || 這是你的外套，請穿上。
A: put on it | put it on* | put on
W: 代名詞 it 遇到可分片語動詞，要放中間。
Q: Who will ___ your dog when you're away? || 你不在時誰會照顧你的狗？
A: look after* | look for | look up
W: 照顧是 look after。
Q: We've run ___ sugar. Can you buy some? || 我們的糖用完了，你可以去買一些嗎？
A: out of* | out | off
W: 用完是 run out of + 名詞。
Q: I can't ___ his rude behavior anymore. || 我再也受不了他無禮的行為了。
A: put up | put up with* | put with up
W: 忍受是三字片語 put up with，受詞放最後。

# err
S: The music is too loud. Please turn [down it|it down]. || 音樂太大聲了，請轉小聲。
W: 代名詞 it 要放在可分片語動詞的中間。
S: I'm [looking|looking for] my glasses. Have you seen them? || 我在找我的眼鏡，你有看到嗎？
W: 「尋找」要用 look for。
S: She came [up|up with] a smart solution. || 她想出了一個聰明的解決辦法。
W: 「想出」是 come up with。

# mk
- 代名詞 it 要放在中間：picked it up。
- look after 是不可分的，受詞緊接在後面，very well 放句尾。
- 三字片語 put up with 不能拆開，受詞放最後。
