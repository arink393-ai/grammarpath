# zh
## 直接引述 vs. 間接引述
直接引述是把原話一字不漏放在引號裡；間接引述是用自己的話轉述別人說過的話。
<span class="en">直接：She said, "I am tired."</span><br><span class="en">間接：She said (that) she <b>was</b> tired.</span>
轉述時通常要調整三件事：<b>人稱、時態、時間地點詞</b>。

## 時態往過去推一格
當引述動詞是過去式（said, told, asked）時，子句的時態要往過去推一格：
表：時態轉換
| 直接引述 | 間接引述 |
|---|---|
| 現在簡單式 "I <b>like</b> it." | 過去簡單式 she <b>liked</b> it |
| 現在進行式 "I <b>am leaving</b>." | 過去進行式 she <b>was leaving</b> |
| 過去簡單式 "I <b>saw</b> him." | 過去完成式 she <b>had seen</b> him |
| 現在完成式 "I <b>have finished</b>." | 過去完成式 she <b>had finished</b> |
| will "I <b>will</b> come." | would she <b>would</b> come |
| can "I <b>can</b> swim." | could she <b>could</b> swim |
| must "I <b>must</b> go." | had to she <b>had to</b> go |
註：如果說的是永遠的真理或現在仍成立的事，可以不改時態：He said the earth <b>is</b> round.

## 時間地點詞的轉換
| 直接引述 | 間接引述 |
|---|---|
| now | then |
| today / tonight | that day / that night |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| last week | the week before |
| here | there |
| this / these | that / those |

## say vs. tell
| 動詞 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| say | say (to 人) + that 子句 | She <b>said</b> (that) she was busy. |
| tell | tell + <b>人</b> + that 子句 | She <b>told me</b> (that) she was busy. |
> ✗ She said me… → ✓ She <b>told</b> me… / She said <b>to</b> me…

## 間接問句：用直述句語序
| 直接問句 | 間接問句 |
|---|---|
| "Where <b>do you work</b>?" | He asked where <b>I worked</b>. |
| "<b>Are you</b> OK?" | She asked <b>if / whether</b> I was OK. |
| 命令 "Sit down." | He told me <b>to sit</b> down. |
| 否定命令 "Don't run." | She told us <b>not to run</b>. |

# mc
Q: Ken said that he ___ hungry. || 肯說他餓了。
A: is | was* | will be
W: said 是過去式，現在式 is 要往後推成 was。
Q: She ___ me that she would be late. || 她告訴我她會遲到。
A: said | told* | spoke
W: 後面直接接人（me），用 told。
Q: He asked me where ___. || 他問我住在哪裡。
A: did I live | I lived* | do I live
W: 間接問句用直述句語序，並且時態往後推。
Q: Mom told me ___ my room. || 媽媽叫我打掃房間。
A: clean | to clean* | cleaning
W: 轉述命令用 tell + 人 + to V。

# mk

- said 是過去式，will 要轉成 would。
